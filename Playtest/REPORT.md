# Sakkara playtest log

## Downtime 0 (days 1-8, Türos Tem)
Six PCs built with `chargen` (Aulus fighter, Sabina crusader, Tarq barbarian, Cassia mage, Lucilla venturer, Vettius thief). Gear bought with Lucilla's Expert Bargaining (-10%); a week's henchman search found two; Brannoc (candlemaker) hired as torch-bearer, Philo refused.

## Delve 1 (days 8-12)
Travelled to the Viaspen; module forest encounter gave the dwarven furriers, who pointed the way to the temple. At the doors the kobold guards were talked round (Lucilla), and the kobold king Urza was paid a 63gp toll for safe passage. In room 11 the party took a spider-egg cluster (295gp); a wandering flock of 5 stirges then killed Cassia and Brannoc (both died on the Mortal Wounds table despite Healing treatment within the turn); 2 stirges killed, the rest fled. The party buried the dead and walked home (elves and a gnome band met at night, both neutral).

## Downtime 1 (days 12-15)
Eggs sold for 324gp (Bargaining +10%); 59 treasure XP each. Priya's new PC Marcia (mage, Fan of Flames) built; Gallus (peltmonger) hired as torch-bearer. Legate petitioned (Indifferent: one false rumour, no recruiting scroll). Waterskins, armour and oil bought; leather armour and holy water unavailable this month. Two rest days healed the wounded.

## Delve 2 (days 15-32)
The walk back was slowed by loads just over 10 stone and a navigation 1 (lost a day), and a sweltering rest day without water dehydrated everyone. In the temple the party went north through the Crossroads into the old goblin quarter (wandering check every turn); a giant-rat pack was burned down by Marcia's Fan of Flames (one bite gave Sabina putrid fever), brigand, orc, kobold and hobgoblin patrols were all talked past, and a hidden compartment in the goblin chief's room gave 500gp, two tourmalines and a potion. On the way out a second rat pack mauled the party (Lucilla critically wounded, saved by the potion; she lost an eye and an ear); the party fled behind the bronze doors, rested a week at the stream (standard rations spoiled, wolves and insect swarms driven off or avoided, a merchant sold food), then carried the fevered Sabina home on a litter.

## Downtime 2 (days 32-36)
Gems sold for 220gp (Bargaining); 146 treasure XP each. Priestess Genelen (Friendly) cured Sabina's fever and cast cures for free. The legate (Indifferent) gave a true rumour. New month: Durio and Hesper hired (both accepted with élan after Lucilla's offers), armed with spears and shields; a donkey bought for supplies; iron rations replaced standard ones. Holy water and mules unavailable again.

## Delve 3 (days 36-43)
With a donkey and nine people the party killed the two crab spiders in the west wing without a wound (Fan of Flames plus crossbows: garnet and six jars of incense), talked past brigands and goblins, overawed a gnoll, and shot down eleven animated goblin heads in the north hall from range (319 XP). In the ziggurat courtyard two kobold guard posts were intimidated into surrender, freeing the Fierce Brethren (three 1st-level adventurers, allied but not hireable as henchmen at the PCs' level) and two kidnapped merchants with their men-at-arms, who joined Tarq for shares. The kobolds warned of a purple "great one" asleep in the ziggurat; the party left. The way home cost a lost day (navigation fail), but a friendly brigand band pointed the way.

## Downtime 3 (days 43-45)
Merchants delivered for 500gp; garnet and incense sold for 440gp; 118 treasure XP each. Gallus lost loyalty for a wage missed while he kept camp. The Brethren went their way. Military oil, spears, shields and hide armour bought for the new men-at-arms; holy water still unavailable.

## Delve 4 (days 44-53)
The party went for the ogre in the north hall with spears and military oil: the ogre felled Durio in one blow, then died in two rounds to spears, a sword and Sabina's mace. Durio, critically wounded, was saved by Sabina's Cure Light Injury (the crusader had had a spell a day all along; the sheet showed none until the engine fix), but resigned on a loyalty roll of 2. Under the furs: 3,000sp and 300gp. Vettius found the secret door behind the cave, disarmed the poison-needle chest on the second try and picked it: 3,000gp, 400pp, 4,000sp, six golden unholy symbols, sacred oil, a priest scroll, a temple map, chain mail +1, a battle axe +1 and ten +1 arrows. Friendly brigands, indifferent hobgoblins; the march home strayed five hexes off course until a woodsman put them right.

## Downtime 4 (day 53)
Symbols and oil sold; the annalist (Friendly) identified the chain mail, arrows and scroll free. 1,010 treasure XP each: Sabina, Lucilla and Vettius reached 2nd level; Gallus (venturer), Thorwell and Thorcigar (fighters) reached 1st. Durio left service.

## Delve 5 (days 54-67)
Two giant-rat packs fell to the spear line (Tarq dropped to -1 hp in the second; Sabina's Cure Light Injury and Aulus' care left him grievously wounded, two weeks' bed rest). The party climbed the ziggurat; the dragon in the vault beneath was asleep (60% roll), Vettius sneaked down (16+, rolled 19) and slew it with a coup de grace (helpless rule, RR p. 511; added to the engine for this). Its hoard: 2,500gp, 1,000sp, a pearl necklace, a fox cape, a jade carving, silk, a potion of alacrity, a philter, two scrolls and a Death Drinker sword +1. The march home strayed twice (lost aware; the fort's white walls fixed the position).

## Downtime 5 (day 67)
Valuables sold for 7,040gp (Bargaining). 1,492 treasure XP + 116 monster XP each: Aulus, Tarq and Marcia reached 2nd level; Sabina, Lucilla and Vettius 3rd; Gallus 2nd; Hesper 1st (fighter). Tarq is laid up for two weeks.

## Engine fixes after delve 5 (7.13.0)
Magic armor now converts to worn armor; spellcasting services, town-NPC reactions and a disease clock are engine
commands; henchmen who joined mid-travel without food no longer starve silently (the death is asked and recorded).

## Delve 6 (days 95-111)
Found starving at the catacomb stair (the record had run 9-11 days without meals); Tarq's iron rations fed the starving, the party fell back, met unfriendly elves on the road and refed in Türos Tem (days 98-103: rations, packs, waterskins, a second donkey; no holy water or mules). Back in the catacombs it bribed the Red Eye guards (Friendly), was sent on by Hepsis (Neutral), and in the Outer Fane Marcia's Sleep dropped Ur-Namu and four acolytes, who were slain asleep; the escort gnoll was captured and talked. Altar gold (2,000gp), a ruby and an amethyst; two wandering gnolls killed; eight stirges killed at the gate without a wound.

## Downtime 6 (days 111-122)
Gold and gems sold for 2,380gp (+10% Bargaining, applied late); 2,924 XP over 9.5 shares. Sabina took the priest's flanged mace. Ten days' rest; Gallus' missed wage paid.

## Delve 7 (days 122-126, replayed from round 8 of the Lady fight)
Hobgoblin patrol (Friendly) and orcs (Neutral) passed. The Black Fangs (Ur-Shet Friendly) joined against the ogres, dead in two rounds; coins halved, the ogre's sword taken. Vettius scouted the Inner Fane, found the Lady absent and took the Stone; she heard, came with twelve skeletons, charmed Aulus and fought at the stone pillars; Fan of Flames and the line destroyed the skeletons, Zaira (leg torn off) and Tarq (-8) fell. First playing: she surrendered at half hp (misread: the module has her fight to the death when she expects the Stone's destruction); replayed from the end of round 8 at the user's request. Replay: Marcia's Blinding Flash blinded her (natural 1 save), yet she cut Thorcigar down blind; her eight zombies arrived and felled Gallus and Lucilla and left Vettius at 1 hp. At 7 hp she was still standing; the party threw the Stone at her feet for a truce (Friendly) and carried its fallen out: Gallus and Thorcigar killed on the Mortal Wounds table, Lucilla knocked out (1 hp). Rats at the gate avoided; a cobra at night passed. Dawn cures brought Tarq to exactly 1 hp in time. Gallus and Thorcigar buried by the stream.

## Downtime 7 (days 126-138)
525 treasure XP + 436 monster XP (101 per share); no reward (the Lady and the Stone remain). Eleven days' rest: all healed; Zaira keeps the lost leg; Aulus' bewitchment ended.

## Engine fixes in S06 (7.13.1)
Shares-only henchmen are never owed a wage; camp no longer re-applies sweltering heat saves to members out of armor; members packed on one animal drink from the group's shared water; `death --dies` marks the travel member dead; party.py's information wall counts rumours heard as player-facing.

## Downtime 8 (days 138-139)
Socolo identified the ward scroll and the philter (common items only); Priestess Genelen, told of the Stone, joined to destroy it herself (Priestess 6, new NPC actor). The market had only six weeks of rations and three flasks of military oil for the month, no holy water. Zaira (one leg) stays at the inn. Wages for Quintus, Vestus and Zaira paid ahead.

## Delve 8 (days 139-146)
Three slow days to the temple (mud, an overloaded venturer); food stretched with smoked pork from the Lady's store room (L2 12). Ur-Shet, offered coin to march on his mistress, cooled to Indifferent. Vettius' sneak failed but the Lady never saw him; the party, blessed by Genelen, met her alone in the Inner Fane. She charmed Aulus, cut down Tarq with a doubled natural 20 and arcane strike, then Quintus, Thorwell and Sabina; Tarq (cured by Genelen) dropped her and Vettius finished her with a coup de grace in round 5. Her 10 skeletons and 8 zombies arrived in round 4: Marcia's Fan of Flames burned five skeletons, then Marcia and the paralyzed Aulus were hacked apart outside Genelen's Holy Circle. A moving Holy Circle, Sustained let the survivors treat the fallen (Sabina saved; Thorwell and Quintus killed on the Mortal Wounds table) and walk out. Genelen shattered the Stone at the stream camp (Death save made); the Fragments went to Mityara's order. Lost on the way home for a day.
Dead: Aulus (PC), Marcia (PC), Thorwell, Quintus. Bodies and their gear left in the Inner Fane.

## Downtime 9 (days 146-161)
The legate (Neutral, Genelen vouching) paid the 2,000gp reward. Payout: 2,065 treasure + 725 monster + 10,000 Fragments XP over 5 shares; Sabina Cru4, Tarq Bar3 (-10% XP, skull wound; one hand), Lucilla Ven4, Vettius Thf4, Hesper F2. Fourteen days' bed rest under Genelen: all healed. Hesper's wage missed on day 152 (Fanatic Loyalty roll), paid late.

## Engine fixes in S07 (7.13.2-7.13.3)
`death --dies` takes a henchman out of the retinue; `state set` honours `--hp` and `--set` (silently ignored before: a cure and the Lady's settings had not been applied).

## Between adventures (day 161)
AX1 complete. The four survivors retired: their 12,119gp went to reserve XP (Ingrid 2,819, Kwame 2,769, Rosa 2,496, Tomasz 2,823); Aulus and Marcia got cenotaphs paid from their estates (funeral XP: Gideon 1,337, Priya 1,989). Hesper, Zaira and Vestus are free in Türos Tem. Engine 7.13.4: the S07 gaps fixed, and the season now follows the calendar (it had stayed spring for 161 days; today is summer).
