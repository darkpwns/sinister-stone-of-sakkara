ENGINE FIXES, ENHANCEMENTS AND SUPPORTING TOOLS
Proposals from the Sakkara playtest, run 2, sessions 15-16 (days 132-148), with earlier open findings.
Sources: Playtest/judge-notes.txt ("Run 2 S15", "Run 2 S16"), Campaign/slips.jsonl, Campaign/lessons.yaml.
Priority: P1 = caused wrong play or a Judge's correction this run; P2 = cost time or needed hand work.

====================================================================================================
0. PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENT (applies to every item below)
====================================================================================================

0.1 No noticeable slowdown. No change may make the engine noticeably slower than engine 7.24.0 as used now.
    Baseline: engine/docs/speed/after-7.24.0.json (the measured per-command timings of the current release).
    Acceptance: on the same benchmark set, the median wall time per command and the time of a typical combat
    round and a typical turn of exploration stay within the noise of that baseline; any single command that
    grows must be justified and stay below what a player would notice at the table.
0.2 Faster is preferred, only without loss of accuracy. A speed-up is welcome only if outputs are unchanged:
    the same results for the same seeds, the same logged rolls (labels, visibility, order), the same refusals,
    the same state files byte for byte where nothing changed, and every rule number and source still named.
    Verify with golden runs (recorded command sequences replayed before and after) and `acks check`.
0.3 Design notes for the items below:
    - New checks (leak checks, load and water checks, label checks) must reuse state already loaded in the
      process (stateio, fastload caches) and work incrementally; no extra full replays of the inventory log.
    - New scans that run at save time (leak check, front-matter validation) run once per save, not per command.
    - New commands that replace many calls (one-command rounds, route walking) should make play faster overall
      by cutting process starts; run them in one process as `acks batch` and `jacks.py` already do.
    - Record each release's timings in engine/docs/speed/ as now, so a regression is visible.

====================================================================================================
A. ENGINE FIXES (bugs seen in play)
====================================================================================================

P1
A1. round-auto skips declared actions.
    Seen: it passed Faustina's declared spell three times (S14, S16 x2); it turned Galerius' declared
    withdrawal into an attack (S16).
    Fix: stop at any combatant with a declaration (spell, retreat, withdraw) whatever his standing order;
    resolve retreat and withdrawal as movement, never as an attack.

A2. Zone frontage does not block a passage.
    Seen: `state frontage mouth 2` limited attackers per defender, but two troglodytes walked from the hall
    into the 'tunnel' zone behind the two-man front and attacked Faustina (S16, Judge's correction).
    Fix: zone links with access through another zone (e.g. `state zone tunnel --via mouth`); while the via-zone
    is held (its frontage filled by defenders), movement into the zone behind is refused.

A3. Healing outside a fight goes to the wrong file.
    Seen: `cast ID "Cure Light Injury"` after a fight wrote the healing into the leftover combat-state.json,
    not the actor (Galerius stayed at 4 hp while the line said 4 -> 20; S16).
    Fix: `state end` archives and clears the combat state; `cast` with no active fight writes the actor.

A4. Conditions expire early.
    Seen: a 3-round Halt Humanoids paralysis was gone by round 3; rounds showed no countdown (S16).
    Fix: audit condition timers at round-end against their stated durations; show rounds left in `state show`.

A5. Nonlethal knockouts roll Mortal Wounds.
    Seen: a large raptor knocked out with nonlethal damage for capture died at `state end` (S16).
    Fix: a foe felled only by nonlethal damage is unconscious, not dying; `state end` asks for `capture`
    (or lists it as a JUDGE item) before closing.

A6. Load checks disagree and the march refusal has a hole.
    Seen: `move-rate` leaves out carried food and water, `travel` counts them (Galerius 9.2 st vs 11.2 st);
    load-before-march refuses only when a pack animal has room; three slips (S15 days 132, 133, 137).
    `play.py depart` fails a member who carries no food although the rollover shares food; satisfying it
    overloaded Varro.
    Fix: one weight calculation used by every command (gear, treasure, coins, supplies); `travel` refuses
    while any member is over his band and a lighter member or an animal could take the difference, naming the
    items to move; `depart` checks food for the party as shared, not per member.

A7. Water is not filled at known sources.
    Seen: the party camped beside the 21.20 well and the Krysivor without filling skins; the rollover took
    2-7 CON from everyone (S15, Judge's correction).
    Fix: `camp` or `travel` ending in a hex whose travel block names a water source fills the skins, or refuses
    while anyone carries under a day (ACKS_ACK to override).

P2
A8. Surprise note penalty refused after a reaction.
    Seen: the troglodytes' chameleon -2 (S15) and the giant toads' (S16) could not be applied once `reaction`
    had run.
    Fix: allow `--apply-note-penalty` until the first action, or apply it at the surprise roll with a waiver flag.

A9. Monster spells.
    Load a monster's spell list from its bestiary note (the Lady's was typed in by hand). Add Choking Grip
    (Death save at each of the caster's initiatives, concentration, target cannot act) and Holy Blessing
    (party-wide +1 attack, AC, morale, saves vs magical fear, 6 turns) to `cast`.

A10. "N or M" attack routines.
    Seen: Troglodyte "3 or 1 (2 claws, bite or weapon)" made one attack whatever the routine.
    Fix: parse alternatives into selectable routines; round-auto uses the first unless told otherwise.

A11. `encounter fight --perceived "x {n}"` leaves {n} in every label (S14, S15).

A12. `state aura add` has no save modifier; troglodyte stench is Death at -2. Add `--save-mod`.

A13. Weather for one day differs by command.
    Seen: `weather --at 22.20 --day N` (region R05.05) and `travel` (R06.05) gave different weather for the
    same days. Fix: one day's weather per hex whichever command rolls it first.

A14. `adventure start NAME` takes the name as the id ("adventure"). Refuse a positional name or take --name.

A15. A room's wandering rate leaks to the site.
    Seen: room 15's "none" (set with --wandering at the room) stayed after leaving it; a walk ran with no
    checks (S15). Fix: a rate set while in a room is the room's and ends when the party leaves it.

A16. `wander-check` does not reset the turn count; the next `turn` rolled a second check at the same minute.

A17. Open-roll labels are not checked for secrets.
    Seen: a label named Galerius' weapon "sword +1" before the players knew its bonus; only the snapshot
    caught it and the roll log cannot be edited (S16).
    Fix: `roll` (and every command that logs an open roll) refuses a label containing an unidentified lot's
    true name or a monster's name the party has not learned.

A18. Compaction breaks `inv check`.
    Seen: the archived lighting of lanterns still burning failed the check (S14 twice); fixed by hand with
    settings.lights_before. Fix: the checkpoint carries the ids of lights still lit.

A19. Earlier findings still open:
    - a wandering band's `reaction --faction` overwrites the whole faction's attitude (S14);
    - `restore` without --apply rolls and logs; `restore --apply` does not revive (holder status, hp, body
      items, party membership) (S12);
    - waterskins on donkeys or on a dead carrier do not serve the party (S12, S13);
    - `disease expose` and `cast` refuse a companion actor outside a fight (S14);
    - the helpless automatic hit is limited by size in `attack` (rules check wanted; S14).

====================================================================================================
B. ENGINE ENHANCEMENTS
====================================================================================================

B1. Retention offers. `retain ID --offer "50gp/month; item w32"` for a henchman whose loyalty roll resigns:
    rolls the hiring reaction and returns the answer as a CHOICE for the employer (e.g. "stays to the month's
    end: accept or withdraw?"); nothing is recorded until the choice. (S16: the Quintus offer was recorded as
    if accepted; user's correction.)

B2. Objects with hit points and triggers. `object add stone --hp 20 --harmed-by magic,str16-heavy
    --on-damage "call abominations 2000'" --on-destroy "striker Death save; within 5' Death save or mutation"`;
    blows through `attack`. (The Stone of Sakkara was resolved with plain rolls.)

B3. Compulsion as a condition. `state cond ID compelled:<object>`: the creature changes side when someone
    threatens the object; the condition ends when the object is destroyed or out of sight. Also serves charm
    effects and a master's control of undead.

B4. Site population lists. A lair's roster (who, hp, where) that wandering results and placed encounters draw
    from and subtract from; an empty roster gives "no one" instead of a phantom encounter (S16 needed
    `encounter end --unplayed` for it).

B5. Alarms within a site. `site alarm W3 --heard-in W6 --rounds 3`: a shriek or fight brings defined
    responders after N rounds (hand-ruled each time: the Lady, the catechists, the warren).

B6. Monster leaders from the bestiary's ecology lines: `state add --leader champion|sub-chieftain|chieftain`
    for any statblock whose ecology lists leaders (troglodyte leaders were set by editing the state file).

B7. Missiles and held lines: the precise-shooting refusal names which members can shoot past a held mouth,
    from the zones and engagements.

B8. Schedules that end themselves: `until_faction_destroyed: ID` (the troglodyte raid item was retired by hand).

B9. `party status`: one line per member with hp, spells left, load including supplies, food and water days,
    light, conditions and fatigue; one call before each decision point.

====================================================================================================
C. TOOLS OUTSIDE THE ENGINE (scripts that drive it)
====================================================================================================

C1. One command per fight round: `play.py round DECL.yaml`. Declarations, standing orders and positions in a
    small file; the script runs round-start, sets order `none` for declared casters and withdrawers so the
    runner stops for them, adds auras named in monster notes (stench, fear) and prints a compact log.
    Today a fight takes 10-20 tool calls; this makes it one per round (and faster overall: fewer processes).

C2. Departure check that fixes problems: `play.py ready --route ...`. Loads with supplies, water sources on the
    route, food shared across the party, light, wages, downtime records, training courses to pause, where the
    animals are stabled; writes an `inv give` / `supplies give` / `train pause` batch to fix what fails.
    (Would have caught the water, overload, training-pause and donkey slips of S15-S16.)

C3. Route walker from a site map: `play.py route 15 38 26 31 W6`. Reads the plain-text map in a site hub as a
    graph with distances; walks with turns, rests, relights and wandering checks; applies and ends room
    settings; stops at keyed rooms, encounters and anything left to the Judge.

C4. Site builder: `build_site.py` (the unprepped-sites steps as a script). Refuses to start until Campaign State
    `session:` is current (the S16 build rolls landed under session 15); rolls population from the bestiary
    ecology, hp lists, treasure and stocking; writes the hub and room skeletons, the wandering table from the
    roster and the Rulings row; checks that every [[link]] resolves.

C5. Terms before the roll: `ruling.py --terms "..." --roll 1d6`. Writes the Rulings row, then rolls hidden with
    a label citing it; searches existing rulings for a precedent first.

C6. Plan check against the player profiles: `party.py check-plan "<call>"`. Lists which profile breaking point
    or heuristic the call touches (Rosa leaving treasure, Ingrid's turn-back point, the combat-failure table
    rule, giving value away for nothing). Cheap compared with a party-agent call.

C7. Session log composer. Drafts "Where they went / Who they met / Treasure and XP" from the session's location
    changes, encounters, payouts and levels; builds the Judge block from the hidden-roll dump and the judge
    notes; validates the front matter of every state note (an unquoted colon in Campaign State broke dice.py
    in S16).

C8. Leak check on player-facing writes, run by save.sh: party notes, memory notes and session logs scanned for
    Judge-only facts and unrevealed item properties (S15: a memory note implied the chest's needle was real).

C9. Rest planner: `play.py rest-plan`. From wounds, conditions, bed rest and spell slots: days of rest needed,
    cures per day, food and water cost; checks that the place is a ruled safe rest (no wandering checks).

C10. Recurring wandering groups. Unique groups (Drusus' lost patrol, a troglodyte gang) tracked so a repeat
     result is the same band with its earlier attitude; a band led out does not reappear.

C11. Slip catcher. Watches commands for known slip patterns and records the slip automatically so lessons
     escalate without the Judge remembering: `state end` with a nonlethal-felled foe and no capture; travel or
     camp with skins under a day beside a known source; a trainer leaving town with a course running; a
     reaction rolled after a surprise note penalty was offered.
