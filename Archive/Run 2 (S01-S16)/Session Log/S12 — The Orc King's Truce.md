# Session S12 — The Orc King's Truce

(Replayed at the user's instruction: the first run of S12 is void; new table rule: until the Buried Temple is done,
travel only between Türos Tem and the temple. Rulings R2 S12; `Campaign/archive/S12-void/`.)

## Recap
Under the new rule the company set its sights on the temple and the level below. Severina stayed with her pups;
Hrodric waited for his pups' first trick and then marched. On the road: a ruined castle above the river near the fort,
a beetle nest skirted, dire wolves talked past, a lost day in the forest. In the temple the hobgoblins, bled by two
orc raids, sheltered the company and told it what lies below; the next morning the company bought them a truce from
the orc king. The week ends in the orcs' toll room, the stair below next.

## Where they went
- Day 82: Hrodric's three pups learned their first trick; out (Galerius, Faustina, Callista, Corvus, Hrodric; Varro, Quintus, Zaira, Marius). A ruined castle on a rise in 22.20. Giant bombardier beetles at their nest in 21.20: skirted. Camp.
- Day 83: farmers at night (passed); 20.19; dire wolves at night (Corvus: Indifferent).
- Day 84: navigation failed: lost in 21.19; at dawn Corvus sighted his farmland and fixed the way.
- Day 85: the stirges on the dead scout (Viaspen 4), passed by; heavy rain. Day 86: the temple (afternoon).
- Day 86: through to the hobgoblins: two orc raids since day 72 (repulsed; six hobgoblins dead). Thethamun told of level 2. Night in his barred chamber (stirges outside).
- Day 87: Crossroads goblins (Indifferent); the toll orcs (won over); King Apsa: a truce for the hobgoblins to day 120 for 100gp, the company's toll waived, and a hint about the Black Fangs.

## Who they met
