# Session S16 — The Warren Below

## Recap
Back to the temple through heat, rain and mud. Ur-Shet, who had lost a gnoll to the stinking ones, would not come at first. On day 141 the company followed their trail south-east: a flesh-thing burned with oil, then an ambush by five scaly men on the path round the bloody water; two died, one was taken, two fled. Zaira fell and lost a hand. In the Black Fangs' cavern the captive, overawed, told of the warren through Zaira; a bigger offer brought Ur-Shet and three gnolls. On day 143 the company went down the crack to the warren, held the tunnel mouth into the chieftain's hall, and broke the warband with sleep and holding prayers; the chieftain and every warrior died. Galerius beat the Stone half to pieces and Faustina broke it with Varro's mace; the red cloud passed her by. The Inner Fane's altar was smashed and hallowed. Home on day 145: the fragments to Genelen for Mityara's order, the legate's last reward, and levels for nearly everyone. Zaira left Callista's service; Quintus would stay only to the month's end for the Lady's axe and a double wage, so Galerius withdrew the offer and Quintus left as well.

## Who they met
- Ur-Shet and the Black Fangs (Friendly; partners against the stinking ones, paid in their coin).
- The stinking ones (troglodytes): the ambushers, their chieftain and his warband (destroyed); a captive (let go south).
- Drusus' lost brigands (two bands; pointed to the stair). Two starving hobgoblin deserters (fed). Ghouls in their hole (watched us pass).
- Feral war-mastiffs and giant toads on the farm tracks (let be); a great hawk (killed).
- Genelen (the fragments received). The legate (paid in full). Zaira and Quintus (left service).

- The captive (through Zaira): the warren's lay, the 'Heart' on a pillar of skulls, the chief who fed it a hyena-man.

- The stinking ones' warband is gone; their females and eggs remain below.
