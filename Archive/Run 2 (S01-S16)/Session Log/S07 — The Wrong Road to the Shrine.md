# Session S07 — The Wrong Road to the Shrine

## Recap
Downtime to day 45 (Severina hired Titus; Hrodric's pups tamed day 46), then five PCs set out as pilgrims for the
Shrine of the White Lady. Raiders were talked into friendship; the rain and a natural 1 sent the party east of the
shrine; nineteen haunts were evaded; the party came out at Türos Aster, where the legate's standing offer (clear the
bugbears from the Ruined Imperial Fortress: a baron's rank and the stronghold) became the kingdom's next goal. On the
road home a cache under a waystone held 12,300gp in gold and jewelry. The week ends on the road a day from Türos Tem.

## Where they went
- Day 45: Titus, a bookbinder, signs with Severina (the market out of backpacks, spears and shields: kitted from company stores).
- Day 46: Hrodric's pups tame; his courses paused. Pilgrims: Galerius, Faustina, Hrodric (and his eagle), Callista, Corvus; Severina, Marius, Petra, Titus stay. Sheep in 22.19; 24 raiders in 23.19 (Corvus surprised: no evasion; Neutral, then Friendly to Faustina's "poor pilgrims"). Camp in 23.19.
- Day 47: heavy rain; navigation failed (natural 1): strayed north-east. A rust-red ore seam (24.18; Callista could not name it). Nineteen haunts in 25.18, all surprised: evaded. Camp, lost unaware.
- Day 48: a day's march for a shrine that was not there: aware of being lost (25.17). Camp.
- Day 49: south-west in the hope of the forest's edge; strayed again; out at the Krysivor ford below **Türos Aster** (25.19). A night in the camp-followers' village.
- Day 50: Legate Nicus Balens (Indifferent): the offer on the Ruined Imperial Fortress (26.19). From the watchtower: the broken fort across the ford, smoke, hairy figures on the walls. Home by the road: **a cache under a waystone** (25.19): 6,000gp and four pieces of jewelry. Camp on the road in 24.19.
