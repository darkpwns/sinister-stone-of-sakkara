# Session S05 — The Ogre's Furs

## Recap
Four days of downtime in Türos Tem, each character at work: Hrodric and Severina training the war-dog pups, Corvus
surveying the farm tracks (now familiar: Pathfinding), Faustina working in Genelen's hospital (Genelen now Friendly),
Callista courting Malyn's apprentices (no introduction), Galerius drinking with the quartermaster (now Hostile). With
the new month Severina hired two 0th-level henchmen. Hrodric stayed in town with the pups while the rest (7 HD) went
for the ogre of L1 44: it shrugged off Slumber, broke Lupa's leg, then broke and was killed. Its bag held 3,000sp and
300gp. Six stirges and five friendly orcs on the way out; Lupa resigned. The week ends at dawn on day 35 in the forest,
a day from the fort.

## Where they went
- Day 27: Faustina at the hospital (Genelen **Friendly**: the nightmares, a curse; the sealed place in the Viaspen); Callista with the apprentices (Neutral); Galerius and Thales (**Hostile**). Corvus begins his survey.
- Day 28: Faustina and Genelen's good word with the legate: Indifferent (corrected), one rumour (feral elves). Apprentices Indifferent.
- Days 29-30: apprentices Neutral, then Indifferent; Corvus finishes (days 27-29). Month 1 ends.
- Day 31: Faustina's search for 0th-level henchmen: a cantina keeper (Marius) and a butcher (Lupa); both refused Faustina, both accepted Severina's offer with a month's signing. Padded and hide armour, spears and shields; 3 metamphora; 3 flasks of military oil.
- Day 32: Severina's pups paused; Hrodric stays. Out with Corvus guiding (Pathfinding saved the throw); seven pilgrims (Indifferent). Camp in 20.19.
- Day 33: the temple at 14:00; to 44 in four turns and a rest. The ogre surprised on its furs; Callista's Slumber resisted; Marius' spear, Corvus' and Severina's arrows; the ogre's club dropped Lupa (Faustina's treatment: leg crushed, amputated; her Cure saved him); the ogre broke and cowered; Callista's dart (natural 20) felled it, Galerius slew it. Knucklebones (120gp in the two jars to hand), the bag (3,000sp, 300gp). Lupa resigned (loyalty: calamity). Six stirges on the way out, all killed (Corvus -1 hp; parts lost, no jars). Five orcs (Friendly: the purple one's toll). Camp in the trees; a redcap at 22:40 (Indifferent).
- Day 34: Pathfinding again; camp in 20.19.
