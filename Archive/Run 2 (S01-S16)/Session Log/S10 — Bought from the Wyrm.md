# Session S10 — Bought from the Wyrm

## Recap
Rain kept the tracks muddy; the company waited two days in town while the pups trained, then went back to the temple
by way of its hobgoblin allies. The chief told of the wyrm's prisoners; the company went to the stepped tower openly,
gold in hand, and bought all seven from Idimmu: the two missing merchants, their men-at-arms and the three lost
adventurers of the inn's poster, with the adventurers' spellbooks. The only steel drawn was against a rat swarm,
and the company shut a door on it. The week ends at the well in 21.20, half a day from the fort.

## Where they went
- Days 66-68: Türos Tem; drizzle and heavy rain; downtime (pups, hospital, hunting, drill).
- Day 69: out (Galerius, Faustina, Callista, Corvus, Varro, Marius); Varro overloaded made the day slow (loads moved to the donkeys).
- Days 70-72: the farm tracks and the forest; to the temple on the afternoon of day 72.
- Day 72: a Red Eye collector (Indifferent) at the Crossroads; 13 giant rats in the north corridor: the company withdrew into the hobgoblins' guard room and barred the door (Galerius killed one; bitten once, no fever). The chief's news. The courtyard: the wyrm awake and gracious; seven prisoners and two spellbooks for 800gp. Kobolds twice and goblins (Neutral) on the way out. Camp at the spring.
- Day 73: four stirges feeding on a body on the trail: the company slipped around them. Camp at the well in 21.20.

## Who they met
- Chief Thethamun and Shulgi (Friendly).
- Idimmu, the purple wyrm (Friendly): vain, wheezing, glad of gold.
- The freed: Daros Thenes and Odysios Panas (merchants), Thorwell and Thorcigar (men-at-arms), Quintus the Rager, Vestus the Unlikely, Zaira the Beautiful.

## Treasure and XP
- Adventure A6 open: 800gp spent; the merchants' reward (500gp from Karanos) to collect in town; one giant rat killed (5 XP).

## Rumours given
- Idimmu: the Lady's black-robed priests buy prisoners from him for her altar; Abgal went below past the Red Eyes.

## Next time
Dawn of day 74 at 21.20 with the seven freed. Into Türos Tem; the merchants to Karanos; payout A6; who of the
rescued joins the company.
