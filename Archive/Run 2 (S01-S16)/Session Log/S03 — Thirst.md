# Session S03 — Thirst

## Recap
The lost party spent two more days in the Viaspen without water. Tertia, already maimed, died of thirst at the
day-9 rollover; a band of friendly brigands gave the rest water and the way, and the company reached Türos Tem on day 9.
The party now pools its finances (Faustina keeps the purse). The take was sold, Tertia buried, her cousin Severina
joined as Rosa's new character, and the company rested ten days, bought donkeys, waterskins and rations, and began
training five war-dog pups: the kingdom's first beasts.

## Where they went
- Night of day 7-8 (23.21 believed): five wraiths came to the camp on Faustina's watch; both sides surprised; Neutral. Faustina's Holy Circle held the huddled camp; they drifted west.
- Day 8: no water found; the march at dawn went astray again (navigation failed); four stirges on a deer carcass were evaded; camp in 22.18 (still lost). At midnight all lost CON to thirst; **Tertia died** (CON 0).
- Night of day 8-9: two treeherders (Neutral; Hrodric talked; "South. The water of men").
- Day 9: out into the farmland (22.19): four brigands, Friendly and Helpful, shared their water and pointed the way; position known again. Türos Tem at 13:00; a caravan on the road.
- Day 9: payout of adventure A1 (1,605 XP over six shares); the purse pooled; Tertia buried (5gp); Severina made (Venturer 1, 281 XP from Rosa's reserve; Tertia's scale armour, sword and bow from the company chest).
- Day 10: Callista won Malyn's apprentices round to Neutral: the gnoll teeth, the stirge proboscises and the 15 crab spider eggs sold (448gp); the two tourmalines sold at the forum (200gp). A second donkey, 23 more waterskins, 6 weeks of iron rations, oil and torches bought. Five war-dog pups bought from a cataphract veteran (15gp); Hrodric and Severina began their guard training. Annalist Socolo took against Callista (Unfriendly); Subaltern Miren was Neutral.
- Days 10-19: rest at the Traveler's Inn; thirst and fatigue healed.

## Who they met
- Severina (new PC, Rosa): Tertia's cousin from Cyfaraun, sharp-tongued, healthy as a mule.
- A cataphract veteran (grey, limps, talks to his dogs more than to people): sold the pups.
- A band of four brigands in the farmland (unwashed, cheerful, not asking questions): gave water.

## Treasure and XP
Sales after the payout: 448gp (parts and eggs) and 200gp (gems), divided by shares and pooled. XP: A1's payout (Galerius 295, others 281) plus the eggs' treasure XP by hand (Galerius +52, others +50; Tertia's 50 to Rosa's reserve). The purse: about 1,420gp (day 19).

## Rumours given
None.

## Changes to the world
- Tertia dead (Characters/former/); Severina joins.
- Training courses C21-C25 (war dogs).

## Next time
Day 19, 06:00, Türos Tem; everyone whole. The party is at 6 HD (six 1st-level PCs). A new month's henchman search from day 31. The pups need their trainers in town to be tamed by day 40.
