# Session S14 — The Wyrm, the Red Eyes and the Lady's Door

## Recap
Back to the temple through the mud. On the way up from below the company found the purple wyrm asleep in his vault, ringed him, and killed him when his nerve broke; his hoard came home (the sword, potions, scrolls, jewels and his tribute coins). Home, paid, three levels gained. Galerius fell ill from a rat bite and was cured in town. With Ur-Shet's Black Fangs the company destroyed the Red Eyes (Hepsis slain in his sleep), then took the Outer Fane: the high priest Ur-Namu captured and handed to the legate, the altar stripped and cleansed. With copied unholy symbols and the Black Fangs, the company went for the Lady herself, but she heard them, gathered her dead, saw Marius in the squeeze and did not believe the gnolls' 'captives'. The company withdrew to the Black Fangs' cavern.

## Where they went
- Days 95-97: Türos Tem road to the temple (the freeholder's well, the spring in 20.19); Marius and the donkeys with Apsa's orcs; the wyrm paid (900gp); the Red Eye stair guards bribed; the Black Fangs (L2 8-9).
- Day 97: back up: the wyrm asleep in his vault: slain; his hoard taken. Diseased giant rats at the toll room. Out at dusk.
- Days 98-100: home (lost no time); payout; levels; the hoard's goods sold; 9,688gp to Karanos.
- Days 102-105: out again; lost to the river (naiads); mud; Galerius fell ill (bilious fever) on day 104; back to Genelen's temple: cured (and the eagle).
- Days 106-112: back to the temple; stirges at the Crossroads; two nights with the orcs; below: the Red Eyes at the stair foot (bribed), the Black Fangs forgiving; the strike: five Red Eyes, then Hepsis and his bowmen; the loot pile shared; home.
- Days 112-118: a week in town (Callista's formula from Malyn: Illusory Duplicates).
- Days 119-123: out; the Outer Fane (22) taken with the Black Fangs: Ur-Namu captured, the acolytes killed, the altar stripped and cleansed, the priests' couch ripped open (a ruby, an amethyst). Home past eighteen zombies on the farmland.
- Days 126-131: the symbol copies; out; the Black Fangs join against the Lady; a mutated Red Eye and five zombies killed in the old Black Fang cave; the squeeze into the Inner Fane; the Lady on guard; withdrawal to the Black Fangs' cavern (15).

- The purple wyrm is dead: the long stair is free. The Red Eyes are destroyed; the Black Fangs hold their cavern. The Outer Fane is cleansed and empty. Ur-Namu is the legate's prisoner.
- Eighteen dead killed three farmfolk near Türos Tem; the legion destroyed them.
- The Lady knows enemies are at her door.
