# Session S13 — The Ogres' Den

## Recap
The company paid the purple wyrm's toll and went down the long stair. The Red Eye guards at its foot knew them as the
hobgoblins' friends but took a bribe and let them pass; the exiled Black Fangs, won over with food and talk, joined
them against the two ogres of the west caves. A Slumber failed; the gnolls carried the fight, the ogres felled Galerius
and three gnolls before they died; every fallen one was saved. Ur-Shet took half the ogres' gold, the company the
rest, the curved sword and the den. With a day's water and short food they went home, sold the knucklebones, rested,
and set out again on day 94 with Severina, skirting a host of thirty-odd brigands outside the fort.

## Where they went
- Day 87: Marius and the donkeys left with the toll orcs (L1 27) for the cask of wine; skins filled at the orcs' pool. Idimmu awake: 800gp for eight. Below (L2 1): three Red Eye guards (a week's bribe, 10gp). West through the pillared room to the Black Fangs (8, then Ur-Shet in 9): Friendly after food (seven days of rations) and talk. Two hobgoblin deserters (passed by), five raiding orcs (backed off). The ogre den (7): the fight. Faustina purified the Black Fangs' seep. Night in the den.
- Day 88: cures for Galerius and three gnolls; back up the long stair (Idimmu let them pass); brigands drinking in the Long Hall (Friendly: shared a drink); orcs at the Crossroads (waved on). Out at 10:52; camp in 21.20.
- Day 89: Türos Tem (afternoon). Days 90-93: rest in town (weather: balmy, then chilly with drizzle on 92, balmy and overcast on 93).
- Downtime (days 89-93): Galerius mended and drilled Quintus with the curved blade; Faustina worked in Genelen's hospital, Zaira with her; Callista found the blade magical (Discern Magic) and drew the level-2 map with Corvus, who also fletched; Hrodric trained his pups (from day 89); Severina trained hers; Varro prayed to Ammonar; Marius cooked at the inn.
- Day 94: Severina's pups learned Heel; out with Severina (her and Hrodric's courses paused). A host of brigands on the road in 22.20, unaware of us: slipped away, briefly turned around, Corvus set us right. Mud: camp in 21.20.

## Who they met
- Idimmu (Friendly): 100 a head each time we go down.
- Red Eye guards at the stair foot (Neutral after a bribe; they know us as the hobgoblins' friends).
- **Ulmash** (Black Fang champion, broken Common) and **Ur-Shet** (Black Fang chief: tall, grey-maned, a black-dyed muzzle, hoarse; speaks Common well; bitter at Hepsis of the Red Eyes): Friendly; partners against the ogres, and next the Red Eyes. 'The red water changes you: two of our cubs were born wrong.'
- Brigands in the Long Hall (Friendly, drinking): Drusus lost a patrol down the spiral stair when the dead came up.

## Treasure and XP
- Adventure A7 paid on day 89: 400gp, the ogres' knucklebones (sold 180gp), the curved sword (to Galerius; it radiates magic), 2 ogres: 1,520 XP over 7 shares. **Hrodric is a Shaman of 3rd level** (17 hp; his eagle 8 hp).

- Adventure A8 opened on day 94 (the ten of us).

## Rumours given
- Ur-Shet: the Lady never leaves her inner fane; black-robed priests at the fane east of the stair test visitors; the Red Eyes tend her breeding pool by their cavern; the dead guard her doors; the stinking ones (troglodytes) stalk the tunnels and hate her.
- The Long Hall brigands: Drusus lost a patrol below when the dead came up the spiral stair.

## Changes to the world
- The ogres of L2 7 are dead; their den is ours to rest in. The Black Fangs (Ur-Shet, Ulmash, six gnolls) are our friends.
- Galerius' face is scarred (the ogre's club).
- A brigand host of about thirty-five on the road near Türos Tem (day 94).
