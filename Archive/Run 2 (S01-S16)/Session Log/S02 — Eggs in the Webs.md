# Session S02 — Eggs in the Webs

## Recap
A week of play (days 1-7). Türos Tem was cold to the newcomers: no henchman would sign, the guildmaster and the
quartermaster took against them. The party found the Buried Temple in the Viaspen on the first hour's search, made
two short delves (day 4 and day 5), and came out with a clutch of giant crab spider eggs, the goblin chief's hidden
hoard and a potion. On the way home four stirges on the forest trail nearly killed Tertia (one leg torn off), and on
day 6 the party lost its way; at the end of day 7 it is still lost in the forest's eastern fringe, short of water.

## Where they went
- Day 1, Türos Tem: the courtyard bulletins (the legate's 2,000gp, the missing merchants, the Fierce Brethren, Drusus' 750gp bounty, the nightmares). Faustina petitioned the legate (Neutral) and called on Priestess Genelen (Indifferent); Callista met Malyn's apprentices (Indifferent); Tertia met Guildmaster Karanos (**Unfriendly**); at the inn Hrodric lost the quartermaster's goodwill over kyrpeges (**Unfriendly**), Faustina talked with Centurion Ilarion (Neutral). A week's search for 0th-level henchmen (Callista, 1gp) found one man, Ansgar the lumbermonger: he refused Callista, hesitated at Tertia's offer and refused her sweetened one. Night in the inn courtyard.
- Day 2: a donkey and 12 waterskins bought (Tertia bargaining); out on the north-west track. Galerius' banded plate held the party to 6 miles a day until loads were shifted to the donkey; camp in the farmland (21.20). A merchant caravan of 20 wagons passed at dawn (Neutral).
- Day 3: water from a freeholder's well; into the Viaspen (20.19). Corvus found beastman tracks in the first hour; followed them into 19.19 and camped by a spring.
- Day 4: the temple: a black granite dome over a rocky hill, the crack. **First delve** (10:00-11:00): the door kobolds turned hostile at Hrodric's greeting: Tertia shot one dead, Callista's Slumber took the other three, bound and later handed back to the guard room (Indifferent). Two drunk brigands passed by. In the Great Hall a lone gnoll was cut down (Galerius, Hrodric) and its teeth harvested (Faustina). A hobgoblin patrol looked the party over (Neutral). Room 11: **15 giant crab spider eggs** taken from the webs around a dead adventurer. Room 12 left alone. Out at 11:00 (winded: a missed rest turn), camp in the forest.
- Day 5: **second delve** (08:00-10:45) by the Crossroads into the butchered goblins' quarter: four mourning goblins (Indifferent), eight kobolds with rats (Indifferent), the hobgoblin scout again. In the goblin chief's room Corvus found the **hidden compartment**: 2 pink-green stones, 500gp, a flask of brown syrup. The **eleven severed goblin heads** in the northern Great Hall were shot to pieces from range. The ogre's cave was opened and closed again (Indifferent; the party backed out). **Seven stirges** in the west corridor: three slumbered and slain, four shot down; no wounds; proboscises harvested. Three Red Eye gnolls on the way out were Friendly (a rumour). Out by 10:45.
- Day 5, the march home: four stirges draining a dead brigand scout on the forest trail (20.19). Tertia fell; Faustina treated her (Mortal Wounds: grievously wounded, **one leg torn off**) and saved her with Cure Light Injury; Callista and Corvus drained. Tertia rides the donkey. A refused load shift slowed the afternoon; camp.
- Day 6: the navigation throw failed: the party strayed north through the forest and realised at 15:43 that Türos Tem was not where it should be. Night: a lone northern berserker came to the camp, Unfriendly; Hrodric talked him down in his own tongue (Neutral) and he pointed east-south-east.
- Day 7: east-south-east through the forest; still lost at dusk; no water found today.

## Who they met
- Ansgar, a lumbermonger (35, slow-witted, mild; asks 12gp a month): refused service twice.
- A northern berserker in the Viaspen (bare-chested, axe, speaks only Jutlandic; outcast's manners): Neutral.

## Treasure and XP
Carried (adventure A1 open; nothing paid out): L1 gnoll teeth (Faustina), L2 5gp (Tertia), L3-L4 two pink-green stones (Tertia), L5 500gp (Faustina), L6 brown syrup potion (Faustina), L7 seven stirge proboscises (Tertia), L8 four stirge proboscises (Callista); 15 giant crab spider eggs (captives K1-K15, Corvus). Monsters defeated: 1 kobold, 1 gnoll, 11 bodiless heads, 11 stirges (and 3 kobolds captured and returned).

## Rumours given
- Day 5, the Red Eye gnolls: the Lady Below rewards a band that wipes out one of her lesser tribes.

## Changes to the world
- Tertia: one leg lost; two weeks' bed rest; 2 hp.
- Buried Temple: see World State (rooms 1-3, 11, 19, 33-34, 39, 42-44).

## Next time
Lost in 21.18 (believed 23.21) at dusk, day 7; water short (Galerius carries most); food 2-3 days each. Farmland and wells lie a hex to the south-east. In town: sell or keep the eggs (an exotic trainer or Malyn's workshop), bank, rest Tertia, buy armour and metamphora, try again for henchmen (a new month's search from day 31).
