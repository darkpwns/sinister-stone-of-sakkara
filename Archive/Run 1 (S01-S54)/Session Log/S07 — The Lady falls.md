# Session S07 — The Lady falls

## Recap
With Priestess Genelen, the party returned to the catacombs and met the Lady alone in the Inner Fane. She charmed Aulus and cut down four before Tarq felled her and Vettius finished her. Her dead came: Aulus, Marcia, Thorwell and Quintus died; Genelen's holy circles brought the rest out. Genelen shattered the Stone by the stream and took its Fragments for Mityara. The legate paid his reward.

## Treasure and XP
Legate's reward 2,000gp; the Lady's great axe and ring; 12,790 XP over five shares (10,000 for the Fragments). Sabina, Lucilla and Vettius reached 4th level, Tarq 3rd, Hesper 2nd.
