# Session S53 — The Road North

## Recap
Day 817, evening, the fort of the Ford. The stone sisters are dead; the barony's first month is done; Pullus' old
lieutenant has taken the Family's street; the Jutlanders went north to the Ram. The company rides for Cyfaraun at dawn.

## Council (day 818)
- **Gideon (Cotta):** call: north by the road, nine of us; Tiberius holds the fort. Feed the beasts; don't stop for strangers.
- **Ingrid (Neris):** (day 825, Türos Orn) we're crawling: pack animals, and the coppers in the bag. (Her own waterskins were back at the fort; Brennus lent his.)
- **Tomasz (Vettius):** Thana first, then Mordax.
- **Priya (Vestus):** (day 827) a legion gate from the Ford to the city. The stones point north-east at something. Noting that.
- **Rosa (Lucilla):** stones in a milestone and a sword in a tree: the road pays.
- **Kwame (Brennus):** the Tower man may look at my fort, with my engineer at his elbow.

## Where they went
- Days 818-819. Down the road past the ford in a heatwave, then drizzle and mud. Under a toppled Imperial milestone in 26.17 Ennia's horse kicked loose a lead box: three cut stones like frozen fire. In 27.17 Vettius stopped us at a tripline across the road: a log hung in the trees above. In 27.16, a sword wrapped in oilcloth in a hollow oak.
- Days 820-822. Sheep, farms, mud. In 26.14 a ruined shrine of the old legion beside the road: a basalt stone that, prayed into, shows ill intent as Neris' prayer does. We lay a day in camp (the Judge's slip).
- Days 823-825. A burned hut and a nest of vipers (Ennia could not tell their venom; we let it be). Türos Orn: an inn, beds, iron rations, three donkeys, a mule and a pack horse; the coppers into the bag. Everyone tired from six days in the saddle.
- Days 826-828. The lake road. In 20.07 a camp of some sixty bandits in the trees by the road; they never saw us. In 19.07 a merchant caravan going south, and with it Lucan Vesper of the Tower, thin, ink-stained, talking to his mule: the Regent sent him to survey the old legion fort at the Ford for 'the legion's gate to the city'. Brennus gave him a letter of leave to Tiberius. Vestus remembered: the legions' gates opened only for one holding a tessera of passage, and when a fort was abandoned its gate-priests carried the tesserae away to their nearest house.
- Day 829. Cyfaraun by the Mirmen bridge. At the Silk Pony: Thana, big with child, and Bassus with his knives. Vettius asked Thana to be his wife; she said yes. Vettius' spies had news within the hour: the Gilded Ram's men, Lentulus Mordax's gangs and the forty Jutlanders come at the Pony the night after next.
- Day 829, evening. Council: rest tomorrow; the Pony held, not abandoned: Bassus' crossbows on the roof, Vestus and Phaedra upstairs with fire, the rest in the yard and the lane, Neris inside; Vettius goes for Mordax when they come. The wedding after, at the Grey Sisters, Neris to say the words.

## Who they met
- Lucan Vesper, mage of the Tower: thin, ink-stained, talks to his mule ("The Regent sends his regards. Well, he sends me, which is not quite the same.").
- Lentulus Mordax (not met yet): Pullus' old lieutenant, boss of the street since the month's end; broken nose, gold rings ("The boss is a lord now. Lords don't count dice.").

## Treasure and XP

- Vettius' spies: the Ram, Mordax and the Jutlanders come at the Pony the night of day 831.

Day 829, evening, the Silk Pony, Cyfaraun. A day's rest, then the night of 831: the Ram, Mordax and the Jutlanders come. After it: the wedding; selling through the Family; the Tower's library about the gate.
