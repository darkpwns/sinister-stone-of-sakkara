# Session S37 — The Doom Throne

## Recap
Day 562, the Solar Citadel. Ten at full strength. The Teacher is loose below, hurt, gathering servants. The lance and
the hilt are at the Tower until day 570, month 19's end. Tiberius owes the Patriarch his month's report.

## Where they went
- Day 563. Tiberius made his month's report to the Patriarch: the Teacher awake, the reliquary emptied.
- Days 564-565. The Order: Neris drew a Krysean trader who had broken a man's skull in a brawl; Brennus' name was a
  man of the Family again, so Vettius paid the blood-price and sent him away (he talked on his way out). Night of 565:
  the trader walked home from the Emporium with three Jutland guards; Neris held two, Phaedra slept the third and the
  trader; Neris finished it. The guards woke to his body and could describe a woman in grey and a huge scarred woman.
- Day 567, descent 79, toward the doom temple's throne. In the cave of the falling star a many-armed shade came at the
  ward; Neris' prayers did not unmake it; its touches drained Hrodvar and Tiberius; Brennus' axe and Vettius' blade
  from behind destroyed it. Two of the company weakened and the Captain at a handful of breaths: they turned back.
  Hrodvar, shaken, left Vettius' service in town and sold him his tomb-jacks.
- Day 568. A Watch officer came about the trader; Tiberius in his regalia and Lucilla's tongue sent him away content.
- Day 569. Tiberius mends under the oculus with Neris' prayers.

## Who they met
- Phanes Kordaxis, Krysean trader ("Everything has a price, sister. Even a skull."). Dead.
- A Watch officer of the Emporium watchpost: left satisfied.
- Hrodvar: gone his own way, to the Jutland hostel, asking about the road north.

## Treasure and XP
- A130 (Kordaxis): 2,080 XP over 4.5 shares. A131 (descent 79): 2,600 XP (the shade) over 8 shares.

## Rumours given

  Family's ears).

## Changes to the world
- Hrodvar has left the company. Tiberius is weakened (enervated) until his saves come good.
- A shade of the falling-star cave is destroyed.

## Next time
Day 569, the Citadel. Nine in the company. Month 19 ends on day 570 (the lance and the hilt back from the Tower; the
month's accounts). Nuvicus holds a dangerous secret. Below: the doom throne, the Teacher's growing following.
