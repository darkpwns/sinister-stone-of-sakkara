# Session S25 — Accounts Paid

## Recap
Day 449, evening, the Solar Citadel. Twelve, nearly whole. Neris is a sister of Calefa. No Order errand in hand. Month
15 ends tomorrow; the selling day follows. Vettius means to change what the Family is for.

## Where they went
- Day 450, the fifteenth month's end. The Guildhouse took its fees; the textile caravan paid well, the coast caravan is
  held up for half a year, and the salt caravan came back with something its master had found on the road. The
  Family's month went to Lucilla for its first two weeks and to Vettius for the rest. Iulia's report to the Patriarch
  brought a charge: the Inquisitor has named a woman of Nasga in the Festival District.
- Day 450, the Silk Pony. Vettius put the Family to new work: listening in the taverns and the bathhouses, buying and
  selling secrets, moving goods, fencing; no more houses broken, no more rackets by the fist, no more blood on the
  guild's business. The old crews shrugged and took it. That night, before Destrio in the assembly chamber with Neris
  at his side, he asked that the Family's people no longer be put on the Order's list for what they did at his word.
  Destrio heard him out, hands still. "Calefa keeps the accounts. If the Family pays its own, the goddess has nothing
  left to collect." Any death the Family's men cause it will answer for itself, with blood-price to the kin and the man
  cast out; the Order will take the Family's word of anyone seeking hired killers. Then he looked at Neris a long time
  and bowed his head to her, as to a sister of the goddess.
- Day 451, selling day. The gilded sarcophagi, the statuettes, the tablet, the candelabrum, the death's-head mask,
  the black opals and the rest went through the Family's dealers at their full worth; a scholar bought the Twelve Arts.
  The Tower's alchemist named the bubbling vial (a draught of freedom); the rest of the year's magic went to the Tower
  for two weeks and 18,000 gold. The henchmen's wages were set to their ranks, with back pay. Destrio's word on the
  steward's note: the man he wanted dead has been warned.
- Day 452. The Order's week: an errand for Vestus, a horse-breeder's boy who raced his chariot through the market and
  killed a fishwife's little girl. Vettius' people found the wharf man who had sold the Family's business, and instead
  of the river Vettius gave him a choice; he chose to sell lies to his buyers, nervously. That night the Captain and four
  knights of the Winged Sun, with Brennus, Marcellus and Hrodvar, went down under a perfumer's shop on the Street of
  Lamps into a cellar shrine to the Serpent: three acolytes died, and the painted priestess fell under Marcellus' blade;
  Iulia's prayer kept her alive for the Patriarch's court.
- Night of 453, the West Gate stables. The boy shouted and ran for the horses; Phaedra's sleep caught him and Vestus
  did the Order's justice. The groom hid in the loft till morning.
- Day 455, the fifty-ninth descent. Through the great temple's galleries of graves, past a wrapped corpse that
  shambled out of the dark (Iulia's prayer failed against it; the blades did not, though Brennus and Marcellus took
  its rot and Neris cured them), to a slab in the western gallery. It gave at the first pull of Hrodvar's rope. Inside,
  a painted bronze sarcophagus shaped as a beautiful elf woman; its lid moved; Iulia raised the Rod and the thing
  cowered in its own tomb while the blades finished it. A crystal-tipped rod, a gold boat, a jewel box with a choker
  and a belly chain, a gold brazier with six strange lumps of charcoal, a bronze box with a book of gilded copper
  plates, four thousand silver. An orange ooze was left to creep. Out at 16:52.
- Day 456. Another note for "Vasan" at the Nicean stalls: a merchant wants his wife dead; it goes to Destrio, with
  the name of last week's steward, which the Family's ears found. Another wharf man who sold the Pony's ways was
  found and turned. A Watch optio asked the Captain about the wine merchant found dead in his bed; she knew nothing.
