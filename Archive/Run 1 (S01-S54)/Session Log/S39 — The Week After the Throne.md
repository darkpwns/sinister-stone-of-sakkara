# Session S39 — The Week After the Throne

## Recap
Day 574, mid-morning, the Solar Citadel. Nine in the company, the doom throne's garnets sold. Wounds to mend; the
weekly business of the Family and the Order; the Teacher still gathers servants below.

## Where they went
- Days 574-575. Neris' cures; the company whole. The Family's week: three more spies; a man who had sold the Pony's
  back ways to Nuvicus' people once sold them again, was traced and took the purse out of the city.
- Day 576. The Teacher gathers the troglodytes. Vettius paid Phaedra's month ahead.
- Day 577, descent 81. To the white apes' caves by the ant caves. Seven young apes came roaring out of the tube at the
  lantern: fire and steel, four dead, three fled to a great statue. The company sealed itself behind the alchemists'
  bronze door to sleep; a stinging swarm got in at midday and was burned out with torches. Evening: three apes held a
  split pillar and burned; at the statue of a hunting god the great white ape and six more foamed and fought to the
  last. Under the statue: a vault of trophies, coin and gold-copper ingots.
- Day 578. Out with the first load; three giant flies at the cesspool: Phaedra's face eaten by acid, Neris brought
  her back. Tiberius rose to the fourth rank and his strength came back with it. The Patriarch repaired Phaedra's
  face; she lies abed two weeks. The Order's word for Vestus: a Watchman who beat a boy to death over dice.
- Day 579. Back down for the second load (ingots, ivory, horns); out by mid-morning. That night Vettius caught the
  Watchman in a Fish Market lane before his whistle reached his lips. The river has him.
- Day 580. The Imperial Vanguard went down after the white apes.
- Day 581. The Order's commission came.

## Who they met
- Gnaeus Sabucius, tesserarius of the Watch ("Roll, or get off the bench."). Dead.
