# Session S43 — The Gate Toll

## Recap
Day 602, morning, the Solar Citadel. All nine whole. The canary trap is set for three wharf betrayers. Brennus' errand:
Marcus Galeo, optio of the Traveler's Gate, who walks home at dusk with two of his men. The Teacher knows where the
company sleeps.

## Where they went
- Day 603. The Family's ears: a black figure on the Citadel's roof by night. The company keeps its own watch now. The
  wharf men did not bite on the planted tale.
- Day 604. At dusk the optio of the Traveler's Gate came along his toll round with two men: Vettius' blade, Phaedra's
  sleep on the two, Brennus' axe. The two woke in the street with their optio gone.
- Day 606. The Order's word for Vettius: a centurion who ran a cooper down with his chariot. Descent 84: Vestus flew
  across the slime-filmed sludge of the slimy tunnel and looked into a small cave beyond: a floor polished like black
  glass, runes on every wall, air easier to breathe. He could not read the runes and did not step in. Rats gave way on
  the road home.
- Night of 608. The centurion staggered home from the Ox and Plough; Neris' prayer held him and Vettius finished it.

## Who they met
- Marcus Galeo, optio ("The gate is open. The gate is also hungry."). Dead.
- Lucius Caper, centurion ("Out of the road, or under the wheels."). Dead.
