# Session S49 — The West Wall

## Recap
Day 743, a minute after sunrise, inside the bugbears' fort across the ford from Türos Aster. The war-chief, his
shaman and witch doctor and the south barracks are dead; the rest of the tribe holds the west wall against Tiberius'
soldiers at the ford. The company stands in the lane between the barracks.

## Where they went
- Day 743, a minute after sunrise. The tribe at the west wall did not wait to be taken from behind: the chieftain with
  the bright sword brought every warrior and archer round the hall and down on the company in the lane between the
  barracks, while the gate tower's watch kept shooting at the soldiers by the ford.
- Vestus read both his scrolls of fire into them as they came; Phaedra and Neris sang them to sleep three at a time;
  the archers' arrows found Neris three times and dropped her to her last breath before she reached the barracks
  door and drank a potion. The spears broke on Brennus, Lucilla, Vettius and Cotta; Vettius went through the
  spear-captain from behind and then through both chieftains in one breath. Phaedra's thunderbolt took the archers.
  The last big one threw down his axe.
- The sleeping and the fallen were given the knife. The tower's three came down with their hands open. The tribe's
  women and young went over the north rampart and scattered into the forests. Lucius, the ferryman's boy, was alive
  in the wicker pen.
- The Imperial Vanguard rode across to the noise and found it over. Gabriol bowed to the gods' decision; their
  priestess Asrya bound two of the wounded. Neris healed the rest.
- In the strongroom under the hall: sacks of silver and electrum, the legion's old pay-chest, jewellery, and behind the
  altar the bones of the last garrison. Neris gave them their rites; the room went quiet and warm, and every one of
  the ten who stood there felt the Lady's hand on the shoulder.
- Across the ford to Türos Aster with four bugbear prisoners and the boy. The Legate came over himself, looked at the
  dead, put his seal on a warrant of claim, and wrote his report to the governor: a patent for Brennus, in a month or
  so. Tiberius and the soldiers hold the fort.
- The march's treasure was counted and shared: Vestus is a Mage of the eighth circle, Ennia an outrider.

## Who they met
- Lucius, the ferryman's boy, fourteen, thin as a reed ("They said the old one would eat me at midsummer. He didn't wake up.").
- Gabriol Eirenikos and the Imperial Vanguard, gracious in defeat ("The Lady of Victory has decided. We ride west.").
- Asrya Raija, priestess of Mityara, veiled, quick hands.
- Legate Nicus Balens, at the fort itself ("Bugbears hang. Barons sign. Both today, if the ink holds.").
