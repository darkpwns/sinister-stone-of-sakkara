# Session S45 — The Last Tombs

## Recap
Day 614, before dawn, the Solar Citadel. The Great Teacher is dead. Oppius brings four Order errands. The company means
to finish the Nethercity: the warlord brothers of the northern caves, the old warlord by the entrance and the sleepers
of D6, the cave of 288 graves, the laboratory's sealed rooms, and the great gallery of the temple.

## Where they went
- Day 614. Oppius brought four errands. The Teacher's grey silk and syringes went to the Tower to be named. Tiberius
  told the Patriarch the herald of the Awakening lay dead under his own catacombs.
- Day 615. The old warlord's tomb by the entrance: Neris' prayer unmade him in his second round. The broken bust set
  back on its pedestal turned the chariot panel, and all forty-eight skeletons of the gallery rose: fire, the Rod,
  Neris' prayer and the axes put them down, and none could touch anyone inside the Captain's light. Six thousand
  gold in an amphora behind the panel.
- Day 616. The three elven brothers of the northern caves: their faces froze eight of nine, but they could not pass
  the Captain's light; Neris' prayers destroyed all three. Three thousand gold in the urns beyond.
- Day 618. Marcan's sword opened every sealed door of the alchemists' laboratory as Vettius walked by: formulae,
  scrolls, flasks, the strange metal of the great apparatus, and sixty jars of its library. Lucilla rose to Merchant
  Prince. Tiberius rose to Vicar, and left Vettius' service. That night the sellsword of the wharf died with his two
  friends.
- Day 619. Tiberius came once more as Captain: a mummy on the way, then the temple's great hall, where Vestus flew
  to the stars of the ceiling and pried out all twenty-eight while the whole hall's dead rose and stood helpless
  against the Captain's light. Tiberius took his leave at the Citadel gate; the company went back to the Silk Pony.
- Day 620. Fourteen of the Family's men carried out the rest of the library, past a dropping slime and five wights.
  Vestus rose to the eighth rank.

## Who they met
- Sextus Rubrius, sellsword ("Every roof needs a wall, friend."). Dead.
- Tiberius Maro, Captain of the Underwatch ("I serve the office now, not the purse. One more hall."). Gone his way.
