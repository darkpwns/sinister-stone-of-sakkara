# Session S46 — Leaving Cyfaraun

## Recap
Day 620, the Silk Pony. The Nethercity's great tombs are empty. The company means to bring up the last heavy goods with
porters, get free of the Order of the Eclipse, pool its wealth, make it portable, hire soldiers, and ride for Türos
Aster, where the Legate offers a barony for the Ruined Imperial Fortress.

## Where they went
- Day 621. Lucilla spoke for the deathbringers before Destrio: they may go when their last five errands are done.
  The company made one purse of its wealth.
- Nights 623-627. The herb-seller and the charm-seller (who charmed Vettius for a breath) on the night of 623; the
  reed-witch, held by Neris' word, and the Krysean trader with his three guards on the night of 624; the wise-woman
  with the crow fled screaming into a neighbour's house, went to the Watch, and was found by Vettius asleep in her
  bed three nights later. Destrio struck the company from the Order's roll on day 629.
- Day 625. Soldiers hired: heavy and light foot, bowmen, crossbowmen and light horse; more the next week: 64 in all.
- Day 626. Porters brought up the brothers' bronze coffins and the two great dragon horns.
- Day 628. The Tower named the Teacher's silk (armour) and syringes (five years without ageing; healing).
- Day 631. The selling day through the Family. Aelia heard of a Krysean spice-lord with a bag that has no bottom.
- Day 632. The bag bought for 120,000; 200,000 in platinum and gems inside it; the rest at the Guildhouse against
  letters of credit. Tiberius laid down the captaincy and the Underwatch regalia to ride south with Vettius.
- Days 633-635. South on the Imperial road in snow and mud, six miles a day; back to the city to buy horses.

## Who they met
- Destrio, the Order's head ("Calefa keeps her ledger, and yours is closed. Go with her fortune.").
- Diokles Antipatros, Krysean spice-lord ("It eats a wagonload and asks nothing. Like a good wife.").

## Treasure and XP
- The Order's last commissions: 1,000 (Rubrius), 1,500 (Ilaria, Livia), 3,500 (Thalassa, Kleon), 1,500 (Corvina).
- A152 (the coffins and horns): 3,100 XP. The selling day: about 125,000gp. Vettius: 6,508 hijink XP (month 21).
- A correction: the laboratory's library, formulae, scrolls and metal had been taken months ago (days 515-530);
  the second taking written in S45 was struck out, with its XP. Vestus is of the seventh rank again.
