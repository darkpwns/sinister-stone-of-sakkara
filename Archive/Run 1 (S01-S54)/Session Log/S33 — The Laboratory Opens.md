# Session S33 — The Laboratory Opens

## Recap
Day 510, morning, the Solar Citadel. Nine in the company, whole (Hrodvar gone). Vestus studies Flight to day 513.
Day 512 the weekly block; day 515 the Order; Phaedra's wage day 520; Brennus' contract on Cnaeus Lento due by day 540.

## Where they went
- Day 510: the Family's ears found the sellsword Cnaeus Lento the same day: he lodges with the Jutlanders by the Bridge
  Gate and walks home late along the river stair. That night Vettius hid on the stair; Brennus and Vettius missed in the
  dark, Lento backed off shouting, and Phaedra's second sleep took him; Brennus finished it. Nobody came; the river took him.
- Day 511: Brother Oppius paid the commission (1,500gp). Month 18's stock: no antivenom. Neris and Iulia had a prayer
  of Restore Life and Limb written for each of them (with Cure Major Injury and Holy Blessing); Vettius bought a Potion
  of Delay Poison.
- Day 512: the Family's ears: the Argollëan embassy has hired a sage to learn what killed Irial below.
- Day 513: Vestus learned the flying spell.
- Day 514, the seventy-first descent. At the foot of the princes' stair, the bronze door forced: a fist-shaped cave
  walled with graves, a skeleton with sword and shield in each. Inside the wards the company crossed toward the west
  tunnel; a minute in, the dead began climbing out. Forty-eight followed into the tunnel and were burned, thundered,
  smitten and cut down at its mouth without a wound. A boulder in the tunnel wall rolled aside on a stair down to the
  river tunnel. Back across the cave (more dead rising) and the bronze door spiked behind. The princes' gilded coffins,
  great tusks and small tusks carried home and sold (5,100gp). A wandering pack of black widows shied from the torches.
- Day 515, the seventy-second descent, to the sealed laboratory. Its bronze doors opened for Vettius. The embalming
  room's clutter, charms and two glyphed orbs; the vivisection room (a patchwork ogre-spider, chained living on its
  table for ages: Neris had it unchained and it died); the machine room's four iridescent hemispheres (a rare alloy,
  Vestus said) stripped; the library: 102 jars of notes carried off, with 13 jars of arcane scrolls and 8 of alchemical
  formulae. Nine rats burned in the gallery. Home by noon. Phaedra named two flasks: a cure and a Potion of Youth.
  Sold: 90,820gp in all.
- Day 516, the seventy-third descent. Rats covered in weeping sores burned in the sewer caves. In the pentagram room,
  Lucilla alone tried the orbs: the dark one opened the west arch on a jungle under a lizard-men's pyramid, the
  south-west arch on a passage, the south arch on a dead land under a lightless sky (Iulia: the Chthonic gods' own
  sphere); the electrum one filled the room with fire (Lucilla's ring halved it). 158 more jars carried off and sold
  (23,235gp). Eighty remain.
