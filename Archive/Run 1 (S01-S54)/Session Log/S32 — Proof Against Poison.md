# Session S32 — Proof Against Poison

## Recap
Day 503, morning, the Solar Citadel. Nine in the company, whole; Hrodvar gone. Four of them died of a monster's
poison six days ago and were brought back. Day 505 the weekly block; day 508 the Order; month 17 ends day 510.

## Where they went
- Day 503, the Citadel. The table took stock after the four poison deaths: Neris and Iulia both know the prayer that
  holds poison off (Delay Poison) and the one that clears it (Neutralize Poison). The temple and Citadel scriptoria
  wrote three scrolls to order: Neutralize Poison and Delay Poison for Neris, two Neutralize Poisons and a cure for Iulia.
  No antivenom potion was to be had this month.
- Day 505: the Family's ears: Irial of the Imperial Vanguard died below the day before; the Argollëan embassy asks how.
- Day 506, the seventieth descent. A clear quivering block in the sewer caves froze Iulia for a moment; Neris freed her.
  Down the winding stair from the temple's Y-gallery to a slab tomb, opened at the first heave while its dead were still
  rising: a crowned mummy, a mummy in a ruby tiara, and four bronze warrior statues. Iulia's prayer unmade the princess
  and Neris' the prince before either left the tomb; Phaedra's thunderbolts and the front line broke the statues. Crown,
  tiara, a spear, a bow, black-fletched arrows, electrum, two spellbook rolls came away; the gilded coffins and the great
  tusks were left. Four giant flies on the way out.
- Days 507-510, the Citadel. The bow (+1) to Aelia and the spear (+1) to Brennus. Vestus read the two rolls and began a
  week's study of the flying spell. The Order named two Family men (blood-prices paid, both sent away) and gave Brennus a
  contract. Iulia made her report. The month ended.
