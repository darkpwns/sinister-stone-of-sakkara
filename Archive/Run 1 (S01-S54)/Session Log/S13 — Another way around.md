# Session S13 — Another way around

## Recap
Day 273 dawn, Cyfaraun, the Silk Pony Inn. Everyone whole; Gnaeus abed to about day 298, so eleven go below. The
great temple's tombs are empty; the stair behind the priest's fresco ends on a beach by a cold black river with no
banks to walk. Four alabaster amphorae wait in the burial cave above the beach.

## Where they went
- Day 273, in town. Vestus sold the astrologer's spellbook through Liber Faunus (20,720gp, eight parts in ten, clean
  coin), shared among those who brought it out. Lucilla left the pink swan-feather cloak with the Tower of Knowledge
  for full naming (2,000gp; ready day 287). Military oil (twelve flasks) and two bundles of torches bought; loads
  shared so that nobody is slowed; coin above 150gp banked at the Guildhouse. A night at the Silk Pony.
- The table's call for the next descent: no raft and no swimming yet. The river is cold, deep and wall to wall; a
  raft would have to be built below and anything in the water is unknown. Instead, look for another way along it by
  land: the sloping gallery west and then south of the crossroads, past the inquisitor's door, has never been walked,
  and it runs downhill toward where the water was heard. Then the amphorae on the way out.
- Day 274, twenty-fourth descent (eleven), by the far grate. In the sewer caves a rat swarm came at the light and
  fled from the torches; Marcellus breathed the stink through his mask and shook it off resting in the first tomb. By
  the temple doors two giant ants backed away from the fire. Rest on the west balcony.
- The sloping gallery: west from the crossroads past the inquisitor's door, the hallway turns south, runs 20' and
  drops steeply. Vettius heard water echoing up it. At its foot, an oval cave cut from the rock, 30' wide and 20' deep,
  its walls etched with Ancient Argollëan (Vestus: prayers to the Chthonic gods); every breath echoes like a shout. A
  hole 5' across in the middle of the floor; the lantern lowered on a rope showed a 10' shaft opening in the ceiling of
  a wider stretch of the river, about 50' across, the water some 30' below the cave floor, a great pillar carved as a
  tree trunk beside the hole, its stone boughs spread across the ceiling. No bank under the hole: it is another way
  onto the river, not around it.
- Back the way they came, and down the hidden stair to the beach: up the winged-sun steps to the burial cave, where
  Brennus, Varro, Marcellus and Hrodvar each shouldered one of the four alabaster amphorae. On the slow climb back
  through the temple an insect swarm hung back from the torches. Then, in the curving corridor, two pale hooded
  shapes came out of the dark and were caught unready: Vestus' fan of flames scorched both, Iulia raised the Rod of
  the Dawn and one fled cowering; the other flew at Brennus and its touch left her cold and diminished. Brennus'
  axe, Lucilla's flask and Marcellus' sword finished it. The other did not come back.
- Out by the far grate at half past two, rats scattering from the torches in the tunnel. The amphorae went to Liber
  (960gp, shared). Phaedra rose to the fourth rank (Magician).
- Days 274-281, in town. Brennus slept two nights on the floor under the Solar Citadel's oculus with Neris' prayer
  of rest on her, and the cold of the touch left her (she keeps the loss). The company bought four hide-and-frame
  river canoes with paddles from the Lake Laman fishermen (144gp) and spent the week learning to handle them on the
  lake shallows while Vestus studied the thunderbolt formula and Phaedra the levitation formula she bought at the
  Tower. Wages paid ahead for Phaedra, Varro and Hrodvar.
- The call for the next descent: carry the canoes down to the beach and follow the river.
- Day 282, twenty-fifth descent, the canoes carried down in bundles. In the cesspool tunnel two giant toads gave way
  to the torches. Resting in the priest's tomb before the stair, a streaming, tattered ghost came through the stone;
  Brennus' charge cut it and Vestus' new thunderbolt tore it apart before it touched anyone. The thunder brought a
  bandaged dead thing down the gallery within minutes: Iulia's Rod sent it cowering, Varro and Marcellus pinned it,
  and Vestus' and Phaedra's fans with Marcellus' sword burned and cut it down. No one hurt.
- On the beach Hrodvar lashed the canoes together and the company paddled upstream past the carved tree under the
  well. Between the pillar and a cave mouth on the west bank a huge chalk-white fish came up under the second canoe:
  bite and whipping feelers. Three canoes closed on it; Vettius, Varro, Marcellus and Hrodvar's lucky blow drove it
  down into the dark, hurt (Neris badly bruised). It followed below for a while and did not come back.
- Ashore at the cave mouth, Vettius crept up the north-west tunnel alone: a stone arch carved like a woman's sex,
  steps up into a warm oval cavern, its walls wet and glistening, and on a shelf at the far tip a white diamond of
  extraordinary size. The table decided to come back with every spell and flask ready and the walls in mind.
  Downstream to the beach, two giant crabs backing off from the fire; out by the far grate at twenty past three.
- Iulia rose to the sixth rank (Rector).
- Days 283-287, in town, healing. On day 287 the Tower returned the pink cloak, named: a cloak of skinchanging that turns
  its wearer into a swan. Sold unused through Liber (30,400gp, eight parts in ten), shared among those who brought it
  out of the princess's tomb. More military oil bought; everyone's loads checked.
- Day 288, twenty-sixth descent, for the white diamond, with twenty-six flasks. Eight Horned Rat tappers in the
  avenue tunnel were indifferent and went on tapping; the company waited until they had gone before the hole. The
  canoes down again; on the river nothing rose. Hrodvar held the canoes at the cave mouth.
- Neris blessed the company in the tunnel. From the steps below the arch Brennus threw the first flask at the
  glistening wall, and the wall came off the stone: a black, flowing mass that filled the cavern and came down the
  steps after them. The line gave ground down the passage, throwing as it went; Iulia in her plate held the front,
  shield up, while the acid ate at her; Brennus took one blow and Neris' cure. When the flasks ran out they threw
  lit torches, Vestus made the burning oil on it spit sparks, and Phaedra's last flask finished it. Iulia was down to
  her last breath (Neris brought her back up). Aelia could not put a name to the diamond. Vettius took it from the
  shelf.
- Back down the river (the pale shape followed below and did not strike), out through the temple, a tremor shaking
  dust from the ceilings of the central caves, and out by the far grate at a quarter past three. The diamond went
  into the company's chest in the Grey Sisters' vault.
- Days 289-290 in town, healing.
