# Session S35 — A New Shield

## Recap
Day 523, morning, the Solar Citadel. Nine in the company (strength 59); Iulia dead, the Rod and the Underwatch regalia
back with Arëtar; Phaedra abed to day 524; Hrodvar back in Vettius' service. The plan: hire a crusader to stand where
Iulia stood; Neris petitions Arëtar for the regalia; Vestus' contract on Marius Corvo due by day 540.

## Where they went
- Day 523. Vettius posted a notice for a crusader. Night: Vestus, Vettius and Brennus waited for the hunter Marius Corvo
  (the Order's contract) in the North Gate lanes. He ran at the sight of Brennus; Vestus' darts hit him twice, but he
  got away, and went to the Watch.
- Days 524-526. Cotta, a face Corvo had never seen, hired him to guide a fowling trip on the Mirmen marshes. Nothing
  flew. That night Vettius and Vestus came into the camp; Corvo woke, too late: Vestus' dart, then his blade. The
  marsh took the body. A band of thirty brigands came along the river path at midnight; Lucilla paid their toll and
  they went on. Oppius brought the commission on day 526.
- Day 528. **Tiberius Maro**, a hermit crusader of the Winged Sun, took Vettius' third offer (a year's pay on signing).
  Neris brought him to Patriarch Arëtar with the hierophant's black scroll and word of the golden-skull shrine:
  **Arëtar named Tiberius Captain of the Underwatch** and gave him the spear, the shield and the Rod of the Dawn.
- Days 529-531. Two more Order errands: the sellsword Decimus Ruga (Brennus'), put to sleep by Phaedra on his way home
  and finished by Brennus; the hedge-witch Ammia Thrax (Vettius'), caught gathering herbs by night in the Eastgate
  meadows. Her crow stayed with the body and the fowlers found her.
- Days 532-534. Neris broke the rune on the golden-skull shrine; inside were four jars and a dark crystal with a
  screaming face in it, now at the Tower. The Tower named the ring (fire protection: Brennus), the bracers (armour:
  Phaedra) and the rod (elemental spheres, its four words: Vestus). A Watch officer asked about the herb-woman.
  Lucilla's men brought a note left for "Vasan": a wine merchant wants the new Captain dead. It went to the Order.
- Day 535, descent 77: the library's last 80 jars and the last two coffin pieces out of the sealed laboratory and the
  hierophant's tomb. Two grave ghouls in the alchemists' hall fled from Tiberius' first prayer. Sold for 17,795gp.
- Days 536-540. A last errand for Vettius: the apprentice Faustus Cerco, killed asleep in his room in the Scribes'
  Row. Tiberius made his report to the Patriarch. Month 18 ended.
- Day 540. Patriarch Arëtar prayed over Hrodvar and Phaedra: Hrodvar's belly is whole (he says the gods shook him);
  Phaedra's foot grew back, with a lidded third eye; she lay abed two weeks.
- Days 540-555, downtime. Vestus scribed a scroll of Fireball in the Tower's workshop; his scroll of Dispel Magic came
  to nothing. Neris' scroll of Death Ward failed in the Grey Sisters' scriptorium. Hrodvar's first set of iron
  tomb-jacks cracked in the smith's yard; the second holds, for lifting slabs and coffin lids. Lucilla sold the
  golden-skull shrine and heard the city's talk: a wine merchant's bravos were watching the Citadel; Brennus, Tiberius
  and Hrodvar faced them, they named the merchant and left the city; the Patriarch dealt with it quietly.
- Day 547. The dark crystal came back from the Tower: a soul gem of the old elven necromancers, full of divine
  power, an elven soul inside. Vestus spoke with the soul, Caogan, an old rival of the hierophant: the reliquary's
  vault opens by the right-hand lever in the hidden room, never by spells; its glass cases open to a thought or break,
  and either wakes the sleeping Teacher; he sleeps behind the coiled serpents in the vestibule, down the beetle stair,
  in cold glass. Neris promised to free Caogan when the gem's power is spent.
- Day 545. Eight new thieves came to Vettius' Pony.

## Who they met
- Tiberius Maro (day 528): tall, sinewy, sun-darkened, bearded; few words. "The light does not ask where you slept.
  Only whether you rose." Captain of the Underwatch.
- Marius Corvo, hunter ("He drew first. I just drew faster."); Ammia Thrax, hedge-witch ("Mules are honest beasts.
  They go where they're sent."); Decimus Ruga, sellsword ("She was mine first."); Faustus Cerco, apprentice ("Lamps tip
  over."). All dead.
- Patriarch Arëtar: gave the Captaincy to Tiberius.
