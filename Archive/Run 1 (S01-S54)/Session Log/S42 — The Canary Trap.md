# Session S42 — The Canary Trap

## Recap
Day 595, morning, the Solar Citadel. All nine whole. The Teacher knows where the company sleeps. Vettius has let the
Family's turned betrayers live as double agents since month 16; he means to end that.

## Where they went
- Day 596. Vettius set a fresh tale among the wharf men to find those who still sell the Family's word. A Watch officer
  called at the Citadel about the Pony's back ways; the Captain sent him away content.

  the troglodyte shaman seen with a tall black figure at the old wharf.

- Day 600. Month 20's accounts: the Family's take (less the dead), Lucilla's houses and caravans.
- Day 601. The Wolf-Spear sold through the Family for 31,000 gold.
