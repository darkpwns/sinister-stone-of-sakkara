# Session S06 — The Lady Below

## Recap
Starving at the catacomb stair, the party fell back to town to eat. Delve 6: bribed past the Red Eye gnolls, slept Ur-Namu and his acolytes in the Outer Fane and slew them, stripped the altar's gold, killed stirges at the gate. Delve 7: allied with the Black Fang gnolls to kill the ogres, stole the Stone from the empty Inner Fane, and fought the Lady and her skeletons at the pillars. She would not yield while thieves held her Stone (replayed from round 8 after a misread): blinded by Marcia's flash she still felled Thorcigar, her zombies cut down Gallus and Lucilla, and the party threw the Stone back for a truce. Gallus and Thorcigar died; Tarq was healed to 1 hp in time; Zaira lost a leg.

## Treasure and XP
Delve 6: 2,620 treasure XP + 304 monster XP. Delve 7: 525 treasure XP + 436 monster XP. The Lady and the Stone remain.
