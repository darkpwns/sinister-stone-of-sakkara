# Party notes

The players' shared notebook, written by the Judge at state saves. Player-facing: only what the party has been told or
has seen, in the players' own understanding (mistakes included; the Judge does not correct them from hidden knowledge).
Prefix bullets with the session (`S03:`). Run 3 (from 2026-11-25).

## Our aim
- S01: We came to Türos Tem for the legate's call: 2,000gp to whoever puts a stop to the beastmen raids out of the
  Viaspen Forest. We mean to find their lair and end the raids at the root; every session goal serves that. Animal
  Training stays in the party.

## Rumours and what we make of them
- S02: The dwarves' cave on a rocky hill is the black granite hill in 19.19, a long day from town; its crack is
  guarded by four small dog-headed scaly folk who call it "the Lady's house" and say Urza takes ten gold a head.
- S02: A place sealed by the gods under a mountain of rock (the legate): the black hill fits.
- S02: The Black Fang and Red Eye gnolls are the toughest warbands (Octavia). An elven wizard-lord behind the raids
  (Thales) and purple dragon scales (the legate) sound like tavern talk.
- S02: Drusus, a scheming brigand, is wanted (750gp from the quartermaster, posted in the fort).

- S03: Inside the black hill: a long hall of frescoes; kobolds hold the west (Urza, paid: they leave us alone for good);
  brigands hold the east (Drusus is their king; the guards at the double doors dislike us, but a drinking gang
  liked us and asked us to join); orcs somewhere north-west; hobgoblins in the north-east (our friends now). The
  hobgoblins say "the Lady" calls the tribes and takes sacrifices; their witch doctor meant to give her their chief.
- S03: The goblins of the old warrens were butchered by the hobgoblins; a few newcomers remain, angry.
- S03: Spider eggs in a web in the kobolds' ground: whatever laid them is behind the curtain with the jars.
