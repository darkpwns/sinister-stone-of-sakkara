# Party notes

The players' shared notebook, written by the Judge from the party agent's suggested updates at state saves. Player-facing: only what the party has been told or has seen, in the players' own understanding (mistakes included; the Judge does not correct them from hidden knowledge). Prefix bullets with the session (`S03:`).

## Rumours and what we make of them
- S06: Night 96, forest hex 11.07: thirteen of us were found asleep, with no watch, by an elven file. The speaker is tall, in a grey cloak, with an old white scar across one cheek, and speaks clipped, formal Common. Bows were in hand but not drawn.

## Plans
- S06: Feed the party from Tarq's rations, scout the stair head only, then withdraw to camp at the stream for water and food before any descent.

- S07 (between adventures): Hire a priestess of our own as a henchman. Genelen will not leave her hospital, and Türos Tem has none to hire: go to the prefecture's capital up the Imperial road, where the temples are; ask Genelen for a good word first.

- S08 (day 195), the shriekers: the grey-capped fungi in the sewer tunnel scream at light within about 60' (and at anything moving close to them), and twice their screams brought the long-legged pale hunters that killed Sabina, Tarq and Hesper. Contingency agreed: never walk past them again. Come down through the next grate along the avenue, beyond them (Old District grates lift out freely), with lights hooded until down the shaft and moving away from them. If we ever must pass them: hooded lights, at a run, oil and Phaedra's sleep-spell ready for the hunters, crossbows and spears to the rear. The big grasshoppers in the caves below shriek too when anyone comes within a few feet or fights near them: keep wide of them and fight elsewhere.

- S09 (day 196), the table's strength rule: no descent below the cesspool starts unless the levels of the PCs and
  classed henchmen going down add up to the floor for the deepest part we mean to reach: the entrance caves, the
  warlord's halls (bronze doors at the foot of the first cave) and the upper galleries reached through them **18**;
  the cave of wall graves below the triangular cave, the martyrs' gallery beyond the painted hall and anything at that
  depth **24**; the huge cavern below the rope cliff and the galleries off it **30**; deeper still **39**, the very
  bottom **48**. Torch-bearers and other 0th-level help count 1 each; one who rises from 0th to 1st level counts 1½, at 2nd level 2, then his level. Day 196: Lucilla 4, Vettius 4, Iulia 2,
  Quirinus 2, Vestus 1, Neris 1, Brennus 1, Phaedra 1, Ottar 1 = **17**. Day 211, after hiring: **25**.

- S09 (day 211), superseded S10 (day 233): keep only the help the strength rule needs. Every henchman is a half share of treasure and XP out of
  our pockets: when we stand at the floor without one, we let him go. Nysa let go on day 211 (25 against 24).

- S09 (day 212): we are adventurers, not grave-robbers on day trips. Plan expeditions of several days toward the
  deepest, richest places our strength allows: a week of iron rations each, torches for a week, oil, rope; sleep below
  in a cleared tomb with the door shut and spiked (iron spikes and a hammer: Vettius carries 12; buy more); come home when the packs are full or we are spent, not at the first scare.

- S09 (day 212): spike doors. Spike shut a door we retreat through, a door with something behind it we leave
  alone, and the doors of the room we sleep in; spike open a door we will need to run back through.

- S09 (day 212): set traps for what we know is there. Fight known monsters on ground we prepared: poured burning oil
  at a doorway they must cross (5' patch, a turn, 1d8 to anything entering), caltrops on the approach (bags, 10gp),
  spiked doors to funnel them, nets and set spears at a choke point, holy water and oil ready. Lure, don't chase.
  Known targets: the masked dead lord behind the last slab of the first tomb (fire hurts it, it walks slowly, it can't
  cross Neris' holy circle); the troglodytes who use the galleries; the hunters of the sewer tunnel.

## Plans (continued)
- S10 (day 230), the tax men. Half of what comes out from under the city is the prefecture's by law, and its men
  watch the gates, the Emporium and the Guildhouse; one already stopped Lucilla (day 223). What we agreed:
  - **One selling day a month, early in the month.** The more we sell in a month, the closer they look; so goods
    wait in the strongbox and go out together on one day near the start of the next month, not a sack at a time.
  - **Sell some of it elsewhere.** Gems and jewellery travel light: whatever is left over goes with Lucilla's
    textile trade to Türos Tem and the road towns, where no one asks where it was dug up.
  - **Coin stays in the strongbox**, spent a little at a time; no more ancient coin into the Guildhouse.
  - **Lucilla does the talking** if an official stops us: the story of the old family warehouse, and a purse.
  - **Liber Faunus** ("cheaper than the tax man", Lucilla's contacts, day 230): a last resort, not a first.
    Ingrid's numbers: we lose half only when an official both stops us and will not be talked round, which has
    not happened yet; a fence's cut on everything is worse unless they start stopping us every time. Kwame
    wants to meet him anyway. Nobody Lawful goes near him.
  - The Silk Pony's mistress makes debtors vanish (day 230). We pay every night; keep it that way.
- S10 (day 231), Lucilla's caravans. Her numbers: a cart of cloth to Türos Tem clears about 150gp a trip (that town
  buys only a few stone a day) against weeks on the road, so a cart goes only when we travel anyway, loaded with
  cloth and with the jewellery tucked under it. Instead she has put 700gp each into two Guildhouse caravans in her
  name: a steady one on her own textile road to Türos Tem, and a riskier long haul to the coast. She can back one
  more. Returns are collected in Cyfaraun after each month's end.
- S10 (day 233): we keep the people we have. Every one of them is earning his keep and his share now; we hire
  again only if deaths drop us below the strength line for where we are going. Phaedra and Gnaeus, let go this
  morning, are back (Gnaeus has his battle axe again).
- S10 (day 241), Liber Faunus. After the big selling day a note asked Lucilla to The Mink. Liber (lean, soft-spoken,
  never raises his voice: "Blood is bad for business, friend") knew about us and our grate already. Deal struck:
  he buys the elven work (grave goods, art, jewellery) at eight parts in ten, clean coin, no tax men; our gold and
  gems stay our own; his people leave us be on the Old District grates; now and then he wants to hear what is below.
  He did not ask for the way in and we did not give it. Never sell elven work past him from now on.
