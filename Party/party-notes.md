# Party notes

The players' shared notebook, written by the Judge at state saves. Player-facing: only what the party has been told or
has seen, in the players' own understanding (mistakes included; the Judge does not correct them from hidden knowledge).
Prefix bullets with the session (`S03:`). Run 2 (from 2026-11-24).

## The kingdom
- S01: Our aim: found a magical kingdom the like of which the world has not seen since ancient times. We keep Animal
  Training and the harvesting proficiencies in the party always; every session goal advances the kingdom.

## Rumours and what we make of them
- S06: A white unicorn at the Shrine of the White Lady (north-east, up the river): holy to Mityara, says Faustina.
- S02: Red Eye gnolls (hyena-men) in the buried temple say 'the Lady Below' pays any band that wipes out one of her lesser tribes. So the beastmen there serve a mistress, and she sets them on each other.

## Plans

- S06: Pilgrims say a white unicorn drinks at the Shrine of the White Lady, a day or two north-east up the Krysivor. Hrodric wants to see it; Faustina says the White Lady is Mityara and her beast is not ours to take. We go as pilgrims first, and look.
- S06: The pups will be tame about day 46 (Hrodric's) and day 49 (Severina's). Galerius found no war-horse foal to train. The officers at the fort have turned against us (Ilarion, Miren, Thales): stop asking them for things.
- S05: Downtime: Faustina worked in Genelen's hospital and won her friendship (she fears the nightmares are a curse); Corvus walked the farm tracks till he knows them (no more getting lost with him leading); Callista got nowhere with Malyn's apprentices; Galerius fell out badly with the quartermaster. Hrodric stays in town training the pups.
- S05: Severina hired Marius (a cantina keeper, good cook) and Lupa (a butcher). The ogre broke Lupa's leg (amputated) and he quit; we carry him home.
- S05: Killed the ogre of the north hall; its bag held 3,000 silver and 300 gold; its knucklebones kept in jars. Carry more jars inside: we lost six fliers' parts for want of them.
- S05: Orcs say the purple one in the stepped tower takes a hundred a head to let anyone down to the Lady.

- S04: Lost twice more on the farmland between the forest and the fort (days 22, 24). We need a guide or a better map of the farm tracks; follow the river.
- S04: Seen near the fort: fourteen skeletons marching by day toward the forest (they ignored us), and three hairy men by the ford who watched us.
- S04: The purse holds about 2,050gp (day 27). Henchmen can be sought again from day 31.
- S03: Tertia died of thirst on day 9, lost in the forest after the stirges took her leg. Her cousin Severina (venturer) joins us. We buried Tertia at the fort.

- S03: The kingdom's first beasts: five war-dog pups, three trained by Hrodric and two by Severina as guards; tame by day 40 if they stay in town to work with them, fully trained in five months.
- S03: Every member carries five waterskins' worth: the two donkeys carry 35 skins. Never again into the forest without a day's water each and a known spring.
- S03: The town is cold to us: the guildmaster, the quartermaster and the annalist are unfriendly. Malyn's apprentices will buy monster parts. No henchmen for hire until next month (day 31).
- S02: The temple is in the Viaspen, a day and a half NW of the fort: a crack in a black granite dome. Kobolds hold the west end of the great hall (they took their three captives back and leave us be); brigands to the east; hyena-men, hobgoblins and an ogre (west of the north hall: demands meat or gold) further in. Clutches of giant crab spider eggs are worth keeping or selling to a trainer or mage.
- S02: In town: sell or keep the spider eggs (a trainer able to raise giant vermin is what the kingdom needs; raising them ourselves needs 4 ranks of Animal Training); bank the gold; heal Tertia; buy armour and more metamphora; find henchmen next month.
- S02: Lost in the forest on the way home (day 6-7): head east-south-east for the farmland and the river.
