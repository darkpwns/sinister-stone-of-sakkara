# Party notes

The players' shared notebook, written by the Judge at state saves. Player-facing: only what the party has been told or
has seen, in the players' own understanding (mistakes included; the Judge does not correct them from hidden knowledge).
Prefix bullets with the session (`S03:`). Run 2 (from 2026-11-24).

## The kingdom
- S01: Our aim: found a magical kingdom the like of which the world has not seen since ancient times. We keep Animal
  Training and the harvesting proficiencies in the party always; every session goal advances the kingdom.

## Rumours and what we make of them
- S08: The legate says the Black Fang and Red Eye gnolls are the toughest beastman bands about, and the borderland has grown dangerous since the garrisons were stripped for the western wars. An old imperial column by the road near the fort shows legionaries riding winged beasts: such riders were real once.
- S06: A white unicorn at the Shrine of the White Lady (north-east, up the river): holy to Mityara, says Faustina.
- S02: Red Eye gnolls (hyena-men) in the buried temple say 'the Lady Below' pays any band that wipes out one of her lesser tribes. So the beastmen there serve a mistress, and she sets them on each other.

## Plans
- S11: Karanos paid 500gp for his merchants and is friendly now (3% to draw our gold early). Petra and Titus went home with a month's pay; **Quintus** (fighter, Galerius') and **Zaira** (elven spellsword, healer, Callista's) joined; Vestus took a gift and went his way. Callista copied Mage Missile and Beguile Humanoid into her book.
- S11: Severina's two pups learned their first trick (day 79). Hrodric's are due day 82.
- S11: **The arch on the knoll above the river** (near the fort): at sunset it showed us a pool among white birches, a white shrine and a white horned beast drinking. Genelen says it is Mityara's pool: a pure Lawful pilgrim who drinks may be healed of dark magic's harm, but the Lady lays a charge on them; the beast suffers no drawn weapon near it. **Next: a pilgrimage up the river to the shrine once the ground dries** (Corvus now knows the tracks north of the fort).
- S10: Mud again kept us in town two days (the pups trained); then back to the temple through the hobgoblins (they beat off an orc raid while we were gone). The chief told us the purple one keeps human prisoners by his tower and sells them. We bought all seven from him, the two missing merchants (Daros Thenes, Odysios Panas: 250gp each from Guildmaster Karanos alive), their men-at-arms Thorwell and Thorcigar, and the three 'Fierce Brethren' (Quintus, Vestus, Zaira), for 700gp, and the two spellbooks he took from Vestus and Zaira for 100gp more. Not a blow struck but at a rat swarm we shut a door on.
- S10: The purple one says the Lady's priests come up to buy prisoners for her altar, and that Abgal went down past the Red Eyes.
- S10: In town: deliver the merchants to Karanos (he holds our 13,000gp; this should warm him). The brothers and the Brethren want to serve us; we hold four henchmen already. Decide whom to keep. Callista can copy the Brethren's spells.
- S09: **The hobgoblins are our friends now.** We went to them with a cask of wine and talk, not swords. Shulgi (a champion) told us the witch doctor Abgal and Hepteth meant to give their wounded chief Thethamun to the Lady. Faustina healed the chief; his warriors stood with him and with us; Abgal, Hepteth and four others ran north toward the old stair, likely to the Lady. Thethamun paid us 300gp and four silver winged-sun symbols of Ammonar (Varro holds them: sell them or give them to a temple of his god?), gave us free passage, and let us take the beetle nest his people feared.
- S09: Two Slumbers took four glowing beetles and their eggs without a blow. Two broke loose on the way home; the rest sold in town (150gp). Raising beetles needs four ranks of Animal Training; we have none yet.
- S09: The orcs still take us for the Lady's recruits. One band had come to raid the hobgoblins and left when they saw us with them; another told us the Red Eyes guard the stair under the stepped tower and the Black Fangs below are out of the Lady's favour.
- S09: In the forest, 'merchants' with a bloodied wagon heading away from every track tried to lure us in; Corvus saw through it and we slipped away in the rain. Seven elves on the farm tracks ignored us.
- S09: Watch for Abgal: he knows our faces and has gone below.
- S08: Home on day 52. The cache paid 12,930gp; the five pilgrims are 2nd level. 13,000gp is with Guildmaster Karanos for a month (free if we leave it till day 82; he takes 15% if we draw it early). The purse (Faustina) about 1,700gp.
- S08: Faustina hired **Varro**, a priest-crusader of Ammonar (banded plate, mace; rebukes the dead): good for the temple's lower levels. Four henchmen now: Marius, Petra, Titus (Severina's), Varro (Faustina's). More only from day 61.
- S08: The legate is now friendly to us: he gave Faustina a scroll to recruit soldiers at the Parade Ground (one in four of those drilling will listen) and introduced Callista to Malyn. Malyn (cold, but civil) sold her a copy of his Discern Magic formula for 500gp: she knows it now.
- S08: Next: the buried temple's level below (we have the strength our rule asks for, 12 Hit Dice, if Hrodric and Severina come: their pups' training stops while they are away). The purple one in the stepped tower takes a hundred a head to let us down; or we find another way. Then the fortress, much later, with soldiers.
- S07: The pilgrimage went wrong: lost in the rain east of the shrine (days 47-49), we came out at Türos Aster. On the way: a rust-red seam of ore by a stream in the forest north-west of Aster (we couldn't tell what), a host of pale spirits (we slipped away), and on the road back a hidden chest of gold and jewelry under a waystone.
- S07: **The kingdom's first ground:** the legate of Türos Aster will make whoever clears the bugbears out of the ruined imperial fortress across the ford a patrician, with a claim to the stronghold. We want it. But what Corvus saw from Aster's watchtower is a whole tribe behind a palisade (archers on the towers, scores of tents, smoke everywhere): six of us and three cooks would be eaten. **Not yet.** First: levels (the buried temple, level by level, at the strength we agreed), proper henchmen (at 2nd level we may hire 1st-level ones), and later mercenaries for an assault on a walled camp. Scout the fortress properly before any plan (Tomasz: count them first).
- S07: Hrodric's three pups are tame (day 46). Severina hired Titus, a bookbinder (day 45).
- S06: Back from the ogre: 720gp of treasure; the purse about 2,500gp. Lupa quit and rests at the inn. Severina hired Petra (a seamstress) on day 38.
