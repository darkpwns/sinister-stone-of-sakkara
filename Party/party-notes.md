# Party notes

The players' shared notebook, written by the Judge at state saves. Player-facing: only what the party has been told or
has seen, in the players' own understanding (mistakes included; the Judge does not correct them from hidden knowledge).
Prefix bullets with the session (`S03:`). Run 2 (from 2026-11-24).

## The kingdom
- S01: Our aim: found a magical kingdom the like of which the world has not seen since ancient times. We keep Animal
  Training and the harvesting proficiencies in the party always; every session goal advances the kingdom.

## Rumours and what we make of them
- S02: Red Eye gnolls (hyena-men) in the buried temple say 'the Lady Below' pays any band that wipes out one of her lesser tribes. So the beastmen there serve a mistress, and she sets them on each other.

## Plans

- S03: We pool everything now: Faustina keeps the company purse (about 1,420gp at day 19) and the company chest at the Traveler's Inn.
- S03: The kingdom's first beasts: five war-dog pups, three trained by Hrodric and two by Severina as guards; tame by day 40 if they stay in town to work with them, fully trained in five months.
- S03: Every member carries five waterskins' worth: the two donkeys carry 35 skins. Never again into the forest without a day's water each and a known spring.
- S03: The town is cold to us: the guildmaster, the quartermaster and the annalist are unfriendly. Malyn's apprentices will buy monster parts. No henchmen for hire until next month (day 31).
- S02: The temple is in the Viaspen, a day and a half NW of the fort: a crack in a black granite dome. Clutches of giant crab spider eggs are worth keeping or selling to a trainer or mage.
