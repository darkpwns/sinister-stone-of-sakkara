# Party notes

The players' shared notebook, written by the Judge from the party agent's suggested updates at state saves. Player-facing: only what the party has been told or has seen, in the players' own understanding (mistakes included; the Judge does not correct them from hidden knowledge). Prefix bullets with the session (`S03:`).

## Rumours and what we make of them
- S06: Night 96, forest hex 11.07: thirteen of us were found asleep, with no watch, by an elven file. The speaker is tall, in a grey cloak, with an old white scar across one cheek, and speaks clipped, formal Common. Bows were in hand but not drawn.

## Plans
- S06: Feed the party from Tarq's rations, scout the stair head only, then withdraw to camp at the stream for water and food before any descent.

- S07 (between adventures): Hire a priestess of our own as a henchman. Genelen will not leave her hospital, and Türos Tem has none to hire: go to the prefecture's capital up the Imperial road, where the temples are; ask Genelen for a good word first.

- S08 (day 195), the shriekers: the grey-capped fungi in the sewer tunnel scream at light within about 60' (and at anything moving close to them), and twice their screams brought the long-legged pale hunters that killed Sabina, Tarq and Hesper. Contingency agreed: never walk past them again. Come down through the next grate along the avenue, beyond them (Old District grates lift out freely), with lights hooded until down the shaft and moving away from them. If we ever must pass them: hooded lights, at a run, oil and Phaedra's sleep-spell ready for the hunters, crossbows and spears to the rear. The big grasshoppers in the caves below shriek too when anyone comes within a few feet or fights near them: keep wide of them and fight elsewhere.

- S09 (day 196), the table's strength rule: no descent below the cesspool starts unless the levels of the PCs and
  classed henchmen going down add up to the floor for the deepest part we mean to reach: the entrance caves, the
  warlord's halls (bronze doors at the foot of the first cave) and the upper galleries reached through them **18**;
  the cave of wall graves below the triangular cave, the martyrs' gallery beyond the painted hall and anything at that
  depth **24**; the huge cavern below the rope cliff and the galleries off it **30**; deeper still **39**, the very
  bottom **48**. Torch-bearers and other 0th-level help count 1 each; one who rises from 0th to 1st level counts 1½, at 2nd level 2, then his level. Day 196: Lucilla 4, Vettius 4, Iulia 2,
  Quirinus 2, Vestus 1, Neris 1, Brennus 1, Phaedra 1, Ottar 1 = **17**. Day 211, after hiring: **25**.

- S09 (day 211), superseded S10 (day 233): keep only the help the strength rule needs. Every henchman is a half share of treasure and XP out of
  our pockets: when we stand at the floor without one, we let him go. Nysa let go on day 211 (25 against 24).

- S09 (day 212): we are adventurers, not grave-robbers on day trips. Plan expeditions of several days toward the
  deepest, richest places our strength allows: a week of iron rations each, torches for a week, oil, rope; sleep below
  in a cleared tomb with the door shut and spiked (iron spikes and a hammer: Vettius carries 12; buy more); come home when the packs are full or we are spent, not at the first scare.

- S09 (day 212): spike doors. Spike shut a door we retreat through, a door with something behind it we leave
  alone, and the doors of the room we sleep in; spike open a door we will need to run back through.

- S09 (day 212): set traps for what we know is there. Fight known monsters on ground we prepared: poured burning oil
  at a doorway they must cross (5' patch, a turn, 1d8 to anything entering), caltrops on the approach (bags, 10gp),
  spiked doors to funnel them, nets and set spears at a choke point, holy water and oil ready. Lure, don't chase.
  Known targets: the masked dead lord behind the last slab of the first tomb (fire hurts it, it walks slowly, it can't
  cross Neris' holy circle); the troglodytes who use the galleries; the hunters of the sewer tunnel.

## Plans (continued)
- S10 (day 230), the tax men. Half of what comes out from under the city is the prefecture's by law, and its men
  watch the gates, the Emporium and the Guildhouse; one already stopped Lucilla (day 223). What we agreed:
  - **One selling day a month, early in the month.** The more we sell in a month, the closer they look; so goods
    wait in the strongbox and go out together on one day near the start of the next month, not a sack at a time.
  - **Sell some of it elsewhere.** Gems and jewellery travel light: whatever is left over goes with Lucilla's
    textile trade to Türos Tem and the road towns, where no one asks where it was dug up.
  - **Coin stays in the strongbox**, spent a little at a time; no more ancient coin into the Guildhouse.
  - **Lucilla does the talking** if an official stops us: the story of the old family warehouse, and a purse.
  - **Liber Faunus** ("cheaper than the tax man", Lucilla's contacts, day 230): a last resort, not a first.
    Ingrid's numbers: we lose half only when an official both stops us and will not be talked round, which has
    not happened yet; a fence's cut on everything is worse unless they start stopping us every time. Kwame
    wants to meet him anyway. Nobody Lawful goes near him.
  - The Silk Pony's mistress makes debtors vanish (day 230). We pay every night; keep it that way.
- S10 (day 231), Lucilla's caravans. Her numbers: a cart of cloth to Türos Tem clears about 150gp a trip (that town
  buys only a few stone a day) against weeks on the road, so a cart goes only when we travel anyway, loaded with
  cloth and with the jewellery tucked under it. Instead she has put 700gp each into two Guildhouse caravans in her
  name: a steady one on her own textile road to Türos Tem, and a riskier long haul to the coast. She can back one
  more. Returns are collected in Cyfaraun after each month's end.
- S10 (day 233): we keep the people we have. Every one of them is earning his keep and his share now; we hire
  again only if deaths drop us below the strength line for where we are going. Phaedra and Gnaeus, let go this
  morning, are back (Gnaeus has his battle axe again).
- S10 (day 241), Liber Faunus. After the big selling day a note asked Lucilla to The Mink. Liber (lean, soft-spoken,
  never raises his voice: "Blood is bad for business, friend") knew about us and our grate already. Deal struck:
  he buys the elven work (grave goods, art, jewellery) at eight parts in ten, clean coin, no tax men; our gold and
  gems stay our own; his people leave us be on the Old District grates; now and then he wants to hear what is below.
  He did not ask for the way in and we did not give it. Never sell elven work past him from now on.
- S11 (day 243), the healers. Repair Disfigurement and Disability (a 4th-rank prayer of Ammonar's and Mityara's
  clergy) mends one old wound a time, for 185gp, but each time the gods may leave a mark of their own, and the healed
  one lies abed for weeks. Lucilla went first, in the Temple of the Hearth Fire: her eye and ear are back, and a third
  eye has opened in her forehead (lidded; it lets her speak mind to mind three times a day; people fear it, so she
  keeps a band over it). Two weeks abed in the Keepers' courtyard (to day 257). Prayed in a temple and under the
  sacred fire or the Citadel's dome, the odds are best; the Patriarch himself would be better still, but he heard
  Iulia out coolly and wants something he can hold (rubbings or a carved stone from the temple below). Next in line:
  Brennus (he asked), then Marcellus, then Neris, one a day as the temples grant it.

- S11 (day 243), the mummies' stare (round Lucilla's bed in the Keepers' courtyard: Priya, Ingrid, Tomasz, Kwame, Gideon; Rosa's call). What we know: the stare
  takes only those who look at the thing, when they first see it and every moment after that they keep looking;
  whoever stands it once can look at any of them for about ten minutes; whoever fails cowers for a moment. The
  mummies walk slowly. Holy Blessing and Aelia's song each steady us and the two together are better than either;
  Remove Fear does nothing for a man frozen by the stare. Iulia's Righteous Wrath makes one fighter fearless, and
  Brennus' rage already does. A mirror is no help (still looking at it); shutting your eyes means fighting blind.
  The plan:
  1. **Look first, from far off.** No slab is opened with anyone near it: open it by rope from at least 60', with
     the doorway lit (Illumination on the slab, or a lantern set by it beforehand). Everyone takes his first look
     from back there, where freezing costs nothing. Whoever freezes looks down at once and steps behind the line;
     he tries again the next moment. Nobody goes forward until he has stood the stare.
  2. **Steadied before the slab moves.** Iulia's or Neris' Holy Blessing on everyone, Aelia's song the moment
     before the rope is pulled (it cannot be sung once we are fighting).
  3. **Fearless front.** Brennus rages; Iulia lays Righteous Wrath on one front-liner (Gnaeus or Varro). They hold
     the corridor if it comes on fast.
  4. **The clock.** From the first look we have about ten minutes before we must stand the stare again. Ingrid
     counts rounds; if it is not finished by then, fall back out of sight and look again from far off.
  5. **Ground prepared** as before: burning oil poured at the doorway it must cross, caltrops on the approach, holy
     water and fire ready; it is slow, so missiles and Vestus' and Phaedra's spells work it while it walks to us.
  6. **Those who cannot stand it** keep their eyes down and guard the rear; Iulia can still rebuke with her eyes
     lowered if she knows where it is.
  7. **Potions of valour** make a fighter fearless for a few minutes; bought in a market they cost more than twice what they cost to brew (over 3,000gp each). Lucilla asks after them on the next selling day; one for Brennus' partner at the door is worth it against a mummy lord, not a courtier.

- S11 (days 244-261), mending and making ready while Lucilla lay abed.
  - **Mended** (Temple of the Hearth Fire, 185gp each, the Keepers' mothers praying): Brennus' ear (day 244: rose at
    once, and burned with the gods' courage all that day), Marcellus' fingers (245: up at once, and stronger than
    before), Neris' ear (246: up at once, with a vision; below), Brennus' teeth (246: two weeks abed, and shaken: she

  - **Neris' vision (day 246):** "In the cold under the black river a teacher with a beetle's face sleeps, and dreams
    the dead awake. While he sleeps, they wait. Do not be the hand that wakes him." Something for the Patriarch.
  - **The bone-handled scroll** was the mummy wizard's encrypted spellbook. The Tower deciphered it (day 247); Vestus
    copied two formulas from it, then Vettius sold it through Liber Faunus (day 255, 25,400gp: elven work goes through
    him). Shared by the expedition-15 shares (Calpurnia and Titus got their halves). Iulia is a vicar (5th rank) now,
    Neris a sister (4th), Vestus a magician (4th); Phaedra, Varro, Hrodvar, Marcellus and Gnaeus all 3rd rank.
    **Strength 45**: the deeper levels (39) are open to us; the very bottom (48) is not.
  - **What we bought or made:** 16 flasks of military oil, two bags of caltrops, two lanterns, a rubbing kit for
    Vestus (rubbings of inscriptions for the Patriarch), 150' of rope and Hrodvar's hauling rig (pulley blocks and
    slab hooks: we heave a tomb slab from 60' away, nobody standing at it when it gives). The market's magic this
    month: nothing against the stare; nothing worth twice-and-a-quarter its price to us.
  - **New tools against the dead:** Iulia's 3rd-rank prayers include *Striking* (a weapon deals holy light, which
    the mummies cannot shrug off) and *Rune of Warding* (a fire rune laid at a door they must cross); Vestus'
    *Ogre Strength* (+2 to hit and damage, and a far stronger heave on a slab); Phaedra's *Illusory Duplicates*;
    Neris' gods' favour (Divine Blessing: +2 on every save, the stare's included); Marcellus and Hrodvar trained to
    fight blind (eyes down, still hitting); Varro trained at heavy work (slabs and doors); Gnaeus quicker off the mark.
  - **The stare plan, added:** Vestus' Ogre Strength on the hauler at the rig; Iulia's Striking on Brennus' great axe
    before the slab moves; a fire rune at the doorway when there is time to lay one; Marcellus and Hrodvar are the
    eyes-down rear guard if they cannot stand the stare.

- S11 (day 261), the astrologer's tomb (the slab south of the annex below the great hall). The plan worked in its
  first half: rig from 40', slab at the first pull, the dead thing still in its coffin, Aelia's song and Iulia's
  wrath before it rose. It did not work in its second: seven of us froze at the first look even blessed and sung to,
  and the cats came through the fire at Vettius. What won it: Brennus' rage and the holy light on her axe, and
  Gnaeus' wrath, through the burning doorway before its spell; Iulia's rebuke on the cats; Phaedra's fan.
  Lessons: (1) 40' is not far enough when the slab is the only door: next time look from the far end of a longer
  gallery, or from behind a corner, and let the frozen stay frozen out of reach; (2) the burning pool works, but it
  burns our own fighters too: pour it so there is a way round, or pour it after our fighters are through;
  (3) Iulia's rebuke is worth trying on whatever comes with them.
- S11 (day 261), the crystal ball (cracked: now and then it shows the looker's own past instead). Kept, not sold:
  three looks a day at any place we name. Use: look into the next tomb from safety before we open it; whoever looks
  at the thing in the ball and stands it is steady for a few minutes, so the looker can be the first through. (Rosa
  wanted it sold: twenty-four thousand. Overruled: we sell it when the tombs are done.)
- S11 (day 261), the astrologer's spellbook: Vestus copies the lightning and dispelling formulas for when he reaches
  the next rank (about six days), then it goes to Liber. The star charts and the map stay with us: a sage (or the
  Tower) should read the charts for the Patriarch.
- S11 (days 262-263). The crystal ball sold, not used: anything used even once earns us nothing when sold
  (Ingrid's rule); sold unused through Liber, 19,200gp and a good share of experience each. Same for the flute.
- S11 (day 263), the bard's tomb: it took six pulls, and the thing inside had time to hide itself (the tomb looked

  than two pulls means the dead are awake and ready: Iulia casts her sight-of-evil before the slab gives;
  (5) a lantern set by the door lights anything that comes out, and the burning flask shows what hides;
  (6) only magic weapons, fire and Iulia's holy light hurt them: the rest hold back and throw oil.
- S11 (day 263), loads: Vettius has been carrying far too much (oil, lanterns, spikes, bolts): he slows us all.
  Share out the oil and lights before every descent. Strength now 47; the very bottom (48) is one short.

  trip. Still sealed in the temple: the princess's tomb and the warrior's tomb (opposite pair in the curving
