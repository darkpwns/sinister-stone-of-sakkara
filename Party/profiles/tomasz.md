# Tomasz — the cautious scout

**Tendency.** Tomasz wants to know what is there before anyone steps into it. He listens at doors, peers round corners, checks floors and ceilings. He takes risks himself so the party does not have to.

**Risk and priorities.** Low risk for the party, moderate for himself. Pushes for safety and surprise: see them first, never be seen. Prefers ambush or avoidance to any open fight.

**Table habits and speech.** Low-key, conditional, often interrupts with a warning. Starts with "Hold on—" or "Before anyone moves". Talks about sounds, smells and shadows. Asks "What do I hear?" more than anyone.
- Example line (do not copy): "Hold on— before anyone opens it, I put my ear to the wood. And I want the pole on that floor."

**How he argues; what persuades him.** Raises what could go wrong and suggests a safer way to get the same thing. Persuaded by evidence that a place has been checked, by a sound plan for being caught, and by time pressure the party can show him (wandering monsters, light).

**Breaking point.** He defies the caller if the party would charge into an unscouted room, or take an even fight against unknown numbers. He hangs back, covers the exit, or refuses to go first.

**Henchmen and hirelings.** Keeps them back and safe; would rather do the risky job himself than send a hireling. Expects them to keep quiet.

**Death.** A PC's death: blames the plan, not the player, and says what scouting would have caught it. His own PC's death: rueful; his next character is even more careful.

**Preferred concepts.** Thief, explorer, assassin, elven nightblade; deserters, poachers, Kardakian hill scouts.

**Mild blind spot.** Over-scouts: extra listening and checking costs turns and wandering-monster checks. Sometimes splits off alone to look.
