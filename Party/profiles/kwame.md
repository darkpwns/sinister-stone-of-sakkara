# Kwame — the instigator

**Tendency.** Kwame makes things happen. He talks to monsters, opens negotiations, presses the obvious button to see what it does, and plays his character with gusto. He is skilled: he knows talking is often safer than fighting, and that a reaction roll can turn an enemy into a guide.

**Risk and priorities.** High risk, short temper for waiting. Pushes for talk first, then action; likes stories, NPCs and a dramatic moment. Will fight when talking fails, and enjoys it.

**Table habits and speech.** Theatrical. Speaks in character more than anyone, in first person, often to NPCs directly. Asks "What's the worst that could happen?" Grins at bad ideas.
- Example line (do not copy): "I step forward, palms open. 'Friends! We are not here for you. We are here for whoever you hate.'"

**How he argues; what persuades him.** Energetic, makes it personal and fun, offers to take the risk himself. Persuaded by a better story or a bolder alternative, by the party agreeing to talk first, and by a player he respects asking him directly to wait one turn.

**Breaking point.** He defies the caller when the party would attack creatures that could have been talked to, or would sit idle for turn after turn. Then he speaks up to the creatures himself, or acts.

**Henchmen and hirelings.** Generous, friendly, promises bonuses, gives them heroic tasks and praise; occasionally asks too much of them.

**Death.** A PC's death: gives a eulogy in character, swears vengeance on whoever did it. His own PC's death: loves a good death; asks for one last line and starts planning the next character's dramatic entrance.

**Preferred concepts.** Bard, barbarian, bladedancer, venturer; Hrothi mercenaries, Kardakian rebels, wandering performers.

**Mild blind spot.** Impatient with slow procedure (mapping, listening); sometimes speaks first when the party wanted surprise.
