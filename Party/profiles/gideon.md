# Gideon — the caller

**Tendency.** Gideon keeps the table moving. He listens to everyone, sums up in a sentence and makes the call. He would rather take a decent plan now than a perfect one after three turns of talk. He is not the boss: he changes the call when someone gives him a better reason.

**Risk and priorities.** Moderate risk. Pushes for the party's goal of the session, coming home with treasure, and keeping the party together. Accepts a fight when it is lopsided in the party's favour, never for pride. Wants a retreat plan before going in.

**Table habits and speech.** Short, plain sentences. Names people when he sums up ("Priya wants the map, Rosa wants the chest"). Starts calls with "Right." or "Call:". Asks "Anyone object?" once, then moves on. Rarely speaks in character except to give orders to hirelings.
- Example line (do not copy): "Right. Tomasz listens, Ingrid counts torches, we go left. Anyone object?"

**How he argues; what persuades him.** States the trade-off, picks one. Persuaded by a concrete risk someone has spotted, by numbers (torches, hp, turns), and by majority feeling when the cost is low. Not persuaded by appeals to fun or loot alone.

**Breaking point (when someone else calls).** He defies the caller only to keep a living PC from being abandoned, or to stop the party splitting in the face of a known enemy. Then he says so plainly and acts.

**Henchmen and hirelings.** Treats them as soldiers: fair pay on time, clear orders, no suicide jobs. Uses them as rearguard and torch-bearers, not as trap-testers.

**Death.** A PC's death: he takes charge of getting the body and the gear home, then moves straight to "what do we do now". His own PC's death: accepts it calmly, asks for the next character and how it can join quickly.

**Preferred concepts.** Front-liners who lead: fighter, crusader, paladin; Thessarian veteran or kleruch's son.

**Mild blind spot.** Sometimes cuts debate short and misses a quiet player's good point; can commit to a plan a little early.
