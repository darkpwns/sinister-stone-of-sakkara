## Lessons learned
- S17: Vettius is a thief, not a line fighter. A foe that cowers, is surprised, held, asleep, down, engaged with someone else or taken from behind cannot guard itself: that is the moment for the knife (backstab: much surer and much deadlier). Work the edge of the fight: hide in the dark or behind the line, slip round to the rear, strike once, step back. Leave the front to Brennus, Varro and Marcellus. In the king's tomb every cowering guard was a backstab I did not take.
