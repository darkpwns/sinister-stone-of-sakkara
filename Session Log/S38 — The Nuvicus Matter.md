# Session S38 — The Nuvicus Matter

## Recap
Day 569, morning, the Solar Citadel. Nine in the company; Tiberius mends under the oculus. Month 19 ends tomorrow:
the lance and the hilt come back from the Tower. Celictor Nuvicus knows the Family's boss pays the Order's
blood-prices and means to sell it to the Prefect's man.

## Where they went
- Day 569. The company's council at the Citadel: Vettius' spies set on Nuvicus and learned his hour with the Prefect's
  man (tomorrow, the second hour after noon). Lucilla bought the Jutlander apothecary's written record of Nuvicus' poison
  buys for a week's pay; the Family sold it to the Prefect's man the same evening.
- Day 571. The Order's word for Brennus: a Krysean horse-coper who trampled a water-seller for a wager.
- Night of 572. Vettius found him alone among his horses at the Emporium's pens; one thrust from the dark. The river
  has him.

## Who they met
- The Jutlander apothecary of the Bridge District: pale, nervous, counts coin twice. "Names and dates, lady. Nothing more."

- Day 570. Month 19 ended: the Family's take, Lucilla's houses and caravans (the salt caravan found something worth a
  tenth more), the Tower's dues. The Tower sent back the lance (a fire lance with five crystals, for a fighter's hand)
  and the hilt (a sword of blue lightning, for a mage, eight rounds of blade left). Tiberius woke whole of his weakness,
  but the strength the shade took stays taken. Nuvicus went to the Prefect's man and came out red-faced.

- Dorion Makrenos, Krysean horse-coper ("Everyone steps aside for a good horse."). Dead.

## Treasure and XP
