# Session S29 — The High Priest's Shade

## Recap
Day 477, 17:42, the Solar Citadel. Twelve, whole. No goods unsold; coin banked. The Order's roll and month 16's end
fall on day 480; the Imperial Vanguard goes down by the 12U cesspool on day 481.

## Where they went
- Day 478, the sixty-fifth descent. Down by the Old District grate at first light; a grey-capped shrieker stood well
  off the way in the sewer caves and never saw the torches. In the temple's long colonnade the five kneeling shades were
  burned and broken before they rose from prayer: Iulia's and Neris' prayers against the dead destroyed three, and
  Vestus' fire took the last two. Nobody heard them whisper.
- The vestry behind the narthex: four cedar chests. Vettius opened three and Brennus broke the fourth: bundles of old
  temple records in the elven script, vials of precious scent, sticks of frankincense; rotten linens and a rotten hanging.
  A black jackal in dark flame and a pale hooded shape came on while the chests were worked: Iulia's light drove both,
  the fighters pinned them and Vettius finished each from behind. Ten stone-shelled prawns in the great serpent hall
  were burned and cut down. Iulia's spear shaft snapped on a prawn's shell.
- The python among the boulders north of the serpent hall seized Brennus and crushed her nearly to death before the
  company hacked it apart; Neris' prayers closed her wounds.
- The company sealed itself in the font room and spiked the door. Two shades came through the walls in the dark that
  afternoon; Iulia lit her spear with its word and her prayer unmade them both. The night passed quietly.
- Day 479. A clear, quivering block filled the passage to the chancel and was cut apart. Then the chancel: the company
  at the north curtain, Vettius under Neris' death ward up on the statue of the dragon-winged queen with his knife at
  her black eyes. The tall shade of the temple's last high priest came out of the south-west passage, and Iulia's prayer
  unmade it at the first word. Both onyx eyes came out; the gold and ivory were stripped from the statue. Behind a
  marble panel in the south-west passage lay the high priest's crushed bones under fallen rubble, and a silver key in his
  robe. A grey ooze was shot to death from a distance. Out by early afternoon; everything sold through the Family the
  same day.
- Day 480, month's end. The Family's month came to Vettius; Lucilla's houses, venture and caravans paid. Iulia spent the
  day at the Citadel: the Patriarch's charge is the courtesans' circle that Varinia named. Its mistress keeps the House
  of Green Silk on the Street of Lamps. The Watch's men watch the house and will send word on the night the circle
  gathers. The Order's word went out: Oppius will come tomorrow with contracts for Lucilla and Vettius.
