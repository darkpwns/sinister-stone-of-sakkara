# Session S01 — Five Lives Each (run 3)

## Recap
A fresh campaign of AX1 *The Sinister Stone of Sakkara* (run 3). Runs 1 and 2 are archived. Each player rolled five
characters' attributes (RR p. 13 optional rule, Generating Multiple Characters; JJ p. 387) and kept the best as a
1st-level PC, built without templates (3d6 × 10 gp, gear at list price, proficiencies and spells chosen). The table's
goal is to finish AX1 (Table rules). Eskil the shaman is the party's animal trainer (Animal Training, Animal
Husbandry, a dog totem); Silvanus the explorer also trains animals and brought a hunting dog. No play yet: the party
stands in Türos Tem on the drizzly, warm morning of day 1, kitted out.
