# Session S06 — The Lady Below

## Recap
Starving at the catacomb stair, the party fell back to town to eat. Delve 6: bribed past the Red Eye gnolls, slept Ur-Namu and his acolytes in the Outer Fane and slew them, stripped the altar's gold, killed stirges at the gate. Delve 7: allied with the Black Fang gnolls to kill the ogres, stole the Stone from the empty Inner Fane, fought the Lady and her skeletons at the pillars until she yielded at half strength. Tarq died of his wounds on the way home; Zaira lost a leg. The legate paid 2,000gp, had the Stone destroyed and sent the Fragments to the Empire's clerics.

## Treasure and XP
Delve 6: 2,620 treasure XP + 304 monster XP. Delve 7: about 6,750 treasure XP + 1,100 monster XP + 10,000 XP for the Fragments.
