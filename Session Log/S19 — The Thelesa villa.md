# Session S19 — The Thelesa villa

## Recap
Day 358, noon, Cyfaraun, the Solar Citadel. Thirteen, all whole. Rufio Calvus is dead and the Watch is satisfied.
Vestus finishes his week of study on day 360; the month ends that day and the selling day is 361. The Order has
given Vestus a name: Una of the Thelesa villa.

## Where they went
- Day 358, afternoon, strong wind. Lucilla bought her old contacts at the Mink a day's drink and asked about the
  Thelesa villa, south-west of the city. They knew it: an old patrician house gone to brambles beside the Türos
  Quell road, a madwoman living in it alone, and shepherds who will not pass the lane after dark because a grey
  shape walks the top of its wall at night.
- Day 359, overcast, a very strong wind. Vettius and Cotta walked the six miles to the villa. A rendered wall
  round a garden of brambles, a dry fountain, the house shuttered on the north side, a gap in the back wall to a
  dead olive orchard and a stone tomb-house under the trees. Vettius crept through the brambles to the north
  hall's shutters and listened: a woman's voice inside, sing-song, praying and talking to "Grandfather", and the
  smell of tallow. He did not go in. On the way back farmers on the road spat and told him to keep away from that
  house. Phaedra bought a fresh bundle of torches.
- Day 360, drizzle, mild. The twelfth month ended. The Guildhouse took its storage fees; Lucilla's two caravans
  paid their return (the salt caravan half again over its rate). Vettius paid Cotta's wage; Vestus paid his
  Tower dues and closed his books: the fireball is his now, the thunderbolt laid aside. Brother Oppius came to
  the Citadel gate with a sealed purse for Brennus: the Order's 1,500gp for Rufio.
- Iulia made her month's report to the Patriarch. Arëtar heard her out, then gave her a task. Lady Lipsus had
  come to the Citadel: her son Kyrus was thrown and killed on the Türos Quell road at the lane to the Thelesa
  villa in the autumn storms, and the country folk say a grey walker haunts its wall. "Lay the dead man to rest.
  The woman, if she is what they whisper, bring her to me in chains, or end her if she will not come." Four
  Knights of the Winged Sun will ride with the Captain.
- Day 361, the first of the thirteenth month, warm and clear. Selling day. Revered Mother Doarë opened the vault.
  Lucilla, Vestus and Aelia turned the rainbow disc over for an hour and could not name it; the company paid the
  Tower of Knowledge 2,000gp to have it named (two weeks: day 375), and Brennus and Hrodvar carried it to the
  Tower's counter. The jade vine ring stays in the vault: it was the Provost's, and no one in the company will show
  it at the Tower. Liber took the elven grave goods at four-fifths (9,442gp): the jars, the bowl and the bronze
  dragon, the mask and collar, the shrine and the gilded chair, the hooks, natron and urns, the charms, the grave
  goods of the Falling Star, and the rainbow metal from the reliquary walls. The garnets, the two amethysts and
  Vasan's wine cask went on the open market (4,940gp); no tax-collector came asking. Everyone banked their coin at
  the Guildhouse. Brother Oppius had no new names for anyone this week.
- Day 362, overcast and warm. Thirteen, with the cart, and four Knights of the Winged Sun in yellow cloaks, walked
  the Türos Quell road to the Thelesa villa. A farm family by the road was glad to see the Citadel: the old man
  said the madwoman steals their hens, and that the grey one on the wall wears an old toga and has no face. The
  knights held the gate with the cart and Cotta.
- Inside, the house was silent. Vettius lifted the bar of the west bedchamber with a blade; the woman inside was
  already awake, white-faced on her heap of furs. Iulia called on her to yield in the name of the Sun; she looked
  for the window instead. Brennus caught her and she twisted free; Vettius spoke the sword's word, "Freeze", and
  she stood rigid, and Vestus finished it. Una of the Thelesa is dead; the Order's work is done.
- The cold came across the atrium from the shrine door: a grey figure in a mouldering toga, its face a wax mask.
  It touched Lucilla and the cold went into her bones (she is weaker for it, and may grow weaker yet); her mace
  answered it. Then Iulia lifted the Rod of the Dawn, and the thing came apart like ash in a wind.
- Nine big rats came boiling up the cellar stair at the noise; Brennus held the trap and Phaedra's fan of flames
  burned six of them on the stair; the rest died at the top. Under the cedar chest in the shrine, where the
  family's twelve wax death masks hang, Brennus knocked through a false floor: jars of electrum, a silver box of
  stones, and the founder's death mask carved in dark wood and silver. In the cellar Vettius found a newer flag in
  the floor: an iron-bound box with a thousand electrum and a leather roll of jewels. Marcellus poured holy water
  on the black altar in the dining hall and Iulia and Neris broke it.
- Back at the Citadel by half past four. Iulia gave Arëtar her account, the witch's prayer book and her black
  serpent symbol; the knights bore her out. "The road is clean again. Lady Lipsus will hear it from me." He gave
  the Captain 750gp for the company. The stones and jewels wait in the barracks for next month's selling day; the
  coin went to the Guildhouse.

- Day 363, heavy warm rain. Lucilla's enervation held off (she felt the cold ebb a little each morning). She paid
  Tertulla two more weeks for the barge. Vestus spent the day in the Tower's archive with the Argollëan fragments
  and found a copied homily, "On the Teacher Below": the elves' Great Teacher, beetle-headed, who "slept in a cold
  coffin beneath the Falling Star", whose "watching eggs" on the cases of his wonders "called him from his sleep to
  keep what was his"; his fists broke stone, and he bore "a sword of light" and "a rod that unmakes the mind".
  Rosa still wants the lance. Gideon: "Not until we can fight whatever that is with the door already open behind
  us. Not this week."
- Day 364, misty and still. Brother Oppius at the Citadel gate with a sealed purse for Vestus: 2,000gp for Una.
  "The mother has been told her son is answered."
- Day 365, overcast. A day of rest; by evening the cold had gone out of Lucilla's bones for good, though her
  strength did not come back.

- Day 366, cloudy. Lucilla sounded Liber at the Mink about the disc: "Bring it when the Tower names it. The Family
  has buyers past the mountains." Neris counted torches and oil; Gideon set the next descent for the narrow way
  east of the Cave of the Falling Star, where the hammering is.
- Day 367, drizzle. Forty-ninth descent, eleven strong (Cotta and Saturnin at the Citadel). Down the steep cut
  stair from the Falling Star cave into a 20-foot tunnel walled with resin-skinned niches like a hive, a bronze
  door with a death's-head handle half open in its south wall, and beyond, a great cave full of grey lizard-men:
  a dozen tearing two white-furred apes apart, a dozen chipping stone, more asleep in the niches. Vettius slipped
  on the stair (nobody heard over the hammering). Vestus called out to them in the old elves' tongue; they hissed
  back and reached for their bone darts.
- Vestus' fireball burst among them as they rose: eleven burned. The women and young fled shrieking east. A
  second wave came out of the dark behind a big lizard-man in leather and a scarred one with a painted axe;
  Vestus' second fireball took fourteen more and Phaedra's fan the rest of their nerve; Neris' prayer put the
  scarred one to sleep and Vettius cut his throat; the big one fled. A third band came from the east; two fans of
  flame broke it. Darts found Hrodvar twice and he went down with a dart through the lung; Neris' prayer held him
  in this world. Forty-two lizard-men dead in the tunnel. The company went home with nothing but their lives:
  Gideon would not take a hurt company into the hive. Hrodvar's lung was mended at the Citadel that afternoon
  (Neris paid), but he lies abed two weeks.
- Day 368. Brother Oppius' word came to two of them: for Vestus, Hrad the Blood Axe, a Jutland barbarian who
  killed young Umic in an illicit duel at the Mink (his father asks); for Iulia, Balkar the Krysean, a dealer in
  charms who bewitched a merchant's daughter until she killed herself (her father asks).
- Day 369. Neris healed Brennus and Iulia. Lucilla's contacts at the Mink knew the Jutlander: he drinks there till
  midnight and staggers home along the river stair. Vestus asked at the Tower: they know the Krysean for a
  charlatan who sells fortunes at the baths and lives two streets from the Chapterhouse.
- Night of day 369. On the river stair the Jutlander saw Vettius in the shadows and came at him with his axe; he
  missed, and Phaedra's whispered spell dropped him asleep on the steps. Vestus finished it. The river took him.
- Night of day 370. Vettius opened the Krysean's door and the man never woke; Iulia did the Order's work.
- Day 371. Oppius brought 2,000gp for the Jutlander.
- Day 372. The Nicean wine-seller had a second sealed note for "Vasan": "Morning dew. For the house of Vasan
  Tikala: one commission." Lucilla knows that password from the ledger: it is Liber Faunus. The table argued (Rosa:
  "He's our buyer!"); Gideon called it: the oath stands, the Order is told. Word went to the Temple of Justice. The
  same Watch optio came to the Citadel about the Krysean found dead in his house: Vestus had asked after him at
  the Tower. Iulia told him the man was a charlatan the Tower despised; the optio wrote it down and went away
  uninterested.
