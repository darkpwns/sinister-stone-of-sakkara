# Session S54 — The Night at the Silk Pony

## Recap
Day 829, evening, the Silk Pony, Cyfaraun. Thana has said yes. The Gilded Ram's men, Mordax's gangs and the forty
Jutlanders come at the Pony the night after next. The legion's gate from the Ford to the city is somewhere to be found.

## Council (day 829)
- **Gideon (Cotta):** call: we don't go out to them; they come to us. Bassus' crossbows on the roof, Vestus and Phaedra upstairs with fire, Brennus and Lucilla behind the yard gate, Cotta and Ennia on the stable roof over the lane. Vettius takes Mordax. Neris blesses us all first.
- **Kwame (Brennus):** and after, a wedding.
- **Tomasz (Vettius):** Mordax comes by the lane. He's mine.
- **Ingrid (Neris):** fatigued: a full day's rest first.
- **Rosa (Lucilla):** the Ram's houses at the North Gate are ours after.
- **Priya (Vestus):** the street is straight: a fire down the middle of them.

## Where they went
- Day 830. Rest at the Pony.
- Night of day 831, the second hour. They came: forty Jutlanders up Gambler's Row under the raven banner, Mordax and his toughs and the Ram's bravos by the back lane. Vettius stepped out of the shadows behind Mordax and cut him down. Vestus' fire burst among the axemen at the gate, Phaedra's lightning ran the length of the street, and the second round did it again; Sigvald fell in the fire. The rest fought on to the last man; the toughs broke in the lane. One of Bassus' knives died on the roof; Cotta took a bravo's arrow. The Watch came at dawn, heard Lucilla and the baron, and called it a lawful defence; they took the bodies and a wounded bravo, who named the Gilded Ram's owner. He fled the city that morning.
- Day 832. The Ram's men at the North Gate dice-houses gave way to Bassus' knives without a fight; the houses are the Family's. The street is the boss's again. Lucilla hired 200 laborers and 25 masons for the fort.
- Day 833. **Vettius and Thana were married before Calefa in the Grey Sisters' house, Neris saying the words**; a feast for the Family and half the Festival District.
- Day 834. Lucilla and the baron called on Regent Aurëus: the Tower keeps its books.
- Night of 840-841. Vettius climbed into the Tower's scriptorium and came away with an archivist's fresh copy of the legion survey: the city's end of the gate lies in the buried legion camp under the Old District's old baths.
- Day 841. Selling day through the Family: the three cut stones, the medusae's parts, the sisters' gems and jewellery, the dragon's blood (about 17,000).
- Days 841-850. South with the labour column. The bandit camp by the road came out at us, two bands with bows: fire and lightning again, and they ran. Giant spiders took three laborers from the edge of camp in the night. A washed-out bridge, a patrol of imperial horse, sinkholes under the road (Neris and her mule fell thirty feet; the mule died), a rat pack. Day 850, the fort: Lucan Vesper had found the arch in the strongroom's west wall and written to the Regent; Hesk's masons opened it (day 851): a black arch of legion stone with the eagle cut over it.
- Days 852-857. North-east for the cache: lost twice (the road again), seven dead donkeys, a spring where unicorns drink by the river (27.18), a broken wizard's tower (27.18), deer.
- Day 857. The Ruined Monastery: four gnolls on the gatehouse (sleep, arrows, axes); a spiked pit on the crypt stair (Vettius, 18 hp); the bell rang. The cult of the Worm Below came out of their hall and burned; the gnolls came down the passage and burned; the hierophant died to Ennia's arrow; the rest of the gnolls ran. A silver cup and trinkets in the gnoll chief's chest, a fortune in the hierophant's strongbox (a needle trap, found). Eight skeletons in the ossuary; behind them a secret door sealed with lead and the legion's eagle: a dead priest in white, two thousand electrum pieces, a whip, and **three bronze tesserae of passage**. Vettius read the watchword.
- Days 857-859. Home: a draugr on a hilltop at dusk watched us pass; rats again; hobgoblins who were glad the gnolls are dead; a hill with a view over all the barony and a spring beside it (26.19).

## Who they met
- The Watch's night captain of the Festival District: friendly to the baron.
- Regent Aurëus of the Tower: courteous and cold.
- Hesk again: 'Legion stone under legion stone. They meant this to be found.'

## Changes to the world
- Mordax, Sigvald and the Jutlanders dead; the Gilded Ram's owner outlawed and fled; the North Gate dice-houses are the Family's.
- Vettius and Thana are married.
- The fort's arch is open; the company holds the three tesserae.
- The cult of the Worm Below and its gnolls are broken; the monastery is empty but for its pit.
- Twenty-one families in the ford meadows; 197 more laborers and 25 masons on the walls (phase 1 about day 880).
