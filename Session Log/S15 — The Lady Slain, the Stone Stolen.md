# Session S15 — The Lady Slain, the Stone Stolen

## Recap
From the Black Fangs' cavern the company went back to the squeeze. Marius was seen again; the Lady called out instead of sending her dead, and Hrodric talked with her: she wanted three living souls from the surface and Ur-Shet bound as proof of service. The company waited in the dark until her dead had gone back to their posts, then Hrodric went back "to talk" with Faustina behind him. The Lady read the trap and reached for Faustina with a choking spell, but Faustina shook it off and held her fast with a prayer; Hrodric killed her where she stood. Her black plate, great axe, ring and symbol were taken, two prisoners cut from the altar, and her chest hacked open. The noise drew eight scaly, colour-shifting troglodytes ("the stinking ones"): they wanted only the Stone. Ulmash, strangely, stood guard over it; in the fight three troglodytes died, but one wrenched the Stone from the altar and ran south-east into the dark, and the rest followed. Ulmash chased it and came back without it. Home with the prisoners; paid; levels for Hrodric, Severina and Varro; the legate paid 500gp for the Lady's death. Out again on day 137 through heat and mud, camped in 21.20 on the night of day 138.

## Who they met
- The Lady (Zakiti), in her Inner Fane: slain.
- Troglodytes, eight (three slain; five fled with the Stone).
- Ur-Shet and the Black Fangs (Friendly; the Lady's coin halved with them). Ulmash (stood over the Stone; came back without it, sullen).
- Teukros Dimas, a grey freeholder from a farm by the fort (hoarse, two fingers missing, prays to Mityara) and Ianthe, a potter's girl of Türos Tem (silent, fierce), freed from the altar and brought home.
- Legate Ulrand Valerian (Friendly and helpful: 500gp, a writ for oil and arms from the fort's stores). Priestess Genelen (lore of the Stone). Annalist Socolo (read the Lady's letter).
- A troop of the legion's light horse on the farm tracks at night (wary, then let the company be).

## Rumours given
- Ur-Shet: the stinking ones come up from cracks in the deep south caves, beyond the ghoul hole and the slime caves.
- Genelen: the Stone is a shard of the Tablet of Destiny; broken, its pieces are holy to Mityara's order; its corruption seeks the hand that breaks it.
- The Lady's letter (read by Socolo): to a "Lord of Secrets", offering herself and the Stone's power to his "dark mission".

## Changes to the world
- The Lady Below is dead. Her dead stand at their posts without a mistress.
- The Stone is gone below with the troglodytes.
- The legate holds the rest of his reward until the raids stop.
