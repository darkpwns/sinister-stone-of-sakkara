# Session S04 — The Forest Will Not Let Go

## Recap
From the marsh edge (20.16) the company turned south for home with four days of food. On day 16, an hour into
21.18, a second bone-spiked giant wolf caught them; four of them were caught flat-footed. Its first bite threw
Eskil down at −3; Octavia's Slumber failed against it; Aurelia ran to Eskil and her cure brought on his roll:
grievously wounded, his gut torn. Her laying on hands the next round held him at the edge (2 hp, two weeks' bed
rest; CON 13 → 9, −4 to reactions). The wolf then dropped Gaius Rufo (0 hp) and bit Lepidus before the spears and
Lepidus' sword brought it to 2 hp and it fled into the trees. Aurelia's Healing saved Gaius from bleeding out, but
the wolf took his foot; her prayer at dawn on day 17 closed his wounds (a week's bed rest). Three giant frogs came to
the camp on the night of day 16; Silvanus saw them first and led the camp away. With two bed-ridden men the company
walked at half pace; the forest turned them twice more, and on day 21 they reached a stand of trees
where Türos Tem should have been, and knew themselves lost. The food ran out on day 18; foraging found nothing. At
dawn of day 22 all nine are hungry and underfed, wet and fatigued, somewhere in the southern Viaspen.
