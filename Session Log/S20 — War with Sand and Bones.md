# Session S20 — War with Sand and Bones

## Recap
Day 393, evening, Cyfaraun, the Solar Citadel. Thirteen, all whole. The lanista Maximus is dead and his storeroom
of human flesh exposed; men in the Festival District say Sand & Bones is hiring gladiators. Lucilla heads the
Argollëan Family. The Order has not yet named Selene.

## Where they went
- Night of day 393. Lucilla sent Cotta to The Mink with a word for Aranth: men at every Family door tonight, and
  Hesta's girls on the Silk Pony and the new gladiators. The company slept at the Citadel.
- Day 394. Iulia told the Patriarch the lanista's end and what followed: his beastmen in the arena's cells, Sand &
  Bones hiring swords. Arëtar: the beasts are the Prefect's to dispose of; "the woman who shared his bed and his
  board, if she shared his table, is yours as he was. Bring me proof." At the hideout under The Mink, Hesta's girls
  had little: eight gladiators hired from Pluris and Nuvicus, lodged at the Silk Pony; Selene not seen at her tables
  since the lanista died. The Family keeps its doors watched; the table waits for the Order's day (396). Neris asked
  what would lift Lutarius' pallor: a gods' mark, as lasting as a Tampering with Mortality; only a miracle or a wish.
  In the Citadel yard Vettius worked the lanista's sword (+1), Marcellus his spear (+1), Cotta the humming sling
  bullets (+1); Vestus sipped the three potions: monstrous form, energy invulnerability, comprehension. Vestus began a
  week's study to set Invisibility in his repertoire, Phaedra Beguile Humanoid (to day 400).
- Day 395. Lucilla tried Liber's ledger word on his magic-locked strongbox at the hideout: it stayed shut; she
  left it to the Family. That evening Vettius took a corner table in the Silk Pony's common room, a cup and a few
  small bets: eight footpads on the floor, two hoods, a dozen-odd gamblers, one big scarred gladiator drinking with
  a hood. Vettius heard them: "Not yet. Jonus wants the Captain's lot counted first: where they sleep, where they
  drink. And the Mink's new lady." "The one with the eye?" The hood laughed. "The boss wants that one for herself."
  Selene knows who heads the Family. Gideon's call at the Citadel that night: nobody walks alone in the Festival
  District, Vettius counts the counters, the Order's day is tomorrow.
- Day 396. The Order named no one this week. Vettius watched the Citadel steps for Jonus' counters and saw none.
  Iulia took Brennus, Neris, Varro and Lucilla to the Amphitheater cells, where the Prefect's guard holds the
  lanista's twelve beastmen; the optio hummed over the badge until Lucilla's ten gold settled it. The beastmen
  only stared at the Captain who had freed them. Lucilla lifted the lid of her third eye on the larger bugbear and
  saw what it remembered: a sunken pit of black sand under a gambling house, lamplight, a laughing woman with hair
  to her waist, and the master carrying a dead man through a wall behind the barrels. A blood pit under the Silk
  Pony; Selene's; and a hidden room where the dead went. Gideon's call: Arëtar wants proof, so the proof is that
  wall. Vestus' invisibility is ready on day 400; until then the company watches and waits.
- Day 397. Ingrid would not take the hive without the mages' fire, and the mages are bound to their books until
  day 400: the hive waits. Lucilla set Hesta's girls on Thana Nyssand, the Pony's bookkeeper (the soft one, Gideon
  says); they found no quiet hour yet. The Family's men at the Emporium found something else: the cheerful Krysean
  with the sausage cart by The Mink's corner, who watches who comes and goes and walks to the Festival District
  each night. Kwame wanted him asked; Tomasz wanted him used. Rosa's call: leave him be and let him count what we
  want counted. From tomorrow Lucilla leaves The Mink at dusk with only Aelia and Saturnin beside her, the same
  street each night, and the company walks the rooftops and the alleys behind her.
- Day 398. Hesta's girls still had no quiet hour for Thana. At dusk Lucilla walked home from The Mink with Aelia
  and Saturnin; the sausage cart was packed up before she reached the corner. Vettius on the roofs, Brennus,
  Varro, Marcellus, Hrodvar, Neris and Cotta in the alleys behind. Nothing came.
- Day 399. Vettius paid Phaedra's month. Hesta's girls still had no hour for Thana. At dusk Lucilla left The Mink
  again with Aelia and Saturnin. Halfway down the lane eight gladiators in arena harness burst from an alley mouth
  ahead, and from a doorway behind came a lean man in black with two maces and two burly axemen. Lucilla never saw
  the first blow: an axe across her back, then the maces, and she went down in the gutter. Aelia turned on the man
  in black; Saturnin met a two-bladed gladiator with his spear. Neris, thirty feet back, said her prayer of sleep
  over the alley mouth and three gladiators dropped where they stood. Vettius came down off the roof onto the man
  in black's back; Cotta's arrow took a heavy-armoured gladiator. Then Varro reached them: the netman stooping to
  lift Lucilla, an axeman, the spearman, the shieldman and the man in black fell to one sword in one breath, and
  Vettius finished the last two. Neris got to Lucilla within the minute: alive, in shock, her face broken (nose,
  cheek and ear). Her prayer brought her round.
- The Old District watch came running and went away again: Watchtower III is the Family's. Seven gladiators and the
  two axemen were dead; the man in black lived, three fingers gone; three gladiators still slept. Under Brennus'
  stare the man in black talked: Selene sent him; she wants "the woman with the eye" alive, for the pit at the next
  Blood Moon; she has not come up into the Pony since the lanista died, she keeps to the rooms below; Jonus called
  the job a fool's errand and would not lead it. The gladiators knew only that a man from the Pony paid them 150
  each to fetch "a debtor". The prisoners went to the cellars under The Mink; Lucilla was carried to the Citadel
  and put to bed.
- Day 400. Lucilla woke at 10 hp with a face she did not know. Iulia asked the Citadel's priests for the prayer that
  mends a broken face; the priest on duty would not cast it today ("come back, Captain"); Ingrid reminded the table
  that the mending touches the gods' patience (Tampering with Mortality); Lucilla wants it anyway. Lucilla's men at
  the Emporium brought her a sealed note left for "Vasan" at the Nicean wine stalls, signed with the ruby lips: the
  Captain of the Underwatch, before the moon is full. Lucilla told Iulia; Neris carried it to Brother Oppius for
  Destrio (his answer in two days). Rosa's call on the prisoners: the man in black is Hesta's (the Family's
  blood, Iulia knows nothing of it); the three gladiators walked home to Pluris and Nuvicus with Lucilla's
  compliments and word that the Pony sent their brothers to die. Pluris swore he would rent the Pony nothing more
  until it paid for his dead; Nuvicus said nothing. Vestus and Phaedra closed their books: Invisibility is in
  Vestus' repertoire, Beguile Humanoid in Phaedra's.
- Day 401. The Citadel's priest again asked the Captain to come back another day for Lucilla's face. That night
  Vestus made Vettius vanish. Into the Silk Pony with the gamblers, down the cellar stair past the footpad (he
  heard boots that were not there and shrugged), among the snoring cooks; the cellar door's lock beat him, but a
  thug came down for wine and Vettius slipped through behind him. Below: a long lamplit room, curtained booths, a
  five-foot pit of black-stained sand, buckets and barrels in the south-west corner; eleven men at dice past two in
  the morning, hoods and axemen, a man with a branding iron, a quick-handed rogue and two big arena veterans. No
  Selene. Vettius ran his fingers over the wall behind the barrels and found no seam before the room grew restless.
  He let himself out by the door (a hood swore at the draught and shut it) and was back at the Citadel by dawn.
- Day 402. Rosa doubled down: half again the fee, and the Citadel's priest laid hands on Lucilla's face under the
  oculus. The face came back; the gods took their price. She remembers little of it, she must lie abed two weeks
  (to day 416), and her right arm is not always her own. Brother Oppius brought Destrio's word: the Order will name
  the woman of the ruby lips. Hesta's girls found Thana's hour (she walks home from the Pony at dawn with one
  footpad); Lucilla dictated a letter from her bed: when the Pony's mistress falls, its honest trade is Thana's to
  run under the Family's roof, no pit, no flesh. Thana sent back one line: "Tomorrow, at dawn. Alone, or not at all."
- Day 403, before dawn. Lucilla went in a curtained chair to the portico of the old baths on Silk Street, Vettius
  in the shadows across the street, the bearers sent off. Thana Nyssand came alone: tired eyes, ink on her fingers,
  a merchant's courtesy. "Profit is profit. The rest is just risk with blood on it, and I am tired of the blood."
  Her terms: the Pony's honest trade (the stalls, the cantinas, the houses) hers to run under the Family; Jonus
  lives. What she gave: Selene sleeps below behind the wall since the lanista died and comes into the long room in
  the evenings; Jonus is sullen but still at her side; on the night before the full moon the pit opens as always,
  and Selene has promised the crowd "the Mink's lady". Only Selene and the dead lanista worked the wall behind the
  barrels. And a key: the cellar door's, pressed into Lucilla's hand. "Tomorrow night I pay the house at the bar.
  Every man below comes up for his silver; for a quarter of an hour the long room is near empty." The Order named no
  one this week.
- Night of day 404. Vestus made Vettius vanish again; Thana's key turned in the cellar lock (a cook on a cot sat up
  at a scuff, saw nothing, lay down). Behind the door Vettius waited while the house went up for its silver; two
  men stayed below, dicing in a booth. He felt along the wall behind the barrels for a quarter of an hour and found
  nothing. Then the wall found him: the iron bucket-hook in the corner turned, the wall behind the barrels swung
  inward on a pivot, a breath of old rot came out of the dark, and Selene Nictorian walked into the lamplight in
  black silk with a big scarred woman behind her carrying a morning star. "Seven of Pluris' boys in the gutter and
  Corvinus not home. Jonus was right, and I do hate it when Jonus is right." She drank, counted the house's silver,
  and an hour after midnight went back the way she came: the hook, the wall, the dark. Vettius went out with the
  dice-players at dawn.
- Day 405. The company slept off the night's work, then made ready for the hive: iron rations for Marcellus and
  Cotta, a pack of torches, waterskins filled at the Citadel well. Lucilla paid Tertulla for two more weeks at her
  landing (to day 419). At the Prefect's sale of the lanista's goods, House Pluris bought the orcs, hobgoblins and
  gnolls and House Nuvicus the bugbears and the ogre.
- Day 406. Varro's and Hrodvar's month paid. While the company was below, Aelia and Saturnin, carrying Lucilla's
  word to The Mink with two of the Family's bravos, were jumped in a lane by a wiry man with two hatchets and four
  hooded toughs. One bravo fell; Saturnin's spear and Aelia's blade and the other bravo's morning star left all
  five Pony men in the mud. The Family carried its dead man home.
- Day 406, the fiftieth descent, ten strong (Lucilla abed; Aelia and Saturnin with her). Down by the Citadel's
  catacombs and the old ways to the Falling Star stair, into the lizard-men's tunnel, quiet now, and through the
  great cave where the dead white apes have dried to leather. Vettius went ahead down the tunnel toward the hammering
  and looked into the chieftain's cave: a female at her knapping saw him and shrieked. He ran; the company held the
  tunnel. The big chieftain came on with three scarred champions and a dozen warriors throwing barbed darts. Neris'
  blessing; Vestus' fireball took seven of them, the chieftain scorched; Neris put two to sleep; Iulia's spear met
  the first charger; the champions' axes found Varro and Brennus and the chieftain's found Hrodvar (down to his last
  breath); the reek of them turned Brennus', Varro's and Marcellus' stomachs. Vestus' sleep took a champion and
  Vettius cut his throat; Iulia's spear finished the chieftain; the warriors broke and cowered and were cut down,
  the sleepers slain where they lay. In the chieftain's cave the females hissed from their niches and did not
  come out. Vettius found the hoard in a sealed grave after two turns of breaking resin: gold, electrum, bone
  figurines, bronze combs and rings, copper goblets, circlets and necklaces.
- In the decorated cave an old, pale lizard-woman put down her chisel and pointed at her carvings, the newest of
  them ten thin figures heavy with weapons. Vestus followed her old elvish well enough: peace for what is left of
  her people. The company swore to leave the females and the eggs be and left her a week's meat. She took both and
  told them nothing more; "when the eggs have hatched and the oath is kept", perhaps. A great python slid past in
  the dark on the way back and did not care for them. Phaedra's torches ran out on the long climb and Hrodvar's
  carried them home. The Citadel's gate near midnight.
- Day 407. The company rested at the Citadel and Neris saw to the hurt. The lizard-men's trinkets went through
  the Family at their worth (255gp). Aranth sent word to Lucilla's bedside: a Family man had sold the Watch at
  Watchtower III the news that the Captain's companion heads the Family; the tower is the Family's, and the word
  went no further than Aranth's desk. One of the oath-takers is a traitor; nobody yet knows which.
