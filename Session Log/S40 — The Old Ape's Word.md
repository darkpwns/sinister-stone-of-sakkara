# Session S40 — The Old Ape's Word

## Recap
Day 581, morning, the Solar Citadel. Nine in the company, Phaedra abed to day 592. The white apes' leader is dead and
Nargund's vault mostly emptied; the Imperial Vanguard went down after the apes on day 580.

## Where they went
- Day 581. The wolf-butted spear to the Tower to be named (two weeks). Word that the Imperial Vanguard came up from
  the apes' caves with Nikolaus on a litter and the lion limping.
- Day 582. The Family's week: two more spies; the Bridge Gate tower asks the Fish Market taverns after its missing
  tesserarius.
- Day 583, descent 83. A clear quivering mass in the ant caves burned by Vestus. At the hunting god's statue the old
  grey ape, the band's leader again, came out to look; Neris spoke to him in the beasts' tongue: his tribe's "magic
  rock" carried hunters to "an endless cavern without walls or ceiling", somewhere in the caves of the ants. The
  company left his young alone and carried out the dragon and wyvern horns.

  spell-seller who charmed a girl into the lake. Tiberius paid as a curate now.
