# Session S36 — The Beetle Stair

## Recap
Day 555, dawn, the Solar Citadel. Ten at full strength and provisioned. Caogan's answers (day 547) put the sleeping
Teacher behind the coiled serpents of the vestibule, down the beetle stair, in cold glass: "Strike while he still
dreams, or kneel." The reliquary's three glass cases wait for whoever dares wake him.

## Where they went
- Day 555, descent 78. By the sewer caves and the galleries to the vestibule; Vettius disarmed the blade behind the
  coiled serpents; down the beetle stair; the carved beetle-man turned on its pivot. Neris blessed the company and
  warded Brennus and Tiberius against death. In the shaft below stood a metal canister with a frosted window and a
  sleeper inside. Brennus, Hrodvar and Tiberius hacked at it for three rounds; it opened on its own. The Teacher stepped
  out, tall and black under a beetle's carapace, and raised a small device: Brennus and Tiberius, then Aelia, went
  rigid and lost the minutes around it. Phaedra's fire, Lucilla's mace and Vestus' missile hurt him; Neris' word and
  Vestus' charm did not take. He folded space and was gone. Neris' cures freed the three.
- Then the reliquary: Lucilla's third eye opened the three glass cases (the grey eggs pulsed). Five coloured orbs, a
  lance with a mouth and a grip, a bladeless hilt. The canister came out on three backs and went to the Family's fence.
- Days 556-562. The lance and the hilt to the Tower (day 570). Two Order errands: Lucilla's on a Watch optio (slept by
  Phaedra on his way home before dawn, Lucilla's mace, the river); Brennus' fell on a man of the Family, so Vettius
  paid the blood-price and sent him away with a purse.

## Who they met
- The Great Teacher: a tall, jet-black figure under a beetle's carapace, a device that freezes minds; he spoke into
  their heads before he left. He knows them now.
- Optio Rufus Calenus of the Watch ("The report says river rats."). Dead.

## Treasure and XP

- A129 (Calenus): 1,550 XP over 3.5 shares.

- The reliquary is empty.
