# Session S11 — The Arch Above the River

## Recap
The company brought the merchants home: Karanos paid and turned friendly. Two 0th-level henchmen went home and two of
the rescued adventurers, a fighter and an elven spellsword, took their places. On the way in, an old standing-stone
arch above the river showed, at sunset, the Shrine of the White Lady and its unicorn. A wet week kept the company in
town; it prepared the pilgrimage.

## Where they went
- Day 74: through the fort's fields (the arch on the knoll); Türos Tem by evening. The merchants to Karanos (500gp; Friendly); the brothers home with them. Payout of the prisoners' adventure. Petra and Titus released with a month's pay; Quintus signed with Galerius, Zaira with Callista; Vestus thanked and gone. The Brethren's old inn bill paid (the innkeeper Friendly: "the raids have grown worse every year since the earthquake"). Karanos' shipping contracts declined (they pay too little).
- Day 75: Corvus and Callista at the arch at sunset: the pool, the white shrine, the white horned beast.
- Days 76-80: rain; downtime.
