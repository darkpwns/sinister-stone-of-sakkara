# Session S05 — The Black Boar

## Recap
Starving in the southern Viaspen on day 22, the company hunted: Silvanus' hound and four hunters brought in game for
three days. Following the stream they had camped by, Silvanus found the trail south to the ambush site of ten dead
merchants and guards, and knew the way: by the evening of day 25 they were in the farmland of 21.20, a short day from
Türos Tem. At 22:40 a huge black boar with tusks like sickles came out of the dark. Lepidus and Sextus were on watch;
it would not be talked to, and no blade bit it. The company ran. It caught Lepidus first, then Aurelia; when Silvanus
and Sextus went back for Aurelia it gored both. The others fled into the night. At dawn Octavia, Fulvia and Ione came
back: Lepidus had been eaten; Aurelia and Sextus were dead of their wounds; Silvanus still breathed, but with Aurelia
gone no one could close his throat, and he died within the turn. Fulvia took the silver suns from Aurelia's body. The
five survivors (Octavia, Fulvia, Eskil and Gaius Rufo carried on the march, and Ione) walked into Türos Tem on the
afternoon of day 28. Fulvia sold the suns at the forum (480gp); Malyn the Misshapen, who took to Octavia at once,
bought the spider eggs (295gp). Gideon now plays Gaius Rufo. Ingrid and Tomasz have no characters yet.
