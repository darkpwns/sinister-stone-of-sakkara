# Session S47 — Winter Quarters

## Recap
Day 635, dusk, the Silk Pony. The company is back from a march that died in the winter mud. The Legate's barony waits
for spring (day 721); the winter is for horses, mules, provisions and each member's own work. Vestus wants a hunter to
bring him the parts of beasts for his magic.

## Where they went
- Day 636. The company keeps its sixty-four soldiers through the winter, quartered outside the Bridge Gate; Brennus
  drills them. Vestus orders a workshop for his scrolls, fitted in the Silk Pony's back house. Two hunters turned
  down Vestus' offer; the second, Ennia Ursa, a young monster-hunter, took Lucilla's.
- Day 638. Ennia rode east with five of the light horse to hunt the forest beyond the Imperial road for giant spiders.
- Day 645. Vettius learned the Pony's cook had been selling kitchen talk to a Watch informer; given the choice, the
  cook took a purse and left the city.
- Day 650. The workshop is ready. Vestus began his first scroll of Webbing.
- Day 651. Ennia came back with nothing: a column of giant ants and a flock of great bats, too many to take, and
  boars that were only meat. The company sends her out again under Tiberius, with Aelia and thirty light foot.

- Days 651-664. The second hunt, under Tiberius, saw two lynxes and nothing worth a jar. Month 22 closed: the
  Family's take came in, and Vettius made his street lieutenant pay over a side-purse; the man has been seen
  drinking with the Gilded Ram's people since, and will not run the street work in spring.
- Day 664. Vestus finished his first scroll with market herbs and webs in place of spider parts. The working went
  wrong: a fold of grey light, and the workshop was empty. Vestus is gone. Neris asked Calefa where he is and was not
  answered.
- Day 665. The company's council: the hunts end (no one is left to use the parts); the workshop is to be packed
  for the barony; Phaedra takes Vestus' place in the line. Cotta, with Ennia and Brennus, will ride south to look at
  the bugbears' fort across the ford before the thaw.

## Who they met
- Ennia Ursa, monster-hunter (Explorer 1): sharp-eyed, quiet, counts everything twice ("Ants don't sleep, and they don't forget a trail.").
- Silvanus, an older hunter who would not work for a mage.
