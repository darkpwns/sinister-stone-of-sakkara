# Session S11 — Healers and the stare

## Recap
Day 243 dawn, Cyfaraun, the Silk Pony Inn. Everyone came home alive from the seventeenth descent and was cured of the
mummies' rot, but the tombs took their toll: Neris lost an ear, Marcellus two fingers, Brennus a toe. The party means
to find a priest who can make them whole, and to find an answer to the mummies' dreadful stare.

## Where they went
- Day 243 dawn: Vettius paid the stalls for two weeks. At the Keepers' hospital Neris laid hands on Brennus and
  Marcellus, and she and Iulia spent their morning prayers on the wounded; by breakfast only Aelia and Gnaeus were
  still bruised.
- Day 243 morning: Iulia and Neris (both versed in the faith) told the table what can be done: a fourth-rank prayer,
  Repair Disfigurement and Disability, known to Ammonar's and Mityara's clergy, mends one old wound at a time; but
  every such mending tampers with mortality, and the gods may leave their own mark. In a temple, and under sacred
  fire, the odds are best. Neris took the request to her own order. At the Temple of the Hearth Fire Mother Drakima,
  who runs the Keepers' hospital, prayed over Lucilla before the eternal flame (185gp). Lucilla's eye and ear grew
  back whole; and a third eye, lidded, opened in the middle of her forehead. She can open it to touch another mind.
  She must lie abed two weeks; she lies in the hospital courtyard under Mityara's torch, a band over her brow.
- Day 243 late morning: Iulia, with Vestus to read out the inscriptions, asked the Friendly vicar of Ammonar to bring
  the party's news of the Awakening to the Patriarch. Arëtar heard her after noon prayers, grave and unhurried; he
  listened to everything and promised nothing. He wants something he can hold. Iulia asked for a second healing that
  day; the temples sent word that none could be spared (try again tomorrow).
- Day 243 afternoon, round Lucilla's bed: the table worked out a plan against the mummies' stare (party notes).
- Days 244-247, mending: Lucilla, from her pallet, asked the Keepers each morning (twice with a gift equal to the
  fee). Brennus' ear (244) and Marcellus' fingers (245) mended with no rest needed; Marcellus came out of it
  stronger. Neris' ear (246) mended, and she woke from the prayer shaking, with a vision. Brennus' teeth (246) cost
  her two weeks abed and her confidence; her toe (247) came back whole, and Brennus woke a woman.
- Day 244: Vettius bought oil, caltrops and lanterns; Hrodvar built a hauling rig of pulley blocks and slab hooks;
  Phaedra's alchemy named the potion Neris has carried since the first temple (healing). Vestus spent the week in
  study (Burning Sparks). Lucilla's network showed what magic Cyfaraun had for sale this month: nothing to the
  purpose at two and a quarter times its making.
- Day 247: the Tower returned the bone-handled scroll deciphered: the mummy wizard's spellbook. Vestus copied two
  formulas from it (days 251-254). Day 250: Phaedra's wage paid.
- Day 255: Vettius took the spellbook to The Mink; Liber Faunus paid 25,400gp in clean coin. The expedition-15 share
  holders were paid (Calpurnia and Titus their halves); everyone banked his coin at the Guildhouse. Iulia rose to
  vicar, Neris to sister, Vestus to magician, and Phaedra, Varro, Hrodvar, Marcellus and Gnaeus each a rank.
- Day 257: Lucilla rose, a band over her brow; Varro's and Hrodvar's wages paid; she went round her old contacts
  (two rumours). Days 255-261: Vestus studied Ogre Strength and Phaedra Illusory Duplicates.
- Day 261 dawn: Brennus rose from her pallet. The company is whole, rested, and stronger than it has ever been.
- Day 261, eighteenth descent: down the far grate at seven with a day's water each. An insect swarm drifted at them
  in the southern galleries by the crossroads; the company stepped through the east cedar door and shut it. In the
  annex below the great hall Hrodvar set his rig on the south slab and set a lantern beside it; Neris' augury said
  good. With Neris' blessing on all, Iulia's holy light on Brennus' axe and Vestus' ogre strength in Varro's arms,
  the haulers pulled from forty feet: the slab rolled at the first heave. In the black-marble tomb nothing stirred
  yet. Aelia sang; Iulia laid her wrath on Gnaeus; Vettius ran to the doorway and poured a burning pool across it.
  The bronze coffin's lid lifted and a wrapped figure rose: seven of the company froze at the sight of it. Two
  mummified black cats sprang through the fire at Vettius and clawed him. Iulia's holy symbol sent the cats
  cringing; Vettius cut one down and Phaedra's fan of flame burned the other. Brennus, raging, charged through the
  fire and struck the mummy before its spell left its lips; Gnaeus came through the flames beside her. Its blows
  missed Gnaeus twice; in the fifth round Brennus and Gnaeus hacked it apart. In the tomb: a bronze far-seeing
  glass, a cracked black crystal sphere, canopic jars, a scroll jar with a spellbook in gold ink, rolls of star
  charts and a map of the old elven kingdom. Vestus and Aelia could not read the charts' meaning. Giant rats in the
  curving corridor gave way to drawn steel. Out by noon.
- Day 261 afternoon: Vettius cured of the rot at the temples. Lucilla named the sphere a crystal ball (it shows
  far places, three times a day); Vestus read the spellbook. Liber Faunus took the far-seeing glass and the jars
  (1,420gp). The crystal ball, the charts, the map and the spellbook are kept for now.
- Day 262: Ingrid pointed out that a magic item used even once earns nothing when sold; the table sold the crystal
  ball to Liber unused (19,200gp). Vestus began copying from the astrologer's spellbook.
- Day 263, nineteenth descent, to the bard's tomb off the west balcony. An ochre jelly slid up the ramp from below
  while Hrodvar set the rig; it burned under thrown oil and the fans of Vestus and Phaedra. The slab took six heaves.
  Inside: nothing but dust and cobwebs. Tomasz did not believe it; Iulia called on Ammonar to show her evil, and saw a
  crimson shape slip out of the tomb along the wall, by the lantern. Vettius' flask splashed it and for a moment a
  wrapped figure stood outlined in fire; nearly everyone stood the sight of it. Brennus, Marcellus and Gnaeus fought
  it half blind; it smashed Gnaeus to his last breath and rotted him. Phaedra's fan scorched it, Iulia's rebuke left
  it cringing, and Brennus and Marcellus cut it down. In the tomb (the illusion gone): a gold lyre, perfume, a cedar
  pan-pipe, a book of dirges, the bard's spellbook in blue ink, and thousands of spilled coins. Out by half past
  eleven; Gnaeus cured of the rot at the temples. Liber took the relics (14,440gp); the coin went into the strongbox.
  Vettius became a scoundrel (6th rank), Aelia an annalist (4th).
- Day 264: over breakfast Rosa did the sum: more than sixteen thousand in old gold sat in an unguarded box in
  Selene Nictorian's inn. Nobody slept easy after that. Hiring guards meant strangers in Sand & Bones' house; the
  Guildhouse meant the tax men. Iulia and Neris remembered where Septima's coin went: the Grey Sisters keep
  testators' chests in a vault under the Shrine of the Ancestors, behind an iron door with one key. They loaded the
  coin into an ironbound chest on the cart and drove it to the House of Mourning with every blade beside it.
  Revered Mother Doarë heard them out, cool at first; Neris told her the vision of the sleeper below the black
  river, and the old oracle's face changed. "I have dreamed of the river," she said. The chest went down into the
  vault, unopened, ten gold a month. The opals went in with it. Vettius left his spare gear at the inn.
- Day 265, twentieth descent, to the facing tombs in the curving corridor. Something translucent stirred in the
  entrance caves and lost them at the shaft (they never knew). At the facing slabs the haulers pulled at the west
  one four times without luck; then a shadow came silently out of the side passage. Everyone was caught flat-footed:
  its touch drew something out of Aelia, then Vettius, that did not come back. Lucilla's mace and Gnaeus' axe
  unmade it. The company waited a turn in silence and pulled again: the slab rolled. Six vipers poured out; the fans
  of Vestus and Phaedra, Vettius' fire, Brennus, Varro, Gnaeus and Marcellus killed them all without a bite. Then the
  princess came to her door: half the company froze; her slam rotted Brennus; Iulia's rebuke failed. Brennus' axe
  and Marcellus' sword drove her back, and she drew up the hood of her rose-pink cloak and flew off up the corridor
  as a swan. Her tomb held a black cobra statuette, an opal cameo, silver bangles and a belly chain, perfume, silver
  plates and spilled gold. Out by ten; Brennus cured; Liber took the relics (3,364gp); the gold went to Doarë's
  vault. Brennus became an exemplar (5th rank) and the gods' slow mark on her lifted.
- Days 265-267: Neris laid Salving Rest on Vettius and Aelia and they lay under the Citadel's oculus; each passed two
  mornings in a row and the withering stopped. What it took stays taken (Vettius 19 hp at most, Aelia 11).
- Day 267, twenty-first descent, to the warrior's tomb (east of the facing pair). A rat swarm fled from the torches;
  a grey goo crept up the corridor while Hrodvar rigged the slab and died under Brennus' and Gnaeus' axes. The slab
  rolled at the first pull; eight skeletons were still climbing out of their graves when Brennus reached the door,
  Iulia's holy symbol scattered five, and two fans of fire finished them. The company waited a turn in silence, then
  pulled the inner slab: a warrior-mummy in bronze scale rose from his coffin straight into Iulia's rebuke, and cowered
  against the wall while Brennus, Vettius, Gnaeus and Marcellus (eyes down) cut him apart and Vestus' bolts struck
  him. Not a scratch on anyone. A gold shield rimmed with ivory, a silver bowl and 6,500 in coin came out. The
  princess's coffin lid would not move for Brennus and Varro; they left her for a day with more strength.
  Coming out, a shimmering wall of jelly in the caves burned under Marcellus' and Brennus' flasks and Phaedra's fan.
- Day 267, in town: the table had had enough of the cesspool's stink. Phaedra worked out masks of linen and
  charcoal soaked in vinegar and camphor; a dozen for twelve gold. Liber took the gold shield, the jars and the bowl
  (2,628gp); the old coin went to Doarë's vault. Lucilla became an enterpriser (6th rank), Neris a sister-disciple
  (5th: she can now cure the mummies' rot herself), Varro a hero (4th).

## Who they met
- Revered Mother Doarë of the Grey Sisters: 59, a bard's voice, a face overcast with great sadness; speaks of things to come in the past tense ("I have buried you already, child, in three dreams of four"). Friendly; keeps the party's chest.
- Mother Drakima, priestess of Mityara (mother, 7th rank), who runs the Keepers' hospital: square-handed, grey
  braid pinned up, smells of vinegar and lye; brisk, practical, prays as if giving orders ("Lie still. The goddess
  does the work; you do the lying still.").
- Patriarch Arëtar of the Solar Citadel: tall, aquiline, 35, purple and gold; grave and unhurried, listening for
  something behind the words; asked what they had seen before he asked what they wanted. Neutral: wants proof he
  can hold.
