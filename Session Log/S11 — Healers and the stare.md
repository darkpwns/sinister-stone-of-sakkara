# Session S11 — Healers and the stare

## Recap
Day 243 dawn, Cyfaraun, the Silk Pony Inn. Everyone came home alive from the seventeenth descent and was cured of the
mummies' rot, but the tombs took their toll: Neris lost an ear, Marcellus two fingers, Brennus a toe. The party means
to find a priest who can make them whole, and to find an answer to the mummies' dreadful stare.

## Where they went
- Day 243 dawn: Vettius paid the stalls for two weeks. At the Keepers' hospital Neris laid hands on Brennus and
  Marcellus, and she and Iulia spent their morning prayers on the wounded; by breakfast only Aelia and Gnaeus were
  still bruised.
- Day 243 morning: Iulia and Neris (both versed in the faith) told the table what can be done: a fourth-rank prayer,
  Repair Disfigurement and Disability, known to Ammonar's and Mityara's clergy, mends one old wound at a time; but
  every such mending tampers with mortality, and the gods may leave their own mark. In a temple, and under sacred
  fire, the odds are best. Neris took the request to her own order. At the Temple of the Hearth Fire Mother Drakima,
  who runs the Keepers' hospital, prayed over Lucilla before the eternal flame (185gp). Lucilla's eye and ear grew
  back whole; and a third eye, lidded, opened in the middle of her forehead. She can open it to touch another mind.
  She must lie abed two weeks; she lies in the hospital courtyard under Mityara's torch, a band over her brow.
- Day 243 late morning: Iulia, with Vestus to read out the inscriptions, asked the Friendly vicar of Ammonar to bring
  the party's news of the Awakening to the Patriarch. Arëtar heard her after noon prayers, grave and unhurried; he
  listened to everything and promised nothing. He wants something he can hold. Iulia asked for a second healing that
  day; the temples sent word that none could be spared (try again tomorrow).
- Day 243 afternoon, round Lucilla's bed: the table worked out a plan against the mummies' stare (party notes).

## Who they met
- Mother Drakima, priestess of Mityara (mother, 7th rank), who runs the Keepers' hospital: square-handed, grey
  braid pinned up, smells of vinegar and lye; brisk, practical, prays as if giving orders ("Lie still. The goddess
  does the work; you do the lying still.").
- Patriarch Arëtar of the Solar Citadel: tall, aquiline, 35, purple and gold; grave and unhurried, listening for
  something behind the words; asked what they had seen before he asked what they wanted. Neutral: wants proof he
  can hold.
