# Session S16 — The bronze guards

## Recap
Day 309, dawn, Cyfaraun, the Silk Pony. Twelve, all whole or nearly. Neris' charm on the white fish is a week old and
may not hold. The basin's treasure waits in the Grey Sisters' vault for the selling day (331); the black barge under
Tertulla's sails. Above the river, behind a hole lined with black marble, a tomb with four bronze guards.

## Where they went
- Day 309, thirty-fourth descent, for the tomb above the black-marble hole. The table knew the fish's charm was a
  week old: a fresh sheep for bait and Neris with her charm ready. Phaedra's wage paid. In the temple halls two
  shadows came along the wall at the rear: one touched Marcellus (his strength sinks: enervated); Vettius' blade and
  Brennus' axe ended them. Tunnel prawns scuttled from the fire; four giant toads at the beach backed into the water.
- The white fish came up to the half sheep in the shallows. Neris' first charm slid off it; her second took. It is
  the company's friend again.
- Under the hole Vettius climbed and tied a rope. Four black jackals wrapped in a flame that gives no light came
  into the tomb and leapt on him; every bite missed, but the black fire burned him before he slid back down. From the
  canoes Iulia's prayer turned one; the black flames could not reach past her shield. Vestus' and Phaedra's fans,
  Aelia's and Gnaeus' arrows, Vestus' bolts and Marcellus' holy water burned and shot them to nothing at the lip.
- Then eight went up into the tomb close round Iulia. When Gnaeus lifted the gold box from the throne the four
  bronze warriors at the coffin's corners moved, and the gilded coffin opened: a dead king in bronze scale with a
  crown of red and black stones. Iulia could not look at him at first; then she raised the rod, and he cowered. The
  bronze guards beat at the air a hand's breadth from the company and could not touch them, while the pots were
  broken for their coin and Brennus, Vettius, Varro and Gnaeus cut the king down. Hrodvar rigged the throne and the
  chariot's parts down the well into the canoes; the gilded coffin was too heavy to take. The guards were still
  hammering at nothing when the last of them went down the rope.
- Two giant crabs on the way down were turned off. The canoes were drawn up and hidden against the cliff by the crone.
  On the crone stair a sudden gust put out the lantern and every torch at once; Iulia's spear burned on. Out by the
  grate at three, the throne and the chariot on four bearers' backs.
- Neris rose to the sixth circle. Coin banked; the crown, the box and its gems, the jars, the throne and the chariot
  to the Grey Sisters' vault. Oil and holy water bought.

- Day 310, dawn. Marcellus woke a little warmer. Lucilla asked Liber Faunus about a great elven king buried by the
  river bend: "Marcan the Conqueror, who broke the dwarves in the Bitter War. That white stone you sold me was his
  plunder. They buried him with four of his guard, a host of bronze and a sword with a crown for a hilt that made
  men obey. His name opened doors in his day. Bring me his regalia and name your price." The table: the bronze and
  the dead cannot pass Iulia's shield, but a commanding word can; not this week.
- Day 310, thirty-fifth descent, for the gilded coffin. A sweet stink of dying flowers in the temple halls. The fish
  came up for its sheep. On the way upstream a drowned troglodyte came down the river swollen and writhing with
  grave worms; the white fish rose under it and took it whole. Up the well again inside Iulia's ward: the bronze
  guards woke when the coffin was moved and battered at nothing while Hrodvar's rig lowered it into a canoe. Out by
  two with the coffin on four backs; the canoes hidden at the beach again.

- Days 310-311, in town. Lucilla left word at the Emporium's wine stalls that a buyer wanted Vasan Tikala's best
  Nicean, cash, no questions. Nothing came back the first day; on day 311 a boy brought a reply: midnight of day 312,
  at the Nicean outlet where the old sewer runs out to the river below the Emporium wharves. Tomasz: "Bodies keep
  turning up after these meetings." Marcellus' cold left him on the third morning (a little less strength).
