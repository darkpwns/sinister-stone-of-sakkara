# Session S44 — The Hunt for the Teacher

## Recap
Day 609, morning, the Solar Citadel. All nine whole. The company means to hunt the Great Teacher: he knows where they
sleep, his troglodytes waited for them below, the troglodyte shaman was seen with a tall black figure at the old
lizard-men's wharf, and a black figure watches the Citadel's roof by night.

## Where they went
- Day 609. Vettius' fence named the buyer of the Teacher's canister: a Tower steward, for the Regent. Spies on the old
  lizard-men's wharf and the roofs; a dark watch in the bell-turret. Neris' Divination: "Where the old one carves her
  people's story, the master sits among the names of the dead. The names hide a stair: stop it before you knock."
- Day 610. The Family's ears: the Imperial Vanguard tell of troglodytes gathering in the caves south of the great temple.
- Day 611, descent 85. The southern caves: thirty-one troglodytes burned and cut down in two rounds at the big burial
  cave. The tall black figure came out of the dark, froze Tiberius, Cotta and Lucilla with his device, and fell under
  Vestus' charm. He gave Vestus the device; Vestus turned it on him twice and failed; he broke free, took Brennus' axe,
  and vanished. Neris slept the old shaman and broke the master's spell on her; freed, she will lead the company to the
  magic rock when it brings gifts for her tribe. Seven skeletons in the temple. His trail in the old lizard-men's den
  under the Festival District ended at the water.
- Day 612. Vestus warned the Regent of the Tower that the beetle-man wants his box; the Regent was cold.
- Night of 613. The black figure stood on the chapel roof before dawn and saw the watch. Lucilla's third eye touched his
  mind: "under the bones of their priests, where the river cannot reach."
- Day 614, before dawn. Down through the Catacombs of the Patriarchs and out by the west door into a drowned elven
  cellar: the Teacher. Brennus' and Tiberius' blows, his naked face (four cowered), his grip on Neris' throat (she threw
  it off), Phaedra's fan of fire, and Neris' word: "Die!" He fell senseless and Lucilla's mace finished him.

## Who they met
- The Great Teacher ("Little ones. You came all this way to be taught."). Dead.
- The troglodyte shaman, freed ("The master's voice is gone from my head. Bring gifts; my people are few.").
- The Regent Aurëus ("And who, thaumaturge, tells you what the Regent buys?").
