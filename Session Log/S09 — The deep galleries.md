# Session S09 — The deep galleries

## Recap
Day 196 evening, Cyfaraun. Five descents into the caves below the agora cesspool have brought home about 1,300gp of
ancient grave goods. The party (Lucilla, Vettius, Vestus, Neris, Brennus, Iulia; Quirinus, Phaedra, Ottar) rests at
the Silk Pony.

## Where they went
- Day 196 evening: the table set itself a rule: no descent unless the levels of those going down add up to the
  depth's floor (the entrance caves 18; the party stood at 16).
- Day 197: Lucilla's network turned up five first-level hands. Hired: Varro (fighter, Lucilla's), Hrodvar (dwarven
  vaultguard, Iulia's), Septima (explorer, Vettius', after a signing bonus). Kaeso still hesitates. Strength now 19.
  Oil, torches and a week's iron rations for the new hands.
- Day 198, sixth descent: through the far grate to the triangular cave. Twenty heaves on the round stone slab moved
  nothing; Vettius heard many armed things stirring behind it. Out for a crowbar, back by nine; thirty more heaves and
  then, at the forty-third of the visit, the slab rolled. Twelve skeleton warriors with curved bronze swords stood in
  ranks behind it. Iulia's rebuke broke eight of them; Brennus, raging, split two; Vestus' missile, Vettius, Varro
  and Septima's arrows finished the rest at the mouth, and the cowering eight were cut down in the hall beyond. Nobody
  hurt but Brennus' bruise from the slab.
- Day 198, cont.: the west slab rolled at the second heave: a small vault with a gilded cedar shrine, canopic jars and
  vases. The east slab, also at the second heave: eighteen more skeletons climbing from their graves. Iulia's rebuke
  failed; Varro poured burning oil at the mouth and the dead walked into it; Brennus fell, critically hurt, and Neris'
  prayer brought him back (six teeth gone, a week in bed). The antechamber beyond: four terrible gods carved among
  the Empyrean ones (Iulia named Iskara, Bel, Nasga and Dirgion), and a slab whose ceiling block hangs on it. Home by
  noon; the shrine, jars and vases sold for 1,516gp.
- Day 199, seventh descent: Hrodvar propped the hanging block with oak beams; the slab rolled at the first heave: the
  tomb's treasury (silver bowls, pots, a golden plumed fan, an ivory-inlaid chest, frankincense, a bronze pendant, a
  bag of seeds). A swarm of millipedes that had followed the party in burned under three flasks. A grey slick in the
  cesspool was left behind. Sold: 1,705gp. One slab is left, at the north end of the antechamber's east wall.
- Day 199, eighth descent: back by ten with fresh prayers and the stable's oil. The last slab rolled at the third heave
  onto a black burial chamber; a tall wrapped figure in a gold mask was already standing. One look and eight of the
  eleven shrank down, unable to move; it struck Vettius, and a cold sickness crept from the blow. Neris raised her
  holy circle and the dead lord could not cross it; the party ran, Neris last. It followed as far as the first hall.
  In town a priest cured Vettius' sickness for 45gp.
- Days 199-211, Cyfaraun: Vestus could not place the seed bag, and no sage sat at the Tower this month. Quirinus
  trained a day with the hepatizon great axe and Vettius with the black sword: both +1. Lucilla's second week of
  searching brought Aelia (a bard; her eye for enchantments named the black sword a death drinker that feeds its
  wielder when he fells a foe, and the bag as faerie seeds that turn the plants of a place to the planter's side) and
  Marcellus (a fighter). Brennus left his bed on day 205. With the new month: Gnaeus (an explorer of the second rank)
  and Nysa (an assassin). Strength 24.
- Day 211, ninth descent: two turns of searching the first tomb hall found nothing, until the cracked south-east
  corner gave way under Nysa: a shaft into a natural tunnel below. She fell badly; Neris climbed down and her prayer
  brought her round, shaken and seeing the faces of the dead. Home by nine with Nysa carried between two.
- Day 211: strength counted again (torch-bearers count): 25 against the 24 needed below; Vettius let Nysa go.
- Day 211, tenth descent: a red fungus in the triangular cave burned under oil. Down the shaft by rope into a natural
  tunnel; Vettius' sneak south was heard, and four horned beetles came out of a resin-coated side cave: Phaedra put
  three to sleep and they died in their sleep; the fourth fell to Brennus. Fifteen graves in the bean-shaped cave
  (30gp). North, heavy bronze doors opened on a temple of the dead: a font of clear water under an inscription
  ("Cleanse yourself of unbelief if you would Awaken"); nobody washed. A curving corridor of hundreds of wall graves,
  painted with the dark gods (Iulia named Dirgion, Galmorm and Ravanor), and a commoners' tomb with a serpent eating
  its tail on the floor: 266 graves looted, 1,228gp of grave goods. Giant rats fled from Brennus' roar. Two slab doors
  facing each other, worn smooth by many hands, were left alone.
- Day 212, the eleventh descent, planned as a long expedition: torches for days, oil, spikes, caltrops. First the
  masked lord: the slab rolled at the thirteenth heave; Aelia sang them brave while they kept their eyes down; then
  oil and holy water from a distance, and Lucilla's mace and Vettius' sword when it closed. Half the party shrank from
  its gaze at one time or another; it struck Brennus, Vettius and Lucilla (down to her last breath) with its rotting
  blow, and still it burned and broke and fell. Behind them a grey slick no one had seen crept up the hall and took
  Ottar, dissolving his leather and then him; Brennus' axe and Gnaeus' sword finished it. The gold mask came off the
  dead lord's face. The rot sent them home: three cures and a donation at the temples, Lucilla first. Vestus reached
  the second rank and bought a new spell's formula at the Tower. Mask sold for 743gp.
- Days 212-218, Cyfaraun: a week abed for Lucilla and Vettius under Neris' hands; Vestus learnt Fan of Flames. The
  magic weapons went to the fighters: Brennus the hepatizon great axe, Marcellus the black death-drinker, Quirinus the
  battle axe.
- Day 219, the twelfth descent, back to the temple of the dead: the cast-iron scarab-faced figure in the northern
  alcove (Vestus and Iulia named it Khepri) stepped down and fought; Brennus fell in shock before Hrodvar's mace broke
  it. The rest of the great curving corridor, the southern galleries and two commoners' tombs: 324 graves, 1,604gp.
  By the crossroads a grey-robed hooded figure with a bronze hook came out of the dark: its touch withered Quirinus
  for good; Septima's flask burst in her own hand and she burned to death; Vestus' fan of flames, Vettius' sword and
  Lucilla's mace destroyed it. Home with Septima's body and Brennus carried.

## Who they met
- Septima, explorer, Vettius' henchwoman, burned to death by her own flask by the temple crossroads (day 219).
- Ottar, peltmonger and torch-bearer since Cyfaraun, killed and eaten by a grey ooze in the first tomb (day 212).
- Aelia (bard): sharp-tongued, plain-faced, sings under her breath when she is afraid.
- Marcellus (fighter): broad, cheerful, a city watchman's son.
- Gnaeus (explorer): grey at the temples, quiet, chews a stalk of something.
- Nysa (assassin): small, watchful, says little and never sits with her back to a door.
- Varro (fighter): lean, close-cropped, ex-watchman; answers in one word.
- Hrodvar (dwarf vaultguard): squat, grey-braided, slow and precise; hums while he works.
- Septima (explorer): wiry, sun-browned, a hunter's bow; counts paces under her breath.
- Galla and Opimius (venturers), Kaeso (fighter): not hired.
