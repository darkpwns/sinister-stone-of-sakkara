# Session S09 — The deep galleries

## Recap
Day 196 evening, Cyfaraun. Five descents into the caves below the agora cesspool have brought home about 1,300gp of
ancient grave goods. The party (Lucilla, Vettius, Vestus, Neris, Brennus, Iulia; Quirinus, Phaedra, Ottar) rests at
the Silk Pony.

## Where they went
- Day 196 evening: the table set itself a rule: no descent unless the levels of those going down add up to the
  depth's floor (the entrance caves 18; the party stood at 16).
- Day 197: Lucilla's network turned up five first-level hands. Hired: Varro (fighter, Lucilla's), Hrodvar (dwarven
  vaultguard, Iulia's), Septima (explorer, Vettius', after a signing bonus). Kaeso still hesitates. Strength now 19.
  Oil, torches and a week's iron rations for the new hands.
- Day 198, sixth descent: through the far grate to the triangular cave. Twenty heaves on the round stone slab moved
  nothing; Vettius heard many armed things stirring behind it. Out for a crowbar, back by nine; thirty more heaves and
  then, at the forty-third of the visit, the slab rolled. Twelve skeleton warriors with curved bronze swords stood in
  ranks behind it. Iulia's rebuke broke eight of them; Brennus, raging, split two; Vestus' missile, Vettius, Varro
  and Septima's arrows finished the rest at the mouth, and the cowering eight were cut down in the hall beyond. Nobody
  hurt but Brennus' bruise from the slab.

## Who they met
- Varro (fighter): lean, close-cropped, ex-watchman; answers in one word.
- Hrodvar (dwarf vaultguard): squat, grey-braided, slow and precise; hums while he works.
- Septima (explorer): wiry, sun-browned, a hunter's bow; counts paces under her breath.
- Galla and Opimius (venturers), Kaeso (fighter): not hired.
