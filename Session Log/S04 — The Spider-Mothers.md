# Session S04 — The Spider-Mothers

## Recap
A week's expedition with one aim, live beasts: the party went back to the Buried Temple, put the two giant crab
spiders of room 12 to sleep with Callista's Slumber and netted them, took the room's jars and a garnet, and carried the
spiders home on the donkeys. One spider escaped in the rain on the night of day 22; the party lost its way twice
between the forest and the fort; the other spider was sold alive to Malyn's workshop. Payout of A2 (698 XP); the purse
stands at about 2,050gp. The war-dog pups' training paused days 19-25 and resumed.

## Where they went
- Day 19: training paused; a week's rations from the company chest; out by the north-west track. The party strayed south-west (navigation failed) but found its way; a merchant (Neutral); a boggart in the hedges (Unfriendly, kept its distance). Camp.
- Day 20: on course to the temple hex; camp by the spring.
- Day 21: into the temple (07:00). The door kobolds let the party by (Indifferent, as left). Room 11; Corvus and Severina could not make out the ceiling of 12. A hobgoblin patrol of three passed (Neutral). Hrodric prodded the bodies in 12 with his spear: both spiders dropped, both sides were startled; Callista's **Slumber** took both (failed saves); netted and bound. Ten jars of incense and a garnet taken. Four brigand drinkers stared and kept off (Indifferent). Out at 08:19, a spider on each donkey; marched to 20.19, camp.
- Day 22: navigation failed in the rain; the party circled back to the temple hill. That night the larger spider broke its bonds and fled into the forest.
- Day 23: back across the forest; eight giant dragonflies over the wet fields of 21.20 (Unfriendly; they circled and veered off). Camp in 21.20.
- Day 24: lost again in the farmland edge (believed at the fort, truly in the forest's fringe); camp.
- Day 25: fourteen skeletons marching north-west through 22.19 by day (Indifferent; the party could not slip away but they paid it no heed). The fort at 14:00; three bristle-bearded men by the ford watched (Neutral). Sold the spider (140gp), the garnet (250gp) and the jars (250gp); payout; the purse pooled. Dog training resumed.
- Day 26: rest.
