# Session S10 — Debts Paid

## Recap
The new company spent ten days in Türos Tem rebuilding. The legate heard Severus out coolly and gave no scroll for
the Parade Ground; the market had no war dogs and no thieves' tools. On the new month Caelia hired Gnaeus Fossor, a
labourer, and Octavia hired Flavia Scriba, a scribe; Valeria's notices brought only a girl from the inn, who refused.
They took the company chest's kit, bought lamp oil for throwing, and left on day 91.

They forced the march past the boar's farmland, met a band of nine brigands who let them be, rested a day at the
forest edge, and reached the black hill on day 94. Lucan went looking for the sprites' door: in a thicket-choked
gully on the dome's north-west flank Severus found a stone that moves. Behind it a tunnel runs down two hundred feet to
a beach on a crescent of warm red water under a low roof, roots everywhere bleeding. They looked, and came out.

Through the crack, past the kobolds, through the hall and the crossroads without a sound, they reached the hobgoblins
and slept in their common room. In the morning Caelia found the goblin chief's hiding place in the wall of his ruined
room: a bag of gold, two tourmalines and a vial of brown syrup. They walked out unmet, evaded thirty-five goblin
raiders in the farmland and came home on day 96.

On day 98 they went back to settle a debt. Urza took Septimia's promised hundred gold for his dead and gave back the
thieves' tools and the swords; the silver and the coin he kept. In the Great Hall six blood-sucking fliers came out of
the dark; Octavia put three to sleep, but two latched on her and drained her to the floor before the rest were cut
down. Valeria's hands brought her back; she lost an ear. On the way home Caelia stared down a monstrous spider at the
camp, and the company reached Türos Tem on day 104 and rested out the month.
