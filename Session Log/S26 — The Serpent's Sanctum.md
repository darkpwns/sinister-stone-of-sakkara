# Session S26 — The Serpent's Sanctum

## Recap
Day 456, evening, the Solar Citadel. Twelve; Brennus and Marcellus a little hurt. The Family deals in secrets now and
the Order leaves its people alone. No errand of the Order in hand. The Tower has the year's magic until day 465.

## Where they went
- Day 457, the sixtieth descent. Through the great nave and the narthex to the long gallery of graves north of it, two
  stone-shelled scavengers cut down on the way; behind its heavy bronze door, the temple's sanctum: a purple-marble hall
  and against its south wall a colossal statue in ivory and gold, a woman with snakes for hair and a serpent's body, on
  an obsidian pedestal. Neris' prayer showed it burning with magic. They did not go near it. Vestus' fireball and
  Phaedra's fan burst against it from the hall's mouths; it moved, and five blades shattered it before it could do
  anything at all. Under the pedestal, shifted at the first heave: a gold tripod with an ivory censer, a casket of
  twenty engraved amethysts, five great plumes wrapped in lotus leaves, a gold serpent tiara with diamond fangs, an
  amulet with a flawless black sapphire, and thirty-six bars of pure gold; the statue's gold and ivory besides. Out at
  16:42. Phaedra has risen to the sixth rank of her art, Cotta to the fourth of his.
- Day 458. Destrio's word: the merchant's wife has been warned. The Family heard that House Nuvicus is buying a new
  champion off the Arganos road.
- Day 459. The Order's week: nothing.
- Day 460, the sixty-first descent. Phaedra's month paid. A seething carpet of rats burned; another wrapped corpse in
  the galleries, cowed by the Rod and cut apart; then four turns with every hand at the graves of the long galleries,
  three hundred and sixty niches emptied of their rings, pins, cups and coins; five grey grave-things came on the
  company at it and died before they could bite. Out at 16:16.
- Days 461-463, the Citadel. Rest. The Family found the Pony man who had been selling its business since spring and
  Vettius turned him; its ears say the Imperial Vanguard means to go down into the Nethercity within the month. The
  captive priestess of the Serpent has started talking to the knights.
