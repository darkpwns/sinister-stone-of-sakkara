# Session S30 — The Vanguard's Sign

## Recap
Day 487, 21:18, the Solar Citadel. Ten in the company: Iulia abed to day 492 (her hand restored), Brennus mending;
Marcellus and Varro gone. Day 490 Phaedra's wage; day 491 the weekly block; day 494 the Order.

## Where they went
- Days 488-495, the Solar Citadel. Neris' prayers closed Brennus' and Lucilla's wounds. The company restocked: arrows,
  bolts, the market's last two flasks of holy water, a lantern and oil, spikes. Vestus read the two elven books from the
  temple tombs: both are spellbooks. For a week Vestus studied the enchantress' painted plates and Phaedra the consul's
  roll; each came away with a new spell. Vettius' ears told of the Imperial Vanguard: up from the company's own cesspool
  on day 481 with a dead couple's grave goods and a bronze cauldron, a sign nailed in the caves below the grate, and down
  again on day 490 for the great temple. Phaedra's wage was paid. Iulia rose from her bed on day 492 and Neris made her
  whole. The Order sent no word. Phaedra soaked the masks.
- Day 496, the sixty-seventh descent. Down at first light; a crawling swarm in the sewer caves passed wide. Masks on, the
  lantern shut, Iulia's spear lit over the sludge. Past the four-way Hrodvar called the air clean. From the cliff over the
  soldiers' den the company threw torches down: four great soldier ants came for the narrow stair. One reached Iulia;
  Vestus' fire and Phaedra's first thunderbolt killed the other three; the first fled to its queen.
- The queen's cave: Vestus' spell took the queen's mind and she stood still while the company struck; then she fought,
  and fell. Eighteen eggs and the niches' grave goods came away. Vettius found a hidden panel in one of the egg niches.
- Out by the sludge caves; two giant flies were cut down on the way. Up by ten in the morning. The eggs went through the
  Family that afternoon.
