# Session S06 — Silver and Graves

## Recap
A week in Türos Tem. Two newcomers joined the company: **Septimia**, a young priestess of Mityara from Cyfaraun
(Ingrid), and **Aedo**, a broad, handsome explorer from Türos Spen (Tomasz). Octavia asked Malyn the Misshapen what
kind of boar steel does not bite: a *devil boar*, he said with relish, a man-eater that wears the shape of a fat
man by day, and only silver or enchanted weapons will hurt it. At the company's word the legate sent a patrol with
a cart to the farmland of 21.20 by daylight; it brought back Aurelia, Silvanus and what was left of Lepidus with
their gear, but Sextus' body had been stripped (his legion kit gone). On day 30 Septimia said the rites and the
three and Sextus were buried below the fort. Octavia spent the day in Malyn's library and found what Matthius Scale
wrote in *Fallen Empires of the Known World*: Suetonius' legend of a Zaharan temple in the Viaspen that the clerics
of Law buried under a dome of rock; Scale found only "a natural dome of black granite." On day 31 Eskil paid Ione's
second month, and at the Parade Ground the company took on three of the legate's men: **Tullus Brocchus** (heavy
infantry, Octavia's), **Decimus Ruga** (heavy infantry, Eskil's) and **Herennius Bow** (bowman, Fulvia's). Fulvia
bought a silver dagger and three silver arrows for Herennius. Aedo took Silvanus' scale armour from the company chest.
Gaius Rufo rose from his bed on day 35 and was fitted with a wooden peg. Eskil has a week of bed rest still to go.
