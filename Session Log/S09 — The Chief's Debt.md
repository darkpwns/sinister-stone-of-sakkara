# Session S09 — The Chief's Debt

(Replayed at the user's instruction: the first run of S09 is void; see Rulings R2 S09 and `Campaign/archive/S09-void/`.)

## Recap
The table adopted two rules before setting out: combat is a failure state, and dungeon factions carry no lasting
obligation. The company went back to the Buried Temple to make friends, not to fight. It found the hobgoblins
split: the witch doctor Abgal plotting to give his wounded chief to the Lady. Faustina healed the chief, the
warriors rallied to him, and Abgal's faction fled without a blow. The grateful chief paid in coin and silver and
gave up the beetle nest; two Slumbers took four beetles and their eggs alive. Home on day 64, payout, rest to day 66.

## Where they went
- Day 59: Hrodric and Severina stay to train the pups (Petra and Titus with them); Galerius, Faustina, Callista, Corvus, Varro and Marius (lent by Severina) set out with a cask of wine as a gift. The town week's weather showed the mud had dried (a sweltering day 55).
- Days 59-62: the forest; a natural 1 on navigation brought the column out at 21.20 on day 60 (a lost day); giant leeches cowed by Varro at night; heat fatigue on day 61; camp at the spring by the temple on day 62.
- Day 63: the hobgoblin wing (45): wine and talk; Shulgi; the chief healed; the plotters cowed and fled; the chief's reward. A band of orcs that had come to raid the hobgoblins left when it saw the company with them. The beetle nest (50): Slumber twice, four beetles and a clutch of eggs taken. Out by 08:35 (winded: a rest turn missed).
- Day 63, the forest: Antonius' "merchants" with a bloodied wagon: Corvus saw through them; evaded in the rain (lost a while). Seven elves on the farm tracks (Indifferent). Two beetles escaped by day 64.
- Day 64: Türos Tem. Beetles and eggs sold (150gp). Payout. Days 64-66: rest in town; the pups' training continued throughout.

## Who they met
- Shulgi (hobgoblin champion): scarred, a broken tusk, glances over his shoulder, speaks in short bargains.
- Chief Thethamun: proud, slow-healing, grudging gratitude that turned to friendship when pressed.
- Abgal (witch doctor) and Hepteth: seen only as they backed out of the chamber.
- Orc bands (twice); Antonius' brigands (as merchants); seven elves.
