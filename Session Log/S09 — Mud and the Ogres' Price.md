# Session S09 — Mud and the Ogres' Price

## Recap
The company set out for the temple's second level and found every track mud. Fog and a strayed march sent it back to
the fort to restock (day 61); the forest took three more days. Forty-seven goblins passed the spring camp in the night.
Inside, the goblins (now camped in the north hall) were friendly, the purple wyrm took 800gp and let the party down,
and the Red Eye guards waved it on. The ambush on the two giants west of the stair failed: Slumber resisted, Marius
killed, four more felled, Hrodric's eagle dead. Severina bought the company's lives with the purse. Out by 09:00;
the week ends at the spring camp, dawn of day 67.

## Where they went
- Day 59: restocked (rations, oil; no metamphora or holy water left this month); Petra and Titus stay with the pups (training paused). Fog and mud: the day's march never left the fort's fields. Camp.
- Day 60: month's end. Farmers in 21.20 (Unfriendly: off our fields). Mud halves every pace; the forest would take four days each way: Ingrid's food count says turn back. Camp at a freeholder's well.
- Day 61: back to the fort; a second week of iron rations, two metamphora, three flasks of military oil (month 3's market). No war-horse yearling (Galerius).
- Days 62-64: out again: sheep in 21.20; the Viaspen; water from the known spring in 20.19; to the temple hex.
- Night of day 64-65: forty-seven goblins passed the camp (Hrodric hailed them: Indifferent). Day 65: a rest day at the spring.
- Day 66: into the temple. The door kobolds let the party by. The goblins of the night are camped in the north hall: Friendly; they ask help against the hobgoblins who butchered their kin. Three hyena-men collecting tribute in the courtyard (9gp). The purple wyrm awake and gracious: 800gp for eight. Down the long stair (07:00): the hyena-men at their guard room waved the "Lady's new men" on.
- Day 66, the giants' den: both surprised; both shook off Slumber; a six-round fight. Marius killed; Galerius, Faustina, Hrodric, Corvus felled; the eagle killed. Weapons down; Severina offered the purse; the giants took it (846gp). Callista's Healing and Varro's prayer saved Faustina, Hrodric and Corvus; the honey syrup did not save Marius. Out of the temple at 08:50; camp at the spring.

## Who they met
- The goblin band's chief (a kobold-skull helm, missing an ear, talks fast): wants the hobgoblins' blood.
- Idimmu, the purple wyrm: bloated, wheezing a rotten gas, vain and wet-voiced ("a hundred a head, little meat").
- Red Eye gnolls (tribute-collectors; the guards below).
- Two giants (ogres) in their den below: one with a curved sword, one with a broken stalagmite.
