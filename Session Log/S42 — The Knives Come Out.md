# Session S42 — The Knives Come Out

## Recap
Day 595, morning, the Solar Citadel. All nine whole. The Teacher knows where the company sleeps. Vettius has let the
Family's turned betrayers live as double agents since month 16; he means to end that.

## Where they went
- Day 596. Vettius ended the mercy he had shown since month 16. The Family's knives went out by night: the four men who
  had sold the Family's word and been kept on as false tongues went into the river, and a fresh tale planted among
  the wharf men drew out one more the next day, who followed them. Three are still unknown. A Watch officer called at
  the Citadel about the Pony's back ways; the Captain sent him away content.
- Day 597. Tiberius' report to the Patriarch: the doom temple's terror gone; the troglodyte shaman seen with a tall
  black figure at the old wharf.
- Day 598. A messenger of the Order came to the boss: one of the dead, Pullus, had been the Order's own eyes in the
  Family. Destrio named a blood-price of 2,500 gold; Vettius paid it the same hour.
- Day 599. The Order's word for Brennus: an optio of the Traveler's Gate who beat a carter to death for his toll.
- Day 600. Month 20's accounts: the Family's take (less the dead), Lucilla's houses and caravans.
- Day 601. The Wolf-Spear sold through the Family for 31,000 gold.

## Who they met
- Destrio's messenger: grey-cloaked, soft-spoken, never sits. "The Order lends its eyes. It does not give them away."

## Treasure and XP

  sale XP over 7.5 shares.
