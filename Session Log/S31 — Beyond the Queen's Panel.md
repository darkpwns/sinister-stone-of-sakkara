# Session S31 — Beyond the Queen's Panel

## Recap
Day 496, evening, the Solar Citadel. Ten in the company, back from the ant caves: the colony destroyed, the eggs sold.
Iulia 20/40, Hrodvar 17/19. A hidden panel in the queen's egg niches stands shut. Day 498 the weekly block; day 501 the
Order.

## Where they went
- Day 497, the sixty-eighth descent. A pack of giant rats in the sewer caves burned and cut down in one round. Through
  the ant caves to the queen's cave and down through the hidden panel into a plastered hall of the old alchemists: bronze
  doors without keyholes, sealed. In the long hall a bronze door swung open by itself as Vettius passed it, and a
  scorpion-thing with a fan of stingers came out. Its poison killed Vettius and Brennus in the first moments, then
  Iulia, then Lucilla; Neris' and Iulia's prayers against poison brought each of them back. Fire, thunder, Iulia's spear
  and the mages' bolts brought it down; Hrodvar was felled and raised. The chart room's anatomical drawings came away.
- Day 498, the Citadel. The priests' prayers made everyone whole.
- Day 499, the sixty-ninth descent. The lion door from the ant caves into the hall of heroes forced with a crowbar; the
  slab off the mosaic gallery heaved open at the eleventh try. Four four-armed skeletons in bronze helms stood off the
  company's circles of light; Iulia's prayer unmade two, and steel and bolts broke the other two. Hrodvar went down
  under four blades; Neris saved his life, but his belly is ruined and he has left Iulia's service. Sixteen elven
  short swords sold through the Family. Hrodvar was carried out past beetles and creeping fungus.
- Days 500-503, the Citadel. The Order sent no work; Oppius brought word that the Order has entered the name of the
  patrician's steward's spouse in its Codex. The vault fee with the Grey Sisters paid ahead.
