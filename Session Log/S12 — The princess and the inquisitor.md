# Session S12 — The princess and the inquisitor

## Recap
Day 268 dawn, Cyfaraun, the Silk Pony Inn. Everyone whole and rested. In the Great Temple below, the warrior's, bard's
and astrologer's tombs are empty; the princess lies in her open tomb with her coffin shut; the inquisitor's slab by
the crossroads is still sealed, and the stair behind the priest's fresco leads down to running water. Phaedra's masks
are ready for the cesspool. Month 9 ends on day 270; the next selling day is 271.

## Where they went
- Day 268, twenty-second descent, by the far grate. Eight giant rats in the cesspool kept their distance and let the
  column by; a dead baby rabbit floated in the sewage. In the first tomb an insect swarm came up the hallway as the
  company rested; it followed them to the shaft, and Brennus' and Aelia's flasks burned it out of the air.
- The princess's tomb: Vestus' ogre strength on Varro, and the stone lid slid at the first heave with Brennus and
  Gnaeus. She lay still stirring; two of the watchers froze at the sight of her, but before she could rise Brennus'
  axe and Vestus' fan of flames ended her. Her pink swan-feather cloak came off her body.
- The inquisitor's tomb by the crossroads: the rig set, the haulers round the corner in the north passage. The first
  pull went wrong (the rope tore through Varro's and Brennus' hands), the second failed; a turn of silence; the fourth
  pull rolled the slab. Iulia's rebuke failed. Four raging axemen came round the corner and cut Gnaeus down in a
  moment; Vettius dragged him back and Neris' prayer caught him at the edge of death (his throat pierced). Brennus
  felled two, Phaedra's fan the other two. The dead priest came after them through the burning oil Vettius had
  poured in the crossroads; five of the company froze at his face. Vettius took a terrible blow; Brennus, raging, with
  Iulia's holy light on her axe, cut him down in the burning patch, taking two blows herself. Neris cured the rot on
  the spot. His tomb held the regalia of the temples his kind once burned: holy symbols of Türas, Mityara and Ianna,
  seven ivory carvings, white lotus incense, seven vials of holy water (kept), 700 coins stamped with the winged sun,
  and a rod of dawn redwood crowned with a golden winged sun.
- Out by half past one, slowly, at Gnaeus' pace. Every tomb of the great temple is now empty but the chamber of the
  star map and the priest's fresco still uncut.
- Day 269: Neris' charts reading (the astrologer's charts): the astrologer sought the date of the Conjunction of the
  Void, and found it about three years from now. Iulia brought the regalia, the rod and the charts' reading to
  Patriarch Arëtar. He heard them out for an hour and was moved: the rod is the Rod of the Dawn, Ammonar's own scepter,
  lost when the temples burned; once a day it lets its bearer rebuke the dead and pray as one far greater, for a few
  minutes. He gave Iulia leave to bear it below until the work there is done, bought back the other temples' relics
  (3,400gp), and prayed over Gnaeus himself: his throat whole again, and a strange gift with it (he can speak with
  the dead once a week). Gnaeus lies abed for a month.
- Day 270: Vestus studied; the rest of the company rested. Vettius and Varro mended.
- Day 271 (month 10): Lucilla heard the city's talk; Doarë opened the party's chest (the old coin of the inquisitor's
  amphora went in); the four small opals sold (400gp); the Citadel bought the incense (630gp); Liber took the jars and
  amphora. Everyone banked his coin above 150 at the Guildhouse.

## Who they met
- Patriarch Arëtar again: grave, unhurried; when he took the rod from Iulia's hands his voice failed him for a moment.
  Friendly now. He dreams of the river, he said.
- Revered Mother Doarë (vault, day 271).
