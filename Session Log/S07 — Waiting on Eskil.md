# Session S07 — Waiting on Eskil

## Recap
The company waited in Türos Tem for Eskil to rise. Octavia called again on Annalist Socolo, who had heard she was
Malyn's friend and cooled further. Lady Celena had Octavia to dine on day 37 with the legate alone; over the wine
Octavia told them of the black hill, the kobolds and hobgoblins, and the "Lady" the beastmen speak of, and the
legate said the reward stood on evidence that the raids had stopped. The next morning the legion's quartermaster
sent Eskil a bill for Sextus Varo's lost banded plate, shield and spear (63gp), and Eskil paid it. The new soldiers
drilled with the company. On day 41 Ingrid's list was bought: two weeks of iron rations for everyone, sacks to carry
them, a second donkey (Fulvia's) and oil; Octavia lent Aedo ten gold. On day 42 Eskil left his bed, gut still
ruined but on his feet.
