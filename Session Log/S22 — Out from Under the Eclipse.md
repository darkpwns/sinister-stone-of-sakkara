# Session S22 — Out from Under the Eclipse

## Recap
Day 428, evening, the Solar Citadel. Twelve, whole or nearly; Saturnin gone his own way. A subaltern of the east gate,
Kalbo's friend, has asked the Captain why she wanted Kalbo's roster before his death. The company has had enough of the
Order of the Eclipse's errands: Iulia must be got out from under it.

## Where they went
- Day 429, morning, the Citadel. The table's council (Rosa calling): Gideon wanted Iulia out of the Order cleanly or
  not at all; Ingrid wanted the Order's own law on it before anyone spoke to Destrio; Rosa had a second plan if he
  refused (the company is six of the chapter's thirteen blades). Neris went down to the hideout by day and read the
  Codex of Calefa. Among its rulings: Sister Aula, a deathbringer called to an abbacy of the Sisterhood of Mourning,
  who laid down her blade before her chapter, swore the silence anew, and was struck from the rolls of assignment.
- Night of 429, the assembly chamber under the Catacombs. Iulia stood before Destrio with Neris and Lucilla beside
  her and asked to lay down the blade: four contracts done for the Order, an office of the Sun that the Order's last
  errand had put at hazard (the Watch's questions about Kalbo), and Sister Aula. Destrio listened with his hands still
  and said at last: "Calefa keeps the accounts. Yours is settled, Captain." Iulia swore the silence again and her name
  was struck from the rolls. The Order will see to the Watch's questions: the Temple of Justice has a word for the
  Prefect when the old justice has been done.

- Day 430. Phaedra's month paid. The fifty-sixth descent, twelve strong, to the trapdoor under the great nave's
  broken tiles. Neris raised her ward at the foot of the ladder; Hrodvar rigged the west slab, and it rolled at the
  first heave. A gaunt robed corpse sat up in a gilded bronze sarcophagus, and all down the gallery and in the tomb
  beyond the arch the reptilian skeletons began to climb out of their graves, a score a round, until a hundred and
  twenty stood around the ward with curved bronze swords and could not pass it. The corpse-priest came to the edge of
  the circle and three times commanded Iulia to stop, and three times she stood silent; between his words she raised
  the sun, and Vestus and Phaedra burned the crowd with fireballs and fans. Half a turn of it and three more, until
  the last skeleton fell and only the priest stood outside the ward, wrapped in a shimmer of light. Iulia's sun would
  no longer move him. Neris let the circle fall and seven blessed and magic blades cut him apart in a single round.
  His tomb: mosaics of a mighty elf priest before a cheering crowd, a partly gilded bronze sarcophagus, four statuettes
  of ivory and gold, an alabaster tablet of prayers, a gold candelabrum set with ivory and white topaz, and thousands
  of gold and silver coins among broken pottery. The east slab they did not touch. Out at 15:59. Vestus has risen to
  the seventh rank of his art.

- Day 431. Brother Oppius came to the Citadel with three sealed words: retribution asked of Vettius (a legion
  veteran who strangled his mistress), of Neris (a hired pit fighter who beat a boy to death in an Emporium tavern),
  and of Brennus (a woman thief who poisoned her lover). Iulia's name was not on any of them. The table took all three,
  to be done when the delving allows; but Rosa said aloud what Ingrid had been thinking: one week three errands, and
  the next maybe five. Neris, at the hideout for the papers, read the Codex once more on how a chapter gets its head:
  Arganos names him, on the word of the head before him. Destrio's elite blades watched the company's people come and
  go with no great love.
- Days 432-434, the Citadel. Rest. Word from the Old District that the Watch had closed its questions about the dead
  subaltern of the east gate: "the workings of fate". Vestus began to study the spellsword's book for a new spell.
- Day 435. Lucilla's men at the Nicean wine stalls brought her a sealed note for "Vasan": a grain factor of the east
  gate named Decimus Rullo, who did well out of Kalbo's gate, wants the Captain of the Underwatch dead. Nobody will
  answer it as he hopes. The company means to put it in front of Destrio.
