# Session S14 — The white fish

## Recap
Day 290 morning, Cyfaraun, the Silk Pony. Iulia is mending (13 of 31); Gnaeus is abed until about day 298. The white
diamond is locked in the company's chest at the Grey Sisters'. The fire is spent and the month's military oil is
nearly sold out. Between the beach and the west-bank cave a huge chalk-white fish rules the river.

## Where they went
- Day 290. The table's problem: the river is the only way on, and the white fish owns it. What was said:
  - Priya: the beach below the crone is dry rock, 15' wide and 60' long, and the fish has never come out of the water.
  - Tomasz: it found us by the paddles; it followed under the canoes, never above them.
  - Kwame: Neris could charm it, and with her beast-speech we would have a river guard. Rosa: or sell it by the pound.
  - Ingrid: a charm on a beast that size lasts days, not months; whatever we do, nobody stands in the water.
  - Gideon's call: ask the people who catch these for a living, then do it their way from the beach, with Neris'
    charm as the first throw and Vestus' thunderbolt and the fighters if the charm fails.
- Lucilla went back to the Lake Laman fishermen who sold the canoes. Old Tertulla (two fingers gone, chews fennel,
  talks in grunts) warmed to a few coins: "The white ones come up out of the deep by smell. Blood in the water and they
  come for miles. They feel the water with their whiskers, and they know nothing of what's on the bank: stand on dry
  rock and they can't find you. We take them with a shark hook on a chain, a whole beast for bait, the chain staked to
  the rocks. Let it run, then haul it in to the shallows and keep clear of the head. The alchemists pay well for fresh
  gills and feelers." She sold one of her hooks on twenty feet of chain (10gp); a sheep was bought and quartered for
  bait; lamp oil and torches; only seven flasks of military oil to be had this month.
- The plan: down to the beach by the hidden stair; Hrodvar stakes the chain to the beach rock; the bait goes in the
  water on the hook and the blood in after it; everyone back from the water's edge. When the fish takes the hook,
  Neris tries her charm; if it fails, Vestus' thunderbolt, then the haulers on the chain drag it into the shallows
  where Brennus, Marcellus, Vettius and Lucilla can reach it from the rock.
