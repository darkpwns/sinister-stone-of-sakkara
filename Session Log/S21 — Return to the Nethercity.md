# Session S21 — Return to the Nethercity

## Recap
Day 421, evening, Cyfaraun, the Solar Citadel barracks. Thirteen, whole or nearly. Selene is dead and Sand & Bones
belongs to the Family; Iulia's charges are done. The company turns back to the Nethercity: the serpent door, the
sealed cases, the cave beyond the Falling Star, the half-open bronze door off the lizard-men's tunnel.

## Where they went
- Night of day 421 at the Citadel. Day 422, morning: four packs of torches and a week of iron rations for Saturnin;
  every purse above 150 gold into the Guildhouse.
- Day 422, the fifty-first descent, thirteen strong. Down through the sewer caves and the old galleries to the
  Falling Star stair and the lizard-men's tunnel, and through the half-open bronze door with the death's-head
  handle: a grey-and-black marble antechamber, a cedar door ajar to the south (its grain twisted into skulls and
  strange holy signs), and a slab in the west wall worn smooth by countless hands. Hrodvar rigged it; it gave at
  the second heave. A grim robed corpse was rising from a black marble sarcophagus, pale worms writhing in its
  wrappings. Iulia raised the Rod of the Dawn and six worms turned to ash; Phaedra's fan and Vestus' fireball burned
  the corpse and the rest of the worms; Iulia's second rebuke ended it. Behind its far slab, a vault: a gilded cedar
  canopic shrine with gold skulls, a small ebony box with a jet scarab holding three moonstones and a silver ring
  set with amber, and a vulture-painted urn full of gold.
- Past the cedar door, a domed narthex with a black marble basin of water. Vestus read its line: "Wash yourself of
  shame before stepping into the sacred halls." Five lizard-women came in with jars, filled them and went out
  without a look. Nobody washed. Inside Neris' moving circle the company went down the four steps into the nave of
  fallen pews; eight flat black man-shapes poured from the columned hall beyond and pressed at the ward, and could
  not pass. Iulia named the great mosaics: Dirgion the Doombringer hurling men into fire, and the dead rising in the
  Awakening. Up four steps, a dais and an apse with a gilded throne studded with gems under a cracked half-dome; side
  rooms north (seats and a stone organ) and south (seats and a stair up). They mapped it and went back out the way
  they came; the shadows stayed in the hall.
- East through the lizard-men's grand cave (empty) to the junction caves: a buzzing insect cloud came out of the
  dark and was burned. Vettius found a niche whose back slides up: a short tunnel with bronze pipes, a bone key in
  the largest, and a door at the far end that opens into the templon of the doom temple behind the north mosaic. The
  graves of the junction, the long caves and the memorial were emptied of their goods (the old shaman's cave left
  alone). In the memorial, a panel in the rune-pillar opens on a ladder down to a gallery of duelling warriors and a
  six-armed demon. Out at dusk with their loads.

- Day 423, the fifty-second descent. A faint quivering in a gallery on the way down: a gelatinous mass, cut apart by
  Brennus and Vettius before it moved. In the lizard-men's grand cave six lizard-women chipping stone watched the
  company cut the stinking resin from the sealed slab in the north-west wall; they did nothing. Hrodvar's rig, one
  heave, and the slab rolled; a gust of dead air from the tomb blew out the torch. In the dark Iulia raised the Rod
  of the Dawn; when Aelia's torch caught, there were two painted bronze sarcophagi on bronze daises, and the second
  lid was rising when the thing beneath it went to ash with its brother. Mosaics of two elf brothers in a last stand
  against bronze-armed dwarves; their tomb gave up spilled silver, griffin bowls, canopic jars, a dark metal
  six-armed demon (Iulia: Bel, the Slaughterprince), an ivory war-horn, two fine bronze battle axes, and the two
  sarcophagi themselves, carried out on spear poles by eight.
- Going home laden: eight lizard-women with bone darts met in the tunnel, hissed, and let them by. In the galleries
  five gaunt figures with burning eyes came out of the dark; Neris' circle held them off and Iulia's rebukes sent all
  five fleeing. Eight bronze-armed skeletons after them, ash at Iulia's word. Then in the sewer caves three giant ants
  that would not be frightened off: Hrodvar and Marcellus went down under their mandibles, and Neris brought both back
  to their feet; the ants died. Out at 16:38. Marcellus has risen to the sixth rank of his calling; Saturnin, the old
  cantinakeeper, has become a scout.

- Day 424. Neris and Iulia spent the morning's prayers on the wounded; by midmorning everyone was whole, and Rosa
  had them down again for the doom temple's upper rooms. The fifty-third descent. At the black basin the lizard-women
  came for water again. Brennus, remembering Lammala's font, washed in it: the water was cold as grave-earth and
  something settled on those shoulders like ill luck. In the nave the flat shadows poured out once more, slid past
  Brennus as if nothing were there, and pressed at Neris' circle around the rest. Through the templon and into the
  south side room, up a marble stair: a sacrarium with a white font ("The purified body does not putrefy with death",
  Vestus read), a vestry of rotted grey robes, a gallery with two locked cedar doors. The bone key fitted both. The
  sacristy: scroll jars and old temple records (Vestus: a list of the hierophants of the secret mysteries, the last
  of them Orthanach), four cedar chests (one jammed and smashed open): mouldy linens, hangings with a skull in a
  grey cowl (Iulia: Dirgion's sign), seventeen rotten robes and one sound one, dark, deep-cowled, and frankincense
  and myrrh. A gaunt rotting elf with red eyes drifted through the wall murmuring things no one should hear; Iulia's
  Rod unmade it, but Brennus had heard too much and has not been right since. Vettius found six thumb-holes in the
  far door's threshold and plugged them; inside, two black jackal statues came to life and cowered before Iulia's
  sun until the swords finished them. The reliquary: a gold bowl, basin and incense dishes, a gold thurible covered in
  holy figures, four gold goblets set with obsidian and garnets, a gold skull mask, a grinning iron skull mask, a vial
  of clear bubbling liquid, and an ebony chest with electrum, gold, three polished rubies, a bone wand carved with a
  black skull and an obsidian ring set with bloodstone. Going down the stair Neris raised her circle again, and six
  more shadows came at it out of the side room and could not pass. Under the great mosaic of Dirgion they all felt a
  terrible eye weigh them and find them wanting. A hunched grey ghoul in the galleries; Brennus' axe. Out at 18:38.
- Evening of 424: Brother Oppius at the Citadel with a sealed word for Iulia: the Order asks retribution on a man.

- Day 425, the fifty-fourth descent. Neris' prayer lifted the whispers out of Brennus' head at dawn. Down through the
  memorial pillar and the ladder into a gallery of duelling warriors, a room of fallen racks and green bronze, and a
  sand arena stained dark brown, where a ghostly lizard-man in bronze with a double axe, torn open, rose from the sand
  and came at the circle; Iulia's Rod drove it off. Beyond: graves in an L-shaped gallery and a long grave gallery
  (emptied of their goods), a hall of thousands of carved names, and a gallery of mosaics of a dwarven stronghold
  besieged and destroyed, with a slab door in its west wall (left shut). Vettius found the last mosaic slid aside onto
  a corridor of black marble slabs, each carved with a winged sun; they walked down the middle. At its end, a vault: a
  rune-etched curved sword of reddish-gold metal in an ebony scabbard, a bone-white bow and an ebony quiver, a cloak and
  boots of shimmering many-coloured cloth, two bronze chests of gems and silver, and a heap of silver, electrum and
  gold coins crowned with trophies: a garnet goblet, a gold collar and earrings, a carved ivory horn, a gold trumpet,
  and an ivory-and-gold dwarf with a hammer. Tens of thousands of coins went into every pack.
- Going home: the ghost of the arena came again and fled again from Iulia's sun. In the galleries an ochre mass oozing
  across the floor, burned with two of Vestus' fireballs and Phaedra's fan; then a writhing heap of pale worms, ash
  under Iulia's rebukes and the magic blades, but Varro and Vettius were bitten and the rot is in them (Neris cured
  Varro). Out at 18:52.
- Saturnin, made a scout in the Nethercity, came out of it richer than he ever was behind a cantina bar, and that
  night told Lucilla he was done: he means to go his own road. He left with his kit and his pay.

- Day 426, at the Citadel. Brother Oppius' word for Iulia: the Order asks retribution on Kalbo "Baldy", a veteran
  subaltern of the City Watch, who killed a merchant at the gate by night for refusing his bribe. The Captain asked at
  a watchtower for his duty roster and was given it freely: the east gate by night, home at dawn to the Festival
  District with three of his men.
- Dawn of day 427, a lane in the Festival District. Phaedra and Neris spoke from a doorway; Kalbo and his three men
  lay down in the street and slept. Iulia did the Order's justice; the watchmen were left sleeping, unharmed. Oppius
  brought the Order's 2,000 gold before noon.
- Day 427, the fifty-fifth descent, twelve strong. Beyond the Falling Star cave, the bulbous cave of the basalt
  stalagmites: Vettius climbed one and saw two huge long-legged spider-things waiting behind them; Vestus' fireball
  burned one to death and Varro killed the other in the gap. Their den is a cave of gnawed lizard-man bones. Then the
  vestibule of the serpent loop and its west bronze door, forced at once: a great nave of white-flecked black marble,
  its floor heaved and cracked in one corner. Under the broken tiles Vettius found a trapdoor; below it a ladder into a
  gallery whose walls are full of graves, each with a reptilian skeleton holding a curved bronze sword and a round
  shield, a slab door on either hand and an arch at the far end. They closed it again and went home past a tide of
  rats and three ants that ran from the torches. Out at 18:54.
