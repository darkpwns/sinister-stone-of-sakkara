# Session S03 — The Chief's Knife

## Recap
A second week. Lepidus' notice brought a courtesan, whom nobody hired. Back to the black hill with Gaius Rufo, Ione
and Sextus Varo and a week's rations each; on the way a hobgoblin warband of some two dozen crossed the farmland of
21.20 and let the company stand aside, and a farmer fleeing it passed the camp at night. In the Viaspen the company
saw a dormant arch of living yew in a glade (20.19). At the crack (day 10) Urza, the kobold chief, came to the doors:
Fulvia beat his toll down to nine gold a head, 81gp for nine, for passage through his ground for good. Ione kept the
donkey at the crack. Inside, the frescoes of the long hall defied Octavia and Aurelia; the brigands at the hall's
east doors were unfriendly and the company backed away; but a gang of eight brigands sneaking a drink in the
corridor north of the crossroads invited them to share it. The hobgoblins' guards greeted them warmly, and their
chief Thethamun, near death from the war on the goblins, warmed to them when Aurelia laid hands on him. That night
his champion Shulgi led Fulvia, Lepidus and Octavia to the sleeping witch doctor Abgal and the champion Hepteth, who
meant to sacrifice the chief to "the Lady": Lepidus and Fulvia cut their throats in their sleep. The chief gave the
company four silver winged suns of Ammonar; Octavia took Abgal's blood-inked spellbook. On day 11 they took a web of
crab-spider eggs from an abandoned room in the kobolds' ground (and left the jars beyond its curtain alone), went out
past the kobolds, and turned for home. A diseased giant wolf with bone spikes through its hide caught them at dusk
lost in the north of the forest (19.17) and died on Sextus' spear and Lepidus' sword. Two more days lost in the mist
(20.17, then the marsh edge at 20.16) emptied the skins: on the night of day 13 everyone went without water, and
Aurelia lost 6 CON. A bald knoll above the marsh fixed their position; water was found in the marsh on day 14 and the
company rested beside it, a giant frog watching from its pool.
