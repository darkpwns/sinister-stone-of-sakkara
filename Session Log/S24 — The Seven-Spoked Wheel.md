# Session S24 — The Seven-Spoked Wheel

## Recap
Day 442, evening, the Solar Citadel. Twelve, nearly whole. Two of the Order's errands in hand (a housebreaker for
Lucilla, a hired swordswoman for Neris); the Family quieter for the river's work; the troglodyte shaman ready to show
the way to the magic rock.

## Where they went
- Day 443. The vault fee paid to the Grey Sisters for two months. Vettius' people learned the housebreaker's crew and
  its habits; the swordswoman bunks at the Mercenary Guildhouse and drinks late. That night Neris dreamed of a hearth
  gone cold, white ash and no flame, and woke with her hands cold.
- Day 444. Aelia's and Marcellus' wages paid. That night the housebreaker came, on the boss's word, to an empty house
  of Lucilla's where the company waited in the dark. He shook off Phaedra's sleep, Neris' hold and Neris' sleep again,
  and Lucilla and Vettius cut him down; Lucilla did the Order's justice. Vettius' men took him to the river.
- Day 445. The Order's week: another errand for Neris. That night, in an Old District lane, the red-braided
  swordswoman shook off two spells and fought; Brennus' axe and Vettius' blade wounded her, Neris' second prayer held
  her where she stood, and Neris finished it. The river again. Neris dreamed of a wheel of seven spokes turning in the
  dark, and of coins being counted, one by one, by someone who never lost the count.
- Day 446. Oppius with the housebreaker's gold and the new errand: a wine merchant of the Emporium who poisoned his
  brother for the shop. That night Vettius picked the shop's back door, Neris laid silence on the two of them, they went
  up past two sleeping bravos, and the merchant did not wake.
- Day 447, the Citadel. Ingrid would not take Neris below until she knew what was answering her prayers. That night
  the dream came whole: a woman veiled in grey, a wheel in her hand. "Since the new moon after the Provost I have
  answered you. The Hearth turned her face when you took the life. I keep the accounts."
- Day 448. Oppius with the gold for the swordswoman and the merchant. Neris went to the Grey Sisters with Lucilla.
  She knew the wheel of her dreams for Calefa's, the Lady of Fortune in her aspect of the Deathbringer. Revered Mother
  Doare listened, and asked for a sign: at the dusk prayer before the goddess's wheel, the lamps on the mosaic flared
  grey and steadied while Neris prayed. Doare received her as a sister of Calefa and put a holy wheel of black ash in
  her hands. Neris will keep Calefa's ways: prayer at dusk, grey in public, rites for the dead, no life taken but in
  justice, defence or war, and every account paid.
- Day 449. Lucilla's men at the Nicean stalls brought another note for "Vasan": a patrician's steward wants a rival
  merchant dead; it goes to Destrio. An optio of the Watch asked the Captain whether the Underwatch knew of a
  pickpocket who vanished from the Thieves' Quarter; she did not, and said so. Vettius' people found the wharf man
  who had sold word of the Family's business to House Nuvicus' men; he went into the river, and came ashore.
