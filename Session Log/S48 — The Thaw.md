# Session S48 — The Thaw

## Recap
Day 690, the Silk Pony, the last month of winter. Vestus is gone, lost in time; the Revered Mother says to look for
him where he last worked when the thaw comes. Phaedra and Neris work the workshop. The scouts have seen the bugbears'
fort across the ford. Horses, mules and provisions before the march.

## Where they went
- Day 691. The selling day: the milestone jewellery sold through the Family. The market's whole stock of good horses:
  two medium riding horses, a medium war horse, a steppe war horse, a light war horse, two riding mules; saddles.
- Days 691-720. Phaedra and Neris shared the workshop all month: five more scrolls of sleep (seven in all) and ten
  more potions of healing (thirteen). The Watch's centurion came once more about the vanished mage and took his
  week's pay. Pullus' old lieutenant went over to the Gilded Ram's owner. The camp sickness came back; Neris cured it.
  Word came that the Imperial Vanguard had ridden south early for the bugbear bounties, and that the Gilded Ram's
  owner means to take the North Gate dice-houses the night Vettius leaves. The Tower's Regent wants to buy the workshop.
- Day 720. Month 24 closed; spring begins tomorrow.
