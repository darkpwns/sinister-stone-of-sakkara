# Session S48 — The Thaw

## Recap
Day 690, the Silk Pony, the last month of winter. Vestus is gone, lost in time; the Revered Mother says to look for
him where he last worked when the thaw comes. Phaedra and Neris work the workshop. The scouts have seen the bugbears'
fort across the ford. Horses, mules and provisions before the march.

## Where they went
- Day 691. The selling day: the milestone jewellery sold through the Family. The market's whole stock of good horses:
  two medium riding horses, a medium war horse, a steppe war horse, a light war horse, two riding mules; saddles.
- Days 691-720. Phaedra and Neris shared the workshop all month: five more scrolls of sleep (seven in all) and ten
  more potions of healing (thirteen). The Watch's centurion came once more about the vanished mage and took his
  week's pay. Pullus' old lieutenant went over to the Gilded Ram's owner. The camp sickness came back; Neris cured it.
  Word came that the Imperial Vanguard had ridden south early for the bugbear bounties, and that the Gilded Ram's
  owner means to take the North Gate dice-houses the night Vettius leaves. The Tower's Regent wants to buy the workshop.
- Day 720. Month 24 closed; spring begins tomorrow.

- Day 721. The thaw. Vettius left Bassus, Thana's brother, to run the street work; the Gilded Ram's men came to the
  North Gate houses that night and were turned away. Ennia stayed behind at the Pony to wait for Vestus. The column
  rode out: the company, sixty-four soldiers, two wagons. Phaedra's horse could not carry her fifteen waterskins; two
  days were lost before the load was moved to a mule.
- Day 724. Before dawn Vestus stepped out of grey light in the empty workshop, two months gone for the world and a
  moment for him. Ennia met him; they caught the column the same morning.
- Days 724-735. South on the Imperial road at the soldiers' pace: a field of bitterwood, a rockslide that missed Cotta
  and Brennus, a fallen bridge over a ravine and a day's detour, a wagon's broken axle and spoiled rations.
- Night 735. Two black boars came into the camp and killed two sleeping soldiers. Vestus charmed one; the other burned
  and died on Brennus' spear and Vettius' sword. The charmed one, in the shape of a fat young man, led the company
  back to his father's farm, where his cousin Porcia kept the pigs under his spell. He slept in the straw at Vestus' word
  and Brennus killed him. Under the barn: coin, stones, jewellery, potions and a blade. Porcia rides with the company.
- Day 738. Word on the road: a band of bugbears out raiding the farms east of the ford was cut down by the Imperial
  Vanguard.
- Night 740, outside Türos Aster. Three more of the black boars came at the pickets and killed two men. Vestus' fire
  and Phaedra's thunder broke two of them, and Vettius and Brennus finished them; the third ran off with a dead man.
- Day 741. Türos Aster, the whole company together.

- Day 741. The Legate received Lucilla with the company at her back: towers, the legion's old plan of the fort, army
  rations, a home for Porcia. The Imperial Vanguard, camped at the ford with nine bugbear ears on a string, would not
  join anyone. That night Vettius crossed the ford in the dark and came back with the east side of the fort in his
  head: a collapsed wall, a lane between two barracks, a bear pit, a hall with a bearskin door.
- Day 743, sunrise. Tiberius' horns at the ford; the company over the rubble at the east. The south barracks burned
  and slept and died; the bears burned; the witch doctor slept and did not wake; the old war-chief with the broken
  tusk fell at Neris' word and died on Brennus' axe; his shaman followed. At the west wall the rest of the tribe still
  holds, and does not break.

## Who they met
- Legate Nicus Balens, warmer now ("Sixty men and four boars' heads. That is a petition I can read.").
- Gabriol Eirenikos of the Imperial Vanguard: courteous, golden-cloaked, sure of the gods ("The Lady of Victory decides, not the Legate.").
- Ghorbash Old-Tusk, war-chief of the bugbears (dead).
- Glutto, son of Porcius the swineherd, in the shape of a fat young man ("Father said the road would feed us. It did.").
- Porcia, his cousin, sixteen, freed from his eyes when he died.

Day 743, a minute after sunrise, inside the fort's east side: the lane between the barracks. The tribe at the west wall
(a sub-chieftain, champions, a dozen warriors, the gate tower) still fights; the soldiers at the ford; the central hall
