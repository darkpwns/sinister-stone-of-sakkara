# Session S08 — The road to Cyfaraun

## Recap
Secrets of the Nethercity begins. The six (Sabina, Tarq, Lucilla, Vettius, Hesper, Vestus) leave Türos Tem for the
prefecture's capital to hire a priestess of their own, carrying a letter from Priestess Genelen.

## Where they went
- Day 161, Türos Tem: the fort's market had only three weeks of iron rations and three of standard rations for the
  month; bought all (Lucilla haggling). Skins filled at the fort's well. Genelen wrote a letter of recommendation to
  the Keepers of the Hearth Fire.
- Days 161-164, to Türos Aster (3 hexes; mud, then sweltering heat): a giant slug on the road shrank from Tarq's
  bellowing; at night a large brigand band stumbled on the unlit camp and, talked round by Lucilla, parted friendly; a
  rocky crevice gave a safe night. Bought what little food Aster's market had.
- Days 164-166, to Türos Spen (4 hexes): an Imperial cavalry patrol rode past; shepherds, goats. Spen's market had
  one week of each ration left.
- Day 166, 26.14: an undercut river bank gave way under Vettius at the rear of the column: a 30' fall onto rocks.
  Sabina reached him within the turn; her Cure Light Injury kept him alive, but his neck was broken (paralyzed).
  Carried on a donkey.
- Days 167-170: a forester's track off the road found hacked and burned; the skins ran dry in heat over 100°F; a
  freeholder's well in 25.13 (6sp); a rest day there for the column's fatigue.
- Day 171, Türos Orn: a priest of the fortress temple, offered double, restored Vettius' spine (370gp, Repair
  Disfigurement and Disability); he needs two weeks' bed rest and rides in a mule cart under Sabina's care. Iron
  rations for all.
- Days 171-173, to Cyfaraun (7 hexes): shepherds; a friendly merchant camp at 21.09 (a rumour of the Argollëan
  Family's shadow war); a large cavalry patrol; across the Mirmen. Arrived day 173, 08:20.
- Days 173-186, Cyfaraun: at the Temple of the Hearth Fire a sister of Mityara read Genelen's letter and turned the
  party away coldly. Vettius lay twelve days in the Keepers' hospital (12gp). Sabina searched the city for a priestess
  and found one novice: Neris, who took service at the third offer (35gp a month and a month's bonus). Lucilla hired
  Ottar, a peltmonger, as torch-bearer (12gp), and passed on a scrawny fighter and a chandler who would not commit.
  Lodged at the Silk Pony (beds 2sp); evenings in the taverns and the Mink gave three rumours, one about a mad
  patrician who may live with the lepers in the undercity. Day 186: Vettius rose; Neris' and Sabina's cures made him
  whole. Iron rations for everyone; the animals and cart stabled at the Silk Pony.

## Who they met
- A brigand band (Friendly, night of day 161); a merchant camp (Friendly, 21.09).
- An unnamed priest of Türos Orn (healed Vettius).
- A sister of Mityara at the Temple of the Hearth Fire (Unfriendly): tall, ash-smudged sleeves, reads with her lips pressed thin; "Genelen sends us soldiers now?"
- Neris, novice of Mityara (hired by Sabina): slight, sun-freckled, very quiet; holds her holy symbol in both hands when nervous.
- Ottar, peltmonger (hired by Lucilla): broad, slow-moving, smells of tannery; laughs at his own jokes.
