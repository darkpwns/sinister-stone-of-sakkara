# Session S27 — Rivals Below

## Recap
Day 463, evening, the Solar Citadel. Twelve, whole or nearly. The sanctum's treasure and the galleries' grave goods
await a selling day; the Tower's answers on the year's magic come on day 465. The Imperial Vanguard means to go below.

## Where they went
- Days 464-465. The Tower's answers on the year's magic: a grey-cowled robe that wraps its wearer in shadow, a censer
  that brings prophetic dreams, a bone wand of fear, an obsidian ring that guards life itself, a reddish-gold short
  sword of valour, a cloak and boots of the elven rangers, a dragonbone bow, and the ivory horn that calls the great
  eagles. The robe and the horn went through the Family's dealers (30,000 gold); the rest the company kept: the sword
  to Vettius, the bow to Cotta, the cloak and boots to Aelia, the ring to Iulia, the wand to Vestus, the censer to
  Neris. The sanctum's gold, the amethysts, the tiara and the amulet, the galleries' grave goods and the enchantress'
  treasures sold at their full worth. Iulia has risen to the eighth rank of her order, a Bishop of the Winged Sun.
- Day 466. The Order's week: nothing. Day 467: Varro's and Hrodvar's months paid.
- Day 467, the sixty-second descent. A wall of clear jelly in the sewer tunnel nearly took Vettius; Brennus dragged him
  back and the company ran for a side passage. A giant fly; then in the galleries a cold shining shade that only
  enchanted steel could touch: it was cut apart, but its touch drew some of Vettius' life away for good. At the G8
  junction Iulia's moving circle held, the slab gave at the first pull of the rope, and twenty-four skeletons, lizard
  and ape, came out of the dark against the circle and could not cross it; Vestus' two fireballs and Phaedra's fan left
  nothing standing. Then eight howling axe-men out of nowhere, burned and cut down at the circle's edge; and something
  unseen behind them that Iulia's Rod sent fleeing. In the consul's tomb, as the company opened his cedar chest, it came
  back unseen and struck at Cotta, and missed; it was a tall wrapped corpse in a courtier's robes, and it died there. A
  gilded canopic shrine, an alabaster lamp, a statuette of a dragon-winged woman, electrum, four black candles, seven
  slices of strange bread, a parchment roll, and his painted sarcophagus. Fourteen pale worms on the way out. Out at 16:18.
- Days 468-470, the Citadel. Vettius fights the cold in him each morning; it took a little more on the third day. In the
  Family the old killings have become talk. A Watch optio came about the priestess the Captain took, and left friendly,
  offering the Watch's help against her circle. The Family's ears say the Imperial Vanguard will go down by the Old
  District cesspool on the 481st day.
