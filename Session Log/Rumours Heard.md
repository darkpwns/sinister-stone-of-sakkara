# Rumours

## Rumours heard by the party

- Day 1, Türos Tem: Cassia already knew a tale (Loremastery) that an elven wizard-lord is behind the beastman raiders, bent on revenge against the Empire.
- Day 12, Türos Tem: the legate, asked for help, mentioned that elves still live in the forests of Southern Argollë but have gone feral and crave the flesh of men.
- Day 32, Türos Tem: the legate said the beastman raids have grown worse and worse ever since the earthquake a few years ago.
- Day 32, Türos Tem: Priestess Genelen confided that villagers and soldiers suffer nightmares; she suspects a curse.
- Day 38, the buried temple: drunken brigands, friendly over a shared bottle, said the hobgoblins butchered the goblins, an ogre at the west end of the northern hall takes tribute (gold or meat), the orcs charge 20 a head, and their chief Drusus would welcome hard folk.
- Day 38, the buried temple: overawed kobold guards babbled about 'the great purple one' who sleeps in the stepped tower in the courtyard, and whom they serve in terror.
- Day 48, the buried temple: friendly orcs, boasting, said 'the Lady Below' rewards any band that wipes out one of her lesser tribes, as she rewarded the Red Eye gnolls.
- Day 171, the Imperial road (21.09), a friendly merchant camped by the road: the Argollëan Family has stepped up its shadow war on Sand & Bones in Cyfaraun, murdering merchants who would use Selene's services instead; hence the mutilated corpses on the river banks.
- Day 176, Cyfaraun, drinkers in a tavern: adventurers who want to prove themselves as gladiators before the next festival should try the Silk Pony: good coin if you don't mind blood.
- Day 177, Cyfaraun, drinkers in a tavern: the Order of the Eclipse beheads the bodies in the river; beware those who serve the Lady of Fortune, a dark goddess of buried riches.
- Day 178, Cyfaraun, a patron of the Mink, asked about the sewers: poor Tavus Ionicus, last of a ruined landless patrician house, went mad and lost what little he had; maybe he lives with the lepers in the undercity.
- Day 195, the Undercity cesspool, six filthy, twitchy wall-tappers (friendly): the regent of the wizards' chapterhouse wants to meet adventurers heading past the border forts into the Waste, and pays for credible news of threats from there.
