# Session S28 — The Mummy King's Hall

## Recap
Day 470, evening, the Solar Citadel. Twelve; Vettius carries a specter's cold. The consul's goods wait for a buyer.
The Imperial Vanguard means to go down by the Old District cesspool on the 481st day.

## Where they went
- Correction: since day 468 Vettius has slept under the Citadel's oculus with Neris' prayer of salving rest over him; the
  cold in him let go on the morning of day 470 and took nothing more after the specter's touch.
- Day 471. Vettius woke warmer (first pass). The Family's dealers took the consul's shrine, the alabaster lamp, the
  dragon-winged statuette, the canopic jars and the four corners of the painted sarcophagus: 3,375 gold, shared as the
  descent's shares. Kept: the two spellbooks, the crystal-tipped rod, the black candles, the charcoals and the waybread.
  Iulia now wears the obsidian ring; Aelia the many-coloured cloak and boots.
- Day 472, the sixty-third descent. Vettius woke warmer again. Through the sewer caves and the temples without a meeting;
  at the junction of the galleries six pale worms came at the company as it rested and died against Iulia's ward. The
  west corridor's 176 graves gave up their goods. The slab of the western tomb gave at the first pull of the rope; two
  dozen snouted skeletons with swords and shields came out and were burned and broken by fire and Iulia's light. Down the
  winding stair, the king's empty tomb: the four bronze elf-warriors stirred and came on; Vestus' fire felled three, and
  the last was hacked down. The company carried the four statues' bronze out on spear poles. Two swarms of rats near the
  way out fled from Lucilla's torch. Out at 14:43.
- Day 473. The cold took a little more of Vettius on waking. A word from the Order came to the boss: one of the old river
  crews had killed a boy in a wine-shop brawl, and the boy's mother had gone to the Order. Vettius paid her the price of
  blood and put the man on a barge for Arganos. Aelia's and Marcellus' months paid. The Family's dealers took the bronze
  and the grave goods: 5,609 gold.
- Day 474, the sixty-fourth descent. Vettius woke colder again. A swarm of rats in the sewer caves fled from Lucilla. In
  the warriors' galleries, six green-pitted bronze weapons of dwarven make hung in a prayer alcove: taken. In the old
  dormitory beyond, four gaunt grey-robed shades came out of the walls at the edge of Iulia's ward and whispered; the
  whispers turned Vettius', Aelia's, Iulia's and Brennus' thoughts sick and strange, and a cold touch bit Varro. Iulia's
  light drove one back and Vestus' fire burned all four away. Neris and Iulia lifted the madness with their prayers on
  the spot. Eight holy symbols of a serpent swallowing its tail from the trunks. Out at 12:42; the weapons and symbols
  sold the same afternoon.
- Days 475-477, the Citadel. Vettius' cold comes and goes: it took a little more on the 476th day and eased on the
  477th. A centurion of the Watch came to the Captain about the courtesans' circle, eager to move; she told him what she
  could, and he went away content. The Family's ears named another of the river men who had sold the Family's business;
  given the choice, he took a purse and left the city.

## Who they met
