# Session S34 — Beyond the Grey Arch

## Recap
Day 516, noon, the Solar Citadel. Nine in the company; Lucilla 31/42. Eighty library jars remain in the sealed
laboratory; the passage behind the pentagram room's south-west arch is unexplored. Day 518 the barge's mooring; day 519
the weekly block; day 522 the Order.

## Where they went
- Days 516-517. Lucilla healed. Descent 74: the dark orb in the pentagram room's south-west hollow opened a hidden passage
  (Aelia and Cotta kept the orb); Vettius found a pressure plate under a slab and everyone stepped over it; at the end
  a grey arch opened to the electrum orb on a hidden tomb. Four bronze warriors from its guard room were broken in the
  gallery. The tomb door took four heaves; behind it three dead in gold-plated coffins: a masked one with a rod, a
  serpent-crowned one, an armoured one with a black sword. Iulia's Rod of the Dawn unmade the armoured one and the
  serpent-crowned one. The masked one's stare cowed Phaedra, Neris and Lucilla in turn; his ring of agony held Lucilla;
  **a word from him killed Iulia**; a sphere of black water engulfed the casters until Brennus' axe broke it; a cone of
  freezing water dropped Phaedra. Neris walked into the tomb wrapped in silence and he could cast no more; Vettius'
  blade ended him and his body burst into insects. His swarms followed the company out inside Neris' circle and were
  shut behind the pentagram room's door. Phaedra lives, a foot lost to frost; a week abed.
- Days 517-518. Four prayers to restore Iulia failed (Neris read both scrolls, one in the House of Mourning; Patriarch
  Arëtar prayed twice at the Citadel). The Rod and the Underwatch regalia went back to the Citadel. **Gideon now plays
  Cotta.** Neris cured Brennus' mummy rot and the company's wounds.
- Day 519: the ring, bracers and rod went to the Tower for identification (ready day 533).
- Day 520, descent 75: the shrine of golden skulls (a deadly rune: Brennus carried it under Neris' Death Ward; sealed in
  the Grey Sisters' vault), the two other shrines; the treasury behind the glyphed door (Vestus broke the rune on the
  second try): chests, jewels, gold vessels, statuettes, and the hierophant's black scroll (Vestus keeps it).
- Day 521, descent 76: four coffin pieces and the armoured mummy's lamellar; prawns killed on the way out.
- Day 522: the Order gave Vestus a contract; Oppius brought it on day 523.
- Day 523: **Hrodvar came back.** Out of his sickbed, he heard of Iulia's death at the Citadel and asked to stand where she had stood; Vettius took him into his service.
