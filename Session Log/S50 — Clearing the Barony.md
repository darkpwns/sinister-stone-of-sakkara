# Session S50 — Clearing the Barony

## Recap
Day 743, mid-morning, Türos Aster. The fort across the ford is the company's by the Legate's warrant; the patent for
Brennus is a month away. A month of waiting: rest, study, and clearing the fort's hex of whatever else lairs in it.

## Where they went
- Day 743. Rations for a month from the Legate's granary; the horses and mules into his stables; the company moved
  into its own fort with the soldiers.
- The same afternoon they walked the fort's six miles of meadow and river-bank: red deer by the river; a ring of old
  standing stones that Vestus read as a map (its sun-line points thirty miles north-east); a hollow of black, oily
  blood that Aelia knew for a dragon's and would not let anyone touch; twenty wild war-horses that watched and went.
- In the willow woods along the river the elves of the Willow Hall were waiting for them: a fastness hidden in the
  trees, far more of them than anyone had guessed. Lucilla spoke; they had watched the bugbears burn and liked what
  they saw. A treaty: they keep their hall and their woods and hunt the forests, and keep the baron's peace. They
  showed us the bad spring. The hex is clear.
- Thirty days of rest and work. Vestus studied three new spells out of the dead enchantress' book. Neris kept the
  shrine. Ennia hunted the forests east and south: deer, and a good deal she ran from.
- Day 755. Ennia found a giant spider's web-lair in the forest east. It dropped on the column out of the trees and
  caught six of us flat-footed; Vettius killed it with one charge before it bit anyone. Silver and stones among its
  victims' bones; its spinneret and legs for Vestus' work.
- Day 764. In the forest east, a ring of stone men round a cave mouth: Ennia did not go closer. Day 765, in the forest
  south, a lodge of four friendly woodsmen who asked a great many questions about the fort.
- Day 773. Wages paid ahead; the spider's silver shared at Türos Aster. Ennia is a forester now.

## Who they met
- The warden of the Willow Hall and his elves: courteous, silent, everywhere at once ("We watched the fire in the bear-men's hall. We will watch your walls as well.").
- Four woodsmen of the south forest: hearty, curious, generous with their beer.

## Treasure and XP
- A156 (clearing the barony's hex) paid at Türos Aster: 955 XP (the spider and its lair). Ennia reaches Explorer 3.
- Month 25: Vettius 6,083 hijink XP (the Family's take, paid into the Guildhouse while he is away).
- Unsold: four ornamental stones and a fine-wood trinket (Cotta); the spider's parts (the fort's stores).

## Rumours given
- (from Bassus' runners) a Krysean caravan will reach Türos Aster about day 790; Thana turned down the Gilded Ram.

## Changes to the world
- The fort's hex is cleared; the elves of the Willow Hall keep the baron's peace.
- Something lives behind a ring of stone men in the forest east; four woodsmen keep a lodge in the forest south.

the standing stones point at.
