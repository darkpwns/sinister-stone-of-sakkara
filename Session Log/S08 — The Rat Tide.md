# Session S08 — The Rat Tide

## Recap
With two weeks' food and two donkeys the company marched for the black hill on day 43. In thick fog they passed a
nest of eight great birds (small rocs) and a pack of giant rats in the farmland of 21.20 without a fight, but the
fog held them in the boar's ground for the night; a forced march could not get them out, and they camped there
with every watch doubled. The boar did not come. On day 45 they slipped past four stirges feeding on a dead brigand
scout and reached the hill. The kobolds let them by. In the corridor north of the crossroads, a few paces from the
hobgoblins' door, a tide of some fifteen giant rats came squealing out of the dark. Tullus and Decimus held the
passage two abreast, but the rats got past and in among them: Decimus was bitten down and fell back; Aedo took his
place and went down; Octavia's Slumber put three to sleep; Tullus fell; the hobgoblin guards of 45 heard the fight
and came out with their spears, and Septimia, Eskil and their prayers kept everyone alive. Eskil went down and was
cured; Aedo's gut was torn as Eskil's had been (two weeks' bed rest, CON 9), Tullus lost two fingers, and nearly
everyone was bitten. The hobgoblins took them in for the night. Too hurt to go on, they turned for home on day 46
and walked into Türos Tem on the evening of day 49. Eskil is shaking with a fever; others feel unwell.
