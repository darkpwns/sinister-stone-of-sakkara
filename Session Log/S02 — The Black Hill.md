# Session S02 — The Black Hill

## Recap
A week from the company's first morning in Türos Tem. The legate heard them out coolly (one rumour); the annalist,
the priestess and the guildmaster were polite and no more; Quartermaster Thales took to Lepidus over supper. Lepidus
posted for hands and hired Gaius Rufo, a light-infantry sergeant; Eskil won over Ione, a florist, with a week's wage
as a gift. With a donkey for the packs the company marched into the Viaspen in heavy rain (day 2), searched four
hours without a trace, and met twelve dwarf furriers who had seen beastmen at a cave in a rocky hill and gave the
way. Day 3 brought them to the black granite hill. Short of water, Silvanus, Fulvia and Eskil went thirsty that
night and lost strength; a stream was found next morning. Silvanus and Fulvia watched the 130-foot crack in the
hill unseen: four small, scaly, dog-headed guards at a pair of bronze doors. Rosa argued to go in; Kwame talked
instead, and after a bad start the guards grew indifferent: this is the Lady's house, and Urza takes ten gold a
head. The company turned for home, slipped away from two giant spiders at dusk in the farmland, and reached Türos
Tem on day 5. Waterskins for everyone. On day 6 Octavia charmed Lady Celena Valerian in the bathhouse and was asked
to dine with Eskil; over dinner the legate warmed to them (Friendly): two more rumours and his scroll to recruit at
the Parade Ground. On day 7 Eskil took on Sextus Varo, a heavy infantryman; two other volunteers hesitated.
