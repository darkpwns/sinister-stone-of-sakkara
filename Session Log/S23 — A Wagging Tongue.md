# Session S23 — A Wagging Tongue

## Recap
Day 435, morning, the Solar Citadel. Twelve, whole. Three of the Order's contracts in hand (Vettius, Neris,
Brennus); Vettius is boss of the whole Family now and means to rule it hard; a grain factor's note wanting the
Captain dead, to put before Destrio; the priestess's tomb behind the east slab, below.

## Where they went
- Day 435, the Old District. Vettius' own people watched a retired legion veteran, a limping old decurion who drills
  hired swords behind the Mercenary Guildhouse, and learned his evening: the veterans' wineshop, then home alone down
  the lane. That night Phaedra and Neris tried to put him to sleep from the doorways; he shrugged off two spells and a
  third, drew, and limped for the lane's end shouting. Phaedra's fan of flame caught him and Vettius cut him down. The
  Watch called it a robbery. Brother Oppius brought the Order's 2,000 gold the next morning, and took with him the
  grain factor's note against the Captain, for Destrio.
- Day 436 on, the Family. Vettius set one of his own at the wharves to listen, and the ten wharf men who had once
  sold the Family's word to the Pony and been fed false tales since went into the river, quietly, over three nights.
  Word went round the members that a wagging tongue means a rolling head. Nobody said whose.
- Day 437. Varro's and Hrodvar's wages paid. Vestus finished his week with the spellsword's book and has a new spell:
  a step through space to a place within a few hundred feet.
- Night of 437-438, Gambler's Row. The pit fighter with the bull on his scalp came back from the Emporium taverns with
  one friend; Neris held him where he stood, Phaedra put the friend to sleep, and Neris did the Order's justice. The
  friend slept on in the lane and saw nobody.
- Days 437-438, the Thieves' Quarter. The poisoner was one of the Family's own pickpockets. She did not trust the
  summons Vettius sent through her crew chief and went to ground; the Family's people found her hole, and when she
  came out for food the next night Neris' spell dropped her in the street and Brennus finished it. Vettius' men took
  her to the river. To the members she is one who would not come when the boss called, and was not seen again.
- Day 438. The Order's week: two more errands, for Lucilla (a housebreaker who killed a goldsmith) and for Neris (a
  hired swordswoman who knifed a washerwoman in a dockside brawl), 2,000 gold each.
- Day 439. Oppius with 4,000 gold for the fighter and the thief, the two new errands, and Destrio's answer about the
  grain factor: his name is written in the Codex; fate will see to him; nobody will kill him. The company told the
  Captain someone of the east gate had wanted her dead and that it was in hand. That night Neris asked Destrio,
  before the chamber, to name her his heir. "Ambition is not a virtue of the Eclipse." The three elite blades heard.
- Day 440, the fifty-seventh descent, twelve strong. In the sewer caves two great many-legged crawlers came at the
  company in the dark: Lucilla and Vettius were stung stiff before Brennus' axe and Varro's sword ended them, and Neris'
  prayers loosened them again. A grey thing in the great nave died under two blades; a creeping grey ooze was left to
  creep. Down the trapdoor to the empty gallery, Hrodvar's rig on the east slab: it gave at the first pull of the rope
  and a sheet of fire leapt across the empty doorway. Inside, behind a gilded bronze sarcophagus, a mummy in a gold
  death's-head mask. Vestus' fireball barely singed her; Iulia stepped into the doorway with the Rod and the thing
  shrank back into the corner of its tomb and cowered there while seven blades took it apart in five rounds. Her tomb
  gave up the mask, the sarcophagus (in four corners on spear poles), a cedar chest of electrum, gold and three black
  opals, ten thousand silver from the broken urns, the urns themselves, and a bronze scroll jar of four illuminated books:
  the Twelve Erotic Arts of Nasga, Vestus says, in Ancient Argollean. Three grey grave-things on the way out; one
  bit Varro, and Neris cured the fever before it could take. Out at 15:24. Hrodvar has risen a rank.
- Day 441, the fifty-eighth descent. Down to the troglodytes' old shaman by the F18 stair, past stone-shelled lobster
  things, a whirring insect cloud and rats that thought better of it. She remembered the oath and the eggs, and told
  Lucilla the way to the cavern of the ants where the magic rock lies; one of the young females will lead them when
  they choose to go. Not today: the fans were spent and the ants are many. On the way out a green slime dropped from
  the ceiling where Brennus had been a moment before Hrodvar hauled him back; the torches burned it. Out at 13:47.
- Day 442, the Citadel. Rest.
