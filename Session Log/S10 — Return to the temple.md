# Session S10 — Return to the temple

## Recap
Day 219 midday, Cyfaraun, the Silk Pony Inn. The twelfth descent stripped the temple of the dead's corridor and
southern galleries (1,604gp) but cost Septima, burned by her own flask; a hooded thing's touch withered Quirinus, and
Brennus lies in shock. The party stands one level short of the strength the temple's depth demands (23 of 24).

## Where they went
- Day 219 afternoon: Neris laid Salving Rest on Quirinus, whose strength the hooded thing's touch keeps draining, and
  he and Brennus were carried to the Keepers' hospital to lie in the courtyard under Mityara's ever-burning torch.
  Septima was buried by the Grey Sisters from her own purse; the rest of her coin is held for her kin. Vettius took on
  Decimus, a sharp-witted explorer and mapmaker, met in the market a week before.
- Days 220-221: Brennus rose from his bed whole. Quirinus woke weaker each morning (twice). Lucilla went to the Solar
  Citadel: a vicar of Ammonar, cool at first, warmed to her gift and to her account of the temple below the city whose
  inscription calls the faithful to "Awaken", and let Quirinus lie under the great dome's oculus, where the sun's
  holiness is strongest.
- Day 221, thirteenth descent, while Quirinus lay abed: by the far grate to the warlord's halls, keeping well clear of
  the six-armed statue's dais. In the chapel Vettius and Gnaeus found that the scored grey altar slides in a groove like
  the tomb slabs; it moved at the second heave. Beneath: a gold candelabra, a gold bowl of ancient coins, four gold and
  ivory cups, and a padded cedar box with a great blue gem and two blue metal eggs. When Lucilla lifted the box, the
  two black jackal statues woke wreathed in black flame; Aelia had sung them brave beforehand, and in three rounds
  Vestus' fan, Brennus' axe, Hrodvar's mace and Iulia's mace destroyed both. Four tunnel prawns that came up behind
  fled. Home by half past ten.
- Day 221, in town: Lucilla named one egg a death orb, a fire-bomb of the ancients; it sold for 3,300gp, and its twin
  is kept for the temple. The gold and the gem sold for 5,170gp. Iulia became a priest of the third rank (and her
  prayers to Ammonar, long unspoken, are now counted every morning), Brennus a warrior of the second, Neris a
  daughter of Mityara.
- Day 222 dawn: Quirinus woke no weaker under the oculus. Day 223 dawn: weaker again.

## Who they met
- Decimus (explorer, Vettius' henchman): lanky, ink-stained fingers, counts everything twice, quick dry wit.
- A vicar of Ammonar at the Solar Citadel: grey-templed, measured; asked what they had seen before what they wanted.
