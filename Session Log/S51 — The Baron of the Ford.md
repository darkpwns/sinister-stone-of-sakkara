# Session S51 — The Baron of the Ford

## Recap
Day 773, Türos Aster. The fort's hex is clear and the elves keep the peace; the patent is due. Stone men in the
forest east, four curious woodsmen in the forest south, a map in standing stones pointing north-east, and a Krysean
caravan coming about day 790.

## Council (day 773)
- **Rosa (Lucilla, caller):** sell at the caravan; then the stones' cache.
- **Kwame (Brennus):** the patent and the castle: an engineer, workmen, peasants.
- **Ingrid (Neris):** stores for the soldiers; buy rations; nothing near the stone men without a plan.
- **Tomasz (Vettius):** the woodsmen: watch them before anyone drinks their beer again.
- **Priya (Vestus):** ask the elves what lives in these woods; map before we go.
- **Gideon (Cotta):** call: ask the elves first; Vettius watches the lodge; Ennia takes the dragon's blood; the patent;
  an engineer from Türos Tem; the caravan; the stones' cache and the stone men wait for next month. Anyone object?
  (Rosa: "Next month, then. With interest.")

## Where they went
- Day 773. Rations for the month. The elves told us what lives in the woods: in the forest east, a cave where three
  snake-haired sisters turn men to stone ("do not look at them"); in the forest south, a lodge of 'woodsmen' their
  hounds will not go near.
- Day 774. Ennia drew off the dragon's blood into two stoppered vials. That night Vettius lay up outside the woodsmen's
  lodge: four men who did not sleep, and no smoke at dawn.
- Day 775. We visited the lodge in daylight. They were all smiles and beer. Neris' prayer showed every one of them black
  with ill intent; Gideon gave the word. They tore their faces off into grey, hairless things with too many teeth, and
  half of us froze at the sight. Phaedra's fan of fire, Vestus' darts, and four rounds of steel; Ennia was bitten to her
  last breath and Neris brought her back. Coin in barrels, stones and trinkets under the hearth.
- Day 778. The patent: Brennus is a patrician, the baron of the Ford, and the fort and its six miles are his.
- Days 779-786. Lucilla and Aelia went to Türos Tem for builders: no engineer to be had; four masons and old Hesk,
  a master stonecutter. A rider took letters north: the Family and Lucilla's factor found an engineer and 225 laborers
  in Cyfaraun.
- Day 780. Seven families came to plough the ford meadows on Brennus' seed and oxen.
- Ennia hunted on: brigands going south, three dark shapes at dusk that chilled the air, centaurs, a den of great
  worms, ticks. She came home every time.
- Day 790. The Krysean caravan at Türos Aster: the gems and jewellery of the march and the lodge sold (Lucilla
  haggled). Arrows for Cotta at last; a mirror shield for Brennus; three steel mirrors for the stone sisters.
- Day 801. The labour column came down the Imperial road with Septimus Calvo, the engineer. The breach and the south
  gate tower first: a season's work, he says.
- Day 803. The lodge's treasure shared at Türos Aster. Vettius is a master thief now; Cotta a wayfinder.

## Who they met
- Four 'woodsmen' of the south lodge (dead): hearty, then not.
- Hesk, master mason of Türos Tem: one-eared, Krysean ("Legion stone. They don't cut it like this anymore.").
- Septimus Calvo, engineer: bald, precise, hums over sums ("Forty feet of breach is a month of stone and a season of mortar.").
- The Krysean caravan's traders at the ford.

## Treasure and XP
- A157 (the barony month) paid at Türos Aster: 6,210 XP (the lodge and the dragon's blood). Vettius reaches Thief 10;
  Cotta Explorer 7.
- Month 26: Vettius 6,083 hijink XP.
- The caravan sale: the gems and jewellery of A155-A157, about 29,900gp.

## Rumours given
- From Bassus' runners: Thana is with child; the Regent no longer wants the workshop; a Tower mage bought a map of the
  southern fords; the ledger-keeper skimmed (made to pay it back).

## Changes to the world
- Brennus is the baron of the Ford; seven families farm the ford meadows; the works on the fort have begun.
- The south forest's lodge is empty.
