# Session S52 — The First Month of the Ford

## Recap
Day 803, Türos Aster. The lodge's treasure is shared; Brennus is the baron of the Ford; the engineer and 225
laborers are walling the breach. The barony's first month closes on day 810.

## Council (day 803)
- **Rosa (Lucilla, caller's ear):** the sisters first, they're next door and we bought the mirrors; then the cache, no dawdling.
- **Ingrid (Neris):** wages a month ahead before anyone leaves; rations for us and the soldiers. Question to the Judge: does a mirror protect against the gaze? (Judge: no; a reflection is looking at her (Rulings S11). Only a creature within 60' that can see her is at risk; eyes shut means fighting blind.)
- **Tomasz (Vettius):** I look at the cave first, alone, from the trees. And then north, before the child.
- **Priya (Vestus):** they see in the dark; we don't. Stay past sixty feet and use fire.
- **Kwame (Brennus):** they might talk. I'm their neighbour now.
- **Gideon (Cotta):** call: rations and wages today; Vettius scouts today; tomorrow at dawn we all go, ring the clearing past sixty feet, sleep or arrows on anything that comes out. Back here for the month's end; the cache after. Anyone object?

## Where they went
- Day 803. Rations from the Legate's granary for us and the soldiers to the month after next; wages paid ahead. Vettius crept up to the cave mouth: a clearing ringed by sixteen stone men facing a cleft in grey rock; a stool and a spindle in its shadow; smoke and cooked meat; no sound.
- Day 804. At dawn the company went east. Seven beetles the size of men in a hollow on the way: they let us by. At the clearing a veiled woman sat at the cave mouth, spinning. Brennus stepped out and hailed her as the baron of the Ford. She hissed, rose and fled into the dark, shrieking; Neris' sleep did not take her. Nobody came out. Vestus sent a fireball into the dark thirty feet in: screams. When the burning rugs had smoked out, Vettius found two snake-haired women dead in the hall and heard breathing up a passage north; Vestus' second fireball ended it. A chest by a cold pool in the back: a black sapphire, other stones, a silver hand mirror, a necklace and a torque. Neris cut the eyes and tongues from the two that were not burned to cinders. Home by noon.
