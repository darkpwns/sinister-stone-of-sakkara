# Session S15 — Upriver

## Recap
Day 303, dawn, Cyfaraun, the Silk Pony. Twelve, all whole or nearly (strength 53). The white fish of the river is
Neris' friend for as long as her charm holds; the four canoes wait in the stables. Upstream: the stone with marks, the
cave with the black boat and the cold thing in the water. Downstream: the roof goes down into the water. Iulia, Captain
of the Underwatch, has the month to find where the Horned Rat cult worships.

## Where they went
- Day 303, thirtieth descent, upriver. The table: Rosa wants the river's far end while the fish is a friend; Ingrid
  counts lamp oil (Gnaeus gave Vettius two pints) and torches; Priya wants the stone with marks on her map. Down by
  the Emporium grate with the four canoe bundles, the sewer caves (Brennus, Varro, Aelia and Gnaeus swallowed the
  sludge air despite the masks; it passed after a rest away from it), the shaft, the temple halls and the crone stair.
- On the beach Hrodvar lashed the canoes; Marcellus threw half a sheep into the shallows, and three turns later the
  white fish came up out of the black water to it. Neris spoke with it as it fed and asked it to swim with the canoes
  upstream as far as it liked. While they rested, a grey slick came down the steps from the burial cave above,
  creeping like wet stone: Gnaeus and Aelia unslung their bows and shot it to pieces from forty feet before it had
  crawled ten. Six arrows lost to its acid.
- Upstream with the fish below the canoes: the carved tree; the west-bank cave; then a hole in the roof lined with
  black marble, ten feet over the water. Vettius climbed it from a canoe with a torch and looked without stepping
  down: a black-marble tomb, a gilded bronze coffin on a dais with a bronze elf warrior at each corner (curved swords,
  round shields), an ebony throne with a gold box on its seat, the gilded parts of a dismantled chariot, dozens of
  clay pots and jars, and a corridor going off to a stair. He came down without touching anything. Rosa priced the
  room by the stone; Tomasz said four bronze guards do not stand at a coffin for show. Noted for another day.
- The stone with marks: a basalt column wrapped in the old elven script, a west bank with two dozen wall graves.
  Lucilla, Gnaeus and Varro emptied the graves in a turn (rings, cups, amulets and old coin, about 3 st). Vestus read
  the column: a prayer to Lammala, Mother of the Murky Depths, that her black waters wash the faithful clean of shame,
  her waves wear away her enemies, "and that those who plunder her precious treasures become creatures of her watery
  abyss". The fish would go no further.
- The great cavern beyond: a funerary barge with a long pole beached at the water's edge, and between two tunnel
  mouths a 25' limestone horror under algae, its tentacles spread over the cave. Aelia named it Lammala, the Sea
  Mother. Nobody touched the boat. The south-east tunnel runs 40' to a stair that ends against the rock (Vettius
  found the stone there is a door that rolls, not opened), with a side tunnel 100' to a cave where a basin of clear
  salt water murmurs; Vestus read its line: "Anoint yourself with the sacred waters of the Sea Mother."
- Resting there, the company was found by four shadows out of the side tunnel. Every one of them was caught napping;
  the shadows touched Hrodvar once (2, and a cold that stays in him). Brennus' axe destroyed two in one sweep and
  half of a third; Vestus' fan of fire finished it; Iulia spoke the word and her spear blazed, and the last shadow
  would not come into its light; Phaedra's fan and Vestus' bolts of force burned it out in the dark of the tunnel.
- Kwame's question was what the font does. Brennus washed his face in it: the salt burned, his limbs went heavy and
  clumsy, and his luck sat cold on his shoulders for an hour. Under it he walked up the north-east tunnel alone while
  the company waited at the far tunnel mouth, well back from the water. Nothing rose from the river. He found a
  bean-shaped cave and a stone basin heaped with hundreds of shells, shark's teeth, coral and pearls, white, pink,
  black, even gold, its sides cut with long loops joined by sweeping strokes. He took nothing and walked back.
- Downstream past the stone with marks, six skeletons in bronze stood on the bank among the emptied graves; the
  current carried the canoes past them before they knew it. At the beach a great grey legless thing like a blind worm
  slid up out of the river; twelve people with fire and steel roaring at it sent it back under. Up the crone stair:
  three gaunt dead in green-black elven mail in the temple halls, caught unawares; Iulia's rod broke one into dust and
  the spear, Brennus' axe and Marcellus' sword the others. Out by the grate at five, before the light went.
- Day 303 evening: the grave goods to the Silk Pony strongbox for the selling day; oil, torches, a fresh sheep.
  Iulia asked Patriarch Arëtar about the Sea Mother. "Lammala's priests cursed their holy things against thieves: the
  thief sickens into one of her children of the deep in seven days. A remove curse in the first day from a priest of
  the sixth circle unmakes it; each day after needs a stronger hand, and by the seventh only the highest. Bring such a
  man to me on his first day and I will see to him myself." And, gently: the rat-worshippers' shrine before the
  month is out. Neris and Hrodvar slept under the oculus; Hrodvar woke a little warmer.
