# Session S09 — The Hall of Rats

## Recap
The fevers caught from the rat bites were the first enemy. Priestess Genelen put the company off four days running,
then cured Septimia, Fulvia, Eskil and Aedo on day 53 and Octavia on day 54 (45gp a casting); Gaius and Decimus rode
theirs out. The company marched for the black hill on day 57 in pouring rain, lost its way twice in the mud and came
back to the fort on day 60. It waited for Aedo to finish his bed rest and set out again on day 65; a black bear
nosed round the camp in the boar's farmland and left, sprites giggled at Aedo's watch near the hill, and on the
morning of day 68 the company walked into the black hill.

A pack of giant rats came at them in the Entry Way. Octavia's Slumber took three; she was bitten down twice and lost
two fingers' worth of hand, but the pack was killed. Eight orcs came to see the noise and were paid to go away. Then,
a few paces into the Great Hall, ten more rats poured out of the dark. Octavia fell again, Fulvia fell, Eskil's dog
fell; the line tried to walk back to the bronze doors and could not. Eskil went down, then Gaius, Tullus and Herennius.
Septimia ran out to the kobold door-guards and bought their help with a promise of ten gold a head and the rats for
the pot; four came, then six from the guard room, and they died beside the company. Septimia went down and bled out;
Aedo was the last on his feet. Seven kobolds from the dormitory finished the rats, then stripped the fallen of their
coin and their best blades while Octavia watched from the doors.

There was no one left who could heal. Eskil died on the morning of day 69; Gaius, Tullus, Herennius, Aedo and the dog
the same day; Fulvia on day 70. Ione buried them by the stream under the black hill and led the donkeys home with
Octavia, sick with the flux, on donkey-back. They evaded a pack of ghouls in the farmland and reached Türos Tem on day
72. Four new adventurers took up the work there: Valeria, Severus, Lucan and Caelia. Severus hired a hornworker,
Pallas Cornu. Decimus Ruga would not serve Octavia and left.
