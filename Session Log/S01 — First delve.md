# Session S01 — First delve

## Recap
The party gathered at Türos Tem, hired a torch-bearer and followed dwarven furriers' directions to a building buried in a granite hill in the Viaspen. Small scaly guards let them in for a toll paid to their king. Bloodsucking flyers killed Cassia and Brannoc. The survivors brought out a cluster of spider eggs, sold it in town and took on Marcia and Gallus.

## Where they went
Türos Tem (13.08) → 12.07 → 11.07 → the buried building (10.07) → back.

## Who they met
Dwarven furriers (helpful); kobold guards and their king (toll 63gp, safe passage); elves at night (wary); gnomes at night (wary); the legate (indifferent).
