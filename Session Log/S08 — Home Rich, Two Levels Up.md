# Session S08 — Home Rich, Two Levels Up

## Recap
The pilgrims came home along the muddy imperial road, camped a night outside the shut gate and walked into Türos Tem at
dawn on day 52 with the waystone cache. Severina sold the jewelry; the payout made all five pilgrims 2nd level. The
company banked 13,000gp with Guildmaster Karanos, hired Varro (a crusader of Ammonar), won the legate's friendship and,
through him, a formula from Malyn the Misshapen. A week of downtime followed (days 52-59).

## Where they went
- Day 50 evening: camp on the imperial road in 24.19.
- Day 51: the road west in mist and mud. Thirteen pilgrims passed (civil, nothing more). In 23.20 an old imperial victory column stands off the road: legionaries riding winged beasts over a fallen horde, an inscription in Classical Auran nobody could read. Dusk in the fort's own hex: farmers by their farmstead (civil). The gate shuts at dusk; with 6,000gp on the donkey the company camped by the farm.
- Day 52: in at dawn. Severina sold the four pieces of jewelry (6,930gp; she bargained). Payout of the pilgrimage. Coin pooled with Faustina; 13,000gp deposited with Karanos (free if left a month). Faustina's search for a 1st-level henchman brought one man: **Varro**, a priest-crusader of Ammonar, hired at 25gp a month with a month's signing bonus. Faustina petitioned the legate: **Friendly**. Callista, introduced by him, called on Malyn (Neutral) and copied his formula of Discern Magic (500gp).
- Days 52-59: downtime in Türos Tem.
