# Stone Sisters' Cave (hex 27.19)

> [!player] Commonly known
> The elves of the Willow Hall: in the forest east, a cave where three snake-haired sisters turn men to stone. Ennia saw a ring of stone men round a cave mouth there (day 757).

A coven of three medusae (MM p. 235) in a limestone cave in the forest east of the fort, about **4 miles from the east breach** (Judge's call S52: a deer path through the forest; 2 hours' walk). They took the cave years ago; the woodsmen, brigands and a knight who came looking for them stand in a ring round the clearing.

## Running Stone Sisters' Cave
- Dungeon level: 1
- Wandering monsters: none (a lair; the forest's tables outside)
- Light: none inside (the sisters see by Lightless Vision 60'); daylight reaches 10' into the mouth (M2).
- **Habits (Judge's call S52):** the sisters keep the night. By day (06:00-18:00) they sleep in M4; one sits veiled in the shadow of the mouth (M2) watching the clearing on 1-3 on 1d6 (hidden, each time the party first looks). At dusk all three wake; on 1-2 on 1d6 (hidden) a night they go out together into the forest (back before dawn).
- **Alarm:** a fight or loud noise in M1 is heard in M2 at once and in M3-M4 by sleepers on 1-2 on 1d6 per round (earshot); a fight in M3 wakes M4 at once. Woken, they veil themselves (1 round) and come to the threat together.
- **Tactics:** veiled until 30'-60' away, then unveil and gaze (a cone 60' long, 30' wide; every creature in it beholding her saves vs Paralysis or is petrified; MM p. 235); bites (1d6 + Death save or die of poison in 1d4+2 turns) on anyone who closes with eyes shut. Morale 0; they fight to the death in M4 (the hoard), flee into the forest from M1-M2 when broken. They speak Draconic and the local tongue and like handsome men (MM): a parley is possible (they want a man, or the company gone).
- **Beholding (RR p. 507; Rulings S11):** only a creature within 60' with line of sight in light it can see by beholds her; a reflection is beholding (Rulings S11); eyes shut = blinded (RR p. 508: melee -4, Blind Fighting -2, no missiles or sighted spells, speeds 1/3).
- **Plain-text map:** M1 the clearing (90' across, the deer path enters from the west) → M2 the mouth on the clearing's east side (a cleft 10' wide, 15' high; a passage 30' east, sloping down) → M3 the hall (50' × 40', smoke-hole) → a passage 20' north → M4 the grotto (30' × 30', a cold pool).
