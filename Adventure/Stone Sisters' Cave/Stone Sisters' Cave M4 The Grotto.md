# M4 The Grotto

A round grotto 30' across, a cold black pool in the middle under a dripping roof, three beds of furs on a ledge round it. A cedar chest at the foot of the beds.

### Monsters
- **The three sisters (Medusa, MM p. 235):** AC 1, HD 4***, bite 1d6 + poison (Death save or die in 1d4+2 turns) or gaze (cone 60' × 30', Paralysis save or petrified), save F7, morale 0, 245 XP each. hp 14, 14, 18 (rolled S52). By day asleep here (one in M2 on 1-3). Fight to the death here.

### Treasure
- **H221** (TT H, MM p. 235): in the cedar chest (unlocked): a black sapphire (1,000gp), a spinel (250gp), a moonstone (50gp), a piece of hematite (10gp), a hand mirror of wrought silver (300gp), a glass-bead necklace (140gp) and a copper torque (110gp) (amended S52 before discovery: the recover slip, judge-notes). The rest of H221 (a brass armlet 140gp, a bone comb 25gp) is on the stone men of M1 (petrified).

### Checks and saves
- Gaze and bite as above; the pool is 8' deep and cold (nothing in it).

### Exits
- South (20'): [[Stone Sisters' Cave M3 The Hall]].
