# M1 The Ring of Stone Men

A clearing 90' across in old oak forest, grass knee-high. **Sixteen grey stone men** stand in a rough ring round it, all facing the outcrop on the east side where a dark cleft opens. Some hold weapons, one shields his face with his arm, one is half-turned to run. Moss on the older ones.

### Features
- The statues are petrified men (woodsmen, brigands in leather, a knight in a surcoat, two elves): every detail of hair and stitching in stone.
- Sightlines: 90' across the clearing; the forest beyond is thick (visibility in forest by the engine).

### Monsters
- None by day; the sisters cross it at dusk when they go out.

### Treasure
- On the stone men, petrified with them (reachable only by restoring them; MM p. 235): a brass armlet (140gp) and a bone comb (25gp): part of **H221** (the necklace and torque moved to M4's chest, S52 amendment).

### Checks and saves
- Sneaking across the clearing toward M2: a watcher in M2 (if there) is the encounter's surprise and Hiding against Vettius (engine).

### Exits
- West: the deer path to the fort (4 miles). East: [[Stone Sisters' Cave M2 The Mouth]].
