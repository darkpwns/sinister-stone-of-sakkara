# M2 The Mouth

A cleft in grey limestone, 10' wide and 15' high, curtained with ivy. Daylight reaches 10' in; beyond, a passage slopes down east into darkness, 30' to a wider space. A smell of smoke and cooked meat.

### Features
- A three-legged stool and a drop-spindle just inside the shadow (the watcher's seat).

### Monsters
- By day, on 1-3 on 1d6, one sister sits here veiled, spinning, watching the clearing (Lightless Vision 60').

### Treasure
- None.

### Exits
- West: [[Stone Sisters' Cave M1 The Ring of Stone Men]]. East (30'): [[Stone Sisters' Cave M3 The Hall]].
