# M3 The Hall

A cave 50' × 40', the roof 12' high, a smoke-hole over a stone hearth (embers by day). Rugs of deerskin, a loom with grey cloth, deer bones in a heap. By the hearth a stone knight stands with three cloaks hung on his arm.

### Features
- The loom's cloth: hooded robes and veils in the making.
- A passage 20' long leads north out of the back wall.

### Monsters
- None by day (asleep in M4); at night when not out, the three sit here by the hearth.

### Treasure
- None (the cloaks are common wool).

### Exits
- West (30', up): [[Stone Sisters' Cave M2 The Mouth]]. North (20'): [[Stone Sisters' Cave M4 The Grotto]].
