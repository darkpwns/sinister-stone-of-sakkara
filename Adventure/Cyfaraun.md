# Cyfaraun (hex 18.05)

Judge note. Capital of the Borderlands prefecture and its largest city: a former elven metropolis (Cyfarawn) on the **Mirmen River** at the north tip of **Lake Laman**, ruled by **Prefect Justirius Tavicus Basilio** (11th level). City of 2,550 families (class III market); the road hub of the Borderlands with six routes. The ambitious, bold and greedy gather here as the border forts grow isolated.
- **The Nethercity** lies beneath: reached through the city's sewers and cesspools (the cesspit under the agora collapsed about fifty years ago); see the Secrets of the Nethercity module notes: hub [[SOTN Hub]], entrance [[Nethercity A - Entrance Caves]] (Old District #12u), background [[Nethercity History]].
- City detail (districts, encounters, rumours, factions, guilds): AX3 *Capital of the Borderlands* in the Library (`Adventures/Capital of the Borderlands/`); transcribed under `Adventure/AX3/` (hub [[AX3 Hub]]): city chapters [[Organizations of Cyfaraun]], [[Buildings of Cyfaraun]], [[Doing Business in Cyfaraun]], and one note per district (below). The Nethercity itself is not broken down (SOTN, separate module notes).
- Roads: Türos Orn 7 hexes south-east along the lake; Türos Quell south along the lake's west shore; Samos west (about 48 miles, trade route); Kruantem and Mityethtem north.

> [!player] What any visitor knows on arrival (AX3 p. 35)
> Cyfaraun is the capital of the Borderlands prefecture of Southern Argollë, an elven city the Auran Empire conquered a few hundred years ago; much elven building still stands in the old districts. The Prefect, Justirius Tavicus Basilio, governs from the Palace. Like any Auran city it has great sewers, but here they run through the ruins and rubble of an older elven city; storytellers say there are catacombs, chthonic temples and criminal hideouts down there, and the townsfolk say the storytellers, like the sewers, are full of it. Over the red tile roofs rise the Tower of Knowledge, the Imperial Amphitheater and the great dome of the Solar Citadel. Eight gates in the Imperial Walls charge a toll on merchandise (customs on imports); goods are bought and sold at the Emporium. The city is crowded and its streets wind with the river.

## History in brief (AX3 p. 35)
- **Public:** an elven city conquered by the Empire a few hundred years ago; elven architecture in the old districts; sewers built through an older elven city's ruins.
- **Secret** (Judge): **Cyfarawn I** was founded by elves from Northern Argollë at the Mirmen-Lake Laman junction in the late 41st century BE. Its explorers woke a **[[Khepri Hierarch]]** in the lava caves beneath, who became their "Great Teacher", taught sorcery and the **Chthonic** faith (the slumbering gods will rise at the **Awakening**; their faithful will be bodily resurrected, the Empyrean faithful cast into torment). The elves turned from cremation to mummification, dug temples, crypts and catacombs in the caves (**the Nethercity**), and bred the **Thrassians** (lizardmen). Then the gods of earth and darkness levelled the city with an earthquake (almost 2,000 years ago); the surviving elves were slain by their Thrassian slaves; the Nethercity was sealed with its mummified dead in torpor awaiting the Awakening.
- A thousand years later returning elves built **Cyfarawn II** on the rubble; on rediscovering the catacombs the elders forbade exploration and built a **Temple of Demara** over the entrance to seal it. Five hundred years later (over 500 years ago) an eruption from Lake Laman buried Cyfarawn II in ash ("**Ancient Cyfaraun**"). Beastmen from the Waste overran the region; centuries later the elves freed it and built **Cyfarawn III** on the ash, walls on the old walls: a trading hub of Meniri dwarves, Argollëan elves and Aurans.
- Three centuries later the Auran Empire took it, made it the prefecture's capital and renamed it **Cyfaraun**; Cyfarawn III became "**Old Cyfaraun**". Auran sewers follow Ancient Cyfaraun's buried streets (easier to cut through soft tuff); the Aurans also dug dungeons, crypts and cellars, and the Prefectural Palace, Solar Citadel, Imperial Amphitheater, Tower of Knowledge and Hall of Mourning have large underground parts linked to the ruins and sewers; the Merchant Guild has vaults, the Imperial Bathhouse cisterns. Together: **the Undercity**. The Nethercity lies deeper, sealed (AX3 p. 36; SOTN).

## The eight districts (nomoi; AX3 pp. 36-38)
The **Imperial Walls** (Auran) ring all eight; the elven **Old Walls** ring the Old and Bridge Districts; the **Prefectural Walls** guard the government buildings. Outer gates (tolls): Traveler's Gate #15, West Gate #37, Plaza Gate #43, River Gate South #34, River Gate North #46, East Gate #53, Watchful Gate #57, Capital Gate #76. Inner gates: Patrician's Gate #1, Solar Gate #35, Lake Gate #70, Prefectural Gate #79. (POI numbers follow the district chapters; the overview misnumbers some.)
1. [[Old District]] (Nomos I): densest; most of Old Cyfaraun; craftsmen, labourers, traders, thugs; City Watch HQ #5, Emporium #7, Merchant Guildhouse #8, Thieves' Quarter #9, Mercenary Guildhouse #10, the Mink #11 (Argollëan Family); the way to the Nethercity (#12u).
2. [[Festival District]] (Nomos II): Imperial Amphitheater #20, three gladiator schools #21-23, Gambler's Row #24, Silk Pony Inn #25 (Sand & Bones); street fairs, mummers; Traveler's Gate to the northern highways.
3. [[Bridge District]] (Nomos III): the three bridges (Northern #30, Myrdin's #32, Southern #31), the Docks #33, the domed Solar Citadel #36 of Ammonar.
4. [[Plaza District]] (Nomos IV): arcades and piazzas serving as specialised markets; warehouse block #38.
5. [[Tower District]] (Nomos V): the Tower of Knowledge #50 dominating the skyline, the Public Baths #51; alchemists, mages, sages.
6. [[Temple District]] (Nomos VI): Temple of the Hearth Fire #58, Hospital, Almshouse and Orphanage of the Keepers #59-61, House of Mourning #62, Temple of Justice #63, Hall of Travelers #64, Temple of the Blade and Veil #65, Shrine of the Ancestors #68; clergy's homes.
7. [[Lake District]] (Nomos VII): Star Lake #71 and the great houses' villas (Drakomir #72, Ambador #73), Imperial Charter Inn #74; a vagrant colony in the Undercity below the servants' tenements (#75u).
8. [[Palace District]] (Nomos VIII): Prefectural Palace #80, Prefectural Guard Barracks #81, Imperial Courthouse #82; rich patricians, bureaucrats, officers.

## Government (AX3 p. 39)
- **Prefect** Basilio holds civil and military authority, but has been erratic for months; **Lady Syrena Austiria Basilio** and Court Magist **Nicodos Essir** rule almost as regents.
- **Municipal Council:** eight councillors from the 60 richest patricians; split between **Arjenus Drakomir** (wants the Prefect replaced; his uncle the Exarch eyes the throne) and **Valens Ambador** (loyalist).
- **Prefectural Guard** (Legate Gundus Jannion): 600 troops; Palace, roads and 18 road watchtowers.
- Domain (AX3 p. 27): Lawful, civilized; monthly revenue 137,700gp, expenses 75,300gp, income 62,400gp; base morale +1; vassals at Menicos, Samos, Siadanos and Türos Orn (7,150gp tribute each).
- Details, aims and hooks: [[Organizations of Cyfaraun]].

## The City Watch (AX3 pp. 39, 69)
- **240 officers of the watch** (heavy infantry) at the gates and towers of the 8 districts (e.g. 15 at the Patrician's Gate, 5 per small watchtower, 10 per large one), plus a reserve at **Headquarters #5**: 4 subalterns, 35 veteran heavy infantry, 40 heavy infantry, 20 composite bowmen for crowds, riots and violent fugitives. Commander **Tribune Narmirio Drakomir** (Fighter 5), who would back his father's coup.
- Duties: streets and walls, petty crime, thieves, public buildings (not the Palace), fires.
- **Gates** open sunup to sundown; patricians and Imperial officials pass at any time, anyone else needs a reasonable justification and a **Friendly reaction** to have one opened by night (Patrician's Gate, Capital Gate).
- Watchtower III's officers take Argollëan Family pay.

## Laws and punishment (summary; full procedure in [[Doing Business in Cyfaraun]])
- **No public prosecutors:** citizens bring cases, at the **Imperial Courthouse #82** (disputes, theft, assault, murder ...; magistrates and citizen juries) or, for crimes against the Empire and gods (bribery, corruption, heresy, necromancy, diabolism, treason), the **Temple of Justice #63** (templars; accuser must be of equal or higher status).
- **Punishments:** fines and fees (Temple vault), confiscation, flogging, indenture (labour for criminals; debtors may sell themselves), crucifixion for heresy, public execution in the Amphitheater; death for acts against the Empire. Slavery is illegal; indenture lawful. The Keepers of the Hearth Fire can grant mercy; the Order of the Eclipse kills those the courts let off (sanctioned retribution).
- **Treasure:** half of anything found in the Undercity (and Nethercity) is owed to the treasury; tax collectors question sellers of antiquities (5% + 1% per 1,000gp sold last month) and claim 50%.
- **Trade:** gate tolls 4d6+2gp per 5,000 st and 2d10% customs on imports; shops with staff need a Merchant Guild licence (10% of earnings a year).
- Prostitution is legal (most bawdyhouses are syndicate-run); drunken outrage, gambling, vandalism and trespass are lesser charges that rarely mean the dungeon (AX3 p. 187); the pit fights under the Silk Pony are illegal (AX3 p. 38).
