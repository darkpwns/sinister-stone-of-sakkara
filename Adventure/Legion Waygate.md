# The Legion Waygate (Judge only)

In the Argollëan War the legion that built the fort at the Ford (26.19) joined it to its base in Cyfaraun (18.05) by a
**portal** (JJ Portal: a link between two places that allows travel in limited circumstances).

## The two ends
- **The fort's arch:** in the principia's strongroom under the sacellum (K6, K6b), the west wall: a 7'-high arch of
  dark legion stone mortared over with rubble when the legion abandoned the fort. Found by a search of that wall
  (RR Searching: a hidden feature) or by knowing where to look; opening it is 1 day of masons' work (Hesk).
- **The city's arch:** in the buried castra of the legion under Cyfaraun's Old District (the Nethercity's upper
  works: reached from the undercity the company knows). Not yet built as a site: prep it as a small dungeon when the
  party goes looking (`references/unprepped-sites.md`).

## How it works (Judge's ruling S53)
- Both arches must be open (unblocked). The gate wakes when a creature holding a **tessera of passage** (a bronze
  token stamped with the legion's eagle and the gate's two names) touches the arch and speaks the legion's
  watchword (on the tessera's reverse in Classical Auran).
- It opens for **one turn at sunrise and one turn at sunset**, one direction at a time (the side whose tessera
  touched it). In that turn up to **four man-sized creatures (or one horse and its rider) a round** pass, with what
  they carry: about 40 creatures a turn. The far side is not guaranteed safe.
- **The tesserae:** the legion's priests carried the gate's tesserae away when the fort fell, to their house in the
  hills north-east: **the Ruined Monastery (31.17)**, the site the standing stones point at; they are part of its
  cache (H218) when it is prepped (three tesserae, special treasure, TT special treasures by substitution, JJ p. 66).

## Who knows
- **The Tower of Knowledge:** its library holds the legion's survey of the Argollëan War camps, which marks a
  'porta' in the Ford fort's principia and 'the castra gate' in the city. The Regent wants the gate (for the Tower:
  a road to the south no one else has). He sent **Lucan Vesper** (Mage 5, College of Conjuration) to survey the
  fort (S53, day 827).
- **Research:** a week in the Tower's library (or a sage's), Loremastery 11+ (RR Loremastery), learns the two ends,
  the tesserae, the times and that the priests took the tesserae north-east. The Tower charges 500gp a week to
  non-members (Judge's call); Vestus was struck from the roll (Rulings S46).
