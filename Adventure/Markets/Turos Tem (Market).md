# Türos Tem (Market)

Class **V** market, 110 urban families, hex 13.08 (AX1 pp. 25-28). Forum open six days in seven; Guild Merchant Aeropos Karanos at the weights table mid-morning to early afternoon. Imports grain, beer, wine, pottery from Siadanos; fish from Türos Spen; wood and rare furs from Türos Luin; monster parts from Türos Aster. Demand modifiers: AX1 p. 26 table (not transcribed; 0 assumed for adventuring gear).
