# SOTN Magic Items

Judge only. Hub: [[SOTN Hub]]. Spells: [[SOTN Spells]]. Pages are SOTN's printed pages. SOTN is written for ACKS I: each entry gives SOTN's item in ACKS II terms (spell names, saves, conditions), then the ACKS II Treasure Tome (TT) entry where the TT has the same or a near item.

**Conventions (Judge's calls, apply throughout):**
- **TT version first.** Where the TT has the same item (most of the HFH and Axioms items reprinted here are in the TT), run the **TT entry** in play: it is the same item revised for ACKS II and the engine reads its vault note. SOTN's differences are listed so the Judge can keep SOTN's version for a particular find.
- **Item saves.** A Spells save against an effect from an item becomes an **Implements** save (ACKS II item rule, [[Magic Items - Miscellaneous Items]]); Paralysis, Death and Blast saves are unchanged.
- **Value.** "Value" is SOTN's (ACKS I) price; the TT **base cost** and **apparent value** are given where a TT entry exists. Magic items count for XP only when sold. SOTN's "(effect: … cantrip; base cost; base time)" builds are kept for crafting; ACKS II has no cantrips.
- **[A] / [D]**: usable by arcane / divine casters only.
- Energy drain → enervation; charm → bewitch; hold → halt; turn undead → rebuke undead; infravision → [[Lightless Vision]].

**Where:** placements are taken from the area notes ([[Nethercity Features]] treasure conventions).

**Magic items (SOTN pp. 130-136).** SOTN notes that the cloak of skinchanging and collar of conversation come from the Heroic Fantasy Handbook, and the candle of comfort, charcoal of phantasms, lyre of the lullaby, oil of relaxing respite and woodwind of the woodlands from Axioms (SOTN p. 130).

## Bag of Faerie Seeds
- **Looks:** a plain leather bag holding **2d4** seeds. Bag and seeds are both magic: the bag keeps the seeds from disease, rot and spoilage forever. Putting anything else in the bag **destroys any seeds left** in it; it protects only its faerie seeds.
- **Effect (per seed):** planting a seed enchants all plants within **240’** radius in the planter's favour, as [[Forest Enchantment]] (and [[SOTN Spells#Forest Enchantment]]). The planter and allies he "introduces" pass through the plants unharmed; the plants harry his enemies.
  - Planter and allies **+1 to surprise** and better movement multipliers; enemies **−1 to surprise** and worse multipliers:

    | Terrain | Allied move multiplier | Enemy move multiplier |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Hills, forest, scrub | ×1 | ×1/2 |
    | Jungle, swamps | ×2/3 | ×1/3 |
  - Normal plants: no save at planting; **each season** the Judge rolls one save for all of them as a **1 HD monster** to break it. Severe damage to the plants caused or allowed by the planter (clear-cutting) ends it at once; gathering deadwood, picking fruit and flowers, trimming and gardening do not.
  - Plant monsters in the area: no save under 2 HD; **2 HD or more** save to resist (SOTN: vs Spells → **Implements**, item rule). Once bewitched, it lasts until [[Dispel Magic]] or a successful save, rolled **each day at 7+1 HD or more, each week at 5-6 HD, each month at 4 HD or fewer**.
  - No special means of talking with the plants.
- **ACKS II:** [[Faerie Seed]] (TT): base cost **2,500gp**, apparent value 0gp; one magical seed per bag; plant creatures save vs Implements or are bewitched as [[Beguile Humanoid]]; saves at 9+ / 5-8 / under 5 HD intervals; ACKS II multiplier rows are "hills, forest, scrubland" and "mountain, jungle". SOTN's bag holds 2d4 seeds (Judge's call: keep SOTN's count).
- **Value:** 2,500gp per faerie seed.
- **Where:** B4 ([[Nethercity B - Early Chthonic Tomb]]) — 5 seeds

## Bathtub of the Goddess
- **Looks:** a hepatizon cauldron engraved with scenes of forbidden pleasure from the iconography of Nasga (beauty, pain and lust).
- **Effect:** a living creature that bathes in it for **one turn** becomes a creature of the same kind but the **opposite sex**, gaining every primary and secondary characteristic of the new sex (external and internal, reproductive capacity if of breeding age), like an opposite-sex twin of itself — as [[Bath of the Goddess]] (and [[SOTN Spells#Bath of the Goddess]]).
  - A sexually altered creature (e.g. a eunuch) suffers the equivalent alteration in its new form. A single-sexed species: no effect. A species with several sexes, or an intersexed creature of a mostly two-sexed species: the bather chooses the result; a male or female may become intersexed if desired (rumoured requirement of some mystery cults' high figures).
  - Permanent until [[Dispel Magic]] or a second bath, which restores the original sex. Unlimited uses; one bather at a time, one turn each.
- **ACKS II:** no TT item; the effect is the ACKS II spell [[Bath of the Goddess]] (Divine 6, perpetual; it becomes permanent if the creature births or sires children in its new sex — Judge's call: apply that rule to the bathtub too).
- **Value:** 82,500gp.
- **Where:** C5 ([[Nethercity C - Central Caves]])

## Candle of Comfort
- **Looks:** a foul-smelling taper of waxy white fat.
- **Effect:** once lit it burns fast, **6 rounds** in all if not snuffed. Each round it burns it deals **1d8** damage to the **lowest-HD swarm** within **30’**, no save. Only swarms of ordinary bats, rats and insects (no giant animals or vermin). Named for ridding noble bedchambers of bedbugs.
- **SOTN build:** exterminate pest cantrip; base cost 600gp; base time 9 days.
- **ACKS II:** [[Candle of Comfort]] (TT): base cost **600gp**, apparent value 5gp; damage is extraordinary necrotic, at the end of each round; a snuffed candle can be relit for its remaining rounds.
- **Where:** G11 ([[Nethercity G - Grand Chthonic Temple]]) — 4 candles

## Censer of Prophetic Dreams
- **[D].** **Looks:** a gold or silver incense burner covered in intricate religious iconography.
- **Effect:** a divine caster lights a stick of rare incense worth **20gp+** in it on his god's holiest day of the week; it gives off a heavenly-scented magical smoke. If he then sleeps within **10’**, he wakes **2d4 hours** later from a vivid prophetic dream ([[Prophetic Dream]]) carrying a short, true and important piece of information about a person, settlement, domain, realm, object or event of interest to him. The symbolism may be misread; if misread or not acted on, conditions may change until the information is useless.
- **Misuse:** on the wrong day, or by anyone not a divine caster: night terrors deny the sleeper his night's rest.
- **ACKS II:** [[Censer of Prophetic Dreams]] (TT): base cost **18,000gp**, apparent value 600gp; usable **1/week**.
- **Value:** 21,000gp.
- **Where:** J15 ([[Nethercity J - Doom Temple of Dirgion]]) — the gold thurible

## Charcoal of Phantasms
- **Looks:** a briquette smelling of peat and exotic woods.
- **Effect:** burned in a brazier, it shows a **1’ × 1’ × 1’** visual illusion of an object, creature or area in the flames: no sound, smell, texture or temperature. A lighter who knows its powers controls the image by concentration; otherwise (or if no one concentrates) the image is random (Judge's discretion). It lasts as long as concentrated upon, or **1 turn** if not.
- **SOTN build:** phantasm of fire cantrip; base cost 100gp; base time 1½ days.
- **ACKS II:** [[Charcoal of Phantasms]] (TT): same item; base cost **100gp**, apparent value 1gp.
- **Where:** G9 ([[Nethercity G - Grand Chthonic Temple]]) — 6 pieces

## Cloak of Skinchanging

- **Effect:** the wearer becomes the animal of the cloak's type, gaining its physical characteristics, movement types, attack routine and special abilities while keeping his own mind. He may stay transformed indefinitely and return at will; slain, he reverts. After he returns to his own form the cloak cannot be used again for **1 hour (6 turns)**.
- **Type (1d4, or Judge's choice; other animals may be made):**

  | 1d4 | Animal | Characteristics |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | 1 | Bear | Move 120’, AC 3, 2 claws/1 bite, 1d3/1d3/1d6, bear hug |
  | 2 | Hawk | Move 480’ fly, AC 1, 2 talons, 1d2/1d2, swoop attack |
  | 3 | Swan | Move 360’ fly, AC 3, 2 wings/1 bill, 1/1/1d2 |
  | 4 | Wolf | Move 180’, AC 2, 1 bite, 1d6 |
- **ACKS II:** [[Cloak of Skinchanging]] (TT): base cost **38,000gp**, apparent value 1,200gp; **1d6** table (bear, crow/raven, hawk, seal, swan, wolf; hawk AC 3 with dive attack); the wearer keeps his own hp and can cast if the form can gesture and speak; **3 turns** before reuse. ACKS I "swoop" = ACKS II dive.
- **Value:** 24,000gp.
- **Where:** H6 ([[Nethercity H - Great Temple of Awakening]]) — swan; worn by the mummy princess

## Collar of Conversation
- **Looks:** a jewelled leather collar.
- **Effect:** fastened round an animal's throat, it lets the animal understand and speak the language of any intelligent creature within **60’**. For speech the animal has effective **INT 6 ± its Trainability Modifier** (e.g. a [[Cave Gorilla]] (white ape), TM +3: INT 9; a crocodile, TM −1: INT 5). On a sapient creature it has no magical effect.
- **ACKS II:** [[Collar of Conversation]] (TT): base cost **100,000gp**, apparent value 1,000gp; range **90’** (as [[Tongues]]); TM per [[Animal Training]].
- **Value:** 100,000gp.
- **Where:** not placed in SOTN's room keys (Judge's choice; the M notes mention it as a way to talk with white apes).

## Faerie Flute
- **Looks:** a beautifully made syrinx (pan flute).
- **Effect:** played in a **forest for one turn**, it summons faeries, nymphs or similar creatures, who arrive as the turn of playing ends. A flutist with **Performance (flute)** ([[Performance]]) chooses the result; otherwise roll 1d10:

  | 1d10 | Summoned creatures |
  | --- | --- |
  | 1 | 3d6 [[Sprite]]s |
  | 2 | 2d6 sprites, 1d6 [[Pixie]]s |
  | 3 | 2d6 pixies |
  | 4 | 2d4 [[Elf|elves]] |
  | 5 | 1d4 elves, 1d3 [[Dryad]]s |
  | 6 | 1d6 dryads |
  | 7 | 1d3 [[Centaur]]s |
  | 8 | 1 centaur, 1d6 elves |
  | 9 | 1 centaur, 1d4 dryads |
  | 10 | 1 [[Treeherder]] (SOTN: treant) |
  - They are not hostile but cautious and may stay hidden at first (a flutist who does not know the flute is magic may never learn he called anything). The flutist must prove worthy: gifts of food or treasure, or fine poetry or song from a character with **two or more ranks** of the proficiency. Properly entreated, they help him for **up to one day**. Any hostile act by the flutist or his allies makes them fight or, if overpowered, flee and perhaps return with kin for revenge.
  - Works magically **once per week**.
- **ACKS II:** no TT item (nearest in kind: [[Tokay Flute]], a calling flute). The 1-day service is a "calling" in JJ terms.
- **Value:** 15,000gp.
- **Where:** H13 ([[Nethercity H - Great Temple of Awakening]])

## Funerary Barge of the Cults
- **Looks:** a black, flat-bottomed river boat about **25’ × 10’**, weight **2,000 lbs**; a tall prow capped by a brass skull; a single oar in a rowlock at the stern; a cabin with blinded windows filling most of the deck, room for **four coffins or 400 stone** of cargo.
- **Effect:** needs no rowing: dip the oar and the barge moves by magic at a speed set by the oar's angle and depth, **maximum 60’ per turn (36 miles per day)** (as printed; Judge's call: read it as a combat speed of 60’ and a voyage of 36 miles a day). Rowing the oar in the barge's own secret pattern **teleports** it to any body of water within **360’**, **once per day**, and only while **at least one dead body** lies in the cabin.
- **Combat:** **AC 1, 45 SHP** (SOTN: 45 SP, ACKS I structural points; [[Vehicle Combat]]). Creatures inside the cabin get **+4 AC against missiles** and **+4 to Blast saves**.
- **ACKS II:** no TT item (ACKS II small barge: [[Vessels]]).
- **Value:** 68,000gp.
- **Where:** P11 ([[Nethercity P - Sub-Abyssal River Banks]]) — the oar pattern is engraved on the P12 basin

## Horn of the Eagles
- **Looks:** a rune-carved ivory hunting horn.
- **Effect:** blowing it calls a **[[Large Roc]]** to serve as a winged steed, flying to the blower at its flying speed: **1d6 hours** if called in a 24-mile mountains hex, **+1 hour per 24-mile hex** between the blower and the nearest mountains hex. If the blower cannot be reached (deep in a dungeon), it comes as near as it can and circles the sky there.
  - The roc understands the blower. For a **Lawful** blower it is a loyal mount for him or whoever he directs; it serves a **Neutral** blower or rider only grudgingly; asked to do a Chaotic act or help a Chaotic creature, it leaves at once. Otherwise it stays until slain, dispelled, or **one day** passes.
  - No saddle or tack, and no riding skill: unskilled riders take the roc into battle at their own risk ([[Riding]]).
  - Usable **once per week**.
- **ACKS II:** [[Horn of the Eagles]] (TT) is a different item: base cost **25,000**, apparent value 200gp, **1/day**, calls **2d4 [[Giant Raptor]]s** as henchmen for a day. (SOTN's text says "spell" and "caster" here, left over from a spell write-up.)
- **Value:** 21,000gp.
- **Where:** K6 ([[Nethercity K - Hallowed Hall of Heroes]])

## Iron Masks of Cyfaraun
- **Lore:** five masks forged by the corrupt elves of Cyfaraun on an altar of profane darkness at the height of Southern Argollë, given to the masters of the five Chthonic cults (Bel, Dirgion, Iskara, Nasga, Telith) and worn in the presence of the Great Teacher. Sages hold that the Thrassian conquerors melted them down; rumour says some survived ([[Nethercity History]]).
- **Looks:** an iron faceplate and skullcap helm shaped as a gargoyle, skull, demon or snarling beast; some trail a cape of ermine, velvet or sable fastened at the breast with a gold or platinum clasp. Carried, **2 stone** of encumbrance.
- **Donning:** the mask fastens to the face, becomes feather-light and fits snugly; iron bolts at its base sink into the neck (no lasting harm). It cannot be removed except by **severing the wearer's head**, or safely and painlessly by the wearer speaking its **command word**.
- **Benefits:** normal hearing and sight; sees in natural darkness as under a bright full moon (as [[Lightless Vision]]); breathes normally, the air always clean, so poisonous vapours and smell-based effects (e.g. [[Cloud of Poison]] (SOTN: cloudkill), a [[Troglodyte]]'s stench) do not affect him; **immune to enchantment spells and effects**; immune to the "kiss" of the [[Khepri]] and other attacks needing access to the face, eyes or skull.
- **Drawbacks:** **−4 to all reaction rolls with Lawful beings**, and an immediate **hostile** reaction from Thrassians, [[Lizardman|lizardmen]], troglodytes and their like. A constant thrum of unholy whispering in the mind. **Corruption:** the first time a mask is donned, and each day it is worn for more than one hour, the Judge rolls **1d20**: on **3 or less** the wearer's alignment shifts **one step toward Chaotic**. (SOTN's Heroic Fantasy Handbook corruption-point option is not used: no supplements.)
- **SOTN design notes (ACKS I build costs):** invulnerability to enchantments (55), 1 creature, self (×0.75), 1 hour (×1.33), beneficial (×1) = 54.86; invulnerability to gases and vapours (55), same factors = 54.86; current form gains infravision (15), 1 creature, self (×0.5), 1 hour (×1), beneficial (×1) = 7.5.
- **ACKS II:** [[Iron Mask of Cyfaraun]] (TT): base cost **128,500gp**, apparent value 4,000gp; adds **−2 to Listening and −1 to initiative and surprise** from the whispering; no alignment-shift roll (Judge's call: keep SOTN's d20 corruption roll for these, the five originals).
- **Value:** 104,000gp each.
- **Where:** J15 ([[Nethercity J - Doom Temple of Dirgion]]) — the sculpted iron mask; also a key to the L20 door

## Lyre of the Lullaby
- **Looks:** a well-made lyre with a tortoise-shell body painted with scenes of hearth and home; each has its own controlling lullaby (like a command word).
- **Effect:** strumming while singing the lullaby puts one living creature of **less than 1 HD** within touch range (no contact or attack throw needed) into a peaceful, dreamless sleep ([[Slumbering (Condition)]]). An unwilling target saves vs **Paralysis**, at **−1 per rank** of the lyrist's [[Performance]] (not required). The lyrist must stay stationary and concentrate; the effect ends when he stops, though a willing, tired subject may sleep on. Used for fretful children; wealthy patricians keep one for their child-minders.
  - Sleepers are helpless and can be slain in one round by unengaged foes; a slap or wound wakes them, noise does not. No effect on creatures already unconscious, undead, constructs, oozes, or anything that does not rest.
  - Magic usable **once every 8 hours**; an ordinary instrument otherwise.
- **SOTN build:** lullaby cantrip; base cost 1,200gp; base time 10 days.
- **ACKS II:** [[Lyre of the Lullaby]] (TT): base cost **1,200gp**, apparent value 100gp; **3/day, not more than 1/hour**.
- **Where:** E7 ([[Nethercity E - Deep Level Central Complex]])

## Rod of Elemental Spheres
- **[A].** **Looks:** a rod of four intertwined materials (narwhal horn, sandstone, sky-iron, obsidian) topped by a glass sphere in which motes of water, earth, air and fire swirl.
- **Effect:** four powers, one per element; **four uses per day in total**, in any mix.
  - **Water:** a **20’ diameter** sphere of water anywhere within **120’**, may be evoked on objects or creatures. A creature that starts its initiative in it or enters it saves vs **Paralysis**: failure, it begins [[Drowning (Condition)|drowning]] and **dies after 10 rounds** unless rescued or the sphere is dispelled; success, it is pushed out by the shortest path, and may drag out a drowning ally of its size or smaller with a successful **Dungeonbashing** throw (SOTN: open doors; [[Doors and Dungeonbashing]]). Line of sight enters but does not pass through. Lasts until dispelled or the wielder stops concentrating.
  - **Earth:** a **5’** boulder appears beside the wielder and rolls **20’** in a direction he chooses, then **20’ more each round** at the start of his initiative. Each creature in its path saves vs **Blast**: success, **1d6** bludgeoning; failure, **2d6** bludgeoning and knocked [[Prone (Condition)|prone]]. (Duration: concentration, per SOTN's design note.)
  - **Air:** a **10’ radius** sphere of howling wind around the wielder, as [[Circling Winds]]: creatures coming within **5’** of its outside are attacked as by a **2 HD monster, 1 attack, 1d8**; a creature trying to pass in saves vs **Paralysis** or is pushed back **5’** (**+4 per size category above man-sized**); it blocks small non-magical missiles (arrows, sling stones, bolts, thrown daggers) from entering but not from leaving, and does not stop those inside from leaving. Evoked on creatures, those who save vs **Blast** may dive in or out unharmed. Lasts while the wielder stays stationary and concentrates, unless dispelled.
  - **Fire:** a **10’ diameter** sphere of fiery plasma anywhere within **30’**, may be evoked on objects or creatures. Each round on his initiative, in lieu of attacking, the wielder may move it up to **20’**. Each creature it touches during his initiative takes **2d4** fire damage, half on a **Blast** save. Lasts while he concentrates.
- **SOTN design notes (ACKS I build costs):** Sphere of Water: drowning (10), impermeable to vision (5), force back (10), must be sphere (×0.75), 500 sq ft (×0.75), 10’ wide (×2), evoked where creatures are (×2), range 120’ (×1), concentration (×0.5), save for half (×0.75) = 16.8. Sphere of Earth: 2d6 damage (20), impermeable (25), 100 sq ft (×0.5), 5’ thick (×1.75), moves away 20’/round (×1.5), range 0 (×0.4), concentration (×0.5), save for half (×0.75) = 8.8; plus knock down (10), impermeable (25), same factors, save for nothing (×0.5) = 11.5. Sphere of Fire: 2d6 damage to those passing through (20), sphere (×0.75), 100 sq ft (×0.5), 5’ wide (×1.75), moved by caster 20’/round (×3), evoked where creatures are (×2), range 30’ (×0.6), concentration (×0.5), save for half (×0.75) = 17.7. (SOTN's fire note says 2d6 and 5’; the power's text says **2d4** and **10’ diameter**: use the text.)
- **ACKS II:** [[Rod of Elemental Spheres]] (TT): base cost **40,000gp**, apparent value 700gp; **each power once per day**; water drowning kills after **5 + CON modifier** rounds; earth: **2d6**, Blast save halves and prevents knockdown; damage types extraordinary.
- **Value:** 40,000gp.
- **Where:** O3 ([[Nethercity O - Sub-Abyssal Secret Tomb of the Hierophant]]) — Orthanach

## Soul Gem
- **Lore:** first made during the elven necromantic research that led to the [[Mummy Lord|mummy lords]], as prototypes of their phylacteries; the elves found the gems stored divine power drawn from the souls of the newly dead.
- **Looks:** an elongated octahedron of dark crystal with a screaming, damned soul visible inside. **Small** (holds up to **50** divine power), **medium** (**500**), **large** (**5,000**).
- **Capture:** whenever a living creature is reduced to **0 hp within 5’** of a gem with room left, it saves vs **Death**; a creature slain by **blood sacrifice in a sinkhole of evil** ([[Sinkholes of Evil]]) fails automatically. Failure: the gem takes the residue of its soul and its **XP value** is added to the gem's divine power, up to capacity. Success: nothing.
- **Uses** (by a spellcaster holding it):
  - Pay the **special component** cost of any magic research, point for point.
  - Cast a spell from his repertoire **without using a slot**: 1st level **50**, 2nd **100**, 3rd **150**, 4th **225**, 5th **300**, 6th **400**.
  - Cast **any healing spell on himself** without a slot, even if not in his repertoire, at the same costs.
  - Cast [[Speak with Dead]] on any soul in the gem, free.
- **Victims:** a creature whose residue is in a gem cannot be reached by speak with dead and its body yields no special components. It can still be raised or reincarnated, but counts as dead **20 days longer** and loses **10%** of its accumulated XP.
- **Destruction:** fragile; can be **sundered** ([[Special Maneuvers]]) or destroyed by its carrier as an action in lieu of attacking. Breaking it releases all its divine power and lifts the victims' penalties; a creature **holding** it when it breaks saves vs **Death** or loses **10% of its accumulated XP** (SOTN: energy drained; Judge's call: an XP loss as printed, not the ACKS II [[Enervated (Condition)|enervated]] condition).
- **ACKS II:** no TT item. In ACKS II a creature's death residue equals its XP value ([[Divine Power of Creatures]]), so the capture rule fits; ACKS II's own spell costs in divine power are lower ([[The Cost of Spells]]: 5/15/30/50/75/105). Judge's call: keep SOTN's costs for the gem.
- **Where:** O5 ([[Nethercity O - Sub-Abyssal Secret Tomb of the Hierophant]]) — large; holds 5,000 divine power and the residue of Caogan
