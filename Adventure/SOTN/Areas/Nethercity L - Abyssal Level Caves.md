# Nethercity L — Abyssal Level Caves

Natural lava caves at the bottom of the sludge river, lined with the wall graves of commoners from 2900 to 2200 BE (the oldest at the sludge end, the youngest toward M and N). Most are on the **Abyssal Level**; the book calls the sludge pockets L2-L3, the queen's cave L13 and the pool cave L20 **Sub-Abyssal** (each a short drop below its neighbours). The sewer sludge from C2b pours into L1 and spreads through L2, L3 and half of L5, and its miasma here is **thick enough to explode**. A **colony of giant ants** has turned the caves into a nest, farming fungus on sludge in the wall graves; the white apes of M raid their farms. **L18 is a blighted sinkhole of evil.** In L3 a spreading sage slime chokes the way to **L4, a working teleportation platform** to the Istrith Forest. No light. Hub [[SOTN Hub]]; standing rules [[Nethercity Features]] (sewer sludge, miasma, fungal infection, sinkholes, wall graves, grave goods). Pages are SOTN's printed pages.

Running: Sewer Caves column, dungeon level 2 — throw 1d6 every 3 turns, 6+, then 2d10 on `Tables/sotn-wandering-sewer-caves.yaml` (sewer caves never clear). **Miasma over the sludge (L1, L2, L3, L5) is combustible:** anyone carrying a torch or other open flame over the sludge has **1 in 6 each turn** of setting off a **6d6 fireball** (20’ diameter, save vs Blast for half) centred on himself; lighting the sludge, fire attacks on the L3 slime, or [[Fireball]], [[Wall of Flame]] and the like set off a **6d6 fireball covering all the sludge in L**; the blast burns the miasma off its area for 1d6 days. The usual miasma save vs Death each turn applies too. **Fungus-farm graves** take twice as long to loot (5 graves per person per turn) and the looter saves **vs Death at +4** or catches fungal infection.

## Layout
- **Entrances:** (a) the sludge river from C2b ([[Nethercity C - Central Caves]]) over a ledge and steps into **L1**; (b) the lion-head bronze door south of **L9** to K1 ([[Nethercity K - Hallowed Hall of Heroes]]); (c) the secret drawbridge panel in **L13** to N8c and the heavy bronze, magic-locked door in **L20** to N1 ([[Nethercity N - Sub-Abyssal Alchemic Laboratory]]); (d) the steps up the ledge in **L19** to M1 ([[Nethercity M - Northern Caves]]); (e) the **L4 teleportation platform**, from a stone platform in the Istrith Forest.
- **Sludge arm (west):** L1 → steps down NW → L2 → stair down N into the sludge-flooded tunnel **L3** (8’ of sludge, sage slime on top) → stair up NW → **L4** (teleporter; dead end).
- **Central spine:** L1 → tunnel NE, slightly down → **L5** (triangular cave; sludge ends here). From L5: NW the dead-end finger **L6**; S the tunnel **L7** → **L9** → S 15’ to the K1 door, SW the dead-end side tunnel **L10**; N to the four-way **L8**.
- **L8:** W → **L11** (10’ drop at its north wall, steps in the east corner) → **L12** (guards' clover cave) → N tunnel → **L13** (the queen; secret door N to N8c). N → **L15** (farm). E → **L14** (farm), whose far side opens S-ward from **L16** (farm). L15 and L16 open N into **L17** (farm raided by apes; map).
- **North end:** L17 → N tunnel, ceiling rising → **L18** (alcove of the Poisoner, west side) → **L19** (shelf): W ledge steps up to M1; E a 12’ drop with stairs at its north end down to **L20** (pool; bronze door NE to N1).

## Factions
- **The giant ant colony** (24 workers in L5, L14, L15, L16; 4 guards in L12; the queen in L13; 18 eggs). Vermin, not a parleying faction. They farm fungus in the wall graves on organic refuse carried from the sludge; when the sludge runs thin they catch cavern locusts, millipedes and crayfish for the farms. **The whole of L is their nest:** they are automatically Hostile to intruders (MM nest defence) and fight at **ML +4 while the queen lives** (−1 once she is dead). Terrestrial mechanoreception: they feel footsteps through the rock at line-of-sight range and cannot be blinded; flying or levitating creatures go unfelt.
  - **Alarm:** in L5, three ants attack and three run for help to L15, L14 and L16. Each farm cave's six then come (Judge's call: 1d3+1 rounds later; SOTN gives no time), so up to 21 ants can converge on L5. Any attack on the queen in L13 brings **every surviving ant** to her.
  - **Eggs:** the 18 eggs in L13. Judge's call, from the MM nest rule: if one hatches, 1d20 — 1 queen, 2-6 soldier, 7+ worker. Losses: SOTN gives no rate (see "Replacement of casualties", [[SOTN Hub]]).
- **The white apes of M** ([[Cave Gorilla]], [[Nethercity M - Northern Caves]]): raid the ants' farms (40 of the L17 graves stripped), killed the two ants now risen in L18, and drink at the L20 pool. Ants and apes are enemies; a party can set one on the other. Away from their den apes have +1 on reaction rolls.
- **The troglodytes of I** ([[Nethercity I - Deep Level Southern Cave Complex]]): their forebears hunted through L4 for centuries until the ants came; their shaman (I13) still knows the "magic rock". See [[Nethercity Factions]].

## Rooms (SOTN pp. 94-100)

### L1. Abyssal Level Cave Entrance (2900 BE)
- **Exits:** SW ledge and steps up to C2b (the sludge comes in here); NW steps down to L2; NE a tunnel sloping gently down to L5.
- > [!player]
  > An hourglass-shaped cave 60’ long with a 20’ ceiling, about 30’ across at its widest and pinched to 15’ in the middle. Black sludge pours over the ledge and steps of the south-west wall, then runs down steps to the north-west and down a tunnel to the north-east. The air is thick and stinks of rotten eggs.
- **Trap:** the miasma is combustible (Running line): a torch or other flame anywhere over the sludge here, 1 in 6 each turn, 6d6 fireball centred on the bearer; setting the sludge alight or casting [[Fireball]], [[Wall of Flame]] or similar fire magic here, 6d6 fireball over all the sludge in L. (SOTN p. 94)

### L2. Sub-Abyssal Eastern Slimy Cave (2800 BE)
- **Exits:** SE steps up to L1; N a stair down to L3.
- > [!player]
  > A heart-shaped cave 30’ by 30’, reached down steps from the south-east. Ankle-deep sludge, the spill of years from above, covers almost all of it and flows on down a stair to the north. The air is hazy and so heavy it feels almost liquid; the reek makes your eyes stream.
- **Trap:** combustible miasma, as in L1. (SOTN p. 94)

### L3. Sub-Abyssal Eastern Slimy Tunnel (2800 BE)
- **Exits:** S stair up to L2; NW (40’ on) a stair rising out of the sludge to L4.
- > [!player]
  > Sludge pours down a roughly cut stair from the south into this tunnel; after a few steps the stair disappears under it. Forty feet to the north-west another stair climbs out. Between them the surface is covered by a slimy green film, so the depth cannot be seen. Now and then the film heaves, like a foul lung breathing the stinking air.
- **Monster:** the film is **4 patches of sage slime** (SOTN green slime) → [[Sage Slime]]: MV 1’ / 3’, AC n/a (always hit), HD 2****, hp 16, 12, 10, 7, engulf 9+, D: digests 1 st of worn armour or clothing per action, then 1d8 extraordinary acid each action (SOTN: metal in 6 rounds, flesh in 1-4 rounds), Save F1, ML n/a, AL N, XP 56 each (SOTN prints XP 38). Immune to everything but fire (and fire here is a disaster: see Trap); fire damage is shared with anyone engulfed; [[Cure Disease]] kills a patch outright. Each grows 1 hp per point of damage it deals and splits on doubling its hp. They came through the L4 platform and spread on the rich food: **once the party has met them, add another 10’ × 10’ patch every 3 days** until destroyed.
- **Trap:** combustible miasma, as in L1. **Any fire-based attack on the slime ignites the sludge: 6d6 fireball over all the sludge in L.**
- **Trick:** between the stairs the tunnel floor is 10’ below the floors of L2 and L4; only the first two steps at each end are above the sludge, which is **8’ deep**. To cross, characters hold their breath and walk the bottom, or swim through the sludge (and the slime). Either way the sludge is full of rot and disease: save **vs Death at +4** or catch fungal infection ([[Nethercity Features]]). (SOTN p. 95)

### L4. Abyssal Level Teleportation Platform (2800 BE)
- **Exits:** SE the stair down into L3 (only); the platform.
- > [!player]
  > At the top of the stair, north-west of the slimy tunnel, is a 30’ by 20’ cave. Its rock floor is strangely shiny, polished almost to the gleam of obsidian, and arcane runes are carved over every wall. The air here is lighter and easier to breathe.
- **Trick — the platform:**
  - Detect magic: the cave radiates **strong magic**.
  - **Anyone who steps into the cave is teleported at once** to a stone platform hidden in the wild **Istrith Forest**, many miles to the east. No throw, no save, no command word, no error; the Nethercity's teleportation ward ([[Nethercity Features]]) does not affect it. What the traveller carries goes too. It takes one creature or many: everyone who steps in goes.
  - **Return:** stepping **off** the forest platform and **back on** sends the traveller back here, into this cave. Judge's call: an arrival here is not sent off again until he leaves the cave and steps back in (mirroring the forest end).
  - **Only these two ends.** There is no other destination and no way to choose one.
  - **Where (Judge's call):** SOTN places it only "in the Istrith Forest, many miles to the east". In this campaign the Istrith lies east of the Krysivor (AX3, Borderlands Background; [[Turos Veren|Türos Veren]], hex 39.04, ferries into it). The Judge fixes the forest platform's hex the first time anyone uses it and records it; from there the way home is an ordinary wilderness journey. The forest end is a stone platform in deep woods: the "endless cave with no walls or ceiling" of the troglodyte legend is open sky.
  - **Traffic:** before the city's sewers broke into the Nethercity (c. 50 years ago), this platform was one of the only ways in or out. It still brings in strays: the sage slime of L3 came this way, and so does the songbird of the unsettling events (event 1). Anything that blunders onto the forest platform turns up here.
  - **Reading the room without entering** (Judge's call): the runes and the shine are visible from the top of the L3 stair; a careful party can study the cave from there.
- **Lore:** Loremastery or Collegiate Wizardry: the runes belong to movement magic.
- **Lore:** the forebears of the troglodytes (I) and the white apes (M) used the platform for centuries for hunting trips, until the giant ants arrived after the sludge flood and ended it. Only the **troglodyte shaman (I13)** and the **grey white ape (M14)** still remember it. Given a way to talk ([[Tongues]], [[Speak with Beasts]]), they may tell of the "magic rock" that carries brave hunters to and from "an endless cave with no walls or ceiling". (SOTN pp. 95, 97)

### L5. Abyssal Level Central Cave (2800 BE)
- **Exits:** SW tunnel up to L1 (the sludge comes in); NW to L6; N to L8; S to L7.
- > [!player]
  > A triangular cave 50’ by 50’. Sludge from the south-west spreads over about half the floor and peters out. Tunnels leave to the north-west, north and south. Dozens of horizontal grave niches are cut into the rock, and six ants as big as men are carrying sludge to them. The air is hazy and heavy with rotten eggs.
- **Monster:** **6 giant ants** → [[Giant Ant Worker]]: MV 60’ / 180’, AC 6, HD 4, hp 21, 20, 18, 16, 14, 13, bite 7+, D 2d6, Save F2, ML −1 (+4 while the queen lives; SOTN prints ML +4), AL N, XP 80 each. Three attack; the other three run to L15, L14 and L16 for reinforcements (see Factions).

- **Graves:** 36, c. 2800 BE, usual inscriptions, remains and goods: expected total 72gp (1d3, avg 2gp each). The ants are turning them into farms (not yet farms: normal looting). (SOTN p. 97)

### L13. Sub-Abyssal Cave of the Queen (2500 BE)
- **Exits:** S tunnel to L12; secret door N to N8c ([[Nethercity N - Sub-Abyssal Alchemic Laboratory]]).
- > [!player]
  > An oval cavern 30’ wide and 20’ long, its walls pierced by 18 upright grave niches. In each sits a glistening yellow egg. Squatting in the middle like a titan of chitin is the giant ant that must have laid them: 15’ long.
- **Monster:** **giant ant queen** → [[Giant Ant Queen]]: MV 40’ / 120’, AC 8, HD 6, hp 47, bite 5+, D 3d8 extraordinary, Save F3, ML +1 (+4 in nest defence), AL N, XP 320 (SOTN prints MV 180’, AC 6, HD 10, bite 2+, F5, XP 850; the MM queen is 7’-9’ long, SOTN's 15’). Judge's call: if the Judge wants SOTN's titan instead, use HD 10, bite 1+, Save F5, XP 850 with the MM queen's other traits. She attacks anything that comes near; **every surviving ant in L comes to her aid**.
- **Graves and Loot:** 18 vertical wall graves with the usual inscriptions and goods (expected 81gp, 4.5gp each), but each holds an **ant egg** instead of remains. Each egg is about 6” tall, worth **115gp**, weighs **1 st** (the MM's eggs weigh 2/6 st; SOTN's figure used). The book's room total counts the eggs only: **2,070gp, 18 st**.
- **Trick:** a **secret door** to N8c hides in the plaster panelling of one of the vertical graves (Searching). Found, it opens by pulling the panel down like a drawbridge. (SOTN p. 98)

### L18. Abyssal Level Poisoner's Alcove (2400 BE)
- **Exits:** S to L17; N to L19 (the alcove is in the tunnel's west wall).
- > [!player]
  > The tunnel's ceiling has climbed to 20’, rippled frozen lava with a dripping stalactite here and there. In the west wall a rough alcove 15’ deep and 30’ wide holds a huge relief of a giant scorpion stabbing the sun with its sting, painted a reddish black. Two maimed, rotting giant ants walk slow circles in front of it.
- **Lore:** Loremastery or Theology: the carving is **Galmorm**, the Poisoner, Chthonic god of lies and treachery.
- **Trick:** the paint was blood from a sacrifice: the alcove is a **blighted sinkhole of evil** ([[Nethercity Features]]). Lawful divine casters turn undead as if **four levels lower** here (with the other blighted-sinkhole effects).
- **Monster:** two ants killed here lately by the white apes have risen as **2 giant ant zombies**, made poisonous by the Poisoner → [[Zombie]] (non-human zombie of a [[Giant Ant Worker]]: HD = living HD + 1): MV 30’ / 90’, AC 6 (the ant's chitin; SOTN), HD 5**, hp 23, 22, bite 6+, D 2d6 extraordinary + **poison** (save vs Death or die after 1 turn), Save F3, ML n/a, AL C, XP 500 each. Clumsy (−2 initiative); immune to enchantment, necrotic and poisonous damage; rebuked as easily as any zombie whatever its HD. (SOTN p. 99)

### L20. Sub-Abyssal North Eastern Cave (2200 BE)
- **Exits:** W the stairs up the cliff to L19; NE a heavy bronze door to N1 ([[Nethercity N - Sub-Abyssal Alchemic Laboratory]]).
- > [!player]
  > A triangular cave 45’ by 30’. Its western side is a 10’ cliff whose northern part is a curving staircase cut into the rock. A heavy bronze door is set in the north-east face. Along the south-east face lies a pool of dark water.
- **Monster:** the pool is the white apes' main water. Each turn the party stays, throw 1d6: on **5+ three white apes** come to drink → [[Cave Gorilla]] (white apes): MV 40’ / 120’, AC 3, HD 4, hp 16 each, 2 claws 7+ (1d4/1d4) or thrown rock 7+ (1d6), Save F2, ML −1, AL N, XP 80 each. +1 on reaction rolls away from their den; pelts as in [[Nethercity Features]].
- **Trick:** the bronze door is **permanently locked by magic** and radiates magic. It unlocks by itself when "approved personnel" come within 5’: any **elf showing a Chthonic holy symbol**, anyone wearing the **shadowcowl robe** (J14; [[SOTN Magic Items#Shadowcowl Robe]]) or an **iron mask** (J15; [[SOTN Magic Items#Iron Masks of Cyfaraun]]), or anyone bearing the **sword of command** (Q5; [[SOTN Magic Items#Sword of Command]]). Otherwise *knock* opens it for one turn (Judge's call: no ACKS II equivalent of *knock*) and [[Dispel Magic]] at caster level **11 or higher** unlocks it for good. Judge's call: as a magic-locked portal ([[Magic Lock]]) it can also be broken by force as a heavy bronze door (−4), the ACKS II remedy; SOTN offers only the two spells.
- **Lore:** Caving, examining the pool: the water wells up from an underground spring; at this depth the caves may be near the water table. (SOTN pp. 99-100)
