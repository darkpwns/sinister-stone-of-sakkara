# Nethercity J — Doom Temple of Dirgion

A temple of Dirgion the Doombringer, Chthonic god of doom, death and undeath, built in **2300 BE** (every room) off the troglodyte caves of area I: antechamber and martyr's tomb, a narthex with a purification font, a nave and colonnaded templon, the chancel with its jewelled throne, two side sanctuaries, and up a stair the sacrarium, vestry, sacristy and reliquary. Grey marble walls speckled with black and green, black marble floors, all cracked and heaved by old quakes. The temple's defences are religious: **purification** at the narthex font (J4) or the sacrarium font (J11) lets a creature pass the shadows and, with the right robe, the relic guardians. The troglodytes of I still come to the J4 font for water. Level: J's headings name none; it opens off I7 at the Deep Level, and J11-J15 lie 10’ higher up the J10 stair (Judge's call). The **chancel J8 is a blighted sinkhole of evil** (around the throne-altar). No light anywhere (Judge's call: the text mentions none). Hub [[SOTN Hub]]; doors, sinkholes, mummies and treasure conventions in [[Nethercity Features]]. Pages are printed pages.

Running: Temple / Tomb column, `level: 4` — 1d6 every 3 turns, 6+ → 2d10 on `Tables/sotn-wandering-temple-tomb.yaml`. **Water-bearers** while the troglodytes of I stand: in J1 and J4, 1d6 each turn (J4: each turn the party lingers), 5+ → 5 breeders with amphorae (see J1). **J9:** playing the stone organ triggers an encounter throw. **Purification:** a creature washing at J4 or J11 is blessed or cursed for **6 turns** (see J4); while it lasts it is spared by the shadows of J5 (J4's font) or of J10 and the jackals of J15 (J11's font, J15 only with the shadowcowl robe). The J2 mummy wakes by the slab-door rule ([[Nethercity Features#Doors (SOTN p. 11)]]).

## Layout
- **Entrances:** (a) the heavy bronze door (death's-head handle) in the south wall of **I7**, standing partly open, into **J1**; (b) the secret tunnel **J7**, whose north end is a hatch at the back of a wall grave in **I9** ([[Nethercity I - Deep Level Southern Cave Complex]]).
- **West (tomb):** J1 → west stone slab door (polished smooth by countless hands) → martyr tomb **J2** → far-wall stone slab door → vault **J3**.
- **Temple axis (west to east):** J1 → south cedar door, partly open → a 15’ walkway → narthex **J4** (font) → four marble steps down east → nave **J5** → 15’ arcade → templon **J6** → ambon of four steps up → chancel **J8** (dais, two more steps, the apse and throne).
- **Off the templon:** north wall, a half-height cedar door to **J9** (organ) and, hidden in the north mosaic, the secret door to **J7** (→ I9); south wall, a half-height cedar door to **J10**.
- **Upper rooms (10’ up):** J10 → marble stairs up 10’ through its east wall → the sacrarium **J11** (south, the second font) and the vestry **J12** (east) (map) → north cedar door → gallery **J13** (15’ × 40’, running north): east wall, locked cedar door to the sacristy **J14** 10’ in, and 20’ further the locked, trapped door to the reliquary **J15**.

## Rooms (SOTN pp. 86-90)

### J1. Doom Temple Antechamber (2300 BE)
- **Exits:** N the heavy bronze door (partly open) to I7; S a cedar door, black marble frame, death's-head handles, partly open, to the walkway and J4; W a stone slab door (worn smooth) to J2.
- > [!player]
  > Beyond the great bronze door is an antechamber 20’ wide and 30’ long with a 15’ ceiling. Grey marble panels speckled with black and green line the walls; the floor is black marble, split and heaved by some old quake. In the south wall a cedar door framed in black marble, with death's-head handles, stands ajar. In the west wall a stone slab door, its face polished smooth as if by the touch of countless hands over centuries.
- **Monster:** the troglodytes' water run (font in J4). If the party got here without beating the troglodytes, throw **1d6 each turn; 5+:** **5 breeders** arrive, each with a terracotta amphora (20gp, 1 st), going to J4.
  - **5 troglodyte breeders** → [[Troglodyte]] (females, fight as [[Hobgoblin]]): MV 40’ / 120’ (SOTN 120’), AC 3, HD 1+1, hp 6 each, 2 claws/bite 9+, D 1d2 each, F1, ML 0 (+1 while their chieftain lives), AL C, XP 15 each. Chameleon scales (−2 to others' surprise rolls); stench (within 5’: Death save at −2 or [[Queasy (Condition)|queasy]]; SOTN save v. Poison or −2 to attack throws). (The same five as I7's and J4's: one group per trip, Judge's call.)

### J2. Doom Temple Martyr Tomb (2300 BE)
- **Exits:** E stone slab door to J1; W (far wall) stone slab door to J3.
- > [!player]
  > A tomb 15’ by 15’, grey and black marble walls and a black marble floor. In the middle stands a black marble sarcophagus carved in the perfect likeness of a grim-faced elf patriarch. In the far wall, another stone slab door.
- **Monster:** a **mummy cultist of Dirgion**, the martyr, infested with **10 sepulchral worms**. He starts to wake when the J1 slab door is first disturbed; depending on how long the party took over that door, he is rising or fully roused when they come in. **Given time:** [[Strengthen the Unliving]] on six of his worms (each gains **1d10+6** temporary hp; 6 HD of undead at caster level 6; SOTN's new spell, now in the RR), then [[Energy Protection]] (fire) on himself (SOTN resist fire).
  - **Mummy cultist of Dirgion** → [[Martyr of the Doom Temple (SOTN)]] ([[Mummy]] + [[Elven Cultist]] 6 of Dirgion): MV 20’ / 60’ (SOTN 60’), AC 6, **HD 6+1*****, hp 28, slam **4+** (SOTN prints 5+; 6+1 HD gives 4+, as SOTN's H22 cultist), D 1d12 + mummy rot, **F6** (SOTN prints F5 here, F6 for the like mummy in H22), ML +4, AL C, XP 1,280. Horrific visage (Paralysis save or cower), mummy rot, immunities as the MM mummy. Class abilities (SOTN pp. 163-164): commands undead as a 6th-level Chaotic caster ([[Crusader]] table, Judge's call; 1 day per level when it succeeds: lordship of the undead); **inexorable** (immune to all fear); **inspire dread** (1 round of oration: foes within 50’ take −1 to attack throws, damage, morale and saves against magical fear for 1 turn, Spells save resists; each foe at most once a day per level, i.e. 6 times). Spells per day 3 1st, 3 2nd, 1 3rd, 1 4th, from the 6th-level cultist of Dirgion repertoire (SOTN p. 164).
  - **10 sepulchral worms** → [[Sepulchral Worm]]: AC 3, HD 1**, hp 4 each (+1d10+6 temporary on six if strengthened), bite 10+, D 1d4 + mummy rot, F1 (SOTN F4), ML n/a, AL C, XP 16 each. Each bites a living target within 15’ on the host's initiative. Horror save against the host: −1 per 3 worms, so **−3** (SOTN: −1 per worm). Holy water destroys one.
- **Loot:** none here (his goods are in J3). The black marble sarcophagus is not valued.

### J3. Doom Temple Tomb Vault (2300 BE)
- **Exits:** E stone slab door to J2.
- > [!player]
  > A vault 15’ by 10’. A canopic shrine of gilded cedar decorated with gold skulls stands here, a small black box on top of it. Tapestries too musty and rotten to be worth anything and dozens of pottery shards lie scattered about, but one large terracotta funeral urn painted with black vultures is intact.
- **Loot:** the **canopic shrine** (1,275gp, 7 st) holding the mummy's **4 terracotta canopic jars** of embalmed organs (25gp, 1/6 st each); the black box is an **ebony jewellery box with a jet scarab** (125gp, 1/6 st) holding **3 moonstones** (50gp each) and a **silver ring set with amber** (300gp); the **funeral urn** (50gp, 3/6 st) holds **750gp**. **Tally: 2,750gp, 8 2/6 st.**

### J4. Doom Temple Narthex (2300 BE)
- **Exits:** N the 15’ walkway and cedar door to J1; E four great marble steps down into J5.
- > [!player]
  > Past the cedar door a walkway 15’ by 15’ leads into the narthex. Its west wall curves round under a half-dome 15’ up. At the exact centre stands a round basin of black marble full of water, with a line of ancient script carved on it. To the east the floor drops by four great marble steps into a broad nave.
- **Monster:** the troglodytes' font. If the party slipped in without beating them, throw **1d6 each turn they linger; 5+:** **5 breeders** with amphorae come for water (statline in J1).
- **Lore / Trick:** Ancient Argollëan readers: "Wash yourself of shame before stepping into the sacred halls." The basin refills forever (a permanent *create water*, [[Create Water]]). **A creature that purifies itself here:** Chaotic → the benefit of *bless* (Judge's call: as [[Holy Blessing|unholy blessing]]: +1 to attack throws, AC, morale rolls and saves against magical fear); Lawful or Neutral → the reverse, *bane* (Judge's call: −1 to the same; ACKS II has no bane). Either lasts **6 turns**, and for that time the purified creature can cross the nave **J5** without the shadows attacking it. The protection works in J5 only.

### J5. Doom Temple Nave (2300 BE)
- **Exits:** W four steps up to J4; E a 15’ by 15’ arcade into J6.
- > [!player]
  > A nave 25’ long and 35’ wide, separated by a 15’ arcade from a colonnaded hall to the east. Cedar pews trimmed with bronze once filled it; nearly all have fallen over. Here and there the black marble floor is torn open as if by ancient tremors.
- **Monster / Trick:** when the party enters, **8 shadows** come out of the templon (J6). They attack every creature that has **not** purified itself at the J4 font (within its 6 turns).
  - **8 shadows** → [[Shadow]]: MV 30’ / 90’ (SOTN 90’), AC 2, HD 2+2***, hp 15, 13, 12, 11, 11, 10, 10, 7, enervating touch 8+, D 1d4 extraordinary necrotic + [[Enervated (Condition)|enervation]] (max hp lowered by the damage), F2, ML +4, AL C, XP 71 each (SOTN prints HD 2+2**, XP 59, and a touch draining 1 STR for 8 turns, a victim at 0 STR becoming a shadow; ACKS II's enervation replaces the STR drain). Silent, −3 to others' surprise rolls; immune to enchantment, poison and all mundane damage (silver deals extraordinary); an incarnation, not undead (MM).

### J6. Doom Temple Templon (2300 BE)
- **Exits:** W the 15’ arcade to J5; E an ambon of four marble steps up to J8; N an arched doorway (half-height cedar door) and windows to J9; S the same to J10; secret door in the north mosaic to J7.
- > [!player]
  > A templon 80’ long and 30’ wide, its 20’ ceiling held up by three pairs of fluted columns. Two columns are visibly cracked and the ceiling mortar is loose and strained in many places. Superb mosaics of glass and coloured stone cover the long walls: on the north, a grey-robed god hurling terrified humans into a sea of fire; on the south, elven dead rising in hosts from earthen graves. Beyond the mosaics, arched doorways with half-height cedar doors and open windows show a small room on each side, north and south. West, the arcade to the nave; east, four marble steps up to the chancel.
- **Lore:** Loremastery or Theology: the north mosaic shows Dirgion, the Doombringer, Chthonic god of doom, death and undeath; the south one the **Awakening**, the promised end times of the Chthonic faith.
- **Trick:** a secret door in the **north mosaic** (Searching): a 5’ section opens onto a short 10’ tunnel (J7).

### J7. Doom Temple Secret Tunnel (2300 BE)
- **Exits:** S secret door (pushes out) into J6; N secret hatch (pulls open like a lid) into the back of a wall grave in I9.
- > [!player]
  > A tunnel 15’ long and 10’ wide with several bronze pipes set into its wall. Both ends are blank, smooth stone.
- **Trick:** sounds made into the pipes carry into the nave J5 and templon J6 at volumes from a whisper to a boom; the priests of Dirgion used them during services.
- **Trick:** a secret door at each end (see Exits).
- **Trick / Loot:** a **bone key** is hidden in the largest pipe: found automatically by anyone who blows or speaks into the pipe, otherwise by Searching (SOTN "detect traps"). It opens the doors of the sacristy **J14** and reliquary **J15**.

### J8. Doom Temple Chancel (2300 BE)
- **Exits:** W the four-step ambon down to J6.
- > [!player]
  > Four marble steps lead up to a dais 30’ long and 25’ wide with 5’ transepts on each side; two fluted columns hold the ceiling 20’ above. Two more steps rise to the chancel, a round apse 25’ wide and 15’ deep, where a gilded throne of acacia wood studded with gems stands at the far side. Two fluted columns climb to the half-dome 25’ up. Above the throne a great crack runs up the east wall to the ceiling, which has partly fallen in, scattering glass, beads and coloured stones everywhere; of the mosaic they made, you can only just make out the spread fingers of a man's hand reaching over the chancel.
- **Trick:** the throne (cathedra) is a **chaotic altar**: the chancel is a **blighted sinkhole of evil** ([[Nethercity Features#Sinkholes of evil (SOTN p. 12; ACKS II sinkholes of evil, RR)]]). Lawful divine casters turn (ACKS II: rebuke) undead **as if four levels lower** here.
- **Monster / Trick:** an **incarnation of terror** lurks unseen in the chancel wall. It emerges and attacks if an **unpurified** creature (not washed at J4) comes **within 15’ of the throne**, or if **anyone** prises its jewels.
  - **Incarnation of terror** → [[Incarnation of Terror (SOTN)]]: MV 120’, AC 12, HD 12***, hp 54, touch −1+ (12 HD), D 1d10 + special, Save M12 (as printed), ML +4, XP 3,900; bone-chilling touch, dreadful to behold, soul-chilling cry, immune to ordinary weapons, undead immunities (details in its note).
- **Trap (pressure plate):** under the throne; pressed down by **100 lb or more** on the throne (someone sitting on it). Found by Searching (SOTN "detect traps"); a Trapbreaking throw jams it **up or down** (thief's choice). **While it is pressed down, the dart trap on the J15 door is off.**
- **Loot:** the mosaic's glass and stones are worthless; the throne bears **20 red garnets and 20 black garnets, 250gp each**. **Tally: 10,000gp** (prising them wakes the incarnation).

### J9. Doom Temple Northern Sanctuary (2300 BE)
- **Exits:** S half-height cedar door and two windows onto J6.
- > [!player]
  > A side room 15’ by 10’ with a half-height cedar door and two open windows giving a clear view of the templon. Old bronze-and-cedar seats, dusty and untouched, face the openings. A stone pipe organ is built into the east wall.
- **Trick:** no monsters or treasure; **playing the organ triggers an encounter throw**.

### J10. Doom Temple Southern Sanctuary (2300 BE)
- **Exits:** N half-height cedar door and two windows onto J6; E marble stairs up 10’ (to J11 and J12).
- > [!player]
  > A side room 15’ by 15’ with a half-height cedar door and two open windows onto the templon, and dusty old bronze-and-cedar seats facing them. In the east wall an opening, and marble stairs going up 10’.
- **Monster / Trick:** **6 shadows** lurk here. They stay hidden from characters coming in from J6, but **attack at once any who come back down the stairs without having purified themselves at the sacrarium J11** (SOTN prints "Sacrarium (J12)"; the sacrarium is J11).
  - **6 shadows** → [[Shadow]]: as J5, hp 14, 12, 11, 10, 10, 8, XP 71 each (SOTN 59).

### J11. Doom Temple Sacrarium (2300 BE)
- **Exits:** to the top of the J10 stairs and on to J12 (map).
- > [!player]
  > A sacrarium 15’ by 10’, with crown mouldings of ebony and ivory at floor and ceiling, grey and black marble walls and a black marble floor. Near the south wall a white marble font stands on the rotted, tattered remains of rich red brocade. Ancient script is carved above it.
- **Lore / Trick:** Ancient Argollëan readers: "The purified body does not putrefy with death." A second permanent [[Create Water]] font. **Purification as at J4** (Chaotic blessed, Lawful and Neutral cursed, 6 turns). While it lasts the creature passes **J10** without the shadows attacking, and, **if wearing the shadowcowl robe** from J14, can take items from **J15** without the jackals attacking. The protection works in J10 and J15 only.

### J12. Doom Temple Vestry (2300 BE)
- **Exits:** W to J11 and the J10 stairs (map); N a closed cedar door to J13.
- > [!player]
  > A vestry 15’ by 10’, ebony and ivory mouldings, grey and black marble walls, black marble floor. A fine table has fallen and shattered, and the grey robes that hung on the wall pegs lie heaped on the floor. A cedar door in the north wall is shut.
- **Loot:** the grey robes are rotten and worthless.

### J13. Doom Temple Upper Gallery (2300 BE)
- **Exits:** S cedar door to J12; E (10’ in) locked cedar door to J14; E (20’ further) locked, trapped cedar door to J15.
- > [!player]
  > The cedar door opens on a gallery 15’ wide and 40’ long, grey and black marble walls, black marble floor. Ten feet along, a closed cedar door in the east wall; twenty feet beyond it, another.
- **Trick:** both doors are **locked**: the J7 bone key opens them, or a Lockpicking throw.
- **Trap (J15 door):** Searching (SOTN "detect traps") notices **six thumb-sized holes** in its threshold. Opening the door while the trap is active fires **6 poison darts** at whoever opened it: **attack throw 10+ each**, **1d4+1** damage per hit and a **Death save** (SOTN Poison) per hit; each failed save adds **2d12** damage and **2d4 turns** of [[Paralyzed (Condition)|paralysis]] (damage and durations stack). Disarmed automatically while **100 lb or more** presses the plate under the J8 throne; otherwise a Trapbreaking throw plugs the holes solidly.

### J14. Doom Temple Sacristy (2300 BE)
- **Exits:** W locked cedar door to J13.
- > [!player]
  > A room 15’ wide and 10’ deep with grey and black marble walls. Five terracotta scroll jars along the east wall have fallen over, spilling old scrolls across the black marble floor. Four cedar chests stand along the south wall.
- **Loot:** the 5 **scroll jars** (5gp, 1/6 st each) and the **religious record scrolls** (675gp, 1 st). The four chests are **locked** (key or Lockpicking):
  1. black and red altar linens, mouldy, worthless;
  2. temple hangings with a skull inside a grey cowl: Loremastery or Theology identifies the holy symbol of Dirgion, the Doombringer;
  3. eighteen grey-cowled robes, all rotten but one that has lasted the ages: a **[[SOTN Magic Items#Shadowcowl Robe|shadowcowl robe]]** (with J11's purification, lets its wearer loot J15 unharmed);
  4. **20 sticks of frankincense** (20gp each) and **2 scented wooden boxes of myrrh** (25gp, 1/6 st each).
  
  **Tally: 1,150gp, 2/6 st** (as printed; the listed weights add up to about 2 1/6 st: jars 5/6, scrolls 1, myrrh 2/6).
- **Lore:** Ancient Argollëan readers of the record scrolls find a list of the Hierophants of the Secret Mysteries in order: Garbhan, Naomhan, Mairenn, Eoghan, Anya, Urard and **Orthanach**. Knowing Orthanach's name lets characters bypass certain traps in the secret tomb of the Hierophant ([[Nethercity O - Sub-Abyssal Secret Tomb of the Hierophant]]).

### J15. Doom Temple Reliquary (2300 BE)
- **Exits:** W locked, trapped cedar door to J13.
- > [!player]
  > A room 15’ wide and 10’ deep with grey and black marble walls. Brackets for long-gone shelves line the walls; the shelves have collapsed, spilling golden temple relics and broken glass across the black marble floor. In the middle a chest of ebony and redwood stands between two life-sized statues of black jackals.
- **Monster / Trick:** the statues are **2 jackals of darkness**. They attack any trespasser, preferably whoever the dart trap paralysed first. A character purified at **J11** and wearing the **shadowcowl robe** from J14 can enter and take anything without fear of them, even if he sets off the trap.
  - **2 jackals of darkness** → [[Jackal of Darkness (SOTN)]]: MV 140’, AC 5, HD 4*, hp 20, 18, bite 7+, D 1d6, F4, ML +4, AL C, XP 135 each (ACKS II 4*, see its note; SOTN prints 4** and 285 here, 190 in its appendix); **black fire** (wreathes one foe within 50’, 1 hp per round), undead immunities (details in its note).
- **Loot (relics):** a gold libation bowl (400gp, 1/6 st), a gold basin (2,500gp, 1 st), 2 gold incense dishes (100gp each), a gold thurible (**[[SOTN Magic Items#Censer of Prophetic Dreams|censer of prophetic dreams]]**, apparent value 400gp), 4 gold goblets set with obsidian and red garnets (300gp each), a sculpted gold skull mask (1,000gp), and a sculpted iron mask (**[[SOTN Magic Items#Iron Masks of Cyfaraun|Iron Mask of Cyfaraun]]**, Dirgion's, apparent value 200gp; 2 st carried). In the broken glass one vial of clear, bubbling liquid survived: a **[[Potion of Freedom]]**.
- **Loot (chest):** the **ebony and redwood chest** (600gp, 4 st) holds **2,000ep, 500gp** and a **locked bronze box** with **3 polished rubies** (1,000gp each) and two oilcloth bundles: a bone wand with a black skull carved on one end (**[[Wand of Fear]]**, 11 charges) and an obsidian ring set with bloodstone (**[[Ring of Life Protection]]**, apparent value 300gp).
- **Tally: 10,400gp, 6 4/6 st** (magic excluded).
