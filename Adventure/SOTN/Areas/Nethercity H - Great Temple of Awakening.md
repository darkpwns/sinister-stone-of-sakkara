# Nethercity H — Great Temple of Awakening

The great temple-necropolis of the Awakening, begun when open Chthonic worship took hold and finished around **2500 BE**, with later tombs and annexes added in 2400, 2300 and 2200 BE. Its builders believed the sleeping Chthonic gods would one day rise and that their faithful dead would rise with them; the temple is both church and dormitory for that day. Construction is mostly plaster over living rock with black marble floors and painted frescoes; the later tombs (H16, H21, H22) are lined with marble and mosaics. Its heart is the **Grand Gallery H14**, a vast hall whose walls hold 480 armed skeletons and whose ceiling glows with a star map that foretells the Conjunction of the Void. Its mummies are of the 2500-2200 BE eras: 6+1 HD mummies keeping half their living class abilities (5+1 HD for the 2200 BE retainer in H25). Pages are printed pages; "(map)" = read from the map, pp. 182-183. Hub [[SOTN Hub]]; general rules (doors, torpor, inscriptions, sarcophagi, wall graves and their goods, mummy reactions, armed mummies, treasure) in [[Nethercity Features]]; background in [[Nethercity History]].

**Levels:** the temple is entered from the **Lower Level** cave C9. An upper storey (H1-H9, H12-H13 and the curving corridor H3) sits level with the two balconies; the lower storey (the floor of H14 and H10b-H25) lies about 20’ lower, reached by the stair from H5 into H14 and by the ramps H9 → H10a → H10b. From the lower storey the slope H23-H24 and the secret stair H26 descend to the **Sub-Abyssal** river (P5, P7). SOTN names no level for area H itself. **Sinkhole:** the Grand Gallery H14 is a **blighted sinkhole of evil**: Lawful divine casters turn undead there as if 4 class levels lower. **Light:** the Grand Gallery is dimly lit by its glowing star mosaic (seen from the balconies H5 and H9, and as a ruddy glow from the side corridor off H3b); everywhere else is dark.

Running: Temple / Tomb column, `level: 4`: throw 1d6 every 3 turns, 6+ → 2d10 on `Tables/sotn-wandering-temple-tomb.yaml` (SOTN p. 18). **Torpor:** every tomb's undead begin to wake when its stone slab door is first disturbed (H6, H7-H8, H13, H16, H21, H22); how far they have roused depends on how long the party took to open the door ([[Nethercity Features]]). H25's mummy wakes when the tomb is disturbed. **Purification:** characters who wash at the font H2 pass the iron statue H3d unmolested while the effect lasts (6 turns), in area H only. **The Grand Gallery H14:** disturbing the star mosaic or any wall grave raises its 4 chieftains and 2d20 skeletons a round, up to 480.

## Factions
No organised group holds area H; its intelligent inhabitants are seven solitary mummies who wake one tomb at a time. Each first reacts with **wrath**; spared or fled from, roll its reaction on the mummy reaction table ([[Nethercity Features]]) and read it through its life: the princess (H6) is vain and seductive, the warlord (H8) a soldier of the Bitter War against the Meniri dwarves, the courtier (H13) a trickster who would rather lure intruders away than fight, the astrologer (H16) obsessed with the Conjunction, the inquisitor (H21) a fanatic who burned the Empyrean temples, the embalmer (H22) a priest of Dirgion. **The embalmer (H22, 2300 BE)** is the mummy a lonely 2300 BE mummy elsewhere seeks out (e.g. the Doom Temple martyr J2, [[Nethercity J - Doom Temple of Dirgion]]). The guardians of H14 are mindless until disturbed. Chaotic characters purified at H2 are the temple's own faithful in its eyes (the statue lets them pass). See [[Nethercity Factions]].

### H2. Great Temple Purification Font (2500 BE)
- **Exits:** cedar door to the upper end of H1.
- > [!player]
  > A plastered room 15’ × 25’ with a black marble floor. Set into the east wall is a font of bronze and black marble brimming with clear, clean water, with a line of ancient script cut above it.
- **Lore / Trick:** read by a character fluent in Ancient Argollëan: "Cleanse yourself of unbelief if you would Awaken" (sense). The font refills itself forever (a permanent [[Create Water]]). Drinking only quenches thirst. **Washing in it:** a **Chaotic** character is **blessed** as by [[Holy Blessing]] (+1 to attack throws, AC, morale and saves against magical fear; 6 turns). A **Lawful or Neutral** character suffers SOTN's "bane", the reverse (Judge's call: ACKS II's reverse, unholy blessing, is not a curse, so apply −1 to attack throws, AC, morale and saves against magical fear for 6 turns). **While either effect lasts** the washed character is "purified": the iron statue H3d lets him pass. The protection ends outside area H.
