# Nethercity D — Warlord's Tomb and Chapel to Bel

The tomb complex of **Eolande**, the most celebrated elven general of the 30th century BE and the first elven noble taught by the Great Teacher ([[Nethercity History]]), with a shrine and chapel to **Bel, the Slaughterprince**, Chthonic god of war, and two galleries of skeletal soldiers guarding a hidden hoard (all 2900 BE). Beyond them, the **Upper Level** grand balcony, staircase and common tomb (D7-D9, 2700 BE) overlook the huge deep cavern C2b and lead west into area E. Rock-hewn rooms with black granite or black marble tile; D9 is untiled volcanic rock. D1-D6 carry no level name in SOTN; they lie at the level of A4 and of the D7 balcony, 20’ above the cavern floor (Judge's call: Upper Level). **Blighted sinkhole:** D5 (the chapel altar). **Light:** none; the area is dark. Hub [[SOTN Hub]]; standing rules (doors, wall graves, skeleton warriors, mummies, treasure) in [[Nethercity Features]].

Running: wandering throws 1d6 every 3 turns, 6+ → 2d10 on the Temple / Tomb column, `Tables/sotn-wandering-temple-tomb.yaml` (level 4). The **troglodyte hunting party** in D8 watches anyone on the D7 balcony; Eolande wakes when the D1-D2 slab is first disturbed; the 48 gallery skeletons wake if a grave is disturbed or the D6b alcove is opened; the gargoyle of Bel wakes for Lawful characters at its dais; the jackals of darkness for chapel robbers.

## Layout
- **Entrances:** (a) the heavy bronze door in the north-east wall of A4 (Warlord's Cavern Entrance, [[Nethercity A - Entrance Caves]]) into **D1**; (b) from the huge deep cavern C2b ([[Nethercity C - Central Caves]]): the stair along its north wall up 20’ onto the **D7** balcony, or the curved staircase up into **D8**; (c) from area E: the cedar door at the east end of E6a into **D9** ([[Nethercity E - Deep Level Central Complex]]).
- **Warlord's tomb (D1-D6):** D1 → stone slab door NW → **D2** (Eolande); D1 → cedar door SE → **D3** (annex). D3 has three cedar doors: NW back to D1, NE to **D4** (shrine), SE to **D6a**. D4 → cedar door in the middle of its NW wall → **D5** (chapel). The gallery **D6a → D6b (alcove) → D6c** runs south-east to a cedar door onto the **D7** balcony.
- **Upper Level (D7-D9):** the **D7** balcony crosses C2b north-south, 20’ above the sludge; at its south end **D8**, a gallery with a curved stair down to C2b and an arched portal south into **D9**, whose cedar door (west wall, near the NW corner) opens on **E6a**.
- **Troglodyte trail:** D8 – D9 – E6a – E6b – E2b – E2c – E4a – E4c – I1, the hunters' way home to their lair ([[Nethercity I - Deep Level Southern Cave Complex]]).

## Factions
- **Troglodytes** (the tribe whose lair is beyond I1, [[Nethercity I - Deep Level Southern Cave Complex]]; see [[Nethercity Factions]]): a hunting party of six comes up through E and D9 to spear locusts from the balcony. They judge the party's strength before acting: against a weak party, darts from cover; against a strong one, they slip away home to warn the tribe and gather warriors. Their trail is followable (Tracking, below).
- **Eolande** is no faction, but a woken mummy left alive or parleyed with follows `Tables/sotn-mummy-reaction.yaml` ([[Nethercity Features]]); he despises humans as the "primitives" of his reliefs.

## Rooms (SOTN pp. 33-37)

### D1. Warlord's Cenotaph (2900 BE)
- **Exits:** heavy bronze door to A4 (entry); stone slab door NW to D2; fretted cedar door SE to D3.
- > [!player]
  > A 30’ × 40’ hall floored in black granite tile. Carved reliefs on the north-west and south-east walls show elven soldiers triumphing over primitive humans; the north-east wall shows an elven warlord in a chariot among cheering crowds, with an inscription cut beneath. A stone slab door leads north-west; a fretted cedar door leads south-east.
- **Lore:** read in Ancient Argollëan, the inscription says "The Triumph of Eolande". A Loremastery or Knowledge (history) proficiency throw identifies Eolande as the most celebrated elven general of the 30th century BE.
- **Door:** the D2 slab is a stone slab door (−12, up to 3 helpers); each attempt advances Eolande's waking ([[Nethercity Features#Doors (SOTN p. 11)|doors]]).

### D2. Warlord's Tomb (2900 BE)
- **Exits:** stone slab door to D1 only.
- > [!player]
  > A narrow 15’ × 20’ tomb cut from the living rock. Reliefs on the walls tell the life and deeds of a great elven warrior. On a low stone dais in the middle stands a limestone sarcophagus carved in the likeness of the warlord from the reliefs.
- **Monster:** **Eolande, mummy** → [[Eolande (SOTN)]] ([[Mummy]], armed): STR 18; MV 20’ / 60’, AC 6, HD 5+1**, hp 31, slam 2+, D 1d12+3 + mummy rot, or **short sword +1** 1+ (−1+ vs humans), D 1d6+4 (1d6+6 vs humans), no rot by weapon, F5, ML +4, AL C, XP 660; horrific visage, mummy immunities (MM). Mummified in 2900 BE, so he kept none of his HD or class abilities ([[Nethercity Features#Necromancy and the mummies (SOTN pp. 16-17)|necromancy by era]]). (SOTN prints MV 60’, HD 5+1*, XP 460, and sword damage 1d8+4/+6; Judge's call: a short sword is 1d6 in ACKS II, +3 armed-mummy strength +1 magic.)
  - He began waking when the D1 slab was first disturbed: 1st attempt, still stirring in the sarcophagus; 2nd, out of it; 3rd or later, ready and unsurprisable.
- **Loot:** he wears a **conical boar's-tusk helmet with a winged crest** (250 gp, 1/6 st) and grips a **meteorite-iron, sickle-shaped short sword** whose hilt is a six-armed demon: **short sword +1, +3 vs humans** (apparent value 650 gp; Judge's call: a plain ACKS II magic weapon with a +3 bonus against one creature type, as [[Composite Bow +1, Manhunter]] is against humans). Tally: 250 gp, 2/6 st (magic excluded; the sword's 1/6 st counted).

### D3. Warlord's Annex (2900 BE)
- **Exits:** cedar doors side by side at the NW (to D1) and NE (to D4) corners; cedar door in the SE corner (to D6a).
- > [!player]
  > A 15’ × 10’ hall of rough-hewn rock and black marble tile with three cedar doors. On the south-west wall a painted relief of an elven warlord on a throne is so lifelike that at first glance a real figure seems to sit there.
- **Lore:** the enthroned figure is Eolande; anyone who has seen his likeness in D1 or D2 knows him.

### D4. Shrine to the Slaughterprince (2900 BE)
- **Exits:** cedar door from D3; cedar door in the middle of the NW wall to D5, carved into the relief as a city gate.
- > [!player]
  > A 30’ × 55’ chamber of black marble tile. Four steps rise to a raised square alcove in the north-east wall, where a black basalt statue of a six-armed demonic warrior holds an axe, a sword or a mace in each hand. The whole north-west wall is a battle relief of elves against primitive humans; a cedar door in its middle is worked into the scene as a city gate.
- **Lore:** a Loremastery or Theology proficiency throw identifies the statue as Bel, the Slaughterprince, Chthonic god of war.
- **Monster:** if any **Lawful** character approaches the dais, the statue animates. **Gargoyle of Bel** → [[Gargoyle]] (variant: cannot fly, six weapon attacks instead of claws, bite and horn): MV 30’ / 90’, AC 4, HD 4**, hp 19, 6 weapons 7+, D 1d6 each (extraordinary), F8, ML +3, AL C, XP 190; construct; immune to enchantment, poison and all mundane damage. (SOTN prints HD 4*, XP 135.)

### D5. Warlord's Chapel (2900 BE)
- **Exits:** cedar door from D4 only.
- > [!player]
  > A 50’ gallery of black marble, its walls cut with a dozen wall graves a side, opens into a semi-domed apse crossed by a 50’ wide transept with a carved column at each corner. On a four-step dais in the apse stands a grey marble altar streaked black and scored with cuts; a black jackal statue sits on either side of it.
- **Graves:** 24 wall graves, 2900 BE, usual inscriptions, remains and goods (48 gp in all).
- **Trick / Loot:** the altar slides aside like a stone slab door (−12, up to 3 helpers) to open a vault holding the chapel treasure: a **gold candelabra** (1,250 gp, 3/6 st); a **gold sacrificial bowl** (850 gp, 2/6 st) holding **600 gp** in ancient Argollëan coin; **four gold and ivory libation cups** (400 gp each, 1 st per 24); a **padded cedar box** with a **blue sapphire** (1,000 gp) and two egg-sized spheres of blue metal, **cobalt death orbs** ([[SOTN Magic Items#Death Orb]]). Tally (SOTN): 4,100 gp, 1 1/6 st. (SOTN prints 4,100 gp, but the listed values sum to 5,300 gp, orbs excluded; Judge's call: use the item values.)
- **Monster / Trick:** if the altar is defaced or anything is taken from the vault, the two statues are revealed as **jackals of darkness** and attack the plunderers → [[Jackal of Darkness (SOTN)]]: MV 50’ / 150’, AC 5, HD 4*, hp 20, 18, bite 7+, D 1d6, F4, ML +4, AL C, XP 135 each (ACKS II count, [[Jackal of Darkness (SOTN)]]); **black fire:** each jackal's dark flame envelops one opponent within 50’ for 1 fire damage per round; undead immunities; rebuked as mummies. (SOTN p. 35 prints MV 140’ and XP 285; the monster entry, SOTN p. 145, gives MV 150’ and XP 190.)
- **Blighted sinkhole of evil:** the altar makes this room one ([[Nethercity Features#Sinkholes of evil (SOTN p. 12; ACKS II sinkholes of evil, RR)|sinkholes]]); Lawful divine casters rebuke the jackals and any other undead here as if four class levels lower.

### D6. Warlord's Gallery (2900 BE)
A 10’ gallery from D3 south-east to D7, its walls honeycombed with graves of armed skeletons, with a statue alcove midway. Its two halves share one trigger: **disturbing any grave in either half wakes that half; opening the D6b alcove wakes all 48 skeletons**, who come at the party from both directions.

### D6a. Warlord's Gallery North
- **Exits:** cedar door at the NW end to D3; open SE to D6b.
- > [!player]
  > A 10’ wide gallery tiled in black marble, its walls pierced by dozens of graves. Each skeleton has been laid out with a sickle-shaped bronze short sword and a round bronze shield. A cedar door closes the north-west end; the gallery runs south-east into darkness.
- **Graves / Monster:** 24 wall graves, 2900 BE, usual inscriptions; no goods value, but each holds a skeleton warrior with bronze short sword and shield. They wake if any of these graves is disturbed or the D6b alcove is opened, and attack at once ([[Nethercity Features#Wall graves (SOTN pp. 14-16)|skeleton warriors]]). **24 skeletons** → [[Skeleton]]: MV 40’ / 120’, AC 3, HD 1*, hp 5 each, short sword 10+, D 1d6, F1, ML n/a, AL C, XP 13 each. (SOTN prints AC 2; ACKS II: AC 2 + shield.) Arms as loot: 1d3 gp per set (era value), not tallied.

### D6b. Warlord's Gallery Alcove
- **Exits:** gallery NW (D6a) and SE (D6c); the secret door in the NE wall to the hidden alcove.
- > [!player]
  > Midway along, the south-west wall opens into an arched alcove: a broken marble bust of an elven warlord in a winged conical helm has fallen from its marble pedestal. Across the gallery a marble panel shows the warlord in a chariot, bow in hand.
- **Lore:** the bust is Eolande; anyone who has seen his likeness knows him.
- **Trick:** the relief panel is a **secret door** (Searching throw to find). It rotates open only while the bust, or something of similar weight, sits on the pedestal; otherwise a lock hidden in the walls holds it shut. **Opening it wakes all 48 skeletons of D6a and D6c**, who attack from both ends of the gallery.
- **Loot (hidden alcove):** dozens of shards of pottery; **four terracotta canopic jars** of embalmed organs (25 gp, 1/6 st each); a **large terracotta amphora** with geometric patterns (60 gp, 3 st) full of **6,000 gp**; a **round cedar shield gilded with gold** (100 gp, 1 st); a **chryselephantine statuette** of a serene priestess, ivory skin and gold-leaf robes, jewellery and hair (300 gp, 2/6 st). A Loremastery or Theology throw identifies her as **Mityara**, the Noble Lady, Empyrean goddess of community, charity and civic virtue. Tally: 6,560 gp, 11 st.

### D6c. Warlord's Gallery South
- **Exits:** open NW to D6b; cedar door at the SE end to D7.
- > [!player]
  > A 10’ wide black-marble gallery, its walls pierced by dozens of graves whose skeletons hold sickle-shaped bronze short swords and round bronze shields. A cedar door closes the south-east end; north-west, the gallery runs into darkness.
- **Graves / Monster:** 24 wall graves, 2900 BE, as D6a: **24 skeletons**, same statline, wake if these graves are disturbed or the D6b alcove is opened.

### D7. Upper Level Grand Balcony (2700 BE)
- **Exits:** cedar door from D6c (north end); balcony south to D8; stair down along the cavern's north wall to the floor of C2b, 20’ below.
- > [!player]
  > A stone balcony with balustrades carved in trees, lotuses and serpents crosses a 50’ wide cavern north to south, 20’ above its floor. It is split in the middle, the northern half 6” higher than the southern. Magma stalactites hang 10’ overhead from a ceiling like a frozen, rippled sea. Below, sewage flows from the dark west under the balcony and east over the precipice that forms the cavern's north-east wall, except where it trickles down 10’ wide rock-cut steps. Dozens of 3’ long stone-grey grasshoppers graze on patches of tawny mould in the sludge. Stairs lead up to the south; another stair climbs 20’ along the north wall from the floor to the balcony. A sharp stench, worse even than the sewage, hangs in the air.
- **Monster:** the cavern floor below is C2b ([[Nethercity C - Central Caves]]): with C2a it holds **21 cavern locusts** and **7 mustard molds** (C2b: 3 patches, 3 locusts at each), keyed and counted there. For reference: **cavern locusts** → [[Cavern Locust]]: MV 20’ / 60’, fly 60’ / 180’, AC 5, HD 2*, hp 9 each, slam + bite or spit 9+, D 1d4 / 1d2 / spit (Death save or vomiting), F2, ML −3, AL N, XP 29; **mustard molds** (SOTN "yellow mold") → [[Mustard Mold]]: AC always hit, HD 2**, hp 14, 12, 11, 10, 9, 7, 6, spores (50%, 10’ cube, Death save or die in 6 rounds), F2, ML n/a, AL N, XP 38 (SOTN prints XP 30). The precipice falls to L1 ([[Nethercity L - Abyssal Level Caves]]).
- **Trick:** the stench is the **troglodytes** of D8 at the balcony's south end. Their chameleon hides makes them hard to see from D7, while they see the party plainly. Against a weak party they throw darts; against a strong one they fall back to their lair to warn the tribe and bring reinforcements.

### D8. Upper Level Grand Staircase (2700 BE)
- **Exits:** balcony N (D7); arched portal S (D9); curved stair on the west wall down to C2b.
- > [!player]
  > A 15’ × 50’ gallery cut from the living rock, from the balcony in the north to an arched portal in the south. The east wall is pierced by a few dozen wall graves; on the west a curved stair winds down. The stink of rotten eggs is overpowering, though there is no sewage here.
- **Graves:** 30 wall graves, 2700 BE, usual inscriptions, remains and goods (60 gp in all).
- **Monster:** **6 troglodyte warriors**, about to hunt locusts from the balcony, hidden by their chameleon hides (others −2 on surprise rolls) → [[Troglodyte]]: MV 40’ / 120’, AC 4, HD 2, hp 13, 12, 10, 9, 8, 6, 2 claws + bite 9+, D 1d4 each, or **barbed dart** (3 each, thrown as javelins, +3 to hit) 6+, D 1d6+1, F2, ML +1, AL C, XP 20 each; **stench** (MM: humans and demi-humans within 5’ save vs Death at −2 or queasy). (SOTN prints ML +1/+2, dart damage 1d6, and the stench as −2 to attack throws.) On a failed morale throw they flee home by **D8 – D9 – E6a – E6b – E2b – E2c – E4a – E4c – I1**.
- **Lore:** a Tracking throw follows them back along that route to their lair; **+4** if helped by scent (a hunting dog), from their slimy, reeking skin.

### D9. Upper Level Common Tomb (2700 BE)
- **Exits:** gallery N to D8 (through the arched portal); cedar door in the west wall near the NW corner to E6a.
- > [!player]
  > A 40’ × 40’ tomb hewn from volcanic rock, joined to the north by a 10’ × 30’ gallery. Every wall is pierced by wall graves, a dozen along each side of the gallery and dozens more in the tomb. The floor is cracked, uneven and bare of tiles. A cedar door stands in the west wall near the north-west corner.
- **Graves:** 99 wall graves (12 on each side of the gallery, 75 in the tomb), 2700 BE, usual inscriptions, remains and goods (198 gp in all).
- **Lore:** a Tracking throw (+4 with scent) finds the tracks of a small band of reptilian humanoids (troglodytes), come from E6a and gone on to D8.
