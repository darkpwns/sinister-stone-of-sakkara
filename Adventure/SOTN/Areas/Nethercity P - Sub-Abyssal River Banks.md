# Nethercity P — Sub-Abyssal River Banks

The **sub-abyssal river**, the lowest water of the Nethercity, and the grottoes, tombs and shrines along its banks (**1900-2200 BE**). It formed where deep aquifers feeding Lake Laman met a major lava tube: a slow, **icy cold**, dark river of muddy water about **20’ deep**, flowing **20’ per round** (just over 1 mph). It runs 10’-20’ wide from tunnel wall to tunnel wall for most of its length, widening at the keyed places. The ceiling, smooth volcanic tuff, is 10’-30’ above the water; at the **north and south ends** of the map it slopes down steeply until the way is passable only under water (north, it eventually joins the **Mirmen River**; south, **Lake Laman**). The river and its banks are on the **Sub-Abyssal** level; the burial cave **P6** and the Womb **P8b** lie a short climb above, on the **Abyssal** level. **P8b** is an edifice of elemental earth (a minor place of elemental power). No light; no blighted sinkhole. Hub [[SOTN Hub]]; doors, wall graves, mummies and treasure conventions in [[Nethercity Features]]. Pages are printed pages.

**Orientation:** SOTN's text for P (as for O and Q) is written with its north where the printed map has west (the river runs east-west on the map); the directions below are the text's. Downstream is north.

Running: Underground River column, `level: 5` — 1d6 every 3 turns, 6+ → 2d10 on `Tables/sotn-wandering-underground-river.yaml`. **Plus the catfish:** for **each turn** the party spends on or in the river, throw **1d6: on 6+ the giant catfish attacks** (SOTN p. 115; this is in addition to the wandering throw). **Water elemental:** sailing the funerary barge (P11) or entering the P12 tunnel without the P14 anointing, or coming back from P12 with its treasures, brings the elemental (P11). Mummies wake by the door rule ([[Nethercity Features#Doors (SOTN p. 11)]]); P6's wakes when the P5 steps are trodden.

## Layout
Along the river from north (downstream) to south (upstream):
- **P1** — the river widens to ~50’ between two grottoes; north of it the ceiling drops into the water (under-water only, towards the Mirmen).
- **P2** — east-bank grotto off P1; two dead-end side tunnels.
- **P3** — west-bank grotto off P1, ending in the stone slab door of **Q1** ([[Nethercity Q - Great King Tomb]]).
- **P4** — north bank where the river bends; a cave mouth and a 60’ winding tunnel north to **Q6**, the king's secret entrance ([[Nethercity Q - Great King Tomb]]).
- **P5** — a rocky east-bank beach: steps up to the burial cave **P6** (Abyssal level); a carved crone that is the secret door to **H26** ([[Nethercity H - Great Temple of Awakening]]).
- **P7** — the river column carved as a tree; a hole in the ceiling up to the well of **H24** ([[Nethercity H - Great Temple of Awakening]]).
- **P8a** — a west-bank cave mouth; tunnel up to **P8b**, the Womb of the Earth Mother (Abyssal level).
- **P9** — a marble-lined hole in the ceiling up to the well of **G14** ([[Nethercity G - Grand Chthonic Temple]]); a basalt column and a small west bank.
- **P10** — the inscribed column and a west bank with 24 wall graves.
- **P11** — the great cavern on the east bank with the funerary barge and the statue of Lammala; tunnels NE to **P12** and SE to **P13**. South of P11 the ceiling drops into the water (under-water only, towards Lake Laman).
- **P13** — tunnel from P11 to a stair ending in a secret boulder door up to the vicinity of **R2** ([[Nethercity R - Abyssal Level Southern Cave]]); a side tunnel 100’ to the font **P14**.
- Other ways in: the shafts from H24 (P7) and G14 (P9), the stair from H26 (P5), the Q tomb (P3, P4), R (P13). The river itself only under water at either end.

**Giant catfish** (the whole river) → [[Giant Catfish]]: a chalky white beast about **15’ long** that lurks in the muck and feeds on troglodytes and apes coming to drink. MV swim 30’ / 90’ (SOTN 90’), AC 5, HD 8, hp 37, bite and 4 feelers 3+, D 2d8 piercing (bite) / 1d4 bludgeoning per feeler, F4, ML 0, AL N, XP 600. Acute olfaction; aquatic mechanoreception (480’, cannot sense anything out of the water).

No factions: the river's undead and guardians are solitary and mindless or bound.

## Rooms (SOTN pp. 115-120)

### P1. Sub-Abyssal Northeastern River Bank (1900 BE)
- **Exits:** the river S (upstream) and N (downstream, soon only under water); grottoes on either side: P2 (east bank) and P3 (west bank) (map; SOTN's boxed text puts them NW and SE).
- > [!player]
  > The river, until now 10’ to 20’ wide and filling the tunnel, opens out here to about 50’ across. The tunnel walls draw back from the water into rocky grottoes on either side. Beyond them the river narrows again and the 10’ ceiling slopes steeply down towards the surface.
- Nothing else here. Beyond the grottoes the ceiling meets the water: onward north only by swimming under water.

### P2. Sub-Abyssal Eastern River Bank (1900 BE)
- **Exits:** W to the river (P1); at the east end two side tunnels, N and S (both dead ends).
- > [!player]
  > The tunnel wall falls back from the water into a grotto about 40’ wide and 50’ long. Near the river the floor is pebbled with worn stone; further in, stalactites and stalagmites of frozen lava stand like teeth in a stone mouth. At the far east end, dark side tunnels lead off north and south.
- **Lore:** a character with Caving, Engineering or Land Surveying who spends **1 turn** examining the side tunnels finds old excavation marks at the end of each: building work planned and long abandoned.

### P3. Sub-Abyssal Western River Bank (1900 BE)
- **Exits:** E to the river (P1); W stone slab door to Q1 ([[Nethercity Q - Great King Tomb]]).
- > [!player]
  > On the river's west side the tunnel wall opens into a grotto with a pebbled floor, roughly triangular: about 40’ wide along the water, narrowing to 15’ at its west end, where a stone slab door is set against the cave wall.
- Disturbing the slab door wakes the four mummy warlords of Q1 (door rule; see [[Nethercity Q - Great King Tomb]]).

### P4. Sub-Abyssal Northern River Bank (2200 BE)
- **Exits:** the river S (upstream, towards P5) and NE (downstream, towards P1); NW cave mouth, winding tunnel north 60’ to Q6 ([[Nethercity Q - Great King Tomb]]).
- > [!player]
  > The dark river comes in from the south and vanishes into blackness to the north-east. Just where it begins to bend, a cave mouth 30’ wide and 15’ high gapes on the north-west bank, and a winding volcanic tunnel runs north from it.
- The tunnel reaches Q6 after **60’** (Q6's own text says 40’: SOTN inconsistency, Judge's call: 60’).

### P5. Sub-Abyssal Central River Bank (2200 BE)
- **Exits:** the river (N towards P4, S towards P7); rough-hewn steps up a tunnel near the NE corner to P6; **secret door** in the crone carving to H26 ([[Nethercity H - Great Temple of Awakening]]).
- > [!player]
  > A rocky beach about 15’ wide runs for 60’ along the river's east bank. Near its north-east corner, rough-hewn steps climb away through a tunnel. South of the steps, the east wall bears a high-relief carving of a grotesque, pregnant crone.
- **Trick:** every step is carved with the **winged sun**, so each footfall crushes the holy symbol of **Ammonar** under the walker's heel; the stair is wide enough for a worshipper of Ammonar to avoid it by treading the edges. No trap and no ill effect either way, but **treading the steps wakes the mummy of P6**.
- **Lore / Trick:** Loremastery or Theology throw: the crone is **Telith**, the Great Earth Mother, Chthonic goddess of the underground. The carving hides a **secret door** to H26 (Searching); found, it opens like a stone slab door by turning on its vertical axis (forcing −12, [[Nethercity Features#Doors (SOTN p. 11)]]).

### P6. Abyssal Level Earth Burial Cave (2200 BE)
- **Exits:** SW down the steps to P5; alcoves NW (the burial goods) and SE.
- > [!player]
  > The steps come up into an irregular cave about 60’ long from north-east to south-west and 30’ wide, with a 20’ ceiling. Two natural alcoves about 10’ by 10’ push out of the north-west and south-east walls. Dozens of basalt stalactites hang from the ceiling like stone chandeliers over rows of volcanic stalagmites and basalt pillars, and in the middle of this forest of stone stands a sarcophagus cut from the living rock.
- **Monster:** an evil priest of Telith chose this cave, among the finest rock formations in the Nethercity, for his tomb; he is now a **mummy cultist of Telith**. He begins to wake when the party first treads the steps at P5; how far along he is depends on how long they took to climb (Judge's call: as the door rule, by rounds spent on the stair: straight up = stirring; one round's delay = out of the sarcophagus; two or more = ready, cannot be surprised). Given time, he casts [[Energy Protection]] against fire on himself (SOTN resist fire).
  - **Mummy cultist of Telith** (8th-level abilities) → [[Mummy]] + [[Elven Cultist]] 8 of Telith: WIS 13, CHA 13; MV 20’ / 60’ (SOTN 60’), AC 6, HD 8+1***, hp 34, touch 2+ (attacks as HD 9; SOTN 3+), D 1d12 extraordinary bludgeoning and extraordinary necrotic + mummy rot, Save F8, ML +4, AL C, XP 2,100. Horrific visage, mummy rot, immunities as the MM mummy. Class abilities (SOTN pp. 163-165): commands undead as an 8th-level Chaotic caster (Judge's call: as a [[Crusader]] of 8th level); **earth awareness** (11+ throw: predicts sinkholes, deadfalls, collapses and rock slides on entering an area; identifies lairs as a land surveyor); **elemental connection** (underground, regains one spent spell slot after 6 turns of meditation; 3 turns in a minor edifice of elemental earth, 1 turn in a major, 1 round in a supreme; at most one slot of each level a day); **rock solidity** (+4 on saves against being grabbed, knocked down, restrained, wrestled or moved). Proficiencies: Elementalism (earth), Engineering, Intimidation, Quiet Magic, Theology, Unflappable Casting. Spells per day 4/4/3/3/1 from the cultist list plus Telith's: [[Pass Without Trace]], [[Sling Stone]]; [[Earth's Excrescence]], [[Shatter Blade]]; [[SOTN Spells#Earth's Maw]], [[Striking]]; [[Battering Ram]], [[Speak with Plants]]; [[SOTN Spells#Fangs of the Earth]], *weave stone* (Judge's call: no ACKS II equivalent found). Judge's call for a ready loadout: 1st [[Sling Stone]] ×2, [[Remove Fear|cause fear]], [[Illumination|tenebrosity]]; 2nd [[Energy Protection]], [[Halt Humanoids]] ×2, [[Shatter Blade]]; 3rd [[SOTN Spells#Earth's Maw]], [[Striking]], [[Cure Blindness|cause blindness]]; 4th [[Battering Ram]], [[Cure Serious Injury|cause serious injury]], [[Animate Undead]]; 5th [[SOTN Spells#Fangs of the Earth]].
- **Trap:** the jagged rock formations: anyone who **charges, runs or withdraws** in this cave saves vs **Blast**; on a failure he falls after **3d6 feet** of movement and takes **1d4** damage (Acrobatics +2 to the save). (The mummy has rock solidity, but SOTN does not exempt it; Judge's call: it moves at a walk and does not need to save.)
- **Loot (the NW alcove):** gold thurible (**400gp, 1 st**) beside an oilskin bundle of 40 sticks of frankincense (**20gp each**); 4 terracotta canopic jars of embalmed organs (**25gp, 1/6 st each**); 6 large, finely carved alabaster amphorae (**300gp, 3 st each**), unbroken by the old quakes, perhaps by Telith's favour; 2 large cedar chests with **2,000gp** each. **Tally: 7,100gp, 21 4/6 st** (SOTN; the listed weights come to 23 4/6 st with the 4,000 coins at 1 st per 1,000: Judge's call, use 23 4/6 st if weights matter).

### P7. Sub-Abyssal Central River Column (2100 BE)
- **Exits:** the river N (towards P5) and S (towards P8a); 5’ hole in the ceiling east of the pillar, up to the well of H24 ([[Nethercity H - Great Temple of Awakening]]).
- > [!player]
  > The river widens to about 50’ and the ceiling rises to 20’ over the water. A basalt pillar 15’ wide and 40’ long rises from the river to the ceiling, carved with care into the trunk of a great tree; above it the ceiling is sculpted in high relief into leafy boughs spreading from the trunk. East of the pillar, a hole about 5’ across has been cut in the ceiling so that it looks like the moon shining through the leaves.
- Climbing up the hole (Judge's call: a Climbing throw from a boat or the pillar, the height being the 20’ ceiling plus the shaft) brings a character out in the well of the Great Temple meditation chamber **H24**.

### P8b. Abyssal Level Womb of the Earth Mother (2200 BE)
- **Exits:** SE down the steps and tunnel to P8a.
- > [!player]
  > The tunnel ends at a stone arch carved in the shape of a vulva. Steps cut from the living rock climb from it through a short passage worked with organic shapes in high relief, into an oval cavern about 20’ wide and 30’ long, 20’ high. It is warm in here, and the walls are wet and glistening, unpleasantly so. At the cave's northern tip, in a recessed shelf about 5’ across, sits a white diamond of extraordinary size and splendour.
- **Loot / Lore:** a Loremastery throw, or a Craft (gemsmithing) throw at −4 ([[Art and Craft]]), identifies it as the **egg of the earth**, a legendary gem the Argollëan elves took from the dwarves of **Azen Khador** in the Bitter War. Worth **25,000gp**, or more to a dwarf noble of Azen Khador descent. (SOTN gives no weight; Judge's call: 1/6 st.)
- **Trap:** disturbing the egg brings down the **huge black pudding** enchanted to guard the Womb: it drops from the ceiling and walls onto the party (Judge's note: it *is* the wet glistening coating on the walls), with **+2 to surprise**.
  - **Huge black pudding** → [[Black Blob]]: CON 18; MV 20’ / 60’ (SOTN 60’), AC 5, HD 10**, hp 90, pseudopod 1+, D 3d8 extraordinary acidic, F5, ML n/a, AL N, XP 2,250 (SOTN AC 3, HD 10*, attack 2+, XP 1,550). Immune to enchantment and to all damage but fire (fire harms it normally); cold paralyses it 1 round per damage die; electrical or seismic damage bursts it, re-forming unharmed next initiative; slashing splits it in two (HD and current hp shared, 2 HD minimum; SOTN: any attack but cold and fire divides it). Its acid eats metal and wood, not stone; anything it drops to 0 hp dies, dissolved.
- **Trick:** the whole Womb is an **edifice of elemental earth**, minor ([[Places of Elemental Power]]; SOTN cites a "resilient edifice" from a supplement this campaign does not use; the effects it lists are the JJ's minor edifice): magic research into earth magic here gains **+1**; earth spell effects are calculated as if the caster were **2 class levels higher**; left unoccupied, **10% every 1d12 days** a group of creatures from the Sphere of Earth manifests here and stays **1d4 days** (none while the egg of the earth is still in the Womb; the JJ's 3% chance of finding such a group present on arrival likewise does not apply while the egg is here: Judge's call). The P6 mummy's elemental connection works faster here (see P6).

### P9. Sub-Abyssal Southern River Column North (2000 BE)

### P10. Sub-Abyssal Southern River Column South (2000 BE)
- **Exits:** the river N (towards P9) and S (towards P11).
- > [!player]
  > The river widens to 30’ around a basalt column about 15’ thick that rises to the ceiling 15’ above, its whole surface covered with Ancient Argollëan script. West of the column the tunnel widens on a narrow bank of rocky volcanic tuff, about 10’ wide and 30’ long. The bank's west wall is lined with two dozen wall graves.
- **Graves:** **24** wall graves, all c. **2000 BE**, usual inscriptions, remains and goods: **336gp in all** (SOTN "average 336gp" = 24 × 14; [[Nethercity Features#Wall graves (SOTN pp. 14-16)]]).
- **Lore:** read in Ancient Argollëan, the column is a prayer to **Lammala**, Mother of the Murky Depths: it asks her black waters to wash the faithful's souls clean of shame, her great waves to wear away and ruin her enemies, and that **those who plunder her precious treasures become creatures of her watery abyss**. That last line is the warning of the curse on the P12 treasures.

### P11. Sub-Abyssal Southern River Bank (1900 BE)
- **Exits:** W the river (N towards P10; S the ceiling soon meets the water, towards Lake Laman); NE tunnel to P12; SE tunnel to P13.
- > [!player]
  > *(From the river:)* The east wall of the tunnel draws back from the water into a cavern 70’ long and 50’ wide, its ceiling 50’ overhead. An ancient funerary barge with a long steering pole lies beached at the waterline. On the far side of the cavern, two dark tunnel mouths about 10’ wide and high lead away north-east and south-east. Between them, under a coat of algae, a limestone statue some 25’ tall of a grotesque horror spreads its long tentacles menacingly over the cave.
  >
  > *(From P13:)* The tunnel comes out in the south-east corner of a rocky cavern about 70’ by 50’ under a vaulted ceiling 50’ high. Its west side slopes down into a dark river about 15’ wide, flowing in from the south and away to the north. An ancient funerary barge with a long steering pole lies beached at the waterline. Another tunnel about 10’ wide and high leaves from the north-east corner. Between the two tunnels, under a coat of algae, a limestone statue some 25’ tall of a grotesque horror spreads its long tentacles menacingly over the cave.
- **Loot: the funerary barge** — 25’ long, 10’ wide, cedar with decorative panels of thin bronze; no sign of age or decay; radiates magic. It is a **funerary barge of the cults** ([[SOTN Magic Items#Funerary Barge of the Cults]], SOTN p. 132; value **68,000gp**). In full:
  - **Build:** a black, flat-bottomed river boat about 25’ × 10’ weighing **2,000 lbs** (200 st). A tall prow with a brass skull; a single oar on a rowlock at the stern (the "long steering pole"). Most of the deck is a cabin with blinded windows, room for **four coffins or 400 st of cargo**. (The room text's cedar-and-bronze description and the item's black boat with a brass skull are the same vessel: Judge's call, black-lacquered cedar with bronze panels and the brass skull on the prow.)
  - **Propulsion:** it needs no rowing. Dipping the oar in the water drives it by magic, at a speed set by the angle and depth of the oar; top speed **60’ per turn** (SOTN: "36 miles per day"; as printed — the two figures do not agree in ACKS terms; Judge's call: 60’ per turn underground, 36 miles a day on open water). The P11 river flows at 20’ a round; the barge makes headway against it.
  - **Teleportation:** rowing the oar in the vessel's **own pattern** (unique to each barge) **teleports** the barge, with all aboard, to **any desired body of water within 360’** (as printed). Limits: **once per day**, and only with **at least one dead body in its cabin**. The pattern for this barge is the one engraved on the **P12 basin** (loops joined by sweeping strokes): a character who has studied it can row it (Judge's call: copying or memorising the pattern takes a turn at the basin; no throw; without it, no teleport). The Nethercity's wards against teleportation **do not affect the barge** (SOTN p. 14, [[Nethercity Features#Teleportation (SOTN p. 14)]]); it arrives on target, no roll on the teleportation table. Waters within 360’ of P11 (Judge's call, from the map): the river anywhere from P9-P10 down to the south end, beyond the drowned ceiling towards Lake Laman if within range; the P14 font is far too small to float it. A body of water out of sight must be known to the rower (Judge's call).
  - **In combat:** AC 1, **45 structural hit points** (SOTN "45 SP"). Creatures inside the cabin gain **+4 AC against missiles** and **+4 on saves vs Blast**.
  - **Its guard:** sailing it without the P14 anointing brings the water elemental (below).
  - Weight 200 st, so it leaves the Nethercity only by teleport or by water; its value counts as magic (not in the tally).
- **Trap:** Loremastery or Theology throw: the statue is **Lammala**, the Sea Mother, Chthonic goddess of the murky depths. The statue is not magical; it is a warning. Anyone who tries to **sail the barge** and/or **enter the NE tunnel to P12** without first being **anointed at the P14 font** is attacked by an **enslaved water elemental** that rises from the depths of the river. Anyone **coming back from P12 with its treasures** is attacked **whether anointed or not**.
  - **Water elemental** (enslaved; supreme tier by its 16 HD) → [[Elemental]]: MV swim 60’ / 180’ (crosses land at its swim speed but never more than 60’ from water), AC 11, HD 16*, hp 76, smash −5+, D 3d8 extraordinary cold, double dice against foes standing in or under water (SOTN: +1d8 if the target is in the water), Save F16, ML +4, AL N, XP 3,300. A smaller creature it damages saves vs Paralysis (−2 per size step) or begins drowning. Immune to enchantment, necrotic, poisonous, cold and mundane bludgeoning, piercing and slashing damage; aquatic mechanoreception; [[Holy Circle|holy circle]] keeps it off; [[Counterspell]] or [[Dispel Magic]] banishes it. (Judge's call: it attacks only those who trespass and fights until they leave Area P or it is destroyed.)

### P12. Sub-Abyssal Southern River Bank Northern Arm (1900 BE)
- **Exits:** S/SW by the tunnel to P11.
- > [!player]
  > The tunnel ends in a bean-shaped cave about 30’ long and 20’ wide. Most of its north-west part is taken up by a rectangular stone basin cut from the living rock, in which hundreds of sea shells, shark's teeth and other treasures of the sea glitter in the light. The basin's sides are engraved with a strange pattern of long loops joined by sweeping strokes.
- **Loot:** in the basin: 245 white nacreous seashells (**10gp each**); 120 great white shark teeth (**10gp each**); 100 pink nacreous seashells (**25gp each**); 60 sardonyx seashells (**50gp each**); 10 pieces of pink coral (**100gp each**); 4 of red coral (**125gp each**); 12 black pearls (**250gp each**); 9 pieces of black coral (**250gp each**); 6 white pearls (**250gp each**); 2 pieces of gold coral (**500gp each**; SOTN prints "(500gp)", but the room total needs 500gp each); 2 gold pearls (**750gp each**); 1 large black pearl (**1,000gp**). **Tally: 20,900gp** (SOTN gives no weight; Judge's call: about 6 st for the 569 pieces).
- **Trap (the curse of Lammala):** anyone who takes even a **single pearl** (Judge's call: any piece from the basin) saves vs **Spells**. On a failure he is cursed to become a squid-like monstrosity over **seven days**:
  - **Day 1:** small hives in patches on his arms and legs.
  - **Day 2:** his hair falls out; his skin feels loose; faint lines appear on his neck.
  - **Day 3:** the hives on his limbs swell into large pustules; his limbs gain a wiry strength and suppleness: **STR +1, DEX +1, CHA −2**. Darker lines ring his neck.
  - **Day 4:** the pustules burst into cup-like suckers; his bones go soft, above all in fingers and toes: **STR +1 and DEX +1 more**, but **−2 on throws needing fine handling, weapon attack throws included**. The neck lines deepen into scar-like grooves.
  - **Day 5:** his eyes grow large; his fingers and toes slough off: **movement −30’**; he can handle objects with the suckers on his arms at **−4** to throws. A bulbous sac starts to grow below his groin; the neck lines become gills.
  - **Day 6:** the bones of his arms and legs melt away, the muscles strengthening to make up for it; he can no longer stand or walk but crawls on his limbs like tentacles at **30’**. The sac makes a black fluid that leaks from his rectum. His teeth fall out. **CHA −4 more.**
  - **Day 7:** the rest of his bones melt; ears, nose and lips slough off; his lungs collapse and he **can no longer breathe air**. He is now a four-tentacled cephalopod.
  - Though the early stages look like disease, neither Healing proficiency nor [[Cure Disease|cure disease]] slows it. [[Remove Curse|Remove curse]] halts it, and reverses it if the caster is of high enough level: **6th level or higher on day 1**, one more level for each day after (7th on day 2 … 12th on day 7). (Judge's reading: a lower-level remove curse halts the change at its current stage without reversing it.)
- **Lore:** the engraved pattern shows the **oar strokes** that make the funerary barge teleport (P11).
- Coming back from here with any of the treasure brings the P11 water elemental, anointed or not.

### P13. Sub-Abyssal Southern River Bank Southern Arm (1900 BE)
- **Exits:** W cave opening to P11; E ascending stair to a secret boulder door (vicinity of R2, [[Nethercity R - Abyssal Level Southern Cave]]); halfway along, a side tunnel NE (100’) to P14.
- > [!player]
  > A tunnel 10’ wide and 10’ high runs 40’ between a cave opening to the west and a stone staircase climbing away to the east. Halfway along, a side tunnel branches off north-east.
- **Trick:** the stair ends at a boulder carved to look like the rock face: a **secret door** (Searching); found, it rolls aside like a stone slab door (forcing −12) onto the vicinity of **R2**. (The printed map draws the stair's head nearer R4: Judge's call, follow the text.) The side tunnel curves slowly north-east for about 100’ to P14.

### P14. Sub-Abyssal Unholy Font (1900 BE)
- **Exits:** SW by the 100’ side tunnel to P13.
- > [!player]
  > The tunnel opens into a sickle-shaped cave 20’ long and 30’ wide, 15’ high. At its east end, a basin of clear water cut from the living rock murmurs softly. An Ancient Argollëan phrase is cut into the basin.
- **Lore / Trick:** read in Ancient Argollëan: "Anoint yourself with the sacred waters of the Sea Mother." The basin keeps refilling with salt sea water by a permanent [[Create Water]]. A **Chaotic** character who spends **1 round** purifying himself at it receives a [[Holy Blessing]] (SOTN bless); a **Lawful or Neutral** one suffers the reverse, [[Holy Blessing|unholy blessing]] (SOTN bane). **While that effect lasts** (6 turns; ACKS II holy blessing), the anointed character, of any alignment, may go down the tunnel to P12 and/or use the funerary barge at P11 without the water elemental attacking him (but see P11: not if he comes back from P12 with its treasures). The protection does not apply outside Area P.
