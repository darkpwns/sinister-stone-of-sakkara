# Nethercity B — Early Chthonic Tomb

The oldest sealed tomb of the Nethercity (3000 BE): an elven lord's sepulchre cut from the living rock behind the round stone slab in A3's west wall, on the **Upper** level (SOTN names no level for B; Judge's inference from A3, and B1's hidden shaft drops to C7 on the Lower level). Glazed red tile floors, cracked by the ancient quake. Its art marks the moment the elves took up the Chthonic faith: a scarab-faced being (Khepri) in the hall, the Chthonic gods standing beside the familiar Empyrean ones in the antechamber, and still-Empyrean symbols in the treasury. Guarded by skeleton warriors in the wall graves and by the lord himself, a plain mummy of the earliest era ([[Nethercity Features#Necromancy and the mummies (SOTN pp. 16-17)]]). Every door is a stone slab; disturbing a slab wakes the undead behind it. No blighted sinkhole. No light. Hub [[SOTN Hub]]; standing rules [[Nethercity Features]]; the gods [[Nethercity History]]. Pages are SOTN printed pages.

Running: wandering 1d6 every 3 turns, 6+ → 2d10 on the **Temple / Tomb** column (`Tables/sotn-wandering-temple-tomb.yaml`), setting level 4 (coordinator's call). **Waking:** each group of undead wakes when the slab door into its room is disturbed; the number of attempts the party needs to open it sets how ready they are (1st attempt: just stirring; 2nd: out of their graves or sarcophagus; 3rd+: formed up, cannot be surprised), and they return to torpor one turn after the door is left shut with no living creature in sight ([[Nethercity Features#Doors (SOTN p. 11)]], [[Nethercity Features#Wall graves (SOTN pp. 14-16)]]).

## Layout
- **Entrance:** the round stone slab in the west wall of A3 ([[Nethercity A - Entrance Caves]]) opens on a 12’ × 20’ hallway into the entrance hall **B1**. Disturbing it wakes B1's skeletons.
- **B1** → two side-by-side slab doors in its north wall: the west one to the annex **B2**, the east one up a 20’ hallway to the antechamber **B3** (map). A shaft hidden under the cracked tiles in B1's south-east corner drops to **C7** ([[Nethercity C - Central Caves]]).
- **B3** → slab door at the south end of the west wall to the treasury **B4** (trapped: falling ceiling block); slab door at the north end of the east wall to the burial chamber **B5** (wakes the mummy).
- No other ways out: the tomb is a dead end apart from the shaft to C7.

## Rooms (SOTN pp. 26-28)

### B1. Early Tomb Entrance Hall (3000 BE)
- **Exits:** E through the 12’ × 20’ hallway and the slab door to A3; two slab doors N (west: B2; east: B3); hidden shaft in the SE corner down to C7.

> [!player]
> Once the slab is rolled aside, a hallway 12’ wide and 20’ long leads into a 35’ square chamber hewn from the rock. Glazed red tiles cover the floor, cracked and heaved as if by some great upheaval. The far (west) wall is carved with a scarab-faced figure among a crowd of elves. Two stone slab doors stand side by side in the north wall. The south wall holds 12 horizontal wall graves, three columns of four, each about 6’ wide, 3’ deep and 2’ high, every one with a skeleton in it. (If the skeletons are already up, describe them climbing out or standing in ranks with curved bronze swords and round shields.)

- **Monster:** the 12 graves hold skeleton warriors, set moving when the A3 slab was first disturbed: just rising, or already in battle formation, depending on how long the slab took.
  **12 skeletons** → [[Skeleton]]: MV 40’/120’, AC 3 (bronze shield), HD 1*, hp 5 each, sickle-shaped bronze short sword 10+, D 1d6, F1, ML n/a, AL C, XP 13 each; immune to enchantment, necrotic and poisonous damage; rebuked as skeletons (not in torpor). (SOTN prints AC 2 and MV 120’; Judge's call: MM AC 2 + shield.)
- **Trap:** anyone who starts his initiative on, or moves into, the south-east corner (the "o" on the map) has a **2 in 6** chance of falling through the cracked tiles into a vertical shaft to the tunnel below (C7): **1d6** damage, lands prone. Unless revealed by accident, the shaft can be found as a secret door (Searching, [[Searching and Listening]]).
- **Lore:** **Loremastery or Theology at −4**: the scarab-faced being is **Khepri**, a mysterious Chthonic god of ancient times (the Great Teacher of [[Nethercity History]]; cf. [[Khepri Hierarch]]).
- **Graves:** 12 wall graves of 3000 BE with the usual inscriptions; their occupants are the skeletons. SOTN gives no grave goods here (Judge's call: the skeletons' arms are the goods; as loot each sword-and-shield set is worth 1d3gp, not counted in the tally: [[Nethercity Features#Wall graves (SOTN pp. 14-16)]]).

### B2. Early Tomb Annex (3000 BE)
- **Exits:** S through the slab door to B1.

> [!player]
> A 20’ square vault with bare rock walls and a red tile floor. In the middle stands a canopic shrine of gilded cedar decorated with bronze cobras, ringed by dozens of smashed vases; a few still stand whole.

- **Loot:** the canopic shrine, 1,250gp, 7 st; in it four terracotta canopic jars of embalmed organs, 25gp and 1/6 st each (embalming equipment: [[Nethercity Features#Treasure (SOTN pp. 17-18)]]); four intact terracotta vases painted with geometric patterns, 7gp and 2/6 st each. Tally: **1,378gp, 9 st**.

### B3. Early Tomb Antechamber (3000 BE)
- **Exits:** S down a 20’ hallway to the slab door to B1; slab door where the west wall meets the south corner to B4 (trapped); slab door where the east wall meets the north corner to B5.

> [!player]
> Past the slab, a 20’ hallway runs north and opens into a 60’ × 30’ chamber cut from the rock, its red glazed tiles broken in places by old tremors. Ceiling-high stone slab doors stand at the south end of the west wall and the north end of the east wall. The north wall is carved with terrible figures (a queen with dragon wings, a six-armed warrior, a medusa with a serpent's body, a sorcerer with a skull for a face) standing among familiar Empyrean gods, all gazing down on an offering of a hundred cattle. The whole east wall is cut into 18 horizontal wall graves, and skeletons with curved bronze swords and round bronze shields are climbing out of them, or already stand ready.

- **Monster:** 18 skeleton warriors, set moving when the B1 → B3 slab was disturbed: just rising or already formed up, depending on how long it took.
  **18 skeletons** → [[Skeleton]]: MV 40’/120’, AC 3 (bronze shield), HD 1*, hp 5 each, sickle-shaped bronze short sword 10+, D 1d6, F1, ML n/a, AL C, XP 13 each. (SOTN prints AC 2, MV 120’.)
- **Trap:** the B4 slab door props up a large ceiling block directly above it. **Opening the door drops the block: 2d10** damage to whoever opened it and everyone within 5’; a **Blast** save avoids it. A search of the door for traps notices the block easily (**+4**). It cannot be disarmed (it is part of the structure), but a character with **Engineering or Siege Engineering** can shore the block up with scaffolding.
- **Lore:** **Loremastery or Theology**: the terrible figures are the Chthonic gods **Iskara, Bel, Nasga and Dirgion**, in that order ([[Nethercity History]]).
- **Graves:** 18 wall graves of 3000 BE, usual inscriptions; the occupants are the skeletons; no goods given (Judge's call as in B1: 18 sets of arms, 1d3gp each, outside the tally).

### B4. Early Tomb Treasury (3000 BE)
- **Exits:** E through the trapped slab door to B3.

> [!player]
> A 20’ × 25’ vault cut from the rock, its red tile floor buckled here and there by an ancient quake. The walls are carved with symbols: the sun, the earth, the moon, a great mountain and a tall tree. Scores of broken pots, amphorae and vases cover the floor, with overturned silver bowls and a few unbroken pots among them. Near the centre a big golden fan with huge plumes lies on a red chest inlaid with ivory. The air is dusty and smells of clay.

- **Loot:** sifting the shards turns up 10 smooth silver bowls (10gp, 1/6 st each) and 4 intact terracotta pots with geometric patterns (5gp, 1/6 st each). A fan of gilded cedar with giant hawk plumes (100gp, 4/6 st). A dawn redwood chest inlaid in ivory with Empyrean holy symbols (850gp, 5 st), holding: a leather bag of tree seeds (apparent value 5gp; in truth a **[[SOTN Magic Items#Bag of Faerie Seeds]]** with **5 seeds**), a bundle of 19 sticks of frankincense (20gp each), and a bronze pendant (100gp). Tally: **1,550gp, 8 st** (magic excluded, as SOTN gives it).
- **Lore:** a **Loremastery** throw, or simply having **Theology**: the carvings are the common Empyrean emblems still used in Northern Argollë for **Ammonar, Mityara, Istreus, Türas and Naurivus**.

### B5. Early Tomb Burial Chamber (3000 BE)
- **Exits:** through the slab door to B3.

> [!player]
> A 30’ × 20’ burial chamber with black walls of living rock and a smooth red-tiled floor. On a low stone dais in the middle lies a limestone sarcophagus carved in the shape of a haughty elven lord. (If the mummy is up, describe it rising or standing, a gold mask on its face.)

- **Monster:** the lord lies in the sarcophagus, now a mummy, set stirring when the B3 → B5 slab was disturbed: still rising, or fully roused, depending on how long the door took. Sliding a stone lid: [[Nethercity Features#Sarcophagi (SOTN p. 12)]]. Woken and then spared or fled from: [[Nethercity Features#Necromancy and the mummies (SOTN pp. 16-17)]] (mummy reactions).
  **1 mummy (elven lord)** → [[Mummy]]: MV 20’/60’, AC 6, HD 5+1**, hp 26, slam 5+, D 1d12 extraordinary bludgeoning and extraordinary necrotic + mummy rot (1 necrotic each dusk, no magical healing until cured), F5, ML +4, AL C, XP 660; horrific visage (Paralysis save at first sight and each round of looking; fail = cower to next round); immune to enchantment and to mundane damage except fire (half) and luminous; extraordinary damage resisted except fire and luminous; rebuked as a mummy. (SOTN prints MV 60’, HD 5+1*, XP 460.) A plain mummy of the earliest era: no class abilities.
- **Loot:** on its face a gold funerary mask in its own living likeness, eyes of lapis lazuli: **675gp, 1/6 st**. Tally: 675gp, 1/6 st (SOTN gives no room total).
