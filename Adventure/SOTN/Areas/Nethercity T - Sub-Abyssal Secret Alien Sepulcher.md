# Nethercity T — Sub-Abyssal Secret Alien Sepulcher

The heart of the Nethercity's secret history. A hidden stair of about 2000 BE runs down from the Grand Chthonic Vestibule (F14) to a prehistoric shaft at the Sub-Abyssal Level, where a **khepri hierarch**, the being the Argollëan elves called the **Great Teacher** ([[Great Teacher (SOTN)]]), sleeps in a cryostasis tube. Woken by the first elven explorers in the 32nd century BE, he taught them sorcery and science, converted them from the Empyrean gods to his Chthonic pantheon, set them building the Nethercity and breeding the Thrassians, then went back to sleep to await the Awakening ([[Nethercity History]]). He is the module's hidden mastermind; most parties will meet him only if they find this stair or break the reliquary cases in F17. No blighted sinkhole is keyed here; the whole Nethercity is a shadowed sinkhole of evil ([[Nethercity Features]]). Unlit. Hub [[SOTN Hub]].

Running: wandering 1d6 every 3 turns, 6+ → 2d10 on the Temple / Tomb column, `Tables/sotn-wandering-temple-tomb.yaml` (`level: 4`). Area rule: the hierarch wakes 1d6 rounds after the secret door into T2 is opened, unless the F17 alarm has already woken him (see T2).

## Layout
- **Entrance:** the secret door in the snake-loop fresco on the south wall of **F14** ([[Nethercity F - Lower Level Central Complex]]; trapped with a scything blade, see F14 and T1) opens on a round tunnel with steps going down into **T1**.
- **T1** runs south about 90’ down rough-hewn steps to a dead end: a high-relief carving of a beetle-headed figure, itself a revolving secret door into the foot of the shaft **T2**.
- **T2** is the bottom of a 65’ vertical shaft. Its top meets the ceiling of the Cave of the Falling Star **F18** (drawn beneath F18 on the map), where it is closed by a plug of concrete-like stone; there is no way through from above without digging.
- Neighbour without connection: [[Nethercity S - Abyssal Level Hidden Tombs]] lies just west.

## Rooms (SOTN pp. 127-128)

### T1. Sub-Abyssal Secret Corridor (2000 BE)
- **Exits:** secret door north (up the steps) to F14; revolving secret door at the south end to T2.
> [!player]
> Just inside the secret door a lever of green-crusted bronze is set in the wall. Past it, rough-cut stone steps lead down into a secret passage. About 90’ on, the passage ends at a wall carved in high relief with a man-like figure whose face is a beetle.
- **Trick:** pulling the lever disarms the scything-blade trap on the F14 side of the secret door (so the hierarch can leave safely). The F14 trap, for reference: whoever pushes the door open saves vs Blast or takes 3d6 damage and loses the hand or foot used (as the permanent wound); it can be opened safely with a 10’ pole once detected, or disarmed with Trapbreaking.
- **Trick:** the relief is a secret door (Searching to find). Found, it turns on a central pivot like a revolving door, into T2.
- **Lore:** a Loremastery throw, or Theology at −4, identifies the figure as Khepri, a mysterious Chthonic god worshipped in ancient times.

### T2. Secret Alien Sepulcher (prehistoric)
- **Exits:** revolving secret door to T1; the shaft rises 65’ to a sealed plug in the ceiling of F18 (no exit).
> [!player]
> The door turns into the bottom of a hollow vertical shaft. Scaffolding of some strange metal climbs its walls to 65’ overhead. On the floor stands a metal canister 7’ tall and 3’ wide, upright on a round base, with a small window at eye height.
- **Lore:** the shaft ends at the ceiling of F18 in a plug of concrete-like stones. A character with Engineering who studies the plug can tell the shaft once went higher and was sealed.
- **Trick (cryostasis tube):** the canister is a [[SOTN Magic Items#Cryostasis Tube]] (15,000gp; 300 lbs, i.e. 30 st., empty). The khepri hierarch inside survived the Day Without Night in it, was woken by the first Argollëan explorers, spent centuries turning them from the Empyrean gods to his Chthonic ones, and returned to it to wait for the Awakening (SOTN p. 128 says about 1700 BE; the timeline on SOTN p. 7 puts it in the late 21st century BE, and T1 is dated 2000 BE: Judge's call, late 21st century BE).
- **Monster:** if the party opened or broke the cases in the grand Chthonic reliquary (F17), their telepathic alarms have already woken him (Judge decides where he is and what he has done since: he can pass the F14 trap with the lever). Otherwise he wakes **1d6 rounds after the T2 secret door is opened** and steps out of the tube in a hiss of steam. What he does depends on how the party behaves (reaction throw, with his Telepathy and Clairvoyancy already reading them):
  - **destroy them** and go back to sleep until the Awakening;
  - **pose as a "great teacher"** able to restore the lost knowledge of the ancients, and recruit them;
  - **dominate and enslave** them (kiss, bewitch monster) and begin a slow exploration of the surface through them;
  - **erase himself:** use the neural scrambler so they forget he exists, and lie down again.
  He is brilliant, ancient and patient, and above all does whatever preserves his own existence: he flees by Arcane Shift or Levitation up the shaft rather than die.
  - **Great Teacher, khepri hierarch** → [[Great Teacher (SOTN)]] (variant of [[Khepri Hierarch]]): MV 120’, AC 8 (MM AC 4 + silkweave suit), HD 11*****, hp 53, 2 fists 0+ D 1d12 extraordinary bludgeoning each, or arcane sword 0+ D 2d8 two-handed (2d6 one-handed), or kiss 0+, or neural scrambler, C11, ML +2, AL C, XP 5,000. True face (opening the carapace: viewers save vs Paralysis or cower), kiss (adjacent cowering or helpless humanoid; Death save: fail dies, pass enslaved), spell-like abilities as [[Khepri Hierarch]] (at will: body equilibrium, clairaudiency, clairvoyancy, choking grip, discern invisible, levitation, telepathy, telekinesis; 3/day: adjust self, arcane shift; 1/day: bewitch monster, phantasmal horror, scry, strength of mind, weakness of mind), plus [[Shimmer]] at will as SOTN lists (Judge's call: kept). (SOTN prints AC 8, HD 11******, Save C8, XP 9,000; MM hierarch: AC 4, HD 11*****, C11, XP 5,000.)
- **Loot (on the hierarch):**
  - Long black robe tied with a gold belt set with black sapphires and red rubies (15,000gp; Judge's call 1/6 st.).
  - [[SOTN Magic Items#Arcane Sword]] (its energy crystal has 27 of its 30 rounds left; arcane casters only; 2d6 one-handed, 2d8 two-handed).
  - [[SOTN Magic Items#Khepri Neural Scrambler]] loaded with 5 [[SOTN Magic Items#Energy Crystal|energy crystals]] (5 uses: up to 24 HD of living creatures within 15’, fewest HD first, save vs Spells or paralysed 30 rounds and the 5 minutes before and after are wiped from memory).
  - Pouch: 2 [[SOTN Magic Items#Anagathics]] and 2 [[SOTN Magic Items#Healing Syringe|healing syringes]] (3d6+3 hp each).
  - Under the robe: a [[SOTN Magic Items#Silkweave Suit]] (AC 4, effectively leather armour +2; temperature protection).
  - The tube itself, [[SOTN Magic Items#Cryostasis Tube]].
  - Tally: 15,000gp (the belt; technological wonders excluded; SOTN gives no room total).
