# Nethercity K — Hallowed Hall of Heroes

A war memorial of the corrupt elves, all of it dated c. 2000 BE: the hall where the heroes of the **Bitter War** against the dwarves were entombed, their names carved, their plunder heaped and their gladiatorial rites held in honour of Bel. Red marble with black veins underfoot, mosaics of elven armies, lizardman auxiliaries and the sack of the dwarven vault **Azen Khador** on the walls; 20’ ceilings unless noted; many floors and ceilings cracked by the ancient quakes. It lies on the **Abyssal Level** (the book's K headings name no level; Judge's reading: K1 opens off L9 on the Abyssal Level and the K11 ladder climbs to I14 on the Deep Level). No blighted sinkhole here (the whole Nethercity is a shadowed sinkhole). No light. Hub [[SOTN Hub]]; standing rules [[Nethercity Features]] (doors, wall graves, grave goods, sinkholes). Pages are SOTN's printed pages.

Running: Temple / Tomb column, dungeon level 4 — throw 1d6 every 3 turns, 6+, then 2d10 on `Tables/sotn-wandering-temple-tomb.yaml`; sealed rest and optional clearing as in [[SOTN Hub]]. Area rules: the **K5 corridor** drops two slabs that seal it (save vs Death or crushed; 30 man-days of air); the **K4 golems** wake when the K3 slab door is disturbed.

## Layout
- **Entrances:** (a) the lion-head **heavy bronze door** in K1's north wall, from L9 ([[Nethercity L - Abyssal Level Caves]]); (b) the **stone ladder** at the south end of K11, which climbs into the hollow pillar of I14, the Memorial of the Fallen ([[Nethercity I - Deep Level Southern Cave Complex]]).
- **West wing (galleries and tombs):** K1 → cedar door west → K2 → archway south → **K3**, a 50’ north-south gallery. In K3's west wall a stone slab door opens on a short passage to the hero tomb **K4**. At K3's south end, by a crack in the floor, a mosaic panel is a **secret sliding door** into the trapped corridor **K5**, which runs 60’ west to the hero vault **K6**. Past the crack K3 turns west and opens into the Chamber of Names **K7**.
- **East wing (graves and arena):** a hallway leaves K1's east wall and runs south as the 70’ grave gallery **K12a** (alcove **K12b** midway on its west side) to the L-shaped outer gallery **K8**. K8 has a cedar door west to K7 and opens south into the blood arena **K9**. South of K9 (map) the gladiators' armoury **K10**; its south archway leads into the curving gallery **K11** and the ladder up to I14.
- So K3-K7-K8 and K1-K12-K8 make a loop; K4, K5-K6 and K9-K10-K11 are dead ends (K11 apart from the ladder).

## Rooms (SOTN pp. 91-94)

### K1. Hallowed Hall Antechamber (2000 BE)
- **Exits:** N heavy bronze door (lion head) to L9; W cedar door to K2; E a hallway that turns south (K12a).
- > [!player]
  > A rectangular hall 20’ wide and 30’ long, its ceiling of raw volcanic stone 20’ up. Red marble streaked with black covers walls and floor. In the north wall a 12’-tall bronze door bears a lion's head in high relief. Mosaics on the east and west walls show bronze-armed elves gathering for war. A cedar door leads west; on the east side a hallway runs off and turns south.
- The bronze door is a heavy bronze door for forcing (−4, see [[Nethercity Features]]); it opens on L9's short south tunnel. (SOTN p. 91)

### K2. Hallowed Hall Inner Gallery Antechamber (2000 BE)
- **Exits:** E cedar door to K1; S marble archway into the K3 gallery.
- > [!player]
  > Behind the cedar door is a 15’ by 15’ antechamber floored in red and black marble. A marble arch in the south wall opens onto a gallery 10’ wide with a 20’ ceiling and the same floor.
- Empty. (SOTN p. 91)

### K3. Hallowed Hall Inner Gallery (2000 BE)
- **Exits:** N archway to K2; W stone slab door (15’ south of the arch) to K4; S end, by the crack, the secret mosaic door to K5; the gallery then turns W to K7.
- > [!player]
  > A gallery 10’ wide and 50’ long running north-south under a 20’ ceiling, floored in black-veined red marble. Four mosaic panels line the east wall: a dwarven stronghold blockaded, worn down, stormed and destroyed. A stone slab door stands in the west wall 15’ past the archway. At the south end a wide crack splits the floor just before the gallery bends west.
- **Lore:** Loremastery, or Knowledge (history) at −4: the mosaics show the **siege of Azen Khador**, which ended the Bitter War.
- **Trick:** the last mosaic panel, beside the crack, is a **secret door** (Searching finds it); it slides open onto K5.
- **Slab door to K4:** stone slab, forcing at −12 with up to 3 helpers adding their STR bonus ([[Nethercity Features]]). Disturbing it wakes the K4 golems (1st attempt: stirring in their graves; 2nd: out; 3rd or later: ready, cannot be surprised). (SOTN prints "the stone slab door from J3"; it means this door from K3.) (SOTN p. 91)

### K4. Hallowed Hall Hero Tomb (2000 BE)
- **Exits:** E short passage and slab door to K3.
- > [!player]
  > A short passage opens into a tomb 35’ by 30’ with a 15’ ceiling, panelled in red and black marble that the old quakes have badly cracked. One upright grave niche is cut into each wall. In each stands something like an elven skeleton with four arms, a conical bronze helmet on its skull and a curved bronze short sword in each of its four hands.
- **Monster:** **4 greater undead bone golems** → [[Greater Undead Bone Golem (SOTN)]] (elven souls bound into golem bodies by necromancy; SOTN p. 144): MV 40’ / 120’, AC 7, HD 8**, hp 40 each, 4 masterwork short swords 2+ (HD 8 3+, masterwork +1), D 1d6+1 each, Save F8, ML +4, AL C, XP 1,600 each. They wake when the K3 slab door is disturbed (see K3) and attack anyone who enters; they fight until destroyed. (Statline as SOTN prints it, converted; the bestiary note governs where it differs.)
- **Loot:** each golem carries four **masterwork short swords** (+1 to attack throws and damage, not magic), 650gp each: 16 swords, 10,400gp. Weight not given (Judge's call: 1/6 st each as ACKS II short swords, 2 4/6 st).
- Tally: 10,400gp, 2 4/6 st (Judge's weight). (SOTN p. 92)

### K5. Hallowed Hall Trapped Corridor (2000 BE)
- **Exits:** E secret door to K3; W to K6.
- > [!player]
  > Behind the panel a corridor runs 60’ west. Its walls are polished black marble; floor and 10’ ceiling are laid in black marble slabs 10’ wide and 5’ long. Carved on every floor slab is a winged sun 4’ across, so that each step lands on it.
- **Lore:** the winged sun is the highest holy symbol of the Empyrean gods; the builders meant every visitor to trample it. A follower of Ammonar can avoid the sacrilege by walking along either edge, beside the sun.
- **Trap:** halfway down, pressure plates are hidden in the **edges** of one slab. Roll 1d6 for each character who walks along the edges: **1-4 triggers them**. (Those who walk on the suns do not touch the plates.) When triggered, two marble slabs 10’ wide, 5’ long and 15’ high drop at once, one at each end of the corridor (east and west). Anyone standing beneath either slab saves **vs Death** (Acrobatics **+2**): failure, killed outright; success, the character dives clear, into or out of the corridor (his choice).
- **Sealed in:** the dropped slabs cannot be moved (SOTN: not even by *knock*; ACKS II has no such spell). Each has **50 shp**. Ways through: [[Disintegration]]; *rock to mud* (Judge's call: ACKS II's [[Earth's Mire]] works only on natural, unworked rock and does not touch the slabs; [[Passageway]] opens a way for 6 turns); or digging through as a **50gp construction project**, at most four workers on a slab. Judge's call from ACKS II construction rates ([[Construction Cost, Rate, and Supervision]]): a character works at 1sp a day (2sp with Labor), so four take 125 days (63 with Labor) per slab — far longer than the air lasts. **Air: 30 man-days** (6,000 cubic feet); no food or water. Someone outside can work on the slab from the other side. (SOTN p. 92)

### K6. Hallowed Hall Hero Vault (2000 BE)
- **Exits:** E the K5 corridor (only).
- > [!player]
  > The corridor ends in a vault 20’ wide and 40’ long. On a weapon rack on the west wall hang a curved, silver-hilted short sword in a gleaming ebony scabbard and a bone-white long bow beside a polished ebony quiver; at the foot of the rack lie a cloak and a pair of boots of shimmering, many-coloured cloth. Two bronze chests, locked, sit against the south wall. In the middle of the floor rises a huge heap of silver, electrum and gold coins, tens of thousands of them, crowned with trophies.
- **Loot (rack):**
  - Rune-etched curved sword of orichalcum, silver-and-ivory hilt: **Ardghal** ("high valor"), [[SOTN Magic Items#Sword of Valor]] — a +2 sword; 3/day the wielder inspires courage as a bard, the blade singing a choral epic for 1 round (ACKS II has the near-identical [[Sword +2, Valor]], TT p. 246). Apparent value 650gp. SOTN calls it a shortsword here, a sword with a 36” blade on p. 136 (Judge's call: a short sword, as the rack shows). Its ebony scabbard inlaid with electrum: 550gp, 1/6 st.
  - Dragonbone **long bow +1** (ACKS II: a magic long bow +1 with no power; Judge's call), apparent value 650gp. Ebony quiver inlaid with electrum: 550gp, 1/6 st.
  - Iridescent spidersilk **elven cloak and boots** (ACKS I items → [[Cloak of the Elven Ranger]] and [[Boots of the Elven Ranger]], Judge's call), apparent value 150gp the pair.
- **Loot (chests):** two locked bronze chests, 125gp and 5 st each. Chest 1: 2,000sp, 100 quartzes (10gp each), 15 bloodstones (50gp each), 8 amethysts (100gp each), 1 ruby (1,000gp). Chest 2: 2,000sp, 40 small crystals (10gp each), 4 emeralds (1,000gp each).
- **Loot (tribute pile):** 10,000sp, 10,000ep, 10,000gp; a gold goblet set with four red garnets (1,000gp); a collar cut from one sheet of gold (800gp); a pair of gold earrings (500gp each); a carved ivory war-horn, [[SOTN Magic Items#Horn of the Eagles]] (summons a large roc as a winged steed, which serves a Lawful blower for up to a day; once a week; SOTN p. 132 — not the ACKS II [[Horn of the Eagles]]), apparent value 750gp; a gold trumpet (1,200gp, 3/6 st); a chryselephantine statuette of a dwarf warrior with a hammer (900gp, 2/6 st).
- **Lore:** Loremastery, or Knowledge (history) at −4: the coins are of the mint of **Azen Khador**, greatest dwarven vault of antiquity (ancient coin: see "attracting attention", [[Nethercity Features]]). Loremastery or Theology: the statuette is a dwarven image of **Türas**, Empyrean god of justice.
- Tally: 30,600gp, 45 st (magic excluded). (SOTN p. 92)

### K7. Hallowed Hall Chamber of Names (2000 BE)
- **Exits:** NE corner a hallway E to K3; S end a cedar door E to K8.
- > [!player]
  > A room 30’ by 35’ whose polished marble walls are cut with the names of thousands of elves, from the red and black floor to the ceiling 20’ above. The ceiling is a mosaic seen from above: a battle between an army of lizardmen led by elves and a dwarven host, too cracked by quakes to tell which battle. A hallway leaves the north-east corner going east; a cedar door at the south end opens east.
- The names are Ancient Argollëan inscriptions ([[Nethercity Features]]): the fallen of the Bitter War. Nothing of value. (SOTN p. 93)

### K8. Hallowed Hall Outer Gallery (2000 BE)
- **Exits:** W cedar door to K7; S to K9; NE to K12a.
- > [!player]
  > An L-shaped gallery, 30’ long and 30’ wide, 20’ high. Red marble with black veins underfoot, black marble flecked with red on the walls, which are pierced with dozens of horizontal grave niches, many cracked and split. A cedar door leads west; the gallery runs on south and north-east.
- **Graves:** 48 horizontal wall graves: three columns of eight on the north wall, two of eight on the west, one of eight on the south. c. 2000 BE, usual inscriptions, remains and goods: expected total 672gp (4d6, avg 14gp each), about 6 4/6 st. (SOTN p. 93)

### K9. Hallowed Hall Blood Arena (2000 BE)
- **Exits:** N to K8; S to K10 (map).
- > [!player]
  > A 60’ by 40’ chamber floored with an inch of brown sand, streaked with great dark-brown stains. Low stone benches run the length of the north wall, their red and black cushions rotted away. On the west wall a mosaic shows gladiators fighting under the gaze of a six-armed demonic warrior; on the east wall the dead are carried off while the living celebrate.
  > As you enter, a ghostly shape forms in the middle of the sand: a lizardman in bronze armour with a double-bladed axe, its body torn open by terrible wounds.
- **Monster:** **flailing spirit** → [[Flailing Spirit (SOTN)]] (SOTN p. 143): MV fly 80’ / 240’, AC 8, HD 7****, hp 35, 4 touches 4+, D 1d6 + energy drain (SOTN: 1d6 × 1,000 XP; the bestiary note gives the ACKS II reading, enervation), Save F6, ML +4, AL C, XP 1,840 (ACKS II 7****, see its note; SOTN prints 7** and 1,300). Immune to mundane weapons and cold; undead immunities. It haunts the arena and attacks at once.
- **Lore:** Loremastery or Theology: the six-armed figure is **Bel**, the Slaughterprince, Chthonic god of war. (SOTN p. 93)

### K10. Hallowed Hall Gladiator's Armory (2000 BE)
- **Exits:** N to K9 (map); S rock archway to K11.
- > [!player]
  > A 25’ by 20’ room. Six hardwood weapon racks have fallen over on the rock floor, scattering old bronze arms everywhere. A rough archway leads south.
- **Loot:** 18 bronze short swords, 6 bronze axes, 6 bronze-headed spears, green with patina and corroded. No value given (cheap goods: [[Nethercity Features]] treasure). (SOTN p. 93)

### K11. Hallowed Hall Gladiator's Gallery (2000 BE)
- **Exits:** N archway to K10; S stone ladder up through the ceiling into the I14 pillar ([[Nethercity I - Deep Level Southern Cave Complex]]).
- > [!player]
  > A gallery 10’ wide curving 50’ from south-west to north-east. An archway closes its north end; at the south end a stone ladder climbs into a hole in the ceiling. Mosaics of warriors fighting ritual duels for the glory of a six-armed demonic warrior cover the walls.
- **Trick:** the ladder comes up inside the inscribed pillar of the Memorial of the Fallen (I14).
- **Lore:** Loremastery or Theology: the six-armed figure is **Bel**, the Slaughterprince. (SOTN p. 93)

### K12. Hallowed Hall Inner Gallery (2000 BE)

### K12a. Hallowed Hall Inner Gallery (2000 BE)
- **Exits:** N end to K1's east hallway; S end to K8 (north-east arm); W midway, alcove K12b.
- > [!player]
  > A hallway 70’ long and 10’ wide; its walls are pierced from floor to the 20’ ceiling with horizontal grave niches. Several have caved in, crushing whatever lay inside.
- **Graves:** 144 wall graves: 12 columns of eight on the east wall, 6 of eight on the west. c. 2000 BE, usual inscriptions, remains and goods: expected total 2,016gp (avg 14gp each), about 20 st. Looting takes the usual 10 graves per person per turn. (SOTN pp. 93-94)

### K12b. Hallowed Hall Inner Gallery Alcove (2000 BE)
- **Exits:** E to K12a.
- > [!player]
  > Halfway along the hallway the west wall opens into an alcove 15’ by 20’. Mosaics on its north, south and west walls show ranks of elven soldiers in an archaic style, forming up for battle.
- **Trick:** when anyone steps into the alcove the mosaic comes alive (a permanent programmed illusion). For one turn an elven wizard rides before the ranks and gives a rousing speech in Ancient Argollëan to the sound of an epic choir. **Elves** who watch it gain the benefit of **Inspire Courage** as from a [[Bard]] (inspired: +1 to attack throws, AC, morale and saves against magical fear, for 1 turn), whether or not they understand the words.
- **Lore:** Loremastery or Knowledge (history), **+4** for a speaker of Ancient Argollëan: the scene is the famous speech of the elven king **Marcan**, victor of the Bitter War. Knowing Marcan's name lets a character bypass certain traps in the Great King Tomb ([[Nethercity Q - Great King Tomb]]). (SOTN p. 94)
