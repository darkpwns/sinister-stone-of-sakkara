# Nethercity A — Entrance Caves

The way in. The burst floor of the Old City's great cesspool (A1, under the public latrines, [[Old District]] #12/#12u) spills the city's sewage down a chain of black volcanic lava caves, the oldest workings of the Nethercity (3200-3000 BE): A1 at the cesspool, A2 on the **Mezzanine** level, A3 and A4 on the **Upper** level (A4's level is not named; Judge's inference from the gradual incline down from A2 and the 20’ cliff down to the C2 floor). The river of **sewer sludge** with its miasma runs through every room (A1 → A2 → A3/A4 → C1/C2a), and millipede-and-crayfish swarms graze on it. No blighted sinkhole here. No light: the caves are dark. Hub [[SOTN Hub]]; standing rules (sludge, miasma, doors, wall graves) [[Nethercity Features]]; background [[Nethercity History]]. Pages are SOTN printed pages.

Running: wandering 1d6 every 3 turns, 6+ → 2d10 on the **Sewer Caves** column (`Tables/sotn-wandering-sewer-caves.yaml`), setting level 2 (coordinator's call). Everyone in or beside the sludge: sewer sludge and miasma rules ([[Nethercity Features#Sewer sludge (SOTN p. 12)]]). Swarms do not attack anyone who flies, climbs the walls or crosses by rope (A2, A3).

## Layout
- **Entrance:** the cesspool A1, 30’ below the latrines at the heart of the Old City. There is no man-sized way down from the latrine itself ([[Old District]] #12); the party reaches A1 through the Undercity sewers (its NW and NE archways, [[Undercity of Cyfaraun]]). A door-sized hole in A1's floor drops into a tunnel and winding rough-hewn steps (the carrion horror waits in the tunnel) down to **A2**.
- **A2** (Mezzanine): the sludge goes on south down rough-hewn steps to **A3** and east down a gradual incline to **A4**.
- **A3** (Upper): rough-hewn steps south down to **C1** ([[Nethercity C - Central Caves]]); an 8’ natural opening east to **A4**; the round stone slab in the west wall to **B1** ([[Nethercity B - Early Chthonic Tomb]]).
- **A4** (Upper): a heavy bronze door in the north-east wall to **D1** ([[Nethercity D - Warlord's Tomb and Chapel to Bel]]); the sludge pours south over a **20’ natural cliff** to the floor of **C2a** ([[Nethercity C - Central Caves]]).

## Rooms (SOTN pp. 24-26)

### A1. Cesspit Cave (3200 BE)
- **Exits:** NW and NE archways into large sewer tunnels (Undercity); the door-sized hole in the floor → tunnel → winding rough-hewn steps down to A2. Up to the latrine: pipes only.
- The cesspool itself, its frescoes and the burst floor are keyed in AX3 as Old District **#12u**: see [[Old District]] (#12 Public Latrine, #12U Leaking Cesspool). SOTN's text for A1 (p. 24) is the same room; the summary there applies, with the details below.

> [!player]
> A pit 30’ beneath the latrines, walls and floor thick with muck, mould and refuse, faint painted figures showing through the filth. Archways in the north-west and north-east open into big sewer tunnels, but most of the slow flow of waste is draining into a door-sized hole in the floor instead.

- **Monster:** in the tunnel below the hole, out of sight from the cesspool, a carcass scavenger feeds on the animal waste and the odd body washed down. **+1 to its surprise roll** against adventurers coming down the steps (its hidden position).
  **1 carrion horror** → [[Carrion Horror]]: MV 40’/120’, AC 2, HD 3+1***, hp 14, 8 stingers 7+, D 1 piercing + paralysis (Paralysis save or paralysed 2d4 turns), F2, ML +2, AL N, XP 170 (SOTN prints MV 120’, HD 3+1, XP 135). It keeps stinging a victim who resisted and devours a paralysed one when nobody engages it.
- **Lore:** anyone can scrape the walls clean (sludge risk: [[Nethercity Features#Sewer sludge (SOTN p. 12)]]) to uncover a procession of white-gowned elven maidens carrying torches. A **Loremastery or Theology** throw then names it the worship of **Mityara** (Empyrean goddess of community, charity and civic virtue; Demara to the elves, patron of the Northern Argollëans).
- **Lore:** **Caving, Engineering or Mapping**, examining the hole: years of sewage pounding on the floor above the tunnel broke it open.
- SOTN's sidebar: A1 is location 12u on the Undercity map.

### A2. Mezzanine Level Cave (3200 BE)
- **Exits:** steps NE up to A1; rough-hewn steps S down to A3; gradual incline E down to A4.

> [!player]
> A kidney-shaped cave of volcanic rock, 30’ by 40’. Sludge pours in down the steps from the north-east and drains away down more rough steps to the south and down a gentle slope to the east. Between the two stairs the whole floor seethes with millipedes and cave crayfish feeding on the muck.

- **Monster:** one swarm covering the whole cave; it attacks anything moving through or near it. Fliers, climbers and those crossing by rope are not attacked.
  **1 insect swarm** → [[Insect Swarm]]: MV 10’/30’, AC 2, HD 4*, hp 24, engulf (automatic in its area), D 2 piercing (4 if unarmoured with natural AC 3 or less; halved for those warding it off or moving 20’+ away; a torch deals it 1d4 fire), then vexed 3 rounds after escaping, F0, ML +3, AL N, XP 135; immune to bludgeoning, piercing and slashing. (SOTN prints HD 4, Save NM. MM area for 4 HD is 20’ × 20’; SOTN has it fill the 30’ × 40’ cave: Judge's call, it covers the floor between the stairs, as SOTN says.)

### A3. Upper Level Cave (3100 BE)
- **Exits:** rough-hewn steps N up to A2; rough-hewn steps S down to C1; 8’ natural opening E to A4; round stone slab door W to B1.

> [!player]
> A triangular lava cave, 60’ by 30’, thick with fumes and a loathsome stench. Sludge runs down rough steps from the north and away down more steps to the south, and out through an 8’ natural opening to the east. Millipedes and cave crayfish carpet the flow and block the ways out, and six dog-sized white prawns are eating them. Against the west wall stands a round stone slab, perhaps half a ton, that might be rolled aside with great effort. The north-east wall holds 15 horizontal wall graves, five columns of three, each about 6’ wide, 3’ deep and 2’ high; most have a skeleton in them.

- **Monster:** three swarms and six tunnel prawns. The swarms attack whatever moves through or near them (fliers, climbers and rope-crossers excepted); if the swarms are disturbed the prawns attack too.
  **3 insect swarms** → [[Insect Swarm]]: MV 10’/30’, AC 2, HD 2*, hp 13, 11, 7, engulf (automatic in area; 20’ × 10’ each), D 2 piercing (4 if unarmoured with natural AC 3 or less; halved if warded off), F0, ML +3, AL N, XP 29 each; immune to bludgeoning, piercing and slashing.
  **6 tunnel prawns** → [[Tunnel Prawn (SOTN)]]: MV 60’, climb 60’, AC 5, HD 1, hp 8, 7, 5, 4, 4, 3, 2 pincers 10+, D 1d2 each, F1, ML +1, AL N, XP 10 each (SOTN's appendix gives ML +2; the room's +1 used). A fresh prawn sells for 3gp (a day's rations of meat).
- **Door:** the slab to B1 is a stone slab door (−12 to force, up to 3 helpers; [[Nethercity Features#Doors (SOTN p. 11)]]). Disturbing it wakes the 12 skeletons in B1; how many attempts it takes sets how ready they are.
- **Graves:** 15 wall graves of about 3100 BE: the usual inscriptions, remains and goods ([[Nethercity Features#Wall graves (SOTN pp. 14-16)]]). Tally: 30gp, about 2/6 st.

### A4. Warlord's Cavern Entrance (3000 BE)
- **Exits:** W to A3; heavy bronze door NE to D1; S over the 20’ cliff (with the sludge) to C2a.

> [!player]
> A volcanic cavern, 50’ by 40’. The sludge comes in from the west and spills south over a natural cliff about 20’ high. A heavy bronze door is set in the north-east wall; the south-east wall is carved in low relief with a line of elven warriors drawn up for battle. The south-west wall is covered by a patch of reddish fungus whose twisted roots reach down into the sludge.

- **Monster:** the "fungus" is a fungal creeper, feeding on the minerals in the wall and the organic matter in the sludge. It has eaten every swarm in the room. It senses warm bodies within 60’ and creeps toward them.
  **1 fungal creeper** → [[Fungal Creeper (SOTN)]]: MV 10’/30’, AC 2, HD 3*, hp 18, appendage 8+, D 1d6; on a natural 19-20 it latches on and deals 1d6 every round thereafter with no further throw, F1, ML 0, AL N, XP 65; senses warmth 60’; immune to bewitch, sleep and halt effects, stunning and poisons not made for plants; vulnerable to spells affecting plants; distracted by fresh meat (rations no more than a day old, or an unconscious body). (No MM equivalent; SOTN appendix p. 143. SOTN prints MV 30’; the appendix gives 30’ (10’).)
- **Door:** heavy bronze door to D1 (−4 to force, 1 helper; [[Nethercity Features#Doors (SOTN p. 11)]]).
- **The cliff:** 20’ down to C2a beside the falling sludge; climbing or a rope (Judge's call: SOTN gives no throw; use the ACKS II climbing rules).
