# Nethercity G — Grand Chthonic Temple

The great temple of the Chthonic gods, raised around the martyr tomb of an early high priest (F4) and finished in the late 26th century BE (c. 2500 BE): galleries and halls panelled in white-speckled black marble (black-whorled purple in the holiest rooms, red-speckled in the warriors' wing and the 2200 BE lizardman tomb), black marble tiled floors, colonnades carved as great trees, mosaics of elven court life, Chthonic rites and wars. Iskara the Demon Queen and Nasga the Serpent are its chief goddesses; the sanctum G16 hides the temple treasury, the high priest's vault G21 its coin. Around the temple proper run the burial galleries G7-G8 and G15 (2500-2300 BE), the tombs of an enchantress (G9, 2400 BE), a consul (G10-G12, 2300 BE) and a king (G13-G14, 2200 BE), and on its northern edge the older caves G17-G18 (2600-2700 BE). Physical level: the main temple lies on the **Lower Level** (bronze door from F14; steps up from G3 to the martyr tomb F4); the King Tomb G14, down the stair from G13, is on the **Abyssal Level** (SOTN names no level for the rest; Judge's reading). **Blighted sinkhole of evil:** the chancel G6a (Iskara's altar-statue); the rest is the Nethercity's ordinary shadowed sinkhole. No light is mentioned anywhere: the temple is dark (Judge's call). Pages are SOTN printed pages. Hub [[SOTN Hub]]; standing rules (doors, inscriptions, sarcophagi, wall graves, mummies by era, sinkholes) [[Nethercity Features]]; background [[Nethercity History]].

Running: wandering monsters on the **Temple / Tomb** column, `level: 4` (`Tables/sotn-wandering-temple-tomb.yaml`): 1d6 every 3 turns, 6+ → 2d10 (SOTN p. 18). Extra throws: smashing the tiles in G1a, and the G16 statue's thundering threat. **Purification (G4):** anyone who washes at the G4 font is ignored by the faewyrd and the temple's other guardians (the G16 statue) inside area G only, for as long as the font's blessing or bane lasts (6 turns, Judge's call from ACKS I bless). **Dark whispers:** a character who makes a Listening throw near a faewyrd (G2a, G2b, G3, G6c, G7b, G24, G26) hears it and must save vs Death or be **maddened** (see [[Lesser Faewyrd (SOTN)]]: −2 Listening, −2 surprise, −1 spellcasting initiative, 1 in 6 per rest of night terrors with no rest, −1 WIS at each initiative while he still hears whispers, −1 to the save per extra faewyrd heard; remove curse by a caster of level ≥ the faewyrd's HD ends it).

## Layout
- **Entrances:** (a) the heavy bronze door (−4) from the Grand Chthonic Vestibule **F14** into the nave **G1a**; (b) steps up through an archway in the east wall of the narthex **G3** to the martyr tomb **F4**; (c) the secret door in the mosaic of **G2a** to the secret alcove **F16** ([[Nethercity F - Lower Level Central Complex]]); (d) the hidden trapdoor under the broken tiles of **G1a**, down to **S1** ([[Nethercity S - Abyssal Level Hidden Tombs]]); (e) the winding stair at the Y-intersection **G8** down to **R1** ([[Nethercity R - Abyssal Level Southern Cave]]); (f) the well in the annex of **G14**, 10’ above the river at **P9** ([[Nethercity P - Sub-Abyssal River Banks]]); (g) the hole in the ceiling of the tunnel from **G18**, 10’ up, to **C11** ([[Nethercity C - Central Caves]]); (h) the cedar door at the east end of **G19**, north to **H19** ([[Nethercity H - Great Temple of Awakening]]).

## Factions
No living faction holds the temple. Its **guardians** (the faewyrd of G2b, G6e and G25, and the G16 statue) spare anyone purified at G4 unless he despoils what they guard (the G6a statue's onyx eyes, the G25 remains) or attacks them. The **tomb occupants** (G9, G10-G13, G14) wake by their slab doors, not by trespass in the temple; SOTN does not say the purification restrains them (Judge's call: it does not, though a purified party may get a hearing from a woken mummy). The G21 cobras spare only elves who show Chthonic holy symbols. The king of G14 is father of the prince and princess entombed in S ([[Nethercity S - Abyssal Level Hidden Tombs]]). See also [[Nethercity Factions]].

### G1. Grand Chthonic Nave (2500 BE)
Two wings of one hall, G1a and G1b (SOTN p. 51).

### G1a. Grand Chthonic Nave East (2500 BE)
- **Exits:** E heavy bronze door (−4) to F14; N 20’ archway (centre of the north wall) to G2a; W 20’ archway to G1b; trapdoor under the tiles (hidden) down to S1.
- > [!player]
  > A hall 45’ wide and 40’ long, walls faced with black marble flecked with white, floor of black marble tiles, raw tuff ceiling 15’ up. A heavy bronze door in the east wall; south-west of it the tiles are heaved up and cracked, as if by an old earthquake. Broad 20’ arches open north (onto a wide gallery) and west (into another wing).
- **Trick:** a character searching the broken tiles finds a **trapdoor** (down to S1: a stone ladder, [[Nethercity S - Abyssal Level Hidden Tombs]]) with a Searching throw (secret doors). **Smashing and lifting the tiles** finds it automatically but triggers an **immediate wandering monster throw**.

### G1b. Grand Chthonic Nave West (2500 BE)
- **Exits:** N a narrower corridor from the middle of the north wall (G6c); E 20’ archway to G1a.
- > [!player]
  > A 30’ square hall in the same white-flecked black marble, tiled floor, bare tuff ceiling 15’ overhead. A narrower passage leaves the middle of the north wall; a 20’ arch opens east.

### G2. Grand Chthonic Templon (2500 BE)
A long colonnaded corridor, G2a to the south and G2b to the north (SOTN p. 52).

### G2a. Grand Chthonic Templon South (2500 BE)
- **Exits:** S/E to G1a (arch); W bronze-inlaid cedar door at the south end of the west wall (to G6d and the chancel); N along the colonnade to G2b; secret door in the east wall to F16.
- > [!player]
  > A corridor 20’ wide, walls of white-flecked black marble set with rich mosaics: elf lords and ladies waited on by white-furred apes and lizard-like creatures on two legs. Black marble floor. At the south end of the west wall, a cedar door inlaid with bronze. 30’ north of it a colonnade begins, columns carved as great trees rising to a 20’ ceiling; two pairs stand in the light, the rest is lost in the dark.
- **Trick:** a **secret door** in the east wall's mosaic: pressing the eye of one elf noble releases the lock and the door turns on its vertical axis. Beyond: the Grand Chthonic Secret Alcove **F16** ([[Nethercity F - Lower Level Central Complex]]).
- **Noise:** a Listening throw here catches blasphemous whispers from the north (the faewyrd of G2b; SOTN says "the greater faewyrd in G2b", an error: G2b holds five lesser ones): save vs Death or **maddened**.

### G2b. Grand Chthonic Templon North (2500 BE)
- **Exits:** S into the dark (G2a); N marble-framed archway to the narthex G3.
- > [!player]
  > The 20’ corridor runs on between ten tree-carved columns over 60’, ceiling 20’ up; white-flecked black marble walls, black marble floor. South, the corridor goes on into darkness; north, a marble-framed arch leads deeper in. Halfway along the colonnade, five figures in dark robes kneel at prayer.
- **Monster:** the five are **faewyrd**. Anyone entering this northern half **without having washed at the font G4** makes them rise from prayer and attack. Purified characters pass unmolested.
  - **5 faewyrd** → [[Lesser Faewyrd (SOTN)]]: MV 40’ / 120’, AC 5, HD 4***, hp 21, 20, 19, 18, 16, chilling touch 7+, D 1d4 + WIS drain, Save E4, ML +4, AL C, XP 245 each (SOTN 4**, 190). Dark whispers within 5’ (−1 to the save per extra faewyrd heard: up to −4 here). SOTN's line here omits their immunity to ordinary weapons; the appendix gives it (applied).
- **Noise:** a Listening throw in G2b hears their whispers: save vs Death or maddened.

### G3. Grand Chthonic Narthex (2500 BE)
- **Exits:** W bronze-inlaid cedar doors near the north corner (to G15b) and at the centre (to G5); E a third such door at the south tip of the east wall (to G4); S 15’ marble-lined archway (to G2b); E steps up through a second archway (to F4).
- > [!player]
  > A 50’ × 70’ hall under a vaulted ceiling 20’ high, white-flecked black marble walls, black tiled floor. Two bronze-inlaid cedar doors in the west wall (one near the north corner, one central) and a third at the south end of the east wall. A 15’ marble-lined arch opens south; through another arch in the east wall, steps climb upward.
- **Noise:** a Listening throw here hears whispers from the south (the G2b faewyrd): save vs Death or maddened.

### G4. Grand Chthonic Purification Font (2500 BE)
- **Exits:** W cedar door to G3.
- > [!player]
  > A 15’ × 25’ room walled in white-flecked marble around a font of bronze and marble, full of water that looks clear and clean. Ancient script is cut into the wall above it.
- **Lore:** the inscription (Ancient Argollëan; others see [[Nethercity Features#Inscriptions (SOTN p. 12)]]) bids worshippers wash off their shame before entering the holy halls.
- **Trick:** the font refills itself forever (a permanent [[Create Water]]). **Chaotic** characters who wash in it gain the benefit of a bless (Judge's call: as [[Holy Blessing]], +1 to attack throws, AC, morale and saves against magical fear, 6 turns); **Lawful and Neutral** characters who wash suffer the reverse, a *bane* (Judge's call: no ACKS II equivalent; −1 to attack throws, AC and saves against magical fear, 6 turns). Drinking the water only quenches thirst. **While the effect lasts, the washer is purified:** the faewyrd and the other guardians of area G do not attack him. No protection outside area G. (SOTN gives the protection to "the purified characters" whatever their alignment, so a Lawful washer suffers the bane but still passes; Judge's reading.)

### G5. Grand Chthonic Vestry (2500 BE)
- **Exits:** S door with bronze serpentine fretwork (to G6b); E similar door (to G3).
- > [!player]
  > An L-shaped side room, 25’ × 20’, white-flecked black marble walls set with hooks. Two doors inlaid with bronze worked in coiling serpents (south and east). Four cedar chests bound in bronze, each shut with a serpent-shaped clasp, have survived the ages.
- **Loot:** the chests are **locked, not trapped**:
  - #1: a bundle of religious record scrolls (**675gp, 1 st**) listing the race, homeland and date of death of sacrificial victims taken in tribute, 2500-1700 BE.
  - #2: black-and-silver altar linens, tattered and worthless.
  - #3: a large temple hanging of a snake coiled in a figure-eight devouring its tail; rotten, worthless.
  - #4: 4 vials of cassia, cinnamon and myrrh scent (**75gp, 1/6 st each**) and 7 sticks of frankincense (**20gp each**).
  - Tally: 1,115gp, 1 4/6 st.
- **Lore:** read in Ancient Argollëan, the scrolls record thousands of elven sacrifices of humans and dwarves, 2500-1700 BE: each victim's race, sex, age, homeland, date and manner of death and the god who received it. Iskara: mature men and women, hearts cut out. Dirgion: elders, skulls smashed. Bel: young men, ritual combat. Nasga: young women, poison or strangling. Dwarves and humans mixed until 2000 BE, almost all humans after. A character who follows these prescriptions gains **+1 to blood sacrifice throws** (ACKS II blood sacrifice).
- **Lore:** Loremastery or Theology throw: the tail-devouring snake is Iskara's holy symbol as **Empress of Eternity**.

### G6. Grand Chthonic Chancel and Environs (2500 BE)

### G6a. Grand Chthonic Chancel (2500 BE)
- **Exits:** N tattered black curtain (to G6b); S two corridors either side of the statue (SW to G6c, SE to G6d).
- > [!player]
  > A chamber 35’ wide and 60’ long, 20’ ceiling, white-flecked black marble walls and black tiled floor. A ragged black curtain hangs across the north wall. Two large half-round marble daises are built into the east and west walls. Filling the middle of the south wall stands an 8’ statue in ivory and gold of a beautiful elf woman with black eyes and dragon's wings; passages lead south on either side of it.
- **Lore / Trick:** Theology throw: the figure is **Iskara**, the Demon Queen, Chthonic goddess of chaos. The statue is a chaotic altar: G6a is a **blighted sinkhole of evil**: Lawful divine casters rebuke (turn) undead as if **four class levels lower** ([[Nethercity Features#Sinkholes of evil (SOTN p. 12; ACKS II sinkholes of evil, RR)]]).
- **Loot / Trick / Monster:** each of the statue's eyes is a **large onyx (1,000gp each)**. If the statue is disturbed, the **greater faewyrd** (G6e) comes in from G6c and attacks the looters. It ignores purified characters (G4) unless they carry an onyx eye or attack the undead. Tally: 2,000gp (the eyes; weight negligible). (SOTN gives the statue itself no value; Judge's call: stripped like the G16 statue it would yield gold 2,000gp, 2 st and ivory 1,000gp, 10 st (SOTN p. 150), and stripping it disturbs it; not in the tally.)

- **Monster:** the last high priest of the temple, killed in the great earthquake that ruined the Nethercity, now a **greater faewyrd**. It lurks unseen in the walls and attacks anyone entering the alcove (unless purified at G4) or defacing the statue in G6a.
  - **Greater faewyrd** → [[Grand Chthonic High Priest (SOTN)]] ([[Greater Faewyrd (SOTN)]]): MV 40’ / 120’, AC 9, HD 8***, hp 39, chilling touch 3+, D 1d10 + 2 WIS drained for 8 turns, Save E8, ML +4, AL C, XP 2,100.
