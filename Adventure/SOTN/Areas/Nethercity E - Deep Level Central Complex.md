# Nethercity E — Deep Level Central Complex

A plaster-walled, black-marble-floored complex of galleries lined with wall graves (2800-2600 BE), reached from the huge deep cavern C2a. Its **Deep Level** (E1-E5) is a long north-south gallery (E2), its tiles heaved up along the centreline by an ancient quake, ending in a T: west to a common tomb (E5), east down a slope to the troglodyte caves at I1. A stair from its midpoint climbs 20’ to the **Upper Level** (E6-E9), a gallery joining D9 to F1 with three sealed tombs. Four mummies of 2600 BE lie in sealed tombs: a **transmogrifier** and her baboon-viper servant (E3), a **courtier** patriarch with his family (E7-E8), and a **nightblade** torturer (E9). Made after 2800 BE, they kept a third of their HD and class abilities ([[Nethercity Features#Necromancy and the mummies (SOTN pp. 16-17)|necromancy by era]]). Frescoes of elves with strange machines and flying silver discs (E2c, E4a) cannot be identified. **Blighted sinkholes:** none. **Light:** none; the area is dark. Hub [[SOTN Hub]]; standing rules (doors, wall graves, sarcophagi, mummies, treasure) in [[Nethercity Features]].

Running: wandering throws 1d6 every 3 turns, 6+ → 2d10 on the Temple / Tomb column, `Tables/sotn-wandering-temple-tomb.yaml` (level 4). Each mummy wakes when its stone slab door is first disturbed (1st attempt: stirring; 2nd: out of the sarcophagus; 3rd or later: ready, unsurprisable, and the E3 and E9 casters will have cast their preparation spells). The troglodyte hunters of D8 pass through E6-E2-E4 on their way between D and I; their tracks are here.

## Layout
- **Entrances:** (a) the heavy bronze double doors in the south wall of C2a ([[Nethercity C - Central Caves]]) into **E1**; (b) the cedar door at the east end of **E6a** from D9 ([[Nethercity D - Warlord's Tomb and Chapel to Bel]]); (c) the heavy bronze door at the west end of **E6b** from F1 ([[Nethercity F - Lower Level Central Complex]]); (d) the cave mouth at the foot of **E4c**, from I1 ([[Nethercity I - Deep Level Southern Cave Complex]]).
- **Deep Level:** E1 → bronze double doors S → **E2a** (stone slab door in its west wall, 15’ south of the doors, to **E3**) → **E2b** (archway and stair up W to E6b) → **E2c** (frescoed alcove W) → the T of **E4a**: W along **E4b** (80’) to **E5**; E down **E4c** (27% grade, 90’) to the cave mouth of I1.
- **Upper Level:** **E6a** runs west 40’ from the D9 cedar door (heavy bronze door to **E7** in its north wall beside it), turns north into **E6b**, which runs 60’ west to the F1 bronze door; in E6b's south wall a stone slab door to **E9**, and 15’ east of it the stair down to E2b. **E7** → stone slab door (E wall, NE corner) → **E8**.
- **Troglodyte trail** (D8 – D9 – E6a – E6b – E2b – E2c – E4a – E4c – I1): tracks in E2b, E2c, E4a, E4c, E6a, E6b.

## Rooms (SOTN pp. 37-42)

### E1. Deep Level Complex Entrance (2700 BE)
- **Exits:** heavy bronze double doors N to C2a (entry); heavy bronze double doors S to E2a.
- > [!player]
  > An antechamber 40’ wide and 20’ deep, ceiling 15’, plastered walls and black marble floor. In the south wall a second pair of heavy bronze doors stands in a marble frame carved with rows of discs.
- **Doors:** heavy bronze (−4, 2 helpers for a double door); they push into E2a ([[Nethercity Features#Doors (SOTN p. 11)|doors]]).

### E2. Deep Level Complex Gallery (2700 BE)
The 15’ wide north-south gallery from E1 to the E4 T-junction. Along its whole length the black marble tiles are heaved up in a ridge, as if something had burrowed beneath. **Lore (E2a, E2b, E2c):** a dwarf, or a character with Caving or Engineering, who spends one turn examining the floor sees the ridge was pushed up by compression in an ancient earthquake. (Its graves are dated 2800 BE in the text, though the heading says 2700.)

### E2a. Deep Level Complex Gallery Northern Terminus
- **Exits:** bronze double doors N to E1; stone slab door in the W wall, 15’ south of the doors, to E3; gallery S to E2b.
- > [!player]
  > From the bronze doors a 15’ wide gallery of plaster and black marble runs south into the dark. Its tiles ripple upward along the whole visible length, as if a land-shark's fin had ploughed beneath them. Wall graves line both sides, broken on the west by a stone slab door some 15’ south of the bronze doors, worn smooth as if by many hands over the centuries.
- **Graves:** 48 wall graves (30 E wall, 18 W wall), stacked four high, circa 2800 BE, usual inscriptions, remains and goods (96 gp in all).
- **Door:** the E3 slab (−12, up to 3 helpers) wakes the transmogrifier and her servant.

### E2b. Deep Level Complex Gallery Midpoint
- **Exits:** gallery N to E2a and S to E2c; 10’ archway in the W wall to a stair curving up 20’ to the T-junction in E6b.
- > [!player]
  > The plastered gallery continues, its black marble floor heaved up along the middle as if by a burrowing beast. In the west wall a 10’ archway opens on a hewn stair curving up and north-west out of sight.
- **Lore:** a Tracking throw (+4 with scent) finds the tracks of a small band of troglodytes, come from E2c and gone up to E6b.

### E2c. Deep Level Complex Gallery Southern Terminus
- **Exits:** gallery N to E2b; S into the T-junction E4a.
- > [!player]
  > In the west wall a 10’ × 20’ alcove bears a faded fresco of elves studying strange machinery, with a few graves cut around it; the east wall is lined with more graves. South of the alcove the gallery meets a T running west and east, and at the T's southern tip another frescoed alcove shows elves flying through the sky on silver discs. The floor tiles still ripple along the gallery as if a worm had passed beneath.
- **Graves:** 48 wall graves (30 E wall, 18 W wall), circa 2800 BE, usual inscriptions, remains and goods (96 gp in all).
- **Lore:** the machines and discs in the frescoes cannot be identified, even with Loremastery. A Tracking throw (+4 with scent) finds troglodyte tracks from E4a toward E2b.

### E3. Deep Level Complex Transmogrifier Tomb (2600 BE)
- **Exits:** stone slab door E to E2a only.
- > [!player]
  > Past the slab, a 25’ × 20’ plastered tomb with a low stone dais in the middle bearing a limestone sarcophagus carved as an elf with hideously misshapen, scaly skin. Frescoes on the north and south walls show the same deformed elf at grotesque surgery on apes, men and lizards. The west wall is one upright grave holding a mummified baboon-like creature with scales and a viper's fangs.
- **Monster:** both undead wake when the E2a slab is first disturbed (see Running). Given time (a party slow with the door), the transmogrifier casts **Ogre Strength** and **Chameleon** on the baboon-viper before it attacks. (SOTN gender: the text calls the transmogrifier both "his" and "her"; Judge's call: she, after the tomb-door line.)
  - **Mummy transmogrifier** → [[Mummy]] with the abilities of a 4th-level [[Elven Wizard]] of transmogrification: INT 18; MV 15’ / 45’, AC 8, HD 5+1****, hp 26, slam 5+, D 1d12 + mummy rot, F5, ML +4, AL C, XP 1,060; horrific visage, mummy immunities (MM); **abominable mutation:** scaly skin, +2 AC, −25% speed; **transmogrification:** casts transmogrification spells at 6th level, targets −2 on saves against them. Spells 2 1st, 2 2nd; repertoire: 1st [[Bane-Rune]], [[Chameleon]], [[Mage Missile]], [[Silent Step]], *unseen servant* (Judge's call: no ACKS II equivalent); 2nd [[Adjust Self]], [[Bloody Flux]], [[Illusory Duplicates]], [[Ogre Strength]], [[Magic Lock]]. (SOTN prints MV 45’, AC 4, HD 5+1***, XP 860; Judge's call: AC 6 + 2 = 8; MM mummy ** plus SOTN's two class asterisks.)
  - **Mummified baboon-viper** → [[Baboon Mummy (SOTN)]] (variant: viper fangs add a poison bite): MV 20’ / 60’, AC 5, HD 5**, hp 25, claw 6+ / bite 6+, D 1d8 + mummy rot / 1d8 + mummy rot + **poison** (save vs Death at −1 or die in 1d4 turns), F5, ML +4, AL C, XP 500; **paralysing dread** at sight (Paralysis save, as baboon mummy: ends when it leaves sight, enters combat, or after 1d4 rounds); harmed only by magic weapons (half damage), spells and fire; undead immunities. Under **Ogre Strength:** +2 melee attack throws and damage (SOTN gives ogre power as +3; ACKS II spell used). Under **Chameleon:** Hiding 12+.
- **Trick / Loot:** a **secret compartment** in the dais floor under the sarcophagus, found with a Searching throw (find secret doors). It holds:
  - a silver inkwell and stylus (10 gp, 1/6 st);
  - a small gilded cedar incense altar (175 gp, 1 st) with 25 sticks of frankincense (20 gp each);
  - a chryselephantine statuette of a unicorn with an onyx horn (500 gp, 2/6 st);
  - a cedar chest of 1,500 gp (1 3/6 st);
  - a long scroll wound on two glyph-carved bone handles: the **transmogrifier's spellbook** ([[SOTN Magic Items#Transmogrifier's Spellbook]], SOTN p. 137; 31,750 gp; 3/6 st);
  - two vials of ginger-scented brown liquid: **potions of healing** (Judge's call: no ACKS II potion of that name; each heals as [[Cure Light Injury]]);
  - six terracotta scroll jars (5 gp, 1/6 st each), each holding a vellum **arcane scroll** in Ancient Argollëan: [[Bane-Rune]], [[Adjust Self]], [[Bloody Flux]], [[Transform Other]] (SOTN "polymorph other"), [[Body Swap]], [[SOTN Spells#Reflesh of the Bones]];
  - four terracotta canopic jars of embalmed organs (25 gp, 1/6 st each).
  - Tally (SOTN): 2,830 gp, 4 2/6 st (magic excluded). (The listed values sum to 2,815 gp and the listed weights to 5 1/6 st with the spellbook; Judge's call: use SOTN's tally.)

### E4. Deep Level Complex Gallery and Intersection (2700-2600 BE)
The east-west gallery at the foot of E2.

### E4a. Deep Level Complex Intersection
- **Exits:** N to E2c; W along E4b; E down E4c.
- > [!player]
  > A long east-west corridor meets the gallery from the north. West, it runs past the reach of the torchlight; east, it slopes down at a gentle grade into darkness. Both ways the floor is black marble and the plastered walls are pierced by more graves than can easily be counted. At the southern tip of the junction a 15’ wide, 10’ deep alcove is painted with elves flying through the sky on silver discs.
- **Lore:** the silver disc cannot be identified with any proficiency. A Tracking throw (+4 with scent) finds troglodyte tracks from E4c toward E2c.

### E4b. Deep Level Complex Gallery West
- **Exits:** E to E4a; opens W into E5.
- > [!player]
  > The corridor runs 80’ west from the junction and opens into a large burial chamber. Black marble floor; dozens of wall graves along both plastered walls.
- **Graves:** 64 wall graves (eight columns of four a side), dated 2700 BE near E4a and running toward 2600 BE westward, usual inscriptions, remains and goods (128 gp in all).

### E4c. Deep Level Complex Gallery East
- **Exits:** W to E4a; the cave mouth at the bottom, to I1.
- > [!player]
  > East of the junction the black-tiled corridor slopes gently down for about 90’ to end at a cave mouth. Dozens of wall graves line both sides.
- **Graves:** 80 wall graves (ten columns of four a side), 2700 BE near E4a toward 2600 BE eastward, usual inscriptions, remains and goods (160 gp in all).
- **Noise:** a successful Listening throw here hears stone hammering on stone, echoing from above and to the east (the troglodytes in I2). Nothing is heard if the party has already dealt with them.
- **Lore:** a Tracking throw (+4 with scent) finds troglodyte tracks from I1 toward E4a.

### E5. Deep Level Complex Common Tomb (2600 BE)
- **Exits:** E to E4b only.
- > [!player]
  > The gallery opens into a chamber 20’ deep and 40’ wide hewn from the rock, every wall pierced by wall graves: two columns of four on each side wall, five on the far wall. The worked stone floor is cracked and uneven.
- **Graves:** 36 wall graves, 2600 BE, usual inscriptions, remains and goods (72 gp in all).

### E6. Upper Level Gallery (2700 BE)
The gallery between D9 and F1, 20’ above E2.

### E6a. Upper Level Gallery East
- **Exits:** fretted cedar door at the E end to D9; heavy bronze door in the N wall beside it to E7; W end turns north into E6b.
- > [!player]
  > A 10’ × 40’ gallery, plastered walls and black marble floor, most of its wall space cut into horizontal graves. At the east end a fretted cedar door, with a heavy bronze door in the north wall right beside it; at the west end the gallery turns sharply north.
- **Graves:** 40 wall graves (six columns of four on the S wall, four on the N), 2700 BE, usual inscriptions, remains and goods (80 gp in all).
- **Lore:** a Tracking throw (+4 with scent) finds troglodyte tracks from E6b toward D9.

### E6b. Upper Level Gallery West
- **Exits:** heavy bronze door at the W end to F1; stone slab door halfway along the S wall to E9; 15’ east of it the stair down to E2b; the E end turns sharply south into E6a.
- > [!player]
  > A 10’ × 60’ gallery of black marble and plaster, its walls cut with many horizontal graves. A heavy bronze door closes the west end. Halfway along, a stone slab door stands in the south wall, and 15’ east of it a stair goes down. Beyond, the corridor turns sharply south.
- **Graves:** 52 wall graves (eight columns of four on the N wall, five on the S), 2700 BE, usual inscriptions, remains and goods (104 gp in all).
- **Lore:** a Tracking throw (+4 with scent) finds troglodyte tracks from E2b toward E6a.

### E7. Upper Level Family Tomb (2600 BE)
- **Exits:** heavy bronze door to E6a; stone slab door on the E wall near the NE corner to E8.
- > [!player]
  > A 20’ × 40’ plastered chamber, a great quake-crack across its floor. Eight horizontal graves in the west wall hold skeletons of different sizes in fine white linen, with an inscription carved above them. The north wall's fresco shows an elven family of nine riding through a dusky valley of great cedars, the eldest wearing a gold circlet. Broken pottery and toys lie scattered about. A stone slab door stands in the east wall near the north-east corner.
- **Graves:** 8 wall graves of the family (no goods value given).
- **Loot:** searching the debris turns up two intact pieces: a **game set**, ebony board with carved obsidian and quartz pieces (445 gp, 1 st), and a **tortoise-shell lyre** painted with scenes of hearth and home (apparent value 100 gp, 1/6 st): a [[Lyre of the Lullaby]] (SOTN p. 133). Tally: 445 gp, 1 1/6 st (magic excluded).
- **Lore:** read in Ancient Argollëan, the inscription says: "By the vile hand of man were you taken from me too soon. By the hand of the gods shall we be restored unto each other come the Awakening." A Loremastery or Theology throw identifies the Awakening as a prophesied end of days in the Chthonic myths. (Judge's note: the eight graves and the patriarch in E8, whose circlet matches the fresco, make the family of nine.)

### E8. Family Tomb Patriarch Burial Chamber (2600 BE)
- **Exits:** stone slab door W to E7 only.
- > [!player]
  > A 15’ × 30’ plastered burial chamber; on a low stone dais in the centre, a limestone sarcophagus in the likeness of an old elven nobleman, his face lined with grief. Frescoes of pastoral family life cover the north, south and east walls. Against the east wall a painted cedar chest stands beneath a large gold fan with great plumes, near several terracotta canopic jars and a number of small black-figure amphorae.
- **Monster:** the **mummy courtier** wakes when the E7-E8 slab is first disturbed (see Running). He wears a **thin gold circlet** (500 gp). → [[Mummy]] with the abilities of a 4th-level [[Elven Courtier]]: MV 20’ / 60’, AC 6, HD 5+1****, hp 25, slam 5+, D 1d12 + mummy rot, F5, ML +4, AL C, XP 1,060; horrific visage, mummy immunities (MM); **classical weapons training** (+1 to hit with missile weapons). Spells 2 1st; repertoire: [[Infuriate Humanoid]] (SOTN "infuriate person"), [[Unliving Puppet]]. (SOTN prints MV 60’, HD 5+1***, XP 860.) Given his grief, a "loneliness" or "regret" mummy reaction suits him if he is parleyed with or spared.
- **Loot:** the cedar chest holds **250 ep** and a long scroll on two bronze handles, the **courtier's spellbook** ([[SOTN Magic Items#Courtier's Spellbook]], SOTN p. 137; 700 gp; 1/6 st). On it lies the **fan** of gilded ebony with griffon plumes (200 gp, 2/3 st). **Four canopic jars** of embalmed organs (25 gp, 1/6 st each). **15 small black-figure amphorae** (5 gp, 1/6 st each), each holding 100 sp (**1,500 sp** in all). Tally: 1,150 gp, about 4 4/6 st (circlet included; spellbook value excluded).
- **Lore:** the amphorae show a man with a beetle for a face. A Loremastery throw, or a Theology throw at −4, identifies him as Khepri, a mysterious Chthonic god of antiquity.

### E9. Upper Level Torturer Tomb (2600 BE)
- **Exits:** stone slab door N to E6b only.
- > [!player]
  > A 20’ × 20’ tomb, plaster and black marble, with a limestone sarcophagus on a low dais carved as a beautiful elven noblewoman with arrogantly arched brows. The frescoes show her, dressed in black leather, torturing and sacrificing primitive humans. By the south wall a heap of fallen white alabaster figurines lies beside four broken canopic jars, their dried organs spilled out and the room heavy with exotic spice. Among the wreckage a black-figure amphora, a long scroll on black handles and a shiny black box are still whole.
- **Monster:** the **mummy nightblade** wakes when the E6b slab is first disturbed. (SOTN says "the stone slab door from 5f", a misprint for E6b.) Given time, she casts **Chameleon** and **Spider Climbing** and lurks on the ceiling; her casting is silent (Quiet Magic: a Listening throw is needed to hear it). She wields the 15’ black **whip +1** she was buried with and uses it to knock targets down. → [[Mummy]] with the abilities of a 4th-level [[Elven Nightblade]]: MV 20’ / 60’, AC 6, HD 5+1****, hp 26, slam 5+, D 1d12 + mummy rot, or whip +1 4+, D 1d2+4 or knockdown (whip +2 to knock down; Combat Trickery), F5, ML +4, AL C, XP 1,060; horrific visage, mummy immunities (MM); **Acrobatics 17+**; **backstab** (+4 to hit, +1 damage die, ACKS II nightblade); proficiencies Combat Trickery (knock down), Quiet Magic. Spells 2 1st; repertoire: [[Chameleon]], [[Spider Climbing]]. (SOTN prints MV 60’, HD 5+1***, XP 860, whip damage 1d2+1 and backstab as double damage; Judge's call: +3 armed-mummy strength on the whip, ACKS II backstab.)
- **Loot:** **seven white alabaster figurines** (10 gp each, 1 st per 24) of squat, hairy, long-armed, bestial humanoids; Beast Friendship or Naturalism identifies them as great apes or gorillas. The **black-figure amphora** (20 gp, 1 st) holds **1,400 sp**. The shiny box is a **lacquered ebony jewellery box** (25 gp, 1/6 st) holding a **gold nose-ring set with a diamond** (1,200 gp). The long scroll is the **nightblade's spellbook** ([[SOTN Magic Items#Nightblade's Spellbook]], SOTN p. 137; 700 gp; 1/6 st). Tally: 1,455 gp, about 3 st (magic excluded; the whip +1 is the nightblade's).
