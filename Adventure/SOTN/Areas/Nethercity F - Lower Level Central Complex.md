# Nethercity F — Lower Level Central Complex

The Lower Level tombs and galleries at the heart of the Nethercity, west of the Deep Level complex and east of the Grand Chthonic Temple. Three eras lie side by side: the **2700 BE** entrance and gallery (F1-F3, martyrs' graves of the Empyrean repression), the **2600 BE** tombs of the early Chthonic faith (F4-F11a: a revered martyr-priest, a curate, a spellsword, an embalming room) and the **2500 BE** works of Chthonic dominance (F11b-F19: the sacrificer of Iskara, the Grand Chthonic vestibule with its reliquary of pre-Cataclysm wonders, the Cave of the Falling Star). All on the Lower Level; ramps take F6-F7 up and F6-F15 / F11b-F14 down, and stairs lead down from F4 (to G3) and F18 (to I7). No blighted sinkhole in this area. No light anywhere (Judge's call: the text mentions none). Plaster-lined walls with frescoes, black marble floors cracked by the old earthquakes. Hub [[SOTN Hub]]; doors, wall graves, skeleton guards, mummies and treasure conventions in [[Nethercity Features]]. Pages are printed pages.

Running: Temple / Tomb column, `level: 4` — 1d6 every 3 turns, 6+ → 2d10 on `Tables/sotn-wandering-temple-tomb.yaml`. **F19 exception:** a sheltered den: throw every **6 turns**, encounter only on **10 on 1d10** (SOTN p. 51). **F11 gallery:** anyone carrying treasure from the F tombs wakes 1d20 skeletons a round (see F11). Mummies behind stone slab doors wake by the door rule ([[Nethercity Features#Doors (SOTN p. 11)]]).

## Layout
- **Entrances:** (a) **F1** east wall, two heavy bronze doors: the northern one to **C12** ([[Nethercity C - Central Caves]]), the southern one to **E6b** ([[Nethercity E - Deep Level Central Complex]]); (b) **F4** west archway: stairs down to **G3**; (c) **F14** west bronze door to **G1a**, and the secret **F16** west door from **G2a** ([[Nethercity G - Grand Chthonic Temple]]); (d) **F18** east finger: stairs down to **I7** ([[Nethercity I - Deep Level Southern Cave Complex]]); (e) **F14** secret south door (the snake-loop) down to **T1**, and the reliquary alarm wakes **T2** ([[Nethercity T - Sub-Abyssal Secret Alien Sepulcher]]).
- **North (2700 BE):** F1 → west bronze door → gallery **F2** (40’) → west cedar door → martyr tomb **F4**. Just before that door the sloping passage **F3** turns south and down to the arcosolium **F5**.
- **Centre (2600 BE):** F5 → south archway → frescoed gallery **F6** (50’ N-S). 10’ south of the arch a ramp climbs east into **F7**; further south a ramp drops west to **F15**; F6 ends in the statue alcove. F7 runs 60’ east: a T-junction with **F11a** (south), the stone slab door north to **F8** (→ west slab door → annex **F9**), and the embalming room **F10** at the east end.
- **The long gallery F11 (2600-2500 BE):** F11a runs 60’ south from F7 to the crossing **F11b** (west: 20’ steep slope down to a bronze door → F14; east, 10’ further south: stone slab door → sacrificer tomb **F12** → east slab door → annex **F13**). **F11c** continues 60’ south (alcoves, the same slab door to F12 on the east wall) to a heavy bronze door into **F18**.
- **Grand Chthonic rooms (2500 BE, lower):** **F14** vestibule: bronze doors north (F15), west (G1a), east (the F11b slope); secret trapped door south (T1). **F15** flying-disc chamber: bronze doors south (F14) and north (sealed, to the reliquary **F17**), ramp up east to F6, secret door west to **F16** (levers; second secret door west to G2a).
- **South:** **F18** Cave of the Falling Star → south opening → **F19** (rhagodessa den, a dead end).

## Rooms (SOTN pp. 43-51)

### F1. Lower Level Entrance Chamber (2700 BE)
- **Exits:** E two heavy bronze doors (N one to C12, S one to E6b); W heavy bronze doorway (centre of the wall) to F2.
- > [!player]
  > A 60’ by 25’ hall, black marble underfoot, plastered walls. Two heavy bronze doors in the east wall, a bronze doorway in the middle of the west wall. The south wall is painted with five figures: a queen with a dragon's wings, a six-armed warrior, a medusa whose lower body is a serpent, a sorcerer with a skull for a face, and a prince with antlers. The north wall, badly damaged, shows seven: a bearded patriarch, a crone in grey, a beautiful woman in bronze armour, a blind old man, a matron in white, a young hunter, a burly man with a hammer. Plaster dust lies along the foot of the north wall.
- **Lore:** Loremastery or Theology throw: the south fresco is the Chthonic gods **Iskara, Bel, Nasga, Dirgion and Ravanor** (in that order). Loremastery throw, or automatically with Theology: the north fresco is the seven Empyrean gods **Ammonar, Calefa, Ianna, Istreus, Mityara, Naurivus and Türas**. Any look shows the north fresco was once plastered over; an ancient quake cracked the plaster off and bared the original.

### F4. Lower Level Martyr Tomb and Staircase (2600 BE)
- **Exits:** E cedar door to F2; W archway onto stairs down to G3.
- > [!player]
  > A 30’ by 50’ tomb. The north wall shows a dark-haired elf praying in deep caves; the south wall shows the same elf burned alive by a crowd of elves in white. Four terracotta canopic jars stand under the south fresco. Both frescoes, and the low dais in the middle of the black marble floor, are split by great cracks. On the dais a limestone sarcophagus carved with that elf's face, its eyes two large glittering amethysts. The dais and the foot of the sarcophagus are covered in a silent, trembling mass of black scarab beetles. Beyond, a west archway framed in ivory tiles with geometric fretwork opens on steps going down.
- **Monster:** the tomb of an early Chthonic high priest revered as a martyr; the temple complex grew around it. The scarabs are an **undead insect swarm**. It wakes if anyone attacks it or disturbs the sarcophagus or the archway (prising off the tiles counts); walking through does not. The **mummy and its worms wake one round after the swarm**.
  - **Undead insect swarm** → [[Insect Swarm]] (3 HD, undead variant): MV 10’ / 30’ crawl (SOTN 30’), AC 2, HD 3*, hp 15, engulf (20’ × 15’), D 2 piercing, 4 if unarmoured with natural AC 3 or less, half when fleeing or warding off (torch: 1d4 fire to it), vexed afterwards, F0, ML N/A (undead), AL C, XP 65. Immune to bludgeoning, piercing and slashing (SOTN "immunity to weapons"). SOTN's undead variant also: immune to enchantment (sleep, bewitch, halt) and poison, **damaged by holy water**, **rebuked as a wight**. No disease (Judge's call: undead scarabs carry none).
  - **Mummy cultist of Dirgion** (the martyr; a 4th-level "curate") → [[Mummy]] with the abilities of a 4th-level [[Elven Cultist]] of Dirgion: MV 20’ / 60’ (SOTN 60’), AC 6, HD 5+1***, hp 25, slam 5+, D 1d12 + mummy rot, F5, ML +4, AL C, XP 860 (MM mummy 660; SOTN's third asterisk for the class abilities). Horrific visage (Paralysis save), rot, immunities and resistances as the MM mummy (SOTN: immune to non-magical, non-fire attacks; half from fire and magic). Class abilities (SOTN p. 162): commands undead as a 4th-level Chaotic caster (ACKS II: as a Chaotic [[Crusader]] of 4th level, Judge's call), inexorable (immune to fear), inspire dread 4/day, lordship of undead. Spells per day 2 1st, 1 2nd, from the 4th-level cultist of Dirgion repertoire (SOTN p. 162; [[Elven Cultist]]).
  - **14 sepulchral worms** in the mummy → [[Sepulchral Worm]]: AC 3, HD 1**, hp 4 each, bite 10+, D 1d4 + rot, F1 (SOTN F4), ML N/A, AL C, XP 16 each. With 14 worms the visage save is at **−4** (MM: −1 per 3 worms; SOTN prints −1 per worm, i.e. −14). Holy water destroys a worm; host destroyed, they last 1 turn.
- **Loot:** 4 canopic jars of embalmed organs (25gp, 1/6 st each); the 2 large amethyst eyes (200gp each); the ivory fretwork cornice tiles (100gp, 1 st together). **Tally: 600gp, 1 4/6 st.**

### F5. Lower Level Arcosolium (2600 BE)
- **Exits:** N archway to F3; S archway to F6.
- > [!player]
  > A 25’ by 30’ plastered tomb. On the east side an arched half-round recess under a half-dome painted as the night sky, too cracked to read any stars; beneath it 12 horizontal niches, each with a reclining skeleton holding a bronze sickle-sword and bronze shield. On the west side a shallow 10’ alcove holds a limestone sarcophagus carved as a grim-faced elf, set with red garnets, two terracotta canopic jars on each side. Archways north and south.
- **Monster / Graves:** the 12 graves (2600 BE, usual inscriptions) hold **animated skeletons**; the sarcophagus a **mummy curate** with 10 worms. All stir if the arcosolium is disturbed in any way (searching, touching graves or sarcophagus, prising garnets); walking through does not wake them.
  - **12 skeletons** → [[Skeleton]]: MV 40’ / 120’ (SOTN 120’), AC 3 (2 + shield; SOTN prints AC 2: Judge's call, MM adds worn armour), HD 1*, hp 5 each, bronze sickle-sword 10+, D 1d6 (as short sword), F1, ML N/A, AL C, XP 13 each. Arms: see [[Nethercity Features#Wall graves (SOTN pp. 14-16)]].
  - **Mummy curate** (cultist of Dirgion, 4th-level abilities) → [[Mummy]] + [[Elven Cultist]]: as F4's mummy but **hp 24**: MV 20’ / 60’, AC 6, HD 5+1***, slam 5+, D 1d12 + rot, F5, ML +4, AL C, XP 860; commands undead as a 4th-level Chaotic caster, inexorable, inspire dread 4/day, lordship of undead; spells 2 1st, 1 2nd (cultist of Dirgion 4, SOTN p. 162). (SOTN's boxed text calls it a "curate", its statline "mummy cultist of Dirgion": curate is the cultist's 4th-level title, SOTN p. 163.)
  - **10 sepulchral worms** → [[Sepulchral Worm]]: AC 3, HD 1**, hp 4 each, bite 10+, D 1d4 + rot, F1, ML N/A, AL C, XP 16 each. Visage save at **−3** (MM; SOTN −10).

### F8. Lower Level Warrior Tomb (2600 BE)
- **Exits:** S stone slab door to F7; W stone slab door (left of the sarcophagus) to F9.

### F14. Grand Chthonic Vestibule (2500 BE)
- **Exits:** N bronze door to F15; W bronze door to G1a; E bronze door to the slope up to F11b; S **secret door** in the snake-loop (stairs down to T1).
- > [!player]
  > A 40’ by 30’ room, plaster walls, black marble floor. The walls are painted with religious symbols, most cracked or peeled away; the largest, on the south wall, is still clear: a serpent coiled in a loop, swallowing its own tail. Bronze doors north, west and east.
- **Trick / Trap:** the circle inside the snake-loop is a **secret door** (Searching); pushed inward it opens on a round tunnel with steps down to **T1**. Pushing it open drops a **scything blade** across the opening: whoever opened it saves vs **Blast** or takes **3d6** and **loses the hand or foot** he pushed with (as the permanent wound: "one hand is cut off" / "one foot is sliced off", [[Mortal Wounds - Slashing]]). Found as a trap (Searching), it can be opened safely with a 10’ pole, or disarmed with a Trapbreaking throw ([[Traps and Trapbreaking]]). On the T side a plainly visible lever disarms it (so the khepri of T2 can come out safely).
- **Lore:** Loremastery or Theology: the tail-eating serpent is Iskara as Empress of Eternity.

### F15. Chamber of the Flying Disc (2500 BE)
- **Exits:** S heavy bronze door to F14; N heavy bronze door to F17 (**magic-locked and sealed**); E ramp up (50%) to F6; W **secret door** to F16.
- > [!player]
  > A round chamber 30’ across, plaster walls, black marble floor, the ceiling painted as a moonless starry sky. Heavy bronze doors face each other north and south; to the east a ramp climbs toward a hallway. In the middle, a raised dais 10’ across under a thick glass dome, dusty to near-opacity in places, with a bronze pipe running from its top into the ceiling. Fallen ceiling blocks have chipped the glass; plaster chunks lie at its base. Inside, dimly, an 8’ disc or shield of shimmering, rainbow-sheened metal hangs about 2’ above the dais.
- **Trick:** the **west secret door** shows as hairline cracks in the plaster (Searching); it pivots on its vertical axis into F16. The **north door** (to F17) is magic-locked at 9th level ([[Magic Lock]]) and physically sealed: it opens only by magic or by F16's **right-hand lever**. (SOTN's "as noted in F14" means here, F15.) Magic: [[Dispel Magic]], or an arcane caster of 12th level or more opens a 9th-level magic lock freely (SOTN "magic such as knock": Judge's call, no ACKS II knock).
- **Trick / Trap:** the dome rises safely with F16's **left-hand lever**, or breaks under a strong blow (**7+ damage** from a bludgeoning weapon). Breaking it releases an **invisible, odourless poison gas** filling the room: **each round** everyone in the room saves vs **Death** (SOTN Poison): fail, **2d6** and **helpless** with bloody coughing; success, **1d4** and can act. The gas clears after **1 turn**.
- **Loot:** the floating disc is a **flying disk** ([[SOTN Magic Items#Flying Disk]]): only an arcane caster can steer it, telepathically, standing on it; flies up to 30’ high at 150’ (50’), carries two man-sized riders (500 lb), one energy crystal (none here) runs it 6 hours; value **78,000gp**.
