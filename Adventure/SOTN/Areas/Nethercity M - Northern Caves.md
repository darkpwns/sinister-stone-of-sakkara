# Nethercity M — Northern Caves

The volcanic tubes north of the abyssal ant caves, cut into wall-grave catacombs between 2200 and 1800 BE and now the home of a band of **white apes** (cave-dwelling offspring of the Argollëan alchemists' experiments in [[Nethercity N - Sub-Abyssal Alchemic Laboratory]], "precursors to beastmen", SOTN p. 10) who raid the giant ants' fungus farms in L. Three physical levels: the **Deep** level (M1-M14, the ape caves, 2200-2000 BE), the **Lower** level (M15, M16, M21, M22) and the **Upper** level (M17-M20); the late caves (1900-1800 BE) are unusually rich in grave goods, and M20 holds the unfinished works and the engineer's map. Other keyed dangers: three mummified elven brothers behind the M6 slab doors (M8), and the blood-rage statue of Nargund at the crossroads M10, with its trophy vault. No blighted sinkhole. No light anywhere. Hub [[SOTN Hub]]; standing rules [[Nethercity Features]]; the gods [[Nethercity History]]. Pages are SOTN printed pages.

Running: wandering 1d6 every 3 turns, 6+ → 2d10 on the **Volcanic Caves** column (`Tables/sotn-wandering-volcanic-caves.yaml`), setting level 3; optional clearing applies. The table's "1d3 white apes" (15) are members of this band: take them from M2/M5 (Judge's call). **Lights draw the apes:** a party climbing into M1 from L19 with torches or lanterns is noticed by the M2 band. **Pelts:** the fur-pelt rule of [[Nethercity Features#Treasure (SOTN pp. 17-18)]] (intact only if killed by one attack, only by blunt weapons, or by cold, death magic, electricity or poison after losing under half its hp to other attacks; damaged pelts 1gp per st; skinning 2 turns, 1 turn with Animal Husbandry, Labor (butchery), Labor (farming) or Trapping). The MM gives cave gorillas Night Vision only, no sight in total darkness (Judge's call: in their own dark caves they go by smell, sound and touch, and see intruders by the intruders' own light; no surprise penalty for them at home).

## Layout
- **Entrance:** from **L19** ([[Nethercity L - Abyssal Level Caves]]) hewn steps climb a 10’ ledge into the east end of **M1**. The apes come and go this way to drink at the pool in L20 (3 apes arrive on 5+ on 1d6 each turn the party lingers there) and to raid the ant farms in L16-L17; the dead ants of L18 are their work. The heavy bronze door in L20's north-east face is the way into area N, not into M.
- **Deep level, the ape caves:** M1 → west into the Y-junction **M2**: north-west arm to **M3** → **M4** (5’ tunnel north to the exile's cave **M14**; hewn steps up west to M15); south-west arm to **M5**, choked by the split basalt pillar, then **M6** (two stone slab doors in the south-east wall to the sepulchre **M8**, slab door on to the vault **M9**) → **M7** → the four-way crossroads **M10** (statue of Nargund): west to the nursery **M11**, south-west through a 5’ tunnel to the leader's cave **M13**, north into the unfinished finger tunnel **M12** (dead end).
- **Lower level:** the sickle cave **M15** (steps down east to M4; a 5’ neck north-west to **M21**; a 15’ tunnel south-west to **M16**); M21 → south to **M22**; **M16** climbs south to the Upper level.
- **Upper level:** **M19**, a long grave-lined tunnel running south, then south-east to the mandolin cave **M20** (dead end: collapsed works in its south corner). M19 also opens west onto **M17**, which leads north to the E-shaped cave **M18**, whose stair drops back to M22 (map). So M15-M16-M19-M17-M18-M22-M21-M15 is a loop.
- **Passing beneath:** the secret corridor N12-N13 of area N runs under M19, M7 and M10 to O1 ([[Nethercity O - Sub-Abyssal Secret Tomb of the Hierophant]]); there is no way between them.

## Factions
- **The white ape band** (M2, M5, M10-M13; about 40 apes, plus the 3 at L20's pool if keyed separately): semi-sapient beasts bred long ago by the elves; no speech, but cries and gestures (and [[Speak with Beasts]] or the [[SOTN Magic Items#Collar of Conversation]], which gives a white ape INT 9).
  - **Leader:** the [[Great White Ape (SOTN)]] (M13), cunning: at any alarm he rallies the band at the statue of Nargund (M10), where injured apes fly into blood-rage.
  - **Young males** (7 in M2, 6 in M5) guard the approaches. They first threaten (chest-beating, thrown rocks); pressed, they call the others; routed, they fall back on M10, alerting the females (M11) and the leader (M13) as they go, and blood-rage there.
  - **Females, children and babies** (M11): the females fight to the death (**ML +4**) while any young lives; then the children fight if they must; the babies are helpless.
  - **The exile** ([[Elderly White Ape (SOTN)]], M14): ousted by the great white ape, ignored by the band and indifferent to it; lonely and curious, **+2 to reaction rolls** with the party. If the great white ape dies, he takes the band back.
  - **Wants:** food (fungus from the ants' farms in L, ant flesh, whatever stumbles in), water (L20's pool), safety for the young. **Reaction:** cave gorillas attack only when their den is approached, and roll **+1** away from it ([[Cave Gorilla]]); the whole of M1-M13 counts as the den (Judge's call), so the M2 and M5 males always threaten and fight if ignored.
  - **Relations:** enemies of the **giant ants** (L), whose farms they plunder; prey of the **troglodytes** (I, who butcher ape corpses in I2); nothing to do with the undead. The ants and troglodytes would both welcome their destruction.
  - **Value to the party:** pelts (50gp adult, 25gp child, 12gp baby, 100gp each for the leader and the exile, intact only under the pelt rule), live young for monster dealers (13 children 185gp each, 14 babies 35gp each; cf. Liber Faunus's live-capture notices in [[Old District]]), and the exile's memory of the teleportation platform (L4).
- **The three brothers** (M8): mummified warlords who wake when the M6 slab doors are disturbed; ordinary mummy wrath ([[Nethercity Features#Necromancy and the mummies (SOTN pp. 16-17)]]).

## Rooms (SOTN pp. 99-105)

### M1. Deep Level Central Volcanic Tube East (2200 BE)
- **Exits:** E a steep 10’ drop with narrow rough steps down to L19; W the tube continues 60’ and more into M2.

> [!player]
> At the head of the steps you stand in a lava tube about 40’ across. Behind you the floor ends in a 10’ drop, with a narrow run of rough steps down. Westward the tube runs on past the reach of your light, 60’ or more. Both walls are honeycombed with horizontal burial niches by the score.

- **Monster:** light carried up from L19/L20 alerts the apes of M2, who come out to meet it (see M2).
- **Noise:** a Listening throw here: now and then, animals shuffling and grunting to the west (M2).
- **Graves:** 60 (28 north wall, 32 south), 2200 BE, usual inscriptions, remains and goods: **270gp in all** (SOTN "average 270gp"; 4.5gp a grave).

### M2. Deep Level Central Volcanic Tube West (2200 BE)
- **Exits:** E (40’ wide) to M1; NW arm (25’) to M3; SW arm (25’) to M5.

> [!player]
> The tube splits like a Y: two rocky passages, each about 25’ wide, run off north-west and south-west, and the way you came, 40’ wide, lies east. Burial niches crowd every wall. Out of the dark to the west come shaggy white apes, big as men, baring savage teeth.

- **Monster:** the home of **7 young male white apes**, who live off the ants' food in L. As the party approaches they beat their chests, throw loose rocks and try to scare it into stopping. Ignored, they give a cry and attack; the **6 apes of M5 arrive two rounds later**. If their morale breaks they flee to the statue of Nargund (M10), calling out to the females (M11) and the great white ape (M13), then blood-rage there.
  **7 white apes** → [[Cave Gorilla]]: MV 40’/120’, AC 3, HD 4, hp 24, 22, 20, 19, 18, 16, 14, 2 claws 7+ (1d4/1d4 slashing) or rock 7+ (1d6 bludgeoning), F2, ML −1, AL N, XP 80 each; Climbing 5+.
- **Loot:** each intact pelt 50gp (pelt rule above; MM: 1 st).
- **Graves:** 36 (12 north-east wall, 12 south-east wall, 12 on the west wall between the arms), 2200 BE, usual contents: **162gp in all**.

### M8. Deep Level Large Sepulcher (2100 BE)
- **Exits:** NW the double slab doors to M6; slab door in the east wall to M9.

> [!player]
> Past the slabs lies an arrow-shaped sepulchre about 35’ by 35’, walls and floor panelled in black marble. A further slab door is set in the east wall. The south wall bears a mosaic of three elves, plainly brothers, fighting for their lives against dwarves armed with bronze, with a line of ancient writing beneath. Three low bronze-plated daises stand in a triangle, each bearing a painted bronze sarcophagus in the likeness of one of the brothers. (If the mummies are up, describe the lids pushed aside and the brothers rising, or already advancing.)

- **Monster:** the brothers are **mummy warlords**, woken when the M6 slab was disturbed; just rising or fully roused depending on how long the door took. They were below 5th level when they fell, so they have only a plain mummy's HD (2100 BE: two thirds of their levels retained).
  **3 mummy warlords** → [[Mummy]] with the class abilities of a **3rd-level [[Elven Warlord]]** (Judge's call: the warlord's +2 damage bonus of 3rd level is SOTN's +2): MV 20’/60’, AC 6, HD 5+1**, hp 30, 25, 23, slam 5+, D 1d12+2 extraordinary bludgeoning and necrotic + mummy rot, F5, ML +4, AL C, XP 660 each; horrific visage (Paralysis save, −1 per extra mummy in view), mummy rot (1 extraordinary necrotic each dusk, no magical healing), damage immunities and resistances as the [[Mummy]] note, immune to enchantment; rebuked as mummies. (SOTN prints MV 60’ and the ACKS I immunities: non-magical non-fire attacks immune, half from fire and magic; the MM table replaces them.)
- **Lore:** fluent in Ancient Argollëan: the mosaic reads, roughly, "My beloved sons, the gods granted you glory and took you into the earth, from which you will come back to us at the hour of the Awakening."
- **Loot:** the 3 painted bronze sarcophagi, **500gp and 20 st each**. Tally: **1,500gp, 60 st**.

### M10. Deep Level Four Way Intersection (2000 BE)
- **Exits:** W a 20’ tunnel to M11; SW a 5’ tunnel to M13; N a 10’ finger tunnel M12; E the lava tube back to M7 (SOTN says M6; M7 lies between).

> [!player]
> Four ways meet here. From the 20’ tunnel to the west come loud whooping ape cries. A narrow 5’ passage to the south-west opens quickly into a cave; a 10’ tunnel pokes north like a finger; eastward the lava tube runs back into the dark. In the middle, on a low pedestal, stands a black marble statue of a snarling wolf-headed man, its muzzle daubed red as if with blood. Cracked bones lie scattered round the base, split as though their marrow had been offered up.

- **Lore:** Loremastery or Theology: **Nargund**, the Dusk Wolf, Chthonic god of predators, hunting and night ([[Nethercity History]]).
- **Trick (blood-rage):** the statue is powerfully enchanted; Nargund is patron of berserkers.
  - Any **injured** creature within 5’ of the statue makes a **Spells** save at the start of its initiative each round; failure: it enters blood-rage. A creature may wound itself on purpose (1 point of damage) to qualify, and may choose to fail the save.
  - **Blood-raging:** foams, bites its own flesh, howls; **+2 to attack throws and damage rolls**, immune to fear; reduces damage from **non-magical attacks by 1 point per die** (to no less than 0 per die). Attacks of monsters of **5 HD or more count as magical** for this, by their ferocity (Judge's call: "magical" = extraordinary damage in ACKS II terms, so mundane damage dice from creatures under 5 HD are reduced).
  - It **cannot retreat** and confuses friend and foe: on a **natural 1** on an attack throw it automatically hits an ally within 5’ instead of its target. It cannot speak coherently, cast spells or use magic items.
  - The rage lasts until **all visible enemies are slain**; then the creature is **exhausted**: no charging or running, −1 to attack and proficiency throws, until it rests 1 turn.
  - The apes know it: routed apes and the great white ape make their stand here, and injured apes (or apes who hurt themselves) choose to fail. PCs can use it too.
- **Loot:** the statue and pedestal can be shifted like a stone slab door (−12, up to 3 helpers: [[Nethercity Features#Doors (SOTN p. 11)]]). Beneath is a vault of hunting and conquest trophies sacred to Nargund:
  - a huge cedar chest with 10,000sp and 7,000gp of ancient dwarven mint: **8,000gp, 17 st**;
  - a cedar coffer with 18 ebony wolf carvings with tiny ruby eyes: **200gp and 1/6 st each** (3,600gp, 3 st);
  - a stack of 22 tumbaga (gold-copper) ingots: **300gp and 2 st each** (6,600gp, 44 st);
  - 2 ancient dragon horns: **800gp and 10 st each** (1,600gp, 20 st);
  - 4 mature adult dragon horns: **400gp and 5 st each** (1,600gp, 20 st);
  - 20 wyvern horns: **80gp and 1 st each** (1,600gp, 20 st);
  - 30 pieces of elephant ivory: **50gp and 3/6 st each** (1,500gp; SOTN prints 12 st, but 30 × 3/6 = 15 st);
  - an ash-wood spear whose butt-cap is a howling wolf (apparent value 650gp): the **[[SOTN Magic Items#Wolf-Spear of the Hunter]]** (SOTN p. 136).
  - Tally: **24,500gp, 136 st** as SOTN prints it (139 st with the ivory corrected; magic value excluded).

  **14 white ape babies**: helpless (MM baby: AC 0, HD 1, 2 scritches for 1). SOTN gives them no statline or XP.
- **Loot:** captured alive, **children 185gp each, babies 35gp each** (MM: cave gorillas train easily if caught young). Intact pelts: **adults 50gp, children 25gp, babies 12gp** each.

### M14. Deep Level Boot-Shaped Cave (2100 BE)
- **Exits:** S the 5’ tunnel to M4 (dead end).

> [!player]
> The cave is shaped like an upturned ankle boot: the heel 35’ across, the leg rising 35’. Graves are cut deep into the north wall. Beside them squats a very big, very old white ape, his fur gone grey and coarse.

  **Elderly white ape** → [[Elderly White Ape (SOTN)]]: MV 40’/120’, AC 3, HD 5, hp 24, 2 claws 6+ (1d6/1d6) or rock 6+ (1d8), F2, ML −1, AL N, XP 200.

He no longer knows the way, only that it is somewhere in the "caves of the ants". He and the troglodyte shaman of I13 are the only ones left who remember it.
- **Loot:** intact pelt **100gp**.
- **Graves:** 30, 2100 BE, usual contents: **135gp in all**.
