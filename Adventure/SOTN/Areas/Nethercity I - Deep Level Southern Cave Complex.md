# Nethercity I — Deep Level Southern Cave Complex

Volcanic caves south of the Deep Level central complex, honeycombed with common wall graves from **2500 BE** (I1) through **2400 BE** (I2-I5), **2300 BE** (I6-I8), **2200 BE** (I9, I12) down to **2100-2000 BE** (I10-I11, I13-I14, the end of the Bitter War against the dwarves). The western half (I1-I7) is the warren of a **troglodyte tribe** of about a hundred (see Factions): they have emptied its wall graves, lined them with foul resin and use them as beds, larders and egg nests; the eastern half (I9-I14) is untouched graves, the shaman's history cave and the war memorial. The brothers' sepulchre I8 is still sealed (resin over its door). Levels: SOTN's headings call I1 "Sub-Abyssal" and I2-I3 "Abyssal", the rest "Deep"; the area's title says Deep Level (Judge's call: treat all of I as the Deep Level band with steps inside it: I1 lies 12’ below I2; stairs climb from I3 up to I6 and drop from I6 down to I9; I7 climbs to F18 on the Lower Level). No blighted sinkhole. No light anywhere (the troglodytes have Lightless Vision). Hub [[SOTN Hub]]; doors, wall graves, mummies and treasure conventions in [[Nethercity Features]]. Pages are printed pages.

Running: Volcanic Caves column, `level: 3` — 1d6 every 3 turns, 6+ → 2d10 on `Tables/sotn-wandering-volcanic-caves.yaml`. **Tracks:** a Tracking throw anywhere in I finds troglodyte tracks (many reptilian humanoids pass often); **+4** if helped by scent (a hunting dog), from their slimy, reeking hide. **Water-bearers:** while the tribe stands, in I7 (and J1, J4) throw 1d6 each turn; 5+ → 5 breeders with amphorae on their way to the font in J4 (see I7). **Hammering:** the stone-on-stone noise from I2 and I6 is heard across the area until those caves are dealt with (see each room's Noise).

## Layout

- **Troglodyte warren (west):** I1 (the entrance cave, 30’ ceiling) → curved steps near its south wall up the 12’ cliff of its west side → chieftain cave **I2** → finger tunnel SW → tunnel **I3**. In I3: an 8’ ledge on the south wall where it leaves I2 (egg nest **I4**, bone ladder) and a 10’ ledge on the north wall near the far steps (egg nest **I5**); at its SW end rock-cut steps climb to the grand cave **I6**.
- **I6** (triangular): stairs down from the middle of its NE face (to I3) and from its east tip (to I9); a resin-sealed stone slab door on its NW face (sepulchre **I8**); west it narrows into tunnel **I7** (stairs up to F18 at its west tip, bronze door to J1 in its south wall).
- **East:** I9 (Y junction at the foot of the I6 stairs): NE a 15’ tunnel **I12** → cave **I13** (the shaman); SE a tunnel widening to 25’ → cave **I10** → **I11**, arcing NE 60’; off I11 a 10’ side tunnel SE → memorial **I14**. ## Factions
**The troglodyte tribe** (one warband; "degenerate mutants made long ago by the elves from Thrassian stock", SOTN p. 10).
- **Numbers:** chieftain, sub-chieftain and shaman; **8 champions** (2 in I1, 4 in I2, 2 in I6); **37 warriors** (11 in I1, 15 in I2, 11 in I6) plus the hunting gang of **6 warriors** on the balcony D8 ([[Nethercity D - Warlord's Tomb and Chapel to Bel]]), whose tracks run D8 – D9 – E6a – E6b – E2b – E2c – E4a – E4c – I1 (I1's tracks show a small band gone north toward E4c); **52 breeders** (27 in I2, 24 in I6, the gross breeder in I4); **40 eggs** (I4, I5). The five water-bearers of I7/J1/J4 come out of these breeders (Judge's call: take them from I6).
- **Leaders:** the **chieftain** in I2 (while he lives the tribe's morale is +2, not +1); the **sub-chieftain** beside him; the **shaman** in I13, the oldest of the tribe, keeper of its history.
- **Attitude:** Chaotic, vicious, territorial. Warriors ambush from chameleon cover (I1) and use darts before closing; the I1 band reinforces I2 if the party comes from there; an alarm (the I4 breeder's screech, burning resin at I8, fighting in a cave) brings the others. Unalerted, the warriors sleep in the graves while the breeders work. **The shaman** tries to negotiate peace for her tribe if not attacked on sight, pointing at her carvings. Language: debased Elvish (MM); an Elvish speaker follows the gist with difficulty (Judge's call); otherwise gestures, or *tongues*-type magic. A wearer of an [[SOTN Magic Items#Iron Masks of Cyfaraun|Iron Mask of Cyfaraun]] meets an immediate Hostile reaction.
- **What they want:** food (they hunt locusts, millipedes, crabs and giant insects, keep them sealed live in resin-walled graves, and kill white apes, I6); water from the purification font in **J4**; bone and stone for tools (the hammering); to be left alone. Their enemies: the giant rhagodessa of **F19** (who prefer troglodyte meat), the giant catfish of the river **P** (it takes them when they come to water), the white apes of **M**, and the giant ants of **L**, whose arrival after the sewage flood ended the tribe's hunts through the platform.
- **The "magic rock":** tribal legend keeps a "magic rock" in a "cavern of the ants" that carried brave hunters to "an endless cavern without walls or ceiling": the teleportation platform **L4** ([[Nethercity L - Abyssal Level Caves]]), which took their ancestors to hunting grounds outside (SOTN pp. 10, 97). Only the shaman (and the grey white ape of M14) still remembers where it is. **She will offer to guide the party to L4** if they agree to harm the tribe no further; she does not know of the [[Sage Slime]] in L3.
- **Treasure:** the warband's hoard is in one grave of I2.

### I6. Grand Cave (2300 BE)
- **Exits:** NE face (middle): stairs down to I3; E tip: stairs down to I9; NW face: stone slab door (resin-sealed) to I8; W into tunnel I7.
- > [!player]
  > A roughly triangular cave, about 50’ north to south and at least 60’ west to east. Every wall you can see is cut with burial niches. Several dozen are sleeping cells with resin beds; others are sealed with a resin skin like the cells of a monstrous hive.
  >
  > (If not alerted:) Near the middle a dozen grotesque lizard-men crouch over the bodies of two white-furred humanoids, torn wide open; the creatures are splashing blood and gore as they work with teeth and claws to break the bones out. - **Monster:** **2 champions, 11 warriors, 24 breeders**. Unalerted, the warriors doze and the breeders gather bones from the dead and hammer stone from the walls. Breeders unarmed; warriors 3 darts; champions hide armour, stone battle axe, 3 darts.
  - **2 champions** → [[Troglodyte Champion (SOTN)]]: hp 16 each, XP 50 each.
  - **11 warriors** → [[Troglodyte]]: hp 9 each, XP 20 each.
  - **24 breeders** → [[Troglodyte]]: hp 6 each, XP 15 each.

- **Graves:** **120** wall graves, all 2300 BE, usual inscriptions, **no remains or goods**; half are larders (millipedes, crabs, locusts behind resin).
- **Noise:** Listening throw in I6: stone hammering to the north-east (I2); silent once I2 is dealt with.
- **Lore:** Animal Husbandry or Naturalism: the dead are white apes, a rare great ape adapted to life underground ([[Cave Gorilla]], white variant; the apes of [[Nethercity M - Northern Caves]]).
