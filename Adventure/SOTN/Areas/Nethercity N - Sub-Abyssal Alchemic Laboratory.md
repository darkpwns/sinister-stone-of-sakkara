# Nethercity N — Sub-Abyssal Alchemic Laboratory

The Argollëan alchemists' laboratory of 2200 BE, on the **Sub-Abyssal** level behind the bronze door in L20: plastered halls where the elves researched spells, bred monsters and vivisected their failures. The white apes of M and the troglodytes of I are its escaped descendants. Here are the research library (N3, 50,000gp of notes plus formulae and arcane scrolls), the **chimerical apparatus** (N4) that fuses two living creatures into one, the **stasis chamber and teleportation disc** (N5) holding a live chimera, the necrosection table (N7) with a dying ogre-spider hybrid kept alive for millennia, a hydropion in stasis behind a trapped door (N9), the embalming room with the two **key orbs** (N10), and the **summoning chamber** (N11) whose orb-keyed archways open gates to other worlds and the secret corridor (N12-N13) to the Hierophant's tomb ([[Nethercity O - Sub-Abyssal Secret Tomb of the Hierophant]]). Every door is magically sealed. No blighted sinkhole. No light (the fallen chandeliers held magic lights, now dead). Hub [[SOTN Hub]]; standing rules [[Nethercity Features]]; the gods [[Nethercity History]]. Pages are SOTN printed pages.

Running: wandering 1d6 every 3 turns, 6+ → 2d10 on the **Temple / Tomb** column (`Tables/sotn-wandering-temple-tomb.yaml`), setting level 4; optional clearing applies. While the laboratory's doors are shut behind the party it counts as a **sealed temple or tomb**: only the entries marked as entering sealed tombs reach it ([[SOTN Hub]]; Judge's call that the magic seals qualify). Teleport spells in and out are hampered by the Nethercity's wards, but **not the N5 disc** ([[Nethercity Features#Teleportation (SOTN p. 14)]]).

**Sealed doors (all heavy bronze doors of N, including the L20 door, SOTN pp. 100, 105):** each unlocks by itself when **approved personnel** come within 5’: an **elf showing a Chthonic holy symbol**, anyone wearing the **shadowcowl robe** (J14, [[SOTN Magic Items#Shadowcowl Robe]]) or an **iron mask** (J15, [[SOTN Magic Items#Iron Masks of Cyfaraun]]), or anyone carrying the **sword of command** (Q5, [[SOTN Magic Items#Sword of Command]]). Otherwise a *knock* spell opens a door for one turn (Judge's call: ACKS II has no knock; any effect that opens magically locked portals does the same), and [[Dispel Magic]] cast at **caster level 11 or higher** unlocks it for good. The doors radiate magic. (Judge's call: they are solid metal doors held like a [[Magic Lock]]: they cannot be picked, bashed or battered without structural damage ([[Doors and Dungeonbashing]]); [[Passageway]] through the plastered stone wall beside a door bypasses it; the N8c secret door from L13 is another way in.) The N9 door is different (see N8a).

## Layout
- **Entrance:** the heavy bronze door in the north-east face of **L20** ([[Nethercity L - Abyssal Level Caves]]) opens into the antechamber **N1**. The apes drink at L20's pool.
- **North range:** N1 → a 15’ hall west from its north-west corner to a sealed door → the gallery **N2** (sealed door in its north wall to the library **N3**; its west end opens into **N4**, map). **N4** (the apparatus): the 15’ opening west, closed by a wall of force, into the stasis chamber **N5**; a sealed door south to **N6**.
- **Middle:** **N6** (chart room): sealed door west to **N7** (necrosection table); archway south into the long gallery **N8a**.
- **South gallery:** **N8a** runs 50’ south to the trapped door east to **N9** (hydropion) and on as **N8b**: 40’ further a sealed door west to **N10** (embalming), 20’ on it turns west as **N8c**: the cedar panel (secret door) in its south wall drops into **L13**, the Ant Queen's cave ([[Nethercity L - Abyssal Level Caves]]); 40’ west it ends at the sealed door of **N11**.
- **Secret way:** in **N11** the hepatizon orb in the south-west archway opens a passwall into **N12**, which runs south-west then north-west under area M ([[Nethercity M - Northern Caves]]) to **N13**, whose grey archway (electrum orb) opens on **O1** ([[Nethercity O - Sub-Abyssal Secret Tomb of the Hierophant]]).
- **Out of the world:** N11's west and south archways open gates to **Kanahu** and the **Sphere of Chthonic Darkness**; the N5 disc sends living creatures to the **Thieves' Quarter of Cyfaraun** ([[Old District]] #9).

(SOTN lists 19 scrolls for 13 jars; some jars hold more than one.)

- Tally: **50,030gp, 171 st** (scrolls and formulae excluded).

### N4. Alchemic Laboratory Chimerical Apparatus (2200 BE)
- **Exits:** E open to N2 (map); W a 15’ × 15’ opening to N5, sealed by a wall of force; sealed door in the south wall to N6.

> [!player]
> A plastered room, 40’ by 30’. Against the north wall sit two great hemispheres of strange iridescent metal, each 10’ across, their rims fitted with manacle clasps; 10’ above them, two identical hemispheres hang from the ceiling like a reflection. In the south wall is a bronze door. West of it, an opening 15’ wide and high shows a chamber beyond, where an astonishingly lifelike statue of a three-headed chimera stands on a metal disc. East of the door, a metal console fixed to the south wall bristles with dials and levers.

- **Trick (wall of force):** the opening to N5 is closed by a permanent, invisible [[Wall of Force]]. It cannot be dispelled, but [[Disintegration]] destroys it, and the thick lever on the console lowers and raises it.
- **Trick (the console):** positions as found:
  - **two small bronze vertical levers**, left and right, both **up**;
  - a **bronze dial** turning left and right, set **two-thirds left**;
  - a **thick bronze vertical lever**, **down** (the wall of force is up; Judge's call: lever up = wall down);
  - a **long bronze horizontal lever**, **left**;
  - an **ivory button**, **up** (not pressed).
- **What they do:**
  - **Small levers:** each works the hemispheres on its side. Down: the ceiling hemisphere lowers onto the floor one, closing a metal **egg**; up: the egg opens again. Each egg safely holds **one creature of up to Large size**.
  - **Dial:** sets which creature dominates the crossbreed: left favours the creature in the left egg, right the one in the right egg.
  - **Long lever:** slid **right**, it runs the chimeric process. **Two living creatures** in the closed eggs: both are **destroyed** and **one crossbred creature appears instantly in N5**. **One living creature** in one egg: it is simply **teleported to N5**. Non-living matter, dead or undead bodies are ignored: objects cannot be fused, nor corpses, nor the living with the dead. The lever must be slid back **left** to reset after each use, and it **cannot move unless both small levers are down** (a safety interlock: nothing is fused or sent outside the eggs).
  - **Thick lever:** raises and lowers the wall of force into N5 (and with it the stasis, see N5).
  - **Ivory button:** works the teleportation disc in N5 (see N5).
- **Trick (using it):** once worked out (SOTN sets no throw; let the players experiment, Judge's call), and given living subjects, the apparatus crossbreeds monsters instantly with no magic research, gold or time; design the results with the ACKS II crossbreeding rules ([[Crossbreeds]]). Its power is nearly spent: **each use has a cumulative 5% chance** that it breaks for good (Judge's call: each slide of the long lever is a use; the wall lever and the button are not). Note that a creature fused or sent into N5 while the wall of force is up arrives **in temporal stasis**.
- **Loot:** the iridescent metal of the four hemispheres can be stripped (destroying the apparatus) and sold or used in magic research: **20,000gp, 4 st**, the same alloy as in the Grand Chthonic Reliquary (F17, [[Nethercity F - Lower Level Central Complex]]: tougher, harder and lighter than steel; a precious material for masterwork or magic arms and armour).

### N5. Alchemic Laboratory Stasis Chamber (2200 BE)
- **Exits:** E the 15’ × 15’ opening (wall of force) to N4; no other.

> [!player]
> Through the opening you see a plastered chamber 30’ square. On its floor lies a single disc of iridescent metal, 15’ across, and on the disc stands a startlingly lifelike statue of a three-headed horror, part lion, part goat, part dragon.

- **Monster / Trick:** the "statue" is a **live chimera**, held in **temporal stasis**: while the wall of force is up, every creature inside N5 has its time stopped completely (no save). Lowering the wall ends the stasis at once, and the chimera, driven mad by its making, **attacks immediately**.
  **Chimera** → [[Chimera]]: MV 40’/120’, fly 60’/180’, AC 5, HD 9*, hp 42, 2 claws 2+ (1d3/1d3 extraordinary slashing), 3 heads 2+ (lion and goat 2d4/2d4, dragon 3d4 extraordinary piercing) or the dragon head breathes fire (50% each round until spent): cone 60’ long and 30’ wide at the end, Blast save or 3d6 extraordinary fire, half on a success, 3 times a day; F9, ML +1, AL C, XP 1,300. (SOTN prints attacks 3+ and a 50’ × 10’ cone; the MM figures replace them. Insane: no parley, Judge's call.)
- **Trick: the teleportation disc** (SOTN pp. 107-108):
  - **The disc:** 15’ across, iridescent metal, flat on the floor in the middle of N5. Its control is the **ivory button** on the N4 console, out of reach of anyone standing on the disc: someone has to stay in N4 to press it.
  - **Effect:** pressing the button sends **every living creature on the disc** by dimension door (ACKS II [[Arcane Shift]]) **to the surface, 100’ straight up**, into **the middle of the Thieves' Quarter of Cyfaraun** ([[Old District]] #9: rotten timber alleys run by the Argollëan Family, [[Organizations of Cyfaraun]]). Gear worn and carried goes along. Dead bodies, undead and loose objects on the disc stay (Judge's call from "living creatures").
  - **No mishap:** the Nethercity's anti-teleportation wards do not touch the disc ([[Nethercity Features#Teleportation (SOTN p. 14)]]); arrivals land exactly, never inside solid matter (Judge's call: on the street, in the middle of the Quarter).
  - **One way only:** nothing in SOTN sends anyone back down; the way home is on foot, through the Undercity entrance under the same district ([[Old District]] #12u, [[Undercity of Cyfaraun]]).
  - **Stasis and the button:** the button works whether the wall of force is up or down. With the wall **up**, whoever is on the disc is frozen in time, and is sent up frozen (Judge's call: the stasis ends on arrival). With the wall **down**, they arrive as they stood.
  - **The chimera:** pressing the button while the chimera stands on the disc drops it, mad and hungry, into the Thieves' Quarter. Whatever it does there, the party learns of it only on returning to the surface (Judge's call: a night of terror in the Old District, Watch and Family casualties, and a reward posted for whoever kills it; the Family will want to know where it came from).
  - **Getting onto the disc:** walk in with the wall lowered (and fight the chimera, or send it up first), or be put in an N4 egg alone and teleported in with the long lever (arriving in stasis if the wall is up: the rest of the party must lower the wall to free you). A crossbreed made in N4 also appears here and can be sent up the same way.
  - The disc radiates magic (Judge's call). Its iridescent metal is part of the device; SOTN gives no value for it.

### N7. Alchemic Laboratory Necrosection Table (2200 BE)
- **Exits:** E the sealed door to N6.

> [!player]
> Past the bronze door is a plastered chamber 25’ wide and 40’ long. In the middle stands a big marble table fitted with green-crusted bronze manacles and chains, and chained to it lies something about 8’ tall: an ogre crossed with a spider, but not a clean blend like a griffin; a patchwork, with spindly palps bursting from fleshy jowls, bristly spider-leg segments growing beside muscular thighs, plates of chitin sewn across hips and ribs. Its belly and face have been cut open, the skin folded back and nailed to the table with iron pins, showing yellow bone overgrown with glossy black chitin. Beside it stand old glass jars of shrivelled organs and brain. It is breathing. Its jaw of bone and chitin works without a sound. Beyond the table, against the west wall, a wooden workbench holds a bronze bone saw, half a dozen scalpels, a box of iron pins and a clutter of copper flasks. A drawing of the creature hangs on the south wall.

- **Trick (necrosection table):** radiates strong magic. A creature on it **cannot die**: whatever its injuries the soul stays in the body and the flesh does not rot; even a severed head goes on seeing, hearing, speaking and thinking, and the body stays warm and fresh. But the table **heals nothing**, and its necromancy **stops healing magic** working on whoever lies on it. Taken off, the creature is back in its mortal state at once. Abominations from N4/N5 too malformed to live were kept here for vivisection; this one has lain in agony for thousands of years. **Taken off the table, it dies messily within 1 round.** (Judge's call: a PC at 0 hp or with a mortal wound laid on the table does not die while there, but makes no Mortal Wounds recovery and gets no healing until taken off; then roll as normal.)
- **Lore:** **Healing (1 rank):** it should be dead of its wounds. **Healing (2 ranks):** it is, or was, being systematically vivisected.
- **Lore / Loot:** the drawing (SOTN places it on the south wall in the boxed text and the west wall in the loot; south wall here) is a detailed anatomical study of the creature (**250gp, 1/6 st**). **Healing (3 ranks)** and study: its anatomy was too deformed to live; it would have died very quickly.
- **Loot (flasks):** 18 copper flasks on the bench, **1/6 st each**; 4 empty. The rest:
  - 1 dark brown, chunky, spinach-scented broth: Potion of **[[Growth]]**;
  - 1 golden, honey-scented fluid: potion of healing (ACKS II Potion of **[[Cure Light Injury]]**, golden and honey-scented in the ACKS II table too);
  - 1 clear water smelling of rotting flesh: potion of invulnerability (Judge's call: ACKS II Potion of **[[Necromantic Invulnerability]]**, which the ACKS II table describes by the same smell; ACKS I's invulnerability potion has no ACKS II equivalent);
  - 1 wine-red aromatic syrup: potion of longevity (Judge's call: ACKS II **[[Potion of Youth]]**);
  - 1 steely, oily balm: Oil of **[[Sharpness]]**;
  - 2 of thick black blood: **dragon blood** poison (ACKS II: instant, Death save at 0 or die);
  - 2 of sticky brown syrup with a tarry smell: **curare** (ACKS II: instant on injury, save 0, 2d12 poisonous damage and paralysis 2d4 turns);
  - 5 of syrupy, tangy fluid: **carcass scavenger** poison (ACKS II [[Carrion Horror]] venom: 1 turn, save +2, paralysis 2d4 turns). Poison profiles: [[Poisons (Equipment)]].
- Tally: **4,500gp, 2 3/6 st** as SOTN prints it (drawing 250gp; the pairs of dragon blood and of curare at 1,500gp a pair; 5 × 250gp carcass scavenger; the 14 full flasks and the drawing weigh 2 3/6 st; potions' value excluded). (ACKS II prices: dragon blood and curare 1,500gp a dose, carrion horror venom 300gp, which would make it 7,750gp; Judge's call: the book's 4,500gp.)

> [!player]
> A man-sized cedar panel carved with ancient script is fixed to the south wall of the hall. The passage runs on 40’ west and ends at a heavy bronze door.

### N9. Alchemic Laboratory Stasis Cell (2200 BE)
- **Exits:** W the trapped bronze door to N8a.

> [!player]
> Behind the door is a plastered cell 20’ wide and 30’ deep. Out of it lurches a monstrous thing like a scorpion, nine venomous stings arched over its back, ready to strike.

- **Monster:** a **hydropion** made by the alchemists and held in temporal stasis since its creation. The stasis ends when the door opens and it attacks at once; it will not attack approved personnel unless they attack it first. It has 9 stingers, so 9 HD at maximum hp (SOTN p. 144).
  **Hydropion** → [[Hydropion (SOTN)]] (no MM equivalent): MV 40’/120’, AC 7, HD 9***, hp 72, 2 claws 2+ (1d10 each) and 9 stingers 2+ (1d4 + poison each), F9, ML +3, AL C, XP 2,500. **Poison:** a creature stung makes a **Death** save or dies instantly. A claw hit on a target gives that round's stinger attacks on the same target **+2**. **Severable stingers:** one stinger is lost for every 8 hp of damage; it dies when the last goes. (SOTN prints attack throws 3+ and no alignment; ACKS II 9 HD attack at 2+; the appendix gives Chaotic.)

### N10. Alchemic Laboratory Embalming Chamber (2200 BE)
- **Exits:** E the sealed door to N8b.

> [!player]
> A plastered room 35’ wide and 30’ long. Two big marble tables stand in the middle, stained black with blood and scored with cuts. In the north-west corner a nook 10’ wide and 15’ deep is crammed with old embalming tools and dozens of terracotta jars, urns and vials.

- **Loot:** in the clutter: 12 bronze hooks (**5gp, 1/6 st each**), 40 terracotta canopic jars (**5gp, 1/6 st each**), 30 vials of blue natron (**15gp, 1/6 st each**) and 3 red-figure terracotta urns (**10gp, 3/6 st each**) holding sacred charms: a set of engraved finger bones (**50gp**), an amber pendant (**300gp**), a bronze brooch (**100gp**), a silver and obsidian amulet (**100gp**), and **two orbs, one of hepatizon and one of electrum, inscribed with eldritch glyphs (1,000gp and 1/6 st each)**. The orbs are not magic themselves; they are the **keys to the portals of N11 and N13**. All this counts as embalming equipment ([[Nethercity Features#Treasure (SOTN pp. 17-18)]]).
- Tally: **3,290gp, 15 2/6 st** as SOTN prints it (the listed items weigh 15 3/6 st).

### N11. Alchemic Laboratory Summoning Chamber (2200 BE)
- **Exits:** E the sealed door to N8c; archways W, SW and S, which open only by the orbs (SW: to N12).

> [!player]
> An arrow-shaped room with plastered walls: 50’ west and 50’ south, then bending south and west for 20’ before closing on the diagonal. The floor is black marble with a silver pentagram inlaid at its centre. In the middle of the west, south-west and south walls stand archways of black-veined purple marble, 5’ wide and 8’ high, each opening onto nothing but bare rock. A small cup-shaped hollow is cut in the stone above the crown of each arch.

- **Trap (electrum orb):** setting the **electrum orb** in the hollow above **any** purple archway sets off a magical explosion: **11d6 fire damage** to everything in the room, **Blast** save for half (Judge's call: extraordinary fire). The orb is unharmed. Permanent and repeatable; it cannot be detected or removed without magic, and only a caster of **11th level or higher** can dispel it.
- **Trick (hepatizon orb):** the **hepatizon orb** in the hollow above an archway works that arch:
  - **West:** a *gate* (Judge's call: no ACKS II equivalent) opens a two-way portal onto a lush jungle under a stepped pyramid adorned with statues of tall lizardmen. **Loremastery at −8**: Kanahu, a fabled world where lizardmen serve Law and men practise black sorcery (SOTN refers to *Barbarians of Kanahu*; not in this campaign: Judge's call what lies beyond).
  - **South:** a *gate* opens a two-way portal onto a bleak, desolate land under a lightless sky. **Loremastery or Theology**: the **Sphere of Chthonic Darkness**.
  - **South-west:** a passwall ([[Passageway]]) opens into the secret corridor **N12**.
  - Each effect lasts **while the orb stays in that hollow, plus 3 turns** after it is taken out.
- **Trapped beyond:** characters who take the hepatizon orb out of the south-west hollow before going into N12/N13 (or Area O) can be shut in. The rock can be removed by [[Disintegration]] or [[Earth's Mire]] (Judge's call: the archway's backing is bare rock, so it qualifies), or **dug through as a 50gp construction project**, up to **4 characters** working at once. (The orbs swap roles in N13: there the electrum opens and the hepatizon blasts. To go through and come back, leave the hepatizon here and carry the electrum to N13; taking either out shuts the way behind it after 3 turns.)
- The secret corridor behind the south-west arch **cannot be detected or reached without magic**. All three archways radiate magic.

### N12. Alchemical Laboratory Secret Corridor (2200 BE)
- **Exits:** NE the south-west arch of N11 (open only while the passwall lasts); the corridor runs 20’ south-west, turns sharply north-west and becomes N13.

> [!player]
> Past the opening, a corridor runs 20’ south-west, then turns hard north-west into shadow. Floor and ceiling are great stone slabs; the walls are bare, unplastered and undecorated except for a row of cone-shaped ceramic sconces set every 10’.

- **Trap (pressure plate):** **70’** down the corridor a small pressure plate lies under a floor slab. A Searching throw to find traps finds it ([[Searching and Listening]]), and once found it is easily stepped over. Undetected, **each adventurer crossing it triggers it on 1-2 on 1d6** ([[Traps and Trapbreaking]]). Triggered, **all the sconces drop and shatter**, each filling a 10’ cube with **yellow mold spores** ([[Mustard Mold]] spores), and since there is one every 10’, the whole corridor fills. **Every creature in N12 makes a Death save at +2** (the spores are old and dry) at the start of its initiative **each round**; failure: it has breathed them and **dies in 6 rounds** unless the poison is neutralized. The spores become harmless after **1 turn**.
- **Trap (sconces):** **14 sconces** in all. Searching for traps on a sconce shows it is hollow, thin and fragile ceramic; succeeding **by 5 or more** also shows it hangs on a mechanical strut that would swing down and drop it were something inside the wall not holding it up.
  - **Removing a sconce hastily:** no throw, but **2 in 6** (SOTN 33%) it breaks and releases its spores.
  - **Carefully:** a Trapbreaking throw ([[Traps and Trapbreaking]]); failure: 2 in 6 it breaks; success: removed safely.
  - A removed sconce can be **thrown like a flask of oil** to burst and spread its spores as above. Anyone carrying one who is **damaged or knocked down**: 2 in 6 it breaks.

### N13. Alchemical Laboratory Secret Corridor (2200 BE)
- **Exits:** back south-east to N12; the grey archway at the west end, opening by the electrum orb onto O1.

> [!player]
> After about 180’ the corridor turns west and runs another 40’ to end at an archway of greyish-white marble, 5’ wide and 8’ tall, with a small cup-shaped hollow above its crown.

- **Trap (hepatizon orb):** setting the **hepatizon orb** in the hollow above the grey arch looses a blast of lightning: **11d6 electric damage** to everything in the room (Judge's call: everyone in the 40’ western leg), **Blast** save for half. The orb is unharmed. Permanent and repeatable; undetectable and irremovable without magic; dispelled only by a caster of **11th level or higher**.
- **Trick (electrum orb):** the **electrum orb** in the hollow makes a passwall ([[Passageway]]) into the **Secret Tomb Gallery O1** ([[Nethercity O - Sub-Abyssal Secret Tomb of the Hierophant]]), open **while the orb stays in place plus 3 turns**. As in N11, taking the orb out before going into Area O can trap the party there. The way through cannot be detected or reached without magic; the archway radiates magic.
