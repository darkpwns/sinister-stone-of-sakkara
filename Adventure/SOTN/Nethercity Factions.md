# Nethercity Factions — groups and NPCs

Judge only. Who wants what in and above the Nethercity, and the NPCs a party may talk to. Statlines are in the linked
bestiary notes and area notes; this note gives the relations, the traits and the voices. Hub: [[SOTN Hub]].

## Inside the Nethercity

### The troglodyte tribe — area I ([[Nethercity I - Deep Level Southern Cave Complex]])
- **Who:** degenerate Thrassian stock bred by the elves (SOTN p. 10). Chieftain, sub-chieftain and shaman; 8 champions;
  37 warriors in I plus a hunting gang of 6 on the D8 balcony ([[Nethercity D - Warlord's Tomb and Chapel to Bel]]);
  52 breeders, 40 eggs. Morale +2 while the chieftain lives (else +1).
- **Wants:** food (locusts, crabs, giant insects, white apes), water from the purification font in J4
  ([[Nethercity J - Doom Temple of Dirgion]]), to be left alone. Five water-bearers go to J4 and back (throw 1d6 each
  turn, 5+, in I7, J1, J4).
- **Enemies:** the rhagodessae of F19, the giant catfish of the river (P), the white apes (M), the giant ants (L), whose
  coming ended their hunts through the teleportation platform.
- **Parley:** warriors ambush from chameleon cover and judge the party's strength first (darts against the weak, a run
  home for help against the strong). **The shaman** will try to negotiate if not attacked on sight, and will **guide the
  party to the "magic rock" (L4)** if they promise to harm the tribe no further. Language: debased Elvish (an Elvish
  speaker follows the gist).
- **Replacing losses:** SOTN gives no rate (hub, Replacement of casualties).

### The giant ant colony — area L ([[Nethercity L - Abyssal Level Caves]])
- Vermin, not a parleying faction: 24 workers, 4 soldiers (L12), the queen (L13), 18 eggs. The whole of L is their nest:
  Hostile to intruders, **ML +4 while the queen lives**. They farm fungus in empty wall graves on sludge. An attack on
  the queen brings every ant.
- **Enemies:** the white apes, who raid their farms; the troglodytes, whose old hunting route (L4) they block. A party can
  set apes and ants on each other.

### The white ape band — area M ([[Nethercity M - Northern Caves]])
- About 40 [[Cave Gorilla]]s: young males guard the approaches (M2, M5), females, children and babies in M11, the
  leader in M13. At an alarm they rally at the statue of Nargund (M10), where the injured fly into blood-rage. Females
  fight to the death while any young lives.
- **Leader:** [[Great White Ape (SOTN)]]. **Exile:** [[Elderly White Ape (SOTN)]] (M14), ousted, lonely, **+2 to reactions**
  with the party; the one ape that remembers the teleportation platform. If the leader dies he takes the band back.
- **Wants:** food (the ants' fungus, ant flesh), water (L20's pool), safety for the young. Prey of the troglodytes.
- **Value:** pelts (intact only under the pelt rule, [[Nethercity Features]]) and live young for monster dealers.

### The sleeping dead
- **Rule for all mummies:** wrath on waking; one spared, parleyed with or fled from rolls on
  `Tables/sotn-mummy-reaction.yaml`, read through its life ([[Nethercity Features]], "Necromancy and the mummies").
  The temples' **guardians** (faewyrd, statues) spare those purified at a font (G4, J11) unless they despoil what is
  guarded.
- **Orthanach's court** (O3, [[Nethercity O - Sub-Abyssal Secret Tomb of the Hierophant]]): the mummy lord hierophant,
  his spouse and his bodyguard; the O2 statues obey him. His name, the password to O3, is in the J14 cult records.
- **Marcan's household** (Q, [[Nethercity Q - Great King Tomb]]): the warlord-king who destroyed Azen Khador, with his
  guards and statues; despises dwarves and anything like a lizardman (a dwarf in the party: −2 to reactions, Judge's call).
- **The Great Teacher** (T2, [[Nethercity T - Sub-Abyssal Secret Alien Sepulcher]]): the [[Great Teacher (SOTN)]], a khepri
  hierarch in cryostasis since the late 21st c. BE, maker of the Chthonic elves, the Thrassians and the Awakening's
  prophecy. The S2 Iskaran cultist would serve him. Waking him is the module's greatest danger and greatest secret.
- **Others:** solitary mummies of the temples (G, H, J, R, S) — each listed below — act alone.

## Above: Cyfaraun (AX3; full in [[Organizations of Cyfaraun]])
- **The Argollëan Family** (SOTN: "Argollëan Brotherhood"; Liber Faunus, The Mink #11/#11u, [[Old District]]): the
  Nethercity lies under its streets. Wants every elven antiquity through its hands: fences at 65% (80% to friends);
  rare coin and art draw its notice on 1% per 2,000 gp spent, sold or banked, after which it finds the source and may
  **hire a rival party** to delve (the [[Imperial Vanguard]] is the obvious hire, Judge's call). Bypassing it brings
  racketeers, then assassins.
- **The Cult of the Horned Rat** (Provost Mentenus Cavië and his wererat apprentices, [[Shrine of the Horned Rat]], #13u):
  searching the sewers for the way in, in shifts of eight. If they find it first they become rival delvers and spread
  the word to the Tower of Knowledge's scholars.
- **The Prefecture's tax collectors:** half of Undercity finds are owed; sellers of antiquities are questioned
  ([[Doing Business in Cyfaraun]]).
- **Buyers and sages:** Damanos, House of Curiosities (Old District #4), buys antiquities and identifies common magic;
  the Tower of Knowledge identifies items (2,000 gp) and would pay for Argollëan lore ([[Tower District]]).
- **The Temple of Mityara / Keepers of the Hearth Fire** ([[Temple District]]): the sealing temple at A1 was Demara's
  (Mityara's); clergy of Law would want the Nethercity cleansed and the Great Teacher destroyed (Judge's call).
