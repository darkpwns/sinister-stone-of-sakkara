# SOTN Spells

Judge only. Hub: [[SOTN Hub]]. Items: [[SOTN Magic Items]]. Pages are SOTN's printed pages.

SOTN reprints, as arcane and/or divine spells, the eldritch spells of the Heroic Fantasy Handbook, plus one new spell, **preservation of antiquity** (SOTN p. 153). Most of them are now ACKS II core spells, often revised. Each entry gives SOTN's version in ACKS II terms (levels by SOTN's lists: ACKS I arcane and divine, and the cultist repertoires of [[Elven Cultist]] and [[Elven Hierophant]], SOTN pp. 163-166), then the ACKS II equivalent and how it differs.

**Judge's call (default):** when an NPC casts one of these spells, or a PC learns it from a Nethercity formula, use the **ACKS II spell** where one exists (the engine and the vault use it); SOTN's version is kept for reference and for the few spells ACKS II lacks. Saves keep SOTN's categories (all are already ACKS II categories). ACKS I terms: charm → bewitch; hold → halt; hear noise → Listening; move silently → sneak; turn → rebuke; energy drain → enervation.

Cultist repertoires in SOTN (for reference): **Bel** 1 bane-rune; **Dirgion** 1 destroy dead, unliving puppet; 3 strengthen the unliving; 4 shrieking skull; 5 preservation of antiquity, summon shadows; **Iskara** 2 display of power; 4 inspire horror; **Nasga** 4 dark whisper; 5 bath of the goddess, guise self; **Telith** 1 sling stone; 2 earth's excrescence, shatter blade; 3 earth's maw; 4 battering ram; 5 fangs of the earth, weave stone (SOTN p. 164).

## Auditory Illusion
- **SOTN:** Arcane 1 · Range 60’ · Duration 1 turn (p. 153).
- Creates a false sound centred anywhere in range, which can move and change there (footsteps coming or going, laughter, voices, animal or monster noises), as loud as **thirty man-sized creatures** (Judge decides the equivalents: thirty men ≈ sixty children ≈ eight ogres). A hearer who **actively tries to disbelieve** saves vs **Spells** to know it is illusory.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Auditory Illusion]] (Arcane 1): range **30’**, duration **1 round per level**; a suspicious hearer saves at the start of its initiative, and on a failure cannot retry without new evidence; adds triggered casting.

## Bane-rune
- **SOTN:** Arcane 1, Divine 1 (Cultist of Bel) · Range touch · Duration 1 turn (p. 153).
- A baleful glyph on a weapon's striking head makes it a **magic weapon +2** against one chosen type: animals, beastmen, enchanted creatures, dragons, fantastic creatures (not dragons), giants, oozes, undead or vermin. An already-enchanted weapon gains **an extra +2** against that type. One casting: **20 arrows, 2 one-handed weapons, or 1 two-handed weapon**.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Bane-Rune]] (Divine 1): ACKS II creature types (adds constructs, humanoids, incarnations, monstrosities, plants); already-magic weapons gain only **+1**; 20 arrows, 2 tiny or 1 small/medium/large weapon; does not stack with itself.

## Bath of the Goddess
- **SOTN:** Arcane 6, Divine 5 (Cultist of Nasga) · Range touch · Duration permanent (p. 153).
- Turns a **willing** living creature into the opposite sex of its kind, with every primary and secondary characteristic (inside and out, reproductive capacity if of breeding age), like an opposite-sex twin. Sexually altered creatures (eunuchs) keep the equivalent alteration; single-sexed species are unaffected; for multi-sexed species or intersexed creatures the caster chooses the result (a male or female may be made intersexed). Permanent until [[Dispel Magic]] or a second casting. No bathing needed; named for the myth of a priest turned bladedancer after bathing in a moonlit pond where Ianna had bathed.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Bath of the Goddess]] (Divine 6): target man-sized or smaller; perpetual, dispelled by counterspell, dispel magic or a second casting; becomes **permanent** if the creature births or sires children in its new sex. Item form: [[SOTN Magic Items#Bathtub of the Goddess]].

## Battering Ram
- **SOTN:** Divine 4 (Cultist of Telith) · Range 90’ · Duration instantaneous (p. 153).
- An invisible ram smashes a wooden or stone object or structure: **1d4 SHP** (enough to fell an oak door or break off a parapet). Flying shards or splinters deal **5d6** to every creature within **5’**, half on a **Blast** save. Cannot target creatures or open ground; an interior floor only if the caster is at least one storey above it.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Battering Ram]] (Arcane 2, Divine 4): range **120’**; target larger than man-sized with at least 1 SHP; **1d4** extraordinary seismic SHP to stone, **1d4 × 5** to wood; shrapnel **1d6 per caster level (max 5d6)** piercing in a 5’ radius, Blast save for half.

## Body Swap
- **SOTN:** Arcane 6 · Range touch · Duration permanent (p. 153).
- Two living creatures take on each other's forms for good: each gains the other's physical capabilities, statistics, physical attacks and special abilities, keeps its own mind and **its own hit points**. Not on incorporeal or gaseous creatures, nor on creatures with more HD than the caster's level. The caster touches both (an unwilling one needs an attack throw); each may save vs **Spells**, and if either saves the spell fails. [[Dispel Magic]] swaps them back; if one has died meanwhile, the survivor saves vs **Death**: failure, it returns to its dead body and dies; success, it keeps the new form.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Body Swap]] (Arcane 6): targets man-sized or smaller; the caster may be one of them; only unwilling creatures save; **perpetual** (ends if the caster dies).

## Boil Blood
- **SOTN:** Arcane 5 · Range 150’ · Duration instantaneous (p. 153).
- A living target saves vs **Blast**: failure, **8d8** damage and **stunned** on its next initiative; success, half damage, not stunned. Either way, if this takes it to 0 hp or less it explodes and dies; each creature or object within **5’** takes **1d6**, half on a **Blast** save.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Boil Blood]] (Arcane 3, Divine 5): range **225’**; **1d8 per caster level (max 8d8)** extraordinary fire; failure leaves it **vomiting** until the end of its next initiative ([[Choking and Vomiting (Condition)]]); the explosion also sets combustibles alight. (SOTN "stunned" → ACKS II vomiting; Judge's call.)

## Bloody Flux
- **SOTN:** Arcane 2 · Range 90’ · Duration special (p. 154).
- A target saves vs **Death** or begins vomiting blood: it cannot attack, cast, move, speak or act, though it defends itself. On each of its initiatives it takes **1d12**, then saves again; success ends the spell. Lasts until dispelled, the target dies, or it saves; no concentration needed.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Bloody Flux]] (Arcane 2): range **120’**; living target; damage at the start of its initiative, extraordinary necrotic; otherwise the same.

## Circle of Agony
- **SOTN:** Arcane 5 · Range 30’ · Duration special (p. 154).
- **One creature per caster level** in range saves vs **Death** or is agonised: helpless (cannot attack, cast or move; no DEX or shield AC; can be ambushed or backstabbed; attacks on it **+2**, **+4** from ambush or backstab, backstab multiplies damage; melee hits automatically if the attacker is its size or larger; an unengaged attacker able to harm it may slay it outright, but may not cleave after). SOTN gives **no end condition and no damage** (book omission).
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Circle of Agony]] (Arcane 5): range **45’**; **one to five** living targets with fewer than **9 HD**; [[Helpless (Condition)|helpless]]; **1d4** extraordinary necrotic at the start of each of its initiatives, then a new Death save ends it on a success. (Judge's call: use ACKS II, which supplies the missing end.)

## Crafting
- **SOTN:** Arcane 4, Divine 5 (Hierophant) · Range touch · Duration special (p. 154).
- The target's Art or Craft proficiency rises **one rank** for one project (one sword, painting, song): unskilled → apprentice → journeyman → master → grand master; a grand master can make **magic items at twice a mage's base time and cost**. Ends when the project is done or a day passes without **8 hours** of work on it.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Crafting]] (Divine 4): duration indefinite; grand masters make magic items as mages of their own level at **twice the component, material and research cost**; recipient man-sized or smaller ([[Art and Craft]]).

## Dark Whisper
- **SOTN:** Arcane 3, Divine 4 (Cultist of Nasga) · Range touch · Duration instantaneous (p. 154).
- Whispered words wound the soul: an attack throw is needed unless the target is helpless. It saves vs **Death**; on a failure the caster picks one effect:
  - whispers from beyond the grave: **−2 to Listening** (SOTN: hear noise) and **−2 to surprise**;
  - constant muttering: cannot sneak or surprise, **−1** to spellcasting initiative;
  - nightmares: each rest, roll **1d6**; on a **1** no sleep, no spells recovered, no healing;
  - drowning horror in consumption: cumulative **−1 to all saves every hour** until it consumes something (smoke, drink, etc.).
  - The caster may invent a whisper of no greater power. No limit on the number suffered. Cannot be dispelled; [[Remove Curse]] by a caster of equal or higher level removes it.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Dark Whisper]] (Arcane 2, Divine 2): range special; a deaf target is immune; removed by [[Dispel Evil]] or [[Remove Curse]] (no level requirement).

## Destroy Dead
- **SOTN:** Divine 1 (Cultist of Dirgion) · Range 150’ · Duration instantaneous (p. 154).
- One undead skeleton or zombie of **8 HD or less** saves vs **Death** or crumbles to dust; an unanimated skeleton or corpse crumbles with no save. No effect on the living or on other undead.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Destroy Dead]] (Divine 1): range **225’**; less than 9 HD (same limit).

## Display of Power
- **SOTN:** Arcane 1, Divine 2 (Cultist of Iskara) · Range self · Duration 6 turns (p. 154).
- The caster shows off his power in a display shaped by his spell signature ([[Spell Signatures]]): **+2 to reaction rolls** with creatures he meets (stacks with ability, class and proficiency bonuses). On a total of **12 or more** the subjects act as if **bewitched** while in his presence.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** none. Nearest: [[Allure]] (Divine 1, self, 6 turns: the benefits of [[Mystic Aura]], or +1 more to reactions if he has it).
- **JJ build (Judge's call):** the JJ enchantment tables have no reaction-roll effect, so it is **esoteric**; keep SOTN's levels (Arcane 1, Divine 2).

## Earth's Excrescence
- **SOTN:** Divine 2 (Cultist of Telith) · Range 60’ · Duration instantaneous (p. 155).
- A **15’ diameter** patch of earth bursts like a boil. Each creature in it saves vs **Blast**: failure, **3d6** and knocked down; success, half and keeps its feet. Huge or larger creatures take damage but are not knocked down. Only from soil, sand, silt, clay or uncut rock; not worked stone, wood, cement, concrete, metal or magical materials.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Earth's Excrescence]] (Arcane 1): range **90’**; **1d6 per caster level (max 3d6)** extraordinary seismic; also damages structures in the area.

## Earth's Maw
- **SOTN:** Divine 3 (Cultist of Telith) · Range 60’ · Duration indefinite (p. 155).
- A **10’ × 10’** area of earth collapses into a pit **10’ deep**, possibly under creatures. Each there saves vs **Blast**: success, it leaps clear; failure, it falls in for **1d6** falling damage unless protected (flight, levitation). Only soil, sand, silt, clay or uncut rock. The pit remains until filled by hand or dispelled; dispelling makes the earth heave up and re-form, depositing those inside on the surface, who save vs **Blast** or take **1d6** and are knocked prone.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Create Chasm]] (Arcane 3): **500 square feet**, range **90’**; unattended objects fall in; perpetual (ends if the caster dies or stops sustaining it).

## Endless Slumber
- **SOTN:** Arcane 4 · Range 60’ · Duration until dispelled (p. 155).
- One target saves vs **Spells** or falls into a deep magical sleep from which **no natural means** can wake it. [[Dispel Magic]] or [[Remove Curse]] may rouse it, with a **5% chance of failure per level** the dispeller is below the original caster. The caster may name a waking condition when casting (spoken aloud; must be possible without magic; classically a kiss from royalty of the opposite sex). With a condition, only a caster of **higher level** than the original can lift it by magic. A divination or fate spell on the sleeper reveals the condition.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** none as a spell. Nearest: [[Slumber, Deep]] (Arcane 4, a 20’ sphere, 1 turn per level, ended by any disturbance) and the item [[Apple of Endless Slumber]] (the same sleep, ended by a caster of 8th level+ or a royal kiss). Sleeper: [[Slumbering (Condition)]].
- **JJ build (Judge's call):** enchantment: target slumbering (15) × only ended with magic or condition (×3) = 45; 1 creature (×1); perpetual (×2.2); 60’ (×0.9); save avoids (×0.5); arcane (×1) = 44.6 → **45 points, 5th level** (SOTN prints 4th; Judge's call: keep Arcane 4 for the Nethercity formulas as an ancient breakthrough, [[Determining Spell Level]]).

## Fangs of the Earth
- **SOTN:** Divine 5 (Cultist of Telith) · Range 120’ · Duration instantaneous (p. 155).
- Jagged pillars erupt to impale **one creature per caster level**, all within one **30’ diameter** area, chosen by the caster; an attack throw per target; each hit deals **5d6**. The pillars then crumble. Only from soil, sand, silt, clay or uncut rock.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Earth's Teeth]] (Arcane 3): range **180’**; **5d6** extraordinary piercing; no ground restriction stated.

## Fillet and Serve
- **SOTN:** Arcane 5 · Range 90’ · Duration special (p. 155).
- A living target saves vs **Death**; failure, its skeleton tears out of its body, killing it, and rises as an undead skeleton of the HD the creature had in life (**1 HD** for humans and demi-humans whatever their level). It obeys the caster mindlessly until dispelled or destroyed, or for **one day**, then falls apart.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Fillet and Serve]] (Arcane 5, Divine 6): range **120’**; Baseline HD; orders rules; no one-day limit stated.

## Flesh to Ashes
- **SOTN:** Arcane 4 · Range 90’ · Duration instantaneous (p. 155).
- A creature of flesh saves vs **Death** or is **disintegrated with its carried gear**. Creatures of **8+ HD or levels** are immune, as are undead, golems and anything not truly alive or not of flesh.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Flesh to Ash]] (Arcane 4, Divine 6): range **120’**; fewer than 9 HD (same limit); the creature is slain and left as dust, but its **gear is not affected**.

## Forest Enchantment
- **SOTN:** Arcane 5 · Range 0’ · Duration special (pp. 155-156).
- Bewitches all plants within **240’** of the caster: he and those he introduces as friends pass unharmed; intruding enemies are harried. **+1 / −1 to surprise** for allies / enemies, and movement multipliers: hills, forest, scrub ×1 allies / ×1/2 enemies; jungle, swamps ×2/3 / ×1/3. Normal plants: no save, then a seasonal save as a **1 HD monster**; severe damage by the caster ends it (gardening does not). Plant monsters of **2 HD or more** save vs **Spells**; the bewitchment lasts until dispelled or a save, rolled **daily at 7+1 HD or more, weekly at 5-6 HD, monthly at 4 HD or fewer**. No speech with the plants.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Forest Enchantment]] (Arcane 5): the same, with terrain rows "hills, forest, scrubland" and "mountain, jungle"; plant creatures save at 9+ / 5-8 / under 5 HD intervals; the plants can never again be enchanted after severe damage; barred from barren, desert, steppe, rocky hills and mountains, low shrubland. Item form: [[SOTN Magic Items#Bag of Faerie Seeds]].

## Forgetfulness
- **SOTN:** Arcane 5 · Range 15’ · Duration special (p. 156).
- Up to **3d6 HD** of living creatures of up to **4 HD** each, fewest HD first. They lose memory of the **30 rounds (5 minutes)** before the casting and form none of the **30 rounds** after; meanwhile they are passive and distracted (defend themselves if attacked, otherwise do not interfere), and afterwards feel they have woken from a daydream. Memories return if dispelled or on a later save vs **Spells**: after a day at INT 13+, a week at INT 9-12, a month at INT 8 or less. SOTN gives **no initial save** (as printed).
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Forgetfulness]] (Arcane 5, Divine 5): range **30’**; **2d4 humanoids**, under 2 HD automatically affected, 2 HD+ save vs Spells; loses **1 round** before and forms none of **1 turn** after; [[Mesmerized (Condition)|mesmerized]] meanwhile; perpetual, memories return when it ends.

## Guise Self
- **SOTN:** Arcane 4, Divine 5 (Cultist of Nasga) · Range self · Duration 1 day (p. 156).
- The caster takes the shape of any other humanoid, controlling height, weight, sex, hair, skin within that kind's normal range; equipment stays worn where possible and otherwise melds in. With a successful [[Disguise]] throw he can look like a specific individual.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Guise Self]] (Arcane 4, Divine 5): the same; the caster must be man-sized or smaller.

## Guise Other
- **SOTN:** Arcane 5 · Range touch · Duration 1 day (p. 156).
- As guise self, on a **willing** target.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Guise Other]] (Arcane 5, Divine 6): the same; recipient man-sized or smaller; the **caster** makes the Disguise throw.

## Illusory Interior
- **SOTN:** Arcane 2 · Range 10’ per level · Duration special (p. 156).
- A purely visual illusion of interior décor and furnishings (no outdoors, no living creatures), all within range and inside one **10’ cube** (more castings for more area). Anyone who interacts with it saves vs **Spells** to disbelieve. Lasts until dispelled. Uses: an illusory wall over a door, a carpet over a pit, plain furniture made splendid, coins disguised as hay.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Illusory Interior]] (Arcane 2): range **20’ per level**; perpetual.

## Incite Madness
- **SOTN:** Arcane 1 · Range 60’ · Duration permanent (pp. 156-157).
- A living creature saves vs **Spells** or goes insane, rolling **2d6** on its initiative each round:

  | 2d6 | Behaviour |
  | --- | --- |
  | 2-5 | Attacks the caster or his allies in melee or with missiles (or closes if it cannot) |
  | 6-8 | Does nothing but babble |
  | 9-12 | Attacks its own party, if any |
  - No special advantage to attack it; attacked, it retaliates on its next initiative if still mad; restrained, imprisoned, alone or unable to act, it babbles. Ended only by dispel, [[Cure Disease]] or [[Remove Curse]].
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Incite Madness]] (Arcane 3): range **90’**; **humanoid** target; [[Mad (Condition)|mad]]; perpetual, ended by dispel or remove curse.

## Inflict Lycanthropy
- **SOTN:** Arcane 5 · Range 60’ · Duration permanent (p. 157).
- A living creature saves vs **Spells** or is infected and becomes a lycanthrope in **2d6 days** (demi-humans and others who cannot become lycanthropes die of it). Before then [[Cure Disease]] cures it; afterwards only a miracle. The caster chooses **wererat, wereboar, werewolf, weretiger or werebear**; the victim gains that kind's physical and mental characteristics and special abilities but keeps its own **HD and hit points**.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** none (lycanthropy as a disease: [[Cure Disease]], [[Delay Disease]]).
- **JJ build (Judge's call):** no JJ effect infects with lycanthropy; nearest is a reversed healing spell, "cause disease" (30), with the disease set to lycanthropy, range 60’, save avoids. Treat it as **esoteric** and keep SOTN's Arcane 5.

## Infuriate Crowd
- **SOTN:** Arcane 3 · Range 60’ · Duration 12 rounds (p. 157).
- Up to **3d6 HD** of living creatures of up to **4 HD** each (under 1 HD count ½; HD bonuses ignored); the caster picks the order in a mixed group; excess HD are lost. Each saves vs **Spells** or is infuriated as by infuriate person.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Infuriate Crowd]] (Arcane 3): range **360’**; every living creature under 5 HD in a **20’ sphere**; duration **1 turn**; also ended by [[Remove Fear]].

## Infuriate Person
- **SOTN:** Arcane 1 · Range 60’ · Duration 12 rounds (p. 157).
- One humanoid (bugbears, dryads, dwarves, elves, gnolls, gnomes, goblins, hobgoblins, kobolds, lizardmen, men, mermen, morlocks, neanderthals, naiads, ogres, pixies, sprites, troglodytes and other man-like creatures no bigger than an ogre with **4+1 HD or fewer**; humans and demi-humans of any level) saves vs **Spells** or is infuriated: each round it attacks any creature it detects (whoever last hurt it, else the nearest), or waits seething; **+2 to attack throws, −2 AC**; cannot talk, retreat or use spells or spell-like items; immune to fear, ignores morale. About to attack its own spouse or child, it gets an extra save at **+5**.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Infuriate Humanoid]] (Arcane 1): range **360’**; under **5 HD**; **1 turn**; [[Infuriated (Condition)|infuriated]]; also ended by remove fear.

## Inspire Awe
- **SOTN:** Arcane 3, Divine 4 (Hierophant) · Range self · Duration 30 rounds · reversible (p. 157).
- Every "evil" creature within **15’** saves vs **Spells** or is frightened: it flees at full speed for the duration, or cowers if it cannot; an engaged one cowers until it can disengage by defensive movement; cowering, **−2 AC** and no attacks, spells, movement or actions. Evil: hostile creatures of another alignment than the caster's, and inherently evil ones (undead, Chaotic summoned creatures).
- **Reverse, inspire horror:** the same against "good" creatures (hostile creatures of another alignment, inherently good ones such as lammasu and Lawful summoned creatures). Cultists of Iskara have it at 4th.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Inspire Awe]] (Divine 4): **3 turns**, **30’**, checked each round; failure makes the creature [[Faltering (Condition)|faltering]] that round (−2 to attacks, proficiency throws and saves against the caster), not frightened; works on incarnations and undead.

## Iron Maiden
- **SOTN:** Arcane 4 · Range 90’ · Duration special (pp. 157-158).
- An invisible crushing vise. The target saves vs **Death**: success, it breaks free taking **1d6** and the spell ends; failure, **1d12** and held helpless (helpless rules as circle of agony). Each time the caster's next initiative comes, if he concentrates, it saves again: success, it breaks out (**1d6**); failure, **1d12** more and still helpless. Ends when dispelled, the caster stops concentrating, or it breaks out.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Iron Maiden]] (Arcane 4): range **120’**; damage extraordinary bludgeoning; otherwise the same.

## Mirage
- **SOTN:** Arcane 5 (SOTN adds "(Grey)", unexplained) · Range 240’ · Duration special (p. 158).
- A fortification or terrain feature, wholly within range, looks, sounds and smells like something else (tower to hill, swamp to lake). Casting takes **one full turn**. Those who actively try may save vs **Spells** to see through it. Lasts until dispelled; used to hide sanctums and elf-lords' fastnesses.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Mirage]] (Arcane 5): range special (the caster must be in or on the feature, within a 480’ sphere); it also **feels** real; perpetual.

## Preservation of Antiquity
- **SOTN:** Divine 5 (Cultist of Dirgion), Arcane 6 · Range 20’ · Duration instantaneous (p. 158). The one wholly new spell.
- Slows corrosion, erosion, patina, rust, weathering and other **inorganic** decay of man-made objects in up to **5,000 cubic feet**: their rate of decay is **divided by the caster level**. An object taken out of the area ages normally from then on, and putting it back does not restore the spell. No protection from wear by use, deliberate or accidental harm, or organic spoilage. Made for grave goods. It does **not** show to [[Discern Magic]] but can be dispelled normally.
- Example: an 11th-level priest of Dirgion casts it on a tomb vault; when the owner wakes 3,000 years later his goods have aged only 3,000 / 11 = **272 years**.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** none.
- **JJ build (Judge's call):** no JJ type builds slowed ageing of objects: **esoteric**; keep SOTN's levels. It explains the good condition of Nethercity grave goods ([[Nethercity Features]]).

## Reflesh of the Bones
Alias: *reflesh the bones* (the name in the spellbooks and area text, SOTN pp. 40, 137).
- **SOTN:** Arcane 6 · Range touch · Duration permanent (p. 158).
- Touching the skeleton of a dead creature regrows its flesh, muscle, organs and tissue at once: a new living creature of the same kind and physical traits. Neither mind nor soul returns; a sapient adult comes back an empty-headed brute (**INT 3, WIS 3, CHA 3**). No effect on the living, undead, constructs or objects.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** none (nearest in theme: [[Restore Life and Limb]], which restores the soul too).
- **JJ build (Judge's call):** transmogrification "transform to the form of a living creature" (35) with "target creature can be dead" (×3), touch (×0.6), perpetual (×3.5) gives about 220 points, far beyond 6th level: **esoteric**; keep SOTN's Arcane 6.

## Shatter Blade
- **SOTN:** Arcane 1, Divine 2 (Cultist of Telith) · Range 30’ · Duration instantaneous (p. 158).
- Destroys one weapon, its blade shattering or melting into smoke. If held, the wielder saves vs **Death**, adding the weapon's magic bonus. The wielder is unharmed. **Two** weapons at 6th level, **three** at 11th; each saves separately.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Shatter Blade]] (Arcane 1, Divine 1): range **45’**; an unheld magic weapon saves as a fighter of its bonus level; an unheld ordinary one shatters automatically.

## Slicing Blow
- **SOTN:** Arcane 1 · Range 30’ · Duration instantaneous (p. 158).
- With a successful attack throw, a necromantic cut of **1d10**, no save. One more blow at **3rd level and every two levels after** (5th, 7th, 9th, 11th, 13th); one target or several; an attack throw for each.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Slicing Blow]] (Arcane 1, Divine 2): range **45’**; extraordinary slashing; otherwise the same.

## Shrieking Skull
- **SOTN:** Divine 4 (Cultist of Dirgion) · Range 30’ · Duration 1 turn (p. 158).
- Animates the skull of one human or humanoid to fly and attack at the caster's will, with a **bite** or a **shriek**. Both cause fear as [[Remove Fear]] reversed (cause fear; save vs **Spells** negates); the shriek affects every living creature within **10’**. When it shrieks, or the spell ends, the skull falls and crumbles. It cannot be rebuked.
- **Shrieking skull (SOTN):** MV 20’ flying, AC 9, HD 4+4, #AT 1, D 1d6 + fear or shriek, Save C5, ML +3, AL C.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Shrieking Skull]] (Arcane 4): range **45’**; the caster gives orders like other animated undead; save vs **Paralysis** or [[Frightened (Condition)|frightened]] for **2 turns**; Spd **60’/180’ fly**, ML **+4**. (SOTN's "MV 20’" is probably the encounter rate of 60’; use 60’.)

## Sling Stone
- **SOTN:** Divine 1 (Cultist of Telith) · Range 180’ · Duration instantaneous (p. 159).
- Magically slings **one object per caster level** at one or several targets; an attack throw each; each hit **1d4**. Objects must be in hand or on the ground within **5’**, of clay, stone or metal, **8 oz** or less. Objects at hand by surroundings:

  | Environment | No. of objects |
  | --- | --- |
  | Room, rubbled | 2d10 |
  | Room, cluttered | 1d10 |
  | Room, sparse | 1d6 |
  | Room, empty | 1d6−3 |
  | Barren / desert / mountains | 1d10 |
  | Hills / jungle / scrub / woods | 1d6 |
  | Clear / grass / swamp | 1d3 |
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Sling Stone]] (Arcane 1, Divine 1): the same (bludgeoning damage).

## Soul Eating
- **SOTN:** Arcane 6 · Range touch · Duration 1 day (p. 159).
- The caster devours a victim's soul (usually bound and helpless; in combat a melee attack throw to touch). The victim saves vs **Death**: success, he dies anyway but his soul escapes; failure, he dies and his soul is eaten (no return to life short of a miracle or wish). The caster gains **1d10 temporary hp + 1 per level of the victim**, and one piece of the victim's knowledge (facts he sought, or a random one by the Judge), as complex as one proficiency rank, a class power, a spell formula or a language. The hp vanish at the end of the duration and the knowledge fades like a half-remembered dream (it can be written down first).
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Soul Eating]] (Arcane 6): the same, and while in effect the caster counts as inherently evil; counterspell, dispel evil or dispel magic strip the gains.

## Strengthen the Unliving
- **SOTN:** Arcane 3, Divine 3 (Cultist of Dirgion) · Range 30’ · Duration 1 turn (p. 159).
- Up to the caster's level in **HD of undead** each gain **1d10 temporary hp + 1 per caster level**, lost first, not restorable by rest or healing, gone at the end. [[Dispel Evil]] or [[Dispel Magic]] strips them unless the undead saves vs **Spells**.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Strengthen the Unliving]] (Arcane 3, Divine 3): range **45’**; no dispel clause.

## Summon Shadows
- **SOTN:** Arcane 4, Divine 5 (Cultist of Dirgion) · Range 10’ · Duration 1 week (p. 159).
- Summons **1d4** [[Shadow|shadows]] from the Outer Darkness, appearing within range. They and any shadows they spawn obey the caster, up to **four** in all (extras depart), but twist every order toward the greatest evil. They vanish when destroyed, dispelled, or the spell ends. **Once per week** at most.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Summon Shadow]] (Arcane 4): **one** shadow; takes one turn to cast; orders as an action; once per week.

## Transform Self
- **SOTN:** Arcane 6 · Range self · Duration 6 turns + 1 turn per level (p. 159).
- The caster becomes another living creature of no more HD than his level; not incorporeal or gaseous, not a construct, elemental or undead, not a specific individual. He gains its physical capabilities and statistics, physical attacks and special, supernatural or spell-like abilities (a dragon form gives claws, bite, tail and breath), keeps his own mind and **his own hit points**; reverts if slain; may end it at will.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Transform Self]] (Arcane 6): at most **one special ability (\*)**; not an enchanted creature's form; caster man-sized or smaller; sterile in the new form.

## Unliving Puppet
- **SOTN:** Arcane 1, Divine 1 (Cultist of Dirgion) · Range 60’ · Duration concentration (p. 160).
- Raises one dead creature in range as an undead skeleton (from a mostly intact skeleton; HD as in life, **1 HD** for humans and demi-humans) or zombie (from a mostly intact corpse; **1 HD more** than in life), under the caster's mental control. HD not limited by caster level, but only creatures of **less than 8 HD**. Lasts until destroyed, dispelled or the caster stops concentrating; then the remains crumble to dust.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** [[Unliving Puppet]] (Arcane 1, Divine 1): range **90’**; creatures of **less than 9 HD**; ends if the puppet leaves range.

## Weave Stone
- **SOTN:** Divine 5 (Cultist of Telith) · Range 60’ · Duration permanent (p. 160).
- Reshapes up to **500 cubic feet** of existing earth and stone (a building, rampart, wall); creates none. Not stone carried or held by creatures or with creatures on it, and not woven into creatures (no pillar under a foe, no pulling a bridge from under him); never thinner than **1"**. Otherwise any shape (boulders, bridges, blocks, walls), free-standing and movable or bonded to surrounding non-living material, always resting on a firm base (no floating). A bridge or ramp spanning more than **20’** must be arched and buttressed, which **halves** the volume. With Craft (stonemason) or [[Engineering]] the result is well worked, otherwise crude but serviceable. As part of a stone construction project, each casting is worth **250gp** of its cost. Permanent, but subject to damage and weathering.
- **ACKS II equivalent:** none. Nearest: [[Weave Water]] (Arcane 4, the same idea for water, 2 turns) and [[Wall of Stone]] (Arcane 5).
- **JJ build (Judge's call):** an elemental (earth) wall spell on the model of weave water; the JJ wall tables have no "reshape existing stone" effect, so treat it as **esoteric** and keep SOTN's Divine 5.
