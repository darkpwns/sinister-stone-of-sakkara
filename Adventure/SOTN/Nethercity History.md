# Nethercity History — background, timeline, gods

Judge only. Hub: [[SOTN Hub]]. The city above: [[Cyfaraun]] (its own "Secret" history paragraph is the same story in
short), [[Old District]], [[Undercity of Cyfaraun]]. Pages are SOTN printed pages.

> [!player] What any Cyfaraun local will tell you (SOTN p. 5)
> Cyfaraun was an elven city, Cyfarawn, until the Empire took it a few centuries ago; the old elven buildings still
> stand in the old districts. Its sewers run through the rubble of an even older elven city, and the storytellers say
> there are catacombs, chthonic temples and criminal dens down there. Most residents say the storytellers, like the
> sewers, are full of it.

## Three cities on top of each other (SOTN pp. 5-6, 10)
1. **Cyfarawn I ("Forgotten Cyfaraun")**, founded by elves of Northern Argollë where the Mirmen meets Lake Laman, late
   41st c. BE. Its explorers found lava caves under the black soil and woke a **[[Khepri Hierarch]]**, who became the
   **Great Teacher**: he taught them sorcerous science and the **Chthonic** faith — the gods sleeping in the underworld will
   wake at the **Awakening**; their sworn faithful will rise in the flesh to everlasting life; Empyrean worshippers will
   suffer forever. The Empyrean custom of cremation gave way to embalming and mummification in underground tombs with
   grave goods. Digging deeper for temples, crypts and catacombs in and around the caves, they made the **Nethercity**.
   Taught by the Teacher, they bred new creatures below — the **Thrassians** (lizardmen) — not seeing that they were
   making their own successors. Their gods levelled the city with an earthquake; the Thrassian slaves killed the
   survivors; the Nethercity was sealed by the quake with its mummies in torpor, awaiting the Awakening.
2. **Cyfarawn II ("Ancient Cyfaraun")**, built ~650 BE by returning Northern Argollëan elves on the rubble (they quarried
   it). Finding the catacombs, the elders forbade exploration and built a **Temple of Demara** (Mityara) over the main
   entrance to seal it (645 BE). Buried by ash when Lake Laman erupted (245 BE); beastmen held the region for centuries.
3. **Cyfarawn III ("Old Cyfaraun")**, built 209 BE on the ash, its walls on the old walls' foundations; a trading hub of
   Meniri dwarves, Argollëan elves and Auran humans. Taken by the Empire (165 IY), renamed **Cyfaraun**, made the capital
   of the Borderlands prefecture. The Auran sewers follow Ancient Cyfaraun's buried avenues (soft tuff, not masonry); the
   Aurans also dug dungeons, crypts, cellars and the underground parts of the Palace, Solar Citadel, Amphitheater, Tower
   of Knowledge and Hall of Mourning, the Merchant Guild's vaults and the Bathhouse cisterns: the **Undercity**
   ([[Undercity of Cyfaraun]]).
- The buried, round **Temple of Demara** became the city's largest **cesspit**, under the public latrines of the agora
  ([[Old District]] #12/#12u). In **329 IY** part of its floor gave way, opening the Nethercity for the first time in a
  millennium and pouring the city's waste into it. The drop in flow to the river went unnoticed (SOTN pp. 8, 10;
  p. 10 prints "329 BE", an error: the timeline and "about fifty years ago" give 329 IY).
- **The Nethercity:** 60’ under Cyfaraun, a warren of lava tubes and caverns (rock that cooled on top and stayed hot and
  wet below), with hanging magma "stalactites" and rippled ceilings; worked as a place of worship, sacrifice, burial and
  sorcerous study for 1,500 years (33rd-17th c. BE) until the earthquake sealed it.

## The Awakening (SOTN pp. 5, 16)
The looming threat the module introduces to the setting: the Chthonic gods' promised return, when their faithful rise.
The Nethercity's mummies wait for it; the Great Teacher (T) set events on course for it before he slept. SOTN says it can
be dropped without harm. Campaign status: kept as background; how near it is, is the Judge's call.

## The two pantheons (SOTN pp. 16, 174-175)
Empyrean faith: burn the body so **Calefa** can carry the soul to judgement by **Türas**; base souls drink the waters of
sorrow and are reborn; heroic souls (the **Exalted**) dwell with **Ammonar** and are venerated. Chthonic faith: the soul
is consumed at death unless kept whole in its body — undeath or oblivion — until the Awakening.
