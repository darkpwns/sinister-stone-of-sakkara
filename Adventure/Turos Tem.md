# Türos Tem (hex 22.20)

Imperial border fort on a sheer 150’ tor between the Krysivor River and the Viaspen Forest, over a 110’ ford. Village of camp followers, shops and traders on the west side of the tor. Legate Ulrand Valerian rules a legature of 29 hexes; personal domain hexes 21.21, 22.20, 22.21, 23.20, 14.09. Domain morale −1 (Demoralized). Garrison only 90 of 600 (legions sent west two years ago). Market note: [[Turos Tem (Market)]]. Pages are printed pages.

## Arrival and the gate (pp. 8-9)
- Main (west) gatehouse 1: open dawn to dusk; anyone approaching peacefully with weapons sheathed is admitted to the Avenue (4). After dark only garrison members; persuading the night guard: reaction roll — **Friendly** opens, **Hostile** sounds the alarm.
- Avenue (4): the duty subaltern (1d6: 1-3 Lynara Miren, 4-6 Peleos Methori) records each visitor's name and purpose; adventurers asking about the legate's call are sent to the Headquarters (18). Mounted visitors stable horses in the empty stable block (10).
- Getting caught stealing (stables, granaries, hospital storerooms, etc.): handed to the legate (ACKS "Getting Caught").

## Who buys loot
- **Forum merchants** (Class V market, toll at the Guild Merchant's table) for trade goods (cloth, glassware, dyes, wine, furs, porcelain).
- **Aeropos Karanos** changes gems, jewellery and coin into gold or silver for the reaction-based fee above; banks and lends.
- Monster parts, eggs (crab spider eggs L1 11, 295gp; fire beetle eggs L1 50, 120gp) are worth their value "to an exotic animal trainer, mage or alchemist": Malyn's workshop is the only such buyer in town (willingness not printed — reaction).
- Arms and armour sell poorly here (−2).

## The legate's reward and evidence (pp. 4, 14, 28)
- Notice in 18a: 2,000gp "to whoever puts a stop to the beastmen raids"; bring evidence to the legate at his office in the villa (19l).
- He demands **evidence of success**. He does not know of the Stone; he may pay on other evidence (e.g. the head of the wyrm Idimmu as "the leader"). When the raids continue (they do until the Stone is destroyed) he insists those paid "finish the job" and **arrests them for desertion** if they do not.
- If he learns of the Stone: demands its destruction; may undertake it himself if the party seems unable; expects the **Fragments** to go to the Empire's clerics.

## Research in town (pp. 15, 21)
- **Archive 18g** (permission via Socolo, Friendly): INT 9+ and Loremastery, Military Strategy, Knowledge or a related Profession. Each hour a hidden magic research throw (INT adjustment). 1st success: patrol reports show raids slowly increasing over the last two years, low before that. 2nd success: two events about 24 months ago — an earthquake, and the redeployment of most troops west.
- **Library 20c** (Malyn's permission): INT 9+ and Loremastery, Collegiate Wizardry or Theology. Each hour a hidden magic research throw. Success: excerpt from *Fallen Empires of the Known World* (Matthius Scale): Suetonius' legend of a Zaharan temple in the Viaspen buried by the clerics of Law under a dome of rock; Scale found only "a natural dome of black granite".

## Adventure motivation (optional, 1d6 per adventurer or for the party; p. 4)
1. Hails from a local village destroyed by beastmen while he was in the fields; sworn vengeance.
2. **1d4+1 family members kidnapped** in the latest raid; promised to rescue or avenge them. (All but one are held in L1 73, guarded by 6 kobolds in L1 72; the last was taken "to The Lady Below" — L2 38 altar. Missing kin may also be among the L2 zombies.)
3. For weeks has dreamt of a bloody red heart beating in a pool of darkness (Lawful: righteous anger; Neutral: dread; Chaotic: lust for power); knows it is linked to the raids.
4. Apprentice or squire of a retired hero, sent to investigate.
5. Scion of a patrician family with borderland holdings; duty to defend clients and lands.
6. Family ruinously in debt; the legate's reward is the only hope.
