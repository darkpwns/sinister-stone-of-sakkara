# Türos Aster (hex 25.19)

Judge note. Fort of the Unbroken Line (built 150 years ago by Tarkaun Audarius XI Ulkyreus) on the north-west bank of the Krysivor, atop a low hill over a broad, shallow **ford**. Village of camp followers (class VI market; JJ: the forts' villages have 75-160 families). Most of the garrison has been sent west.
- **Legate Nicus Balens** wants the bugbears across the ford driven out; no men to spare. Adventurers who clear the **Ruined Imperial Fortress (#30, hex 26.19)** are offered the Imperial rank of patrician (baron) with a claim to the stronghold.
- The ruined fortress is visible from Aster's watchtowers; a bugbear tribe under a crusty war-chief harasses the settlements.
- On the road: Türos Tem 3 hexes south-west, Türos Spen 4 hexes north-east along the river.
