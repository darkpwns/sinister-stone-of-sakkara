# R4 The Gnoll Hall

The monks' old dormitory, 60' × 30', reeking: straw beds, gnawed bones, a fire pit, weapons racked on the pillars.

### Features
- The sub-chieftain's corner behind a hide curtain (east end) with a locked iron-bound chest.

### Monsters
- **The warband** (MM Gnoll): sub-chieftain (4 HD, 20 hp, AC 6, 1d10+2), 2 champions (3 HD, 16 hp, AC 5), 14 warriors (half archers), a shaman (Shaman 2, sub-chieftain stats); drudges in R5. They hunt by night and sleep by day (by day 1d4 awake on 1-3 on 1d6). Morale 0 (no chieftain).

### Treasure
- **H227** (TT G, 1,017gp): in the sub-chieftain's chest (locked): a silver-wrought cup (600gp), a fine-wood casket (200gp), a glass-bead belt (150gp), a copper torque (60gp), a bone fetish (7gp).

### Checks and saves
- The chest's lock (thief's locks).

### Exits
- West: [[Ruined Monastery R3 The Crypt Stair]]. North door: [[Ruined Monastery R5 The Kennels]]. South door (locked from the cult's side): [[Ruined Monastery R7 The Chthonic Shrine]].
