# R2 The Rubble Court

A cloister court heaped with fallen columns; the church on the east side has collapsed into its own crypt. Between the rubble, a dark stair goes down, its steps swept clean.

### Features
- The stair (R3) under the fallen church's apse; old Zaharan carvings of masked figures with serpent staves on the column drums.

### Monsters
- None (gnolls cross it).

### Treasure
None.

### Checks and saves
- Searching the rubble (1 turn): nothing but carvings.

### Exits
- South: [[Ruined Monastery R1 The Broken Gatehouse]]. Down (east): [[Ruined Monastery R3 The Crypt Stair]].
