# R6 The Refectory

A long vaulted hall with black hangings painted with a coiled worm; benches, a bell on a cord in the corner, lamps.

### Features
- The bell (R3's alarm). The cult eats and sleeps here.

### Monsters
- **The cult's flock:** 5 acolytes (Priest 2: chthonic; Cure Light reversed, Command), 9 lay thugs (0-level, clubs and daggers). Awake by night; by day half asleep.

### Treasure
None (their purses: a few silver).

### Checks and saves
- Their acolytes' spells: Judge from the divine list (chaotic: reversed cures).

### Exits
- South (30' passage): [[Ruined Monastery R3 The Crypt Stair]]. East: [[Ruined Monastery R7 The Chthonic Shrine]].
