# R10 The Sealed Vault

A small dry room behind the secret door, sealed with lead and an Imperial eagle stamped in it. Two stone chests; a legion priest's skeleton in a rotted white robe.

### Features
- The lead seal shows the legion's eagle (the company knows it from the fort).

### Monsters
- None.

### Treasure
- **H218** (the standing stones' cache, TT K): 2,000 electrum pieces, a whip (the Tyrant's Whip +1). **Three tesserae of passage** (bronze tokens stamped with the eagle, 'PORTA VADI' and 'CASTRA CYFARAUN', the watchword on the reverse in Classical Auran) in the priest's hand: the Legion Waygate's keys (Adventure/Legion Waygate.md); special treasure, 100gp each as antiquities (Judge's value).

### Checks and saves
- Reading the watchword: Classical Auran (Vestus? the scroll-language rule).

### Exits
- West (secret): [[Ruined Monastery R9 The Ossuary]].
