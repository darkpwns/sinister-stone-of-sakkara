# R7 The Chthonic Shrine

The monastery's old chapel: a round chamber, its floor broken open into a black pit that breathes cold air; before it an altar of fused bones.

### Features
- The pit (80' deep, a fissure into the deep earth): the cult's offerings go down. The altar is unholy (a chaotic shrine: Discern Evil shows it).

### Monsters
- **The hierophant** (Priest 4 of the Worm Below; chaotic; Command, Cause Light Injury, Halt Humanoids, Holy Circle reversed (Unholy Circle)), usually here or in R8.

### Treasure
None in the open.

### Checks and saves
- The pit: a fall is 8d6 (RR falling). Desecrating the altar: Neris' rites (Judge).

### Exits
- West: [[Ruined Monastery R6 The Refectory]]. North door: [[Ruined Monastery R4 The Gnoll Hall]]. East: [[Ruined Monastery R8 The Hierophant's Cell]]. South: [[Ruined Monastery R9 The Ossuary]].
