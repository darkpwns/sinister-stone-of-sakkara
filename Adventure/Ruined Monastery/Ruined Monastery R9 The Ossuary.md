# R9 The Ossuary

Bone-niches from floor to vault, thousands of monks' skulls; eight of the skeletons stand in the niches by the far wall.

### Features
- The far (east) wall's niches hide a narrow stone door (secret door: the legion priests' seal; the cult has not found it).

### Monsters
- **8 skeletons** (MM Skeleton) raised by the hierophant: they attack anyone not of the cult who enters.

### Treasure
None.

### Checks and saves
- The secret door (RR Searching: secret doors).

### Exits
- North: [[Ruined Monastery R7 The Chthonic Shrine]]. Secret east: [[Ruined Monastery R10 The Sealed Vault]].
