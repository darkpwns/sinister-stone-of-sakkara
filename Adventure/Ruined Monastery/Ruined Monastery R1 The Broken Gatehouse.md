# R1 The Broken Gatehouse

A gatehouse of dark Zaharan stone, its upper floor fallen in, faces the goat path up the rocky hill. Arrow slits look down the path; a gnoll smell.

### Features
- Sightlines down the path 300'; the gate itself is a rubble gap 10' wide.

### Monsters
- **Gnoll watch** (MM Gnoll): 1 champion (3 HD, 16 hp, AC 5, 1d10+2) and 3 archers (2 HD, AC 4, long bows). By day they watch the path; one runs to R4 on an alarm (3 rounds).

### Treasure
None.

### Checks and saves
- Approach unseen: sneaking and hiding vs their surprise (engine).

### Exits
- North: [[Ruined Monastery R2 The Rubble Court]].
