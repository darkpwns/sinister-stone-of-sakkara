# R8 The Hierophant's Cell

A monk's cell made a lord's chamber: rugs, a bed, a writing desk, a locked strongbox.

### Features
- The desk: the cult's letters (in Auran: 'the Worm Below' gives power for living offerings; the gnolls bring travellers from the Türos Spen road).

### Monsters
- The hierophant at night.

### Treasure

### Checks and saves
