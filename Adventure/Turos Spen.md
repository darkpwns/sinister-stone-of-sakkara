# Türos Spen (hex 28.16)

Judge note. Fort of the Unbroken Line on a rocky hill with steep talus slopes, guarding the road between the Naungollë and Viaspen forests. From the hill the watch sees the **Bleeding Point**, where the Blood River joins the Krysivor; the confluence is shallow and fordable in droughts, when **Legate Süromus Drakon** doubles the watch and gives interminable speeches about Aedon Lann's great battle ("if you're sent to Spen, pray for rain"). Village of camp followers (class VI market).
- Less than 60 miles from the Dark Wall: expeditions into the Waste often start here.
- Expeditions have seen a Zaharan ruin among rocky hills to the south-east (#31, Ruined Monastery, hex 31.17: beastmen and chthonic cultists below), unplumbed.
- Türos Morn (#23), a fort of ill omen, lies up the Krysivor to the north-east.
- On the road: Türos Aster 4 hexes south-west, Türos Orn 9 hexes north-west between the forests.
