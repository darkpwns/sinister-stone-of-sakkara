# Caeso Lentulus

City Watch subaltern (Old District), found day 324 paralysed and being eaten by grave ghouls in the Undercity near the
abandoned rat shrine (AX3 p. 65, Undercity encounter 19). He had trailed a man of the Argollëan Family into the
sewers. If saved, a friend in the City Watch.
- **Traits:** thirties, close-cropped, a broken front tooth; dogged, humourless, honest; ashamed of being caught alone.
- **Voice:** clipped, by the book: "I'll need your names. For the report. And for my thanks."
