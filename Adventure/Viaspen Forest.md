# The Viaspen Forest

Primeval cedar, yew and oak between the Mirmen and Krysivor rivers; grows into the Viamir Marshes (Lake Laman) to the north. Once elf-managed, now wild. **Thick forest: movement ×½.** Pages are printed pages.

## Procedure
- Türos Tem (13.08) to the forest edge: about 9 miles NW through lightly settled farmland, 1.5-6 hours of long-distance movement.
- The temple lies in **10.07**, 9 miles deeper. Until the party knows where it is:
  - **Each hour searching:** hidden proficiency throw for the party, **17+**, or **13+** with an explorer, elven ranger or a Tracking character → tracks/evidence leading to the temple.
  - **Each hour:** encounter throw 1d6, **6** = encounter: roll 1d6 below.
- Once located: 18 miles (3 hexes, route 13.08 → 12.07 → 11.07 → 10.07), 4.5-18 hours; no searching. Each crossing of **11.07**: 1d6, on 6 a core wilderness encounter (ACKS p. 244-246).
- **Night in the Viaspen:** navigation 7+ or lost (ACKS p. 94).
- The temple: a steep rocky hill under a dome of black granite; a 130’ wide, 30’ high crack in the plateau's side is the entrance (L1 room 1).

## Viaspen encounters (1d6)

### 1. Orc raiders
Six orcs lurking to ambush peasants. **Positive reaction:** they take the party for brigands come to serve the Lady Below and, if the party plays along, give directions to the buried temple.
- Orcs (6) → [[Orc]]: MV 120’, AC 3, HD 1, hp 7, 6, 5, 5, 4, 4, #AT 1, D 1d6, Save F1, ML 0, AL C, XP 10 each.
- Treasure: each 3d6sp and 2d6gp — antique coins of the Zaharan era (an astute character may notice).

### 2. Rabid beast
A dire wolf warped by drinking the corrupted water of the Stone's birthing pool: mangy fur, bone spikes through the skin. Highly aggressive; attacks on sight.
- Dire wolf → [[Dire Wolf]] (set module numbers): MV 150’, AC 3, HD 4+1, hp 18, #AT 1 bite, D 2d4, Save F2, ML 0, AL C, XP 140. No treasure.

### 3. Ambush site
Ten bodies on a forest trail, arrows and grievous wounds: two merchants, eight guards. Nothing of value. **Searching the underbrush** finds tracks leading to the temple. (These were the wagon's owners — see 6.)

### 4. Stirges
A prone figure spasming on a dusty trail: a body being drained by four stirges. The corpse is a brigand scout: leather armour, normal short sword, short bow, 13 arrows.
- Stirges (4) → [[Stirge (Sakkara)]]: MV 30’/180’ fly, AC 2, HD 1, hp 5 each, #AT 1 proboscis, D 1d3 + blood drain, +2 to hit on first attack, Save F2, ML +1, AL N, XP 13 each.

### 5. Dwarven furriers
Twelve dwarves crossing the forest to trade with distant kin. Wary; not aggressive unless threatened or attacked. Carry **10 bundles of deer furs (150gp total, 30 stone)**, gladly sold or traded. Asked about beastmen: they have seen beastmen entering and leaving a cave on a rocky hill nearby, and give directions that lead to the temple in a couple of hours. If the party is in a bad state, a **dwarven vaultguard** might be recruited as a hireling (successful negotiation).
- Dwarves (12) → [[Dwarf]]: MV 60’, AC 5 (chain and shield), HD 1, hp 5 each, #AT 1 weapon, D 1d8, Save D1, ML 0, AL L, XP 10 each.

### 6. The wagon
A battered wagon drawn by four heavy draft horses, dried blood on wheels and frame. Encounter usually starts **2d6×10 yards** away. Six men on light horses surround it, a seventh drives, an eighth — heavily armed and armoured — sits beside the driver: brigands led by **Antonius**, posing as merchants (blaming the damage on an attack they drove off). Small or weak-looking party: they lure it in and attack. Strong, organised party: they keep up the act until it leaves.
- A local, or a character with Naturalism, Navigation, Mapping, Survival or similar, realises the wagon heads away from any settlement or road. They are going to their camp to await the temple beastmen and trade the goods for stolen coin.
- Questioned, Antonius trades his knowledge of the beastmen's lair (a nearby rocky hill, as encounter 5) for his freedom, or under pressure.
- Antonius → [[Antonius (Sakkara)]]: Fighter 2; Str 13, Int 9, Wis 8, Dex 12, Con 11, Cha 8; MV 120’, AC 6 (chain, shield), HD 2, hp 10, #AT 1 sword, D 1d6+2, Save F1, ML +1, AL C, XP 20; Fighting Style (weapon and shield), Intimidation. Carries 50gp.
- Brigands (7) → [[Brigand]]: MV 120’, AC 2 (leather), HD 1, hp 4 each, #AT 1 weapon, D 1d6, Save F1, ML −1, AL C, XP 10 each. Each carries 2d4 ep and 2d4 sp (printed "2d4 ep and sp each").
- Mounts: 6 light horses, 4 heavy draft horses (MM stats).
- Wagon: chest with 650sp; 2 jars of Tirenean red dye (50gp, 5 st each); 2 bags of loose tea from Uttar (75gp, 5 st each); 25 bottles of honeyed Argollëan wine (5gp, 1/5 st each); 1 cord of oak logs (5gp, 8 st); 1 barrel of fish sauce (5gp, 8 st). Printed totals: 450gp value, 42 stone (coins included: goods 385gp + 650sp).
