# Porcius' Farm (hex 26.14)

A swineherd's farmstead a mile east of the Imperial road in 26.14, in a hollow of oaks: a low stone farmhouse, sties,
a barn, a root cellar. Porcius the swineherd fed his wife's flesh to his two sons years ago and all three became devil
boars (MM). The father was killed on the road on day 672 (A154); the elder son on night 735-736 (A155); **the younger
son, Glutto, is bewitched by Vestus** (night 736) and will lead the company here as a friend.

## Running Porcius' Farm
- Dungeon level: 1
- Wandering monsters: none
- **Who is here:** one thrall (MM: 75% 1d3; rolled 1): **Porcia**, the swineherd's niece (16, 0-level, JJ p. 252),
  enslaved by the boars' gaze: she keeps the pigs and the house and will defend her masters' things until freed (freed at
  once when Glutto is destroyed, or by dispel magic). Forty ordinary pigs. No other boars.
- **Treasure:** H216-H217 (MM Devil Boar: TT O and L), in the root cellar (K3) under a trapdoor beneath the straw of the
  barn.
- Plain-text map: the farmhouse (K1) facing the lane; the sties (K2) behind; the barn (K3) with the root cellar under it;
  the bone pit (K4) in the oak hollow behind the barn.

## Contents
- K1 the farmhouse: Porcia, a hearth, three huge beds; a box of the family's old letters (the wife and sons "gone to Türos Tem").
- K2 the sties: forty pigs; a boar's-head shrine of mud.
- K3 the barn and root cellar: the hoard (H216, H217) in sacks, jars and an old Imperial strongbox.
- K4 the bone pit: the bones of a dozen travellers and the farm's own dead (Calefa's rites owed, Neris' strictures).
