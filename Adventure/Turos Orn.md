# Türos Orn (hex 23.10)

Judge note. Great fortress built a millennium ago in the Empyrean War on the shore of **Lake Laman** by the warrior-king Valerian Bellësareus; sacked and rebuilt dozens of times; legend tells of hidden chambers unseen for a thousand years. It guards the crossroads by Lake Laman, is the seat of one of the Borderlands' four palatines, and is the **supply depot** for the adjoining forts; a small, thriving **market town** (class IV, 549 urban families) has grown around it.
- **Palatine Valanna Telpirion**: charismatic former adventurer, steward of a glorious tradition; frequent parades and memorials for Auran victories. Frowns on adventurers plundering the fortress's hidden treasures, but might permit exploration if they deal with her **bandit problem** (actually wererats from the Collapsed Elven Keep, #16, hex 28.10, in the Naungollë).
- Roads: Cyfaraun 7 hexes north-west along the lake shore; Türos Spen 9 hexes south-east; Luseaeum north-east (bandits on that road).
