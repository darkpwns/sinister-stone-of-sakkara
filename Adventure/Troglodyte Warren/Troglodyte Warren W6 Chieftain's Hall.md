# Troglodyte Warren W6 Chieftain's Hall
Up: [[Troglodyte Warren]]

A long cavern, sixty feet by forty, its roof lost in the dark twenty feet up. Bones and husks heap the walls; a dull red glow beats slowly from an alcove at the far end, where a blood-red stone the size of a heart sits on a pillar of stacked skulls. Scaly figures crouch all about it, still as the rock.

### Features
- **The Stone of Sakkara** on a pillar of skulls in the back alcove (it glows faintly red to the eye, beating like a heart; AX1 App. II). Its roots have not taken yet: lifting it off the skulls is one round (no wrenching).
- The heaped coin of the warband in a niche behind the pillar (Treasure).
- Arches: north to W5, east to W7, west back to W3.

### Monsters
- **Chieftain** ([[Troglodyte]] lair chieftain): AC 7, HD 6, 28 hp, natural attacks +3 (+2), a polished stone axe; 320 XP. Morale +2 to all while he lives. Stays by the Stone with the champions; fights to the death for it.
- **Sub-chieftain**: AC 6, HD 4, 20 hp, +2 (+1), stone axe; 80 XP. Leads gang A to an alarm (3 rounds after a shriek from W3).
- **Gang A** (the raiders of L2 38, healed by day 139): champion 16 hp (stone club), warriors 13, 12, 7, 4 hp. They know the company's faces.
- At rest they sleep in heaps around the alcove; one always watches the Stone.

### Treasure
- **H2** (TT G for the warband), heaped in the niche behind the pillar: 22,000cp (22 st), 8,000sp (8 st), 1,000ep (1 st), three ornamental stones (25, 50, 25gp), a trinket of wrought silver (300gp), a trinket of bronze (200gp), and a stoppered clay flask of **Oil of the Secret Fire** (uncommon; it looks like lamp oil with a smell of cloves). `recover --hoard H2 --carrier ID --where "W6"`.

### Traps
- None.

### Checks and saves
- Stench (Death save -2 or queasy) within 5' of any troglodyte.
- **The Stone:** any Chaotic creature that sees it saves vs Spells or protects it with its life (the Black Fangs, if they come: Rulings R2 S15). Damaging it (STR 16+ with a heavy implement, or a magic weapon) calls every abomination within 2,000' (none down here: level 2's are 300' and more above, through rock: Judge decides if they come; they come at full speed); destroying it: the striker saves vs Death or becomes an abomination; everyone within 5' saves vs Death or gains a mutation (AX1 App. II).

- North arch 40': [[Troglodyte Warren W5 Brood Cave]].
- East passage 80': [[Troglodyte Warren W7 The Black Pool]].
