# Troglodyte Warren W4 Locust Grotto
Up: [[Troglodyte Warren]]

A tall chimney of a cave, its walls crusted with pale fungus and cast-off husks the size of a man's forearm. Something chirrs high in the dark overhead.

### Features
- 40' high, 25' across; fungus and husks underfoot; the troglodytes' hunting ground.

### Monsters
- **One [[Cavern Locust]]** (2 hp; AC 5, HD 2*), roosting 30' up. Disturbed (light, noise, a missile): it **shrieks** (an encounter throw every round of the fight; wanderers come in 1d4 rounds), panic-jumps 1d6 × 10' in a random clock direction, then bites or spits. Stocked: JJ Dungeon Stocking "monster" (build roll); its plague is down to one (the troglodytes eat them).

### Treasure
- None.

### Traps
- None.

### Checks and saves
- The locust's spit: as its note (MM Cavern Locust).

### Exits
- Back down 40': [[Troglodyte Warren W3 Guard Cave]].
