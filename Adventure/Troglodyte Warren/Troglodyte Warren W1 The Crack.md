# Troglodyte Warren W1 The Crack
Up: [[Troglodyte Warren]]

Past the far tunnel of L2 31 the cave-in fills the passage to the roof, but a dark gap gapes at its top. Beyond it a fissure, a man's width, slants steeply down into the rock; its walls are scored with claw marks and greased with a musky stink.

### Features
- The gap over the rubble: a 10' crawl on hands and knees (shields and polearms passed through; no throw).
- The fissure: 60', single file, slanting down at about one in four; a column of ten takes 2 turns to pass. Fresh signs: scratches, a dropped barbed bone dart, a smear of red ichor (the Stone's sap from L2 38).
- Searching the rubble finds the troglodytes' trail at once (tracks need no throw: a dozen pass here every week).

### Monsters
- None. A wandering troglodyte gang may be met here: single file, no flanking; the first in line fights alone.

### Treasure
- None.

### Traps
- None.

### Checks and saves
- Caving (troglodytes 11+): none needed for the party at walking pace. Running or fighting in the fissure: Judge's ruling at the time.

### Exits
- Up: the cave-in and L2 31 ([[Level 2 - Catacombs]]).
- Down: [[Troglodyte Warren W2 The Long Slope]].
