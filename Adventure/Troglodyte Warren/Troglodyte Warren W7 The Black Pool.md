# Troglodyte Warren W7 The Black Pool
Up: [[Troglodyte Warren]]

A cave of still black water, forty feet across, its surface unbroken. A ledge of pale rock runs around the left-hand wall a few feet above the water; the far end of the cave narrows to a low crawlway.

### Features
- The pool: 15' deep, icy, clean (the troglodytes' drinking water; the Stone's taint reaches it after a month: from about day 162 water drunk here causes mutations).
- Blind white crabs on the pool's floor (food for the warband; harmless).
- The ledge path: 3' wide, 50' long, to the crawlway.

### Monsters
- None placed (troglodytes come to drink: the wandering table).

### Treasure
- **H3** (TT I, the stocked trap room's treasure): on the pool's bottom under the undercut part of the ledge, the bones of a drowned delver from long ago and an iron-bound coffer, rusted shut: 1,000gp, an aquamarine (500gp), a garnet and a pearl (250gp each), an amber and a piece of jet (100gp each), a moonstone (50gp). Seen only from the water or with a light held low over the ledge's edge (a search of the ledge: the coffer's corner glints below). Raising it: a dive and a rope (Swimming throws; the coffer weighs 2 st with its coin).

### Traps
- **Undercut ledge** (stocked trap, Judge's build): the middle 10' of the ledge is hollowed beneath by the water. It holds the troglodytes one at a time; it gives way under 20 stone or more at once (two men in armour, or one with a heavy load): `explore trap-trigger <who> --trap "undercut ledge"` for each member crossing in marching order (1-2 on 1d6 for a loaded or armoured member; a pole prodding finds the hollow ring of it first: search with --pole). Effect: the walker falls 5' into the icy pool, 15' deep: no falling damage; armour and loads drag him down (Swimming throws as RR Swimming, armoured -; he must shed armour or be pulled out by rope). Found: the ledge can be crossed one at a time hugging the wall.

### Checks and saves
- Searching the ledge (RR p. 266): finds the hollow ring and the glint below.
- Swimming (RR, Adventures: swimming and drowning): for a fallen walker and for anyone diving for the coffer.

### Exits

- The crawlway at the far end: [[Troglodyte Warren W8 The Deeper Way]].
