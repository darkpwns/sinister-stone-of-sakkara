# Troglodyte Warren W5 Brood Cave
Up: [[Troglodyte Warren]]

A low warm cave, thirty feet across, floored with dry sand heaped in mounds. A heat breathes up from a crack in the floor. Hunched shapes rise from the mounds as the light comes in, hissing.

### Features
- Fifteen leathery eggs, each the size of a man's head, buried in the warm sand (live eggs: 35gp each untrained value to a monster dealer, MM Troglodyte; a capture for the kingdom's beast-trade if carried warm).
- The warm crack in the floor (a vent; harmless).

### Monsters
- **Fifteen females** ([[Troglodyte]] females fight as hobgoblins, 1+1 HD, MM ecology; only for the eggs): hp 9, 4, 2, 3, 2, 9, 7, 7, 4, 3, 8, 2, 6, 5, 2. They scream (heard in W6, 40') if the eggs are touched; otherwise they hiss and stay on the mounds. They do not leave the cave.

### Treasure
- The eggs (above). Nothing else.

### Traps
- None.

### Checks and saves
- Stench, as everywhere here.

### Exits
- South arch 40': [[Troglodyte Warren W6 Chieftain's Hall]].
