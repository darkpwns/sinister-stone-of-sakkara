# Troglodyte Warren

Judge only. The lair of the troglodyte warband that carried the Stone of Sakkara off the Lady's altar (L2 38, day 132; Rulings R2 S15). It lies under 20.19, below [[Level 2 - Catacombs]], reached only by the crack past the cave-in beyond L2 31 ("the far tunnel leads to an impassable cave-in after 60'", the module's extension point). Built R2 S16 by the JJ chapter 8 procedure (interim); the build's hidden rolls are in the S16 log's Judge block ("Site build: Troglodyte Warren").

## Running the Troglodyte Warren
- Dungeon level: 3
- Wandering monsters: 1d6 every 2 turns, 6+
- Encounter table: warren-wandering.yaml

(Pass the settings explicitly when the party enters: `acks.sh location --site "Troglodyte Warren" --level 3 --wandering "1d6 every 2 turns, 6+" --table "Adventure/Troglodyte Warren/warren-wandering.yaml"`.)

- **Wanderers are the keyed population:** a troglodyte result is that many from W6 (gang A first) or a hunting gang coming back up W8; deduct them from their room. A locust result: from W4's grotto or the deeper way.
- **Light:** none anywhere; troglodytes have Lightless Vision 60'. A party's light is seen from W3 down the length of W2 (250').
- **Stench** (MM Troglodyte): humans and demihumans beginning their initiative or moving within 5' of a troglodyte make a Death save at -2 or are queasy until clear of it. **Chameleon scales:** others -2 on surprise rolls. Troglodytes do +1 damage with weapons; hunters carry 3 barbed bone darts (+3 to hit thrown).
- **Morale:** +1, **+2 more while the chieftain lives** (MM ecology). **Every troglodyte here has seen the Stone:** they protect it with their lives (AX1 App. II: Chaotic creatures who see it save vs Spells or protect it; Judge's ruling R2 S16: the whole warband failed or chose to: no save is rolled for them); they do not flee from a fight over the Stone.
- **Alarm:** a shriek in W3 or W4 is heard in W6 (150'): the sub-chieftain and gang A come in 3 rounds; the chieftain stays by the Stone with the champions. Females fight only for the eggs (W5). The locust's shriek (W4) brings an encounter throw every round of a fight there.
- **Reactions:** they hate surface folk; they know the company killed the Lady (their enemy) and fought them for the Stone. Talk needs a tongue: they speak a debased Elvish (Zaira is an elf); leaders 10% per HD for another subterranean tongue. Their wants: food (locusts, crabs, flesh), the Stone kept, the surface folk gone. They will trade for meat and coin cannot buy the Stone.
- **The Stone here:** 20 hp; taints the warren's air at once (20 ten-foot spheres a day: the whole warren within the week) and the pool after a month (Level 2 rules). No owner yet: the first creature to make a blood sacrifice to it this month gains its favour; whoever makes the most is its owner (AX1 App. II). Month 5 ends day 150: unless it is fed 200 XP of victims it loses 5 hp. **Clock (Judge):** the chieftain means to feed it; each week from day 139 a hidden 1d6, 1-2: a troglodyte gang raids level 2 for a victim (a Black Fang gnoll, a zombie does not count, a lone wanderer); 3-6 nothing yet.
- **Replacement:** a tribe that loses members: AX1's rule (1d6 a week per cleared room, 1-3: 1d6 more) does not apply: no outside source; the eggs hatch in months.

### Map (theatre of the mind)
```
L2 31 far tunnel -- 60' -- cave-in (a gap over the rubble, crawl 10') -- W1 the Crack (60', single file, slanting down)
W1 -- W2 the Long Slope (250', 10' wide, wet, down about 60') -- W3 Guard Cave (30' across)
W3 -- side passage 40' -- W4 Locust Grotto (40' high chimney cave)
W3 -- tunnel 150' -- W6 Chieftain's Hall (60' x 40', ceiling 20')
W6 -- 40' (north arch) -- W5 Brood Cave (warm sand, 30')
W6 -- 80' (east passage) -- W7 The Black Pool (pool 40' across, ledge path)
W7 -- crawlway 100' then a descending tunnel 200' -- W8 The Deeper Way (ends at a flooded sump: impassable without water breathing; the hunters' route to a lower cave system, extension point)
```
