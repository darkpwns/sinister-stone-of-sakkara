# Troglodyte Warren W2 The Long Slope
Up: [[Troglodyte Warren]]

The fissure opens into a long wet gallery, ten feet wide, sloping down into blackness. Water ticks from the roof; the floor is slick limestone worn into a path. The musk thickens with every step, and far ahead the air feels warmer.

### Features
- 250' long, dropping about 60'; at exploration pace 2 turns for the column (wet: no running without a Caving throw).
- From the bottom (W3) a light coming down the slope is seen along its whole length (the guards' Lightless Vision does not need it).

### Monsters
- None placed. A light carried down the slope alerts the guards in W3 (they hide and shriek when the party is 60' away: see W3).

### Treasure
- None.

### Traps
- None.

### Checks and saves
- Running on the wet slope: Caving throw (RR proficiency) or fall prone and slide 1d4 × 10' (Judge's ruling at the time).

### Exits
- Up: [[Troglodyte Warren W1 The Crack]].
- Down: [[Troglodyte Warren W3 Guard Cave]].
