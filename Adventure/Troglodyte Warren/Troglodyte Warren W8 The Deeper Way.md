# Troglodyte Warren W8 The Deeper Way
Up: [[Troglodyte Warren]]

A low crawlway, then a tunnel dropping steeply into the dark, the walls sweating. The air smells of standing water and rot. Far below, something splashes.

### Features
- Crawl 100' (2 turns for a column), then 200' of steep tunnel to a flooded sump: the troglodytes swim through it to their hunting caves beyond (impassable without water breathing or a long breath-hold swim; an extension point, unprepped).

### Monsters
- **Gang B, out hunting** ([[Troglodyte]]): champion 16 hp (stone club, 3 darts) and five warriors 13, 6, 8, 16, 8 hp (3 darts each). When the company first enters the warren, a hidden 1d6 for the hours until they come back up through the sump with prey (crabs, a giant locust); they come by W7 to W6. Stocked: JJ Dungeon Stocking "monster" (build roll), placed here as the warband's hunters (Judge's review, JJ Step 10).

### Treasure
- None.

### Traps
- None.

### Checks and saves
- Crawling: no throw at walking pace; fighting in the crawl: one at a time, prone (Judge's ruling at the time).
- The sump: Swimming throws (RR) and breath; not for the party without magic.

### Exits
- Back up to [[Troglodyte Warren W7 The Black Pool]].
- The sump (beyond: unprepped).
