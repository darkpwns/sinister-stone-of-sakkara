# Troglodyte Warren W3 Guard Cave
Up: [[Troglodyte Warren]]

A round cave thirty feet across, floored with gnawed locust shells and bones. Two passages lead on: a narrow side passage climbing to the right, and a broad tunnel running deeper. The stink is strong; the walls themselves seem to shift where the light falls.

### Features
- Bone litter (moving quietly across it: Sneaking at -2).
- A ledge 8' up on the far wall where the guards crouch, chameleon scales against the rock.

### Monsters
- **Gang C** ([[Troglodyte]]): champion (AC 5, HD 3, 16 hp, +2 natural attacks, stone club; 50 XP) and one warrior (16 hp). Hidden on the ledge (chameleon: others -2 on surprise). Seeing a light on the slope they wait; at 60' one shrieks (the alarm: W6 hears) and both throw darts (3 each, +3 to hit), then fall back down the tunnel toward W6 rather than die (morale +3 while the chieftain lives).
- They see the Stone's enemies in anyone from the surface; a parley in debased Elvish is possible if the party halts and shows meat or empty hands (reaction; Unfriendly to start: they fought the company over the Stone).

### Treasure
- None (darts only).

### Traps
- None.

### Checks and saves
- Stench (Death save -2 or queasy) for anyone who closes to 5'.
- Surprise: the guards impose -2.

### Exits
- Up: [[Troglodyte Warren W2 The Long Slope]].
- Side passage 40' up: [[Troglodyte Warren W4 Locust Grotto]].
- Tunnel 150' on: [[Troglodyte Warren W6 Chieftain's Hall]].
