# Marius Corvo (Order contract, Vestus, S34; Judge only)

Hunter and guide for hire (Explorer 1, Neutral), about twenty. Order contract (fresh, day 522; Oppius brings it day 523):
killed the boatwright's son Sextus Albinus with a knife in a brawl over a wager at a North Gate wine-stall; Sextus'
betrothed, the weaver Hostilia, petitioned. Due within month 18 (by day 540). 500gp (Vestus).

- **Traits:** sun-browned, quick-eyed, a falconer's glove always on his belt; boasts of the beasts he has taken.
  **Voice:** quick, cocky: "He drew first. I just drew faster. Ask the stall-keeper."
- **Judge's calls (rolled S34, hidden):** sleeps in the **hunters' bunkhouse by the North Gate** (a shared room: no
  kill there); **drinks late and walks home alone** through the North Gate lanes.
- **Arms:** leather, short bow, a hunting knife and a spear.
