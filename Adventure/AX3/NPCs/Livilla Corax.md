# Livilla Corax (Iulia's month-16 task, S29; Judge only)

The circle Varinia Sorex named (day 463): courtesans of the Festival District who keep Nasga's rites. Its head is
Livilla Corax (Witch 3, Chthonic tradition, Chaotic; Judge's call, built as Varinia), about thirty, mistress of the
**House of Green Silk**, a bathhouse and pleasure house on the Street of Lamps. Arëtar's task for the Captain (month
16, the task roll 3: "the city"): end the circle; take its head for the court if she will come, end her if she will not.
The Watch (the optio Friendly, day 470) lends 1d4 watchmen (Rulings S14).

- **Traits:** small, quick, green silk and a serpent-headed hairpin; laughs at everything.
  **Voice:** bright and mocking: "The Sun's own Captain, in my house? You should have sent word; we'd have warmed the water."
- **At night:** the rite in the steam room below the baths with 1d4+1 initiates ([[Cultist of Dirgion (AX3)]] as
  stand-ins: Chthonic cultists).
- **Spells (witch 3):** Beguile Humanoid, Choking Grip (1st); Halt Humanoids (2nd).
- **Tactics:** beguiles the strongest intruder, chokes a caster; flees by the bath's drain to the Undercity if broken.
