# Theron Askalaios (Order contract, Vestus, S40; Judge only)

Krysean theurgist (Mage 2, Chaotic), thirty, a hanger-on of the Emporium's Krysean merchants. Order contract (fresh,
day 585, the theurgist kind; Oppius brings it day 586): squeezed a young wine-seller for protection money with his
charms and, when she would not pay, sent her into the lake bewitched; her lover petitioned. Due within month 20
(by day 600). 1,000gp (Vestus).

- **Traits:** oiled ringlets, a rook on his shoulder, a laugh that stops too early. **Voice:** "Everyone pays. Some pay
  with coin."
- **Judge's calls (rolled S40, hidden):** lodges in **a rented tower room with a warded door** (no kill there);
  **evenings at an Emporium wine-house with friends**; **a rook familiar** that keeps watch; **Slumber** ready.
