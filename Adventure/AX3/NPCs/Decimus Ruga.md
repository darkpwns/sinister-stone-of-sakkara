# Decimus Ruga (Order contract, Brennus, S35; Judge only)

Sellsword (Fighter 1, Neutral), about twenty-five, between caravans. Order contract (fresh, day 529; Oppius brings it
day 530): strangled his lover Tertia, a dyer's wife, in a jealous rage when she would not leave her husband; the husband,
the dyer Lucius Arrius, petitioned. Due within month 18 (by day 540). 500gp (Brennus).

- **Traits:** broad, red-bearded, a broken front tooth; laughs too loud; a leather jack and a short sword always on him.
  **Voice:** blustering, then sullen: "She was mine first. Ask anyone."
- **Judge's calls (rolled S35, hidden):** rents **a room alone** over a cooper's shop by the Bridge Gate; **drinks late
  and walks home alone**; **leather and sword**.
