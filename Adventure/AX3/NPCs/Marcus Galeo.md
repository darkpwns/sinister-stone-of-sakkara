# Marcus Galeo (Order contract, Brennus, S42; Judge only)

Optio of the City Watch at the Traveler's Gate (Fighter 3, Neutral), forty, bald and heavy-jawed. Order contract
(fresh, day 599, the Watch kind; Oppius brings it day 600): beat a Larisan wine-carter to death at the gate by night for
refusing his "toll"; the carter's son petitioned. Due within month 21 (by day 630). 1,500gp (Brennus). An officer of the
Watch: if his body is found, Rulings S36.

- **Traits:** shaved head, a toll-ledger in his belt, a laugh at other men's pain. **Voice:** "The gate is open. The gate
  is also hungry."
- **Judge's calls (rolled S42, hidden):** sleeps **in the watchtower barracks** (no kill there); **collects his tolls from
  the gate traders and walks home at dusk**; **sword, mail and whistle, with two of his men**.
