# Gnaeus Sabucius (Order contract, Vestus, S39; Judge only)

Tesserarius of the City Watch (Fighter 1, Neutral), twenty-four, of the Bridge Gate watchtower. Order contract (fresh,
day 578, the Watch kind; Oppius brings it day 579): beat a fishmonger's son to death in a dice-house brawl in the Fish
Market and his tower hushed it; the dead man's sister petitioned. Due within month 20 (by day 600). 500gp (Vestus).
An officer of the Watch: if his body is found, Rulings S36 (two extra 1-in-6 Watch rolls; the officer comes with two men).

- **Traits:** freckled, quick-tempered, a broken front tooth; spends his pay on dice. **Voice:** "Roll, or get off the bench."
- **Judge's calls (rolled S39, hidden):** lodges **in a room over a cookshop, alone**; **dices in a Fish Market tavern most
  evenings and walks home alone**; **sword, mail and a Watch whistle**.
