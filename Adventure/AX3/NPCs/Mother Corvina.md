# Mother Corvina (Order contract, Neris, S44; Judge only)

Wise-woman of the Fish Market, fifty, crow-black hair, keeps a tame crow (Witch 3, Chaotic). Order contract (fresh, day 613; Oppius brings it day 614): cursed her lover and his new woman to death in jealous rage; the lover's wife petitioned. Not of the Family. Due within the
month (by day 643). 1,500gp (Neris).

- **Traits:** rings on every finger, a crow on her shoulder, never meets an eye. **Voice:** "He swore by my hearth. Oaths are heavier than men think."
- **Judge's calls:** habits (lodging, evenings, company) rolled hidden when the company first watches her or him.
