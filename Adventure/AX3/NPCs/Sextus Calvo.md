# Sextus Calvo, "the Knocker" (Order contract D, S24; Judge only)

Housebreaker (Thief 4, Neutral), about thirty-five. Order contract (fresh, day 438): killed the goldsmith Titus Opsius
when the old man woke during a night burglary of his shop (Plaza District); the widow Opsia petitioned. 2,000gp (Lucilla).

- **Traits:** long-armed, stooped, a mallet-scarred thumb; taps on walls with his knuckle while he talks (hence "the
  Knocker"). **Voice:** soft, reasonable, a craftsman's patience: "Every house has a door. Most have two."
- **Judge's calls (rolled S24):** a **member of the Argollëan Family** (a burglar of the old Family's housebreaking crew,
  now Vettius'); sleeps by day in a cellar room in the Warehouse Block (Plaza #38); **most nights out working a house**
  with his crew's lookout; not wary (the goldsmith's death was put down to a fall).
- **Arms:** leather, a short sword, a mallet and pry-bar, a dark lantern.
- **Tactics:** runs over the roofs (Climb) rather than fight; fights only when cornered.
