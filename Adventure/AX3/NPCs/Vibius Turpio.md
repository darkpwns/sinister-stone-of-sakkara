# Vibius Turpio (Order petition on a Family member, S36; Judge only)

Thief 4 of the Argollëan Family, the wharf protection racket's boss. Order petition (day 557, Brennus' assignment): beat a
net-maker, Sextus Varo, to death for refusing to pay; his brother petitioned. A Family member: by Rulings S25 and S28 the
word goes to Vettius; the blood-price 2,000gp (500gp a level) paid by the boss to the kin through the Order; Turpio given a
purse and a passage out of the city (hidden 1d6 3: he goes quietly), no longer a member.
