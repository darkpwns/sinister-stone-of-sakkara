# Marcus Vedius (Order contract F, S24; Judge only)

Wine merchant (Venturer 4, Neutral), about twenty-eight. Order contract (fresh, day 445; reason 1, petitioner 6): poisoned
his elder brother Gaius Vedius to have the family wine-shop and its Nicean contracts; Gaius' partner, a Nicean wine-seller
of the Emporium stalls (the one who knows Lucilla), petitioned. 2,000gp (Neris).

- **Traits:** handsome, soft-handed, rings on every finger; smells of rose oil; never drinks his own wine.
  **Voice:** smooth and eager to please, a salesman's lilt: "For you, the Chian. For you, a price."
- **Judge's calls (rolled S24):** keeps the shop by day and sleeps above it on the Emporium's wine street (Old #7);
  **two hired bravos** (Fighter 1, Judge's call: hp 6 and 5, leather, clubs) sleep in the shop below since Gaius died.
- **Tactics:** hides behind the bravos and screams for the Watch (Watchtower II/III within earshot of the Emporium:
  1-in-6 a patrol in 1d4 rounds).
