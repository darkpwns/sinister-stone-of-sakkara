# Thalassa of the Reeds (Order contract, Vettius, S44; Judge only)

Hedge-witch of the Lake District reed-beds, of no age anyone agrees on; sells luck-charms to the fishermen (Witch 4, Chaotic). Order contract (fresh, day 613; Oppius brings it day 614): poisoned a fishmonger who refused her 'luck-money'; the widow petitioned (extortion). Not of the Family. Due within the
month (by day 643). 2,000gp (Vettius).

- **Traits:** bare feet always wet, a necklace of fish vertebrae, laughs before she answers. **Voice:** "Luck is a debt, darling. The lake always collects."
- **Judge's calls:** habits (lodging, evenings, company) rolled hidden when the company first watches her or him.
