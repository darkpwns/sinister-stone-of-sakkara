# Rufus Calenus (Order contract, Lucilla, S36; Judge only)

Optio of the City Watch (Fighter 3, Neutral), about thirty-five, of the Bridge Gate watchtower. Order contract (fresh,
day 557; Oppius brings it day 558): strangled his lover, the laundress Petronia, in a jealous rage and had the death
written up as a robbery by "river rats"; her sister Fulvia petitioned. Due within month 19 (by day 570). 1,500gp (Lucilla).

- **Traits:** heavy-jawed, close-cropped grey hair, a Watch badge he polishes with his thumb; smells of clove oil.
  **Voice:** slow, official, contemptuous: "The report says river rats. The report is the truth."
- **Judge's calls (rolled S36, hidden):** a **rented room alone near the Bridge Gate**; his evenings at **a new mistress's
  house in the Lake District**, walking back alone before dawn (Judge's call); **leather and sword**.
- **Danger:** a Watch officer's death is looked into hard: if his body is found, two extra 1-in-6 Watch rolls at the next
  weekly roll (Rulings S36).
