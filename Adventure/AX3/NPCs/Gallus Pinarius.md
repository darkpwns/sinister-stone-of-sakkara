# Gallus Pinarius (Order petition on a Family member, S28; Judge only)

A wharf bruiser of the Argollëan Family (Fighter 3, Neutral), thirty, one of the old river crews. Order petition (fresh,
day 473; reason 5, petitioner 1): beat a cooper's son to death in a wine-shop brawl on the Lake wharves in the spring;
the boy's mother went to the Nicean stalls. A Family member, so the word went to Vettius (Rulings S25): blood-price
1,500gp to the mother (the Order's commission for his level, Rulings S28) and Gallus put on a barge for Arganos with a
purse, day 473. No longer a member.

- **Traits:** broad, broken-knuckled, slow to speak; a lead-weighted sap; drinks alone after a fight.
  **Voice:** a mutter: "He swung first. Ask anyone."
