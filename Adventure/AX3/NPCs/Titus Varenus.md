# Titus Varenus (Order contract G, S25; Judge only)

A horse-breeder's son (Fighter 2, Neutral), nineteen. Order contract (fresh, day 452; reason 6, petitioner 1): raced his
chariot through the Plaza market at dusk and crushed a fishwife's small daughter; the Watch called it an accident and his
father paid a fine; the girl's mother petitioned. 1,000gp (Vestus).

- **Traits:** curly-haired, sunburnt, loud; a whip always in his belt; laughs at his own near misses.
  **Voice:** a boy's bluster: "They should have got out of the way!"
- **Where (Judge's call):** his father's stables by the West Gate (Plaza #37).
- **Tactics:** shouts for the grooms; runs for a horse.
