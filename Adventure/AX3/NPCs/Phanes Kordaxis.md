# Phanes Kordaxis (Order contract, Neris, S37; Judge only)

Krysean horse-and-hide trader (Venturer 4, Neutral), about fifty. Order contract (fresh, day 564; Oppius brings it day 565):
broke a horse trader's skull with a stool in a brawl at the Emporium over a stallion's price; the dead man's wife
petitioned. Due within month 19 (by day 570). 2,000gp (Neris).

- **Traits:** heavy rings on every finger, a lisp, a laugh like a bark; never pays the first price. **Voice:** "Everything
  has a price, sister. Even a skull."
- **Judge's calls (rolled S37, hidden):** lodges at **the Krysean merchants' inn by the Emporium** (guarded: no kill
  there); **dines at the Emporium and walks back with his bodyguards**; **three bodyguards** (Jutland sellswords).
