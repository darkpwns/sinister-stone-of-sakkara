# Kleon Pharsalos (Order contract, Brennus, S45; Judge only)

Krysean trader in wine and slaves at the river quay, forty, soft-spoken (Venturer 3, Neutral). Order contract (fresh, day 620; Oppius brings it day 621): killed a carter (the petitioner's brother) over a spilled cargo; the carter's lover petitioned. Not of the Family. Due within the
month (by day 650). 1,500gp (Brennus).

- **Traits:** a silver toothpick, two Jutland guards, never sits with his back to a door. **Voice:** "Everything has a price, friend; tell me yours and I will tell you mine."
- **Judge's calls:** habits rolled hidden when the company first watches.
