# Faustus Cerco (Order contract, Vettius, S35; Judge only)

Apprentice of the Tower (Mage 1, Neutral), about twenty, ambitious and frightened of failing. Order contract (fresh,
day 536; Oppius brings it day 537): set fire by a spell to the room of his rival apprentice Quintia Livo and let it pass
for a lamp left burning; her brother, the scribe Gaius Livo, petitioned. Due within month 18 (by day 540). 500gp (Vettius).

- **Traits:** ink-stained fingers, a scorched sleeve he never mends, eyes that never settle. **Voice:** fast, clipped,
  defensive: "Lamps tip over. Ask any scribe in the Row."
- **Judge's calls (rolled S35, hidden):** a **rented room alone in the Scribes' Row** (second floor, shuttered);
  **studies in by night** (lamp late, then sleeps); his spell of the day **Fan of Flames**.
