# Pinnius the Cat (Order contract I, S29; Judge only)

A lone housebreaker, no guild's man (Thief 1, Chaotic), about nineteen. Order contract (fresh, day 480): broke into the
spice merchant Septimus Crassus' house in the Plaza District and stabbed him when he woke; Crassus' partner, the
importer Aulus Fabius, petitioned. 500gp (Vettius). Not of the Family: the Family's ears know him as a fence's runner.

- **Traits:** thin, quick, soot under the nails; sleeps in a rented loft.
  **Voice:** a nervous whisper that runs on: "I never meant — he sat up, see, he sat up and I —"
- **Judge's calls (rolled S29):** sells his take through a Plaza District fence (not the Family's); sleeps in a loft over
  a cooper's shop.
- **Arms:** leather, a dagger, a sling.
- **Tactics:** flees over the roofs at the first sound.
