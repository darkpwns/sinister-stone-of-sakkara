# Iunia Vex (Order contract C, S23; Judge only)

A thief (Thief 3, Neutral), about twenty-five. Order contract (fresh, S22 day 431): poisoned her lover, the
lamp-seller Aufidius, in jealousy; his mother petitioned. Commission 1,500gp (Brennus).

- **Traits:** small, quick, a sharp fox face, nails bitten to the quick; a silver hairpin she is never without.
  **Voice:** quick and mocking, always a question back: "And who wants to know?"
- **Judge's calls (rolled S23):** a **member of the Argollëan Family** (a pickpocket of the Thieves' Quarter, Old
  District #9; so now one of Vettius' members); rents a room over a pawnshop in the Thieves' Quarter. **Wary:** she
  knows Aufidius' mother has been at the temples and asking for justice, and sleeps with the bar down and a knife
  in her hand; she trusts no stranger's summons.
- **Arms:** leather, two daggers, a vial of the poison she used (hemlock: Death save or 1d6 damage... Judge's call
  if used).
- **Tactics:** runs and hides (Hide, Sneak) rather than fight; over the roofs if cornered.
