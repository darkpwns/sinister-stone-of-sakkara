# Ammia Thrax (Order contract, Vettius, S35; Judge only)

Hedge-witch (Witch 4, Neutral), about forty, sells charms and remedies by the Eastgate tanneries. Order contract (fresh, day
529; Oppius brings it day 530): in a public quarrel at the Fish Market over a debt she hexed the carter Gnaeus Pullo's
mules; they bolted and crushed him under his own cart. His lover, the fishwife Septimia, petitioned. Due within month 18
(by day 540). 2,000gp (Vettius).

- **Traits:** grey-streaked black hair bound with copper wire; a crow on her shoulder in the street; smells of rue and
  smoke. **Voice:** low, unhurried, mocking: "Mules are honest beasts. They go where they're sent."
- **Judge's calls (rolled S35, hidden):** lives **alone in her own house by the Eastgate tanneries**; **walks out alone
  by night to gather** (the river meadows beyond the Eastgate); **a familiar watches** for her (the crow).
