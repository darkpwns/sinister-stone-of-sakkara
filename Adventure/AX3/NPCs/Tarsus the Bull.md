# Tarsus the Bull (Order contract B, S23; Judge only)

Pit fighter for hire (Fighter 5, Neutral), one of Celictor Nuvicus' men (Festival District #22: hired out for
private bouts and the rougher games). Order contract (fresh, S22 day 431): beat a young man to death in an
Emporium tavern brawl; the boy's mother petitioned. Commission 2,500gp (Neris).

- **Traits:** huge, thick-necked, a broken nose flattened sideways; shaven head with a bull tattooed on the scalp;
  laughs too loud. **Voice:** a slow, rumbling bass, pleased with himself: "Who's next? Eh? Who's next?"
- **Where (Judge's call):** sleeps in the Nuvicus school's barracks (the school's walls, trainers and gladiators:
  not a place to kill him). Most nights (1d6, 1-4) he goes out to the Emporium's taverns (Old District #7) with
  1d3 school mates, back after midnight by the Festival District lanes. Day 435: out tonight, with one companion.
- **Arms:** leather, a gladius and a cestus; no shield off the sand.
- **Tactics:** brawls with fists first, draws when cut; flees towards the school when below half hp.
- **5 HD: immune to Slumber.** Halt Humanoids works (Paralysis).
