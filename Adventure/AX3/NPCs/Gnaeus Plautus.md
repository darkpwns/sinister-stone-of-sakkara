# Gnaeus Plautus (Order petition on a Family member, S37; Judge only)

Thief 4 of the Argollëan Family, loan-shark of the Mink's houses. Order petition (day 564, Brennus' assignment): had a
debtor's husband beaten to death in a public brawl. A Family member: by Rulings S25 and S28 Vettius pays the 2,000gp
blood-price and Plautus goes with a purse (hidden 1d6 5: **he goes, but on his way out tells Nuvicus' men that the
Family's boss pays the Order's blood-prices**: Judge's watch).
