# Lucan Ferrox (Order contract A, S23; Judge only)

Retired legion veteran (Fighter 4, Neutral), about fifty, a pensioned decurion of the Imperial legions. Order
contract (AX3 p. 175, fresh, S22 day 431): strangled his mistress Calpurnia in a jealous rage; her husband, a
ropemaker of the Old District, petitioned. Commission 2,000gp (Vettius).

- **Traits:** broad, bald, a legionary's cropped grey beard; a limp from an old spear wound; drinks steadily and
  never shows it. **Voice:** a drill-sergeant's rasp, short orders: "Feet. Shield. Again."
- **Where (Judge's call, rolled S23):** a narrow two-storey house in the lane behind the Mercenary Guildhouse
  (Old District #10), where he drills hired swords for coin by day. Evenings at a veterans' wineshop by the
  Guildhouse until about the second hour of night; home alone (rolled 1: no servant, no friends at night).
- **Arms:** chain, shield, short sword and spear; sleeps with the sword by the bed.
- **Tactics:** a trained soldier: fights in a doorway, shouts for the Watch if outnumbered (Watchtower II/III within
  earshot of the Guildhouse lane: Judge's call 1-in-6 a patrol comes in 1d4 rounds if he shouts).
