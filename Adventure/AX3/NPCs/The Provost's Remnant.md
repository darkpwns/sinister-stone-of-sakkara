# The Provost's remnant (Judge only)

Built day 345 (S18) when Patriarch Arëtar set the Captain of the Underwatch the task of finding any of Mentenus's
"rat-kind" left in the city (Judge's roll 1d4-1 = 3). Mentenus infected his associates (Basement 2 journals, AX3
p. 157); his two packs (Abimbola's at the Shrine, Terkinos's at the Rat Warren) are dead. Three more were never in a
pack: his own household at the Tower. Leaderless since day 337, frightened, hungry for news. Next full moon: day 356
(Judge's call, 28 days after day 328): all three must change, and will go down by the W6 shaft to the warren's altar.

Statline for each: [[Wererat]] (HD 3*, AC 2 / 0 in rat forms, bite 8+ 1d4 extraordinary + lycanthropy, or weapon;
mundane B/P/S cannot harm them in rat or ratman form; silver and magic can; morale 0; XP 65; revert to human form
at 0 hp). Human-form: living humanoids (Slumber works: under 5 HD; Halt Humanoids works).

## What they do (by day 345)
- They know their master is gone and the warren is dead (Sertor went down the W6 shaft on day 339 and found the
  bodies). Flavia means to take the Provost's papers from Basement 2 before the Regent reads them: too late (the
  Regent opened it day 344). Now she plans to flee the city after the full moon with what she can carry.
- **The blind-alley grate** (Rat Warren W6 top, Tower District): Sertor or Flavia goes down on **1-2 on 1d6 a night**
  (hidden), to fetch the pack's coin cache they believe is still in the warren (it is not: the company took it).
- **The Regent's quiet hunt** (Rulings watch): Aurëus has the journals; decrypting takes him 1d4 days from day 344
  (Discern Gist). Then he confronts Opiter (1-3 on 1d6 he expels him quietly, 4-6 he kills him in the basement) and
  learns Flavia's name. A race the company may win or lose.
- Asked about at the Tower (Vestus, a member): Opiter's fear shows (Judge: a thaumaturge Friendly to Vestus says
  "Vibius hasn't slept since the Provost went; he jumps at shadows"). Asked of the Watch (Caeso, Friendly): an
  expelled adjunct, Flavia Rusca, lodges over the bookbinder's on Quill Street; the Watch had a complaint of rats.
