# Tullus Varga (Order contract H, S29; Judge only)

Caravan guard between hires (Fighter 3, Neutral), about twenty-five. Order contract (fresh, day 480): broke the neck of
young Decimus Arrian, a scribe's son, in a brawl over a dice game in a Festival District tavern; Decimus' betrothed,
the seamstress Cornelia, petitioned. 1,500gp (Lucilla).

- **Traits:** broad, sunburnt, a ring in one ear; sleeps till noon, gambles till dawn.
  **Voice:** easy and loud, apologises for nothing: "He swung first. Ask anyone. Well — ask anyone sober."
- **Judge's calls (rolled S29):** lodges above **The Bronze Bell** (a Festival District dicing tavern); walks home alone
  after the dice, drunk, most nights.
- **Arms:** leather, a sword, a dagger.
- **Tactics:** fights clumsily when drunk (-2, Judge's call while drunk); runs for the tavern if hurt.
