# Galla Ursina (Order contract E, S24; Judge only)

Mercenary swordswoman (Fighter 4, Neutral), about thirty, a Jutland mother and an Auran father. Order contract (fresh,
day 438): stabbed the washerwoman Lenia in a dockside wine-shop brawl over a spilled cup; her husband Orbius, a
boatman, petitioned. 2,000gp (Neris).

- **Traits:** tall, red-braided, a broken front tooth; carries her sword everywhere, even to the latrine.
  **Voice:** loud, mocking, quick to laugh and quicker to shove: "Spill my wine again and I'll spill you."
- **Judge's calls (rolled S24):** bunks at the **Mercenary Guildhouse** (Old #10) while she waits for a contract;
  drinks late in the Old District wine-shops and walks back **alone**.
- **Arms:** chain, a sword, a dagger; no shield off duty.
- **Tactics:** fights hard; shouts for the Guildhouse's sell-swords if near it (they come in 1d4 rounds on 1-2).
