# Cnaeus Lento (Order contract, Brennus, S32-S33; Judge only)

Sellsword between hires (Fighter 3, Neutral), about thirty. Order contract (fresh, day 508; Oppius brought it day 509):
stabbed his partner, the caravan guard Aulus Pelto, in an alley behind the Emporium over the split of a guard purse;
Pelto's business associate, the horse-coper Gnaea Vibia, petitioned. Due by day 540. 1,500gp (Brennus).

- **Traits:** lean, grey at the temples at thirty, a notched left ear; counts coins twice; never sits with his back to a door.
  **Voice:** dry, slow, haggling: "Half was half when we started. He changed the sums, not me."
- **Judge's calls (rolled S33, hidden):** lodges at the **Jutland mercenaries' hostel by the Bridge Gate** (a shared
  dormitory: no kill there); drinks late at a Bridge District wine-shop (**The Split Amphora**, Judge's call) and walks
  home alone along the river stair; goes armed in the street with **sword, dagger and mail under his cloak**.
- **Tactics:** fights carefully (he is sober enough by habit: no drunk penalty, Judge's call); runs for the hostel and
  shouts for the Jutlanders if outmatched.
