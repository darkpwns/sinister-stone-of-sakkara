# Order of the Eclipse — contracts assigned in S18 (Judge only)

Weekly assignment rolls (AX3 p. 175; 1d4 per deathbringer PC in Cyfaraun, a 4): day 347 none; **day 354: Vestus (4)
and Brennus (4)**. Contracts 1d6: Vestus 6, Brennus 6 (a repeat: a fresh contract from the retribution tables:
reason 4, petitioner 1; target level 1d4+1 = 3; class 6). Commission 500gp per level of the target (AX3 p. 175),
paid by the Order through Brother Oppius on proof (Judge's reading: a reward; treasure XP). Delivered by Oppius
on day 355.

## Vestus' contract: Una of the Thelesa villa (AX3 table 6)

- **Where:** sleeps by day in a rented cellar under a dyer's in the Lake District, near the vagrant colony's old

- **Tactics:** alone; flees by the cellar's drain into the Undercity if pressed; carries an unholy symbol of Dirgion
