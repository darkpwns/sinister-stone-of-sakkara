# Varinia Sorex (Iulia's sanctioned mission, S25; Judge only)

A priestess of Nasga, the Serpent (Witch 5, Chthonic tradition, Chaotic; Judge's call, as Una's build), about forty,
named by the Patriarch's Inquisitor (Iulia's month-15 task, day 450): she keeps a perfumer's shop on the Street of
Lamps (Festival District) and a cellar shrine below it where she leads Nasga's rites of pain and pleasure; two young
men of the Festival District have died in them this spring. Sanctioned for the Captain: take or slay, 4 knights.

- **Traits:** tall, painted, a serpent bracelet coiled to the elbow; scent of myrrh and something rotten beneath.
  **Voice:** low and amused, every sentence a caress: "You came all this way to be afraid of me?"
- **At night:** in the cellar shrine with 1d4+1 acolytes ([[Cultist of Dirgion (AX3)]] as stand-ins: Chthonic cultists).
- **Spells (witch 5):** Beguile Humanoid, Choking Grip, Sanctuary (1st); Bewitch Humanoid, Halt Humanoids (2nd), one 3rd.
- **Tactics:** charms the strongest intruder, chokes a caster, flees by the drain to the Undercity if broken.
