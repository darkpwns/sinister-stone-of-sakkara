# Organizations of Cyfaraun

Judge only. Every faction, guild, gang, temple and school of [[Cyfaraun]], from AX3's Organizations chapter (pp. 39-43) with what the district chapters add. Hub: [[AX3 Hub]]. Statlines, full POI detail and special locations are in the district notes ([[Old District]], [[Festival District]], [[Bridge District]], [[Plaza District]], [[Tower District]], [[Temple District]], [[Lake District]], [[Palace District]]). Classes are given as ACKS II classes (an ACKS I cleric is a Crusader).

**POI numbers.** The overview chapter (pp. 36-43) numbers several places one or two lower than the district chapters do. This note uses the district chapters' numbers and gives the overview's in brackets where they differ: Prefectural Palace #80 [#78], Tower of Knowledge #50 [#49 in the district list], Temple of the Hearth Fire #58 [#5], Hospital #59, Almshouse #60, Orphanage #61, House of Mourning #62, Temple of Justice #63 [called "Tribunal of Judgment"], Hall of Travelers #64, Temple of the Blade and Veil #65, Temple Barracks #67 [#65], Shrine of the Ancestors #68, Vagrant Colony #75u [#73u].

## Imperial organizations (AX3 p. 39)

### Office of the Prefect
- **Who:** Prefect **Justirius Tavicus Basilio** (Fighter 11; AX3 p. 189), in office four years, able and honest until recently. For months he has walked the Palace at night whispering to unseen voices: he is going mad from the side effects of having been raised from the dead (p. 45, rumour note 3). His wife **Lady Syrena Austiria Basilio** and the Court Magist **Nicodos Essir** (Mage 7) hold near-regent powers in his name; Syrena works to keep up the pretence that he is well (p. 189).
- **Base:** Prefectural Palace #80 and its dungeon #78u (4d10 prisoners awaiting trial), [[Palace District]].
- **Aims:** keep the Borderlands loyal; Syrena keeps the Prefect in office; Legate Jannion suspects Essir of bewitching him.
- **Adventurers:** if a party tells the Prefect of the curative pool at the Shrine of the White Lady (Borderlands Gazetteer #21), his gratitude is great: titles, lands and offices by the Judge's discretion and the PCs' levels (p. 189).
- **Hooks:** cure the Prefect; find out whether Essir is to blame (he is not, per p. 45: the cause is his return from death); protect the Prefect from the Drakomir coup.

### Municipal Council
- **Who:** eight councillors, each nominally for one district, drawn by law from the city's 60 wealthiest patricians; it serves their oligarchic interests. Long near-unanimous for Basilio, now less agreeable.
- **Drakomir faction:** Councillor **Arjenus Ionicus Drakomir** (Fighter 6; Drakomir Villa #72, [[Lake District]]) leads councillors demanding the Prefect's replacement, using his conduct as pretext; the real aim is Drakomir control of the Borderlands. His uncle, the **Exarch of Southern Argollë**, means to have himself acclaimed Tarkaun as soon as Tarkaun Valros Valuin's death in Somirea is confirmed. His elder son commands the City Watch; he has sounded out Legate Jannion.
- **Loyalist faction:** Councillor **Valens Icus Ambador** (Fighter 6; Ambador Villa #73), head of the Council and elder brother of the Tower's Regent; supports the Prefect, suspects the Drakomir but has no proof.
- **Hooks:** proof of the Drakomir plot is worth a great deal to Ambador; the Drakomir may hire adventurers to discredit the Prefect or to win a marriageable Keeper (see Keepers below).

### Prefectural Guard
- **Strength:** 60 veteran cataphracts, 60 cataphracts, 120 horse archers, 120 composite bowmen, 240 veteran heavy infantry.
- **Who:** **Legate Gundus Jannion** (Fighter 7; p. 188), the Prefect's old comrade, troubled by his madness and suspicious of Essir. Councillor Drakomir has asked him whether the Prefect should step down.
- **Base:** its own barracks #81, [[Palace District]]; guards the Palace (60 quartered inside the Prefectural Walls, 90 veteran heavy infantry and 30 composite bowmen at the Prefectural Gate #79).
- **Duties:** security and sovereignty of the Prefect; vanguard of his legions in war; in peace patrols the roads round Cyfaraun and mans the **18 watchtowers** along them.
- **In the streets:** a patrol of 10 legionaries and a subaltern (street encounter 81-84, p. 61) comes to arrest known criminals; anyone who will not stand aside is shoved aside, and fighting them brings 3d6 City Watch in 1d4 rounds (charges: assault, mayhem and/or murder). See `City/Streets of Cyfaraun.md`.

### City Watch
- **Strength:** 240 officers of the watch (armed as heavy infantry) spread over the gates and towers of the 8 districts, plus an elite reserve at the **Headquarters #5** ([[Old District]]): 4 subalterns, 35 veteran heavy infantry, 40 heavy infantry, 20 composite bowmen, used to disperse crowds, suppress riots and hunt violent fugitives (pp. 39, 69).
- **Who:** **Tribune Narmirio Drakomir** (Fighter 5), elder son of Councillor Drakomir; would back his father's coup. The Palace District subaltern **Avaro Urcebas** knows his boss is ambitious and listens for anything of interest to him (p. 187).
- **Duties:** patrol streets and walls, petty crime, thieves, guarding public buildings (not the Palace), fighting fires.
- **Corruption:** the 5 officers of Watchtower III (#6) are on the Argollëan Family's payroll (p. 69).
- **Hooks:** Watch arrests (see [[Cyfaraun]], laws); a bribed Watch is a Family asset; the tribune's men would be the coup's muscle.

## Criminal organizations (AX3 pp. 39, 43)

### The Argollëan Family
- **Who:** secretly led by **Liber Faunus** (Venturer 10), owner of the Mink, a merchant who claims elven descent and made his fortune stripping elven ruins; he slowly united the gangs that sprang up after the Auran conquest. Lieutenants: **Hesta Faunus** (Bard 7), **Aranth Faunus** (Venturer 6), **Megaravicos Eight-Fingers** (Thief 6); comprador **Bellos** (Venturer 2). As a last resort Faunus can call up 80 mercenaries (Fighter 1) (p. 76).
- **Base:** The Mink #11 and its basement hideout #11u, [[Old District]]; runs the Thieves' Quarter #9 (encounters 5+ on 1d6 by day, 4+ by night; reactions in its alleys −2). Owns 5 medium bawdyhouses, 5 large and 2 medium and 14 small cantinas, 4 large taverns, 2 wharves at the Docks #33, 5 barges (Siadanos run) and a 40-wagon caravan (Arganos run); about 7,000gp profit a month (p. 74).
- **Business:** a genteel syndicate. Rose on a monopoly of illegal trade in unearthed elven relics, now drying up; makes up for it with a protection racket on Old District merchants, blackmailed information and stolen goods; gambling and prostitution as hooks for clients. The Old District's sewer grates are its smuggling routes (removable as an action there; elsewhere an Open Doors throw, p. 58).
- **Services to adventurers** (pp. 56-57, 73): smuggles up to **60 loads a month for 10% of value** (5% chance intercepted; otherwise in a Family warehouse in **2d8+3 days**); buys treasure free of Imperial tax at **65% of value, up to 80% to friends of Liber Faunus**; introductions (monster-hunting contract, p. 71). Offers nothing to **obviously Lawful characters** (paladins, bladedancers, clerics, priestesses).
- **Aims and relations:** sees the Nethercity (in its territory) as the key to beating Sand & Bones; anyone who brings out treasure without fencing through the Family risks a war in the sewers; Faunus has killed troublesome officials, clients who grew a conscience and adventurers who imported elven antiquities without him. Pays Guildmaster Meteros to look away; owns Watchtower III; friends with the Dockworkers' Guild. Losing ground to **Sand & Bones**: blood has been shed, most recently in the sewers near the Temple District catacombs, but no open war yet. Secretly helped Celictor Nuvicus import his lizardmen. The death cult has approached it for murder contracts.
- **Hooks:** fence loot; smuggle cargo; become "friends" (80% rate); be hired against Sand & Bones; the cult's contract offer.

### The Sand & Bones Guild
- **Who:** **Selene Nictorian** (Thief 9), beautiful and ruthless; lover of Maximus. Lieutenants on p. 103: **Jonus Krusaro** (Assassin 7), **Lutarius Sinorix** (Bard 6), **Thana Nyssand** (Venturer 6). About 3,250gp profit a month (p. 103).
- **Base:** Silk Pony Inn #25/25u and warehouses #26, [[Festival District]]; controls the district's bawdyhouses, cantinas and taverns and most businesses round the Amphitheater #20. Its harlots and vagabonds are "indentured" to the guild.
- **Business:** games of chance and trafficking in human flesh. Illegal pit fights in the Silk Pony's cellar once a month (the night before the full moon): 3d6 bouts; to submission 50gp to the winner, to the death 100gp; famous or interesting volunteers up to 5× a henchman's wage for their level, or 10× for a bout to the death (p. 121). Debtors who cannot pay are made to fight; the dead are taken away by Maximus, who eats them (Selene does not know).
- **Adventurers:** earn the staff's trust to fight or bet in the bouts (rumour 2); debts to Selene are dangerous (rumour 4).
- **Relations:** rising rival of the Family (friction, sewer skirmishes); allied with Maximus; approached by the death cult for murder contracts.

### The Brotherhood of Knives
- Founded five years ago as a small assassins' syndicate by **Zene**, an apostate Nicean assassin and former protégé of Destrio of the Order of the Eclipse. Last year **three [[Doppelgänger]]s** infiltrated its hideout, killed the members and took their identities (Aodan, Zene, Uxentio). Nobody knows.
- **Base:** Doppelgänger Den #14u, [[Old District]] Undercity.
- **Now:** a horrific danger to the city. They killed the Nicean wine merchant **Vasan Tikala** and his two assistants and wear their faces, luring buyers to night meetings by a sewer outlet to kill and replace them (rumour 14); a friendly NPC may already be one of them (street encounter 61-64, p. 61). Centurion Cellë's missing pupil **Tavis Drakomir** is tied to the den (p. 168).
- **Relations:** the Order of the Eclipse hunts it. Rumour 15 (still in business) is "mostly true".

### The Death Cult of Dirgion
- **Who:** **Malthusia the Diabolist** (Warlock 4) and a dozen cultists, worshipping Dirgion the Doombringer, chthonic god of death and undeath.
- **Base:** a shrine in the Vagrant Colony #75u, Undercity of the [[Lake District]] (Special Location: Vagrant Colony and Death Cult Hideout).
- **Aims:** has made contacts with the Family and Sand & Bones to take on contract killings, abducting the victims as sacrifices. Already kills outcasts on each new moon, keeps the skulls for its altar and dumps the headless bodies in the sewer trench to the river (rumour 1); its first victim was the mad prophet Tavus Ionicus (rumour 20).
- **Relations:** the Order of the Eclipse has not yet learned of it; inquisitors of the Temple of Justice, after crucifying one cultist, suspect a death cult in the city (street event, p. 169).

## Gladiatorial organizations (AX3 pp. 39, 42)

- **Omnus Pluris** (Bard 5), third-generation lanista, school #21; old rival of Celictor.
- **Celictor Nuvicus** (Bard 5), third-generation lanista, school #22. To beat Maximus he partnered with Liber Faunus to smuggle in **two lizardman warriors, a lizardman champion and a wild giant crocodile** from Arganos. They overpowered their handlers on arrival at night and escaped into the sewers, where they now terrorize the city from the Lizardman Lair #28u and the crocodile den #45u ([[Lizardman]], [[Giant Crocodile]]). He denies it all to avoid scandal and punishment (rumours 6, 16).
- **Maximus Arjentüran**, school #23, "the Strong Silver Sword": a former champion who captures and trains beastmen for the arena; the crowd loves him (Cha 18). Secretly a **[[Devil Boar]]** (AX3 "demon boar") who bewitches his beastmen; Selene's lover; runs the blood sports under the Silk Pony and feasts on the slain. The city wants challengers: killing one of his ogres alone in the arena wins fame, wealth and (for gladiators) freedom (rumour 18).
- **Hooks:** fight in the games; recover or kill Celictor's beasts (he will want silence); expose Maximus.

## Mercantile organizations (AX3 p. 42)

### The Merchant Guild
- **Who:** **Guildmaster Apollonis Meteros** (Venturer 9), corrupt and venal, paid well to ignore the Argollëan Family.
- **Base:** Merchant Guildhouse #8, [[Old District]]; administers the Emporium #7; its warehouse block #38 ([[Plaza District]]) rents depot space to members at 2sp per load per day (about 450 common and 25 precious loads stored; p. 137).
- **Does:** licenses merchants and professionals (a store with staff pays 10% of earnings a year; sole proprietors need no licence but are thought disreputable), runs auctions, brokers trade, precious merchandise, passengers and shipping contracts, banks and lends. Details: [[Doing Business in Cyfaraun]].

### Craft guilds
Many, all docile; their heads are bribed by the merchants they deal with. The **Dockworkers' Guild** and its Family friends do not take kindly to adventurers who load with non-guild labour (p. 56).

### The Mercenary Guild
- **Who:** **Guildmaster Balcadur Brogitarius** (Fighter 5; spelled "Balcaldur" on p. 42), retired centurion placed by House Ambador's patronage, a father figure to his members. He believes the Tarkaun is dead and civil war is coming, and is soliciting for more mercenaries; he may side with the Ambador in a war, or may not (p. 76).
- **Base:** Mercenary Guildhouse #10, [[Old District]]; the only independent guild, a consortium licensed by the Empire for private military service in the Borderlands.
- **Membership:** any able-bodied human or demi-human with STR 9+; dues **12gp** on joining and each year. A mutual aid society: pays the member or his family **100gp** for death, **40gp** for capture (ransom), **40gp** for an arm, **30gp** for a leg, **10gp** for an eye, **5gp** for a finger, toe or ear. Members lodge in the Guildhouse for **1sp a night** and see posted contracts. Members and non-members may post contracts to hire henchmen, mercenaries and military specialists (p. 71).
- **Contracts posted now** (p. 71): the Tower of Knowledge wants expeditions to the Waste (Regent Ambador); monster hunters able to take dangerous prey alive (introduction via Liber Faunus at the Mink; AX3 p. 81); the **Imperial Vanguard** seeks shieldbearers, squires and retainers (word to Brogitarius; see `City/Imperial Vanguard.md`).

## Religious organizations (AX3 pp. 42-43)

### Temple of the Winged Sun (Ammonar)
- Largest religious body: clergy of Ammonar, Lightbringer and Lawgiver, patron of the Empire; holy days, rites of governance, exalting dead Tarkauns. Runs the **Solar Citadel #36** and its Catacombs of the Patriarchs #36u ([[Bridge District]]).
- **Patriarch Arëtar** (Crusader 10) dreams nightly of the Awakening and fears the future. **Prelate Romus** (Crusader 7), his second, is drifting into chthonic apostasy under Mentenus Cavië's influence.
- Rumour 11 (the Night Captain's tomb was desecrated) is false.
- Divine spellcasting services: see [[Doing Business in Cyfaraun]].

### Knights of the Winged Sun
- Warriors and paladins sworn to Ammonar: 180 members, most on duties across the Borderlands; two platoons in the city (60 knights, 15 of them paladins) guard the Citadel and the Temple District, and the iron gates of its Undercity (p. 168).
- **Base:** Temple Barracks #67, [[Temple District]]. **Commander:** Centurion **Cellë** (Paladin 6), Arëtar's loyal henchman, distraught at the disappearance of his pupil Tavis Drakomir.

### Keepers of the Hearth Fire (Mityara)
- Priestesses of Mityara, the Noble Lady: keep the sacred fires and pure waters, regulate public morals, oversee marriages and births; run the **Temple of the Hearth Fire #58**, **Hospital #59**, **Almshouse #60** and **Orphanage #61** ([[Temple District]]). Slightly fewer than Ammonar's clergy, but more visible.
- **Matriarch Aundëmë** (Priestess of Mityara 9), of renowned holiness: by her rank she may marry but has refused. The Keepers hold the **legal power to grant mercy to convicted criminals**, which makes a marriageable priestess a prize for great houses, especially ones planning coups.
- **Offers:** divine spellcasting at the Temple (illuminated pinnacle of good); donations to the Almshouse count toward reserve XP (p. 163); Aundëmë stands in the way of recruiting orphans as henchmen (p. 164).

### Tower of Knowledge (Istreus)
- Scholastic order of philosophy and magic; all members are initiates of Istreus, Master of Secrets. The **Chapterhouse #50** ([[Tower District]]) was founded after the Argollëan War to study the elves' captured artifacts and libraries.
- **Regent Aurëus Ambador** (Mage 10, master of the College of War) sees his duty as gathering intelligence on threats from the beastmen of the Waste and often hires adventurers to go past the frontier: he gives them iron spikes with unique sigils to hammer in at points of interest, scries them with his crystal ball, and pays **100gp to 1,000gp per verified point** (a minor Blood River ford 100gp; an unknown pass up the Dark Wall 500gp; the secret entrance of the fortress of Gorgbad 1,000gp) (pp. 145-146). Younger brother of the Council's head; would be called on in an uprising. Rumour 12 true, 19 false.
- **Provost Mentenus Cavië** (Mage 9), unknown to the Regent, is corrupted by elven lore and necromantic texts: see Cult of the Horned Rat.
- **Services and membership:** spell formulas, identification, apprentices and specialists for hire; members pay 10gp a month and forswear necromancy (pp. 148, 152). See [[Doing Business in Cyfaraun]].

### Cult of the Horned Rat
- Provost **Mentenus Cavië** and his apprentices made themselves **[[Wererat]]s** at the **Shrine of the Horned Rat #13u** ([[Old District]] Undercity), to move easily in the sewers and resist the necromantic diseases they expect, while seeking an entrance to the forgotten ruins they rightly believe lie below (the Nethercity: see SOTN, separate module notes). He also leads Prelate Romus toward apostasy. Rumour 10 is about them.

### Temple of Justice (Türas)
- Religious order of Türas, Marshal of Justice; 38 clerics called **templars** run the **Temple of Justice #63** ([[Temple District]]); **Prelate Ezelus** presides (40% chance present by day). Jurisdiction over apostasy, bribery, conspiracy, corruption, diabolism, heresy, necromancy, regicide, subversion, treason and other crimes against the Empire and its gods; its inquisitors hunt heretics. Procedure: [[Cyfaraun]] (laws).

### Sisterhood of Mourning (Calefa)
- Priestesses of Calefa, goddess of fortune, wealth and funeral rites, called **Grey Sisters** (38): funerals, wills and estates, prayers for the dead, from the **House of Mourning #62**; they also oversee the **Shrine of the Ancestors #68** ([[Temple District]]). Led by **Revered Mother Doarë** (Priestess of Calefa 8). Tavus Ionicus's note begs funeral rites from them (rumour 20).

### Order of the Eclipse (Calefa)
- A covert sect of Calefa whose deathbringers usher souls to the afterlife under ritual contracts: **righteous retribution** (the contractor proves the target caused the wrongful death of kin or a close friend, uncompensated by court, earlier assassination or personal action), **merciful release**, **honorable passage**, and killings of enemies of state abroad at the Emperor's request (pp. 172-173).
- **Base:** Eclipse Hideout #69u, under the [[Temple District]]. **Leader:** **Destrio** (Assassin 8). Cooperates with the Temple of Justice and the Sisterhood but answers to neither. Seeks to destroy the Brotherhood of Knives, founded by his former protégé Zene; has not learned of the death cult. Rumour 13 (it beheads the river bodies) is false.

### Temple of the Blade and the Veil (Ianna)
- Two orders of Ianna (love and war) at **#65** ([[Temple District]]): the Veil's 29 priestesses serve as sacred courtesans and advisers to the great houses (**Mother Aurëlyn**, Priestess of Ianna 7); the Blade's 29 bladedancers are an elite force against chthonic darkness (**Blade-Dancer Valara**, Bladedancer 7) (p. 168). Its courtesans visit the Imperial Charter Inn (rumour 9).

### Hall of Travelers (Naurivus)
- **#64** ([[Temple District]]), under **Rector Süromus** (Crusader of Naurivus 6): hostel 2sp a night, job notices 1d8+5gp, route advice (+4 to avoid getting lost on a planned trip), 1d3 Borderlands rumours a month to friends; sends Waste-bound parties to Regent Ambador (pp. 165, 168).

## Hooks (summary)
- Coup: Drakomir (Council, Watch, the Exarch) against the Prefect (Syrena, Essir, Jannion, Ambador); a Keeper's power of mercy as a marriage prize; Brogitarius undecided.
- Cure the Prefect with the White Lady's pool (Gazetteer #21).
- Fence or smuggle through the Family, or defy it and start a sewer war; the Family-Sand & Bones feud.
- The Silk Pony's pit fights; expose Maximus.
- Hunt Celictor's escaped lizardmen and crocodile (#28u, #45u).
- The doppelgängers of the Brotherhood (#14u): Vasan Tikala, Tavis Drakomir.
- The death cult's new-moon killings and murder contracts (#75u); the Order of the Eclipse as an ally.
- Wererat scholars in the sewers (#13u); Prelate Romus's apostasy.
- Expeditions to the Waste for the Tower (100-1,000gp per point).
- Rumours: `Tables/ax3-rumours.yaml` (procedure in [[Cyfaraun]] and [[AX3 Hub]]).
