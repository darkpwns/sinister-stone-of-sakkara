# Tower District

District of [[Cyfaraun]] (hub [[AX3 Hub]]). Laid out along a south-west/north-east axis: the west bank of the Mirmen bounds it on the north-west, the Old Walls on the south-west, the Imperial Walls on the north-east (where the great River Gate North, #46, closes the river) and the Cesta Tirenium ("Capital Street") on the south-east, beyond which lies the [[Palace District]]. The 75’ **Tower of Knowledge** (#50), the tallest building in the city, dominates the skyline and dwarfs even the Public Baths (#51). Home of alchemists, mages, sages and other scholars. AX3 p. 143 (district map p. 144).

- **Who runs it:** in practice the Tower of Knowledge's Chapterhouse (Regent Aurëus Ambador), whose collegians and apprentices fill the streets; civil order is the City Watch's.
- **Watch:** 20 officers at River Gate North (#46), 10 at Watchtower X (#47), 5 at Watchtower XI (#48), 5 at Watchtower XII (#49). District subaltern **Ethlyn Ornotho**: honest, but a heavy drinker; **10%** chance she is drunk, in which case the guards at #46 can easily be bribed. City Watch statline: [[Organizations of Cyfaraun]].
- **Streets:** scholars' townhouses (most mages live in Tower-owned houses within walking distance of the Chapterhouse), workshops and laboratories, the baths and the great latrine; the undercity below holds the Tower's warded basement (#50u), the baths' boiler rooms (#51u) and the latrine cesspool (#52u), all opening on the sewers. Street encounters: [[Streets of Cyfaraun]]; a "Special!" result uses this district's table below.

> [!player] Arriving in the Tower District
> A white tower, taller than anything else in the city, rises over a quarter of quiet townhouses and workshops; robed scholars in red, blue, black and white cassocks argue philosophy as they walk. Near the river stand the great Public Baths (2cp, open to everyone from sunup to sundown) and a big public latrine. At the Tower's ground floor there is a shop where the mages buy and sell magic items, potions, scrolls and monster parts, and will identify items for a fee. The Tower takes applications from arcane casters and apprentices with a sponsor or citizenship.

## Points of interest

### 46. Pylos Ivor Orë — River Gate North (AX3 p. 143)
Controls the Mirmen from downstream. Two irregular concrete gatehouses, one on each bank, 20’ tall and 60’ square (2,000 SHP, Unit Capacity 3 each); between them a 25’ square architectural pier (1,000 SHP) rising 20’ out of the water. Two 60’ **river chains** (AC 8, 5 SHP each) link the pier to the gatehouses, each on its own windlass: slack and submerged by day, raised at night to close the river. A **heavy ballista** on each gatehouse's top storey.
- **Tolls:** cargo vessels are signalled to moor at the nearby wharves: toll **4d6+2gp per 5,000 st** of merchandise on entry or exit; importers also pay customs duty of **2d10% of market price** ([[Doing Business in Cyfaraun]], AX3 p. 56).
- **Watch:** 20 officers: 4 patrolling the river in gondolas, 3 on duty at each gatehouse, 10 off duty in the ground-storey barracks.
- **Bribery:** when subaltern Ethlyn Ornotho is drunk (10%), the guards here can easily be bribed.

### 47. City Watchtower X (AX3 p. 143)
Rises from the Imperial Walls over the Mirmen: four storeys, 40’ tall, 50’ square, white-stuccoed concrete (3,000 SHP, Unit Capacity 3), joined to the walls at its second storey; **repeating light ballista** on a rotating platform under the terracotta roof. 10 officers: 4 on patrol, 1 on the ballista, 5 off duty in the ground-storey barracks.

### 48. City Watchtower XI (AX3 p. 143)
Two storeys, 20’ tall, 25’ diameter, built into an interior angle of the Imperial Walls (850 SHP, Unit Capacity 0.5), joined at the second storey. 5 officers: 1 manning the tower, 2 on patrol, 2 off duty in the ground-storey barracks.

### 49. City Watchtower XII (AX3 p. 143)
Three storeys, 30’ tall, 40’ diameter, teardrop-shaped (1,875 SHP, Unit Capacity 1.5), joined to the walls at the second storey; **repeating light ballista** on a rotating platform on the top storey. 5 officers: 1 on the ballista, 2 on patrol, 2 off duty.

### 50 / 50U. Saromen Sür — Tower of Knowledge / Tower Basement (AX3 p. 143)
The second-oldest Auran building in the city and, at 75’, the tallest. Five storeys (observatory on the fifth; quarters, libraries, laboratories and halls below) over a basement with a large magical research laboratory flanked by magically warded rooms: the senior mages' private studies, treasure vaults with most of the Chapterhouse's wealth and major magic items, and a restricted library of captured elven research on necromancy and demonology. The ground-floor **storefront** is the city's arcane market (services below). Room by room: see Special locations.

### 51 / 51U. Public Baths / Hypocausts (AX3 pp. 143, 145)
Open to both sexes and every class from sunup to sundown. By custom: lower-class women early morning, patrician women late morning, lower-class men at noon, patrician men early afternoon, soldiers late afternoon, mixed parties early evening.
- **Rooms (4,000 sf):** atrium (attendant takes the **2cp** fee); changing room (lavatory, benches, closets of thick-soled sandals and linens); cold room (cold basins, plunge bath); warm lounge (massage couches, **1d4+4 attendants** with scented oils, bronze strigils, towels — men and women, as the patron prefers); hot room (steam, hot plunge, cold fountain); dry sauna.
- **Patrons:** 1d4+10 in the warm lounge, 1d6+4 in each other room. **+1 to reaction rolls** with bath patrons (collegial mood).
- **Notables (each has 1d3+1 bodyguards/manservants):** late morning, **5% each**: Hesta Faunus, Selene Nictorian, Mara Rëavus, Thana Nyssand, Fatima of the Seven Silks, Matriarch Aundëmë, Blade-Dancer Valara, Mother Aurëlyn, Adara Drakomir, Aria Nuvilyn Ambador, Lady Basilio. Early afternoon, **5% each**: Councilman Valens Icus Ambador, Councilman Arjenus Ionicus Drakomir, Regent Aurëus Ambador, Merchant Guildmaster Apollonis Meteros, Liber Faunus. Late afternoon, **5% each**: Legate Gundus Jannion, Tribune Narmirio Drakomir, Lanista Celictor Nuvicus, Lanista Omnus Pluris, Lanista Maximus Arjentüran, Mercenary Guildmaster Balcadur Brogitarius, Temple Captain Cellë. Early evening: **1% each** of all the above. The Judge may add other notables.
- **51U Hypocausts:** a stair from the atrium leads down to a round chamber joined to two boiler rooms; wood-burning furnaces heat the hot, warm and dry rooms, each stoked by an attendant sunup to sundown. Ash and waste go out into the adjoining sewer tunnels, which drain to the Mirmen ([[Undercity of Cyfaraun]]).
- **Hook:** the easiest place in the city to meet the great and the good informally.

### 52 / 52U. Public Latrine / Cesspool (AX3 pp. 145, 147)
Large concrete facility for scholars in peripatetic discourse and bathers on their way. Two archways on the north-east side; cedar benches with **20 toilets** over the cesspool; a channel in front with sponges on sticks. **2d6 patrons** at any time; **−2 to reaction rolls** (nobody likes being bothered on the toilet).
- **52U Cesspool:** 30’ below; walls and floor coated in muck, mould, filth and refuse; ruined frescoes faintly visible under the sludge. Archways north-east and south-west lead into large sewer tunnels ([[Undercity of Cyfaraun]]).
- **The fresco:** wiping the filth away reveals a white city among giant trees as tall as hills. A following **Loremastery** proficiency throw identifies it as **Aodhan**, capital of Northern Argollë, and recalls that the elves who built Ancient Cyfaraun around 650 BE were immigrants returning from that forest kingdom to reclaim the south.

### The Tower of Knowledge (AX3 pp. 145, 147-148)
A monastic order of philosophy and magic. Every member is an initiate of **Istreus**, the Master of Secrets (god of knowledge, learning and magic), and may perform his holy rites (Collegiate Wizardry includes knowing them). Organised by geography into **Chapters** and by specialty into **Colleges**: Artifice, Divination, Enchantment, Illusion, Summoning, Transmogrification, War. Each College has a Grand Master with Masters under him; each Chapter a **Regent** (chosen from the Masters) with a **Provost** and a **Treasurer**. The Regents form a Council of Regents; the Regent of Aura, the **Archregent**, heads the Council and all the Colleges. ACKS II background: [[Tower of Knowledge]] (JJ pp. 221-223).
- **Cyfaraun's Chapterhouse** ("the" Tower to locals) was founded after the Argollëan War by a Master of War who marched with the conquering legions and stayed to study the captured elven artifacts and libraries. Because of the Wastes, the Archregent usually names a **Master of War** as Regent here. The Regent answers to the Archregent ("Archmage" in AX3) for administration and to the Grand Master of War for that College's interests, and advises the Prefect when a matter is beyond his court magist.
- **Roster (AX3 p. 147):** 123 mages, 17 apprentices, 21 scholars: about one-third College of War, one-sixth Divination, the other half split among the five other Colleges.

### Regent Aurëus Ambador (AX3 pp. 145-146)
Regent of the Chapterhouse and Master of the College of War. Found in his quarters (Third Story 2: 30% by day, 95% asleep there by night), his private study (Basement 1: 25% by day), the library or the baths. Bestiary: [[Aurëus Ambador (AX3)]].
- **Traits:** born to one of the Empire's leading patrician houses; tall, lean, aquiline nose, receding brown hair cropped short; a patriot whose first duty, as he sees it, is to gather intelligence on threats from the Waste beyond the Borderlands. Plays kyrpeges by letter with the Archregent; runs a "role-playing game" for collegians in the lounges (Third Story 4).
- **Voice:** a staff officer's briefing: precise, courteous, always pointing at a map. *"Hammer the spike where the road forks, not where it looks pretty."*
- **Statline:** Mage 10; Str 10, Int 16, Wis 10, Dex 9, Con 13, Cha 16; MV 40’ / 120’; AC 6 (bracers of armor AC 5, ring of protection +1); hp 45; dagger 7+, D 1d4 piercing, or spell; Save M10 (+1 ring); ML +1; AL L; XP 850 (AX3 2,200). Proficiencies: Battle Magic, Military Strategy, Siege Engineering, Leadership, Mapping, Navigation, Mystic Aura. Spells per day 3/3/3/3/2. Repertoire: 1st [[Fan of Flames]], [[Discern Magic]], [[Mage Missile]], [[Arcane Armor]] (shield), [[Wall of Smoke]]; 2nd [[Discern Invisible]], [[Gale of Wind]], knock†, stinking cloud†, [[Magic Lock]]; 3rd [[Dispel Magic]], [[Fireball]], [[Thunderbolt]], [[Indiscernibility]] (ACKS II 4th), [[Deflect Ordinary Missiles]]; 4th [[Illusory Terrain]], [[Sphere of Invulnerability, Lesser]], [[Hidden Host]] (massmorph), [[Scry]], [[Wall of Flame]]; 5th [[Conjure Supreme Elemental]], [[Cloud of Poison]] (ACKS II 4th), [[Deflect Ordinary Weapons]], [[Wall of Stone]]. ## Procedure: joining the Tower of Knowledge (AX3 p. 148)
1. **Eligible:** (a) a citizen of the Auran Empire, or sponsored by a patrician citizen; **and** (b) able to cast arcane spells, brew potions or identify magic items — or a 0-level apprentice with **INT 9+** seeking training.
2. **Apply** at the Grand Foyer (Ground Story 2): fee **10gp**. Wait **1d4 weeks** for an interview with the Provost and the Regent; spellbooks are inspected. Anyone with a **death or necromancy** spell in his repertoire is ineligible.
3. **Admission roll:** an eligible applicant rolls **2d6** + INT bonus + level of the most powerful spell in his repertoire; **+1 each** for Alchemy 3, Loremastery, Magical Engineering; **+1 per 10,000gp** of treasure or magic items gifted to the Tower; **+4** for Collegiate Wizardry (trained at the Tower or a similar academy). **12+**: admitted. Rejected applicants may reapply once their skills improve.
4. **Shortcuts:** 1st level mages with Collegiate Wizardry may begin play as members. Mid- to high-level mages who have done notable service to the Empire may be invited, skipping the process (Judge's discretion). A meaningful contribution to a street dialogue (special encounter 3) can also bring an invitation.
5. **Membership:** oaths to Istreus; a rank by spellcasting ability (arcanist, etc.; mundane scholars: resident scholar); a collegian cassock of the chosen College's colour; a **key** to the Chapterhouse; use of the libraries and laboratories of one's rank. Members of **sorcerer rank (7th level)** or above get a private study in the basement (Basement 5 is free).
6. **Duties:** no study or practice of death magic or necromancy; hand over any black-magic formula or item found; **10gp per month** dues (those who cannot pay work as Tower assistants — usual for 0-level apprentices); leave a copy of any new magic item, spell or automaton formula with the Treasurer; magic items made for sale, and paid spellcasting, only through the storefront with profits split **50/50**; items made for personal use are bequeathed to the Tower at death; attend the weekly sacrifice to Istreus and learn his rites (often neglected).
7. **Outside the Tower:** no one is obliged to join to practise magic, but non-members are seen as hedge wizards or, if powerful, untrustworthy occultists.

**Access by rank:** Second Story library (all members in good standing); Basement (thaumaturge, 5th level, and above; a subaltern guards the stair); Thaumaturge Library (Basement 11: thaumaturge and above); Wizards' Library (Basement 4: Regent, Provost, Treasurer and visiting members of wizard rank, 9th+).

## Procedure: the storefront and services (Ground Story 7, AX3 p. 152)
Staffed by day by 1d4 apprentice mages under a thaumaturge, sage or alchemist; more members can be called. Anyone may trade here.
- **Equipment:** blank spellbooks, parchment, papyrus, ink; commission workshop and laboratory components; availability as a **Class III** market ([[Purchasing Equipment]]).
- **Henchmen:** **1d2 apprentice mages (0 level)** and a **25%** chance of an **arcanist** (1st level) available each month ([[Henchmen]]).
- **Specialists:** an **alchemist**, an **engineer**, and a **65%** chance of a **sage** available each month ([[Specialists]]).
- **Identify an item (all properties):** hire a mage, **2,000gp**; item returned identified after **two weeks**.
- **Identify a potion:** hire an alchemist, **40gp**, one turn.
- **Examine a common magic item:** hire a sage (Magical Engineering), **40gp**, one turn: general nature only, no command words or charges.
- **Arcane spellcasting services** at the standard prices ([[Spellcasting Services]]).
- **Spell formulas** for standard spells up to 4th level (no death/necromancy): 1st **100gp**, 2nd **200gp**, 3rd **400gp**, 4th **1,000gp**.
- **Buy** special components from the Arcane Vault (Basement 12) at **110%** of market value; its magic items, potions and scrolls at **twice base cost**.
- **Sell** magic items, potions or scrolls for their **base cost**, within the market's magic item transactions ([[Magic Items - Overview and Trade]]); special components at **90%** of market value.
- **ACKS II note (Judge's call which to use):** the JJ's own Tower storefront ([[Tower of Knowledge]], JJ pp. 221-223) adds throws and differs in places: potion ID Alchemy **3+**; sage examination Magical Engineering **3+** (Loremastery **18+** for very rare/legendary items); full identification takes **3 weeks** and a Magic Research throw of **8+** (+ spell level in the item); vault items sell at **225%** of base cost; 5th and 6th level formulas 3,000gp / 7,000gp. AX3's figures above are the module's.

## Hooks
- **Spikes in the Waste:** Regent Aurëus pays 100-1,000gp per verified point of interest marked with his sigil spikes (a natural patron for Borderlands exploration).
- **The wererat Provost:** Mentenus's journals (Basement 2) prove his lycanthropy and his search for the Nethercity; delivered to the Prefect, the Regent or the Patriarch, he is arrested. Accusing him without proof gets the accuser hunted by wererats. The bloody Zaharan codex (encounter 11) and the dead blue-cassocked mage (encounter 12) point at illicit magic in the district.
- **The Treasurer's weakness:** the Argollëan Family's hold over Clitus Omnus; Clitus's files list every treasure in the basement.
- **The arena fraud:** Inspector Mara Rëavus fakes her anti-magic duty for Maximus and Selene ([[Festival District]]).
- **Joining the Tower:** libraries (every 1st-4th level standard formula), laboratories, a workshop and crossbreeding lab, a private study at 7th level; dues 10gp a month and a ban on necromancy.
- **The fresco in the cesspool:** Aodhan, and the elven return to the south (#52u).
- **Robbing the Tower:** the basement's vaults and studies (over 30,000gp in coin, 14 special components, 4 chests of magic items, libraries worth hundreds of thousands) behind 8th-10th level wizard locks, a magic mouth alarm, guards and a hundred mages — the Empire's arcane order as an enemy.
