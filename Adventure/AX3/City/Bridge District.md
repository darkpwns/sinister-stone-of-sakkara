# Bridge District

District of [[Cyfaraun]] (hub [[AX3 Hub]]). Named for the three bridges over the Mirmen River: the two Auran concrete viaducts (North Bridge #30, South Bridge #31) and, between them, the elven-era **Myrdin's Bridge** (#32). East of the bridges it meets the [[Old District]] along the Cesta Mirmen Gollë (East Blackwater Street); to the south it ends at the River Gate South (#34). Its great landmark is the domed **Solar Citadel** (#36), Ammonar's cathedral, whose ecclesiastic and lay staff have spilled into the blocks south of the Old Walls (the census redrew the border to include them); the Solar Gate (#35) passes the Old Walls. To the west lies the [[Plaza District]]. AX3 p. 126.

- **Who runs it:** the Solar Citadel's clergy dominate the southern blocks; the Docks belong half to the public, half (two east-bank wharves between South Bridge and Myrdin's Bridge) to **Liber Faunus** of the Argollëan Family, whose 0-level syndicate men are most of the dockworkers. The Dockworker Guild (Bodilkas Gargoris) is on the Family's payroll. Councilor for the district: Drusus Komnaurius Balefanabrio (Drakomir faction; see [[Lake District]]).
- **Watch:** 5 officers at Watchtower VIII (#29), 20 at the River Gate South (#34), 10 at the Solar Gate (#35); district subaltern **Polemon Kastorian**, paid by the Argollëan Family to lower the river chain for smugglers now and then. City Watch statline: [[Organizations of Cyfaraun]]. Watchtower VIII overlooks the Thieves' Quarter across the district line and ignores the Family there (Liber Faunus bribes the Municipal Council to keep it so).
- **Streets:** wharves, bridges and river traffic (barges, river boats, gondolas), temple precincts round the Citadel; the river murders (lizardmen of the Old District's #28u, the giant crocodile of the [[Plaza District]]'s #45u) colour the mood. Street encounters: [[Streets of Cyfaraun]]; a "Special!" result uses this district's table below.

> [!player] Arriving in the Bridge District
> Three bridges cross the Mirmen here: two broad concrete viaducts and, between them, an old white-granite span with bronze railings and two tall piers — Myrdin's Bridge, named for the elf lord who held it alone against the Auran legions. Barges and gondolas crowd the wharves; dockworkers grumble about mates who went to work and never came home. South of the bridges the great white dome of the Solar Citadel rises over everything, and the bells call the faithful at dawn, noon and dusk. The catacombs beneath it are open by day for a tithe of 1gp a head.

## Points of interest

### 29. City Watchtower VIII (AX3 p. 126)
Three-storey, 30’ tall, 40’ square tower of cut white limestone (3,000 SHP, Unit Capacity 3), terracotta roof; a **repeating light ballista** on a rotating platform on the top storey. Once part of the Old Walls, now freestanding. 5 City Watch officers: 1 on the ballista, 2 on patrol, 2 off duty in the ground-floor barracks. It overlooks the Thieves' Quarter to the east (Old District), but by long-bribed custom the officers leave the Argollëan Family alone there.
- **Hook:** a party reporting Family activity here is politely ignored; pressing it makes the Family aware of the party.

### 30. Pontos Orëan — North Bridge (AX3 p. 126)
Auran concrete viaduct, five segmental arches, 125’ span, 25’ wide: a 15’ carriageway with 5’ sidewalks raised 18” on each side behind 2’ railings. Arches 24’ high, so barges and gondolas pass easily.

### 31. Pontos Vothëan — South Bridge (AX3 p. 126)
Twin of the North Bridge: five segmental arches, 125’ span, 25’ wide, arches 24’ high.

### 32. Pontos Myrdin — Myrdin's Bridge (AX3 p. 126)
Argollëan-era beam bridge, 100’ long, 25’ wide, all white granite except 3’6” bronze railings; abutments at both ends and two architectural piers at mid-span that rise like pillars 45’ above the water (20’ above the deck). The Aurans named it for their noble foe, the elven wizard-lord **Myrdin**, who held it single-handed against the Auran army long enough for the city's people to escape. His panoply lies in the [[Catacombs of the Patriarchs]] (room 13).

### 33. The Docks (AX3 p. 126)
Wharves, piers and jetties for up to 60 barges or boats. Usually berthed: 2d10 barges, 2d10 river boats, 2d10 sailing boats. By day 6d10 sailors, rowers and dockworkers at work. The two east-bank wharves between South Bridge and Myrdin's Bridge are **Liber Faunus's**; most of their dockworkers are 0-level syndicate members. The rest are public.
- **Moorage:** 1gp per 10 shp of vessel per day.
- **Hooks:** dockworkers keep vanishing (15 in the last 30 days, see [[Plaza District]] #45u and the Old District's lizardman lair #28u); cargo arriving by river pays toll and duty here (#34).

### 34. Pylos Ivor Vothë — River Gate South (AX3 p. 126)
Two concrete gatehouses, one on each bank, 20’ tall and 60’ square (2,000 SHP, Unit Capacity 3 each), with a 25’ square architectural pier between them rising 20’ out of the water (1,000 SHP). Two 60’ **river chains** (AC 8, 5 SHP each) link the pier to the gatehouses, each worked by its own windlass: slack and submerged by day, raised at night to close the river. A **heavy ballista** on each gatehouse's top storey.
- **Tolls:** cargo vessels are signalled to moor at the Docks (#33): toll **4d6+2gp per 5,000 st** of merchandise on entry or exit; importers also pay customs duty of **2d10% of market price** ([[Doing Business in Cyfaraun]], AX3 p. 56).
- **Watch:** 20 officers: 4 patrolling the river by gondola, 3 on duty at each gatehouse, 10 off duty in the ground-storey barracks.
- **Secret:** subaltern **Polemon Kastorian** takes Argollëan Family money to lower the chain for smugglers now and then (no toll, no duty).

### 35. Pylos Ammon — Solar Gate (AX3 p. 127)
Built after the conquest to replace the elven gate destroyed in the sack: iron portcullis, iron-bound oak doors painted with the winged sun, two 20’ tall, 30’ square gatehouses of white-stuccoed concrete (1,500 SHP, Unit Capacity 2 each). Open sunup to sundown only; at night the Watch opens it only for a reasonable justification **and** a Friendly reaction roll. 10 officers: 3 on patrol, 2 at the gate, 5 off duty in the barracks.

### 36. Ammontürium — Solar Citadel (AX3 p. 127)
The oldest Auran building in Cyfaraun, raised after the capture of the city to honour Ammonar: granite under white marble, crowned by a huge dome with an open oculus. Administered by **Patriarch Arëtar**; his clergy run the Celebration of the Unconquered Dawn (winter solstice) and the Day of the Undying Sun (summer solstice). Prayers daily at dawn, noon and dusk; sacrifice weekly. Audience procedure under Notable residents. Room by room: [[Solar Citadel]].

### 36U. Catacombs of the Patriarchs (AX3 pp. 127-128)
Under the Citadel: catacombs holding the cremated remains of the Borderlands' leading civil and military officials. Most honoured are the Patriarchs' tombs and, nearly as much, the columbarium of the nameless paladin called the **Captain of the Underwatch**, who cleansed Cyfaraun of a wererat infestation after the Grey Plague. Open by day for a 1gp tithe. Room by room: [[Catacombs of the Patriarchs]].

## Notable residents

### Patriarch Arëtar (AX3 p. 128)
Patriarch of the Solar Citadel, found there (leads noon prayers at the south altar; performs the weekly sacrifice at dawn on Ammonadras, "Solar Day").
- **Traits:** tall, handsome, aquiline nose, proud jaw, 35; a patrician who could have lived easily in Aura but was born with the gift of prophecy; earnest and haunted.
- **Voice:** grave and unhurried, a man listening for something behind your words; asks what you saw before he asks what you want.
- **Statline:** Crusader 10 (ACKS I Cleric 10); Str 10, Int 10, Wis 16, Dex 10, Con 9, Cha 16; MV 40’ / 120’ (20’ / 60’ in plate & shield); AC 0 (7 in plate & shield); hp 37; 1 attack, mace +1, +3 vs undead (5+, 3+ vs undead); D 1d6+1 (1d6+3 vs undead) bludgeoning; Save C10 (AX3 +4 to saves: Wis and Divine Blessing); ML +4; AL L; XP 850 (class level as HD). Proficiencies: Command, Diplomacy, Divine Blessing, Leadership, Prophecy, Theology. Spells per day (ACKS II Crusader 10): 3 1st, 3 2nd, 3 3rd, 3 4th, 2 5th (AX3 printed 4/4/3/3/2); repertoire as 10th level crusader. Battle gear: mace +1, +3 vs undead, plate armour, shield. Always: **ring of water walking**, holy symbol (winged sun of Ammonar), holy book *The Laws of the Light*, purple cassock with thick cloth-of-gold fringe, low boots, belt, pouch with a scroll of *protection from evil* ([[Holy Circle]]) and [[Righteous Wrath]] and 100gp for alms.
- **Wants:** to know whether his lifelong nightmare will come true: blood pouring from the winged sun, darkened skies raining fire, the dead rising — the **Awakening of the Chthonic gods**. Ammonar would not answer (the Tablet of Destiny is broken; even the gods cannot see what will be).
- **Knows:** he is perhaps the one man in the Borderlands who will **believe adventurers** bringing evidence that the Awakening is near.
- **Holds:** two sets of regalia: the **spear and shield of the Underwatch** (catacombs room 6) and the **panoply of Myrdin** (room 13). He will never give them away, but may create offices answering to him whose holders bear them while they serve:
  - **Captain of the Underwatch:** a capable, powerful paladin or crusader of Ammonar or Türas who has served Cyfaraun; bears spear and shield to watch over the city's dark places.
  - **Captain of the Bridge:** a Lawful elven spellsword proven honourable, selfless and powerful in Cyfaraun's defence; bears Myrdin's panoply as the fallen hero did.
  - **Requirements:** a Friendly reaction with Arëtar **and** evidence of the Cult of the Horned Rat (Special Location #13u, [[Old District]]), the Death Cult of Dirgion (#75u, [[Death Cult Hideout]]) or the imminence of the Awakening (found in the Nethercity; see SOTN (separate module notes)). Remit: the Borderlands only (he may allow short missions elsewhere of great need). Dishonourable or disreputable conduct: office and badge stripped, by force if needed.

### Prelate Romus (AX3 p. 128)
Second of the Citadel, leads dawn prayers from the east altar. Bestiary: [[Prelate Romus (AX3)]].
- **Traits:** 47, hair receding, eyes dimming, knees and back aching in the rain; feels unappreciated by the Patriarch and threatened by Rector Drusus; afraid of dying.
- **Voice:** soft, aggrieved, full of qualifications; steers every subject back to his own burdens.
- **Statline:** Crusader 7; Str 9, Int 11, Wis 13, Dex 10, Con 9, Cha 13; MV 40’ / 120’ (20’ / 60’ in plate & shield); AC 0 (7 in plate & shield); hp 27; mace 7+, D 1d6; Save C7 (AX3 +1); ML +0; AL N; XP 440. Proficiencies: Apostasy (ACKS I; no ACKS II equivalent: he may cast the chaotic spells *unseen servant* 1st, *necromantic potence* 3rd, *control undead* 4th, *polymorph self* 5th — nearest ACKS II [[Unliving Puppet]], [[Necromantic Potence]], [[Animate Undead]], [[Guise Self]]; Judge's call), Diplomacy, Laying on Hands, Knowledge (occult). Spells per day (ACKS II Crusader 7): 3 1st, 2 2nd, 2 3rd, 1 4th (AX3 printed 2/2/2/1/1, the 5th-level slot for his Apostasy spell; Judge's call whether to keep it). Battle gear: mace, plate, shield. Always: holy symbol, *The Laws of the Light*, 1 week's iron rations, yellow cassock with thick purple fringe, low boots, belt, pouch with 100gp for alms.
- **Secret:** his friend **Mentenus Cavië** (provost of the Tower of Knowledge and secret chthonic cultist; [[Tower District]]) has been feeding him talk of magically longer, healthier lives; Romus now studies Cavië's texts on necromancy and elven alchemy. Not yet a chaos-worshipper, but fear of death is pulling him; Cavië hopes he will become the **Cult of the Horned Rat's first priest**.
- **Wants:** to live longer and be valued. Will try to "personally handle" any investigation of Cavië and keep Arëtar and Drusus out of it.

### Rector Drusus, "the Hammer of Ammonar" (AX3 pp. 128-129)
Third of the Citadel, leads dusk prayers from the west altar. (Not the Brigand King Drusus of AX1.)
- **Traits:** a heroic statue come to life: broad shoulders, strong chin, tanned, battle-scarred; decorated veteran crusader of the Auran Army, badly wounded in Opelenea, saved by powerful clerical magic and sent here to convalesce for a year. Now recovered and guilty at missing the legions' march west against the Skysos.
- **Voice:** clipped parade-ground scorn for able-bodied adventurers who "could have served the Empire and didn't".
- **Statline:** Crusader 6; Str 14, Int 9, Wis 13, Dex 10, Con 13, Cha 16; MV 40’ / 120’ (20’ / 60’ in plate & shield); AC 0 (7 in plate & shield); hp 31; warhammer +1 6+, D 1d6+2 bludgeoning; Save C6 (AX3 +1); ML +2; AL L; XP 320. Proficiencies: Battle Magic, Command, Riding, Unflappable Casting. Spells per day (ACKS II Crusader 6): 2 1st, 2 2nd, 2 3rd (AX3 printed 2/2/1/1); repertoire as AX3 "7th level cleric" — use his own level (Judge's call). Battle gear: warhammer +1, plate, shield. Always: holy symbol, purple cassock with yellow fringe, low boots, belt, pouch with a scroll of *ward against elementals* (ACKS I; nearest ACKS II [[Energy Protection]], Judge's call) and 100gp for alms.
- **Wants:** to get back to the war in the west. So absorbed in it that he resists believing in existential or eldritch threats in the Borderlands.

### Solar Citadel clergy (AX3 p. 129)
53 ecclesiastics and 40 lay servants. Roster by level: 10th 1 (Arëtar), 7th 1 (Romus), 6th 1 (Drusus), 5th 3 (vicars), 4th 5 (curates), 3rd 6 (priests), 2nd 12 (acolytes), 1st 24 (catechists), 0th 40 (lay) = 93.
- **Vicars (3, 5th level):** available in the Citadel sunup to sundown; the gatekeepers to the leadership. Statline in [[Solar Citadel]] (room 3).
- **Curates (5, 4th) and priests (6, 3rd):** often out proselytizing in the city.
- **Acolytes (12, 2nd):** pray at the altars during services, help the three leaders with administration.
- **Catechists (24, 1st):** prayer, sacrifice, theology, arms practice; pairs keep vigil in the catacombs ([[Catechist of Ammonar (AX3)]]).
- **Lay servants (40):** 24 Citadel Guard ([[Knight of the Winged Sun (AX3)]]), 16 custodians, clerks, scribes and traders.
- **Audience:** win a **Friendly reaction with a vicar** at the Citadel; the vicar introduces the party to Romus or Drusus; only if neither can deal with the matter is Arëtar brought in.

### Guildmaster Bodilkas Gargoris (AX3 p. 129)
Master of the Dockworker Guild; at the Docks (#33).
- **Traits:** 34, Krysean, wind-weathered face, bald; gruff and plain-spoken; popular with his crews and respected by the merchants. On the Argollëan Family's payroll: crime is inevitable, and orderly, peaceful criminals like the Family beat the thugs of Sand & Bones.
- **Voice:** short sentences, dockside bluntness; "it's being handled" said like a man who doesn't believe it.
- **Statline:** 0-level human; Str 12, Int 13, Wis 11, Dex 10, Con 13, Cha 13; MV 40’ / 120’; AC 0; HD less than 1 (AX3 HD 1-1), hp 7; crowbar 11+, D 1d4 bludgeoning; Save NM (0-level); ML 0; AL N; XP 5. Proficiencies: Bargaining, Labor (dockworker), Leadership, Profession (guildmaster). Pouch: 1gp, 20sp.
- **Knows / wants:** badly worried by the deaths among his crews (AX3 pp. 41, 123: the river killings); Liber Faunus told him it is being handled. Would welcome — quietly — anyone who actually stops it.

## Special locations
- **#36 Solar Citadel** ([[Solar Citadel]], AX3 pp. 131-134, map p. 215): the domed cathedral of Ammonar, a giant eye in plan; colossus, oculus ring, altars forming a hallowed pinnacle of good, statues of the deified Tarkauns, six god-statues bearing the wing roofs, four great brass doors each guarded by a pair of Knights of the Winged Sun, and the stairs down to the catacombs.
- **#36u Catacombs of the Patriarchs** ([[Catacombs of the Patriarchs]], AX3 pp. 134-135, map p. 215): lit, stuccoed tuff halls of cinerary urns, kept by pairs of chanting catechists and four knights; every valuable is protected by runes of warding (curses and 20-point lightning blasts); a secret treasure vault worth well over 40,000gp; the regalia of the Captain of the Underwatch and of Myrdin. Borders the sewers (room 2's warded door; elevated corridor 8 crosses a sewer).

## Hooks
- **The river killings:** dockworkers vanishing, corpses in the river, a "dragon" sighted: the lizardmen (#28u, Old District) and the giant crocodile ([[Crocodile Nest]], Plaza District). Bodilkas, the dockworkers and the Watch all have theories (rumours #5, #7, #16).
- **Offices of the Patriarch:** Captain of the Underwatch / Captain of the Bridge for heroes who bring Arëtar proof of the Horned Rat cult, the Dirgion death cult or the Awakening.
- **Romus's slide:** watch the Prelate fall to Mentenus Cavië's necromancy; Arëtar would want to know, Romus will obstruct.
- **Smuggling:** Polemon Kastorian lowers the chain for Family smugglers; Liber Faunus owns the east-bank wharves; evidence of either is leverage with the Watch, the Family or the Council.
- **The Citadel's vault:** the [[Catacombs of the Patriarchs]] hold a fortune (room 7) and Myrdin's panoply (room 13), all heavily warded; robbing the Empire's patron god is the gravest of crimes.
