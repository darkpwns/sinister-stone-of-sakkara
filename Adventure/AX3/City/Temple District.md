# Temple District

District of [[Cyfaraun]] (hub [[AX3 Hub]]). The south-east of the city: bounded by the Cesta Vothë (South Street) on the north, the Imperial Walls between the East Gate (#53) and the Watchful Gate (#57) on the south and east, and the Old Walls on the west, where the Lake Gate (#69) opens into the [[Lake District]]. Densely built, it holds five of the city's seven Empyrean orders and most of its ecclesiastic and clerical workers. (Ammonar's clergy sit in the Solar Citadel, [[Bridge District]]; Istreus's scholars in the Tower of Knowledge, [[Tower District]].) AX3 pp. 161, 166.

- **Who runs it:** the temples. Matriarch Aundëmë (Keepers of the Hearth Fire, Mityara) leads the propitiation of all seven gods and runs the city's charities; Prelate Ezelus holds the Temple of Justice (Türas), with jurisdiction over crimes against the Empire and the gods; Revered Mother Doarë leads the Grey Sisters (Calefa); Mother Aurëlyn and Blade-Dancer Valara the two halves of Ianna's temple; Rector Süromus the Order of Travelers (Naurivus); Centurion Cellë the district's Knights of the Winged Sun (Ammonar).
- **Watch:** 15 officers at the East Gate (#53: 4 patrol, 4 gate, 7 off), 5 each at Watchtowers XIII, XIV, XV (#54-56: 1 on watch or ballista, 2 patrol, 2 off), 10 at the Watchful Gate (#57: 3 patrol, 2 gate, 5 off). Temple District officers are conscientious; their subaltern **Justirio Komnaurius** is devout and secretly longs to be a Knight of the Winged Sun: **+2 reaction for Lawful clerics (crusaders, priestesses) and paladins** with him. The **Knights of the Winged Sun** (60 in the city) also guard the House of Mourning, the Temple Barracks and the Catacombs. City Watch statline: [[Organizations of Cyfaraun]]. AX3 p. 161.
- **Streets:** the Cesta Türaduaran (Street of the Mighty Dawn) runs in from the East Gate; its stretch near the temples is the **Boulevard of Icons** (#66), all stalls and shrines. Funeral processions daily, the weekly Keepers' torchlight march, beggars and the sick drawn to the charities. Street encounters: [[Streets of Cyfaraun]]; a "Special!" result uses this district's table below.
- **Mood:** the people take their faith seriously. Gates close sunup to sundown; a reasonable justification and a **Friendly reaction** persuade the Watch to open one by night.
- **Religious titles = class levels** (useful when AX3 lists staff by title): priestess — aspirant 0, novice 1, daughter 2, sister-initiate 3, sister 4, sister-disciple 5, priestess 6, mother 7, revered mother 8, matriarch 9; bladedancer — aspirant 0, blade-initiate 1, blade-daughter 2, blade-singer 3, blade-weaver 4, blade-sister 5, blade-adept 6, blade-dancer 7; crusader (ACKS I cleric) — lay servant 0, catechist 1, acolyte 2, priest 3, curate 4, vicar 5, rector 6, prelate 7. Knights: 0-level heavy infantry; "veteran knight" = paladin 1, "subaltern" = paladin 2 (AX3 p. 165).

> [!player] Arriving in the Temple District
> Past the East Gate, with its iron portcullis and oak doors painted with seven-pointed stars, the Street of the Mighty Dawn becomes the Boulevard of Icons: stall after stall of holy symbols, prayer books, incense, oils, flowers, fruit baskets, and pens of doves and lambs for sacrifice. Domes and porticoes crowd the blocks behind — the star-patterned brick rotunda of the Temple of the Hearth Fire, the white marble basilica of the Temple of Justice, the grey limestone House of Mourning, and a brick temple flanked by two statues of the same goddess, one dancing in veils, one armed for the arena. Grey-robed sisters carry the dead out through the East Gate every morning and bring back urns of ash. Knights in yellow cloaks with purple fringe stand at the doors.

## Points of interest

### 53. Pylos Gollë — East Gate (AX3 p. 161)
Iron portcullis and iron-bound oak doors painted with seven-pointed stars, between two 20’ tall, 50’ square concrete gatehouses (1,500 SHP and Unit Capacity 2 each). Open sunup to sundown (night: justification + Friendly reaction). Caravans for Türos Orn and the eastern border forts leave here.
- **Tolls:** 4d6+2gp per 5,000 st of merchandise each time goods enter or leave; imports also pay customs duty of 2d10% of market price ([[Doing Business in Cyfaraun]]).
- **Funerals:** the grey-robed Sisters of Mourning carry the dead from the House of Mourning (#62) out through this gate to the crematoria outside the walls almost daily, and return with the ashes for interment.
- **Watch:** 15 officers (4 patrol, 4 at the gate, 7 off duty in the ground-floor barracks); subaltern Justirio Komnaurius (see above).

### 54. City Watchtower XIII (AX3 p. 161)
Two-storey, 20’ tall, 25’ diameter tower at the inner angle of two Imperial Wall segments; white-stuccoed concrete (850 SHP, Unit Capacity 0.5), joined to the walls on its second storey. 5 officers: 1 on watch, 2 patrolling, 2 off duty.

### 55. City Watchtower XIV (AX3 p. 161)
Three-storey, 30’ tall, 40’ diameter teardrop tower (1,875 SHP, Unit Capacity 1.5), joined to the walls on its second storey; a **repeating light ballista** on a rotating platform under the terracotta roof. 5 officers: 1 on the ballista, 2 patrolling, 2 off duty.

### 56. City Watchtower XV (AX3 p. 161)
Two-storey, 20’ tall, 25’ diameter tower jutting from the Imperial Walls like a cupola (850 SHP, Unit Capacity 0.5). 5 officers: 1 on watch, 2 patrolling, 2 off duty.

### 57. Pylos Stodia — Watchful Gate (AX3 p. 161)
Sally gate for the nearby City Watch Headquarters (#5, [[Old District]]): iron portcullis and oak doors painted with the Watch's eye over crossed spears; two 20’ tall, 30’ square gatehouses (1,500 SHP, Unit Capacity 2 each). Sunup to sundown only (night: justification + Friendly reaction). 10 officers (3 patrol, 2 gate, 5 off). No major road; **closed to merchants** (sent on to the Plaza Gate). With the Watch next door and no trade, it is the **worst gate in the city for smugglers and thieves**.

### 58. Saraos Bellëdoran — Temple of the Hearth Fire (AX3 pp. 161, 163)
Star-patterned brick over concrete; a great narthex opens into a rotunda with a capped oculus for the smoke of the sacred fire. Seven apses each hold a marble altar to one Empyrean god, Mityara's central and opposite the door. An **eternal flame** rises from a brass brazier under the oculus. The temple is an **illuminated pinnacle of good**. **Divine spellcasting services** from the staff (ACKS II prices: [[Cyfaraun (Market)]]).
- **Day:** 3 daughters, 4 novices, 4 aspirants of Mityara; 60% each a sister-initiate and/or a sister. **Night:** 1 daughter, 2 novices, 2 aspirants; 25% each a sister-initiate and/or a sister. **Matriarch Aundëmë: 15%** at any time.

### 59. Maculos Cetenani — Hospital of the Keepers (AX3 p. 163)
Two-storey, 75’ square, round a 25’ square courtyard where an 8’ marble Mityara holds up an **ever-burning torch**: while it burns the courtyard is an **illuminated pinnacle of good**. Two-storey arcade, 10 wards per storey (20), up to 4 patients each (80), beds parted by white linen curtains embroidered with prayers; stairs at the outer corners, 5’ × 10’ medical storerooms at the inner corners; big open windows everywhere. **Patients: 8d10.**
- **Day:** a chirurgeon, a physiker, 2 healers, 2 novices of Mityara; 60% each a daughter, sister-initiate, sister and/or mother; Aundëmë 15%. **Night:** a physiker, a healer, a novice; 25% each daughter, sister-initiate, sister and/or mother.
- **Run by** the mother **Drakima** (priestess 7, no stats printed), who reports to the matriarch.
- **Hook:** the plague victim of special encounter 9 should be brought here.

### 60. Deprefanos Cetenani — Almshouse of the Keepers (AX3 pp. 163)
Plain brick over concrete. Ground floor: long tables and benches, serving counter, kitchen (porridge, soup, stew for the aged and infirm), pantry of grain and vegetables. Upper floor: dormitory of pallets for the homeless.
- **2d6 × 10 paupers** come for each of breakfast, lunch and dinner (60% children, 20% men, 20% women); **4d6** live here more or less full time.
- **Donations count toward reserve XP.** The Keepers welcome charity warmly.
- **Day:** 3 aspirants, 2 novices; 60% each a daughter, sister-initiate, sister and/or sister-disciple; Aundëmë 15%. **Night:** 2 aspirants, 1 novice; 25% each of the same.
- **Run by** the sister-disciple **Rianna** (priestess 5, no stats printed).
- **Hook:** Milos (special encounter 4) says the paupers' "cult that moved in" drove him out — see [[Death Cult Hideout]] (#75u).

### 61. Prosfygos Cetenani — Orphanage of the Keepers (AX3 pp. 163-164)
Large, clean two-storey brick building. **40+2d10 orphans** (age 3d6−1 years; mostly pre-adolescent) cared for by the Keepers and lay servants. **Donations count toward reserve XP.**
- **Gifted:** 1d6 children have gifts the priestesses have spotted (the children do not know); **2d6** more know they have special skills and hide them. Choose or roll on `Tables/ax3-orphan-gifts.yaml` (1d12; a gift column and a skill column).
- **Recruiting orphans as henchmen** requires a **Friendly (12+) reaction with Aundëmë**: she knows most orphans who follow adventurers die screaming in the dark.
- **Day:** 3 aspirants, 2 novices; 60% each a daughter, sister-initiate, sister and/or priestess; Aundëmë 15%. If the priestess or the matriarch is in, **2d10 orphans** play in the surrounding streets. **Night:** 2 aspirants, 1 novice; 25% each of the same.
- **Run by** the priestess **Pendara** (priestess 6, no stats printed).

### 62. Doros Makrimoro — House of Mourning (AX3 pp. 164-165)
Grey, black-speckled limestone; seat of the **Grey Sisters** (Sisterhood of Mourning, Calefa) under Revered Mother Doarë. Wills drawn up and executed, funerals managed. Bronze doors with Calefa's seven-spoked wheel open on a courtyard: marble statue of the goddess on a mosaic wheel; a small altar of candles, wine, coins and offerings; prayers for the dead offered here. The arcade holds 12 small rooms (10 funerary shrines where the dead lie in repose, 2 meeting rooms for wills) and 3 large (2 assembly halls for ceremonial readings of wills; the **clerical archive** of the city's death records and wills).
- **Day:** 10 aspirants, 5 novices, 3 daughters, a sister-initiate, a sister of Calefa; 60% each a sister-disciple, priestess and/or revered mother. **1d10** shrines hold a body with **3d6 mourners** (a lay servant and a novice attending); **1d2** meeting rooms in use (a daughter with 1d2 citizens); **1d2** assembly halls hold **5d6** family at a reading (by a sister-initiate or sister); everyone else in the archive.
- **Night:** 3 aspirants, 2 novices; 60% a sister-initiate or sister; 25% each a sister-disciple, priestess and/or revered mother (archive or courtyard prayer).
- **Guard, always:** **1d6+3 knights** of the Winged Sun, **1d2+1 veteran knights** (paladin 1) and a **subaltern** (paladin 2) → [[Knight of the Winged Sun (AX3)]], [[Veteran Knight of the Winged Sun (AX3)]], [[Subaltern of the Winged Sun (AX3)]].
- **Hooks:** Sister Ethlyn's 500gp communion (special encounter 1); Doarë's vault under the Shrine of the Ancestors ([[Catacombs of the Ancestors]] 6) holds the temples' treasuries and testators' deposits.

### 63. Saraos Rectan — Temple of Justice (AX3 pp. 164-165)
White marble basilica of the **templars of Türas**. Steps through a vaulted arch to the north atrium and a 12’ marble Türas with scales (left) and hammer of judgment (right); a colonnaded nave runs south to an apse whose half-dome bears the Laws of Light, with a whitewood-and-brass throne of judgment. Side rooms: case archive, vestry, a **vault** for fines and fees, a library of Auran law.
- **Jurisdiction:** bribery, conspiracy, corruption, diabolism, heresy, necromancy, treason, regicide, subversion and any crime against the Empire, the gods or their officers (civil disputes go to the Imperial Courthouse, #82, [[Palace District]]).
- **Procedure:** no public prosecutors; a citizen denounces the defendant before the presiding templar (the **denouncer must be of equal or higher legal status**; a templar may stand in as prosecutor for an important matter with no citizen). Preliminary investigation **1d6+1 days**; if it qualifies, the defendant is brought in (by force if need be). The ranking templar presides; a jury of retired magistrates, councillors and clergy decides guilt.
- **Day:** 2d6 lay servants, 1d8 catechists, 1d4 acolytes, 1d2 priests, 1d2 curates of Türas; **Prelate Ezelus 40%**, presiding. Also **1d4 master, 1d6 licensed and 1d6 apprentice lawyers** researching, representing or touting for work (lawyers are not required, but they make sure to take a fee at every trial).
- **Hook:** bring the **Assassins' Ledger** ([[Doppelganger Den]], #14u) to Ezelus → reward or an introduction to the Order of the Eclipse (see Special locations).

### 64. Ikan Vandelus — Hall of Travelers (AX3 p. 165)
Modest brick hall on the Boulevard of Icons, two blocks from the East Gate (AX3 misprints #52). The **Order of Travelers** (Naurivus) aids explorers, scouts, sailors, merchants and caravaneers.
- **Ground floor:** shrine to Naurivus, Lord of the Winds (statue on a mosaic wind-compass), and a **document repository** of atlases, charts, gazetteers, itineraries and guides for the Ammas Aurë: accurate inside the Empire, comically incomplete north of the Jutting Mountains, south of the Meniri and east of the Dark Wall. **A day spent charting a route between two points in the Empire gives +4 to avoid getting lost for that trip** (lost if the party strays from the route).
- **Upper floor:** 10-bed hostel, **2sp per night**; **2d6+2 patrons** each night (explorers, land surveyors, navigators, pathfinders, ship captains, venturers); **15%** a specialist (random type) is a guest. **Job notices** may be posted for **1d8+5gp** (ACKS II hiring rules).
- **Staff:** Rector Süromus **85%** in the repository by day, with **1d8 cartographers** (0-level, Mapping), 1d2 catechists, 1 acolyte; 60% a priest, curate or vicar. Night: 1d2 catechists; 30% each a priest, curate or vicar. **Divine spellcasting services** (Naurivus's blessings for travellers).

### 65. Saraos Balandëan in Fëlandëan — Temple of the Blade and Veil (AX3 p. 165)
Two-storey brick with geometric lions and 9-pointed stars; a 12’ portico on the west flanked by two statues of **Ianna** — naked but for jewels and veils, dancing; and in gladiatorial armour with two blades.
- **Ground floor (the Veil):** a seraglio of silk divans, myrrh and lavender; curtains hide **9 private rooms**. Present: **1d8 aspirants**, **1d6 novices**, **1d2 sister-initiates** with **2d6 patrons**; **Mother Aurëlyn 33%**, a priestess 33%, a sister and a sister-disciple 67%. Patrons' occupations: 1d100 on the General/Street column (`Tables/ax3-building-occupant-general-street.yaml`), **+25 if any leader is present** (101+ = 100). The courtesans sing, dance, recite, play music, make love; payment is tossed into a **sparkling fountain**, **1sp per hour customary**. The fountain holds **1d × 1,000sp**; a thief is **cursed by Ianna: −4 to reactions with anyone he is attracted to** until he returns the coin. Rude patrons are handled by magic or thrown out by a cohort of bladedancers; anyone who assaults a courtesan is **slain without mercy**, and the slayer at once pardoned by a priestess of Mityara.
- **Upper floor (the Blade):** a training hall; a dozen pells in staggered rows, racks of swords, daggers, spears and polearms. **Day:** 1d6+2 aspirants, 1d3+1 blade-initiates, 1d2 blade-daughters, 1 blade-singer, 1 blade-weaver; **Blade-Dancer Valara 85%**. **Night** (guarding the courtesans): 1d3 blade-initiates and 1 blade-daughter under a blade-sister; 60% each a blade-singer and a blade-weaver. Bladedancer statlines: build from the [[Bladedancer]] class at the title's level (Judge's call).

### 66. Boulevard of Icons (AX3 p. 166)
Dozens of shops, stalls and stands along the Cesta Türaduaran: **holy symbols and holy books** of the Empyrean gods, medicinal herbs, fragrant oils, incense, cut flowers, fruit baskets; doves, calves, kids and lambs brought in daily for augurs, soothsayers and sacrifices. (AX3's note on ceremonial-magic talismans belongs to the Heroic Fantasy Handbook, not used here.)

### 67. Kyrdoros Saraosan — Temple Barracks (AX3 p. 166)
Squat brick-faced concrete under a peaked roof flying the winged sun; home of the **60 Knights of the Winged Sun** in Cyfaraun. Closed to the public; bronze doors guarded always by **2 knights** (0-level heavy infantry, ML +4). Forcing a way in meets **1d6+3 knights, 1d2+1 veteran knights (paladin 1) and a subaltern (paladin 2)**. **Centurion Cellë 70%** by day. Inside: two platoon dormitories, two subalterns' quarters, an armoury, the centurion's two-room suite. (AX3 p. 42 calls it #65; the key is #67.)

### 68 / 68U. Saraos Vectionë — Shrine of the Ancestors / Catacombs of the Ancestors (AX3 p. 166)
Ornate brick and brass; a single long nave inside an arcade of alcoves with braziers, censers and bronze statuettes of the family archetypes (patriarch, matriarch, father, mother, son, daughter). Plaques record patricians' generosity to their ancestors. **2d6 citizens** pray here sunup to sundown, for the dead or for the ancestors' intercession. Stairs at the far end of the nave go down to the **[[Catacombs of the Ancestors]]** (Special Location #68u), where ashes are interred by class (patricians, merchants, commoners, advocates, clergy).

### 69U. Eclipse Hideout (AX3 p. 166)
The hideout of the local chapter of the **Order of the Eclipse**, a secret sect of Calefa that ends lives under ritual contract, lies south of the Catacombs of the Ancestors. Known only to Doarë, Ezelus, a few trusted lieutenants and the Order. → [[Eclipse Hideout]] (Special Location #69u). (Not the Lake Gate, AX3 #69, which belongs to the [[Lake District]].)

## Notable residents

### Matriarch Aundëmë (AX3 p. 166)
Priestess of Mityara, ranking member of the Hearth Fire clergy, responsible for propitiating every god on its holy days; head of the Keepers; the Hospital, Almshouse and Orphanage report to her (AX3 here misnumbers them #58-60). Found at #58, #59, #60 or #61 (15% each).
- **Traits:** 41, ageless beauty, long black hair, brown eyes of compassion without judgment; a faded claw scar on her throat from ghouls she fought in her adventuring youth. No longer bound to celibacy by her rank, she refuses all suitors.
- **Voice:** gentle and unhurried, never raises her voice, asks about your wounds before your errand: "Sit. You are bleeding on my floor, and that we can mend first."
- **Statline:** Priestess 9; Str 8, Int 9, Wis 18, Dex 8, Con 10, Cha 16; MV 40’ / 120’; AC 1 (cloak +2, DEX −1); HD 9, hp 23; staff 9+ (priestess 9 = 8+, STR −1), D 1d4−1 / 1d6−1 two-handed bludgeoning; Save C9: Paralysis 9+, Death 6+, Blast 12+, Implements 9+, Spells 11+, all +7 (WIL +3, Divine Blessing +2, cloak +2; AX3 printed +5 without the cloak); ML +2; AL L; XP 700 (classed NPC: level 9 as HD, no special abilities; RR p. 310). Proficiencies: Divine Blessing, Divine Health, Healing, Leadership, Righteous Rebuke (ACKS I Righteous Turning), Theology. Spells per day (ACKS II priestess 9): 5 1st, 5 2nd, 5 3rd, 3 4th, 2 5th (AX3 printed 5/5/5/3/3); repertoire as 9th level priestess. Gear: holy symbol (white lady of Mityara), slender whitewood **staff of healing** ([[Staff of the Healer]]), matriarch's head-dress (250gp), white silk chiton, gold silk sash, **white cloak of protection +2**, high boots, belt, pouch with **keys to Catacombs 8 and 9** and 100gp for alms.
- **Secret:** a childhood friend of **Lady Syrena Basilio**, she raised Syrena's husband the **Prefect** from death at Syrena's request, and grieves at what came of it ([[Palace District]]). She has heard of a shrine of her goddess that might help but cannot find it: **adventurers who befriend her are asked to find it** → Borderlands Gazetteer #21, the Shrine of the White Lady (`Gazetteer/`).
- **Danger:** the Keepers can legally pardon convicted criminals, so a marriageable matriarch is a prize. Last month **Councillor Arjenus Ionicus Drakomir** sought her hand for his eldest son; she declined. Soon after, one of her novices was hired to give her a **philter of love**; the girl confessed and handed her the bottle. Aundëmë has the evidence ([[Lake District]] for the Drakomirs).
- **Wants:** the shrine found; the orphans kept alive; the Prefect's household mended.

### Revered Mother Doarë (AX3 p. 167)
Leader of the Sisterhood of Mourning (Grey Sisters); House of Mourning (#62).
- **Traits:** 59; a bard's voice, strong, sonorous, reassuring; a face always overcast with some great sadness. An oracle with the gift of Prophecy, consulted for decades on wills, ventures and fortunes, helped by her inherited **medallion of ESP**. Lately all the omens have darkened, and she prefers prayer to telling people of the ill fortune to come.
- **Voice:** slow and resonant, speaks in the past tense of things that have not happened yet: "I have buried you already, child, in three dreams of four."
- **Statline:** Priestess 8; Str 5, Int 17, Wis 16, Dex 6, Con 14, Cha 13; MV 40’ / 120’; AC −1 (DEX −1); HD 8, hp 32; wavy-bladed dagger 10+ (priestess 8 = 8+, STR −2), D 1d4−2 piercing; Save C8: Paralysis 10+, Death 7+, Blast 13+, Implements 10+, Spells 12+, all +4 (WIL +2, Divine Blessing +2), **+8 vs illusions** (Illusion Resistance); ML +1; AL L; XP 600 (classed NPC: level 8 as HD, no special abilities; RR p. 310). Proficiencies: Divine Blessing, Illusion Resistance, Performance (storytelling) 3, Prophecy, Theology. Spells per day (ACKS II priestess 8): 5 1st, 5 2nd, 3 3rd, 3 4th (AX3 printed 5/5/3/3/2); repertoire as 8th level priestess. Gear: wavy-bladed dagger, **medallion of ESP** (see items), holy symbol (wheel of Calefa), grey chiton, low sandals, pouch with 100gp and a keychain of 8 keys (**Catacombs 6, 8, 9**; she alone holds the vault key and the chest keys).
- **Knows:** she is one of the few leaders who believe **the Awakening may be nigh**. With Ezelus, one of only **two people** who can introduce adventurers to the **Order of the Eclipse**.
- **Wants:** someone to believe her omens and act on them.

Gear: gracefully curved **sword +1**, dagger, holy symbol (9-pointed star of Ianna), polished leather armour, **ring of fire resistance** ([[Ring of Fire Protection]]), bladedancer's head-dress (20gp), golden silk cloak, white chiton, high boots, pouch with 50gp.
- **Knows / wants:** she trained young **Zeodarë Ambador** and cannot understand why the promising bladedancer has vanished ([[Doppelganger Den]], #14u). Would back anyone who finds her.

### Mother Aurëlyn (AX3 p. 167)
Head of the Temple of the Veil (#65, ground floor, 33%).
- **Traits:** 32, petite, long brown hair, a beautiful smile; trained as a sacred courtesan in Aura, where her radiance and charm made her a favourite of the Empire's elite. - **Statline:** Priestess 7; Str 7, Int 13, Wis 14, Dex 14, Con 10, Cha 18; MV 40’ / 120’; AC 1 (DEX +1); HD 7, hp 22; stiletto 9+ (priestess 7 = 8+, STR −1), D 1d4−1 piercing; Save C7: Paralysis 10+, Death 7+, Blast 13+, Implements 10+, Spells 12+, all +1 (WIL); ML +3; AL L; XP 440 (classed NPC: level 7 as HD, no special abilities; RR p. 310). Spells per day (ACKS II priestess 7): 5 1st, 3 2nd, 3 3rd, 2 4th (AX3 printed 3/3/3/2/2); repertoire as 7th level priestess. Gear: stiletto, holy symbol (9-pointed star of Ianna), gold silk cloak, veil and sash, scarlet silk chiton, gold bangles (20gp), gold earrings (20gp), high sandals, pouch with 50gp.
- **Wants:** a road back to Aura; useful, ambitious friends.

## Special locations
- **[[Catacombs of the Ancestors]] (#68u, AX3 pp. 170-172; map p. 218):** the excavated elven ruins under the Shrine of the Ancestors where the city's ashes are interred: five lit columbarium halls, a stair knight, two novices of Calefa chanting, the **Vault of the Grey Sisters** behind an iron door only Doarë can open (five glyph-warded chests: 8,000ep, 8,000gp, 9,000gp, 8,000pp and 20,900gp of gems and jewellery), the Empyrean Temple Wall and two iron gates to the sewers and the Vagrant Colony, and the **secret door to the Eclipse Hideout** (10). Undercity rules apply in the sewer tunnels.
- **[[Eclipse Hideout]] (#69u, AX3 pp. 172-175; map p. 218):** the local chapter of the **Order of the Eclipse** — Destrio, 3 elite deathbringers, 3 deathbringers — in elven ruins south of a sewer canal: pit-trapped entry hall, shrine of the black seven-spoked wheel, dormitories, Destrio's room of ciphered letters, assembly chamber with maps of the Borderlands and the city, a 4,000gp golden wheel and a 5,000gp chest. The note also holds the Order's laws, the **vetting** of candidates (`Tables/ax3-eclipse-vetting.yaml`), the **sacred initiation** against the three names in the Assassins' Ledger, **future assignments** (`Tables/ax3-eclipse-contracts.yaml`, `Tables/ax3-retribution-reason.yaml`, `Tables/ax3-retribution-petitioner.yaml`) and the six contract targets with statlines.

## Hooks
- **The Shrine of the White Lady:** Aundëmë's request (Gazetteer #21) — the hope of undoing what her raising of the Prefect has done.
- **The Drakomir philter:** Aundëmë holds the love-philter bottle and the novice's confession; proof of Councillor Drakomir's scheming ([[Lake District]]). Mother Aurëlyn, meanwhile, would back his coup.
- **The missing pupils:** Valara's **Zeodarë Ambador** and Cellë's **Tavis Drakomir** have both vanished — [[Doppelganger Den]] (#14u). Each leader rewards news with friendship (+reaction, Judge's call).
- **The Assassins' Ledger** → Ezelus → the Order of the Eclipse → acolyteship and the sacred initiation (Liber Faunus, Mentenus, Selene), or 1,500gp and an oath of secrecy.
- **Death Cult of Dirgion:** the inquisitors (encounter 2), Milos (4) and the Catacombs' iron gate (8) all point to the Vagrant Colony ([[Death Cult Hideout]], #75u).
- **The Awakening:** Doarë believes it is near; she is a rare ally for a party that has seen the signs.
- **Orphans:** gifted orphans as henchmen, if Aundëmë can be won to a Friendly (12+) reaction.
- **Charity as XP:** donations to the Almshouse and Orphanage count as reserve XP — as the [[Imperial Vanguard]] well know.
