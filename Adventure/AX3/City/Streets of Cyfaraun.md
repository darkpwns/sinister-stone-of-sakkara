# Streets of Cyfaraun

Judge only. Moving about [[Cyfaraun]] and its street encounters. Pages are AX3 printed pages. Hub: [[AX3 Hub]]; below ground: [[Undercity of Cyfaraun]]. ACKS II's general version of these rules is in the Judges Journal ([[Encounters in the Settlement]], JJ pp. 80-84). Where it differs, the module's numbers are used here (ruling order: area note first), and the JJ difference is noted.

Cyfaraun is an old city of twisting roads over the hills and along the bends of the Mirmen. It has no broad avenues or planned grid, but it is as crowded as any Auran city, so moving through it is slow, worse in large groups (p. 58).

> [!player] What anyone sees
> Narrow flagstone streets curve with the river, with raised sidewalks on both sides and stepping-stones across the road at every crossing. Carts creep through the ruts between the stones. Patricians ride in litters above the crowd. Narrow concrete alleys run between the packed buildings, and lead sewer grates are set in the street at every block.

## Types of streets (p. 58)
- **Cesta ("street"):** the streets run parallel or at right angles to the curves of the Mirmen. Only the Palace District has the square, compass-aligned Auran blocks. Fitted flagstones, **15’ wide**, slightly concave. **5’ concrete sidewalks raised 18” (1.5’)** on both sides keep wheels off pedestrians. At crossings, walkways of **18”-tall stepping stones** cross the road. Wheel ruts between them let carts pass slowly, which acts as a speed limit. Carts and wagons are banned from the sidewalks except at **sunrise and sunset**, for loading and setting up stalls. Patricians travel in litters carried by servants.
- **Sewer grates:** **2’ × 2’ lead sluice grates**, usually **one per block**. The concave streets drain rain and filth through them into the old canals under Ancient Cyfaraun. See *Sewer grates* below.
- **Den ("alleyway"):** concrete-paved cut-throughs between the blocks, usually **5’ wide** (narrowest **2’6”**, widest **8’**). Short-cuts through them can speed a trip. The alleys of the **Old and Festival Districts are dangerous, especially at night**.

## Movement in the city (p. 58)
Movement is counted in **city blocks**. Block size, crowds and road conditions vary, so a block is an abstraction. A litter or wagon gives privacy but is no faster.

| | Commuting (familiar area, set destination) | Meandering (sight-seeing, shopping, unfamiliar area) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One block | ~90 seconds (15 rounds) | ~10 minutes (1 turn) |
| Between two POIs in the same district | 1 turn | 1 hour (6 turns) |
| Between POIs in adjacent districts | 2 turns | 2 hours (12 turns) |
| Getting lost | Throw each turn (below) | Never: slow enough to read signs and ask the way |

**Getting lost (commuting only):** each turn, a proficiency throw of **11+** on 1d20 (AX3 calls it a Navigation throw). **+4** if the characters have been to this destination before, but not by this route. Once they have travelled a particular route (e.g. the Mink to the Emporium), no throw is needed on it again. On a **failure**, that turn they end up **1d4+1 blocks** from their destination. ACKS II has no city Navigation throw. Judge's call: one throw for the group, made by its guide, with the [[Navigation]] proficiency's +4 (as for getting lost in the wild); Pathfinding (explorer, venturer) adds as it does in the wild.

**Straggling groups (optional, p. 59):** a party of **6+** characters commutes at **half speed**, **12+** at **quarter speed**. The party may split into smaller groups to avoid this, but **each group makes its own encounter throw**.

## Encounter procedure (p. 59)
A random encounter in the city is not a meeting with ordinary passers-by (there are always plenty). It is an incident or interruption that draws the party's attention or disrupts its plans.

| Where | Throw 1d6 every | Encounter on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Streets by day | hour (6 turns) | 6+ |
| Streets by night, or alleyways by day | 30 minutes (3 turns) | 6+ |
| Alleyways by night | 10 minutes (1 turn) | 6+ |
| Looking for trouble (optional): loitering, loud talk, shoving, accosting strangers | as above | **5+** |

- Some sections are more dangerous (the **Thieves' Quarter** of the Old District, and others in the district notes: [[Old District]], [[Festival District]]).
- On an encounter roll **1d100 on `Tables/ax3-city-encounters.yaml`**, **+30 after dark** (results 01-130). Results **89-100** are the district's special encounter: roll 1d100 on that district's table (`Tables/ax3-old-special.yaml`, `ax3-festival-special`, `ax3-bridge-special`, `ax3-plaza-special`, `ax3-tower-special`, `ax3-temple-special`, `ax3-lake-special`, `ax3-palace-special`).
- AX3 has **no entry for 113-114**. Judge's call: run 109-112.
- Optionally, any city rumour may be replaced by a Random Irritating Rumour about the Imperial Vanguard (`Tables/ax3-irritating-rumours.yaml`, p. 201; [[Imperial Vanguard]]).
- JJ differences (ACKS II general rule, not used here unless the Judge prefers): alleys at night encounter on **5+**. Looking for trouble is **+1** to the throw (the same as 5+ elsewhere, but 4+ in alleys at night). **Holing up** (staying put out of trouble) is one throw a day, 5+. The JJ table (JJ pp. 81-84) is a generic edition of this one with different ranges above 109.
- Dice: the Judge rolls these with the engine's dice. There is no city site in the engine (`acks.sh location` is for dungeons).

## City Watch response (pp. 59-63)
Collected from the table entries:
- Beggars blocking a street are dispersed by **2d4 watchmen after 1d10 minutes** (01-04).
- A Watch patrol that is outnumbered **whistles for 2d6 more in 1d4 rounds** (33-36). The Watch stops a party with visible weapons or armour, and tries to arrest an argumentative or notorious one.
- A fight with Prefectural Guard legionaries brings **3d6 Watch in 1d4 rounds**. Those arrested are charged with assault, mayhem and/or murder (81-84).
- Escorting an escaped prisoner in the Undercity: throw every minute (6 rounds), **5+** brings **1d4+1 Watch officers** (Undercity, Prefect's Dungeon special; p. 67).
- Watch statlines: officers and bowmen are 0-level (HD 1-1, XP 5); veterans are Fighter 1 (XP 10). Organization, headquarters (#5) and watchtowers: [[Organizations of Cyfaraun]] and the district notes.

## Sewer grates (pp. 58, 63)
- One **2’ × 2’ lead grate** per block. They drain into the sewers ([[Undercity of Cyfaraun]]).
- **Old District:** the Argollëan Family uses its grates to move stolen goods. They lift out freely as **an action in lieu of movement**.
- **Elsewhere:** lifting a grate needs a **Dungeonbashing throw** (ACKS I Open Doors): **18+**, modified by ±4 per point of STR adjustment, a pair working together +4 (RR p. 266).
- By day the grates light the tunnel below in a **30’ radius**.

## Encounters needing more than the table cell

### 01-04 Beggars (pp. 59-60)
4d10 beggars (0-level: MV 20’/60’, AC 0, HD 1-1, hp 2 each, abuse but no damage, NM, ML -2, AL N, XP 5). City Watch officers (2d4; the MM [[Patroller]] regulars): MV 30’/90’, AC 5 (chain, shield), HD 1-1, hp 4 each, spear or short sword 11+, 1d6, NM, ML +1, AL L, XP 5; 2sp and 20cp each. A party that bribes or drives off the Watch is taken to the vagrants' friends at the **Vagrant Colony**. AX3 prints #73u here, but the colony is #75u (Lake District; [[Lake District]]), so read #75u. Hook: the death cult hiding in the colony (#75u).

### 17-20 Street magician (p. 60)
Mage 1; STR 10, INT 16, WIL 11, DEX 14, CON 9, CHA 13; MV 40’/120’, AC 1 (DEX), HD 1, hp 4, dagger 10+, 1d4 piercing, save M1, ML +1, AL N, XP 10. AX3 prints 16, but ACKS II scores classed NPCs as HD = level with no ability bonus. Proficiencies: Collegiate Wizardry, Performance (street magic) 2, Prestidigitation. Spells: one 1st-level spell a day. Repertoire: magic rope (no ACKS II spell; JJ p. 82 gives [[Weave Smoke]] instead, Judge's call: use that), ventriloquism = [[Auditory Illusion]], [[Wall of Smoke]]. Gear: showy robes, pipe, 6sp and 20cp. +2 to recruit him as a henchman, unless the party embarrasses him in front of his crowd.

### 41-44 Racing patricians (p. 60)
Patricians (2): Fighter 1; MV 80’/240’ mounted ([[Light Horse]]), AC 2 (leather), HD 1, hp 7 and 6, sword 10+, 1d6+1, F1, ML +1, AL N, XP 10. Proficiencies: Diplomacy, Riding. Military saddle, 6gp and 20sp each. Handing them to the Watch pays **10gp**. Revenge comes **1d4 nights later**: **2d4 hoods** (the local syndicate's hood statline from 109-112).

### 49-52 The poisoned vagrant (p. 61)
On 9-10 he collapses. A Healing throw diagnoses slow-acting pit viper venom (ACKS II *Pit Viper* venom: onset 1d10 turns, save +2, 2d6 poisonous; RR poisons table). [[Neutralize Poison]] revives him. Revived (or on a Friendly reaction in the other cases), he leads the party to the Vagrant Colony (#75u). He is a victim of Dirgion's death cult there.

### 77-80 The fleeing thief (p. 61)
Thief 2; MV 40’/120’, AC 2 (leather), HD 2, hp 6, a pair of daggers 9+ (dual weapon +1), 1d4, T2, ML +2, AL N, XP 20. Skills at Thief 2 with Skulking: Climbing 5+, Hiding 16+, Listening 13+, Sneaking 14+. Backstab +4 to hit and +1 damage die (AX3: ×2). Proficiencies: Gambling, Skulking. **Sack of 2,500sp.** East of the Mirmen he is Argollëan Family, west of it Sand & Bones. Outcomes: a Watch contact if the party points the way. If they mislead the watchman, he returns in 1d10 turns, and on a Friendly reaction introduces them to his syndicate ([[Organizations of Cyfaraun]]).

### 81-84 Prefectural Guard patrol (p. 61)
- Legionaries (10): Fighter 1; MV 20’/60’, AC 7 (banded plate 5, shield +1, Fighting Style Specialization (weapon and shield) +1), HD 1, hp 6 each, spear or short sword 10+, 1d6+1, F1, ML +3, AL L, XP 10. Proficiencies: Fighting Style Specialization (weapon and shield), Manual of Arms. 2sp and 20cp each.
- Subaltern: Fighter 3; STR 13, CHA 13; MV 20’/60’, AC 8 (plate, shield, specialization), HD 3, hp 13, spear or short sword 8+, 1d6+3, F3, ML +3, AL L, XP 50. Proficiencies: Command, Fighting Style Specialization (weapon and shield), Manual of Arms. 1gp and 20sp.

### 85-88 The rooftop assassin (pp. 61-62)
Assassin 5; DEX 16; MV 40’/120’, AC 4 (leather, DEX), HD 5, hp 23, crossbow 5+ or sword and dagger 6+, 1d6+2 plus poison, save as Assassin 5 (F5), ML +1, AL N, XP 200. Hiding 13+ and Sneaking 11+ (Assassin 5 with Skulking). Backstab +4 to hit and +2 damage dies (AX3: ×3). With Sniping he can backstab with the crossbow out to long range. Proficiencies: Disguise, Intimidation, Skulking, Sniping. Gear: 50’ rope, grappling hook, belt pouch with 10gp and 20sp, and **rockfish venom** on his bolts. AX3 gives it as onset 1 round, 4d6, save +1. ACKS II giant [[Rockfish]] venom is onset 1 round, Death save +1, 5d6 poisonous (RR poisons table). Judge's call: use the ACKS II 5d6.
- The 1d10 result says who hired him: the party itself (1-2), the Order of the Eclipse (3-4; Temple District #69u), Liber Faunus of the Argollëan Family (5-6), Sand & Bones (7-8), or Mentenus over Undercity snooping (9-10). Each is a hook into [[Organizations of Cyfaraun]].

### 109-112 Syndicate skirmish (p. 62)
**Argollëan Family** (east bank; their sign is a bloody "A"):
- Slayer: Assassin 4; STR 16, DEX 14; MV 40’/120’, AC 3 (leather, DEX), HD 4, hp 17, sword and dagger 4+ (STR, dual weapon +1, specialization +1) or crossbow 7+, 1d6+4 or 1d6+2, F4, ML +1, AL N, XP 80. Backstab +4, +1 die. Proficiencies: Fighting Style Specialization (dual weapon), Intimidation, Sniping. 4gp and 25sp.
- Hoods (2): Thief 2; MV 40’/120’, AC 2 (leather), HD 2, hp 6 each, sword and dagger 9+, 1d6, T2, ML +1, AL N, XP 20. Backstab +4, +1 die. Proficiencies: Labor (various), Skulking. Thieves' tools and 16sp each.
- Footpads (3): Thief 1; AC 2, HD 1, hp 3 each, sword and dagger 9+, 1d6, T1, ML 0, XP 10. Labor, Skulking. Thieves' tools, 6sp and 20cp each.

**Sand & Bones** (west bank; their victims' mouths are filled with sand):
- Torturer: Assassin 3; STR 16; MV 40’/120’, AC 3 (leather, Swashbuckling +1), HD 3, hp 14, morning star 7+, 1d10+4 bludgeoning, F3, ML +1, AL N, XP 50. Backstab +4, +1 die. Proficiencies: Eavesdropping, Intimidation, Swashbuckling. 1gp and 20sp.
- Hoods (3): Thief 2; AC 3 (leather, Swashbuckling), HD 2, hp 6 each, pair of hand axes 9+, 1d6, T2, ML +1, XP 20. Gambling, Swashbuckling. Thieves' tools and 16sp each.
- Footpads (2): Thief 1; AC 3, HD 1, hp 3 each, pair of hand axes 9+, 1d6, T1, ML 0, XP 10. Gambling, Swashbuckling. Thieves' tools, 6sp and 20cp each.

The first side to lose 2 fighters flees. These are the "hoods" and "footpads (as above)" of 41-44 and 119-130.

### 115-118 Watch raid (p. 62)
Veterans (3): Fighter 1; MV 30’/90’, AC 5 (chain, shield), HD 1, hp 6 each, spear or short sword 10+, 1d6+1, F1, ML +2, AL L, XP 10. Alertness, Intimidation. 6sp and 20cp each. Composite bowmen (3): 0-level; MV 30’/90’, AC 4 (chain), HD 1-1, hp 4 each, composite bow or sword 11+, 1d6, NM, ML +1, AL L, XP 5. 4sp and 20cp each. Someone slips out through a side window. Only characters with Lightless Vision, or the elven knack for spotting secret doors, notice. Hook: follow the escapee to the cult, brothel keeper, bookie or smuggler.

### 127-130 The patrician in the alley (p. 63)
1d4 drunk hoods (local syndicate statline). The woman has CHA 18 and Seduction. Her paramour (1d10) is a power in the city: Liber Faunus, Apollonis Meteros, Clitus Omnus, Narmirio Drakomir or Valens Icus Ambador (see the district notes and [[Organizations of Cyfaraun]]). Gaining the NPC's favour needs a Friendly reaction. Otherwise the party knows too much and may be silenced.

## Hooks from the streets
- Undercity leads: the sealed grate (65-68), the bites of the Crocodile Nest (105-108, #45u), the pit-viper-poisoned vagrant and Dirgion's death cult (#75u), the doppelgänger acquaintance (#14u), and the assassin's victim who snooped below (85-88).
- Syndicate entries: the fleeing thief (77-80), the skirmish (109-112) and the escaped prisoner (Undercity #78u).
- Rival party: the Imperial Vanguard mix-ups (29-32) and the bards who pledge to sing of the party (09-12).
