# Festival District

Up: [[AX3 Hub]] · [[Cyfaraun]] · market [[Cyfaraun (Market)]] · neighbours [[Plaza District]], [[Bridge District]], [[Old District]] · organizations [[Organizations of Cyfaraun]] · streets [[Streets of Cyfaraun]] · undercity [[Undercity of Cyfaraun]]

**Character (AX3 p. 99).** The city's playground, named for the celebrations and games of the Imperial Amphitheater (#20). It runs west from the west bank of the Mirmen, with the Imperial Wall on the north-west (the Traveler's Gate, #15, to the northern highways); the crossing of the Cesta Gundan ("Street of Wolves") and Cesta Nuviën ("Street of Roses") marks the border with the Plaza District to the south-west. The south-east edge was the Old Walls until the last census moved a block of actors, amphitheater hands and festival folk over from the Bridge District. Plazas everywhere with street fairs, mummers and musicians daily. Landmarks: Gambler's Row (#24), the Silk Pony Inn (#25), the three gladiatorial schools (#21-23).

**Who runs it.** Officially the Prefect and the City Watch (Subaltern Tavus Mityario at Watchtower IV, new and honest). In fact the **Sand & Bones Guild** of Selene Nictorian: it owns the bawdyhouses, cantinas and taverns, the amphitheater food trade and the bookmaking, controls Gambler's Row, the Festival Wharf and the warehouses, bribes most of the Watch, and runs illegal pit fights (the **Blood Moon** games) under the Silk Pony. The arena itself is run day to day by the lanista **Maximus Arjentüran**, Selene's lover and secretly a devil boar.

**Watch presence.** Gate #15 (15 officers), Watchtowers IV, V, VI, VII (#16-19, 5 officers each). Night shift at the gate, Watchtower V and Watchtower VI are bought by the Guild; Watchtower VII and Subaltern Mityario are honest. City Watch officer (0-level): MV 30’ / 90’, AC 5 (chain mail, shield), HD <1, hp 4, spear or short sword 11+, 1d6, F0, ML +1, AL L, XP 5; 2sp, 20cp.

**Streets.** Wide processional streets to the Amphitheater and gate; crowded plazas; the filthy unsewered alleys of Gambler's Row (encounters 5+ on 1d6 by day, 4+ by night, alley reactions -2). Elsewhere use the city's street encounter procedure ([[Streets of Cyfaraun]]) with the district's special encounter table below.

> [!player] The Festival District
> Banners, music and the smell of frying sausage: the quarter of the great **Imperial Amphitheater**, where the city goes for plays, races and the gladiatorial games. Three rival gladiator schools keep the gossip boiling: old **House Pluris** (champion Sentonus, "the Stallion of Cyfaraun", the common man's favourite), old **House Nuvicus** (champion Xandrama, "the Somirean Savage", exotic fighters from far lands), and the upstart **Maximus Arjentüran**, "the Strong Silver Sword", a former champion whose beastman gladiators die bloodily to roaring crowds. Fans brawl over who is the true champion.
> Anyone may enter the open sporting events at the Amphitheater for 1sp. Bookmakers in the arena corridors take bets on everything.
> **Gambler's Row** (Cesta Calefan) is a filthy, unsewered slum of gambling dens best avoided after dark. At its north-east end the clean-swept **Silk Pony Inn** is a famous gambling house for adventurers, merchants and worse. Merchants from Larisa enter by the **Traveler's Gate**, which shuts from sundown to sunup.

## Points of interest

### 15. Pylos Vandelus, the Traveler's Gate (AX3 p. 99)
- Iron portcullis and iron-bound oak doors painted with rearing horses, on the Street of Wolves; two 20’-tall, 50’-square concrete-and-stucco gatehouses (each **1,500 SHP, Unit Capacity 2**).
- Open sunup to sundown only; at night a reasonable justification and a **Friendly** reaction persuade the Watch to open.
- **Toll:** 4d6+2gp per 5,000 st of merchandise each time goods enter or leave; **customs** on imports 2d10% of the goods' market price ([[Doing Business in Cyfaraun]], AX3 p. 56). Merchants from the northern town of Larisa use this gate.
- **15 City Watch officers:** 4 on patrol, 4 at the gate, 7 off duty in the ground-floor barracks. The whole night shift is on the Sand & Bones payroll and lets Guild members through at night with no toll or duty.

### 16. City Watchtower IV (AX3 p. 99)
- Three storeys, 30’ tall, 40’ across, concrete and white stucco (**1,875 SHP, UC 1.5**), joined to the Imperial Wall at the second storey; a repeating light ballista on a turntable under the terracotta roof. Commands the district and the northern approaches.
- 5 officers: 1 on the ballista, 2 on patrol, 2 off duty. Base of the new district subaltern **Tavus Mityario** (below), whose inquiries into blood sports at the Silk Pony have angered the Guild; his tour is likely to be short.

### 17. City Watchtower V (AX3 p. 99)
- As IV (1,875 SHP, UC 1.5, ballista); 5 officers (1 ballista, 2 patrol, 2 off duty). Overlooks Gambler's Row, but Guild bribes keep its eyes on the north-west.

### 18. City Watchtower VI (AX3 p. 99)
- Two storeys, 20’ tall, 25’ square, concrete and stucco (**850 SHP, UC 0.5**), joined to the Imperial Wall (AX3's text calls it "VII" here: a slip). 5 officers: 1 in the tower, 2 on patrol, 2 off duty in the barracks **or the Silk Pony**. Charged with watching Gambler's Row; wholly corrupted by Guild bribes and free companionship at the Silk Pony.

### 19. City Watchtower VII (AX3 p. 99)
- Three storeys, 30’ tall, 40’ square, cut white limestone (**1,875 SHP, UC 1.5**), joined to the Old Walls; ballista; 5 officers (1 ballista, 2 patrol, 2 off duty). Honest, but their beat is only between the Amphitheater and the Mirmen, so the Guild leaves them alone.

### 20 / 20u. Imperial Amphitheater and Basement (AX3 pp. 101, 109-115)
- The real centre of the social calendar: victory parades, Nicean masked dramas, monthly gladiatorial games, seasonal festivals. On game days the seats fill, street performers and food vendors work the crowd and Sand & Bones bookmakers take bets in the corridors. Run in practice by Maximus. See Special locations and "Running gladiatorial games and betting" below.

### 21. Gladiatorial School of Omnus Pluris (AX3 p. 101)
- For decades the true fans' favourite stable. Lanista **Omnus Pluris** is near bankruptcy because of Maximus and now bets riskily on his champion **Sentonus**: a string of wins may save him, one loss costs him the school. Stable: Sentonus, 3 veterans, 4 ordinary gladiators; staff 2 trainers, a guard, a part-time chirurgeon.

### 22. Gladiatorial School of Celictor Nuvicus (AX3 pp. 101-102)
- Leading stable for 75 years by its own fans' reckoning. **Celictor Nuvicus** spent much of his fortune on 2 lizardman warriors, a lizardman champion and a wild giant crocodile from Arganos; they escaped the smugglers, and he desperately wants adventurers to recover them ([[Lizardman Sewer Lair]]; #45u Crocodile Nest in the [[Plaza District]]). Meanwhile he fields 4 ordinary gladiators, 3 veterans and the champion **Xandrama**; staff 2 trainers, a guard, a part-time chirurgeon.

### 23. Gladiatorial School of Maximus Arjentüran (AX3 p. 101)
- Opened two years ago by "the Strong Silver Sword"; specializes in capturing and breaking beastmen to kill and die on the sands. 2 trainers; stable of beastmen (15 per p. 101, 12 per p. 106: Judge's call; the basement holds 11, see [[Imperial Amphitheater - Basement]]) dominated by his strength and fierce manner. Among the richest men in Cyfaraun.

### 24. Gambler's Row (AX3 p. 101)
- The Cesta Calefan ("Street of Fortune"), lined with shady gambling dens; one of the filthiest areas of the city, never sewered: residents carry chamber pots to grates or latrines, or empty them in the street. Disease and plague strike here more than elsewhere; people of means avoid it. Run by the brutal Sand & Bones, who dislike trespassers.
- **Encounters 5+ on 1d6 by day, 4+ on 1d6 by night; reaction rolls in its alleys at -2.**

### 25 / 25u. The Silk Pony Inn / Sand & Bones Hideout (AX3 pp. 101, 116-122)
- Gambling den at the north-east tip of Gambler's Row, clean-swept (street-foulers are beaten by its enforcers); Selene's property and the Guild's headquarters and hideout. See Special locations: [[Silk Pony Inn - Ground Floor]], [[Silk Pony Inn - Top Floor]], [[Sand and Bones Hideout]].

### 26. Sand & Bones Warehouses (AX3 pp. 101-102)
- Four warehouses, publicly supplying the amphitheater food trade, secretly holding stolen and smuggled goods. Each holds **2d10 loads of grain/vegetables, 3d10 of preserved fish or pork, 2d10 of beer**; **33%** chance of 1d3 types of illicit merchandise (roll the type on the merchandise list, [[Doing Business in Cyfaraun]]): **4d10 loads** if common, **1d10** if precious.
- Day: 10% chance of 1d6 laborers (0-level Guild members) handling legal goods. Night: 10% chance of 1d6 footpads moving contraband.
- Guards: **1 thug per 3 loads of precious merchandise (round up)** and **1 "3rd level enforcer" per 10 thugs (round down)** (AX3's words; ACKS II title of Assassin 3 is torturer: use the torturer statline, Judge's call). Statlines in [[Sand and Bones Hideout]].

### 27. Festival Wharf (AX3 p. 102)
- Piers on the west bank of the Mirmen: moorage for **16** barges or boats; usually **1d6+1 barges, 1d4+1 river boats, 1d4+1 sailing boats**. By day **5d6** sailors, rowers and dockworkers. Secretly run by the Guild: every dockworker is a 0-level member.

### 28u. Lizardman Lair (AX3 pp. 102, 123-124)
- The escaped arena lizardmen lair in the western sewers under the district and come out each night to kill, eat and rob on the docks and barges. See [[Lizardman Sewer Lair]].

## Notable residents

### The Sand & Bones Guild (AX3 pp. 102-104)
149 members. Selene's monthly revenue from them **7,930gp** (plus about 3,250gp profit from her businesses). Assassins: spies, debt enforcers, Blood Moon security. Bards: entertainment at the Amphitheater and venues. Thieves: racketeers, half protecting the food vendors and bookies at the Amphitheater, half running games and security at the Silk Pony. Venturers: the legitimate front (fast food, bookmaking). Debtors, derelicts, harlots and vagabonds are "indentured" for every sordid task. Arms: small bows and brutal hand weapons (hand axes, maces, morning stars); assassins use poison on operations. Typical proficiencies: Eavesdropping and Skulking (spies), Fighting Style Specialization and Intimidation (loan-sharking), Acrobatics and Prestidigitation (bards), Bargaining, Gambling, Profession (tavernkeeper) (venturers), Fighting Style Specialization, Skirmishing, Swashbuckling (thieves).

**War footing.** Selene has no venture company or security force: if she "takes it to the mattresses" she hires gladiators from Nuvicus and Pluris, and fields Maximus's thralled beastmen only as a last resort (beastmen in the streets would bring the Empire in). Triggers (Judge's call): attacks on Maximus; murder of any member of 5th level or more; a party disrupting the blood sports; big losses by the Argollëan Family (she moves in for the kill).

#### Selene Nictorian — head of the Guild
- **Role / where:** owner of the Silk Pony; secret founder and boss of Sand & Bones. Silk Pony 2 and 6, Hideout 1 and 3 (chances in the dungeon notes). → [[Selene Nictorian (AX3)]]
- **Traits:** tall, willowy, waist-length black hair, green eyes, cruelly beautiful; a sadist who loved humiliating her family's betters (stolen trinkets blamed on servants, seduced sweethearts, purgatives in the food); came back rich from years away adventuring.
- **Voice:** silky and amused, always sounding as if she knows the punchline already. "Debts are such a burden. Let me lift yours."
- **Statline:** Thief 9; MV 40’ / 120’, AC 4 (7 in leather armor +1), HD 9, hp 27, dagger +2 4+ (3+ vs beastmen), 1d4+2, T9, ML +4, AL C, XP 700; Sneaking 6+, Lockpicking 7+, backstab +3d.
- **Wants:** wealth, cruelty, Maximus (bewitched by him, but she really does love him, and is thrilled by his lycanthropy and cannibalism); admires Liber Faunus, hates his sister Hesta.
- **Knows:** everything about Maximus (his nature, the fixed bouts, the Abattoir); Mara is their dupe.
- **Hooks:** dimly senses her love for Maximus was unnatural; her hoard (Hideout 1) is the richest prize in the district.

#### Jonus Krusaro — spymaster and chief enforcer
- **Role / where:** collects Selene's debts; Hideout 3. → [[Jonus Krusaro (AX3)]]
- **Traits:** short, hugely muscled, curly black beard, broken nose, utterly silent on his feet; Selene's former lover, still loyal.
- **Voice:** few words, flat and close, like a hand on your shoulder. "You'll pay. Tonight is better for you."
- **Statline:** Assassin 7; MV 40’ / 120’, AC 2, HD 7, hp 35, pair of hand axes 2+, 1d6+6, or shortbow +1 5+, 1d6+4 + wyvern venom, A7, ML +2, AL C, XP 440.
- **Wants:** Maximus gone and Selene back. **Knows:** Maximus acts strangely at full moon; he suspects a lycanthrope. **Hooks:** carries a scroll of warding against lycanthropes to challenge Maximus once sure: a ready ally for a party that brings proof.

#### Thana Nyssand — business manager

#### Lutarius Sinorix — master of ceremonies of the Blood Moon

### The gladiatorial schools (AX3 pp. 104-107)
Cyfaraun supports three schools with **16 human gladiators** between them. Pluris and Nuvicus are third-generation lanistae whose rivalry fills the city's gossip; Maximus has eclipsed both. Gladiator classes (pursuer, challenger and so on, from "Morituri Te Salutant", *Axioms* 4, not in the rules vault) set arms, armor and proficiencies. Gladiators have two morale scores: in battle / loyalty to their lanista. Economics and rental fees: "Running gladiatorial games and betting" below.

#### Maximus Arjentüran — lanista, "the Strong Silver Sword"
- **Role / where:** school #23; manages the Amphitheater and its basement; co-runs the Blood Moon games; Hideout 1-3. → [[Maximus Arjentüran (AX3)]] (MM [[Devil Boar]])
- **Traits:** over 6’6”, huge frame gone to hedonistic fat; enormously charismatic; beds half the city's women; Selene is his main lover. Former local, then regional legend who indentured himself to pay his family's debts and won his freedom in Cyfaraun, Arganos and Aura, where he killed a werewolf with silver sword and spear (his sobriquet). Infected by the she-boar Nathera, who doted on him; he murdered her in her sleep and fled Aura. Opened his school in I.Y. 379. Each month he rides out with mercenaries to capture beastmen, cowing most without a fight.
- **Voice:** booming showman's warmth that turns flat and cold the instant he is crossed. "The crowd wants blood, friend. I simply deliver it."
- **Statline:** devil boar HD 9**, hp 68; human form MV 60’ / 180’, AC 4, spear +1 and sword +1 -1+, (1d6+1) x2; boar form MV 40’ / 120’, AC 6, gore 2+, 2d6 extraordinary; immune to mundane damage (silver hurts); hypnotic gaze (Spells save -2 or enslaved); F9, ML +5, AL C, XP 2,000.
- **Wants:** wealth, fame, human flesh. **Knows:** everything. **Methods:** charm cuts beastman training from 6-10 months to nothing; fixes bouts (orders his beastmen to throw, or charms the opposing gladiators); the bookmaking with Selene profits; fixed bouts push more people into debt slavery and crime, feeding the arena and the Blood Moon, and he volunteers to "inter" the corpses so he can eat them.
- **Hooks:** the human meat in basement storeroom 7; the Abattoir; Jonus's suspicion; Pluris's 750gp bounty for proof of cheating.

**Maximus's stable (AX3 p. 106).** 12 beastmen: 4 orcs, 3 hobgoblins, 2 gnolls, 2 bugbears, 1 ogre; most fight to the death, so it changes often; all charmed, loyalty +4. Rent per bout: **orc 145gp, hobgoblin 300gp, gnoll 360gp, bugbear 700gp, ogre 800gp**. Statlines (MM numbers, AX3 equipment) in [[Imperial Amphitheater - Basement]]:

## Running gladiatorial games and betting (AX3 pp. 104-105, 112-116)

**Calendar.** Ordinary days: one event in the early afternoon, 1d6 on `Tables/ax3-festival-amphitheater-daily.yaml`. Every three months a **seasonal festival of seven days, seven events a day** from sunup to sundown, 1d10 each on `Tables/ax3-festival-amphitheater-festival-hourly.yaml`. Sub-tables: `ax3-festival-epic-poetry` (with `ax3-festival-epic-accompaniment`), `ax3-festival-theatrical`, `ax3-festival-musical`, `ax3-festival-sporting`, `ax3-festival-popular-entertainment`. The economics also speak of three days of games each season and monthly games (p. 101): Judge's call how they fit; the bout procedure below is for the seasonal games.

**Sporting events.** Ordinary days: open to anyone, **1sp** entry; the winner takes **20%** of the total entry fees and earns the right to compete on festival days. Festival days: prior winners only, **1gp** entry; the winner gets laurels from the Prefect and the right to represent the Borderlands in that event at the annual Great Games in Aura. Resolve with proficiency throws or opposed rolls (Judge's call; AX3 gives no mechanic).

**Gladiatorial bouts (seasonal games).** Munerators sponsor 4-6 bouts. For each bout roll 1d6 (`ax3-festival-bout-type`): 1-4 **to the death** (`ax3-festival-bouts-death`), 5-6 **to incapacitation** (`ax3-festival-bouts-incapacitation`). Keep rolling until the bouts' costs total **at least 3,850gp, minimum 4 bouts** (about 5 on average). A gladiator fights only once per seasonal games: re-roll repeats. Random gladiators: `ax3-festival-ordinary-gladiator` (1d8), `ax3-festival-veteran-gladiator` (1d6). The munerator orders the bouts as he likes. **A PC sponsor** spends what he likes (minimum 3,850gp) and chooses the combatants, one bout each. Fighters wait in basement room 11 for 1d4 hours before their bout. Condemned prisoners: 0-level, use [[Commoner]] (Judge's call). Resolve bouts by running the fight (AX3's "Morituri Te Salutant" quick resolution is not available).

**Rent per bout.** Nuvicus or Pluris: champion **1,125gp**, veteran **265gp**, ordinary **150gp**. Maximus: orc **145gp**, hobgoblin **300gp**, gnoll **360gp**, bugbear **700gp**, ogre **800gp**.

**Economics (background).** Munerators spend at least **0.5gp per family**: **1,275gp per game day, 3,825gp per season, 15,300gp per year**. Formerly split between Nuvicus and Pluris (each **635gp a day, 1,912gp a season, 7,620gp a year**), each keeping 4 ordinary, 3 veterans and a champion, each rented 3 times a year: (3 x 150 x 4) + (3 x 265 x 3) + (3 x 1,125) = **7,560gp**, plus 60gp from animal shows and criminal bouts. Now **600-700gp a game day** goes to Maximus and only **300-350gp** to each of the others, enough to rent two ordinary gladiators a day (2 x 150gp). A typical gladiator wins **50%**, loses and lives **30%**, dies **20%**; a winner earns a **30gp** bonus, a dead man costs **250gp** to replace: expected cost **65gp a day** (15 + 50). Fixed costs (trainers, guards, healers) **100gp a month**: expenses about 165gp, profit about **135gp a month**, under half the old figure. Hence Nuvicus and Pluris want Maximus gone or richer munerators.

**Inspection.** On each game day the Inspector (Mara) is supposed to detect magic on every gladiator and dispel magic during random bouts. She fakes it, so magic on gladiators (and Maximus's bewitchment) passes unchecked.

**Betting (p. 116).** Sand & Bones bookies take bets **up to the value (cost) of the match** at the listed odds on the incapacitation table, or the reverse for the other side (e.g. Baruch over Braig at 2:1, Braig over Baruch at 1:2). Reading (inferred): odds X:Y pay X for every Y staked on a win, stake returned. Bouts to the death have no printed odds (Judge sets them). Team match: odds = average of the team's, cost = sum. **Fixing:** if Maximus's gladiators are on either side, **25%** the bout is fixed and the Guild always wins it. Otherwise run the fight. **Lucky streak:** the Guild always pays, but each time a given character wins a bet roll **2d6 + his CHA modifier - his number of consecutive winning bets**; on **2 or less** an enforcer and 3 thugs visit him to discuss his luck (statlines in [[Sand and Bones Hideout]]).

**PCs as gladiators.** On 96-00 of the incapacitation table, PCs who fight as gladiators meet human gladiators of the Judge's choice (singly or in groups) who have not yet fought these games. Nuvicus (via Agilus) recruits; pay is the Judge's call (AX3 gives none; the rent and bonus figures above are a guide).

### Blood Moon games (AX3 pp. 120-122)
- **When:** once a month, the **night before the full moon** (Corentin says "the next full moon": same event), in the Hideout's Main Room 3. Crowd arrives after dark; bouts begin when the moon reaches its apex. Only patrons with the password **"Kruan Elen"** (or friends of the Guild) may attend and bet; fighters get in by naming Corentin or looking the part.
- **Card:** **3d6 bouts.** About **25% to the death** (Judge's call: 1 on 1d4), the rest to **submission**. Prize: **50gp** to the winner of a submission bout, **100gp** for a bout to the death; famous or "interesting" volunteers up to **5x the henchman monthly wage for their level** (**10x** for a death bout). In death bouts even the winner may be finished off if healing him would cost too much. The dead are dragged to the edge of the pit; the Watch never finds them (Maximus eats them in the Abattoir).
- **Combatants:** 4d6 present, about a third shackled. For each: 1d100 and 1d6 on `ax3-festival-bloodmoon-combatant` (01-76 0-level lout / gambler / townsfolk; 77-88 explorer / fighter / barbarian; 89-00 bard / thief / assassin). 0-level: reason on `ax3-festival-bloodmoon-amateur-reason`. Classed: level on `ax3-festival-bloodmoon-level`, reason on `ax3-festival-bloodmoon-professional-reason`. Lout: as drunken lout sober (AC 0, hp 4, punch 11+); gambler and townsfolk: [[Commoner]] (Judge's call; AX3 gives no statline). Named regulars: [[Yanos (AX3)]], [[The Kid (AX3)]], [[Halgar Halface (AX3)]], champion [[Sigilo (AX3)]] (recruitable as a henchman with +2 by anyone who beats him).
- **Arms:** fighters enter **unarmed and unarmored** at opposite ends. In bouts to the death Lutarius throws **1d3+1** pieces from `ax3-festival-bloodmoon-equipment` (1d10, duplicates allowed) into the centre. Picking up a piece within 5’ is an action in place of moving or attacking if unengaged, in place of attacking if engaged. Lutarius uses Inspire Courage on the fighters to make it bloodier, and his wand of illusion for show.
- **Betting:** a bookmaker (lockbox 5d10 x 20gp) takes bets from 2d6+20 patrons (4d10 x 100gp between them). AX3 gives no odds: Judge's call (e.g. even money, or judge from the fighters). The lucky-streak rule above applies (Judge's call).

## Hooks

- **Nuvicus's beasts:** recover the 2 lizardman warriors, the champion and the giant crocodile alive for the next games (premium); Liber Faunus brokers a discreet meeting through the Mink ([[Old District]]). Fifteen missing dockworkers and body parts in the river; the docks blame the Argollëan Family.

- **The Blood Moon:** Corentin's invitation; the password from Crispus; Sigilo as a henchman; Subaltern Mityario wants evidence and may not live long; disrupting the games triggers Selene's war.
- **Guild weak points:** Thana dislikes the illegal side and loves Jonus; Lutarius would do anything to lose his pallor; Selene's hoard in the Secret Office (9,000ep, 9,000gp, 22,303gp in gems and jewellery).
- **Gambler's Row:** Amira and other debtors; shops paying protection; the corrupt Watch at Watchtowers V and VI and the gate's night shift (smuggling route for the Guild).
- **Open contests:** PCs can enter sporting events for 1sp, or fight in the arena for House Nuvicus.
