# Doing Business in Cyfaraun

Judge only. Trade, services, prices, hiring, lodging, banking, selling loot and the law in [[Cyfaraun]], the Borderlands' largest trading hub. AX3 pp. 56-57 summarise the *Axioms #3* trade rules with Cyfaraun's numbers; where AX3 cites ACKS I pages, the ACKS II RR rule of the same name applies. Market note (demand modifiers, trade routes): [[Cyfaraun (Market)]]. Hub: [[AX3 Hub]]. Factions: [[Organizations of Cyfaraun]].

## Market class and market impact (p. 56)
- Cyfaraun is a **Class III** market. A **venturer** may treat it as **Class II** *or* take **+1 market impact**, whichever helps more.
- **Market impact** (worked out whenever the party enters or leaves with boats, ships, wagons, carts or pack animals) = normal load of all its vehicles and pack animals ÷ **5,000 st**, rounded to nearest (0.5 rounds up). Maximum **10**. At impact **0** a character can still trade but treats the city as **Class IV**.
- A group arriving together chooses to enter the market as one party or separately; if separately, each one's maximum impact is 10 ÷ the number of characters.
- Multiply the number of merchants, passengers and shipping contracts on the table below by the market impact. For each merchant roll the loads he will deal in; for each shipper the cargo to ship.

**Merchants, Merchandise, Passengers and Shipping Contracts** (p. 57; per point of market impact)

| Effective class | Merchants | Loads per merchant | Passengers | Shipping contracts | Cargo per contract |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| II (venturer) | 2d4+1 | 4d6 | 2d4 | 2d4+1 | 4d6 loads |
| III | 2d4 | 3d4 | 1d4 | 2d4 | 3d4 loads |
| IV (impact 0) | 1d4 | 2d4 | 1d4−1 | 1d4 | 2d4 loads |

Shop owners met through [[Buildings of Cyfaraun]] count against these merchants (a master artisan deals 1 load a month, a master merchant 3d4, an independent licensed merchant 1d4+1).

## Tolls, duties, fees (pp. 56, 187)
- **Toll** at any of the eight gates, entering or leaving with goods: **4d6+2gp per 5,000 st** of merchandise (fractions rounded to nearest, 0.5 up). River traffic pays at the river gates' wharves.
- **Customs duty** on imports: **2d10% of the goods' market price** (different officers, different appraisals).
- **Moorage:** 1gp per 10 structural hit points per day. **Stabling** (caravans): 2sp per mule, 5sp per horse, 1gp per cart, 2gp per wagon, per day.
- **Labour fee** whenever merchandise is bought or sold: **1gp per 200 st**, to load it. The Dockworkers' Guild and its Argollëan Family friends resent non-guild labour.
- **Smuggling instead:** the Argollëan Family smuggles up to **60 loads a month for 10% of value**; **5%** chance the goods are intercepted, otherwise they reach a Family warehouse in **2d8+3 days** (p. 56). Through Liber Faunus at the Mink #11 ([[Old District]]). Not for obviously Lawful characters.
- Example (p. 56): Dalefa, venturer 5, 40 large wagons (12,800 st) of 128 loads of metal ingots (25,600gp): impact 3 (+1 venturer = 4: 8d4 merchants); toll 12d6+6gp (51gp); duty 12% (3,072gp); stabling 160gp a day; labour 64gp; two weeks' total 5,427gp.

## Equipment (pp. 56-57)
- Equipment availability: RR Equipment Availability by Market Class, **Class III** (**Class II for venturers**); replenished monthly; market impact does not change it.
- If stock is short, a party may **commission up to 10× the available stock**: buildings and vehicles 1 day per 500gp of value; animals 1 day per 1gp of value (or the training rules); other equipment **1 day per 5gp**; several items are made at the same time.
- A single shop holds about **10% (small), 25% (medium) or 50% (large)** of the city's availability.
- Where: any equipment and common merchandise at the **Emporium #7** (open-air market six days a week, sunup to sundown; its shops and random vendors are in the [[Old District]] note); precious merchandise, shipping contracts, passengers and investments at the **Merchant Guildhouse #8**. Specialist sellers: the Tower of Knowledge storefront (spellbooks, parchment, ink, laboratory and workshop components, Class III availability; p. 152); Emporium vendors such as the elven pioneer Niall (leather armour, bows, gear; commissions masterwork leather and bows) and the mountebank Thaddeus (healing salves 10gp; quacksalves) (p. 81).

## Mercantile investment (p. 57)
- In a market, a character may invest 1gp or more as a **business establishment**, **commercial expedition** or **money lending**, at a risk level. Each month the party may place up to **10,000gp per type per risk level**.
- Monthly yield: **safe 0.25%**; **cautious 0.5% + 1d2% − 1d2%**; **balanced 1% + 1d3% − 1d3%**; **risky 3% + 1d10% − 1d10%**; **perilous 9% + 1d20% − 1d20%**. Vagaries of investment: *Axioms #3* (not in the vault; Judge's call). Brokered at the Merchant Guildhouse (up to 10,000gp a month in opportunities, p. 71).

## Banking: Merchant Guildhouse #8 (pp. 70-71)
- Run by Guildmaster Apollonis Meteros ([[Organizations of Cyfaraun]]). **Becoming a client:** domain rulers automatically; others need a **Friendly reaction** (+1 for a venturer, +1 per rank of Bargaining).
- **Deposits:** 0.25% a month; withdraw on demand; pay guild merchants by bill of exchange.
- **Lending to the bank:** 30-day terms at 0.25% interest.
- **Loans:** up to 10,000gp a month; adventuring expeditions pay **3% a month with collateral, 9% without**.
- **Money-changing:** 0.25% fee.
- **Licences:** a store with staff pays **10% of earnings a year**; sole proprietors unlicensed (thought disreputable).
- The vault holds 16 talents of gold, 137 of silver, 158 of copper (187,680gp, 1,866 st).
- **Warehouse space** (warehouse block #38, [[Plaza District]]): 2sp per load per day, guild members only (p. 137).

## Selling loot and the treasure law (p. 57)
- **Auran law:** treasure is an ancient, forgotten deposit with no present owner. Found on one's own or unowned land, the finder keeps it; on another's land, half goes to the landowner. **The Undercity (and the Nethercity below it) is city property: half of anything found there is legally owed to the public treasury.**
- **Tax collectors** watch the gates, the Emporium and the Guildhouse and are eager for revenue: each transaction disposing of treasure openly has a **5% chance, +1% per 1,000gp of treasure the party sold or banked in the last month**, of an official questioning the party about where the antiquities came from, with **50% of the assessed value** at stake.
- **The Argollëan Family** buys treasure at **65% of value**, **up to 80%** for friends of the syndicate; it fences elven relics for 100%+ (p. 73). Selling outside the Family risks its anger.
- **XP:** the party earns full XP for treasure brought to the city whatever taxes it pays.
- Other buyers: gemcutters, jewellers, goldsmiths and silversmiths (precious metals, stones, gems), hornworkers and taxidermists (monster parts, ivory), furriers (rare furs), booksellers (rare books) via [[Buildings of Cyfaraun]]; Karthas the gladiator auctioneer buys captured humanoids (bugbear 75gp, gnoll 56gp, goblin 16gp, hobgoblin 40gp, kobold 16gp, lizardman 45gp, minotaur 320gp, ogre 275gp, orc 40gp; p. 81); the exotic animal trainer Celictor at the Emporium buys captured animals (p. 80).

## Spellcasting, healing, identification
- **Spellcasting services** (RR Spellcasting Services; Class III castings per day): divine 1st 4d4×10 (5gp), 2nd 4d6 (10gp), 3rd 2d6 (45gp), 4th 2d3 (185gp), 5th 1d4 (900gp), 6th 1d8−6 (3,600gp); arcane 1st 2d4×10 (5gp), 2nd 2d6 (10gp), 3rd 2d3 (45gp), 4th 1d3 (185gp), 5th 1d2 (900gp), 6th 1d8−7 (3,600gp). The caster must still be recruited; divine casters never serve the opposite alignment and may charge double outside their faith.
  - Divine: Temple of the Hearth Fire #58 (Mityara; an illuminated pinnacle of good, p. 163), Hall of Travelers #64 (Naurivus, p. 165), the Solar Citadel #36 (Ammonar; see [[Bridge District]]). Street event: hospitalists of Mityara give a free 1st-level casting to those who wait 1d6 turns (p. 169).
  - Arcane: Tower of Knowledge storefront (members must sell through it and split profits 50/50).
- **Tower of Knowledge** (p. 152): identify a magic item fully **2,000gp, two weeks**; alchemist identifies a potion **40gp, one turn**; sage identifies a common magic item's general nature with Magical Engineering **40gp, one turn**; spell formulas (no death or necromancy) **1st 100gp, 2nd 200gp, 3rd 400gp, 4th 1,000gp**. Membership: 10gp a month (or work as an assistant), no necromancy, black-magic finds handed over, formulas copied to the Tower, items made for personal use bequeathed to it (p. 148).
- **Hospital of the Keepers #59:** 80 beds, staffed by a chirurgeon, a physicker, 2 healers and novices; no fee printed (use RR healer rates or a donation; Judge's call). Almshouse donations count toward reserve XP (p. 163).

## Legal matters (pp. 61, 114, 164, 172, 187-190)
- **No public prosecutors:** a private citizen brings the case. Most lawsuits go to the **Imperial Courthouse #82** ([[Palace District]]): disputes between individuals (contracts, property, delicts and felonies against private persons and property: adultery, assault, breach of contract, burglary, defamation, embezzlement, kidnapping, murder, rape, robbery, theft, trespass, vandalism). The citizen accuses before one of **seven magistrates**, who qualifies the claim; the defendant is then brought in, by force if needed. Patrician magistrates preside; **juries of citizens** (paid 1sp a day, so often poor or old) decide; a patrician defendant may choose the magistrate instead. **2d10 trials a day** (40% contract and property, 40% delicts, 20% felonies). Lawyers are optional but present: 1d6+1 master, 2d6+1 licensed and 4d6+3 apprentice lawyers solicit there daily.
- **Temple of Justice #63** ([[Temple District]]): crimes against the Empire, its gods and officers (bribery, conspiracy, corruption, diabolism, heresy, necromancy, treason ...). A denouncer must be of the same or higher legal status as the accused; the presiding templar investigates for **1d6+1 days**; if qualified, trial before the ranking templar with a jury of retired magistrates, councillors and clergy. A templar may prosecute an important case that lacks a citizen.
- **Prisoners** await trial in the Prefect's Dungeon #78u (4d10 at a time: 65% capital charges, 30% serious felonies, 5% petty).
- **Punishments seen in the city:** public flogging by the Watch (smugglers, thieves, bread thieves), crucifixion of heretics by inquisitors (street events, pp. 169, 190), public execution in the Amphitheater (fed to lion, bear or wolves, boiled in the brass bull, torn apart by horses, slain by magic; p. 114), confiscation of property and indenture (labour) as sentences (p. 172), death for any act against the Empire (the Emperor's person). Debtors may sell themselves into indenture. The **Keepers of the Hearth Fire may legally grant mercy** to the convicted. The **Order of the Eclipse** takes contracts of righteous retribution when a wrongful death went uncompensated by the courts (p. 172).
- **Arrest in the streets:** fighting the Prefectural Guard brings 3d6 Watch in 1d4 rounds; charges of assault, mayhem and/or murder (p. 61).
- **Undercity treasure** owes half to the treasury (above).
