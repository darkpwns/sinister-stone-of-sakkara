# Lake District

District of [[Cyfaraun]] (hub [[AX3 Hub]]) holding all of **Star Lake** (Nymfë Zeo, #71). The Cesta Tirenium (Capital Street) narrowly separates it from the [[Old District]] to the west; the Old Walls divide it from the [[Palace District]] and [[Temple District]] to the east, with the Lake Gate (#70) into the Temple District. The richest families of the city line the lake, Ambador and Drakomir among them; their servants and retainers live in the tenements at the district's southern tip, and under those tenements, in the Undercity, a **vagrant colony** (#75u) has grown up in recent years — with a **death cult of Dirgion** hidden inside it. AX3 p. 178.

- **Who runs it:** the patrician houses, above all the rival councilors **Arjenus Ionicus Drakomir** (#72) and **Valens Icus Ambador** (#73). Councilor for the district: Drakomir himself.
- **Watch:** 10 officers at the Lake Gate (#70); district subaltern **Gundus Balicato** takes generous "donations" from the patrician families to overlook their misdeeds: **the Watch here always sides with the patricians** in any incident (see special encounters 3, 6, 7: 4d6 officers arrest adventurers in 1d10 rounds). Household guards everywhere. City Watch statline: [[Organizations of Cyfaraun]].
- **Streets:** lakeside villas, gardens and pleasure barges; the servants' tenements to the south; below them the vagrant colony in excavated elven ruins. Street encounters: [[Streets of Cyfaraun]]; a "Special!" result uses this district's table below.

> [!player] Arriving in the Lake District
> A warm, sparkling lagoon, Star Lake, stretches 600’ through the district, its waters fizzing faintly from mineral springs; gondolas, sailboats and pleasure barges drift about a little wooded islet of lilac and plum blossom where patricians picnic. Huge villas crowd the shore: one of red-and-black marble roofed in red brass, another of golden marble with a crimson flag showing a golden lion. At the north-east end, the two-storey portico of the Prefectural Palace. The Watch here is polite to the rich and quick with everyone else. In the south, the servants' tenements; beggars say there is a whole town of the destitute in the old tunnels beneath.

## Points of interest

### 70. Pylos Nymfë — Lake Gate (AX3 p. 178)
Gate between the Lake and Temple Districts: iron portcullis, iron-bound oak doors painted with a fresco of the lake, two 20’ tall, 30’ square white-stuccoed concrete gatehouses (1,250 SHP, Unit Capacity 1 each). Open sunup to sundown; at night only for a reasonable justification and a Friendly reaction roll. 10 officers: 3 on patrol, 2 at the gate, 5 off duty in the barracks. Subaltern **Gundus Balicato** is bought by the patrician families.

### 71. Nymfë Zeo — Star Lake (AX3 p. 178)
Elven-era artificial lagoon, 600’ long, 200’ wide, 30’ deep, fed by warm mineral springs (pleasantly warm, effervescent). The north-east shore laps the two-storey portico of the Prefectural Palace (#78, [[Palace District]]); villas of the great families line the rest, including Ambador (#73) and Drakomir (#72). In the centre the wooded islet **Nymfënasos** ("Lake Isle"), purple lilac and white-flowered plum, a patrician picnic spot. At any time 1d10 gondolas, 1d10 sailboats and 1d10 pleasure barges on the water.

### 72. Drakomir Villa (AX3 p. 178)
Contemporary, assertive, opulent: concrete faced with black-speckled red marble, roof plated in red brass. The courtyard holds much marble statuary, the largest a black marble **segreant dragon** on a red-brass stand; hypocaust-heated floors with mosaics of the Drakomir ancestors' deeds.
- **Household:** Councilor **Arjenus Ionicus Drakomir**; his ambitious wife **Tara Drandanna Drakomir**; cousin **Gundus Azeo Drakomir**; first son **Tribune Narmirio Drakomir** (often barracked at City Watch Headquarters); second son **Malynurio Drakomir**; daughter **Adara Drakomir**; third son, the paladin **Tavis Drakomir** (see the Doppelgänger Den, Special Location #14u, [[Old District]]). Staff: a cook, 2 scullions, 3 maidservants, **10 household guards** under the Jutlandic mercenary **Chief Osvald Ulfreksson**.
- **Who is home:** by day Arjenus 25% (clients, household business); Tara, Malynurio and Adara 75%. At night Arjenus, Tara, Malynurio and Adara 95%. Narmirio 5% day or night. **6 guards** always present, **3 on duty**; Ulfreksson and the other guards go with the Councilor.

### 73. Ambador Villa (AX3 p. 178)
Classical, harmonious, understated: walls of polished golden marble, sloping red-marble tiled roof, the Ambador banner (golden lion rampant on crimson) on the roof-peak. Classical plan round an inner courtyard with reflecting pool and garden; hypocaust floors with mosaics of Empyrean myth.
- **Household:** Councilor **Valens Icus Ambador**; wife **Aria Nuvilyn Ambador**; elderly mother **Oma Zelluin Ambador**; younger brother **Aurëus Ambador** (the Regent, who lives mostly at the Tower of Knowledge, [[Tower District]], [[Aureus Ambador (AX3)]]); second son **Jonus Ambador**; daughter, the bladedancer **Zeodarë Ambador** (see the Doppelgänger Den, #14u, [[Old District]]). Staff: a cook, 2 scullions, 4 maidservants, **8 household guards** under retired centurion **Unibelos Colchas**.
- **Who is home:** by day Valens 25%; Aria, Oma and Jonus 75%. At night Valens, Aria, Oma and Jonus 95%. Regent Aurëus 5% day or night. **4 guards** always present, **2 on duty**; Colchas and the others go with the Councilor.

### 74. Imperial Charter Inn (AX3 p. 179)
Luxury inn for councilors, guildmasters, magistrates, merchants, patricians and visiting dignitaries (special encounters call it the "Noble Charter Inn"). Common room: 12 small tables, 3 large, 3 private booths.
- **Prices:** food 1gp (appetiser) to 10gp (five-course feast); drink 2sp a mug of Dwarf-brewed beer, 1gp a glass of good local wine, 5gp-100gp a bottle of fine Northern Argollëan wine. 12 private rooms at **2gp/night**, private bath **+1gp**. Stables: 26 stalls at **1gp per horse per night**. Carriage house: 4 wagon berths at **2gp/night**.
- **People:** by day 1d3 tavernworkers serve 3d6+2 patrons, 1d4 prostitutes working the room; evenings 3 tavernworkers, 2d4 prostitutes, 6d6+2 patrons. Companionship **1gp an hour, 5gp a night** (often sacred courtesans trained at the Temple of the Veil). Nightly entertainment (use the table for The Mink, [[Old District]]). Patrons' occupations: **1d100+25** on the General/Street column of the Random Occupant by Building Type table (AX3 p. 52; 101+ counts as 100): almost no labourers, about 25% patricians ([[Buildings of Cyfaraun]]).

### 75U. Vagrant Colony and Death Cult Hideout (AX3 p. 179)
In the Undercity near the Temple of the Hearth Fire (#58) and its almshouse (#59) ([[Temple District]]), a colony of the homeless poor. The priestesses of Mityara help them as they can, but walled them off from the Catacombs of the Ancestors (#67) to keep beggars out of the sacred tombs. Unknown above, a **death cult of Dirgion** has settled inside the colony and plans a killing spree through the city. (AX3 sometimes calls it #73u.) Room by room: [[Death Cult Hideout]].

## Notable residents

### The Municipal Council (AX3 pp. 179-180)
Eight members, each nominally representing a district; by law drawn only from the city's **60 wealthiest patricians**, and run in their oligarchic interest. Long near-unanimous for **Prefect Basilio**; lately much less agreeable. Councilors need not live in their district (all live in the Lake or Palace districts).

### Councilor Arjenus Ionicus Drakomir (AX3 p. 180)
At the Drakomir Villa (#72) or council business.
- **Traits:** 50; aquiline nose, high brow, gaunt, receding hair swept back; ambitious, calculating, ruthless; holds an iron grip on his feelings and expects the same of his family.
- **Voice:** cool and exact, never raises his voice; lets silences do his threatening.
- **Statline:** [[Fighter]] 6; Str 10, Int 16, Wis 14, Dex 9, Con 11, Cha 13; MV 40’ / 120’ (20’ / 60’ in plate & shield); AC 0 (7 in plate & shield); hp 31; masterwork sword 6+, D 1d6+4 slashing; Save F6 (AX3 +1); ML +1; AL N; XP 320. Battlefield prowess (ACKS I fighter power). Proficiencies: Alertness, Command, Diplomacy, Intimidation, Leadership, Military Strategy 2. Battle gear: plate, shield. Always: masterwork sword, dagger, signet ring (550gp), red linen patrician's tunic and trousers with black stitching, high boots, belt, pouch with 50gp.
- **Wants:** Basilio replaced and the Borderlands under Drakomir control. His uncle, the **Exarch of Southern Argollë**, means to be acclaimed Tarkaun as soon as Tarkaun Valros Valuin's death in Somirea is confirmed. In a civil war his eldest son **Tribune Narmirio** brings the City Watch; the Tower of Knowledge (led by Ambador's brother) and the Prefectural Guard are obstacles; he is sounding out **Legate Jannion** about regime change.
- **Troubles:** his youngest, **Tavis**, defied him to become a paladin of the Winged Sun, and was seen leaving the city with **Zeodarë Ambador**; he is sure the Ambador bladedancer has ensnared the boy as a pawn against him. Fatima of the Seven Silks ([[Plaza District]]) is said to have sold him clients' secrets.

### Councilor Valens Icus Ambador (AX3 p. 180)
At the Ambador Villa (#73) or council business.
- **Traits:** 52, stern-faced, broad-shouldered, strong grip, belly softened by council sweetbreads; scrupulously lawful, will not tolerate treachery; flawed by overbearing pride that blinds him to risk and folly. House Ambador is famed for honour and loyalty; many Ambadors died for their Tarkauns.
- **Voice:** formal, sonorous, a touch pompous; answers suspicion with lineage.
- **Statline:** [[Fighter]] 6; Str 10, Int 13, Wis 10, Dex 10, Con 8, Cha 16; MV 40’ / 120’ (20’ / 60’ in plate & shield); AC 0 (7 in plate & shield); hp 31; masterwork sword 6+, D 1d6+4 slashing; Save F6; ML +1; AL N; XP 320. Battlefield prowess. Proficiencies: Alertness, Command, Diplomacy, Intimidation, Leadership, Military Strategy 2. Battle gear: plate, shield. Always: masterwork sword, signet ring (500gp), yellow linen tunic and trousers with crimson stitching, high boots, belt, pouch with 50gp.
- **Wants:** to keep the loyalist faction behind the Prefect; suspects House Drakomir's ambitions but has **no proof** (a party that brings it gains a powerful friend).
- **Fears:** his eldest son (in the Emperor's Talons in Somirea) may be dead; his daughter **Zeodarë**, whom he let become a bladedancer, is gossiped to have run off with Drakomir's youngest son; he blames Arjenus for a scheme to shame the house.

## Special locations
- **#75u Vagrant Colony and Death Cult Hideout** ([[Death Cult Hideout]], AX3 pp. 182-185, map p. 219): excavated elven ruins beside a sewer canal, home to hundreds of vagrants; the Temple's wall and the guarded Iron Gate to the Catacombs of the Ancestors (#67u, [[Temple District]]); the murdered seer Velus and his prophetic journal (and the deed to the Ionicus estate); behind a black door with a red skull, the death cult of Dirgion: **Malthusia the Diabolist** (Warlock 4), **Rhankur the Doombringer** (anti-paladin 3), 4 cultists and 7 enforcers, a bone altar that makes a shadowed sinkhole of evil, and a secret way out to the sewers.

## Hooks
- **Council politics:** Drakomir's plot to topple Basilio for his uncle the Exarch (with the Watch via Narmirio, maybe Legate Jannion); Ambador needs proof. Either councilor is a patron; both have family hostages to fortune (Tavis and Zeodarë at the Doppelgänger Den, #14u).
- **The death cult:** decapitated vagrants floating in the river near the southern wall (city rumour #1, AX3 pp. 44-45); the cult wants murder contracts from the Argollëan Family, Sand & Bones and private clients, to abduct the targets as sacrifices. Destroying it is evidence that earns Patriarch Arëtar's offices ([[Bridge District]]). Survivors hide at the Mink or the Silk Pony and hunt the party.
- **Velus's journal:** the vagrant prophet foresaw the party; returning his body and journal to the Grey Sisters can win the **Ionicus estate** beyond the Krysivor River (overrun by beastmen; near the Ruined Imperial Fortress, Gazetteer #40, or Judge's choice).
- **Patrician favours:** invitations, sponsorships for the games, loans of up to 10,000gp at 9% monthly interest, treasure maps for 50% of the plunder, or an assassination contract.
