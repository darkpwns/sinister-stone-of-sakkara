# Palace District

District of [[Cyfaraun]] (hub [[AX3 Hub]]). Bounded by the Old Walls on the west, the Imperial Walls on the east, the Cesta Tirenium ("Capital Street") on the north-east (the [[Tower District]] beyond) and the Cesta Vothë ("South Street") on the south. The **Capital Gate** (#76) leads out through the New Walls onto the road to Arganos; the **Patrician Gate** (#1) passes the Old Walls into the [[Old District]]. Seat of imperial power: the **Prefectural Palace** (#78), the **Prefectural Guard Barracks** (#81) and the **Imperial Courthouse** (#82), among the villas of wealthy patricians, bureaucrats and officers. The Palace's portico overlooks Star Lake (#71, [[Lake District]]). AX3 p. 187 (district map appendix p. 209).

- **Who runs it:** the Prefect's household — in practice, as the Prefect slides into madness, **Lady Syrena** and **Court Magist Nicodos Essir**; the **Prefectural Guard** under **Legate Gundus Jannion** holds the walls, gates and barracks. Patrician household guards police their own blocks.
- **Watch:** 10 City Watch officers at the Capital Gate (#76), 10 at Watchtower XVI (#77). District subaltern **Avaro Urcebas** knows his boss, **Tribune Narmirio Drakomir**, is ambitious, and keeps an ear open for anything that might interest him. City Watch statline: [[Organizations of Cyfaraun]]. Prefectural Guard: 60 within the Palace walls, 120 at each of the Prefectural Gate and Bastion, 360 in the Barracks (below).
- **Streets:** broad, clean streets of walled villas; litters, patrols and household guards who move vagrants on; floggings and pronouncements at the Palace. Street encounters: [[Streets of Cyfaraun]]; a "Special!" result uses this district's table below.

> [!player] Arriving in the Palace District
> Quiet, wide streets of walled villas and purple-cloaked guards. The white-marble Prefectural Palace looks out over Star Lake from a double colonnade crowned by a statue of the White Lady between great purple banners with the golden winged sun; walls, a gatehouse and a bastion ring it on three sides. Behind more walls lie the Prefectural Guard's barracks and stables. Everyone has heard the Prefect has not been himself since his riding accident. The Imperial Courthouse hears cases every day; lawyers solicit clients on its steps.

## Points of interest

### 76. Pylos Tirenium — Capital Gate (AX3 p. 187)
Gate onto the road to **Arganos**, capital of Southern Argollë and seat of its exarch. Iron portcullis and iron-reinforced oak doors painted with the winged sun between two 20’ tall, 30’ square gatehouses of white-stuccoed concrete (1,500 SHP, Unit Capacity 2 each). Open **sunup to sundown** only; at night the Watch opens it only for a reasonable justification **and** a Friendly reaction roll.
- **Tolls:** merchants from Arganos often enter here: toll **4d6+2gp per 5,000 st** of merchandise each time goods enter or leave; importers also pay customs duty of **2d10% of market price** ([[Doing Business in Cyfaraun]], AX3 p. 56).
- **Watch:** 10 officers: 3 on patrol, 2 at the gate, 5 off duty in the ground-storey barracks. Subaltern **Avaro Urcebas** feeds Tribune Drakomir anything of interest.

### 77. City Watchtower XVI (AX3 p. 187)
Guards the north-east approaches: four storeys, 40’ tall, 50’ diameter, white-stuccoed concrete (3,000 SHP, Unit Capacity 3), joined to the Imperial Walls at the second storey; **repeating light ballista** on a rotating platform under the terracotta roof. 10 officers: 4 on patrol, 1 on the ballista, 5 off duty.

### 78. Tirendoros Harbitan — Prefectural Palace (AX3 p. 187)
Very old: built by the elves when they re-founded the city after the Beastman Wars, of perfectly cut white marble. A two-storey portico runs across the south-west face over Star Lake: ten 20’ columns (3’ diameter) below, sixteen more organic, feminine 15’ columns (2’ diameter) above; on the entablature between them an **8’ statue of Demara, the White Lady** (elven cognate of Mityara), flanked by enormous purple banners with the gold winged sun.
- **Grounds:** the **Prefectural Walls** ring it on three sides, entered by the Prefectural Gate (#79) and the Prefectural Bastion (#80). A terraced garden of purple lilac and white-flowered plum trees lies between walls and palace; in the trees' shade about a dozen small buildings (barracks, shrines, stables, servants' quarters, storehouses) stand against the walls.
- **Residents:** Prefect **Justirius Tavicus Basilio**, his wife **Syrena Austiria Basilio**, their children, the household servants, and Court Magist **Nicodos Essir** (lives here to help with magical detection and defence). **60 Prefectural Guards** quartered within the walls: 30 on duty, 30 at ease.
- No interior key in AX3.

### 78U. Prefect's Dungeon (AX3 p. 187)
The elves who built the Palace dug beneath it and found ruins of the Ancient City preserved in volcanic ash; they restored them (pastoral frescoes, tiled floors) as a **museum to their ancestors**. The Aurans, somewhat barbarically, use it as a **prison** for the Empire's prisoners.
- **Prisoners:** **4d10** awaiting trial; about **65%** charged with capital crimes (murder, treason), **30%** serious felonies (assault, mayhem, racketeering, robbery), **5%** unlucky fools held on lesser charges (drunken outrage, gambling, vandalism, trespass): `Tables/ax3-prefect-dungeon-prisoners.yaml`.
- **Guards:** **10 Prefectural Guards** from the Palace keep order; the connection to the rest of the Undercity is **heavily guarded** ([[Undercity of Cyfaraun]]). No map or room key in AX3.
- **Hooks:** a jailbreak; a prisoner who knows something; the frescoed elven museum under the lock-up.

### 79. Pylos Harbitan — Prefectural Gate (AX3 p. 187)
The fortified east entrance to the Palace: iron portcullis, oak doors painted with the winged sun, one gatehouse **100’ long, 30’ wide, 20’ tall** with 5’ concrete walls (2,000 SHP, Unit Capacity 3).
- **Garrison:** **90 veteran heavy infantry and 30 composite bowmen** of the Prefectural Guard. At any time **54 infantry and 9 bowmen** are out at the watchtowers round the city; **23 infantry and 10 bowmen** on duty in the district; the rest (13 infantry, 11 bowmen) off duty.

### 80. Türos Harbitan — Prefectural Bastion (AX3 p. 188)
Concrete redoubt in the north-west corner of the Prefectural Walls, **100’ × 30’ × 20’** (2,000 SHP, Unit Capacity 3), joined to the walls at the second storey and commanding the portcullis and oak doors of the north-west entrance. Another **90 veteran heavy infantry and 30 composite bowmen**, deployed exactly as at #79.

### 81. Kyrdoros Tavusani Harbitan — Prefectural Guard Barracks (AX3 p. 188)
A walled compound of several buildings.
- **Cataphract Barracks:** 75’ × 50’ × 20’, white-stuccoed concrete (2,000 SHP, Unit Capacity 3): the **120 cataphracts**.
- **Barracks Towers** (north and south of it): two, each 30’ tall and 40’ square (1,875 SHP, Unit Capacity 1.5), each housing **90 men: 60 horse archers and 30 veteran heavy infantry**.
- **Stables:** five half-timbered stables between the Cataphract Barracks and the South Tower: **240 horses** for the cataphracts and horse archers and **60 in reserve**. The rest: officers' quarters, granaries, warehouses, workshops.
- **Deployment at any time:** out at the watchtowers: 18 veteran cataphracts, 18 cataphracts, 72 horse archers, 18 heavy infantry. On duty: 6 veteran cataphracts, 6 cataphracts, 9 horse archers, 21 infantry. The rest at ease.

### 81U. Barracks Vault (AX3 p. 188)
Under the barracks: two chambers totalling **65,000 cubic feet**, cut in the volcanic tuff, concrete-lined, entrances closed by iron portcullises. Holds the **Prefectural Guard's payroll** and grain, wine and other goods against a siege. Guarded at all times by **10 infantry** from the barracks. (Payroll amount not given: Judge's call.)

### 82. Rectandoros Tarkaunan — Imperial Courthouse (AX3 p. 189)
100’ × 25’ × 20’ white-stuccoed concrete round an inner courtyard with a peristyle. Hears every legal dispute between individuals: property and contract disputes and all delicts and felonies against private persons and property (adultery, assault, breach of contract, burglary, defamation, embezzlement, kidnapping, murder, rape, robbery, theft, trespass, vandalism and more).
- **Procedure:** no public prosecutors: a citizen accuses before one of the **seven magistrates**, who qualifies the claim (is it actionable?); if qualified, the defendant is brought to court, by force if necessary. Patrician magistrates preside; **citizen juries** decide guilt or liability. Jurors are paid **1sp a day** (so juries are poor or elderly). A patrician defendant who fears the jury may elect to be judged by the magistrate instead.
- **Daily:** **2d10 trials**: about 40% contract and property, 40% delicts, 20% felonies (`Tables/ax3-courthouse-trials.yaml`). Lawyers are not required, but **1d6+1 master lawyers, 2d6+1 licensed lawyers and 4d6+3 apprentice lawyers** are here all day representing clients or touting for work.
- **Hooks:** suing or being sued; hiring counsel after a brawl; an accusation as a weapon against a rival.

### Prefect Justirius Tavicus Basilio (AX3 p. 189)
Prefect of the Borderlands; in the Palace (wandering its halls at night), on its steps for pronouncements, in the streets with 12 guards. Bestiary: [[Justirius Tavicus Basilio (AX3)]].
- **Traits:** 44, the weary look of a man who has been to hell and back — he has. Six months ago he fell from his horse riding with his wife and broke his neck, dying instantly. Lady Syrena carried his body to her friend **Matriarch Aundëmë** of the Keepers of the Hearth Fire and, against grave warnings about **tampering with mortality**, insisted he be raised ([[Tampering with Mortality - Lawful]]). **Something came back with him**: constant whispers from beyond urge him to maim himself, murder his wife and children, burn the city he loves, and worse. Once an able, honest governor; now on the edge of madness, whispering back to the voices at night. He has thought of falling on his sword; fear of what waits in the Outer Darkness stops him.
- **Voice:** courteous and commanding, then a sudden pause mid-sentence, eyes sliding past you to listen to someone who is not there. *"Forgive me. You were saying — no. No, I will not."*
- **Statline:** Fighter 11; Str 14, Int 15, Wis 11, Dex 9, Con 12, Cha 16; MV 40’ / 120’ (20’ / 60’ in plate); AC 0, or 10 armed for battle (plate armour +1, shield +1, Fighting Style Specialization); hp 52; sword +1 1+, D 1d6+6 slashing, or lance 2+, D 1d10+5 (mounted); Save F11 (Paralysis 6+, Death 7+, Blast 8+, Implements 9+, Spells 10+); ML +2; AL L; XP 1,000. Special: Battlefield Prowess; **whispers from beyond: −2 to Listening throws, −2 to surprise rolls**. Proficiencies: Command, Diplomacy, Fighting Style Specialization (weapon and shield), Manual of Arms, Military Strategy 2, Riding. Battle gear: plate armour +1, shield +1, sword +1, dagger. Always: white tunic and trousers, purple cloak, high boots, belt, pouch with **100pp and 100gp**, house ring (**200gp**).
- **Wants:** silence. **Reward:** if he learns from the party of the curative powers of the **Shrine of the White Lady** (Borderlands Gazetteer #21), his gratitude is great: all the titles, lands and offices an Auran Prefect can give (Judge's discretion by PC level). Were he sane he would be the adventurers' best ally in the Borderlands.
- **Rumour:** "the Prefect has gone mad and hired assassins" is half true (mad, no assassins; rumour 3, `Tables/ax3-rumours.yaml`).

### Lady Syrena Austiria Basilio (AX3 p. 189)
The Prefect's wife; in the Palace, at the baths (5% late morning, [[Tower District]] #51) or carried through the streets on a litter with 6 guards.
- **Traits:** mid-30s, tall, slender, high cheekbones, grey eyes, long black hair; never trained in arms or magic but formidable, diplomatic and cunning. Fiercely loyal: she uses every charm and wile to keep up the pretence that the household is in order. **Trusts Nicodos Essir — wrongly.**
- **Voice:** warm, poised, faultlessly gracious; changes the subject with a smile. *"My husband is tired, nothing more. The Borderlands are a heavy charge."*
- **Statline:** 0-level human; Str 8, Int 16, Wis 13, Dex 9, Con 10, Cha 14; MV 40’ / 120’; AC 0; HD less than 1 (AX3 1-1), hp 3; dagger 12+, D 1d4−1; Save 0-level (+1); ML +1; AL L; XP 5. Proficiencies: Art (interior decorating), Diplomacy, Knowledge (history), Performance (singing), Profession (household management), Riding, Theology. Wears a white silk chiton, purple silk sash, high strapped sandals, a **headdress of golden chains with amethyst bangles (2,000gp)**, **golden arm-bangles (500gp)**; purse with 100gp.
- **Wants:** a cure. If the party rises to prominence in Cyfaraun she seeks them out and charges them to **find a cure for her husband**, offering whatever they desire (Judge's discretion).

### Court Magist Nicodos Essir (AX3 pp. 189-190)
The Prefect's court mage, resident in the Palace. Bestiary: [[Nicodos Essir (AX3)]].
- **Traits:** born in the ghettos of Aura, he vowed to join the great; admitted to the Tower of Knowledge despite his low birth for an uncanny untrained talent for contacting other spheres. Court magist to a Prefect is the finest post a mage can hold short of a Chapterhouse regency. 34, short and gaunt from a hungry youth; shaped goatee, manicured nails, shaven head, the finest robes.
- **Secret:** the Prefect's and Lady's trusted confidant, he knows exactly what ails Basilio and is **making it worse**: too much communion with the Outer Darkness has made him vicious; he has learned black magic and laid a **curse** on the Prefect that leaves his mind weak against the whispers. As the Prefect weakens, Nicodos takes more authority; soon he will rule the Borderlands in all but name. He will stop **any expedition to cure the Prefect** by whatever means he can. (The whispers themselves come from the raising; the curse aggravates them. AX3 gives no mechanics for lifting it: the Shrine of the White Lady cure, or the curse ending with Essir's death or being dispelled, is the Judge's call.)
- **Voice:** silken flattery to his betters, a clipped, cold aside to everyone else. *"The Prefect is in the gods' hands. And, in the meantime, in mine."*
- **Statline:** Mage 7; Str 10, Int 18, Wis 13, Dex 10, Con 11, Cha 17; MV 40’ / 120’; AC 0; hp 17; staff 8+ (with the staff of striking as ACKS II [[Staff of Smiting]], a staff +1: 7+, D 1d6+1, a charge adds 1d6 extraordinary bludgeoning; AX3 8+, 1d6), or spell; Save M7 (+1); ML +2; AL N; XP 440 (AX3 1,140). Proficiencies: Collegiate Wizardry, Diplomacy, Knowledge (history), Military Strategy, Sensing Power, Siege Engineering, Soothsaying. Spells per day 3/2/2/1. Repertoire: 1st [[Beguile Humanoid]] (charm person), [[Illumination]], [[Mage Missile]], [[Discern Gist]], protection from evil†, unseen servant†; 2nd [[Illumination, Perpetual]] (continual light; ACKS II 3rd), detect evil†, ESP†, knock†, [[Magic Lock]]; 3rd [[Dispel Magic]], [[Fireball]], [[Halt Humanoids]] (hold person; ACKS II 2nd), [[Indiscernibility]] (ACKS II 4th), [[Telepathy]] (ACKS II 4th); 4th bestow curse†, [[Scry]], summon fantastic creature†. Gear: staff of striking (9 charges), finely made blue diviner's cassock, blue leather sandals and belt, pouch with 50gp.
- **Hooks:** Legate Jannion wants proof against him; Lady Syrena trusts him; a cure expedition makes him the party's enemy.

### Legate Gundus Zelicus Jannion and the Prefectural Guard (AX3 p. 190)
Commander of the Prefectural Guard; at the Barracks (#81), the Palace, or in the streets with 2 centurions. Bestiary: [[Gundus Jannion (AX3)]].
- **Traits:** 34, Tirenean, curly black hair, rippling muscles, a broken nose that roughens patrician features. The Prefect's old comrade: border skirmishes in Opelenea as young officers, junior commanders against the pirate raids on Corcano in the 370s. Troubled by his friend's madness; **suspects Essir** of laying a spell on him and has vowed to find proof before things get worse. Time is short: **Councilor Drakomir** has already asked him whether the Prefect should step down for the city's good ([[Lake District]]).
- **Voice:** blunt soldier's speech, few words, a hard stare while he decides whether you are useful. *"Bring me proof. Not talk — proof."*
- **Statline:** Fighter 7; Str 17, Int 11, Wis 13, Dex 13, Con 14, Cha 13; MV 20’ / 60’; AC 11 (masterwork plate +1 AC, shield +1, Fighting Style Specialization, DEX; AX3 10); hp 45; spear +1 3+ or masterwork sword 3+, D 1d6+6; Save F7 (+1, as AX3); ML +1; AL L; XP 440 (AX3 790). Gear: masterwork plate armour, shield +1, spear +1, masterwork sword, dagger, purple prefectural guard cloak, high sandals, pouch with 50gp.
- **Wants:** proof against Essir; his friend restored; the city kept from Drakomir. A natural patron for a party investigating the magist.
- **The Prefectural Guard:** keeps the Prefect safe and the Prefecture sovereign; in war the vanguard of his legions; in peace patrols the roads round Cyfaraun and mans the **18 watchtowers** along them. **60 veteran cataphracts, 60 cataphracts, 120 horse archers, 120 composite bowmen, 240 veteran heavy infantry.** Each unit has a 5th level commander and four 3rd level lieutenants, with about 3-5 2nd level warriors in the ranks. Each road watchtower has stables and holds **1d3 cataphracts, 2d3 horse archers, 1d3 composite bowmen and 3d3 veteran heavy infantry**; the rest — **42 veteran cataphracts, 42 cataphracts, 48 horse archers, 84 composite bowmen, 132 veteran heavy infantry** — garrison the Palace District. ## Hooks
- **Cure the Prefect:** Lady Syrena's commission (whatever they desire) and the Prefect's gratitude (titles, lands, offices) for the Shrine of the White Lady (Borderlands Gazetteer #21) — opposed by Nicodos Essir by any means.
- **Unmask the magist:** Legate Jannion wants proof of Essir's black magic; Lady Syrena trusts him; the Tower of Knowledge would want to know of a member's death magic ([[Tower District]]).
- **The coup:** Councilor Drakomir sounds out Jannion about removing the Prefect; Tribune Drakomir's subaltern watches the district ([[Lake District]], [[Organizations of Cyfaraun]]).
- **Justice:** the Courthouse (sue, defend, hire lawyers), the Prefect's Dungeon (prisoners, a jailbreak, the elven museum below), the Barracks Vault (the Guard's payroll).
- **Syndicate business:** rescue Tarcalus for the Argollëan Family (200gp and Liber Faunus); Lazareo Ravil hunting someone the party knows.
- **Pronouncements:** the Silver Spear for the [[Imperial Vanguard]], free baths, games for the Prefect's father, and the ban on restore life and limb all ripple through the city.
