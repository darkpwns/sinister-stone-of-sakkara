# Old District

District of [[Cyfaraun]] (hub [[AX3 Hub]]): the most densely populated, covering most of Old Cyfaraun. Bounded by the Cesta Mirmen Gollë (East Blackrock Street) on the [[Bridge District]] side and the Cesta Tirenium (Capital Street) on the [[Lake District]] side; the Old Walls to the north-east part it from the [[Tower District]] and [[Palace District]], crossed at the Patrician's Gate (#1). Landmarks: City Watch HQ (#5), the Emporium (#7), the Merchant Guildhouse (#8), the Thieves' Quarter (#9), the Mercenary Guildhouse (#10) and The Mink (#11). AX3 p. 69.

- **People:** craftsmen, labourers, shopkeepers, taverners and traders; harlots, thugs and vagabonds.
- **Who runs it:** openly the Merchant Guild (the Emporium) and the City Watch HQ; in fact the **Argollëan Family** (Liber Faunus) works its protection racket and smuggling ring from here, with the Guild's master bought and Watchtower III on its payroll.
- **Watch:** 15 officers at the Patrician's Gate, 5 at Watchtower I, 10 at Watchtower II, 5 at Watchtower III, and the Watch HQ (Tribune, subalterns, 95 elite officers). City Watch statline: [[Organizations of Cyfaraun]].
- **Streets:** the vast Emporium plaza and its shops; native merchants' homes; the rotten timber labyrinth of the Thieves' Quarter. Street encounters: [[Streets of Cyfaraun]]; a "Special!" result uses this district's table below. Under it lies the busiest part of the Undercity: encounter throws there succeed on **5+** instead of 6+ ([[Undercity of Cyfaraun]]).
- **Under the district:** The Mink's Family hideout (11u), the leaking cesspool that opens on the Nethercity (12u), the Shrine of the Horned Rat (13u), the Doppelgänger Den (14u).

> [!player] Arriving in the Old District
> The old heart of the city, packed shoulder to shoulder: workshops, taverns, shops and tenements, and at its centre the Emporium, a plaza big enough to swallow a village, crowded six days a week with tents, wagons and hawkers. North of it the Merchant Guildhouse overlooks Memorial Street; south-west the white Mercenary Guildhouse posts its contracts. Beyond the guildhouse the timber alleys of the Thieves' Quarter are dark even at noon, and sensible folk keep out. Everyone knows The Mink, the city's most famous tavern, where the adventurers drink. Notices at the Mercenary Guildhouse seek explorers for the Waste (Tower of Knowledge), live-capture monster hunters (ask Liber Faunus at The Mink), and squires for the Imperial Vanguard (leave word with Guildmaster Brogitarius).

## Points of interest

### 1. Pylos Aurigëus — Patrician's Gate (AX3 p. 69)
The splendid elven-era gate on Capital Street between the Old District and the Tower and Palace Districts: bronze portcullis with a vine motif, bronze-reinforced cedar doors carved with pastoral scenes, between two 20’ gatehouses of perfectly cut white limestone (**1,250 SHP, Unit Capacity 1** each). Elven enchantments keep the bronze from tarnishing.
- **Open sunup to sundown.** Patricians and Imperial officials pass at any hour; anyone else at night needs a reasonable justification **and** a Friendly reaction roll.
- **Watch:** 15 officers: 4 on patrol, 4 at the gate, 7 off duty in the ground-floor barracks.

### 2. City Watchtower I (AX3 p. 69)
Two-storey, 20’ tower on a 25’ teardrop-shaped base, white limestone (**850 SHP, Unit Capacity 0.5**), terracotta roof; joins the Old Walls at its second storey. 5 officers: 1 in the tower, 2 on patrol, 2 off duty.

### 3. City Watchtower II (AX3 p. 69)
Four-storey, 40’ tall, 50’ square, white limestone (**3,000 SHP, Unit Capacity 3**); joins the Old Walls at its second storey. A **repeating light ballista** on a rotating platform under the roof commands the district and the Imperial Wharf (#4). 10 officers: 4 on patrol, 1 on the ballista, 5 off duty.

### 4. Imperial Wharf (AX3 p. 69)
Reserved for government and military passengers and cargo. Four piers into the Mirmen, moorage for 14. Usually berthed: **1d4+1 barges, 1d4+1 river boats, 1d4 sailing boats**; by day **4d6** sailors, rowers and dockworkers at work.

### 5. City Watch Headquarters (AX3 p. 69)
Imposing two-storey building where the Imperial Walls (south) meet the Old Walls (east), over the Cesta Türaduaran (Street of the Mighty Dawn): 100’ × 60’ × 20’, concrete with fired-brick veneer, terracotta roof (**3,000 SHP, Unit Capacity 5**). Seat of **Tribune Narmirio Drakomir** with **4 subalterns** and **95 officers: 35 veteran heavy infantry, 40 heavy infantry, 20 composite bowmen**, an elite reserve to disperse crowds, put down riots and hunt violent fugitives. (AX3 p. 76 says three 3rd level subalterns under the Tribune, plus 3-5 2nd level officers per platoon: the counts differ.)
- Rewards for evidence from the [[Doppelganger Den]]: see Value of information.

### 6. City Watchtower III (AX3 p. 69)
As Watchtower I, rebuilt after the Argollëan War in Auran concrete faced with white limestone (**850 SHP, Unit Capacity 0.5**); joins the Imperial Walls at its second storey. 5 officers: 1 in the tower, 2 on patrol, 2 off duty. **All on the Argollëan Family's payroll** (to keep Imperial entanglements away).

### 7. Emporium (AX3 pp. 69, 78-81)
The largest plaza in Cyfaraun (**600’ × 400’**), heart of its commerce: fine shops round the edge, an open-air market in the middle six days a week, sunup to sundown, the most crowded spot in the city. **Any equipment and any common merchandise** can be bought or sold here; precious merchandise goes through the Merchant Guildhouse (#8). Market rules: [[Doing Business in Cyfaraun]], [[Cyfaraun (Market)]].
- **Merchants:** befriended with a Friendly reaction they give city rumours, introductions, information and extra goods. Unless noted, all are noncombatant 0-level ([[Commoner]]: AC 0, hp 3, ML -1) with Bargaining 1 and Profession (merchant) 3 or similar. "Loads" are merchandise loads at market price; "befriended" = Friendly reaction.
- **Central plaza (10):** 1d3+1 interesting travelling merchants on any day: Judge's choice or `Tables/ax3-old-emporium-vendor.yaml`.

### 8. Merchant Guildhouse (AX3 pp. 70-71)
At the Emporium's north edge on the Cesta Thymatas (Memorial Street): HQ of the Merchant Guild, which licenses merchants, runs the Emporium, brokers deals and banks. Guildmaster **Apollonis Meteros** is paid well to ignore the Argollëan Family.
- **Trading hall:** precious merchandise bought and sold; passengers and shipping contracts solicited ([[Doing Business in Cyfaraun]]).
- **Licensing:** a store with staff pays **10% of earnings a year**; sole proprietors need no licence, but unlicensed businesses are thought disreputable and low-class.
- **Bank:** domain rulers are clients automatically; others need a Friendly reaction (**+1 venturer, +1 per rank of Bargaining**). Storage **0.25%/month**, withdrawal on demand, bills of exchange to pay guild merchants. Deposits lent to the bank: **30-day terms at 0.25%**. Loans up to **10,000gp/month**; adventuring expeditions pay **3%/month with collateral, 9% without**. Currency exchange **0.25%**. Investments safe to perilous, up to **10,000gp/month** on offer. Vault: **16 talents gold, 137 talents silver, 158 talents copper (187,680gp, 1,866 st)**.
- Evidence of the fates of Huranos, Ashera and Vason, or merchant survivors, earns rewards (Value of information).

### 9. Thieves' Quarter (AX3 p. 71)
The most rundown, crime-ridden part of the city: rotting timber buildings with patched walls, crammed together, the labyrinthine alleys barely lit. Run by the Argollëan Family; its predations and the Watch's sporadic violent raids make loitering dangerous.
- **Encounters** here on **5+ on 1d6 by day, 4+ by night**; reaction rolls in the alleys **-2**.

### 10. Mercenary Guildhouse (AX3 p. 71)
White limestone at the Thieves' Quarter's south edge on the Cesta Türaduaran, once a temple of **Áine**, elven goddess of love and war (cognate with Ianna); oddly free of graffiti and refuse. Bronze-bound cedar doors open on a training hall with a bronze winged bladedancer statue, ringed by barracks and offices. A consortium of sellswords licensed by the Empire for private military service in the Borderlands; Guildmaster **Balcadur Brogitarius** is recruiting hard, expecting good business.
- **Membership:** any able-bodied human or demi-human with **STR 9+**; dues **12gp** on joining and yearly. Mutual aid paid to the member or his family: **100gp** death, **40gp** capture (ransom), **40gp** lost arm, **30gp** lost leg, **10gp** lost eye, **5gp** lost finger, toe or ear. Lodging **1sp a night**; contract board. Members and non-members may post contracts for henchmen, mercenaries and specialists.
- **Posted contracts:** (1) the Tower of Knowledge seeks expeditionaries to explore the Waste beyond the border forts and report threats and invasion routes: see Regent **Aurëus Ambador** at the Chapterhouse (AX3 p. 145, [[Tower District]]); (2) monster hunters able to take dangerous prey alive and unharmed: contact **Liber Faunus at The Mink** for an introduction to the client (AX3 p. 81); (3) the **[[Imperial Vanguard]]** seeks shieldbearers, squires and retainers: leave word with Brogitarius (AX3 p. 196).

### 11. The Mink (AX3 p. 71)
The city's most storied tavern, between the Cesta Türaduaran and the Emporium among native merchants' homes; owned by Liber Faunus, secretly the Argollëan Family's head, whose hideout is in the basement (11u). Room by room: [[The Mink - Tavern]].

### 11U. Argollëan Family Hideout (AX3 pp. 85-88)
Under The Mink, in excavated ruins of Ancient Cyfaraun. Room by room: [[The Mink - Family Hideout]].

### 12. Public Latrine (AX3 p. 71)
Huge concrete facility serving the Emporium: an antechamber with entrances north, west and south screens the patrons; **34 toilets** on cedar benches drain into the city's largest cesspool (12u); sponges on sticks in the moats before the benches. **5d6 patrons** at any time; reactions with them **-2** (no one likes being bothered on the toilet). No man-sized route down to the cesspool.

### 12U. Leaking Cesspool (AX3 p. 73)
30’ under the latrines at the centre of the Old City; walls and floor coated with muck, mould and filth. Wiping the walls reveals ruined frescoes of a procession of white-gowned elven maidens with torches; a **Loremastery or Theology throw** identifies the worship of **Mityara** (the elves' Demara, their patron).
- Archways NW and NE lead into large sewer tunnels, which carry less than they should: much of the sludge pours into a **door-sized hole in the floor**. Caving, Engineering or Mapping: the pressure of years of sewage burst the cesspool open.
- Below the hole the slow stream of refuse runs into a tunnel and down winding rough-hewn steps: the **entrance to the Nethercity**, see SOTN (separate module notes).
- **Carcass scavenger** in the tunnel below, eating animal waste and the odd corpse; unseen from the cesspool; **+1 to surprise** adventurers coming down the steps → [[Carrion Horror]]: MV 40’/120’, AC 2, HD 3+1***, hp 14, 8 stingers 7+, 1 piercing + paralysis (Paralysis save or paralysed 2d4 turns), save F2, ML +2, AL N, XP 170 (MM; AX3 prints 135).
- **Access:** only through the Undercity (a sewer grate or a basement elsewhere, then the sewers). It sometimes climbs the pipes to the latrine (special encounter 5).

### 13U. Shrine of the Horned Rat (AX3 p. 73)
Anyone who willingly prays at its altar on the night of the full moon becomes a wererat. The Provost **Mentenus** of the Tower of Knowledge rediscovered it and made himself and his cohort wererats to search the sewers for the Nethercity. See Special locations.

### 14U. Doppelgänger Den (AX3 p. 73)
The Brotherhood of Knives, an assassins' guild founded five years ago in the Undercity just north of the Family's territory, was killed off a year ago and replaced by doppelgängers, who use the cover to kill and replace notable people. See Special locations.

## Notable residents

### The Argollëan Family (AX3 pp. 73-76)
The Borderlands' largest criminal syndicate: blackmail, stolen goods, looted elven antiquities, and a protection racket on local merchants (those who don't pay see their goods "donated to the poor" or have accidents on their way to the Watch). Compliant merchants are smuggled past tolls and customs for a **10% fee**.
- **Elven relics:** adventurers dodging Imperial taxes can sell finds to the Family for **65% of full value**, **up to 80%** for friends of Liber Faunus; the Family fences them for 100%+ to dealers, rich clients' agents and Auran nobles. It will neither offer nor provide illicit services to obviously Lawful characters (paladins, bladedancers, clerics, priestesses).
- **Rivals:** losing ground to the **Sand & Bones Guild** (Selene, The Silk Pony), which treats flesh and gambling as raw commodities; ruins are picked clean, finds are rarer. Blood has been shed, lately in the sewers near the Empyrean Temple's catacombs, but no open war yet. The Nethercity lies in Family territory: Liber will see it as the key to beating Sand & Bones. Fencing through the Family makes a friend; bypassing it may start a **subterranean war in the sewers**.
- **Members (226):** assassins are enforcers and racketeers (Fighting Style Specialization, Sniping; poisoned blades on jobs); bards entertain at The Mink and other Family houses, some as courtesans softening clients (Eavesdropping, Seduction, some Disguise); venturers run the legitimate front (Emporium trade, caravans, provisioning The Mink; Arcane Dabbling or Magical Engineering); thieves are the smuggling backbone as caravaneers, dockworkers, bargemen and labourers and back the racket (Skulking; Gambling or Labor). Beggars, carousers, derelicts, harlots and vagabonds are "affiliated" as eyes, ears and labour. They fight with short swords and daggers or crossbows. Statlines: [[The Mink - Family Hideout]].
- **War footing:** Liber can add **80 mercenaries** (Fighter 1, normally his caravan guards), only as a last resort (lost profits, exposure). Triggers (Judge's call): attacks on Aranth or Hesta, murder of a senior (5th+ level) member, a run of major rival hijinks in the Thieves' Quarter, or an uncooperative party looting elven ruins near the district.

### Liber Faunus (AX3 p. 74)
Owner of The Mink and head of the Family → [[Liber Faunus (AX3)]]. Found at The Mink's office (7) or the hideout (6, 7).
- **Traits:** handsome, lean, ageless, faintly pointed ears, groomed goatee; descended (family legend) from an Auran legionary of the conquest and his elven war-bride, raised on Argollëan tradition; made his fortune stripping forgotten elven ruins with clues from family tales, then found the Viaspen Forest declared an imperial holding and half his proceeds taxed, and built a syndicate to never pay that again.
- **Voice:** soft, amused, courteous; a merchant prince who never raises his voice. "Blood is bad for business, friend. Let us find a price instead."
- **Statline:** Venturer 10, AC 0 (2), hp 43, elven short sword +1 4+ D 1d6+2, crossbow 6+, ML +2, XP 850.
- **Holdings:** The Mink; 5 medium bawdyhouses, 5 large and 2 medium and 14 small cantinas, 4 large taverns in the district; via his venture company 2 wharves at the Docks (#33), 5 barges on the Siadanos-Cyfaraun run, a caravan of 40 large wagons on the Cyfaraun-Arganos route. Profit about **7,000gp/month** (500 The Mink, 1,000 other houses, 5,500 venture company) plus the syndicate's 12,065gp.
- **Wants:** to avoid violence, corrupt through The Mink's pleasures, control every elven antiquity entering the city; the Nethercity.
- **Knows:** everyone's secrets (blackmail files); uses his medallion of ESP on suspected traitors; orders murders of troublesome officials, ex-clients with consciences and adventurers who sell antiquities past him. Contracted the Brotherhood of Knives (the doppelgängers) against Huranos, Ashera, Vason and Bodilkas; had the seller of the elven bracers of armour killed (Mentenus's notes).
- **Hooks:** the live-capture monster contract; buying the party's relics; the blackmail files; the eyes of the eagle and *Book of Nine Arrows* (AX4 lead).

### Hesta Faunus (AX3 pp. 74-75)
Liber's younger sister → [[Hesta Faunus (AX3)]]. Found at The Mink (snug 4 70% with 10+ patrons).
- **Traits:** platinum hair, silver eyes, an otherworldly allure; almost everyone falls in love with her. Impresario of The Mink's entertainment and madame of its brothel; privately the Family's spy and seductress.
- **Voice:** warm, attentive, laughing, making each listener feel the only one. "Tell me everything. I will keep it safe."
- **Statline:** Bard 7, AC 1 (5 armed), hp 32, hand axe +1 5+ D 1d6+4, ML +2, XP 440.
- **Wants / knows:** favours and secrets for the Family; 325gp an evening. **Hooks:** Megaravicos murders patrons she favours; Brogitarius pines for her.

### Aranth Faunus (AX3 p. 75)
Liber's younger brother, manager of The Mink and his partner in the venture company → [[Aranth Faunus (AX3)]]. Behind the bar 70% of evenings.
- **Traits:** ex-adventurer, elven ears, unkempt black hair; lives vicariously through the tomb-raiders' tales.
- **Voice:** eager, nosy, pours another round to hear the end of a story. "And then? Don't stop there!"
- **Statline:** Venturer 6, AC 0 (3), hp 22, short sword 7+ D 1d6+1, XP 320.
- **Hook:** the easiest Faunus to talk to; what the party tells him reaches Liber.

### Megaravicos Eight-Fingers (AX3 p. 75)

- **Statline:** Thief 6, AC 3 (6), hp 23, short sword and dagger 6+ D 1d6+1, XP 320.

## Hooks
- **Fence or fight:** the Family pays 65-80% for elven relics; selling elsewhere (Damanos tells them) or delving the Nethercity under its streets without cutting Liber in brings racketeers, then assassins, then a sewer war.
- **The monster contract:** Liber brokers a client who wants dangerous beasts taken alive (Mercenary Guild notice).
- **The vanishing merchants:** shuttered shops, Lazar's and Bassida's worries, Vason's night sales by the river lead to the Den; the ledger then puts the party between Liber, Mentenus, Selene and the Temple of Justice.
- **Tavis Drakomir and Zeodarë Ambador:** two noble heirs, possibly captives in the Den; their families would be grateful and their affair is a scandal.
- **The Horned Rat:** a full-moon altar that makes wererats; the Tower of Knowledge's Provost leads the cult; the Solar Citadel and the Underwatch's memory want it ended.
- **The blackmail files** (Hideout 6): 24,000gp to Sand & Bones, or leverage over half the city.
- **The Nethercity** below the leaking cesspool (12u): see SOTN (separate module notes).
- **AX4 leads:** the orichalcum armband (Aedon Lann and the Bow of the Great Eagles), *The Book of Nine Arrows*, Malethor's quest.
