# Plaza District

District of [[Cyfaraun]] (hub [[AX3 Hub]]), named for its many arcades and piazzas: mostly commercial, specialised markets that take pressure off the Emporium. The Imperial Walls bound it west and south, from the West Gate (#37) to the Plaza Gate (#43) and on to the River Gate South (#34, [[Bridge District]]). The Cesta Gundan (Street of Wolves) and Cesta Nuviën (Street of Roses) divide it from the [[Festival District]] to the north-east; the old line of the Old Walls once divided it from the Bridge District, but the last census moved two blocks of homes across. AX3 p. 137.

- **Who runs it:** commerce: Merchant Guildmaster **Apollonis Meteros** owns the Warehouse Block (#38) and is protected by both syndicates; imperial monuments (#39, #40) are kept by the Prefectural Guard. Councilor for the district: Nicus Celdor Lazareo (Drakomir faction; see [[Lake District]]).
- **Watch:** 10 officers at the West Gate (#37), 5 at Watchtower IX (#41), 15 at the Plaza Gate (#43); district subaltern **Viktir Korbis** (based at #37) keeps them well disciplined: **−1 to reaction rolls for bribery** here. A pair of Prefectural Guards always walks the Colonnade (#40). City Watch statline: [[Organizations of Cyfaraun]].
- **Streets:** caravan routes from Kavala (west) and Siadanos (south), plazas, shops and townhouses, the fish market by the river, warehouses. A crocodile has been living in the sewer under the public latrine (#45u). Street encounters: [[Streets of Cyfaraun]]; a "Special!" result uses this district's table below.

> [!player] Arriving in the Plaza District
> Arcades and piazzas full of stalls; wagons from Kavala and Siadanos grinding toward the warehouses. A red granite obelisk carved with the names of fallen legionaries towers over one plaza, and a long white colonnade leads to a gold-and-ivory statue of the conqueror Tarkaun Mennic II, paced by two guards in full panoply. Near the river the fish market stinks; nearby a big concrete public latrine does a steady trade. In a little courtyard, a row of small shrines to petty gods draws the common folk. The Watch at Watchtower IX shrug off talk of "a dragon in the sewers".

## Points of interest

### 37. Pylos Tellë — West Gate (AX3 p. 137)
Gate on the Cesta Thymatas (Memorial Street): iron portcullis, iron-bound oak doors painted with the legionary eagle, two 20’ tall, 30’ square white-stuccoed concrete gatehouses (1,500 SHP, Unit Capacity 2 each). Open sunup to sundown; at night only for a reasonable justification and a Friendly reaction roll. Kavala's merchants usually enter here.
- **Tolls:** **4d6+2gp per 5,000 st** of merchandise on every entry or exit with goods; importers also pay customs duty of **2d10% of market price** ([[Doing Business in Cyfaraun]], AX3 p. 56).
- **Watch:** 10 officers: 3 on patrol, 2 at the gate, 5 off duty in the barracks. Subaltern **Viktir Korbis** is based here; bribes suffer **−1** on the reaction roll.

### 38. Warehouse Block (AX3 p. 137)
A whole block owned by Merchant Guildmaster **Apollonis Meteros** (AX3 p. 76): 2 huge depots (second-storey lofts to keep grain dry, sunken floors to keep wine cool), 4 large (2 with lofts, 2 with sunken floors), 7 medium and 6 small depots, the rest single-storey.
- **Rent:** to Merchant Guild members at **2sp per day per load**.
- **Stock:** on average **450 loads of common** and **25 loads of precious** merchandise.
- **People:** by day at least 1d6 depot workers; 50-100 when a big caravan arrives. Meteros lets **both** Sand & Bones and the Argollëan Family store contraband here, which keeps both syndicates off it; still, **25 mercenaries under two 3rd-level sergeants** guard the block at night.
- **Hooks:** contraband of both syndicates under one roof; Meteros as patron or target (special encounter 10).

### 39. Stelos Ulkyrusani — Obelisk of Heroes (AX3 p. 137)
A single piece of red Meniri granite, shipped by river, rising 65’ from the centre of a 50’ plaza; its gilded pyramidion is level with the Solar Citadel's oculus to the east. The four faces carry the names of the Auran Army's fallen legionaries.

### 40. Stelosi Telandrasan — Colonnade of Triumph (AX3 p. 137)
Built for the 50th anniversary of the capture of the city: a 150’ × 75’ plaza of polished grey flagstones, a colonnade of 35’ white marble pillars leading to a 50’ wide white marble shrine holding a heroic **chryselephantine statue of Tarkaun Mennic II**, conqueror of Cyfaraun. **2 Prefectural Guards** in full panoply always walk the colonnade in homage (spears by day, torches by night); they are relieved every four hours (special encounter 6). Prefectural Guard statline: [[Organizations of Cyfaraun]].

### 41. City Watchtower IX — Square, South-west (AX3 p. 137)
Two-storey, 20’ tall tower 25’ across, white-stuccoed concrete (850 SHP, Unit Capacity 0.5), joined to the Imperial Walls at its second storey. 5 officers: 1 manning the tower, 2 on patrol, 2 off duty. They have had complaints of **a dragon stalking the sewers** and do not take them seriously (it is the giant crocodile, #45u).

### 42. Courtyard of Nine Shrines (AX3 p. 137)
A small plaza inside a block of townhouses and shops, with a row of minor temples running south-west to north-east to demigods, heroes, nature spirits and petty gods of the common folk. By day 2d10 commoners at the shrines. Caretakers: **Fatima of the Seven Silks** and **Lubbo the Immaculate** (below). Festivals: Tarquellus (special encounter 4), Aulysëa (13).

### 43. Pylos Pumilos — Plaza Gate (AX3 p. 138)
Gate on the Cesta Pumilos (Plaza Street): iron portcullis, iron-bound oak doors painted with the winged sun, two 20’ tall, 50’ square gatehouses of white-stuccoed concrete (1,500 SHP, Unit Capacity 2 each). Same hours and night rule as #37; Siadanos merchants enter here; same tolls and duties. 15 officers: 4 on patrol, 4 at the gate, 7 off duty.

### 44. Fish Market (AX3 p. 138)
Open-air market on the west bank near the Docks (#33, [[Bridge District]]), a few hundred feet below the confluence of the Mirmen and the stream that drains Lake Laman. Every morning fishermen land the catch to fishmongers, who sell on to cantinas, inns, taverns and households. The fishy stench gives the surrounding buildings some of the lowest rents in the city. **Loads of preserved fish** can be bought and sold here.

### 45. Public Latrine (AX3 p. 138)
Large concrete facility used by the plazas' merchants and mongers; two archways on its south-east side. Inside, cedar benches with **20 toilets** draining to the cesspool below (#45u); a channel before the benches holds sponges on sticks. At any time 2d6 patrons; **−2 to reaction rolls** with them (no one likes being bothered on the toilet).

### 45U. Crocodile Nest (AX3 p. 138)
The cesspool under the latrine is now the nest of a **giant crocodile** imported by Celictor Nuvicus that escaped into the sewers; fresh human bones litter it. Room by room: [[Crocodile Nest]].

## Notable residents

### Fatima of the Seven Silks (AX3 p. 139)
Caretaker of the Courtyard of Nine Shrines (#42).
- **Traits:** 20, Shebatean beauty (dark olive skin, almond eyes); famed for her lascivious wit; former sacred courtesan and priestess of Ianna, **expelled** for passing clients' intimate secrets to a patrician — rumoured to be **Councilor Arjenus Ionicus Drakomir**. Now serves the petty gods, and is still a fountain of information.
- **Voice:** warm, teasing and quick, every remark a little double-edged; she trades a confidence for a compliment.
- **Statline:** [[Priestess]] 5; Str 9, Int 11, Wis 15, Dex 13, Con 10, Cha 16; MV 40’ / 120’; AC 1; hp 15; dagger 9+, D 1d4 piercing; Save C5 (priestess saves as crusader; AX3 +1); ML +2; AL N; XP 200 (class level as HD). Proficiencies: Mystic Aura, Performance (dancing), Seduction, Laying on Hands (AX3's own substitute for Village Wisdom, an *Axioms #6* proficiency). Spells per day (ACKS II Priestess 5): 3 1st, 3 2nd, 2 3rd (AX3 printed 3/3); repertoire as a 5th level priestess. Gear: curved dagger, silk chiton of seven colours, silk veil, golden earrings (50gp), high strappy sandals, silk sash, silk purse with 50gp.
- **Wants / knows:** on a **Friendly** reaction shares **1d3 Borderlands rumours** (AX3 p. 7) and **1d3 city rumours** (AX3 p. 44), and can introduce the party to **Hesta Faunus** ([[Hesta Faunus (AX3)]]) or **Councilor Drakomir** ([[Lake District]]). Leads the night rites of Aulysëa (special encounter 13).

### Lubbo the Immaculate (AX3 p. 139)
Second caretaker of the Courtyard of Nine Shrines (#42).
- **Traits:** Krysean from nearby Pyrgos; once a merchant mariner by day and carouser by night, until drink and wenching palled in the Sunset Kingdoms; two decades of ascetic study of the Logos at the Yali Monastery of Uttar, Somirea. Teaches that wisdom lies in contemplating the small, ordinary, beautiful things rather than the great and dreadful. Popular with commoners, scorned by ambitious patricians.
- **Voice:** slow, gentle, answers a question with an observation about the cup in your hand.
- **Statline:** [[Mystic]] 5 (ACKS I class rebuilt by JJ ch. 12: `Classes/Mystic.md`); Str 10, Int 9, Wis 17, Dex 13, Con 10, Cha 12; MV 40’ / 120’; AC 2 (unarmoured 0, DEX +1, Graceful Fighting +1; AX3 3); HD 5, hp 20; staff 7+ (mystic 5 = 7+, STR 0), D 1d6+2 bludgeoning (two-handed 1d6, class +2), or war rings (as cestus) 7+, D 1d3+2 (AX3 6+, 1d4+2); Save F5 with WIL +2: Paralysis 8+, Death 9+, Blast 10+, Implements 11+, Spells 12+; +2 to avoid surprise; ML 0; AL L; XP 200 (level 5 as HD, no special abilities; RR p. 310). Powers (Mystic 5): Graceful Fighting (Blade-Dancing, +1 AC), Meditative Focus (5/day: +1 attack, saves, proficiency throws and initiative for a turn), Mindfulness (Alertness), Speed of Thought (Animal Reflexes, +1 initiative), Strength of Spirit (immune to fear), Purity of Body and Soul (Divine Health, immune to disease), Probability Trance ([[Divination]] once a day), Holy Fervor (+1 morale to hirelings of his faith; added by the ACKS II build). Proficiencies: Theology (class power), Laying on Hands, Wakefulness, Seafaring; one general proficiency (5th level) unnamed in AX3: Judge's call. Gear: staff, 5 war rings, red robe with head wrap, sandals, belt, pouch with 50gp.
- **Wants / knows:** an offering of a **well-made but functional object** gives **+1 to reaction rolls** with him; knows **1d3 city rumours**; his **probability trance** can give insight to those seeking answers: [[Divination]] once a day (Mystic power; the Judge makes the magic research throw secretly).

## Special locations
- **#45u Crocodile Nest** ([[Crocodile Nest]], AX3 p. 141, map p. 216): the cesspool under the public latrine and the sewer tunnel to the Mirmen; a 30’ **giant crocodile** (hp 74) that escaped from Celictor Nuvicus. Celictor pays a premium to get it (and his lizardmen, Old District #28u) back alive; Liber Faunus will broker the meeting through the Mink, discreetly.

## Hooks
- **The escaped beasts:** Celictor Nuvicus and Liber Faunus imported two lizardman warriors, a lizardman champion and a giant crocodile to beat lanista Maximus's beastmen in the Amphitheater games; all escaped into the sewers, and 15 dockworkers and bargemen have vanished in 30 days. Celictor will pay a premium for any of them returned unharmed before the next major games; Liber Faunus wants Maximus humbled (Maximus's fortunes are tied to Selene, Liber's rival for the underworld). Meetings brokered at the Mink.
- **Lysa** (special encounter 5): a lost child in the sewers near the crocodile.
- **Fatima's contacts:** Hesta Faunus and Councilor Drakomir; she was expelled for selling secrets to (probably) Drakomir.
- **The Warehouse Block:** both syndicates' contraband in one place, guarded at night by 25 mercenaries and two sergeants.
- **Petty gods:** Tarquellus (+1 against getting lost) and Aulysëa (+1 to Tampering with Mortality) blessings for the asking.
