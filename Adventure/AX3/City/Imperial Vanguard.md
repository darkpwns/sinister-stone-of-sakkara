# Imperial Vanguard (NPC party)

A rival adventuring company of [[Cyfaraun]] (hub [[AX3 Hub]]). Acclaimed through the Borderlands: courageous, charitable, honourable, always impeccably turned out; they see themselves as heroes of old whose deeds will save the Empire — an overconfidence in their own destiny that will probably get them killed one day. They irritate the city's more bloody-minded and mercenary adventurers with wholesome antics and aggressive kindness. Between fights against the spawn of Chaos they feast with patriotic patricians and help the poor. Symbol: a **red inverted chevron on a field of gold** (AX3 p. 140 prints it as "a golden v on a field of red" — same badge, colours reversed; Judge's call: gold field, red chevron). AX3 p. 196.

AX3's designer note (p. 195): they began as an improvised rival who had swept a dungeon clean before the players arrived — literally swept, dead buried in neat graves, a sign posted "This dungeon cleared by Imperial Vanguard" — and drove the player party to outdo them. Use them the same way.

## How to use them
- **Aims:** fame, the Empire's safety, and **getting there first**: they race the party for dungeons, bounties and patrons' favour. They want a **paladin as their seventh member** (one for each Empyrean god) and are recruiting **shieldbearers, squires and retainers** (notice at the Mercenary Guildhouse, AX3 p. 71: "leave word with Guildmaster Brogitarius"). Their charity is sincere — and also buys **reserve XP** (rumour 8 below is true).
- **Rival play:** a dungeon the party planned to hit is found already cleared, swept and its dead decently buried, with a signboard. A patron the party courted is feasting with IV when they return. The party is mistaken for IV in the street (AX3 p. 60, street encounters 29-32: a traveller wants autographs; each bard the party has hired to sing its deeds gives 1 in 6 that the traveller asks for the party instead). Patricians wave and shout "Imperial Vanguard!" (AX3 p. 191). Their banner is unfurled on the Colonnade of Triumph to public praise (AX3 p. 140, [[Plaza District]]).
- **Where found:** at patrician feasts in the [[Lake District]] and [[Palace District]]; on the way to **The Mink** (AX3 p. 77, [[Old District]] encounter 7); **prying open a sewer grate** to enter the Undercity (AX3 p. 78, encounter 12); Asrya at the Keepers' charities ([[Temple District]] #59-61); Gabriol or Valenus at the Tower of Knowledge ([[Tower District]]); **Brother** the lion in a pen at the **Imperial Amphitheater** in the care of Maximus Arjentüran ([[Festival District]] #20; Aghilas knows nothing of what has become of Maximus). In the wilderness they ride (see Wealth and equipment).
- **Reactions** (if the party carries heraldry or gear marking them as adventurers, roll reaction; AX3 pp. 77-78): *at The Mink* — Friendly: drinks and swapped rumours (they know 1d4); Hostile: a **duel** challenge; otherwise they ignore the party. *At the sewer grate* — Friendly: invite the party to explore the Undercity together; Hostile: the same invitation, planning to **betray** them at an opportune moment; otherwise they warn the party off their turf. **If fighting breaks out in the streets, 2d4 City Watch arrive in 1d10 rounds, let the two parties fight it out, then arrest the survivors.**
- **Tone:** they are Lawful and genuinely good; a party that murders them becomes the villain of the city. Out-do them, embarrass them, outrace them, or win them as allies against the Awakening. AX3's own campaign history ended with IV broken at the ruins of Zahar and the survivors becoming the players' henchmen.
- **Rumours about them:** AX3 p. 201's *Random Irritating Rumors* (1d10, any time a city rumour would be given; `Tables/ax3-irritating-rumours.yaml`, [[Streets of Cyfaraun]]): Gabriol is "not a mage, something more"; Aghilas was nursed by a lioness and animals bow to him (partly true); none could think ill of Asrya; Irial is the reincarnation of Aedon Lann come to reclaim the Waste (false); Nikolaus is a god-chosen whom age, fear, sickness and poison never touch; Valenus is secretly a powerful sorcerer of the Tower (mostly false); they are all sworn Talons of the Eagle (false); they gave a whole treasure haul to the Orphanage (true — they needed the reserve XP); their name is written in gold on the Logos (false); they are hiring henchmen at the Mercenary Guildhouse (true).

## Membership
Six members (AX3 p. 196): Gabriol Eirenikos (founder, wizard), Aghilas "the Lion of the Desert" (former gladiator), Asrya Raija "the Lotus of Somirea" (priestess of Mityara), Irial Ui Aedon (elven courtier of Northern Argollë), Nikolaus the Nicean (god-gifted hero), Valenus Mirens (antiquarian dabbler). Plus Brother, Aghilas's lion.

## Wealth and equipment
- **Company funds:** **19,400gp** in coin, gems and jewellery **on deposit with the Merchant Guild** (beyond each member's own gear).
- **Mounts in the wilderness:** members with Riding (Aghilas, Irial, Valenus) on **light warhorses**, the rest (Gabriol, Asrya, Nikolaus) on **light riding horses** → [[Light Horse]] (Judge's call for the MM entry; AX3 names ACKS I horse types). The Judge may add expedition gear.
- **Common kit:** golden cloaks with the company's chevron (Gabriol's and Aghilas's are cloaks of protection +1; Irial's is mundane embroidered silk).
- **Magic items** (each listed under its owner; all appended to the AX3 item list): Gabriol — bracers of armour (AC 5), hooded cloak of protection +1, ring of protection +1, sword +1/+3 vs beastmen, wand of magic missiles (11 charges), potion of extra-healing, scroll (3 spells); Aghilas — light gladiatorial armour +1, cloak of protection +1, ring of protection +1, spear +2, potion of extra-healing; Asrya — whitewood staff +1 (light 30’), scroll of ward against undead, potion of sweet water; Irial — gracefully curved sword +1, gleaming chain mail +1; Nikolaus — white linothorax +1, ash-shafted spear +2, enchanted acacia sprig (ring of protection +1); Valenus — ring of protection +2, dagger +1, wand of detecting secret doors (8 charges).

## Conversion notes (Judge's calls)
- **Gabriol**, ACKS I/HFH *wizard*: AX3's own fallback — a **mage**, wizard class abilities ignored. Kept: his sword use (the wizard's martial training) so his sword +1 works; strict ACKS II would make him a 0th-level fighter with it.
- **Irial**, ACKS I *elven courtier*: no ACKS II class; built as an **[[Elven Spellsword]] 5**, keeping her courtier's inspire courage and magical music as listed abilities.
- **Nikolaus**, HFH *chosen*: no ACKS II class; built as a **[[Fighter]] 5** with his chosen powers kept as listed immunities (AX3 says use them as written).
- **Aghilas**: **[[Barbarian]] 5** with AX3's Besheradi regional origin (not in ACKS II's table): natural proficiency Swashbuckling; weapons club, composite bow, dagger (jambiya), hand axe, javelin, lance, short sword (scimitar); styles two-handed and dual weapon. His spear +2 is not on that list as printed — allowed (Judge's call).
- **Spells** by ACKS II name; ACKS I spells with no ACKS II equivalent are replaced and marked. **XP** from the ACKS II table: HD = level, ** for mage/priestess/elven-spellsword spellcasting, * for thief backstab and for Nikolaus's immunities.

## Aghilas, the Lion of the Desert (AX3 pp. 198-199)

- **Look and story:** lean, superbly athletic, early 20s, light brown skin, close-cut hair; exquisite lion-themed gladiatorial armour, the weapons of the **Besheradi** nomads of the Sunset Kingdoms, a golden chevron cloak. Left his tribe vowing to fight in every realm "until every man shall know my name"; became arena champion from Alakyrum to Aura. His one regret: never facing his rival **Maximus Arjentüran**. ## Asrya Raija, the Lotus of Somirea (AX3 pp. 199-200)
Bestiary: [[Asrya Raija (AX3)]].
- **Look and story:** 5’ tall with a presence that makes her seem far taller; aristocratic Somirean features, almond-shaped dark brown eyes, wavy blue-black hair, light olive skin; plain but well-made linen chiton, veiled in public. Keepers on a diplomatic mission brought her into the order; she travelled the Sunset Kingdoms for years. A year ago a vision of the Mother of Mercy warned her of peril in the Borderlands; in Cyfaraun she met Gabriol. - **Traits:** serene, self-denying, quietly fierce in mercy.

## Irial Ui Aedon (AX3 p. 200)

## Nikolaus the Nicean (AX3 p. 200)
Bestiary: [[Nikolaus the Nicean (AX3)]].
- **Look and story:** 5’6”, well muscled, moves with a cat's grace; the handsomest of Nicean features, deep-set dominating green eyes, glossy black hair cropped for campaign, flawless ageless bronze skin; a mellifluous tenor that commands without being raised; breezy, welcoming, effortlessly drawing people in. His enchanted linen cuirass never stains. Sole survivor of a pox-struck hamlet as a newborn, found with a sprig of acacia (his dead mother's charm) on his swaddling; at three, bitten thrice by a viper, he twisted its head off and went back to his toy soldiers. Pox took his foster family too; raised from five in an orphanage beside the Winged Sun temple's library and the Knights' drill-yard: soldier by day, classics by night, several languages by nine.
