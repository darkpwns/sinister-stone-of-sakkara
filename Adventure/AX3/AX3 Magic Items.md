# AX3 Magic Items

Judge only. Hub: [[AX3 Hub]]. Pages are printed pages. AX3 is ACKS I: each item below keeps AX3's description, base cost and base time (price/value as given) and is read in ACKS II terms (spell names, saves, conditions). Items without a description here are standard magic items: use the ACKS II Treasure Tome entry named in the room note. The other districts' placed items are merged below by the coordinator.

## New Magic Items (Appendix A, AX3 p. 194)

### Clockwork Auto-Stabilizing Light Ballista
- **AX3:** effect *automaton*; base cost **4,000gp**; base time **11 days**.
- **What it is:** a miniaturised, man-portable light ballista fired from the shoulder, accurate to **120’** thanks to auto-stabilising gears.
- **ACKS II:** fires light ballista bolts dealing **1d10 piercing** damage; each bolt weighs 1 lb (counts as **1 item** for its size) and costs **1gp**. The operator must be a machinist, or be trained in its use **and** proficient with the **arbalest** ([[Weapons]]). He uses his normal attack throw **+2** (auto-stabiliser), and any proficiency or ability that applies to arbalests or missile fire applies to it. **Cleave:** when the operator's attack triggers a cleave ([[Cleaving]]), the bolt passes through the target and strikes the enemy directly behind it, if any; at most **twice per round**. **Reload: one round** (large bolts); the operator may move freely while loading or firing. As an automaton it has no spell effect to dispel (Judge's call).
- **Where:** for sale by the machinist in the Emporium, **25%** chance per appearance, **4,000gp** (AX3 p. 81; [[Old District]]).

### Healing Salve
- **AX3:** effect *cure minor wounds cantrip*; base cost **100gp**; base time **1 ½ days**.
- **What it is:** enchanted liniment of camphor, wormwood and oil.
- **ACKS II:** rubbed on a wound, heals the creature **1 point of damage**; any creature can benefit only **once per day**. ACKS II has the same item, [[Healing Salve]] (Treasure Tome: base cost 400gp, found with 1d3+1 doses); AX3's 100gp is the module's price (ACKS II has no cantrips; Judge's call which cost to use for crafting).
- **Where:** Mountebank Thaddeus, 1d6 at **10gp** each (AX3 p. 81; [[Old District]]).

### Herbal Tonic
- **AX3:** effect *remedy cantrip*; base cost **100gp**; base time **1 ½ days**.
- **What it is:** an amber tea or infusion smelling of garlic, ginger, honey and vinegar.
- **ACKS II:** helps the drinker recover from a minor acute illness such as the common cold: the drinker immediately makes a **saving throw vs Death**; success, he recovers in **half the usual time**; failure, no benefit. **One a day**: further tonics that day cause stomach distress and cannot aid recovery. (Major diseases: not affected; Judge's call.)
- **Where:** Mountebank Thaddeus sells quacksalve tonics (1d6, **10gp**; 5% chance of the opposite effect) (AX3 p. 81); Lanista Omnus Pluris treats his stomach with them (AX3 p. 107; [[Festival District]]).

### Moon Tea
- **AX3:** effect *moon's call*; base cost **100gp**; base time **1 ½ days**.
- **ACKS II:** the drinker immediately enters menses unless it succeeds on a **saving throw vs Death** (AX3: "versus Poison", ACKS I's Poison & Death category). Used to end unwanted pregnancies: if the pregnancy was more than **50%** of its term, the subject suffers a stillbirth in **2d6+7 days**; otherwise the pregnancy ends with the menstruation. A creature incapable of menses instead bleeds from the nose and takes **1 point of damage**.
- **Where:** not placed in AX3 (Judge's call: apothecaries, temples of the hearth, the mountebank).

### Mother's Tea
- **AX3:** effect *mother's ministry cantrip*; base cost **100gp**; base time **1 ½ days**.
- **ACKS II:** drunk by a woman in labour, it eases the pain of childbirth: a mild analgesic effect until she gives birth or dies (no game penalty from pain; Judge's call). Anyone else who drinks it bleeds from the nose and takes **1 point of damage**.
- **Where:** Mountebank Thaddeus, quacksalve mother's teas, 1d6 at **10gp** (5% chance of the opposite effect) (AX3 p. 81).

### Shield of the Underwatch
- **AX3:** effect *+1 protection, 1st level spell 1/turn*; base cost **21,500gp**; base time **100 days**.
- **What it is:** the storied Captain of the Underwatch's shield: wood in an archaic hourglass shape, plated with silver.
- **ACKS II:** a **shield +1**. Borne by a **Lawful** character it radiates protection from evil, as [[Holy Circle]] centred on the bearer with a **10’** radius: **+1 to AC and saving throws** for the bearer and allies within 10’ against attacks by evil creatures, and enchanted creatures cannot make bodily contact with them. The contact barrier lapses if the bearer or any protected creature attacks, casts a spell on, or tries to force the barrier against a blocked creature; it **recharges after one turn** (10 minutes). Silver-plated: counts as silver if used to bash (Judge's call).
- **Where:** [[Catacombs of the Patriarchs]] room 6, atop the Captain's columbarium, each item guarded by two runes of warding set by Patriarch Arëtar (AX3 p. 135; [[Bridge District]]); borne by any Captain of the Underwatch he appoints.

### Spear of the Underwatch
- **AX3:** effect *+2, +3 weapon, at-will 1st level effect*; base cost **50,000gp**; base time **122.5 days**.
- **What it is:** the Captain's famous weapon: an archaic Auran spear with a bronze butt-spike and silver-coated steel head.
- **ACKS II:** a **spear +2, +3 vs lycanthropes**; its silver-coated head also counts as silver. Speaking the command word **"audar"** makes it shed light as [[Illumination]], or puts the light out if lit (at will). Against the wererats of the Cult of the Horned Rat ([[Shrine of the Horned Rat]]) this is the weapon it was made for.
- **Where:** as the shield: [[Catacombs of the Patriarchs]] room 6 (AX3 p. 135).
