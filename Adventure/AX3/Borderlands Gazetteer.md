## Garrisons of the Unbroken Line
AX3 pp. 16-17. Most of the Auran Army is in the west fighting the Skysos; the Borderlands keep a fraction (troop types: the warfare chapter's Auran troops).

- In three weeks a legate fields twice the one-week force while keeping 12 men per watchtower. The rest of his troops sit in fortlets, watchtowers and vassal estates (tribunes and castellans).
- **Watchtowers:** each legate has **18** on the line, about a mile apart, each in sight of the next. 75% hold 8 infantry, 25% (cavalry towers, with a 20' × 15' stable for 4 horses) 8 infantry and 4 cavalry. At present a tower holds **1d3 composite bowmen, 1d3 veteran heavy infantry and 2d3 heavy infantry**; a cavalry tower adds **1d3 cataphracts and 2d3 horse archers**. Reinforcements from neighbouring towers arrive in **1d4+4 minutes (cavalry)** and **1d6+6 minutes (infantry)**. Tower: 20' square, 30' tall, 2.5' stone walls, ladder to a second-storey door; stronghold value 8,000gp (8,250gp with stable). Crews are wary of strangers (bandits pose as adventurers) but once trust is made they give information and supplies to those fighting Chaos.
- **Tower stores** (one month for twelve men): 3d6+2 bushels of grain (5sp, 4 st each), 1d2 barrels of smoked pork (5gp, 8 st), 1d2-1 bags of lentils (5sp, 4 st), 1d3 pints of olive oil (22cp; 15 bottles per stone), 1d8 salt bricks (7sp, 3/6 st), 1d8 cheese wheels (3sp, 2/6 st); cavalry towers also 4d6+2 × 10 bales of hay (115sp, 3 st each). A soldier's daily ration costs 7.1cp; a cavalry horse eats 20 lbs of hay a day.

## Stocking the chaotic domains
AX3 pp. 25-26. The Waste is the realm of **Sebek, Lord of Secrets** ([[Ruins of Zahar]]). Beastman clanholds scrape a living and wait for the day they take the civilized lands. Only the **12 dire orc tribes** are on the map: **8 under Sebek at Zahar**, 1 each under his vassals at [[Nurgarog]], [[Arwia Oasis]], [[Morgot]] and [[Nabu-Kudur]]. Each vassal also has **6 ordinary tribes** around him and **two sub-vassals with 7 ordinary tribes each**: 20 ordinary tribes per vassal, **100 in all**. (The [[Fortress of Gorgbad]] is a fifth vassal with the same layout; the count of 100 includes it, the count of 12 dire orc tribes does not.)
- Roll each ordinary tribe on `Tables/ax3-beastman-tribe.yaml` (1d100: bugbear, gnoll, goblin, hobgoblin, ogre, orc, troll, with territory, families and family make-up); run each as a wilderness lair (MM) or as the table's average tribe. At most **125 beastman families per 6-mile hex**; ogre and troll families count as 4.
- **Muster** of the Lord of Secrets: **1,152 dire orc**, 680 bugbear, 680 gnoll, 1,920 goblin, 1,740 hobgoblin, 760 ogre, 3,840 orc and 250 troll warriors: **11,022**. (AX3 gives these as Domains at War companies: 10 dire orc, 6 bugbear, 6 gnoll, 16 goblin, 15 hobgoblin, 13 ogre, 32 orc, 4 troll; this campaign plays battles with the rules vault's warfare chapter, not D@W.)
- **Dire orcs** have no MM entry and no statblock here (Judge's call).
