# Crocodile Nest — Plaza District #45u

A stretch of the Auran sewers under the Public Latrine (#45) of the [[Plaza District]], [[Cyfaraun]]: tunnels cut from solid tuff outside Old Cyfaraun, rough-hewn and unsurfaced. **Unlit.** Over a month ago Celictor Nuvicus, partnered with Liber Faunus ([[Liber Faunus (AX3)]]), smuggled in two lizardman warriors, a lizardman champion and a wild giant crocodile to beat lanista Maximus's beastmen in the games; the ploy failed and all escaped into the sewers. 15 dockworkers and bargemen have vanished in the last 30 days to the crocodile or the lizardmen; body parts are turning up in the river. The lizardmen are in the Old District's Lizardman Lair (#28u; [[Old District]]). Pages are printed pages; "(map)" = read from the map, p. 216. Hub [[AX3 Hub]]. `level: 1` is a Judge's call (one 15 HD resident).

Running: AX3 makes no encounter throws inside a special location until it is cleared; afterwards Undercity throws: 1d6 every 3 turns, 6+ → 1d100 on `Tables/ax3-undercity-encounters.yaml`. AX3 gives no wandering table of its own; the one special roll is the crocodile at the river exit (2). The Undercity table's special entry for this site (80+, within 120’): a torn-off green scale on a rusty grate — Animal Husbandry or Naturalism: a crocodile or giant lizard at least 30’ long; Tracking follows a thin blood trail to the nest (AX3 p. 67).

## Layout
- **Entrances:** the latrine's 20 toilets drain into the cesspool **1** (from the Public Latrine, #45, above); four sewer tunnels meet at 1: **NW** and **NE** (into the wider sewers), **SW** (forks south after about 40’), and **SE**, which runs about 100’ south-east then turns east for some 200’ to the **sewer exit 2** on the Mirmen River (map). A short dead-end stub opens off the SE tunnel's south-west side near 1 (map).

## Factions
- **Celictor Nuvicus** wants the beasts captured alive before the next major games and pays a premium for each returned unharmed; **Liber Faunus** wants lanista Maximus humbled (Maximus's fortunes ride on Selene, Liber's rival for the underworld) and will broker a discreet meeting with Celictor for adventurers who frequent the Mink ([[Old District]]).

### 1. Crocodile Nest
- **Exits:** up the latrine drains to #45 (not passable for anything larger than a child); sewer tunnels NW, NE, SW and SE (SE leads to 2) (map).
- The round cesspool under the latrine, littered with fresh human bones and remains. **Healing** proficiency throw: remains of **at least 15 people**.
- **Giant crocodile** (30’ long), here by day, submerged in the filth: characters meeting it take **−2 to surprise throws** → [[Giant Crocodile]]: MV land 20’ / 60’, swim 30’ / 90’, AC 8, HD 15, **hp 74**, bite −4+ (MM; AX3 printed −1+), D 3d8 piercing, Save F8, ML +1, AL N, XP 1,800. Night vision, acute olfaction; stealthy underwater (−2 to others' surprise, the same −2 AX3 gives).
- **Treasure:** none (AX3).
- **Capture alive:** Celictor's premium is unpriced in AX3 (Judge's call).

### 2. Sewer Exit
- **Exits:** W/NW along the tunnel to 1; E out into the Mirmen River (map).
- The tunnel's mouth on the river. In the **hour after sunset** and the **hour before dawn**, the crocodile may be met here on an encounter throw of **5+ on 1d6** (going out to hunt or coming back).
