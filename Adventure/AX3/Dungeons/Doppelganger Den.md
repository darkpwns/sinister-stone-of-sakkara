# Doppelgänger Den (Special Location 14u)

In the Undercity just north of the Argollëan Family's territory ([[Old District]], [[Cyfaraun]]). Doppelgängers were bred by the long-dead **Khepri** as secret police to watch their elven protégés and human slaves; they still steal the identities of people placed to observe the influential, murder the originals, kill anyone who grows suspicious, and move on to better-placed victims. Five years ago three assassins founded the **Brotherhood of Knives** here, a would-be syndicate selling murder; a year ago three doppelgängers joined, killed them and took their faces. Their clients (crime bosses, merchants, fallen clergy, nobles) and their targets give a treasure trove of identities; they now kill and replace notable people across the city. AX3 pp. 73, 92-96. "(map)" = read from the map.

Construction: an excavated pocket of Ancient Cyfaraun (elven ruins cleared of tuff), originally (circa 600 BE) four small separate stucco-walled buildings; pointed-arch window holes show unexcavated tuff. **Doors:** makeshift driftwood and salvaged hardwood, very creaky: **-2 to open them without noise**. **Secret doors** look like a bricked-up passage and old repair work. **Lighting:** none, except when they write in the ledger (2); the darkness may tip off attentive adventurers to the occupants' alien nature.

Running: Undercity encounter throw every 3 turns, **5+ on 1d6 (below the Old District)**, 1d100 on `Tables/ax3-undercity-encounters.yaml` (written for [[Undercity of Cyfaraun]]); its 80+ special result near here = one doppelgänger out on a mission (1d3 which, then its identity table), avoiding hostilities if it can (AX3 p. 67). No throws inside the den until it is cleared.

## Layout
- The den sits inside a rough quadrilateral of sewer tunnels (north, east, south-east, west) (map).
- **Inner rooms:** barracks **1** (north) has a **secret door E** onto the northern sewer tunnel; a passage runs S from 1 to the ledger room **2**; from 2 a winding passage runs west past the abattoir **3** (door) to the cell **4** (barred door, west end) and on south-west through trap **6** (SW) to the western sewer tunnel; eastward it runs through trap **6** (E) to the eastern sewer tunnel (map).
- **Abandoned hideout 5** lies apart in the north-west, behind a **secret door** off the north-west sewer tunnel (map).

Seen in natural form, or suspecting that the party knows: **attack at once**.

## Rooms (AX3 pp. 92-94)

### 1. Assassins' Barracks
- **Exits:** secret door E to the northern sewer tunnel; passage S to 2 (map).
- The Brotherhood's old living quarters, clean by Undercity standards: four cots, three chests, an arms rack. **All three doppelgängers are here** unless met elsewhere first (identities as above).
- **Chest 1:** **3,000sp**; aquamarine (**500gp**), bloodstone (**50gp**), emerald (**1,000gp**).
- **Chest 2:** two wrought gold armbands (**900gp each**), two wrought gold anklets (**700gp each**), two wrought silver armbands (**400gp each**).
- **Chest 3:** onyx (**50gp**), obsidian (**10gp**), diamond (**1,000gp**), tiger eye (**25gp**), zircon (**75gp**), ivory pendant (**900gp**), wrought orichalcum circlet (**4,000gp**), wrought gold necklace (**700gp**), a battle axe with polished steel blade and rune-carved oak haft (apparent **650gp**; actually a **battle axe +1**).

### 2. Assassins' Ledger
- **Exits:** N to 1; W and E along the winding passage (to 3, 4 and the traps 6) (map).
- Cots heaped with clothes sorted by kind: (1) plain city dress, (2) rich merchants' clothes, (3) poor travellers' cloaks, (4) dandy bards' costumes, (5) guardsmen's armour and surcoats, (6) camouflaged explorers' cloaks, (7) formal clerical and priestly vestments. A writing table and a workbench.
- **The Assassins' Ledger** beside an unlit oil lamp: the victims of the original assassins and then of the doppelgängers, with names, occupations, where each could be found, where each was killed, and **who paid**. Readers with INT 13+ or able to read languages notice the handwriting change midway through the fourth year's entries.
- **Handout (summary, AX3 p. 97):** each patron's entry gives the targets, the reason and the patron's password:
  - **Liber Faunus** — Huranos (Nicean jewel merchant, to learn the worth of the patron's services), Ashera (Opelenean spice merchant, for disrespect), Vason (Nicean wine merchant, for lack of candour), Bodilkas (Krysean explorer whose caravans spurned the patron). Password **"morning dew"**.
  - **Mentenus** (Provost of the Tower of Knowledge Chapterhouse) — Gundus (Tirenean Watch officer who wanted patrols sent into the Undercity), Fionntan and Tomman (elves obsessed with ancestral relics in the Undercity). Password **"sludge mold"**.
  - **Selene** (owner of The Silk Pony, secretly head of Sand & Bones) — Olyma (Nicean bard and a Sand & Bones member, for loving her mistress's lover without leave), Riara (young Tirenean priestess, too lovely and nearly won the patroness's lover). Password **"ruby lips"**.
- Turning the ledger or survivors in: **Value of information**, [[Old District]] (Special locations).

### 3. Abattoir
- **Exits:** door onto the passage W of 2 (map).
- Nauseating stench through the ramshackle door. Rotting remains of **1d4 humans and 1d4 elves** slain by the doppelgängers. Identified by speak with dead or other means: **1d8** on `Tables/ax3-old-human-victim.yaml` / `Tables/ax3-old-elven-victim.yaml`.

### 4. Cell
- **Exits:** door E to the passage (map).
- Door barred with thick oak. A **hear noise (Listening) throw** catches low, agonised groans. Inside, **1d3 humans and 1d3 elves** shackled to the walls, malnourished, kept for questioning until the doppelgängers know enough to pose as them, then to be killed. Who: **1d12** on the human and elven victim tables. Nara (elven victim 11-12) is an [[Elven Enchanter]] 1: statline in that note. (Pesca and Seanan are known to be held here: Emporium 5 and 6.)

### 5. Abandoned Hideout
- **Exits:** secret door to the north-west sewer tunnel (map).
- An overturned weapon rack, rusted weapons strewn about; a faded painted sign: a triangle of three knives. Three rotted cots, a beetle-covered corpse on each: **Aodan, Zene and Uxentio**, founders of the Brotherhood (identifiable only by speak with dead or other magic).
- **Disturbing the bodies** angers a beetle swarm → [[Insect Swarm]]: MV 10’/30’, climb 10’/30’, fly 20’/60’, AC 2, HD 3*, hp 15, engulf 20’ × 15’: 2 piercing at the end of the victim's initiative (4 if unarmoured, natural AC 3 or less), fleeing or warding halves it (a torch deals 1d4 fire to the swarm), vexed 1 (2) per round for 3 rounds after escaping, immune to bludgeoning, piercing, slashing, disease 10%, save F0, ML +3, AL N, XP 65.
- **Lore:** a Knowledge (local) throw or a Carousing hijink in Cyfaraun: the Brotherhood of Knives began killing for hire some years ago; still active, few members, not recruiting, very hard to find or contact even for clients.

### 6. Traps (two)
- **Exits:** the sewer-tunnel mouths of the den's passage, SW and E (map).
- Tripwires across the tunnels tied to jars of **military oil** hung from the ceiling: each character passing has a **2-in-6 chance** to snap the wire. The oil pours out and **ignites on contact with water**, an oil fire over the area shown on the map: anyone who begins his initiative in it or enters it takes **1d8 fire damage** and keeps burning **one round after leaving**. The fire burns out after **1 turn**.
