# Tower of Knowledge — Fifth Story (the Observatory)

[[Tower District]] #50. The open-air rooftop, 75’ above the city: the highest point in Cyfaraun. Pages are printed pages.

Running: inhabited building; **no wandering monster checks** (AX3 gives none). Dungeon level 1 for treasure is a Judge's call. Used to train and practise **blast, summoning, transmogrification and wall** magic.
- **Sunup to sundown:** an apprentice, 1d3 arcanists, 1d3 seers; **50%** a theurgist, **33%** a magician or thaumaturge, **25%** an enchanter or sorcerer; **5%** each officer, teaching; and **one tower guard** on post using the sextant to watch the lands (statline: [[Tower of Knowledge - Ground Story]] room 1).
- **At night:** a sage, 1d2 journeyman scholars and 1d4 apprentice scholars making astrological observations and records.

## Layout
- **Stair:** NW buttress, down to the orrery ([[Tower of Knowledge - Fourth Story]]).
- **Centre:** the sextant **1** on a seven-step platform, surrounded by the 40’ brass dial **2** (concentric rings on the map).

## Features (AX3 pp. 155-156)

### 1. Sextant of Cyfaraun
- **Exits:** atop the 7-step platform in the centre of the roof.
- Bronze sextant, **permanently enchanted with eyes of the eagle**† (ACKS I spell; nearest ACKS II effect: the [[Visor of the Eagle]]'s far sight, Judge's call). Built to track the heavenly bodies, it can be set to watch the land beyond the walls to the horizon for the movement of enemy armies, above all south toward the Waste. A tower guard keeps watch with it by day.

### 2. Sextant Mechanisms
- **Exits:** around 1.
- Complex gearing inside the sextant and under its platform keeps the measurements exact. A hollow bronze rod reaches from the sextant's base to the edge of polished brass plates forming a circular dial **40’ across**, marked in 360 degrees with 60-second subdivisions between degree marks.
