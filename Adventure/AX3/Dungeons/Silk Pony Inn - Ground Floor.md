# The Silk Pony Inn — Ground Floor (25)

Notorious gambling den at the north-east tip of Gambler's Row, popular with adventurers, merchants and thieves; owned by Selene Nictorian and the headquarters of the **Sand & Bones Guild**. Its street is clean: anyone fouling it is brutally disciplined by the inn's enforcers. Dungeon level 1 is a placeholder (Judge's call): a building, not a delve.

Running: hub [[AX3 Hub]], district [[Festival District]]. **No wandering checks** inside; occupants by time of day as keyed. Gambler's Row outside: encounters on 5+ on 1d6 by day, 4+ by night, reactions in its alleys at -2. Upstairs → [[Silk Pony Inn - Top Floor]]; cellar and hideout → [[Sand and Bones Hideout]].

## Layout
- **Doors and windows:** the common door and the stable doors open onto the crossing of **Cesta Calefan** ("Street of Fortune") and **Cesta Lumen** ("Silk Street"). Hard oak bound with iron, two bars each (-4 to proficiency throws to bash open); double-padlocked when the inn is closed. Windows: iron frames, glass panes, stout oak shutters.
- **Plan (map; the map's north points left):** common room **1** in the middle; two sturdy doors in its south side to the private rooms **2** (Gambling Snug) and **6** (Backroom); kitchen **3** with pantry against the north/west side by the hearth and the ascending stair to the top floor; the annex with crate, keg and the descending stair **4** to the cellar; stables **5** along the long side, double doors in the south-east.
- **Patrons:** when needed roll Random Occupant by Building Type (General), AX3 p. 52 ([[Buildings of Cyfaraun]]); otherwise normal men and women: AC 0, hp 3, ML -1.

### 1. Common Room
- **Exits:** street door; doors south to 2 and 6; annex with stair down 4; stair up beside the hearth to the top floor; kitchen 3; stables 5 (map).
- Beer-stained gambling hall seating about 40 at three large and four small tables of heavy iron-banded oak. L-shaped bar south of the smoky hearth; a stuffed pony's head in a silk caparison above it; two beer kegs; a slate menu (below).
- **Menu (p. 116):** bran bread loaf 1cp; wheat porridge 3cp; salted chickpea pancakes 3cp; two hardboiled eggs in fish sauce 1cp; two fried eggs 1cp; fried green beans in fish sauce 2cp; fried pork sausage with mushrooms 2cp. Vinegar and water 1cp; fermented wheat porridge 1cp; Krysean barley beer with honey 2cp; Corcanosi raisin wine with honey 5cp; Opelenean plum wine with rose water 5cp; Nicean resinated dry white 5cp; Tirenean dry white with honey 1sp.
- **Day:** 1d3 tavernworkers (first behind the bar), 2d6+2 patrons gambling and eating, 2d3 footpads on security (one watching the stairs 4, the rest the gamblers), 1d4 prostitutes (2sp an hour, 1gp a night; upstairs rooms rented separately).
- **Evening:** 3 tavernworkers, 2d4 prostitutes, 4d6+2 patrons, 2d4+1 footpads and 1d3 hoods (one hood always on the door of the Snug 2). **70%** Thana Nyssand is behind the bar → [[Thana Nyssand (AX3)]].
- **Safe** built into the back of the bar, locked (keys: Selene, Jonus, Thana): **3,000cp, 2,000sp, 20gp**.

### 2. Gambling Snug
- **Exits:** door to 1, lockable from either side (all Guild leaders hold keys) and barrable from inside (map).
- Opened only in the evening, a hood on the door. High-stakes house games: needs at least a Neutral reaction from the Guild and a **10gp** minimum buy-in. Round table, eight chairs.
- House game, 1d6 per night (`Tables/ax3-festival-snug-game.yaml`): 1-2 **Calefan Esevi** (49-card deck, nearest to 7 beats the dealer; 1:1, 3:2 on a 7); 3-4 **Draxës** (three d6 against the house; doubles 4:1, triples 25:1); 5-6 **Nomen** (house rolls three d6; bet a number; one ordered match 3:2, two 8:1, three 100:1).
- **1d6+1 patrons** with a croupier; a tavernworker and 1d4 prostitutes serve. With 6+ patrons, **30%** Selene Nictorian is present → [[Selene Nictorian (AX3)]] (with her destroyer, Judge's call, as in 6).
- Each gambler's bankroll 10+1d10gp; on a 10, add 1d100gp. The dealer's bank: **1,000gp + 100gp per gambler** in an iron lockbox (mostly silver, some copper and gold); topped up from the Backroom 6.

### 3. Kitchen / Pantry
- **Exits:** to 1; door in the north wall to the pantry (map).
- Masonry stove with grill in the west wall, cook pots in the flames, a barrel of river water, worktable with bowls, cauldrons, kettles, pans, jars; cutlery rack and roasting spit. Business hours: a **cook and a scullion**.
- **Pantry:** 12 bags of wheat, chickpeas, green beans, mushrooms (6gp, 48 st total); 2 amphorae of honey (40gp, 12 st); 4 amphorae of fish sauce (80gp, 24 st); 4 amphorae of preserved pork (20gp, 16 st); 5 amphorae of olive oil (100gp, 30 st).

### 4. Stairs to Cellar
- **Exits:** stair down to the cellar (Hideout 4); the annex of 1 (map).
- A footpad from 1 blocks everyone but Guild members and staff. **Blood Moon nights:** plus **2 enforcers** (Assassin 2, above), who admit only friends of the Guild, those who say the password **"Kruan Elen"** ("blood moon" in Classical Auran), anyone who says **Corentin** sent them, or anyone who looks like a blood-sport thug (Judge's discretion).

### 5. Stables
- **Exits:** double doors to the street (south-east); into the inn (map).
- Unkempt barn, six stalls for donkeys, horses or mules; **2sp a day** (warhorses get nothing extra); hay and a trough each. A tongueless, bruised **stablehand** feeds and grooms.

### 6. Backroom
- **Exits:** door to 1 (two-room suite) (map).
- Door: two reinforced bars (-4 to bash) and an intricate lock (-4 to open); keys Selene, Jonus, Thana. Writing table and chair, a **locked ironbound chest**, shelves of neat paperwork (mostly legitimate records in Thana's hand).
- **70%** Thana at her desk late afternoon or night; **30%** Selene in the evening, guarded by a **destroyer** (Assassin 5, above).
- **Chest** (keys Selene, Thana, Jonus): a week's working capital: **650gp, 3,000sp, 3,000cp**.
