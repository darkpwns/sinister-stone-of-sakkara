# W2. Atrium
A buried atrium 30' square, its roof held by four fat columns; the impluvium is a pool of black silt. Doorways N, W and
S; a ragged curtain over a gap in the east wall of the N cellar.
### Features
- Empty (stocking). Chalked rat-heads mark the doorways.
