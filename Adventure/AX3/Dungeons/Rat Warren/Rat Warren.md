# Rat Warren (Terkinos's new lair)

Judge only. Built day 323 (S17) by the interim procedure (`unprepped-sites.md`): where Terkinos's pack went when it
abandoned the [[Shrine of the Horned Rat]] the night of day 306 (Rulings S16, user's ruling). Hidden build rolls:
`Session Log/S17 — The ledger.md` Judge block, "Site build: Rat Warren".

## Running Rat Warren
- Dungeon level: 3
- Wandering monsters: 1d6 every 3 turns, 5+
- Encounter table: ../../Tables/ax3-undercity-encounters.yaml (the Old District Undercity; its 80+ special = the pack itself while it is out)
- **Approach:** the Shrine's north-west room 2 secret door opens on a low tuff tunnel (5' wide, 6' high) running
  north **800'** under the Old District's northern edge; two side passages (at 250' and 520') end in collapses after
  30'. Floor of silt and filth (soft: tracks show). At 800' the tunnel ends at a rubble slope into the buried cellars
  of an Ancient Cyfaraun house (ash-buried 245 BE): rooms W1-W6. No light inside (the pack sees by its own).
- **The pack:** Terkinos (Mage 3 wererat, hp 17), 2 arcanists (Mage 1 wererats, hp 16, 15), 5 wererats (hp 22, 12, 9,
  12, 14); statlines at [[Shrine of the Horned Rat]] and [[Wererat]]. Enchanted creatures: a holy circle (and the
  Underwatch shield) keeps them off; in rat or ratman form only silver, magic or other extraordinary damage harms
  them (mundane non-physical resisted); the Underwatch spear is +3 against them.
- **Routine:** out searching the Undercity noon to midnight; asleep in W3 (and Terkinos in W4) by night, **75%
  present**, in human form unless alerted. **Full moon:** no searching; they clean up, buy a month's provisions at the
  Emporium by day, are back by sunset, and pray in W6 until midnight. **Mentenus does not come** (warned; Rulings S16;
  he now knows from Prelate Romus that the Citadel holds his seer and papers: Judge's roll day 323, 3 = yes).
- **Alarm:** W1's tripwire of bone rattles is heard in W3 and W4; alerted, the pack takes ratman form and fights from
  W2's dark, or flees by W6's shaft if the fight goes against it (morale 0; Terkinos's death breaks it).
- **Map:** tunnel (800') → W1 breach (rubble crawl, 15') → W2 atrium (30' x 30') → W3 sleeping cellar (N, 20' x 25'),
  W4 Terkinos's room (E of W3 through a curtain, 15' x 15'), W5 store and nest (W, 20' x 15'), W6 new shrine (S,
  painted triclinium 25' x 20'; in its SE corner a drain shaft climbs 40' to a grate in a Tower District alley).
