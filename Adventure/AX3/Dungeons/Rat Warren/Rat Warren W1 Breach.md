# W1. Breach
The tunnel ends in a slope of ash-packed rubble; a crawlway 15' long, 3' high, worms through it into darkness. Rat
droppings and the smell of wet fur.
### Features
- Fresh tracks of many bare and booted feet in the silt before the crawl (Tracking throw +4 creatures, +4 soft, -4 dim).
### Traps
- **Tripwire of bone rattles** across the crawl's far mouth (Searching or the thief's Traps skill to see it; a
  stepped-over wire is avoided): sets off the alarm (Running). Judge's call, stocking result "trap".
- **Deadfall:** the crawl's roof is shored with one loose beam; knocking it (anyone forcing through carrying more than
  10 st, or a failed Traps throw to disarm) brings rubble down: 2d6 bludgeoning to the one in the crawl (Blast save
  half), and the crawl is blocked (a turn's digging). (A 2d6 rolled at the build on day 323 is void: roll at trigger.)
### Checks and saves
- Searching / Traps (thief) to find the wire; Blast save for the deadfall (RR saving throws).
### Exits
- S: the tunnel to the Shrine (800'). N: [[Rat Warren W2 Atrium]]. Back to [[Rat Warren]].
