# W6. New Shrine
A painted dining room of the buried city: faded banquet frescoes, and against the north wall a crude altar of rat
skulls stacked round a horned rat cut from a roof tile, candle stubs in its eyes.
### Features
- Unique (stocking): the pack's new altar; at the full moon they pray here until midnight.
- **Drain shaft** in the SE corner: iron rungs 40' up to a grate in a blind alley of the Tower District, two streets
  from the Chapterhouse (Mentenus's way down; the grate's bar is greased).
### Checks and saves
- Climbing the rungs: no throw (rungs). Lifting the grate from below: an open action; from above it is locked
  (Locks throw).
### Exits
- N: [[Rat Warren W2 Atrium]]. Up: the shaft to the Tower District. Back to [[Rat Warren]].
