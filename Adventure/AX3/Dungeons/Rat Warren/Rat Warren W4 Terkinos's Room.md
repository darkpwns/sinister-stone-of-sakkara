# W4. Terkinos's Room
A small vaulted room behind a curtain: a cot, a writing board on two amphorae, a cedar chest under straw.
### Monsters
- Terkinos (Mage 3 wererat, hp 17; [[Terkinos (AX3)]]), by night 75%, asleep.
### Treasure
- **H39** (TT G, the pack's own, carried from the Shrine): 8,000sp and 3,000ep in the cedar chest under the straw, and
  a wrought-silver rat figurine (500gp) wrapped in a stocking.
- Terkinos's report book (the Undercity's walls searched, by sector; his notes on "the cold under the river") and a
  letter in Mentenus's hand (silky, unsigned, the seal a rat's skull): "Keep from the old shrine. The Citadel holds the
  girl and my papers; our friend in the Bridge District tells me the Patriarch keeps his own counsel for now. Do not
  come to me at the moon; I will send. Search the north." (Clues: Mentenus knows; Romus is his friend; he expects
  the Citadel to move.)
