# Eclipse Hideout (Special Location #69u)

Hideout of the Cyfaraun chapter of the **Order of the Eclipse**, a secret sect of Calefa (Lady of Fortune, goddess of wealth and protector of the dead) whose members, the **deathbringers**, end lives under ritual religious contracts. Elven ruins south of a main sewer canal, below the Empyrean Temple Undercity of the [[Temple District]], reached through the secret door 10 of the [[Catacombs of the Ancestors]]. Each room is the dug-out interior of a free-standing elven building of c. 600 BE: frescoed stucco walls and tiled floors under grime and ash, pointed-arch window holes onto unexcavated tuff. Long tunnels between follow elven streets: rough-cut tuff walls and ceilings, here and there the face of a buried elven building, old cobbled road underfoot. "(map)" = read from the map, AX3 p. 218 (PDF 220); the map is printed sideways, so directions below are as the map sits on the page (Judge's call). Dungeon level 1 is a Judge's call (AX3 gives none).

Running: hub [[AX3 Hub]]; **no wandering checks inside the hideout while the Order holds it** (AX3 p. 64); once it is cleared or abandoned, and in the sewer canal outside, use the Undercity rule: **1d6 every 3 turns, encounter on 6**, 1d100 on `Tables/ax3-undercity-encounters.yaml` ([[Undercity of Cyfaraun]]).

**Light:** the dormitories, Destrio's room, the assembly chamber and the supply corridor (3-6) are lit by oil lamps. The secret entrance and the corridor beyond it are **dark**. Deathbringers carry lamps or torches into the Undercity.

## The Order of the Eclipse (AX3 pp. 172-173)
- **Origin:** founded in Aura's earliest days, when vendettas among patrician families wiped out whole bloodlines, as a sanctioned channel for violence beside, and as an alternative to, the courts; less open to corruption by the rich than the courts are.
- **Contracts (strict):** *righteous retribution* (most common) — the petitioner must prove the target is responsible for the **wrongful death of a family member or close friend**, and that death must not already have been compensated by a court or tribunal, an earlier assassination, or **personal action** already taken. A righteous-retribution death is never wrongful, so the Order never has to avenge its own work. Others: *merciful release* (killing someone kept alive against his will for others' benefit), *honourable passage* (killing someone at his own request to redeem shameful deeds), and a few esoteric forms. At the **Emperor's** request the Order kills enemies of state abroad (acts against the Empire are acts against his sacred person).
- **The Codex of Calefa** records every contract, who was killed and why; only members and the Emperor may read it. Famous rulings (all own-words summaries): a Varias murderer pardoned by his Keeper sister was killed at the Lazar son's petition; an armiger back from the legions too late to sue got retribution on the neighbour who raped and killed his daughter; a templar convicted at court of murdering his wife (property confiscated, five years' indenture) could not be targeted for her father — already punished; a Valerian knight who had already cut off the hand of the Drakomir duellist who killed his brother was refused — personal action taken.
- **Names:** patricians call it "the old justice"; commoners never name it, only "the workings of fate". Reformers who tried to abolish it have vanished or met ruinous misfortune.
- **The local chapter:** its location is known only to **Revered Mother Doarë**, **Prelate Ezelus**, a few trusted lieutenants and the members. **Intruders are warned away once, then attacked without mercy.** If intruders look strong enough to overrun the hideout, the members **evacuate with their copy of the Calefan Codex** and strike back later at a time and place of their choosing. Facing a threat beyond them, they call on the **senior chapter at Arganos**, the exarchate capital, for powerful retribution. AX3 p. 44 adds: the chapter is sanctioned by both the Temple of Justice and the Sisterhood of Mourning but answers to neither; Destrio is hunting the chaotic **Brotherhood of Knives**.

## Coming here as candidates (AX3 pp. 173-174)
- **The way in:** bring the **Assassins' Ledger** from the doppelgängers ([[Doppelganger Den]], #14u) to **Ezelus** (or win Doarë's trust; she is the only other who can introduce outsiders). Candidates are **bound, blindfolded and brought in by night** and vetted by **Destrio**. Reviewing the evidence, he explains that the **Brotherhood of Knives** was founded by **Zene**, a heretic Nicean assassin who left the Order and has been hunted since; the Order is glad that she and hers met a just end at the doppelgängers' hands, and that the beasts are dead too.
- **Vetting:** a reaction roll on `Tables/ax3-eclipse-vetting.yaml` (2d6 + leader's CHA modifier; +2 with an assassin in the party; +2 with a Lawful divine caster or paladin; +2 for an honourable reputation in the city; −2 for a dishonourable one; −4 if suspected of Chaotic members or Chthonic worship): **2- threat, attacked and killed if possible; 3-5 rejected as unworthy; 6-8 not yet worthy, may reapply after gaining a level; 9-11 worthy but watched 1d4 days first; 12+ invited at once as acolytes.**
- **Acolytes** (provisional members) swear utter secrecy and faithful obedience to the Order's lawful judgments; they serve as spies, scouts, assistants and specialists, but are too secular to fulfil sacred contracts for outsiders. To become a **deathbringer** (full member) an acolyte must complete a **sacred initiation**: a contract of righteous retribution in which the Order itself petitions for forgotten victims.
- **The initiation for the Ledger's bearers** — the criminals the Ledger exposes:
  - **Liber Faunus**, owner of The Mink, for murdering several merchants and a member of the Order of Travelers ([[Old District]]).
  - **Mentenus**, provost of the Tower of Knowledge, for murdering 2 elven travellers who did not deserve death ([[Tower District]]).
  - **Selene**, owner of the Silk Pony Inn, for murdering one of her servants and a priestess of Mityara ([[Festival District]]).
- **Refusers and the rejected** are offered **1,500gp for the Ledger** in exchange for an oath of secrecy about the Order, the Brotherhood and the Ledger. **Anyone who refuses both, or who rolled 2 with Destrio, is attacked and slain** — now if possible, later if necessary. Otherwise they are bound, blindfolded and returned to the surface.

## Layout
- **Entrance:** the secret door (Catacombs 10) gives onto a passage that reaches the **sewer canal**; the hideout's own **secret entrance 1** is a concrete-wall panel on the canal tunnel's inner side (map label "To Catacombs of the Ancestors" along the canal).
- From 1 a corridor runs inland (W on the page) — **pit trap** just inside — to a wide hall with a **second pit** at its far end (map shows two; AX3's text describes one: Judge's call, both identical), opening into the **central pillared hall** (a line of pillars down its middle).
- Round the central hall (map): the small shrine **2** off its east end, north side; dormitories **3** to the north and to the south-east; **Destrio's room 4** at the north-west; the **assembly chamber 5** at the west; the **supply corridor 6** links the hall's south-west to 5 and the south dormitory (two exits).

## Rooms (AX3 p. 174)

### 1. Secret Entrance
- **Exits:** E: the secret panel onto the sewer canal tunnel near the Catacombs; W: the dark corridor to the hall (map).
- From outside, a plain concrete wall in a sewer tunnel (find as a secret door). Just past it, the wide hall hides a **pit trap** triggered by a **cobblestone pressure plate** → JJ [[Rock-Cut Pit Trap]]: depth not given; Judge's call **10’ deep, 1d6 bludgeoning** (1st-level pit, no save). Find/disarm as a trap (Searching, Trapbreaking). The second pit nearer the central hall (map): same.
- The members know the plates and step round them; prisoners led in blindfold are guided across.

### 2. Shrine of the Seven-Spoked Wheel
- **Exits:** S into the central hall (map).
- A **seven-spoked wheel** (Calefa's holy symbol) of ash wood painted black hangs on the wall. A **Theology** proficiency throw recognises it as Calefa praised in her aspect of **deathbringer**.

### 3. Deathbringer Dormitories
- **Exits:** each opens on the central hall (north room S; south room N); the south one also on the supply corridor 6 (map).
- Each: **3 sleeping couches**, chests of personal effects, round tables with stools, an **oil lamp** on each table. (Treasure: the members' pouches only; personal chests Judge's call — AX3 gives no contents.)

### 4. Destrio's Dormitory
- **Exits:** E into the central hall (map).
- Bed, a chest of personal effects, and a **writing desk littered with correspondence in cipher** (readable with [[Discern Gist]] or code-breaking, Judge's call; likely Order business with Arganos and the hunt for the Brotherhood of Knives). A lit oil lamp on the desk.

### 5. Assembly Chamber
- **Exits:** E into the central hall; S to the supply corridor 6 (map).
- A **round table** for 10 on the north side. On the west wall a large **map of the Borderlands** painted on stitched leather panels; on the north-east wall a **ground-level map of Cyfaraun** on one great leather panel. Against the south-east wall a second **shrine to Calefa**: a **small golden wheel (4,000gp)** on a strong oak table; beneath it a **locked chest with 5,000gp**. The open floor before the shrine is used for training and prayer. Oil lamps on the table and on either side of the shrine.
- **Note:** the Order's copy of the **Codex of Calefa** is not placed by AX3; Judge's call: kept by Destrio (4 or 5) and taken in any evacuation.

### 6. Supply Corridor
- **Exits:** two exits (map: to 5 and to the central hall / south dormitory).
- Four small barrels along the north wall: **cured pork, pickled vegetables, hardtack, wine**. A narrow table and a small stove on the south wall between the exits; an oil lamp on the table.

## Treasure summary
Golden wheel 4,000gp and chest 5,000gp (5); members' pouches (Destrio 200gp; elites 10gp + 20sp each; deathbringers 1gp + 20sp each); Destrio's magic: sword +2, leather armour +2, amulet against scrying and ESP. Taking it makes the Order — and the senior chapter at Arganos — the party's enemy.
