# Tower of Knowledge — Fourth Story (the Great Orrery)

[[Tower District]] #50. One open round hall dominated by a great orrery of the heavens. Construction as [[Tower of Knowledge - Ground Story]]. Pages are printed pages.

Running: inhabited building; **no wandering monster checks** (AX3 gives none). Dungeon level 1 for treasure is a Judge's call. Used to train and practise **divination, enchantment, illusion and protection** magic.
- **Sunup to sundown:** an apprentice, 1d3 arcanists, 1d3 seers; **50%** a theurgist, **33%** a magician or thaumaturge, **25%** an enchanter or sorcerer; **5%** each officer, teaching. Also, most of the time, **1d2 journeyman scholars and 1d4 apprentice scholars** recording the astrological movements.

## Layout
- **Stairs:** NW buttress spiral stair: up to the rooftop ([[Tower of Knowledge - Fifth Story]]), down to the quarters ([[Tower of Knowledge - Third Story]]).
- **Centre:** the orrery **1-4** within a ring of six columns; the celestial sphere **5** is projected on the walls all round (keyed at N, E, S, W) (map p. 154).

## The orrery (AX3 p. 153, 155)
The Tower's astrologers count seven *quellari* ("wandering lights", planets) in the sphere of Cybele, named for the Empyrean gods (or the gods for them, or the gods *are* them, depending on the cosmogonist): **Ammonar, Mityara, Naurivus, Ianna, Türas, Istreus and Calefa**. Until the 2nd century IY the Tower held that all seven circled Cybele; the current model, used here, has the sun (Ammonar) and moon (Mityara) circling Cybele and the other five circling the sun. World, moon and sun are physical objects moving on gold-hued brass tracks; the other planets and the stars are a **dynamic permanent illusion** that floats them in the air and changes with the orrery. The illusion is multi-sensory: the chiming harmonies of the Celestial Spheres, the gentle warmth of the Empyrean fires.

## Features (AX3 p. 155)

### 1. Cybele
- **Exits:** centre of the orrery.
- The motionless central globe: a mosaic of stones showing the known continents and oceans (seas aquamarine, land sardonyx). One city only is marked: **Aura, by a diamond** labelled in gold filigree. Accurate around Aurëpos, conjecture elsewhere. (AX3 gives no value for the stones or diamond; Judge's call if prised out.)

### 2. Moon and Lunar Orbit
- **Exits:** around 1.
- Mityara as a **silver orb**; the dotted line marks its orbit round Cybele every **30 1/3 days**.

### 3. Sun and Solar Orbit
- **Exits:** around 1.
- Ammonar as a **gold orb**; its orbit round Cybele takes **364 days**. All the other planets orbit the sun.

### 4. Planetary Orbits
- **Exits:** the large dotted region round 3.
- The orbits of Naurivus, Ianna, Türas, Istreus and Calefa round the sun; as the sun moves, their illusory orbits shift to stay centred on it, tracing intricate epicycles.

### 5. Celestial Sphere
- **Exits:** the walls (N, E, S, W).
- The night sky projected onto the walls. Along the ecliptic, eleven great *zeogramma* (constellations), each about 1/12 of the ecliptic: the **Empress, the Cowl, the Eye, the Manticore, the Scorpion, the Naga, the Prince, the Wolf, the Kraken, the Egg and the Thunderbolt**; the last twelfth is a starless patch, **the Void**, counted as the twelfth constellation.
