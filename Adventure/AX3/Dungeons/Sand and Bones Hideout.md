# Sand & Bones Hideout (25u)

Cellar and secret rooms under the [[Silk Pony Inn - Ground Floor]], dug in volcanic tuff (stuccoed) by privately hired workers who all later died in "accidents". Outside Old Cyfaraun; **isolated from the rest of the Undercity**: the only way in is the inn's cellar stair. Lit by oil lamps (30’) in the Secret Office, Main Room and Cellar when in use; the Abattoir is dark even when Maximus feeds. Home of the monthly **Blood Moon** pit fights. Dungeon level 1 is the Judge's call.

Running: hub [[AX3 Hub]], district [[Festival District]] (Blood Moon procedure and betting there). **No wandering checks** (isolated; AX3 p. 64 rule for special locations); occupants as keyed by time of day. No wandering table.

## Layout
- **Entrance:** stair from the inn's annex (4 on the ground floor) down to the **Cellar 4** (north-east corner).
- **Cellar 4 → Main Room 3:** a stout oak door in the cellar's north wall, a short passage, and a stout oak door in the south wall of 3; one intricate lock (-4 to open; keys: Selene, Jonus, Thana, Lutarius, Maximus and every member of 4th level or more). Open on Blood Moon nights, locked otherwise.
- **Main Room 3 → Abattoir 2:** secret door (false wall) behind the buckets/barrels in 3's south-west corner, west wall (map).
- **Abattoir 2 → Secret Office 1:** secret door (false wall) in 2's west wall with a **portcullis trap**, then a long narrow passage west to 1 (map).
- Selene and Maximus are the only living people who know of room 1.

### 1. Secret Office
- **Exits:** passage east to the trapped secret door into 2 (map).
- Selene's financial records and private hoard. Two oak benches, a fine cedar writing desk, an upholstered cedar chair, three heavy iron-banded chests.
- After dark, **30%** Selene and Maximus are here (if not met already) → [[Selene Nictorian (AX3)]], [[Maximus Arjentüran (AX3)]].
- **Portcullis trap** on the secret door (false wall): unless disarmed, the first character through saves vs Blast or takes **3d6**; the way is then blocked and the party may be split. Raised by a lever hidden in the floor under the writing desk (on this side).
- **Chests** (intricate locks, -4 to open; keys Selene and Maximus only): (1) **9,000ep**; (2) **9,000gp** (three months of Guild operations); (3) relics of Selene's adventuring days: flawless faceted black sapphire (**10,000gp**), faceted star ruby (**6,000gp**), faceted star sapphire (**6,000gp**), necklace of human finger bones (**23gp**), two wrought copper armbands (**140gp each**).

### 2. Abattoir
- **Exits:** secret door (false wall behind two barrels) east to 3; trapped secret door (false wall, portcullis trap as in 1) west toward 1 (map).
- Dried blood, broken humanoid bones; **1d4-1 fresh corpses**. Maximus carries the Blood Moon dead here after the patrons leave and comes back through the month to eat them.
- After dark within a week of a Blood Moon night: **10%** Maximus is here feeding (then Selene is in the office 1).

### 3. Main Room
- **Exits:** stout locked oak door in the south wall (passage to the cellar 4); secret door (false wall behind the buckets) in the south-west corner to 2 (map).
- West side: three booths with long tables and chairs, tall curtained screens between them for discreet business. East side: a **5’ sunken pit of black-stained sand**, the Blood Moon arena. South-west corner: four open buckets, three of river water and one of assorted weapons and armor (`Tables/ax3-festival-bloodmoon-equipment.yaml`).
- **Ordinary times (round the clock):** 1d6 hoods, 1d4+1 thugs, 1 enforcer, 1 robber; 30% each a torturer, a slayer, a burglar, a rogue. Late afternoon to late evening, 30% each Selene (with her destroyer) and Jonus Krusaro → [[Jonus Krusaro (AX3)]].
- **Loot on the tables (not on Blood Moon nights):** 1d100gp and 1d100 x 10sp; 2 sets of superior thieves' tools (200gp each); 6 sets of thieves' tools (25gp each); a vial of wyvern venom (700gp; Death save at +1 or 7d6 poisonous damage after 1 round); 2 vials of henbane (350gp each; Death save +2 or 1d6 damage and madness 1d4 hours); 2 vials of carcass scavenger venom (250gp each; carrion horror venom: save +2 or paralysis 2d4 turns); a vial of yew (200gp; save +4 or 1d10 damage). (Values as AX3; ACKS II list prices 600 / 80 / 300 / 65gp.)
- **Blood Moon nights:** standing room only. **4d6 contestants** (about a third in shackles), **2d6+20 patrons** betting, a **bookmaker**, **Lutarius Sinorix** presiding → [[Lutarius Sinorix (AX3)]]; security **6 hoods, 6 thugs, 1 enforcer**. The leaders and high-level members above are each **60%** present; Maximus **30%**. Coin: Lutarius 1,200gp in a belt pouch (prize money); the bookmaker's iron lockbox **5d10 x 20gp**; the patrons together **4d10 x 100gp**. Equipment is cleared away. Procedure, bouts and combatants: [[Festival District]] ("Blood Moon games").

### 4. Cellar
- **Exits:** stair in the north-east corner up to the inn's annex; stout locked oak door in the north wall (to 3) (map).
- East half: storeroom with three barrels and a wine rack. West half: four cots, a long table and two benches for the staff.
- **Late night and early morning:** the cook, scullion and 1d2 tavernworkers asleep. **Other times:** 1d3 prostitutes, 1d2 tavernworkers, 30% one hood eating. **Blood Moon nights:** one hood directing guests into 3.
- Stores: barrel of vinegar (10gp, 8 st), barrel of barley beer (10gp, 8 st), barrel of fermented wheat porridge (5gp, 8 st), rack of 40 small amphorae of wine (5gp each, 1 st per 5).
