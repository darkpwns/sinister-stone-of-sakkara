# The Silk Pony Inn — Top Floor (25)

Guest rooms above the gambling hall. Dungeon level 1 is a placeholder (Judge's call).

Running: hub [[AX3 Hub]], district [[Festival District]]. **No wandering checks**; occupants as keyed. Ground floor → [[Silk Pony Inn - Ground Floor]] (statlines there).

## Layout
- **Entrance:** the stair up from beside the hearth in the common room.
- **Plan (map):** a central hallway with seven rooms off it (three on each long side, larger angled rooms in the wings).

## Wandering monsters
None (see Running).

### 7. Guest Rooms
- **Exits:** stair down to the common room; seven doors off the hallway (map).
- Seven rooms, each with two sleeping couches, two oak chests, a small oak table with stools. Nominal prices: bed **2sp** a night, room **5sp** a night; few lodge here. Most patrons rent a room for an hour (**1sp**) to consort with the inn's prostitutes.
- **1d6+1 rooms occupied**, each with **1d2 prostitutes** and **1d3-1 patrons**. A **footpad** (Thief 1, hp 3) patrols the hallway listening for trouble.
