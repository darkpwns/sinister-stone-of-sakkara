# The Mink — Argollëan Family Hideout (Special Location 11u)

Liber Faunus's hideout, redoubt and base of operations under [[The Mink - Tavern]], in an excavated pocket of Ancient Cyfaraun where the volcanic tuff has been cut away from the ruins. The **inner hideout (1-7)** was a villa (circa 650 BE) round a central courtyard and great hall: chipped stucco walls with vine-pattern crown moulding, pointed-arch window holes every 10’ in the outer walls showing unexcavated tuff. Lit by oil lamps every 30’. The **outer ring** (8-13) is left unlit so that it looks abandoned. Family background, members and revenue: [[Old District]] (Notable residents). AX3 pp. 85-88. "(map)" = read from the map.

Running: Undercity encounter throw every 3 turns, **5+ on 1d6 (below the Old District)**, 1d100 on `Tables/ax3-undercity-encounters.yaml` (Undercity table, written for [[Undercity of Cyfaraun]]); its 80+ special result near here = a Family robber, 1d3 hoods and 1d6 footpads moving one load of contraband to a warehouse (AX3 p. 66). No throws inside the hideout until it has been cleared (the occupants keep intruders out).

## Layout
- **From The Mink:** the stairwell (Mink 5) descends into the wine cellar **1**, in the inner block.
- **Inner hideout:** a square ring corridor encloses the inner block (wine cellar 1, dressing room 2, the secret treasure room 3 between them, and the flop-space hallway 5 on the west side). Guard post 4 at the ring's south-west corner; private office 6 off the ring's west side; Family headquarters 7 off its east side (map).
- **Hideout entrances:** **8** (south, end of a short passage from the ring's south side) and **9** (the ring's north-east corner), iron-reinforced oak doors to the outer ring (map).
- **Outer ring (unlit):** tuff tunnels run round the villa: west to the long north-south passage with the warehouse **11** (west) and the dungeon **10** (south-west); north to unexcavated ruin rooms; east to a long north-south passage; south to a large sewer tunnel running east-west along the bottom of the map. Access grates **12** (south, to the sewer) and **13** (north-east) (map).

## Mobilizing for war (AX3 p. 85)
If the Family is at open war with Sand & Bones and/or the party: up to **80 mercenary guards** here, and the numbers of (or chances for) the NPCs below **tripled**. 30% of the guards on duty: six each at 4, 8 and 9, six on patrol, the rest in the flop space 5 with the extra NPCs.
- **Mercenary guard:** Fighter 1: MV 30’/90’, AC 5 (chain and shield), HD 1 (hp 6), sword 10+, D 1d6+1, save F1, ML +1, AL N, XP 10; Alertness, Endurance; 6sp and 20cp.

## Inner hideout (AX3 pp. 85-87)

### 1. Wine Cellar
- **Exits:** stair up to The Mink (Mink 5); into the inner villa (to 2 and the ring corridor) (map).
- Vault of wine and spirits: 3 large amphorae vinegar (**10gp, 8 st each**), 3 large amphorae barley beer (**10gp, 8 st each**), 4 large amphorae Argollëan ambrosia (**200gp, 16 st each**), a rack of 140 small amphorae of wine (**5gp each, 1 st per 5**).

### 2. Dressing Room
- **Exits:** to 1 and the inner villa; the secret door to 3 lies in this block (map).
- Four dressing tables and two big wardrobes. Evenings: **1d2 comely prostitutes** (Bard 1, see [[The Mink - Tavern]]) refreshing their looks.
- **Cosmetics and perfumes:** 75gp per table (**300gp total**).
- **Wardrobes:** cleric's cassock (7gp), mage's robe (6gp), priestess's silk chiton (20gp), six silk dancer's breastwraps and skirts (12gp total), six hooded cloaks (6gp total), two armiger's dresses (40gp total), noblewoman's dress (100gp), two armiger's tunics (40gp total), nobleman's tunic (100gp); belts, gloves, hats, sashes, skullcaps and shoes (66gp total); costume jewellery (armlets, bracelets, earrings, necklaces, rings, waist chains; **500gp total**).
- With Disguise proficiency, the make-up and clothes allow many false identities.

### 3. Treasure Room
- **Exits:** secret door only (inner block, between 1 and 2) (map).
- **Secret door** sealed by **Magic Lock** (ACKS I wizard lock, 3rd level caster). Inside, a **blast rune** (Rune of Warding; ACKS I blast glyph of warding, 5th level, by a friendly cleric): entering without the password triggers it, **10 damage, Blast save for half (5)** (2 per caster level). Password, Classical Argollëan: **"cyfrinachaur cyndeidiau"** ("secrets of the ancestors").
- Vault of looted antiquities the Family buys and sells. Five chests: **15,000ep and 10,000gp**, all Argollëan mint. Sixth chest, elven orichalcum: armband engraved with battle scenes (**3,000gp**), laurel wreath (**4,000gp**), royal torque set with sapphires (**9,000gp**). Seventh chest: carefully wrapped crystal lenses in a patinated bronze frame, **eyes of the eagle** (apparent 250gp, actual **75,000gp**). Against the wall: **elven spear +1**, orichalcum leaf-shaped tip (apparent 650gp, actual **10,000gp**).
- **Traps:** all chests locked; the sixth and seventh chests' locks carry **alchemical poison: Death save (poison) or die**.
- Liber sells any of it to friends of the Family at the listed value.
- **Lore:** Knowledge (history) or Loremastery throw: the armband shows **Aedon Lann** using the **Bow of the Great Eagles** at the Battle of the Bleeding Point (the Bow: AX4, *Ruined City of Cyfandir*).

### 4. Guard Post
- **Exits:** the ring corridor's SW corner (map).
- Always **2 footpads** (statline above, plus crossbow 10+; Labor, Skulking; keys to 8 and 9; 6sp, 20cp each). If they hear 8 or 9 being bashed, one investigates and the other runs to the headquarters 7.

### 5. Flop Space
- **Exits:** the inner west hallway (map).
- A pair of pallets for members lying low. At war, the whole hallway fills with bedrolls, mercenaries, thugs, footpads and senior members.

### 6. Private Office
- **Exits:** door to the ring corridor's west side (map).
- **Door:** iron-reinforced oak, intricate lock (-4 to open; only Liber has a key) **and Magic Lock (3rd level caster)**.
- Writing desk, upholstered chair, locked iron safe, locked bronze chest (only Liber has keys); a brocade curtain hides an annex with two bookshelves and a file cabinet. **Liber here 33%** late afternoon to late evening, an enforcer outside the door.
- **Traps:** safe and chest locks: **poison needles, alchemical venom: Death save (poison) or die**; using the right key disarms them.
- **Iron safe** (the month's payroll): **4,000gp, 5,000sp, 15 amethysts (100gp each), 2 star sapphires (750gp each)**.
- **Bronze chest:** Liber's *The Book of Nine Arrows* by the elven general Aedon Lann, the secrets of the elven art of war: Military Strategy, scope 3, complexity 5, 600 pages, reading time 3 days, **1,275gp, 3 st**; 12” parchment codex, ivory clasp, lacquered cover showing nine arrows radiating from a shield.
- **Annex:** inventories and ledgers of all Liber's dealings, legal and illegal; the cabinet holds **246 blackmail files** on clerics, merchants, officials and senior Watch officers (mostly learned in the gambling snug). Worth **24,000gp to the Sand & Bones Guild** as a set; **3d12×5gp each**. Content of any NPC's file: `Tables/ax3-old-blackmail.yaml`.

### 7. Family Headquarters
- **Exits:** door to the ring corridor's east side (map).
- Ancient banquet hall: faded pastoral frescoes, mosaic floor like a flowered lawn, cleaned and refitted. South half: two big oak tables seating eight, a cedar bookcase (east wall), a cedar countertop (south wall). North half behind velvet drapes: six bunkbeds with a chest at each foot (safehouse after hijinks).
- **Occupants (round the clock):** **2d4 footpads, 1d6 hoods, 1d3 robbers**, led by a **burglar (75%)** or a **rogue (25%)**, perhaps with an **enforcer (40%)**, **torturer (20%)** and/or **slayer (20%)**. Late afternoon to late evening: **Megaravicos 33%**, **Liber 33%** (then an enforcer outside the door). Statlines above.
- **Countertop:** masterwork thieves' tools (**1,600gp**), 2 superior thieves' tools (**200gp each**), 4 thieves' tools companion kits (**35gp each**), 10 thieves' tools (**25gp each**); vial of rockfish venom (**500gp**: ACKS II giant rockfish venom, onset 1 round, save +1, 5d6 poisonous damage), vial of foxglove toxin (**275gp**: onset 1d6 turns ingested, save -3, 2d8 poisonous damage and madness 1d4 turns), vial of hemlock toxin (**225gp**: onset 2d4 turns ingested, save +4, 2d12 poisonous damage and queasy 1d10 days), 2 vials of spitting cobra venom (**100gp each**: onset 1 turn, save +2, 1d6 poisonous damage).
- **Bookcase:** maps of Cyfaraun, the Old District and the hideout; training manuals in Alchemy, Bargaining, Disguise, Gambling, Healing, Mapping, Signaling, Tracking and Trapping (scope 1, complexity 1, 1,000 pages, reading time 7 days, **200gp each**).

## Outer ring (AX3 p. 88)

### 8. Hideout Entrance (south)
- **Exits:** N passage to the ring corridor's south side; S/E into the unlit outer tunnels (map).
- Thick iron-reinforced oak door, iron lock; keys: Liber, Aranth, Hesta, Megaravicos and the footpads at 4. Bashing it is heard at 4.

### 9. Hideout Entrance (north-east)
- **Exits:** the ring corridor's NE corner; NE tunnel toward 13 (map).
- As 8.

### 10. Subterranean Dungeon
- **Exits:** off the outer tunnels south-west of the villa (map).
- Always locked; keys only with Family members of 4th level or higher. Lightless cell, moldy straw, stench of piss, sweat and blood; chains and manacles on the walls; an iron-rimmed seven-spoked **breaking wheel** on a wooden mast, a heavy mallet hanging behind it. Captives of the Family are held here, and those thought to know where elven antiquities lie are thoroughly interrogated. Liber dislikes cruelty, but lessons must sometimes be taught.

### 11. Subterranean Warehouse
- **Exits:** off the long west passage (map).
- Always locked; keys only with members of 4th level or higher. Depot for smuggled and stolen goods not yet fenced. **10%**: a major shipment has just arrived: **2d4 hoods and 2d6 footpads led by a burglar** unloading by oil-lamp light.
- Stock at any time: **2d10 loads of 2d4+1 merchandise types**; on average **66 loads worth 20,000gp, 4,620 st**.

### 12. Access Grate (south)
- **Exits:** the outer tunnels; S to the large sewer tunnel (map).
- Sealed iron gate, outer sign: "This Area Is Condemned — Danger of Collapse", stamped with the city seal (a bribed magistrate condemned it a decade ago). A dwarf or Engineering proficiency sees the area beyond is stable. The gate is not really sealed: its hidden latch is found as a secret door.

### 13. Access Grate (north-east)
- **Exits:** the outer tunnels (NE end of the passage from 9); the Undercity beyond (map).
- As 12.
