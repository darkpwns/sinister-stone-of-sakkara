# The Mink — Tavern (Special Location 11)

The most storied tavern in [[Cyfaraun]], in the [[Old District]] midway between the Cesta Türaduaran (Street of the Mighty Dawn) and the Emporium, among the homes of native merchants. Fresh beds, strong drink, rich food, music and companions for hire; well enough appointed to impress adventurers and visiting merchants. Open 16 hours a day (late morning to after midnight); most of the action is at night. Owned by **Liber Faunus**; underneath it lies the Argollëan Family's hideout: [[The Mink - Family Hideout]]. Two storeys: ground floor (1-5) and top floor (6-7). AX3 p. 81. "(map)" = read from the map.

Running: city building, no wandering checks; street encounters outside per [[Streets of Cyfaraun]] and the [[Old District]] special table. Patrons: roll Random Occupant by Building Type (General) ([[Buildings of Cyfaraun]], AX3 p. 52) when needed, otherwise normal men and women ([[Commoner]]: AC 0, hp 3, ML -1). Any fight here brings the Family's footpads and thugs at once, and the Family's enmity after.

> [!player] The Mink
> A busy, smoky tavern down an alley off the Street of the Mighty Dawn: an iron-barred oak door, glazed windows behind stout shutters, music and laughter late into the night. A huge stuffed mink glares down from above the bar, over a slate menu chalked with prices. Upstairs rooms rent by the hour or the night, and the companions are friendly.

## Layout
- **Main door:** onto an alley off the Cesta Türaduaran. Hard oak, iron-reinforced, two bars (-4 to proficiency throws to bash open) and an intricate lock (-4 to proficiency throws to open).
- **Windows:** iron frames with glass panes against break-ins; stout oak shutters can seal them.
- **Ground floor:** common room 1 with the bar; doorways north and east from the bar to the gambling snug 4 (NE) and the kitchen 2 (E), pantry 3 south of the kitchen; stairwell 5 in the common room's south-west corner, up to the top floor and down to the hideout's wine cellar ([[The Mink - Family Hideout]] 1) (map).
- **Top floor:** a hallway from the stair lined with private rooms 6 (four small, five large); the backroom/office suite 7 at its end (map).

## Ground floor (AX3 pp. 82-84)

### 1. Common Room
- **Exits:** main door (alley); doorways N and E from the bar (to 4 and 2); stairwell 5 in the SW corner (map).
- Bar and nine big tables, seats about 60; masonry hearth in the east wall by the bar. Behind the bar three kegs: water, barley beer, vinegar. The stuffed giant mink above the bar, the slate menu below it.
- **By day:** 1d3 tavernworkers (one behind the bar, the rest waiting tables), 2d6+2 patrons eating, 1d3 footpads and 1d3 thugs as security, 1d4 Family prostitutes working the room (2sp an hour, 1gp a night; a room upstairs costs extra, see 6).
- **By evening:** 3 tavernworkers, 2d4 prostitutes, 6d6+2 patrons; **Aranth Faunus behind the bar 70%**; 1d6 footpads and 1d4 thugs as security. Live entertainment every night: roll `Tables/ax3-old-mink-entertainment.yaml` (some acts add patrons).
- **Bar safe:** locked, built into the back of the bar; keys only with Liber, Hesta, Aranth and Megaravicos. Holds **3,500cp, 1,800sp, 60gp**.

### 2. Kitchen
- **Exits:** W to the bar (1); S to the pantry 3 (map).
- Masonry stove and grill in the west wall with cook pots in the flames; a long worktable of bowls, cauldrons, pots, kettles, pans and jars; cutlery rack and roasting spit above; shelves of terracotta tableware and **silverware** (value not given); a barrel of river water. During business hours a **cook and a scullion** (statlines above).

### 3. Pantry
- **Exits:** N to the kitchen 2 (map).
- 12 bags of vegetables (beets, capers, cucumbers, lentils, onions; **6gp, 48 st total**); 2 barrels mullet (**10gp, 16 st**); 2 barrels smoked pork (**10gp, 16 st**); 30 wheels local cheese (**9gp, 10 st**); 10 bricks salt (**7gp, 5 st**); 2 pouches saffron (**30gp**); 2 pouches garlic (**10gp**); 5 amphorae olive oil (**100gp, 30 st**); 2 amphorae honey (**40gp, 12 st**); 2 amphorae fish sauce (**40gp, 12 st**).

### 4. Gambling Snug
- **Exits:** door to the common room 1 (lockable from either side, all Family leaders have keys, barrable from inside); velvet curtains to two back rooms (map).
- Open each night; entry **5sp a head**. Front room: gaming table, seven chairs, bookcase of board games, cards and dice; a half-keg of free barley beer and a portable pantry of snacks (gamblers usually order better). Two back rooms with gaming tables and chairs.
- The Family takes **2% off the top** for guaranteeing security and privacy; thugs from 1 settle rowdy losers or "encourage" payment. Games: one roll per seven patrons on `Tables/ax3-old-mink-games.yaml`; pot usually **2d10gp per player**.
- **Present:** 2d6+2 patrons plus 1 per 5 customers in the common room (maximum 21); a tavernworker and 1d4 **comely prostitutes** per 10 patrons. With 10+ patrons, **Hesta Faunus is here 70%**. Fine bronze tableware, meals to order.
- Courtesans: **5sp an hour or 2gp a night**, private room extra. **Hesta: 325gp an evening**; she usually learns her bewitched companion's secrets.
- Most of Liber's blackmail files come from what is learned here (see Hideout 6).

### 5. Stairwell
- **Exits:** common room 1; up to the top-floor hallway (6); down to the wine cellar (Hideout 1).
- **One thug** from the common room guards the stair at the ground floor at all times.

## Top floor (AX3 p. 84)

### 6. Private Rooms
- **Exits:** off the top-floor hallway (map).
- Four small and five large rooms: brass-and-velvet sleeping couch and an oak trunk each; large rooms add oak table and chairs, one has a hearth. Trunks hold castanets, cords, fans, pessaries and veils.
- **Rent:** small 1sp an hour or 1gp a night; large 2sp an hour or 2gp a night (3sp / 3gp with the hearth). Used for the prostitutes' trade, private parties, gambling, business meetings.
- **Occupancy:** 2d4 rooms in use, each with 1d2 prostitutes and 1d3-1 patrons. **A thug** patrols the hallway listening for trouble.

### 7. Backroom / Office
- **Exits:** single door to the hallway (map); inner door from the office to the bedchamber.
- **Door:** two reinforced bars (-4 to proficiency throws to bash open) and an intricate lock (-4 to proficiency throws to open); keys only with Liber, Hesta, Aranth and Megaravicos.
- **Outer room (Liber's public office):** writing table and stool, round table with two chairs, a large chest; elven antiquities displayed on the table. **Liber Faunus at his desk 30%** in the late afternoon and evening, then guarded by an **enforcer**. Paperwork of little value (his real files are in the Hideout's private office 6).
- **Backroom:** private bedchamber: double bed, writing desk and stool, hearth, another large chest. At night and early morning **Liber sleeps here 30%**, beside 1d4-1 comely prostitutes, with an enforcer on guard in the office.
- **Treasure:** chryselephantine unicorn statuette with onyx horn (**500gp, 2/6 st**); tortoise-shell lyre with mother-of-pearl (**100gp, 1/6 st**); pair of red-figure terracotta bowls of elven heroes (**15gp, 1/6 st each**). The largest chest: **800gp and 3,000sp**. (The second chest's contents are not given.)
