# Tower of Knowledge — Basement (#50u)

[[Tower District]] #50u. Delved by Auran engineers into the volcanic tuff (the Tower stands outside the bounds of Ancient Cyfaraun); rough-hewn walls, floors and ceilings surfaced with stucco. A round central chamber (the Thaumaturge Library) at the foot of the stair, with the workshop, vault and crossbreeding laboratory behind secret doors off it, and a curved corridor arcing north and west round it to a ring of private studies and the treasury, all behind warded secret doors. Pages are printed pages; "(map)" = read from the appendix map.

Running: hub [[AX3 Hub]]. Inside the basement **no wandering monster checks** (AX3 gives none: lit, warded, inhabited); occupants by room. Only members of **thaumaturge rank (5th level)** or above may come down. Dungeon level 1 for treasure is a Judge's call. Beyond the sewer gate (10) the sewers are the Undercity: wandering monsters there from `Tables/ax3-undercity-encounters.yaml` ([[Undercity of Cyfaraun]]).
- **Doors:** every secret door is **wizard locked** ([[Magic Lock]]) at **8th level**, except the Regent's study (1) at **10th** and the Provost's study (2) at **9th**. The wizard-locked chests in the studies are the occupants' own locks (caster level not given; use the owner's level, Judge's call); the six Arcane Vault chests are locked by the Treasurer (8th).
- **Lighting:** *continual light* ([[Illumination, Perpetual]]) on every room's ceiling and every 30’ along the corridor; over 30 members can cast 2nd level spells (AX3; *continual light* is ACKS II 3rd level), so it is easily kept up.
- **Alarm:** the magic mouth at 9 alerts the subaltern at the stair head ([[Tower of Knowledge - Ground Story]] room 7) and every mage on this floor.

## Layout
- **Entrance:** stairs **15** from the ground-story storefront (NW of the stair head) descend **15’** into the north-west side of the round Thaumaturge Library **11**.
- **Off the library 11:** secret doors to the Crossbreeding Laboratory **14** (W), the Arcane Workshop **13** (SW) and the Arcane Vault **12** (S) (map).
- **Curved corridor:** leaves 11 on its east side by the sewer gate **10**, then arcs north and west round the library. Secret doors off its outer (east) side, south to north: Treasury **8**, Transmogrifiers' study **7**, Warmages' study **6**, Unassigned study **5**; then the magic-mouth zone **9**; then along the north arc, east to west: Wizards' Library **4**, Treasurer's study **3**, Provost's study **2**, Regent's study **1** (the corridor ends at 1) (map).
- **Sewers:** a sewer tunnel runs from the north-west round inside the corridor's arc; the locked grate **10** at the corridor's south end opens into a sewer running south-east to a crossing tunnel ([[Undercity of Cyfaraun]]) (map).

## Rooms (AX3 pp. 156-159)

### 1. Regent's Private Study
- **Exits:** secret door (wizard locked, **10th level**) to the west end of the corridor (map).
- Opposite the door, a large **oil-painted map on the leathered hide of a huge bull**: the Borderlands and what little is charted of the Wastes, with points cryptically marked by sigil combinations (the spikes his agents have planted).
- Table by the map: Aurëus's **[[Crystal Ball]]**, a stack of **embossed iron spikes** with unique sigil combinations, hard iron stamping tools and hammers. Adventurers who travel into the Wastes for him are given spikes to drive in at key landmarks (passes, fords); the map marks the points (pay: [[Tower District]]).
- Left of the door: a 7’ oak bookshelf with his private journals, notes on suspected **beastman tribe** locations and appraisals of their war-chiefs and shamans, and **30 codices** on battle magic, military history, siege engineering and cartography (**4,500gp, 15 st**). Beside it a wizard-locked chest of **4,300gp**.
- **Aurëus Ambador** here **25%** by day: [[Aurëus Ambador (AX3)]].

### 2. Provost's Private Study
- **Exits:** secret door (wizard locked, **9th level**) to the corridor (map).
- Opposite the door, a resplendent **15’×10’ tapestry** of Aura at dawn, the sun's rays woven in real gold thread (**1,000gp**). Hidden on its back: **four life-sized anatomical drawings of human and elven vivisection**, male and female, on large vellum panels (**150gp each**). A character with **Healing** who studies them finds them highly accurate: drawn from personal experience or forbidden tomes.
- Left of the door: shelves of a dozen glass jars of small dead animals (frogs, rats, snakes) in embalming fluids, and **ten small stoppered bottles of blood-coloured fluid (6gp each) labelled "rat lycanthropy"**. On the research table, three frogs pinned belly-up in small arcane circles of rare mineral salts, partly vivisected. Under the table a wizard-locked chest of **2,300gp**.
- Right of the door: a 7’ bookshelf with the Provost's **research journals** and **25 books** on astrology, history, lycanthropy and summoning (**3,750gp, 12.5 st**); beside it a fine life-sized tapestry of the Provost in his robes (**250gp**).
- **The journals** are in cipher: readable by magic ([[Discern Gist]]) or a successful proficiency throw to read languages (thief Deciphering; Judge's call for other readers). Decrypted: his research into elven fragments across the Borderlands; his conclusion that elven agelessness came from their ancestors' necromantic experiments, which he means to repeat to give himself and mankind eternal youth; ancient **elven tombs beneath Cyfaraun**; his work with recently expelled adjuncts and scholars to find entrances to the ruins from the sewers; and that he **infected his associates with wererat lycanthropy** to make them better searchers and protect them from the sewers' deadly diseases. **Delivered to the Prefect, the Regent or the Patriarch, they get Mentenus arrested.**
- **Mentenus Cavië** here **50%** by day, **25%** at night: [[Mentenus Cavië (AX3)]].

### 3. Treasurer's Private Study
- **Exits:** secret door (wizard locked, 8th level) to the corridor (map).
- Left of the door: shelves of his private collection of **monster parts** (special components): **100 giant hawk feathers (445gp, 4 st)**, **25 sphinx feathers (405gp, 5 st)**, **24 griffin feathers (170gp, 2 st)**, **16 owlbear feathers (60gp, 4/6 st)**, **8 wyvern horns (320gp, 4 st)**, **2 yali horns (250gp, 4/6 st)**, **a gorgon horn (540gp, 9 st)**.
- Right of the door: a large bookshelf, his library of **21 books** on alchemy, mathematics and magical engineering (**3,150gp, 10.5 st**).
- Centre: a large table stacked with the Chapterhouse's financial files and inventory notes, including **detailed descriptions of the treasure in the Wizards' Library (4), the workshop (13) and the Treasury (8)**. Under it a wizard-locked chest of **750gp**.
- (AX3 heads it "Chapterhouse Treasure's Private Study".)

### 4. The Wizards' Library
- **Exits:** secret door (wizard locked, 8th level) to the corridor, just west of the magic-mouth zone 9 (map).
- Private collection of **226 books (34,000gp, 113 st)**, restricted to the Regent, Provost and Treasurer and visiting members of **wizard rank (9th level+)**. Kept apart centuries ago as too dangerous for young adjuncts and scholars.
- **Forbidden necromantic formulas:** one book holds [[Necromantic Potence]] (**100gp**), another *animate dead* ([[Animate Undead]], **1,500gp**).
- ***The Dawn and Dusk of Argollë*:** captured from the elves; outwardly an epic poem of elven history, it carries esoteric knowledge of the black arts. Format: codex; language: Ancient Argollëan; apparent topic: Performance (epic poetry); esoteric topic: Black Lore of Zahar, Knowledge (history – Ancient Argollë) 4; apparent scope 1, esoteric scope 3; apparent complexity 2, esoteric complexity 6; 500 pages; reading time / reference time 3 days / 3 turns; **apparent value 5,000gp, esoteric value 25,000gp**; 2.5 st. (Book format per AX3's Codex & Scroll supplement; ACKS II [[Black Lore of Zahar]].)

### 5. Unassigned Private Study
- **Exits:** secret door (wizard locked, 8th level) to the corridor, east of 9 (map).
- Kept for a future member of **sorcerer rank (7th level)** or above: a large table, two chairs, an empty bookcase. A PC of 7th level or higher who joins the order may be assigned it.

### 6. Warmages' Private Study
- **Exits:** secret door (wizard locked, 8th level) to the corridor (map).
- Private research room of **two College of War collegians (both 7th level mages)**; **50%** chance each is here by day. Centre: a large table with an intricate **brass orrery (1,000gp, 1 st)**, a miniature of the great orrery. Under it two wizard-locked chests: **1,280gp** and **2,280gp**.
- Left of the door: a bookcase of codices on battle magic and elemental magic, with their research journals (**4,260gp, 14 st**). A character with **Elementalism** who spends **one hour (6 turns)** reading the journals learns they believe **fireballs may deal more damage than expected at high noon on the summer solstice**.
- Opposite the door: vellum panels showing how elemental spell signatures appear at different times of year by the positions of the celestial bodies (**300gp each**; number not given). Right of the door: buckets of sand, charred rags and burnt refuse.
- **Offer:** the warmages pay spellcasting fees to any adventuring mage who writes detailed accounts of the elemental spells he casts (when, where, observed effects) for their study ([[Spellcasting Services]] rates, Judge's call).
- Warmages (2): Mage 7; MV 40’ / 120’; AC 0; hp 7d4 each (Judge's call, avg 17); dagger 8+, D 1d4, or spell; 3/2/2/1 spells (War College: e.g. [[Mage Missile]], [[Fireball]], [[Thunderbolt]]); Save M7; ML 0; AL L; XP 440 each; Battle Magic, Elementalism.

### 7. Transmogrifiers' Private Study
- **Exits:** secret door (wizard locked, 8th level) to the corridor (map).
- Private research room of **two College of Transmogrification collegians (both 7th level mages)**. Two tables, left and right of the door, crowded with highly specialised alchemical apparatus — alembic, calcinatory, retort, beakers, flasks, vats (**7,200gp, 24 st total**). Under each table a wizard-locked chest of **1,000gp**. Opposite the door, between the tables, a bookcase of **30 codices** on alchemy, animal husbandry, baking, cooking and dwarven brewing (**4,500gp, 15 st**).
- Transmogrifiers (2): as the warmages above (XP 440 each); AX3 gives no presence chance (Judge's call: 50% each by day, as room 6).

### 8. Chapterhouse Treasury
- **Exits:** secret door (wizard locked, 8th level) to the corridor's south-east end, near the gate 10 (map).
- Most of the Tower's mundane wealth: two wizard-locked chests of **5,000gp each (10,000gp)**; a crate of fine porcelain (**500gp, 2 st**) used only for refectory banquets; **30 pieces of ivory** hanging on the walls (**1,800gp, 18 st total**).

### 9. Magic Mouth
- **Exits:** a stretch of corridor between 4 and 5 (dotted on the map).
- A **magic mouth**† covers this stretch. Anyone entering it **without first donning a mage's cassock** triggers it: in Common, it greets the trespasser, says his presence is forbidden and promises obliteration unless he returns upstairs at once. Loud enough to alert the guards at the stair (Ground Story 7) and every mage on this floor. († ACKS I spell; no ACKS II arcane equivalent.)

### 10. Sewer Gate
- **Exits:** corridor (N, and W into 11); SE through the grate into the sewers ([[Undercity of Cyfaraun]]).
- A **locked iron grate** to the sewers below. All officers have a key, but Aurëus has also **wizard locked it at 10th level** so no one can open it. (Mentenus has a key to "Basement 10" and every reason to want it open.)

### 11. Thaumaturge Library
- **Exits:** NW stairs 15; secret doors W to 14, SW to 13, S to 12; E to the corridor and gate 10 (map).
- Round reading and research room for members of **thaumaturge rank and above**: **906 books (136,000gp, 453 st)**, including formulas for **every standard 3rd and 4th level arcane spell** of the blast, detection, enchantment, illusion, movement, protection, summoning and transmogrification types. The books are **chained** to three large bookcases round the north-east wall; reading stools by them, a large work desk with two stools on the south-west wall.
- **Sunup to sundown:** 1d3 thaumaturges studying; **60%** an enchanter, **40%** a sorcerer, **5%** each officer (statlines above).
- (Inferred: this round chamber at the stair foot is the "large magical research laboratory used by all members" of AX3 p. 150; the apparatus is in 13.)

### 12. Arcane Vault
- **Exits:** secret door N to 11 (map).
- Stores special components and magic items. Two long tables hold **metamphorae** of special components; **six chests**, all wizard locked by the Treasurer, hold the Chapterhouse's magic items (captured from the elves in the conquest of Southern Argollë, sold by ignorant adventurers, or made by collegians). On sale at the storefront: components at **110%** of market value, items at **twice base cost**. ACKS II readings: [[AX3 Magic Items]].
  - **Chest 1:** potion of ESP†; potion of extra healing (as [[Potion of Recuperation]], Judge's call); potion of gaseous form (as [[Gaseous Form]]); 2 potions of healing (as [[Cure Light Injury]]); 2 potions of poison; potion of polymorph (as [[Potion of Monstrous Form]]); potion of sweet water (purifies water; Judge's call); potion of treasure finding (as [[Locate Treasure]]).
  - **Chest 2:** arcane scrolls in Classical Auran of burning hands ([[Fan of Flames]]), sleep ([[Slumber]]), mirror image ([[Illusory Duplicates]]), stinking cloud†, web ([[Webbing]]) and wall of ice ([[Wall of Frost]]); scroll of ward against elementals ([[Scroll of Creature Warding]], elementals); scroll of ward against magic ([[Scroll of Magic Warding]]); **treasure map to an elven shrine in the Viaspen Forest** (Borderlands Gazetteer #21) and **treasure map to an elven watchtower in the Madoan Hills** (Gazetteer #45), both in Common Argollëan ([[Treasure Map]]).
  - **Chest 3:** ring of fire resistance ([[Ring of Fire Protection]]); [[Ring of Protection]] +1; [[Ring of Water Walking]].
  - **Chest 4:** wand of detecting magic ([[Wand of Magic Discernment]], 15 charges); wand of detecting metals ([[Wand of Metal Location]], 10 charges); wand of detecting secret doors (9 charges; ACKS I item, no ACKS II wand — Judge's call); wand of magic missiles ([[Wand of Mage Missiles]], 14 charges).
  - **Chest 5:** [[Bracers of Armor]] AC 4; brazier of commanding fire elementals (as [[Crucible of Conjuring Fire Elementals]]); elven cloak (as [[Cloak of the Elven Ranger]]); rope of climbing (as [[Rope of Ascent]]).
  - **Chest 6:** contents not given in AX3 (Judge's call).
  - **Metamphorae (special components):** amphisbaena carcass **340gp**; chimera wingtips **400gp**; basilisk gizzard **500gp**; ettin's Y-shaped spinal cord **850gp**; gorgon's horns **400gp**; great white shark's skull **600gp**; hill giant's pituitary gland **700gp**; lamia's tongue **400gp**; sabre-tooth tiger's tusks **300gp**; skittering maw's dorsal fin **500gp**; sphinx's heart **1,200gp**; wyvern's stinger **360gp**; wyvern's talons **360gp**; wyvern's wingtips **300gp** (total 7,210gp).

### 13. Arcane Workshop
- **Exits:** secret door NE to 11 (map).
- Cluttered with magical contraptions and apparatus for making magic items (**45,000gp, 150 st**). Scorch marks all over the stucco from repeated fiery accidents.
- **Sunup to sundown:** 1d3 thaumaturges at magic research; **60%** an enchanter, **40%** a sorcerer, **5%** each officer.

### 14. Crossbreeding Laboratory
- **Exits:** secret door E to 11 (map).
- Two large vats of embalming fluid along the north and south walls, hoses and tubes running to a worktable on the far wall, littered with embryos in jars, anatomical drawings of dissected animals, surgical tools and devices: a small **crossbreeding laboratory (15,000gp, 50 st)** ([[Crossbreeds]]). Rarely used: no current member is of high enough level to crossbreed monsters.

### 15. Stairs
- **Exits:** up 15’ to the ground story ([[Tower of Knowledge - Ground Story]] room 7, guarded by the subaltern); SE into 11.
