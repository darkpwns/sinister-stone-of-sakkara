# Imperial Amphitheater — Arena and Stands (20)

The focus of Cyfaraun's social calendar (to the Patriarch's and the Reverend Mothers' chagrin): victory parades, classical Nicean masked dramas, monthly gladiatorial games and the seasonal festivals that mark the Winged Sun's journey. Public property of the Office of the Prefect, administered by the city's censor; in practice managed by **Maximus Arjentüran**, who rents the cells below and must be consulted for every use of the grounds. Concrete-and-stucco ellipse 260’ × 220’, 7,500 seats round a 10,000 sq ft sanded false floor. Dungeon level 1 is a placeholder (Judge's call): this is a public building, not a delve.

Running: hub [[AX3 Hub]], district [[Festival District]] (its "Running gladiatorial games and betting" section has the full procedure). **No wandering checks** inside; in the surrounding streets use the district encounter rate and `Tables/ax3-festival-special.yaml`. Events: `Tables/ax3-festival-amphitheater-daily.yaml` (ordinary days) or `Tables/ax3-festival-amphitheater-festival-hourly.yaml` (festival days).

## Layout
- **Ground storey (map, upper plan):** an outer ring of massive black piers with arched entrances all round; between them stairways up into the stands, **concession stands** (north-east) and **latrines** (east and south, two blocks). A broad passage runs in from the west straight to the arena wall (the processional entrance) and others cut through from the east.
- **Stands (map, lower plan):** two tiers of seating separated by a walkway with vomitoria; concession stands at the head of the north stairs; latrines south and south-east.
- **Arena:** the central ellipse, sand over a wooden false floor with trap doors over the basement lifts.
- **Down:** the stairs at the southern edge of the arena surface to the guarded basement stairs 10, and a separate stair at the south edge of the sands, under the first tier, to holding room 13 → [[Imperial Amphitheater - Basement]]; the lift trap doors in the arena floor.

## Wandering monsters
None (see Running). On game days the crowd itself is the hazard: the district special encounter 1 (crowd) applies in the approaches.

### Arena floor
- **Exits:** trap doors of lifts 1, 3, 8, 12 (lift 2 broken); the gates to the processional passage (west) and to the basement stairs (south) (map).
- Sand-topped false floor, 10,000 sq ft. Bouts, executions, races (sprint one lap; long run seven laps), animal shows and dramas take place here.

### The stands
- **Exits:** stairways from the ground-storey ring (map).
- 7,500 seats. On game and festival days: full; street performers play among the crowd, vendors sell cheap food, and **Sand & Bones bookmakers** loiter in the corridors leading to the seats taking bets on every competitive event and blood sport (see the district note for odds and the "lucky streak" rule).

### Prefect's Box
- **Exits:** into the east stands (map).
- East wing, room for 50: the prefect's family, guests, guards and attendants. A 10’ statue of **Tarkaun Mennic II** (emperor at the conquest of Cyfaraun from the elves). The ranking officer present, normally the prefect, sits on a raised throne-like seat in the statue's shadow; coloured awnings shade the rest of the party.
- The **Inspector of the Games** (Mara Rëavus, Mage 5 → [[Mara Rëavus (AX3)]]) attends every game day to cast detect magic on each gladiator and dispel magic during random bouts; she now only pretends to.

### Concession stands and latrines
- **Exits:** the ground-storey ring (map).
- Food vending here is Sand & Bones business (Thana Nyssand's legitimate side); half the Guild's thieves protect the vendors and bookies.
