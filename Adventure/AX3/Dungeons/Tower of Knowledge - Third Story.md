# Tower of Knowledge — Third Story (the Chapterhouse Living Quarters)

[[Tower District]] #50. The three officers' quarters along the north wall, recreation lounges beside them, apprentices' screened cubicles down both sides, and the kitchen and refectory in the south. Construction as [[Tower of Knowledge - Ground Story]]. Pages are printed pages.

Running: inhabited building; **no wandering monster checks** (AX3 gives none); occupants by room and time. Dungeon level 1 for treasure is a Judge's call.
- **Magical protections:** while an officer sleeps in his bedroom, its door is **wizard locked** ([[Magic Lock]]) and warded with a **magic mouth**† that wakes him if the door is opened. († ACKS I spell with no ACKS II arcane equivalent: an audible alarm.)
- **Noise:** the dormitory screens are thin; apprentices who make noise late at night are often put to sleep by their elders ([[Slumber]]).

## Layout
- **Stairs:** NW buttress: spiral stair down to the library and **up to the orrery** ([[Tower of Knowledge - Fourth Story]]); NE buttress: spiral stair down to the library ([[Tower of Knowledge - Second Story]]).
- **North:** Provost's quarters **1** (NW, by the NW stair), Regent's quarters **2** (north centre), Treasurer's quarters **3** (NE, by the NE stair), separated by partitions and doors onto a short passage between two columns (map).
- **Lounges 4:** one west and one east of the officers' rooms.
- **Dormitories 5:** screened cubicles filling the west and east sides; latrines on the west and east walls.
- **South centre:** kitchen and refectory **6** (two long tables, stove on the south wall) (map).

## Wandering monsters
None. Occupants per room below. Officers: [[Aureus Ambador (AX3)]], [[Mentenus Cavie (AX3)]], [[Clitus Omnus (AX3)]].

## Rooms (AX3 p. 153)

### 1. Provost's Quarters
- **Exits:** door to the passage before the officers' rooms; the NW stair is beside it (map).
- Fine furniture imported from Aura; a sitting table in the centre with an incense burner for daily meditation.
- **Mentenus Cavië:** **25%** here by day, **75%** asleep here by night (door wizard locked and magic-mouthed). Stats: [[Mentenus Cavie (AX3)]].

### 2. Regent's Quarters
- **Exits:** door to the passage (map).
- Luxurious, furnished fit for the great patricians. A small stand in the centre holds a **marble bust of Quintus Odysios**, first Regent, with fragrant oils burning before it. By the great bed, a sitting table with a **kyrpeges game in mid-play** and a stack of open letters on small pieces of parchment from the **Archregent of Aura**, each a single sentence giving the next move.
- **Aurëus Ambador:** **30%** here by day, **95%** asleep here by night (door wizard locked and magic-mouthed). Stats: [[Aureus Ambador (AX3)]].

### 3. Treasurer's Quarters
- **Exits:** door to the passage; the NE stair is beside it (map).
- Modest and sparse, to remind the officer responsible for all the treasure that he must be above greed. (AX3 calls them the "Provost's" quarters here, a slip.)
- **Clitus Omnus:** **30%** here by day, **95%** asleep here by night (door wizard locked and magic-mouthed). Stats: [[Clitus Omnus (AX3)]].

### 4. Recreation Lounges
- **Exits:** open to the dormitory aisles and the officers' passage (map).
- Couches, small tables, comfortable chairs, bookcases of books, tabletop games and dice. **Evenings:** 1d2 apprentices, 1d10 arcanists, 1d6 seers; **50%** a theurgist, **33%** a magician or thaumaturge, **25%** an enchanter or sorcerer. **+1 to reaction rolls** here (convivial).
- **5%** chance Aurëus Ambador is running a role-playing game in which 1d4+1 members play the crew of an astral warship on a five-year mission to explore other celestial spheres; the party is invited to join. Players earn **ACKS XP equal to 1% of the XP their character earns inside the game** (AX3's joke on how mages advance without adventuring; Judge's call whether to allow it).

### 5. Dormitories
- **Exits:** aisles to the lounges and the refectory (map).
- Most mages live in Tower-owned townhouses nearby; **apprentices** (0-level students) must live here under supervision. Each has a small room with a sleeping couch and a chest of personal belongings, divided by thin wooden screens. Apprentices present: **15%** by day, **50%** in the evening, **95%** at night.

### 6. Kitchen and Refectory
- **Exits:** N aisle between the dormitories (map).
- Two long tables seating 10 each; a masonry stove with a grill built into the wall; counters with terracotta bowls, cauldrons, cook-pots, kettles, pans and jars.
- **Stores:** 6 bags of vegetables (3gp, 24 st total); 10 wheels of local cheese (3gp, 3 st); 10 bricks of salt (7gp, 5 st total); 2 pouches of herbs and spices (10gp total); 2 amphorae of olive oil (40gp, 12 st); 2 amphorae of honey (40gp, 12 st total); 2 amphorae of fish sauce (40gp, 12 st total); past the counters two wine racks of **20 bottles each (200gp, 8 st)**.
- Apprentices rotate weekly through buying, cooking and cleaning. **One of the three officers** is at any given meal; **all three** dine together once a week.
