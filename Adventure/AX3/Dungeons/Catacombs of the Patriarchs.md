# Catacombs of the Patriarchs — Bridge District #36u

Catacombs under the [[Solar Citadel]] ([[Bridge District]], [[Cyfaraun]]), cut by Auran engineers from solid volcanic tuff outside the bounds of Old Cyfaraun and rendered with stucco. Here lie the cremated remains of the Borderlands' leading civil and military officials; the Patriarchs' tombs are the most honoured, and almost as much the silver-adorned columbarium of the **Captain of the Underwatch**, an aged, nameless paladin (an Auran noble by his accent and manners) who led the night patrols into the sewers when wererats were spreading their disease after the Grey Plague and the soldiers would no longer go below, cleansed the city, and stayed on, praying, patrolling and helping the Watch, until he died of age; the city gave him a great requiem. **Lighting:** perpetual light every 30’ keeps the whole level bright as day (sustained by the Patriarch, Prelate and Rector). Pages are printed pages; "(map)" = read from the map, p. 215 (north is up when the page is turned so the title reads normally). Hub [[AX3 Hub]]. `level: 1` is a Judge's call.

Running: AX3 makes no encounter throws inside a special location until it is cleared (its occupants keep intruders out); once cleared, Undercity throws apply: 1d6 every 3 turns, 6+ → 1d100 on `Tables/ax3-undercity-encounters.yaml` (not below the Old District, so not 5+). AX3 gives no wandering table of its own. The Undercity table's special entry for this site (80+, within 120’): 1d4 Knights of the Winged Sun with torches on patrol, hunting vermin "two-legged, four-legged, or worse" (AX3 p. 67). Glyph blasts are heard: a blast draws any surviving clerics or guards in the catacombs, and has a **75%** chance to bring the citadel guards from above (AX3's figure for at least one of eight hear-noise throws). By day visitors pay 1gp at [[Solar Citadel]] 16; at night only on the Patriarch's business.

## Layout
- **Entrances:** (a) stairs **1** from [[Solar Citadel]] 17; (b) the warded, locked oak door in the east wall of **2**, onto the main north-south sewer; (c) a door at the west end of the long hall **4** (map; leads out into the Undercity, unkeyed); (d) the secret doors of **9** are internal.
- **North block:** from the T-shaped landing of 1 a short corridor runs south; **2** (chapel) opens east of it, **3** (Patriarchs) west. 3 opens west onto **4**, the long east-west hall of clergy urns running west along the north side of an east-west sewer. At 4's west end the curving hall **5** bends north-east to **6** (the Captain's columbarium); a secret door in 6's north wall opens into the vault **7** (map).
- **Ring:** the corridor from 1 ends in the circular **elevated corridor 8**, which bridges over the east-west sewer running through its middle (no way down to the sewer).
- **South block:** from the south of the ring the **ceremonial corridor 9** runs south to a T; its east and west end walls are warded secret doors. West and east of 9, two halls run south from the ring: **10** (tribunes) in their northern parts, **11** (centurions) further south; the western hall curves round the south end into **13** (elf lords), which joins **12** (honourable foe, south-east) and the eastern hall. The secret doors of 9 open into these halls (map). A second sewer runs north-south past the east side; across it to the east lie unkeyed pillared rooms (map only).

## Wards
Patriarch Arëtar's **glyphs of warding** → ACKS II [[Rune of Warding]] (effects as AX3 states them):
- **Curse (prime requisite):** save vs Spells or the toucher's prime requisite is halved ([[Remove Curse|bestow curse]]).
- **Curse (combat):** save vs Spells or −4 to attack throws and saving throws.
- **Blast:** lightning, everyone within 7’ takes **20 electrical damage**, half on a save vs Blast. Draws the guards (see Running).

### 1. Stairs
- **Exits:** up to [[Solar Citadel]] 17; S along a corridor to 8; off that corridor W to 3 and E to 2 (map).
- Stairs down from the Citadel ending on a T-shaped landing.

### 2. Chapel of the Honored Dead
- **Exits:** W to the corridor from 1; E a thick, iron-ribbed oak door to the sewer, **locked** and warded with a **curse rune** (whoever opens it: save vs Spells or prime requisite halved).
- Marble altar; behind it a life-sized statue of **Audarius Tarkaun** between two Auran legionaries; six pews. The altar honours the Auran Army's fallen veterans and makes the chapel an **illuminated pinnacle of good** ([[Pinnacles of Good]]).
- **2 catechists** always here chanting for the dead's service to the Empire → [[Catechist of Ammonar (AX3)]]: [[Crusader]] 1, MV 40’ / 120’, AC 0, hp 4 each, mace 10+, D 1d6, Save C1 (AX3 +2), ML +1, AL L, XP 10 each; Divine Blessing, Theology; holy symbol, holy book, white cassock with yellow fringe.
- By day, **25%** for 1d4 worshippers ([[Commoner]]) paying respects.

### 3. Columbaria of the Patriarchs
- **Exits:** E to the corridor from 1; W into 4 (map).
- Four large columbaria, each with an ornate brass cinerary urn etched with suns (**50gp, 1 st each**) under a lifelike marble bust of the former Patriarch inside (**50gp, 3 st each**).
- **2 catechists** chanting (as 2).
- **Wards:** each bust and each urn separately: **touching a bust** → curse (save vs Spells or prime requisite halved); **touching an urn** → blast (20 electrical, 7’, save vs Blast for half; draws the guards).
- **Treasure:** 4 urns 200gp, 4 busts 200gp (16 st).

### 4. Catacombs of the Clergy
- **Exits:** E to 3; W end door out of the complex (map); NW into the curving hall 5 (map).
- Long hall of alcoves with marble pedestals bearing small, dusty terracotta urns: clergy who died ministering to the Borderlands and Citadel Guards who died in the Patriarch's service.
- **50%:** 2 catechists pacing and chanting (as 2); if not here, they are in 5.

### 5. Catacombs of the Guards
- **Exits:** S/W to 4; NE to 6 (map).
- Curving hall of pedestalled alcoves, ancient dusty terracotta urns: City Watch officers and Auran legionaries who died on duty.
- **2 catechists** (as 2) if not met in 4.

### 6. Columbarium of the Captain of the Underwatch
- **Exits:** SW to 5; secret door in the **north wall** to 7, warded with a **blast rune**.
- A great columbarium carved as the Captain himself; resting on top, as if he bore them, the **spear of the Underwatch** and **shield of the Underwatch** (AX3 p. 194; see the items list): spear +2, +3 vs lycanthropes, lights or douses on the command word "audar"; shield +1 that, for a Lawful bearer, gives +1 AC and saves to bearer and allies within 10’ against evil creatures' attacks and blocks contact by enchanted creatures.
- **Wards:** each item has **two runes**: first a curse (save vs Spells or −4 to attack throws and saves), a second later a blast (20 electrical, 7’, save vs Blast for half).
- **4 Knights of the Winged Sun** guard it at all times (the only tomb so honoured) → [[Knight of the Winged Sun (AX3)]]: MV 20’ / 60’, AC 6 (banded plate & shield), HD less than 1 (AX3 1-1), hp 5 each, spear or short sword 11+, D 1d6, Save NM, ML +4, AL L, XP 5 each.
- The regalia go only to an officeholder named by Arëtar (Captain of the Underwatch; [[Bridge District]]).

### 7. Treasure Vault
- **Exits:** secret door S to 6 (warded, see 6).
- The Citadel's temple treasury:
  - **5 red-figure terracotta amphorae** (20gp, 1 st each), each holding **6,000cp** (30,000cp in all).
  - **Locked brass chest (north-east corner):** **2,000pp** and **7,000sp**.
  - **Locked brass chest (south-east corner):** **6,200gp** and an iron jewellery box, **locked and warded with a blast rune**, holding a facet-cut **star sapphire (6,000gp)**, a **black pearl (2,000gp)**, an **imperial topaz (750gp)**, an **aquamarine (500gp)** and a **holy symbol of Ammonar**, star-ruby sun with electrum wings (**13,000gp**).
- **Value:** about 39,550gp (coins 17,200gp, gems 9,250gp, holy symbol 13,000gp, amphorae 100gp).

### 8. Elevated Corridor
- **Exits:** N to the corridor from 1; S to 9; SW and SE to the halls 10 (map).
- Circular corridor raised over the east-west sewer tunnel that runs through its centre; it gives no access to the sewer.

### 9. Ceremonial Corridor
- **Exits:** N to 8; at the T-shaped south end, the **east and west walls are secret doors**, each warded with a **blast rune**, into the halls 10-11 (map).
- Walls inscribed with hymns from the *Seven Radiant Scrolls* praising Ammonar. At the T: south wall fresco of the winged sun at noon, east wall at dawn, west wall at dusk.

### 10. Columbaria of the Tribunes
- **Exits:** two halls, west and east of 9, each N to 8 and S into 11; secret doors from 9 (map).
- Many columbaria with dozens of terracotta urns: Auran officers who died conquering Southern Argollë from the elves.
- **25%:** 2 catechists walking the corridor (as 2).

### 11. Catacombs of the Centurions
- **Exits:** N to 10; the western hall curves S/E to 13, the eastern hall S to 12/13 (map).
- Curving halls of alcoves and pedestals: scores of dusty, age-cracked terracotta urns of centurions who died in the conquest.
- **25%:** 2 catechists (as 2), if not met in 10.

### 12. Catacombs of the Honorable Foe
- **Exits:** to 11 (east hall) and W to 13 (map).
- Six pedestalled alcoves holding scores of lacquered wooden urns: elves who fell honourably against the legions.

### 13. Catacombs of the Elf Lords
- **Exits:** to the western hall 11 (NW) and to 12 (E) (map).
- The ashes of honourable elven lords who fell in the conquest.
  - **North-west wing:** 3 marble pedestals, each with **7 tarnished silver urns (500gp, 1 st each)**: 21 urns, 10,500gp.
  - **South-east corner:** one pedestal with a lone **silver urn (500gp, 1 st)** and before it **Myrdin's panoply**: curved orichalcum warblade with ivory grip (**sword +1, +2 vs spellcasters**, apparent value 850gp), elven composite bow of dawn redwood (**bow +1**, 650gp), elven plate of orichalcum (**plate armour +2**, 6,500gp), round orichalcum elven shield (**shield +2**, 1,650gp), grey-green woven silk cloak (**elven cloak**, 500gp).
- **Loremastery** throw: the gear belonged to **Myrdin**, Argollëan lord and spellsword cataphract who held the main bridge alone until the elven civilians escaped the sack, and died against overwhelming odds; Tarkaun Mennic II, impressed, ordered him interred with all his possessions. (Arëtar may grant it to a Captain of the Bridge; [[Bridge District]].)
- **Wards:** the pedestal carries a **curse rune** (save vs Spells or −4 to attack throws and saves); **each item** carries a **blast rune** (20 electrical, 7’, save vs Blast for half).
- **2 catechists** here praising Myrdin's valour if not met in 10 or 11 (as 2).
