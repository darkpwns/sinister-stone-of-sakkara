# Imperial Amphitheater — Basement (20u)

Cells, storerooms and lift rooms cut by Auran engineers into volcanic tuff under the arena of the [[Imperial Amphitheater - Arena]], walls stuccoed, doors fitted. Outside Old Cyfaraun's borders. Maximus Arjentüran rents it from the censor to house, train and discipline his beastmen, and coordinates every other use of the grounds. Unlit except where stagehands or guards are working (then oil lamps light the whole room). Pages are printed pages; "(map)" = read from the appendix map (p. 212), which is small. Dungeon level 1 is the Judge's call (AX3 gives none).

Running: hub [[AX3 Hub]], district [[Festival District]]. **No wandering checks** inside (AX3 p. 64: no encounter throws in a special location until it is cleared; the basement is sealed and guarded). If someone small enters from the sewers, the sewer side uses the Undercity rate (6+ on 1d6 every 3 turns) and `Tables/ax3-undercity-encounters.yaml` ([[Undercity of Cyfaraun]]).

## Layout
- **Entrances:** (a) the stairs **10** from the southern edge of the arena surface (always guarded); (b) the floor lifts **1, 3, 8, 12** to trap doors in the arena floor (lift 2 is broken); (c) four small lead pipes to the city sewers, wide enough for a child or halfling but not a grown man: the grated holes in cells **4, 5, 6** and the three-hole latrine in **11** (map: the sewer tunnels cross under the east side of the basement).
- **Outer ring (map):** a curved corridor follows the north-west arc of the amphitheater: lifts **1, 2, 3** at its north-east end; a locked iron gate between **3** and cell **4**; cells **4, 5, 6** open off it to the south-east (each double-gated); storeroom **7**; a locked iron gate between **7** and lift **8**; lift 8 at the south-west end; then guard room **9**, a locked iron gate, and stairs **10** in the south.
- **Centre:** central holding room **11** (two west alcoves: latrine to the south, shrine to Ianna to the north, and past the shrine the central lift **12**).
- **Separate:** auxiliary holding room **13**, reached only by its own stairs from the south edge of the arena sands under the first tier of seats (map: south-east, not joined to the rest).
- **Gate keys:** Maximus has both keys of every cell; Mara Rëavus, every arena guard and any stagehand working a lift have keys to the iron corridor gates (lock opens from either side).

### Double-gated cells (4-6)
Outer and inner gate, both always locked, separate keys. Imperial practice: two handlers. The first opens the outer gate, the second steps in; the first locks the outer gate; the second unlocks the inner gate and secures the captive; only then is the outer gate opened. The second handler never carries the outer key, so a captive that mauls him cannot escape with it. Arena guards each carry one or the other key for each cell; only Maximus has both.

### Floor lifts
Wooden lifts to trap doors in the arena floor, 600 lbs each, worked by crews of 8 stagehands; used for wild animals and scenery.
- **Stagehands (8 per working lift):** 0-level; MV 40’ / 120’, AC 0, HD <1, hp 3 each, punch 11+, 1d3 nonlethal, F0, ML -1, AL N, XP 5.

### 1. Northern Floor Lift #1
- **Exits:** the outer corridor (south-west); lift up to a trap door in the arena (map).
- During an event 25% chance in use: 8 stagehands hoisting a 1d10 cargo, `Tables/ax3-festival-basement-lift.yaml` (1-2 lion, 3 bear, 4 wolf, 5 musicians, 6 dancers, 7 actor as a chthonic god, 8 large prop, 9 fake building, 10 model galley; AX3 skips result 2).

### 2. Northern Floor Lift #2
- **Exits:** the outer corridor (map).
- **Broken:** the capstan still turns but the lift frame is smashed, as if by something very strong (the ogre broke it in a past bout while a bear was being brought up).

### 3. Northern Floor Lift #3
- **Exits:** the outer corridor; **locked iron gate** in the hallway toward cell 4 (opens from either side; keys: Maximus, Mara, arena guards, working stagehands) (map).
- As lift 1 (25% in use during an event).

### 4. Double-Gated Cell (ogre)
- **Exits:** double gate to the outer corridor; small iron-grated hole to the sewer tunnel (child or halfling only) (map).
- Big pen built for large animals: straw, torn cushions, water buckets, the hole stained with spoor and urine.
- **Maximus's ogre gladiator**, seemingly content, attacks anyone who comes near except Maximus and his staff → [[Ogre]]: MV 30’ / 90’, AC 3 (hide, no armor), HD 4+1, hp 21, great axe 6+, 1d10+5 slashing (1d10, +3 strength, +2 vicious smash for slashing weapons; AX3 1d10+3), topple on bludgeoning hits only, F4, ML +2 (loyalty +4), AL C, XP 140.

### 5. Double-Gated Cell (orcs and hobgoblins)
- **Exits:** double gate to the outer corridor; rusty grated hole choked with dried filth to the sewer tunnel (map).
- Tattered cots, fouled bedding, water buckets. The hobgoblins are cleaner and drill and spar; the orcs sleep or brawl.
- **4 orc gladiators** → [[Orc]]: MV 40’ / 120’, AC 3 (light arena armor, shield), HD 1, hp 5 each, short sword or dagger 10+ (MM; AX3 9+), 1d6 / 1d4, F1, ML 0 (loyalty +4), AL C, XP 10. Rent 145gp each.
- **3 hobgoblin gladiators** → [[Hobgoblin]]: MV 30’ / 90’, AC 5 (heavy arena armor, shield), HD 1+1, hp 6 each, spear or short sword 9+, 1d6+1 (MM +1 damage; AX3 1d6), F1, ML 0 (loyalty +4), AL C, XP 15; heavy helmets. Rent 300gp each.

### 6. Double-Gated Cell (gnolls and bugbear)
- **Exits:** double gate to the outer corridor; grated hole to the sewer tunnel (map).
- As 5. Every few minutes the gnolls let out a hideous laughing squeal heard through the basement; the bugbear sulks until the noise makes it slap them quiet.
- **2 gnoll gladiators** → [[Gnoll]]: MV 30’ / 90’, AC 4 (light arena armor, shield, hide), HD 2, hp 10 each, battle axe or dagger 9+, 1d6+1 / 1d4+1, F2, ML 0 (loyalty +4), AL C, XP 20; heavy helmets. Rent 360gp each.
- **1 bugbear gladiator** → [[Bugbear]]: MV 30’ / 90’, AC 4 (shield, hide), HD 3+1 (MM; AX3 3+3), hp 18, battle axe or dagger 7+, 1d6+1 / 1d4+1, F3, ML +2 (loyalty +4), AL C, XP 65; foes are -2 on surprise rolls. Rent 700gp.

### 7. Storeroom
- **Exits:** the outer corridor; **locked iron gate** toward lift 8 (opens either side; Maximus, Mara and all arena guards have keys) (map).
- 2 barrels of river water (8 st each); 1 crate of "smoked white meat" (5gp, 8 st); 2 amphorae of "salted tripe" (10gp, 8 st each). Labelled pork; it is **human flesh and viscera**, Maximus's food between Blood Moon nights (a clue to his nature).

### 8. Southern Floor Lift
- **Exits:** the outer corridor (gate to 7; on to 9) (map).
- As lift 1; usually run opposite lift 1 to bring up the opposing side.

### 9. Guard Room and Armory
- **Exits:** the corridor from 8; **locked iron gate** to the stairs 10 (opens either side; Maximus, Mara and all guards have keys) (map).
- Racks: 5 daggers, 5 shields, 5 short swords, 2 swords, 2 spears, 1 net. Tables, stools, benches, cots; a barrel of salted pork (5gp, 8 st), 3 amphorae of wine (10gp, 8 st each).
- **4 arena guards** at all times on Maximus's payroll; **8** on bout days (extras from the other schools or City Watch veterans) → Fighter 1: MV 30’ / 90’, AC 5 (chain mail, shield), HD 1, hp 7 each, short sword 10+, 1d6+1, F1, ML +1, AL N (inferred), XP 10 (AX3 13); belt pouch 6sp and 20cp, keychain of 4 keys; 1d10sp each, mostly on the tables as stakes.

### 10. Stairs
- **Exits:** stair up to the southern edge of the arena surface; gate to 9; passage to 11 (map).
- Always one **arena guard** (as 9) with a loud whistle.

### 11. Central Holding Room
- **Exits:** from 10 (map); west alcoves (latrine, shrine to Ianna and lift 12 beyond).
- 10 pairs of shackles on the north wall for condemned prisoners and unruly gladiators; two stout oak tables, stools, benches.
- Fighters and prisoners due to fight wait here **1d4 hours** before their bout; performers of a play or other event **1d2 hours** before and after. Crowded on festival days.
- South alcove: latrine, three holes to the sewers (small pipes). North alcove: shrine to **Ianna**, goddess of love and war, where the condemned pray for victory and life.

### 12. Central Floor Lift
- **Exits:** past the shrine in 11; lift to the arena (map).
- As lift 1, but used almost only to send condemned prisoners up to die against beasts or gladiators.

### 13. Auxiliary Holding Room
- **Exits:** stairs up to the southern edge of the arena sands, beneath the first tier of seats; no link to the other rooms (map).
- Holding room for gladiators: tables, stools, benches; a small shrine to Ianna in the north wall.
