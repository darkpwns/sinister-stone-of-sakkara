# Tower of Knowledge — Second Story (the Arcanist Library)

[[Tower District]] #50. The whole floor is the main reading room of the Chapterhouse, around an open atrium over the grand foyer. Construction as [[Tower of Knowledge - Ground Story]]. Pages are printed pages.

Running: inhabited building; **no wandering monster checks** (AX3 gives none); occupants below. Dungeon level 1 for treasure is a Judge's call. Open to all members in good standing; non-members need an escort or a reason.

## Layout
- **Stairs:** the grand staircase **4** comes up from the ground story (south wall; [[Tower of Knowledge - Ground Story]] room 5). Two spiral stairs in the north-west and north-east buttresses go **up to the quarters** ([[Tower of Knowledge - Third Story]]).
- **Centre:** the atrium **2** (a ring of six columns) open to the foyer below, with the statue's arm **3** rising through it.
- **Ring:** the library **1** fills the rest, bookcases along the outer wall (north, east and west); latrines on the east and west walls (map p. 151).

## Wandering monsters
None. Present **sunup to sundown** in room 1: 1d6 apprentice mages (0 level), 1d4 apprentice scholars, 2d8 arcanists (Mage 1), 2d4+1 seers (Mage 2), 1d3 theurgists (Mage 3), 1d3−1 magicians (Mage 4), 1d3−1 thaumaturges (Mage 5); **5%** chance each for each Chapterhouse officer (Regent, Provost, Treasurer) with assistants. Mage statlines (Judge's call, AX3 gives none): MV 40’ / 120’, AC 0, hp 1d4 per level, dagger or staff (Mage 1-3 10+, 4-6 9+), spells per day by level (Mage 1: 1; 2: 2; 3: 2/1; 4: 2/2; 5: 2/2/1), Save M by level, ML 0, AL by person, XP by level (Mage 1 10, 2 20, 3 50, 4 80, 5 200).

## Rooms (AX3 pp. 152-153)

### 1. Arcanist Library
- **Exits:** S grand staircase 4; NW and NE spiral stairs up to the third story; open on the atrium 2.
- **3,173 codices (476,000gp)**, including the formulas for **every standard 1st and 2nd level arcane spell** of the blast, detection, enchantment, illusion, movement, protection, summoning and transmogrification types. 15’ floor-to-ceiling oak bookcases along the north, east and west walls; desks, chairs and couches for **120 researchers**.
- Weight (for thieves): at the JJ average of 1/2 st per codex, about 1,587 st (inferred).
- Occupants as above.

### 2. Atrium
- **Exits:** open on all sides to the library 1.
- The floor opens onto the grand foyer below; the six load-bearing columns continue up to carry the third story.

### 3. Arm of Istreus
- **Exits:** in the atrium 2.
- The bronze Istreus's right arm rises through the floor here; its upward-pointing hand lights the room with *continual light* ([[Illumination, Perpetual]]).

### 4. Grand Staircase
- **Exits:** down to the ground story ([[Tower of Knowledge - Ground Story]] room 5); N into the library 1.
