# Thelesa Villa V7 Tomb

Hub: [[Thelesa Villa]].

Under the dead olives, a stone tomb-house with a bronze door gone green; inside, one sarcophagus of the founder, its lid askew, the bones gone; a second, unfinished, empty.

### Features
Stocking: fixed (the wraith's origin: it was the founder). Una flees here and bars the door (a door throw to force).

### Monsters
None unless Una flees here.

### Treasure
None (the founder's goods were taken long ago; the mask is in V4).

### Traps
None.

### Checks and saves
Forcing the door if barred.

### Exits
S to the orchard and V1.
