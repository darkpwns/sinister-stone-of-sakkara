# Thelesa Villa V4 Lararium

Hub: [[Thelesa Villa]].

The family shrine: twelve wax death masks of the Thelesa in niches, the oldest blackened; a cedar chest under them; the air colder than the night.

### Features
Stocking: the wraith's haunt (fixed). It stands among the masks by day on a 1-4.

### Monsters

Weight: 6 st of coin.
