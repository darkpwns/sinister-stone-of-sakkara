# Thelesa Villa V1 Garden

Hub: [[Thelesa Villa]].

Brambles chest-high over the old paths; a dry fountain with a cracked satyr; the house's front, shuttered, on the north side; at the back of the house a gap in the wall to the orchard.

### Features
Stocking: fixed (the wraith's walk). Bare footprints on the muddy paths (one person, small feet; Tracking or Searching).

### Monsters
The wraith, by day on a 5-6 (see hub); by night always.

### Treasure
None.

### Traps
None.

### Checks and saves
Tracking 1 day old prints; Hiding in the brambles (cover); a horse gets no footing in the brambles (lead it).

### Exits
S gate to the lane; N front door to V2; N wall breach to the orchard and V7.
