# Thelesa Villa

Judge only. Built day 359 (S19) by the interim procedure (`unprepped-sites.md`) for Vestus' Order contract
([[Order Contracts (S18)]]): [[Una (AX3)]], last of the Thelesa, Witch 4 (Chthonic), and the [[Wraith]] of the ancestor
who first turned the family to the Chthonic gods. Hidden build rolls: `Session Log/S19 — The Thelesa villa.md` Judge
block, "Site build: Thelesa Villa". Small dungeon (JJ Step 1: 2-10 rooms): seven rooms, ground level and one cellar.

## Running Thelesa Villa
- Dungeon level: 2
- Wandering monsters: none (a walled ruin in civilized farmland; the wraith walks the grounds by its own routine)
- **Approach:** 6 miles south-west of Cyfaraun on the Türos Quell road (hex 17.06), 300 yards off the road up a lane
  gone to brambles. A villa rustica of the old patricians: a 10' wall of rendered rubble round a garden of 200' by
  150', the house in its north half, an orchard of dead olives behind; the family tomb under the olives. Roof tiles
  half fallen; no smoke by day; a single light in a shuttered window some nights.
- **Light:** none inside but Una's single candle (noon and midnight: her obligation, RR Witch, 1 turn each) and the
  atrium's open roof by day.
- **Una:** hp 16, AC 0 (her glamour), dagger; statline [[Una (AX3)]]. By day (Judge's call, rolled on each visit):
  1-3 asleep in V5, 4-5 at her altar in V3, 6 in the garden V1. By night she wanders the house and grounds and goes
  out on the road in storms. Mad: she talks to the masks and to the wraith as "Grandfather". Spells a day: 3 1st, 3
  2nd (note); repertoire (Judge's call, divine list): 1st Cause Light Injury (reversed cure), Holy Circle (as unholy
  circle), Sanctuary, Bane-Rune; 2nd Halt Humanoids, Slumber, Beguile Humanoid. Choking Grip once per 8 hours as a
  spell-like ability (note). Bedazzling Glamour (RR Witch, Chthonic). Morale 0: if her grandfather falls or she is
  below half hp she flees to V7 (the tomb) and bars it.
- **The wraith ("Grandfather"):** hp 20, AC 6, HD 4**, enervating touch 1d8 extraordinary necrotic and permanent
  max-hp loss; immune to mundane damage except fire, silver extraordinary; rebuked as wraiths; enchanted (a holy
  circle and the Underwatch shield bar it). By day 1-4 in V4 among the masks, 5-6 walking V1. By night it walks the
  wall. It defends Una: it comes to any fight in the house or garden in 1d4 rounds; it does not leave the walls.
- **Rats:** 9 giant rats in the cellar V6b (hp 4, 3, 4, 3, 4, 2, 2, 2, 4); they come up to any noise in the kitchen.
- **Map (plain text):** road → lane (300 yards) → V1 gate (south wall, gate leaf fallen) → garden V1 (brambles,
  paths). House on the north side of V1: front door → V2 atrium (30' square, open roof, impluvium) → north V3
  triclinium (40' by 20'); east of V2 V4 lararium (15' by 15', door); west of V2 V5 cubiculum (15' by 15', door
  barred inside by day); north-east corner V6 kitchen (20' by 15', door from V3), stair down to V6b cellar (30' by 20').
  A breach in the north wall of V1 behind the house → orchard → V7 tomb (stone door, 60' north of the house).
