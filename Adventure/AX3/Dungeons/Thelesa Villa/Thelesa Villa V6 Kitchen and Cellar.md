# Thelesa Villa V6 Kitchen and Cellar

Hub: [[Thelesa Villa]].

A blackened kitchen with a collapsed hearth and a wooden trap to the cellar stair. Below (V6b), a vaulted wine cellar of broken amphorae, the floor heaped with rat filth.

Stocking: monster (the rats) and empty-with-treasure (H40, the family's buried store: 1,000 electrum in an iron-bound box under the cellar's flagstones at the north end, with the jewels sewn in a leather roll: gems 500, 1,000, 50, 75gp and trinkets 110, 100, 110, 180, 300gp).
