# Thelesa Villa V3 Triclinium

Hub: [[Thelesa Villa]].

A long dining hall, its couches rotted; at the north end a black stone block for an altar, a bowl of old blood, a ring of tallow candles, and a niche cut into the wall where a bronze serpent hangs.

### Features
Stocking: unique. Una's Chthonic altar: a Lawful divine caster feels the cold of it at once (Theology or Loremastery: an altar of the Chthonic powers, used for years). Desecrating it (smashing the block or pouring holy water on it, a turn's work) brings the wraith at once wherever it is, and Una if she is in the house; afterwards the house is no longer hers to draw on (no game effect beyond the wraith's fury, Judge's call).

### Monsters
Una here by day on a 4-5.

### Treasure
None.

### Traps
None.

### Checks and saves
Theology/Loremastery (the altar).
