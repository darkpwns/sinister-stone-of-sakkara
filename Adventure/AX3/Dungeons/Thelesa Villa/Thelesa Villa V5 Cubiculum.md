# Thelesa Villa V5 Cubiculum

Hub: [[Thelesa Villa]].

A bedchamber: a heap of furs and rags on a rotten bed frame, a candle stub, a mirror of polished bronze, the smell of herbs and old sweat. The door is barred inside by day.

### Features
Stocking: fixed (Una's room).

### Monsters
Una here by day on a 1-3, asleep (slumbering: helpless while asleep, wakes on noise by the RR's wake rule).

### Treasure
Una's purse: 4gp, 25sp (note). Her prayer book (her repertoire; divine; Chthonic) and her unholy symbol (a black serpent swallowing its tail).

### Traps
None.

### Checks and saves
Lock/bar: the bar is lifted from outside only by forcing (door throw) or a blade through the gap (thief's Lockpicking -4, Judge's call).

### Exits
E door V2.
