# Lizardman Lair (28u)

Lair of the escaped arena lizardmen in the western sewers under the Festival District. An excavated pocket of **Ancient Cyfaraun**: the tuff was cleared from an elven temple of about 600 BE (grand stair and portico); the sewer canal follows the old elven street it faced, and only the ash-covered steps up into den 1 show. Unlit (the lizardmen see in the dark). Dungeon level 1 is the Judge's call.

**Story.** Celictor Nuvicus, with money from **Liber Faunus** and other backers, outbid the lanistae of Arganos for 2 lizardman warriors, a lizardman champion and a wild giant crocodile; Liber's syndicate (the Argollëan Family) smuggled them in by night. At the north-western docks the lizardmen killed their handlers, freed themselves and the crocodile, and swam into the western sewers. The crocodile nests elsewhere (#45u, [[Crocodile Nest]] in the [[Plaza District]]). Over 30 days 15 dockworkers and bargemen have vanished and body parts float in the river; the few who notice blame the Argollëan Family. Only Nuvicus and Liber know the truth; Nuvicus pays a premium for any of the beasts returned **alive and unharmed** in time for the next games (and fears Liber's anger over the lost money); Liber, wanting Maximus humbled, will discreetly broker a meeting with Nuvicus for adventurers who frequent the Mink (see [[Old District]]).

Running: hub [[AX3 Hub]], district [[Festival District]]. **No wandering checks inside the lair until it is cleared** (AX3 p. 64); in the sewers around it the Undercity rate, 6+ on 1d6 every 3 turns, on `Tables/ax3-undercity-encounters.yaml` ([[Undercity of Cyfaraun]]). At the sewer exit 4 in the hour after sunset and the hour before dawn, the lizardmen are met on **5+ on 1d6**. AX3 gives no wandering table of its own.

## Layout
- **Entrances:** the sewer canal from the north-east (from a junction chamber with a grate and the long tunnel to the river exit **4**) and from the west/south-west (map); the Undercity tunnels beyond.
- **Plan (map):** the canal runs east-west along the south face of the ruin; ash-covered steps climb north from it into the warrior den **1**; the champion's den **2** opens off the north-east of 1. The trip wires **3** are strung across the canal at both ends of the stretch below the steps (west junction and east junction). North of the lair a sewer junction chamber (grated shaft) links the canal branches; from it a tunnel runs north-east then east-south-east **200’** to the river outfall **4**.

## Wandering monsters
Undercity table (see Running). Lizardmen are [[Lizardman]].

### 1. Lizardman Warrior Den
- **Exits:** ash-covered steps down south to the sewer canal; opening north-east to 2 (map).
- Former temple hall: white marble walls caked with volcanic ash; beds of clothing and cloth taken from victims. They sleep by day and wake at night to hunt.
- **2 lizardman warriors** → [[Lizardman]]: MV 20’ / 60’, swim 40’ / 120’ (MM; AX3 60’ / 120’ swim), AC 4 (scales, shield; 3 when they drop the shield for claws), HD 2+1, hp 10 each, 2 claws and bite 8+ (1d3 / 1d3 / 1d8) or spear 8+ (1d8+1 two-handed, MM +1 weapon damage), F2, ML +2, AL C, XP 35. Hold breath up to 1 turn; Lightless Vision 60’.

### 2. Lizardman Champion Den
- **Exits:** to 1 (map).
- The old inner sanctum, floor littered with white rubble of a shattered alabaster statue; a worn sleeping pad stolen from the docks. Sleeps by day, active at night.
- **1 lizardman champion** → [[Lizardman]] (champion): MV 20’ / 60’, swim 40’ / 120’, AC 5 (AX3), HD 3+1, hp 17, 2 claws and bite 7+ (1d3+1 / 1d3+1 / 1d8+1) or spear 7+ (1d8+2), F3, ML +2, AL C, XP 65.
- **Treasure** under the pad: bag with **22cp, 2 agates (25gp each), a bloodstone (50gp)** from the dead dockworkers.

### 3. Trip Wires
- **Exits:** the sewer canal, at the west and the east approach (map).
- Two wires just above the waterline tied to jury-rigged metal noisemakers on the walls. Found on casual inspection with a successful proficiency throw to find traps (ACKS II: Searching), or with **+4** on deliberate inspection; once found, easily stepped over or removed (+4). Unfound: each character crossing has a **2-in-6** chance to set it off and alert the lizardmen.

### 4. Sewer Exit
- **Exits:** 200’ of sewer tunnel back to the junction; outfall into the Mirmen River (map).
- The lizardmen swim out here to prowl the docks and barges (hold breath, swim). In the hour after sunset and before dawn, the warriors and champion are met here on **5+ on 1d6**.
