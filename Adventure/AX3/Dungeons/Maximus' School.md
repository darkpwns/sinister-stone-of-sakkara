# Maximus' School (Festival District #23)

Judge only. Built day 391 (S19), compact (rooms inline), for Iulia's sanctioned mission against [[Maximus Arjentüran (AX3)]]
(devil boar, HD 9**, hp 68). Rolls: the session log's Judge block, "Site build: Maximus' School".
- **Plan:** a walled yard (training sands, posts) off the street; a gatehouse (2 hired guards awake at night,
  0-level, AC 7, spear); a long low barracks on the yard's east side (2 trainers asleep, Fighter 2, hp 9 and 11;
  the beastman pens empty: the stable lives in the Amphitheater basement); a two-storey house on the north side,
  Maximus' rooms upstairs (bedchamber, a strongroom closet with H43 and H44), a dining hall and kitchen below.
- **Maximus by night:** asleep in his bedchamber (rolled 1: at the school), his spear +1 and sword +1 on a rack;
  a natural sleeper (wakes on a 1 when a non-sneaker passes within 10'; inside Noiselessness, no checks: Rulings
  S19). Awake: human form AC 4, dual weapons -1+ (1d6+1) x2, hypnotic gaze (Spells save -2 or enslaved; one per HD);
  boar form AC 6, gore 2+ 2d6 extraordinary; immune to mundane damage, silver extraordinary; ML +5.
- **H43 (TT O) and H44 (TT L)** in the strongroom closet (locked, a good lock): 16,000cp, 9,000ep, a brilliant 1,000gp,
  a brilliant 750gp, 4 pieces of jewellery (160, 100, 23, 120gp), potions (Monstrous Form, Energy Invulnerability,
  Comprehension), 19 sling bullets +1.
- **His death:** his thralls (the 12 beastmen in the Amphitheater basement; perhaps Selene) are freed (MM).
