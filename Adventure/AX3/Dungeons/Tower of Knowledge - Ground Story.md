# Tower of Knowledge — Ground Story (the Grand Foyer)

[[Tower District]] #50, the Chapterhouse of the Tower of Knowledge in [[Cyfaraun]]. Concrete masonry faced outside with white limestone; interior walls stuccoed; floors polished grey-black granite; ceilings carved hardwood. The tower is a 75’-diameter drum with four square buttresses; inside, a 10’ ornate hardwood wall rings a 75’-diameter circular grand foyer; the space between that wall and the outer wall holds the storefront, the guards' lounge and latrine. Pages are printed pages.

Running: an inhabited building in the city, not a dungeon: **no wandering monster checks** (AX3 gives none); occupants by room and by time of day. Dungeon level 1 for treasure is a Judge's call (AX3 gives none). Alarm: the guard pairs at 1, 4 and 5 reinforce each other within a round or two; 1d6 off-duty guards in 6; the stair subaltern at 7. Procedures (joining, storefront prices): [[Tower District]].

## Layout
- **Entrance:** the outer doors **1** in the north face open into the grand foyer **2**, with the statue **3** at its centre.
- **Inner ring:** the inner gate **4** (south side of the hardwood wall) leads out of the foyer into the outer ring; the grand staircase **5** rises from the south wall to a landing and doubles back to the second story ([[Tower of Knowledge - Second Story]] room 4).
- **Outer ring:** east, the guards' lounge **6** behind painted screens (latrine beside it, east wall); west, the storefront **7** behind chest-high windows in the hardwood wall; in the north-west corner the **stairs down to the basement** ([[Tower of Knowledge - Basement]] room 15), guarded by the tower subaltern (map p. 151).

## Rooms (AX3 pp. 150-152)

### 1. Outer Doors
- **Exits:** N to the street; S into the grand foyer 2.
- Iron-reinforced hardwood doors, **17’ wide and 15’ tall**, into a foyer with a 21’ hardwood ceiling carved and gilded with celestial motifs. The ornate 10’ hardwood wall ringing the foyer (75’ diameter) bears carved and painted reliefs of Auran mages winning the Empire's victories.
- **2 tower guards** at all times: AC 6 (banded plate & shield), MV 20’ / 60’, HD less than 1, hp 5 each, spear or sword 11+, D 1d6, 0-level save, ML +1, AL L, XP 5; each carries a key (to 4), 2sp and 20cp.

### 2. Grand Foyer
- **Exits:** N to the doors 1; S through the inner gate 4; open to the second-story library above.
- The ceiling opens to the Arcanist Library above, carried on **6 load-bearing columns** of polished grey-black granite that rise through the second floor and beyond. Brass letters set in the granite floor between the columns, in Classical Auran, warn that the god's favour is earned only by studying all creation (the god's own admonition).
- Applicants to the Tower present themselves here (fee 10gp; [[Tower District]]).

### 3. Statue
- **Exits:** stands at the foyer's centre.
- A **21’ bronze colossus of Istreus**: an eyeless wizard in a hooded cassock, eyeless gaze and left hand pointing at the viewer; the right hand, enchanted with *continual light* ([[Illumination, Perpetual]]), points to the heavens and rises into the library above, lighting it.

### 4. Inner Gate
- **Exits:** N to the foyer 2; S to the outer ring (stair 5, lounge 6, storefront 7).
- Decorative wrought-iron gate, **8.5’ wide, 7.5’ tall**, in the hardwood wall. Open sunup to sundown; **closed and locked after dark**. Every member, servant and guard has a key.
- **2 tower guards** on its north side (as room 1), able to reinforce the outer doors quickly.

### 5. Grand Staircase
- **Exits:** N to the inner gate 4 and outer ring; up to a landing, then doubling back to the second story ([[Tower of Knowledge - Second Story]] room 4).
- The landing wall has a life-sized mosaic of semi-precious stones of a mage and three companions. Collegiate Wizardry or Knowledge (history) recognises **Quintus Odysios**, who commissioned the Chapterhouse (and was its first Regent), with his companions **Balbus, Marcus and Viktir**.
- **2 tower guards** at all times (as room 1).

### 6. Lounge
- **Exits:** W to the outer ring; latrine on the east wall (map).
- Painted wooden screens enclose benches, cots and weapon racks for off-shift guards. **1d6 tower guards** here by day (as room 1), gambling, practising at arms or napping.

### 7. Storefront
- **Exits:** E (through chest-high windows) to the foyer side; the outer ring; NW: **stairs down 15’** to the basement ([[Tower of Knowledge - Basement]] room 15).
- Chest-high windows cut through the 10’ hardwood wall: the Chapterhouse buys and sells magic items, potions, scrolls and special components here, and identifies items. Tables and chairs behind the counter for the staff. By day **1d4 apprentice mages** under a **thaumaturge, sage or alchemist**; more members can be summoned. Full price list and services (equipment, henchmen and specialists, identification 2,000gp / 40gp / 40gp, spell formulas 100/200/400/1,000gp, vault items at 110% and 2× base cost, sales at base cost / 90%): [[Tower District]] (storefront procedure).
- **Basement stair:** guarded at all times by the **tower subaltern**; only members of **thaumaturge rank (5th level)** or above may go down.
  - **Tower subaltern:** Fighter 3; Str 16, Int 13, Wis 10, Dex 10, Con 14, Cha 13; MV 20’ / 60’; AC 6 (banded plate & shield); hp 17; spear or sword 7+, D 1d6+4; Save F3; ML +3; AL L; XP 50. Proficiencies: Alertness, Combat Reflexes, Leadership, Manual of Arms. Pouch: key (to 4), 1gp, 20sp.
