# Shrine of the Horned Rat (Special Location 13u)

Under the Old City walls of [[Cyfaraun]] ([[Old District]]). Originally (circa 600 BE) a compound of separate buildings dedicated to an elven hero, the **Knight of the Leaf and Petal**, drowned in volcanic ash with the rest of Ancient Cyfaraun; dug out around 175 BE by a heretical elf who added the Shrine (6) as an annex beside the room holding the hero's ashes (5). Mosaics of floral patterns and floors tiled like wildflower meadows show through ash and dirt; pointed-arch window holes reveal unexcavated tuff; the corridors are tunnels through the tuff, sometimes along an unexcavated building's outer wall. **Pitch dark**: the cultists have lightless vision as wererats. AX3 pp. 88-92. "(map)" = read from the map.

**Galmorm, the Horned Rat**, Chthonic god of thieves, treachery and poison, never popular, his worshippers loathed even among servants of the dark powers; some corrupt elves of Southern Argollë revered him. Decades ago his cultists spread lycanthropy through Cyfaraun until the **Captain of the Underwatch** (see [[Catacombs of the Patriarchs]]) killed every one of them, though he never found the Shrine. **Mentenus**, Provost of the Tower of Knowledge Chapterhouse (#50), seeking elven immortality and the Nethercity beneath the elven ruins, read of the wererat terror, reasoned that wererats are immune to the sewers' diseases yet keep their wits and magic, located the Shrine by having his retinue count rats each night inside the Old Walls, and on the next full moon prayed with his followers and became a wererat. The cult now uses the Shrine as the hub of its search of the Undercity for the Nethercity (see SOTN (separate module notes)).

Running: Undercity encounter throw every 3 turns, **5+ on 1d6 (below the Old District)**, 1d100 on `Tables/ax3-undercity-encounters.yaml` (written for [[Undercity of Cyfaraun]]); its 80+ special result near here = a pack of 8 filthy wererats knocking on walls and peering into cracks (Terkinos's pack noon-midnight, Abimbola's midnight-noon), turning ratman if attacked (AX3 pp. 66-67). No throws inside the Shrine until it is cleared.

## Schedule (AX3 p. 89)
- **Terkinos's pack** (Terkinos, 2 arcanists, 5 wererats) searches the Undercity **noon to midnight**; sleeps in 7 by night.
- **Abimbola's pack** (Abimbola, 2 seers, 5 wererats) searches **midnight to noon**; sleeps in 4 by day.
- **Shift change**, about 30 minutes at **midday and midnight**: both packs meet in the Shrine 6 to pray and swap notes and maps.
- **Full moon:** no searching that day and night; they clean up, buy a month's provisions at the Emporium, are back by sunset, and pray at the Shrine until midnight. **Mentenus** comes only then, at midnight, to pray and collect Terkinos's reports.
- They search very slowly, fearing to miss a secret door. Ever more furtive and subterranean.

## Layout
- **Entrance:** a sewer tunnel on the west; the rubble-choked corridor **1** leads east from it (map).
- **Network:** grimy tuff corridors **3** link four filth-strewn rooms **2** (one by the entrance, one north-west at the end of a long northern passage, one north-central, one south-central at a junction); off the network lie Abimbola's quarters **4** (east), the crematory **5** (south; its west wall hides the secret door to the Shrine **6**), and Terkinos's quarters **7** (north-east) with Mentenus's lair **8** behind a wizard-locked door to the east (map).
- **Secret doors:** in the north-west and south-east rooms 2 (irregularities in the mosaic): the NW one opens north to further tunnels, the SE one toward the sewer and cave south-east of the complex (map; destinations not keyed); in 5 to 6.

**Wererat abilities** (MM, all of them): switching form is a movement action; as rat or ratman, mundane bludgeoning, piercing and slashing cannot harm them, other mundane damage is resisted, silver counts as extraordinary; others take **-2 on surprise rolls** against them; a humanoid who loses 50% or more of his maximum hp to their bites is infected (human: becomes a wererat at the next full moon; demi-human: incapacitated in 2d6 days, dead 2d6 days later; [[Cure Disease]] before then cures); revert to human form at 0 hp (so dead cultists leave no proof); AX3 adds **summon 1d2 giant rats in 1d4 rounds** (Judge's call: keep). Ordinary wererats wear freeholder's clothes and sandals and carry short swords. XP for classed wererats: Judge's call (level or wererat 3* as HD, plus 1 ability).

## Factions
- **Cult of the Horned Rat** (Mentenus): avoids Faunus's smuggling dens to the south and his attention; Mentenus thinks his power dwarfs Faunus's. Mentenus hired the Brotherhood of Knives (the doppelgängers) to kill Watch officer Gundus and the elves Fionntan and Tomman ([[Doppelganger Den]], ledger).
- A party exposing the cult: the Solar Citadel (Patriarch Arëtar, [[Bridge District]]) takes such evidence as grounds for an Underwatch commission.

## Rooms (AX3 pp. 89-90)

### 1. Rubble-choked Corridor
- **Exits:** W to the sewer tunnel; E into the complex (to the west room 2 and the corridors 3) (map).
- Choked with broken tuff, rotten timber and old furniture, gnawed and caked in vermin droppings. Clearing it is a **1gp construction project**.
- **Trap (alarm):** a tripwire hidden in the rubble, triggered if the rubble is removed, rings a hidden chime that alerts any wererats present (no sleeping packs; see 4 and 7).

### 2. Filth-strewn Rooms (four)
- **Exits:** onto the corridors 3; NW room: secret door N; SE (south-central) room: secret door toward the south-east (map).
- Floors thick with vermin dung and trash; walls caked with filth almost hiding old elven mosaics (cleaned: white and pink flowers).
- **A rat swarm in each room**, under the wererats' influence, attacks intruders → [[Rat Swarm]]: MV 20’/60’ (AX3 also 30’ burrow), climb 10’/30’, AC 0, HD 3*, hp 15 each, engulf: Paralysis save at the end of the victim's initiative, fail = 1d6 piercing and knocked prone, success = half (moving away 20’+ or warding with torch or weapon +4; warding deals 1d4 to the swarm), immune to bludgeoning, piercing, slashing except the warder's, disease 10% per damaged victim (AX3 5%), save F0, ML -3, AL N, XP 65.
- **Secret doors:** barely noticeable irregularities in the mosaic patterns of the NW and SE rooms mark their edges.

### 3. Grimy Corridors
- **Exits:** link 1, the rooms 2, 4, 5 and 7 (map).
- Filth-caked tuff tunnels; here and there the ruined marble façade of an unexcavated elven building peeks through the ash.

### 4. Abimbola's Quarters
- **Exits:** W to the corridors 3 (map).
- Recently cleaned: violet and pink floral mosaic plainly visible. Eight cots; two jars and a locked chest by the entrance.
- **Abimbola and his 7 wererats (2 seers, 5 wererats): 75% present by day**, asleep in human form unless alerted by the tripwire or fighting.
- **Treasure:** two jars of rare elven spices (**800gp each**); chest: **1,000gp and 4,800sp** in old Argollëan coin.

### 5. Filth-strewn Crematory
- **Exits:** N to the corridors 3; secret door W to 6 (map).
- A massive patinated bronze urn, leaf motifs and coloured enamel flowers, in the middle; walls with a deep purple floral mosaic under years of grime. The urn holds only ashes (the Knight of the Leaf and Petal).
- Inspecting the urn reveals a **3’ by 2” strip free of dust** beside it where a sword lay: it is **Lann Dhuille**, now Abimbola's.
- **Secret door:** a barely perceptible irregularity in the purple pattern on the west wall.

### 6. Shrine of the Horned Rat
- **Exits:** secret door E to 5 (map).
- A small altar heaped with chicken bones that spill onto the floor. Most of the cult is here daily at **midday and midnight** (shift change); **Mentenus only at midnight of the full moon**.
- **Always guarded by 12 giant rats** → [[Giant Rat]]: MV 40’/120’, swim 20’/60’, AC 2, HD ½, hp 3 each, bite 12+ (MM; AX3 10+), D 1d3 piercing + disease, save F1, ML 0, AL N, XP 5.
- The altar makes the room a **shadowed sinkhole of evil**. Any human or demi-human who **willingly prays at the altar for one turn on the night of the full moon** instantly becomes a **natural wererat** in full control of his changes; over time he grows furtive, cunning and Chaotic.

### 7. Terkinos's Quarters
- **Exits:** S/W to the corridors 3; freshly installed wooden door E to 8 (map).
- Walls cleaned enough to show violet and pink floral mosaics; floor grimy but clear. Eight cots along the west wall.
- **Terkinos and his 7 wererats (2 arcanists, 5 wererats): 75% present by night**, asleep in human form unless alerted.
- **Treasure:** a locked chest (**2,640gp**) and a bundle of mink furs (**500gp**) by the cots.
- **East door:** **Magic Lock at 9th level caster** (ACKS I wizard lock); only Mentenus knows the password (bash it or dispel it; see [[Magic Lock]]).

### 8. Mentenus's Lair
- **Exits:** W door to 7 (map).
- Thoroughly cleaned: a cot, wooden table and chair strewn with books and papers, a small shelf of books, an ironbound chest at the foot of the cot.
- **Chest:** Magic Lock (9th level); **2,400gp**.
- **Table:** a partial map of the Undercity and research notes in a spidery, nearly illegible hand. Each turn spent deciphering yields one entry on `Tables/ax3-old-mentenus-notes.yaml`.
