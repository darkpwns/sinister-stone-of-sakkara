# Vagrant Colony and Death Cult Hideout — Lake District #75u

Undercity of the [[Lake District]], [[Cyfaraun]], under the servants' tenements, beside the Temple District ([[Temple District]]). A colony of the homeless grew here to beg from mourners at the Shrine of the Ancestors and to be near the Keepers of the Hearth's poorhouse; the priestesses of Mityara walled the consecrated catacombs off and put an iron gate on them, open by day only. Seven years ago the vagrant **Velus**, tormented by visions, built a shrine to Mityara by the gate. Last winter a band of outsiders took over a niche deep in the colony, painted warning symbols at its edge and began killing anyone who crossed them: the **death cult of Dirgion** (Doombringer, Lord of Death and Undeath) under **Malthusia the Diabolist** and her lover **Rhankur**, with 4 cultists and 7 enforcers. They murdered Velus in front of the vagrants as a warning, sacrificed the protesters on their new altar (beheaded; skulls on the altar, bodies down the sewer trench into the river, found near the southern wall and ignored since the dead were "only vagrants"), and now court the Argollëan Family, Sand & Bones and private clients for murder contracts, meaning to abduct the targets as sacrifices. AX3 sometimes numbers it #73u. Pages are printed pages; "(map)" = read from the map, p. 219. Hub [[AX3 Hub]]. `level: 2` is a Judge's call (a 4th-level leader and a dozen classed followers).

**Construction:** the rooms are the excavated interiors of freestanding elven buildings of about 600 BE: frescoed stucco and tiled floors under grime and ash, pointed-arch windows onto unexcavated black tuff. The long tunnels between them follow the old elven streets: black tuff walls and ceilings, here and there the outer wall of a buried ruin, eroded cobblestone floors. North of a main sewer canal. **Lighting:** dim light (15’) throughout 1-8 (cheap smelly candles, canisters of burning trash); the cultists carry brass oil lamps (30’ radius); the tunnels between are unlit.

Running: AX3 makes no encounter throws inside a special location until it is cleared; afterwards Undercity throws: 1d6 every 3 turns, 6+ → 1d100 on `Tables/ax3-undercity-encounters.yaml`. AX3 gives no wandering table of its own. The Undercity table's special entry for this site (80+, within 120’): a vagrant mad with fear splashes through the sewers, takes the party for cultists and flees; caught and calmed, he says evil cultists live in the colony and killed its leader (AX3 p. 66). Absent cult members (see 11) return in **1d6+1 turns**.

## Layout
- **Entrances:** (a) the **Iron Gate 2** at the east end of the canal-side tunnel, from Catacombs of the Ancestors room 8 (#67u, [[Temple District]]) and the Shrine of the Ancestors above; (b) the sewer canal itself, running east-west along the south of the complex (Undercity); (c) the cult's **secret door** in the south wall of **12** onto a side channel of the canal.
- **South (canal):** a tunnel runs along the north bank of the canal from 2 westward. South of the canal by the gate: **4** (Den of Misery) and **3** (Alcove of Mityara), reached from the gate end (map; how the canal is crossed is not shown — Judge's call: a plank or ford by the gate).
- **Colony (north of the canal):** a short tunnel runs north from the canal tunnel into the big **Fetid Hall 5**, which opens east along a passage to **1** (the Temple's brick wall; beyond it the Temple's holdings). Beside the foot of 5, **6** (Dank Home) opens north onto a long north-south street-tunnel; near its north end **7** (Horrid Hovel) opens east and **8** (Moldy Home) closes the north end (map).
- **Cult hideout (west):** near the north end of the 6-8 street a second, parallel street branches west and south; across it stands the black door **9**. Behind 9 the street runs south to **10** (Malthusia's chambers); partway down, a passage branches west to a third street running south to **11** (barracks), which opens west into the **shrine 12**; 12's secret south door leads to the sewer canal (map).

## Factions
- **The vagrants:** cowed and silent about the cult; grateful to anyone who frees them. They revere Velus as a martyr.
- **The Keepers of the Hearth** (priestesses of Mityara, Temple of the Hearth Fire #58, almshouse #59): feed the vagrants; keep the gate open by day as a mercy. The **Grey Sisters** perform rites for the dead (Velus's request).
- **The death cult of Dirgion:** see the members below. **If the party kills the cultists present and destroys the altar**, survivors abandon the site, pose as travellers at The Mink or the Silk Pony Inn ([[Old District]], [[Festival District]]), find out who killed their brethren and defiled their shrine, and try to **assassinate** them in Dirgion's name. If hard-pressed in the hideout they flee through the secret door in 12 to take revenge later.

### 1. Empyrean Temple Wall
- **Exits:** W along the passage to 5; E: none (the wall).
- A thick brick wall built by the Temple District's clergy: the edge of the Temple's authority, keeping "undesirables" out of its subterranean holdings (#67u).

### 2. Iron Gate
- **Exits:** W along the canal-side tunnel; E through the gate to Catacombs of the Ancestors room 8 (#67u, [[Temple District]]) and on up to the city via the Shrine of the Ancestors; S toward 3-4 (map).
- **Locked by night**, open but guarded by day, so vagrants can go up to the city: a mercy ordered by the chief priestesses of the Keepers of the Hearth Fire.
- **2 Knights of the Winged Sun** on the east side at all times → [[Knight of the Winged Sun (AX3)]]: MV 20’ / 60’, AC 6 (banded plate & shield), HD less than 1 (AX3 1-1), hp 5 each, spear or short sword 11+, D 1d6, Save NM, ML +4, AL L, XP 5 each.

### 3. Alcove of Mityara
- **Exits:** to 4 and the gate end of the canal (map).
- Velus's informal shrine to Mityara, goddess of mercy, of stained but lovingly carved reclaimed wood. **1d4 vagrants** praying; they beg alms. Asked who made it: the virtuous martyr Velus, as a sanctuary for when the Iron Gate is locked.

### 4. Den of Misery
- **Exits:** to 3 and the canal (map).
- **3d4 vagrants** sleeping, huddling over makeshift fires, eating almshouse scraps (#59), rambling, screaming, singing folk songs; they beg from any non-vagrant.

### 5. Fetid Hall
- **Exits:** S by a short tunnel to the canal-side tunnel; E along a passage to 1; W at its foot to 6 (map).
- Excavated chamber, cracked stucco caked with dirt; faded floral frescoes show through in clean patches; tiled floor buckled by ancient earthquakes under litter and waste. **20d4 vagrants**.

### 6. Dank Home
- **Exits:** E/S to the tunnel by 5; N up the long street to 7 and 8 (map).
- Rough black tuff, undecorated; old cobbles under ash and dirt (a street once ran north here). **5d4 vagrants**.

### 7. Horrid Hovel
- **Exits:** W onto the north-south street (map).
- A nauseating smell, noticeable before the corner. North half like 4: **5d4 vagrants**, most showing symptoms of a **rotting disease**, huddled against the north wall. The south walls are cleaner and covered with sinister paintings of a **fiery scarab beetle ringed by worms**. Before them, on a rotting mattress, a **decomposing body**; an overturned stool and table; papers scattered between table and body.
- **Healing** proficiency: a middle-aged human man, throat gashed open. The papers all repeat variations of one phrase hailing "the Scarab on Two Legs, the Living Death Immortal".
- **Under the mattress:** Velus's **leather-bound journal**. First page: a woman holding up a torch (Theology: a symbol of Mityara). The rest, in an educated hand in Classical Auran, records (in sense): his visions of nothing but death and ruin and no rest; that he must warn the temple's servants, who think him mad and feed him sacrificial stew; a plea to be freed from visions of his own throat cut; "a witch, a warrior, and a score of acolytes" who summon and bind the dead — do they come for the scarab, or for him?; and a last entry addressed to "noble travellers" he has seen in dreams: the Lady has sent them to his remains; he asks them to take his body to the **Grey Sisters** for proper rites and show them the journal. He signs himself Velus of the sacked and bloodied lands.
- **Cipher (last page):** deciphered (proficiency or magic), it names the bearer **heir to his estate**. Shown to the Grey Sisters with his body for the rites, the party inherits the deed to the **Ionicus estate**, abandoned beyond the Krysivor River and overrun by a beastman tribe; clear it and the Empire honours the claim (place it near the Ruined Imperial Fortress, Gazetteer #40, or any hex: Judge's choice). (Velus is, by name, an Ionicus; Judge's inference.)

### 8. Moldy Home
- **Exits:** S down the street to 7 and 6; W to the street with the door 9 (map).
- Like 4, but the north wall is carpeted with **unwholesome-looking taupe mould** (AX3 gives it no effect). **5d4 vagrants**.

### 9. Hideout Entrance
- **Exits:** N/E toward 8 (the colony side); S, through the door, down the street to 10, with the branch W to 11 (map).
- A recently installed door across the old street, painted black with a **red skull** (Theology throw: symbol of **Dirgion**, Chthonic god of death and undeath). **Locked** (every cultist has a key) and **barred** on the far side.
- **1 cult enforcer** stands guard behind it → [[Death Cult Enforcer (AX3)]] (statline under Members). Talking with the vagrants in 7 or 8, bashing the door, or anything short of utter stealth: he **rouses the cult** to prepare for battle (Malthusia and Rhankur arm and go to 11).

### 10. Diabolist's Chambers
- **Exits:** N up the street to 9 (map).
- At the end of an excavated street; scrubbed clean to its tiled floor and floral frescoes. A well-kept double bed, plain oak desk and chair, an iron-banded wooden chest; two jars of lamp oil (**20gp, 1 st each**) and minor personal effects in a corner.
- **Chest:** locked (Malthusia has the key), not trapped: **1,000gp and 1,800sp**.
- **By day** Malthusia and Rhankur sleep here unless the alarm is raised (then they are armed and armoured in 11). **At night** they worship in 12.

### 11. Cult Barracks
- **Exits:** N up the street to the branch toward 9; W into 12 (map).
- Cleaned and furnished: **11 cots** in a loose ring, each with a chest: **500sp per chest (5,500sp)**.
- **1d4 cultists and 1d6 enforcers** here (of the 4 cultists and 7 enforcers; one enforcer is at 9). **By day, no alarm:** half asleep, none in armour. **At night or on alarm:** all awake, armed, armoured. Any not present are on errands in the city and return in **1d6+1 turns**.

### 12. Cult Shrine
- **Exits:** E to 11; **secret door** in the south wall to the sewer tunnels (the cult's private way in and out, avoiding the colony; their escape route) (map).
- An altar of human bone bound with sinew, heaped with the severed heads of many victims at all stages of decay. A **naked vagrant chained to the altar** awaits sacrifice.
- **Shadowed sinkhole of evil** until the altar is destroyed ([[Sinkholes of Evil]]: death and necromancy spells as 2 class levels higher; 10% per 1d12 weeks to raise undead; blood sacrifice possible).
- **At night**, if no alarm: Malthusia and Rhankur praying here.
