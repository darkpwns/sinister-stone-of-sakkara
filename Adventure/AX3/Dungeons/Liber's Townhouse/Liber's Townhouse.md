# Liber's Townhouse (Memorial Street)

Judge only. Built day 380 (S19) by the interim procedure for Neris' Order contract on [[Liber Faunus (AX3)]]
([[Order Contracts (S18)]]). Small site: a tall, narrow Old District townhouse on Memorial Street, north of the
Emporium, where Liber sleeps when he does not sleep at The Mink. Rooms are kept in this note (judge-interim, a
compact build). Hidden build rolls: the session log's Judge block, "Site build: Liber's Townhouse".

## Running Liber's Townhouse
- Dungeon level: 2 (town house; no wandering checks: the street outside is the city's).
- **Routine:** Liber comes home after midnight in a curtained chair with 4 bravos (two go back with the chair; two
  stand at the street door all night). A lamp burns in the second-floor bedroom for about an hour; then he sleeps
  until mid-morning. The household: a cook and a houseboy (0-level, asleep in the kitchen), and **Corvo**, a Family
  assassin (Assassin 4, hp 6, AC 6, short sword 1d6, poisoned blade on jobs only; Judge's call) who sleeps across
  the antechamber door.
- **Bravos at the door:** 2 Family thugs (as AX3's Family enforcers: Assassin 1, hp 6 and 4, AC 6, short sword);
  they hear a fight inside on a 1-3 (1d6) a round, and rouse the street (the Family's men from The Mink in 1d4
  turns).
- **Map:** street door → ground floor hall (stair) and kitchen (back, a yard door barred). First floor: the study
  (desk, a cedar strongbox magic-locked by Liber: Magic Lock at 10th level; Dispel Magic or forcing it, a turn and
  noise) and a dining room. Second floor: the antechamber (Corvo on a pallet across the door) and the bedchamber
  (Liber; his sword +1 and the medallion of ESP on the chair beside the bed; a shuttered window over the street).
  Garret above with a roof trapdoor (barred inside: 1-4 rolled 4, barred) and a garret window on the back lane.
- **Ways in:** the roof (from the next house's flat roof: Climbing for a thief; others by his rope), the garret
  window (shuttered, latched: a thief's Lockpicking opens it quietly), the back yard (10' wall, the yard door barred
  inside).
- **Waking:** Liber and Corvo are natural sleepers: each wakes on a 1 (1d6) when someone without Sneaking passes
  within 10' (Rulings S17 analogue); a thief who sneaks wakes nobody. A fight in a room wakes everyone in the house.
- **The strongbox (H42 rolled empty of coin):** Liber's **blackmail files**: letters, ledgers of favours and debts
  on half the city's officials and merchants, the Watch subalterns on the Family's payroll (Polemon Kastorian of the
  Bridge District, Watchtower III), the Dockworker Guild's master (Bodilkas Gargoris), Councilor households. Worth
  no gold to an honest seller; to a syndicate boss they are the Family (Judge: +1 on Change in Management rolls for
  members the files compromise; Rulings S19).
- **Liber in his sleep:** helpless while asleep: a coup de grace slays him (RR p. 511). Awake: Venturer 10, hp 43,
  AC 2 unarmoured (0 in leather), sword +1 4+ 1d6+2; reads a suspected traitor's thoughts with the medallion.
