# Solar Citadel (Ammontürium) — Bridge District #36

The domed cathedral of Ammonar in the [[Bridge District]] of [[Cyfaraun]], the oldest Auran building in the city: cornerstone laid Imperial Year 167, dedicated 49 years later for the 50th anniversary of the conquest. Granite from the Meniri Mountains under a façade of polished white marble from Tirenea; interior statuary of polished marble with gold-coloured brass accents. In plan it is a **human eye**: the "iris" is the great dome, 90’ across and 65’ high at the apex, where an open **oculus** (the pupil) lets the sun in. The domed hall is walled to the ground; the **north and south wings** are open to the surrounding public spaces under a 20’ roof carried by six great god-statues and 48 lesser columns (20’ tall, 5’ thick). Pages are printed pages; "(map)" = read from the map, p. 215. Hub [[AX3 Hub]]. `level: 1` is a Judge's call (a staffed temple, not a dungeon).

Running: no wandering checks inside (a staffed, public temple); outside, city street encounters ([[Streets of Cyfaraun]]) and the [[Bridge District]] special table `Tables/ax3-bridge-special.yaml`. The leadership, audiences and clergy roster are in the district note.

## Layout
- **Entrances:** four 20’ × 30’ polished brass **Great Doors** in the dome wall: **13 East**, **14 South**, **15 West**, **16 North**, each with a pair of Knights of the Winged Sun. The open wings north and south are entered freely from the streets.
- **Domed hall:** the **Colossus 1** stands at the centre under the **Oculus 2**; four **altars 3** surround it at the cardinal points (east altar: Romus at dawn; south: Arëtar at noon; west: Drusus at dusk; north). **Imperial statues 4** line the inside of the dome wall, beginning just south of the East Doors and running clockwise; the newest stand just north of the East Doors: **5** (unfinished statue) and **6** (empty plinths) (map).
- **South wing:** Great Atlantids of **Türas 7** (east), **Istreus 8** (centre), **Naurivus 9** (west), each with a small altar before it (map).
- **North wing:** Great Caryatids of **Ianna 12** (east), **Calefa 11** (centre), **Mityara 10** (west), each with a small altar; the **Stairs 17** (a pair of stairways) lie between the North Doors 16 and Calefa 11, guarded by the knights at 16 (map).
- **Below:** stairs 17 → [[Catacombs of the Patriarchs]] room 1.

## Factions
- **The Solar Citadel** (Patriarch Arëtar, Prelate Romus, Rector Drusus; 53 clergy, 40 lay servants incl. 24 Citadel Guard): see [[Bridge District]]. Any violence here brings the knights from the other doors and, in time, the City Watch; robbing the temple makes the party enemies of the Empire's patron cult.

### 1. Colossus
- **Exits:** open hall in all directions; the altars 3 ring it.
- 24’ marble statue (base to head) of **Ammonar**, Lord of the Dawn, Lawgiver and Lightbringer, patron of the Auran Empire. Its head is crowned with gold-coloured brass solar rays permanently enchanted with *continual light* ([[Illumination, Perpetual]]).

### 2. Oculus
- **Exits:** 65’ overhead, open to the sky (no practical exit).
- An enchanted **ring of black iron** set in the oculus carries the dome's compressive forces (a much larger iron ring at the dome's base, between façade and granite, holds its tension). Gold-inlaid letters in Classical Auran: "For the Glory of the Winged Sun". The ring resists the elements, keeps rain from falling through the oculus, and stores sunlight to shine it back from sunset to sunrise every night: two permanent 2nd-level effects and one daily 2nd-level effect, **base cost 110,000gp** (a fixture; see items list).

### 3. Altars
- **Exits:** open hall.
- Four gilded marble altars round the oculus, each topped by an ever-burning brazier of fragrant woods (**3,600gp value each**). Each altar's holy aura makes the hall an **illuminated pinnacle of good**; under the oculus, where the auras overlap, it is a **hallowed pinnacle of good** ([[Pinnacles of Good]]: +2 class levels to healing and protection spells; hallowed: Lawful rebukers rebuke undead as 4 levels higher; +1/+2 hp per day of rest).
- **Services:** prayers daily at dawn, noon and dusk, an acolyte tending each altar; Romus leads dawn from the east altar, Arëtar noon from the south, Drusus dusk from the west. Arëtar also sacrifices every seventh day at dawn on Ammonadras ("Solar Day"), and at dawn, noon and dusk on the two solstice festivals (Unconquered Dawn, winter; Undying Sun, summer). Attendance: see the table above.
- **Vicar of Ammonar** (1, outside services) → [[Crusader]] 5: Wis 16, Cha 13; MV 40’ / 120’, AC 0, hp 22, mace 8+, D 1d6 bludgeoning, Save C5 (AX3 +4), ML 0, AL L, XP 200. Proficiencies: Divine Blessing, Healing, Prestidigitation, Theology. Spells per day (ACKS II Crusader 5): 2 1st, 2 2nd, 1 3rd (AX3 printed 2/2). Gear: holy symbol (winged sun), *The Laws of the Light*, mace, yellow cassock with purple fringe.
- **Acolytes of Ammonar** (4, at services) → [[Crusader]] 2: MV 40’ / 120’, AC 0, hp 8 each, mace 10+, D 1d6, Save C2 (AX3 +2), ML 0, AL L, XP 20 each. Proficiencies: Divine Blessing, Theology. Spells: 2 1st per day (ACKS II; AX3 printed 1). Gear: holy symbol, holy book, yellow cassock.
- **Congregants** (2d10 to 1d100+450) → [[Commoner]]: MV 40’ / 120’, AC 0, HD less than 1 (AX3 1-1), hp 3 each, punch 11+, D 1d3 nonlethal, Save NM, ML 0, AL L, XP 5 each.

### 4. Imperial Statues
- **Exits:** open hall.
- Life-sized marble statues of every deified Tarkaun of Aura line the dome wall, from **Tarkaun Audarius Valerian** just south of the East Doors, running clockwise in order of reign.

### 5. Statue Under Construction
- **Exits:** open hall.
- A half-chiselled marble statue of **Tarkaun Valros Valuin** on its plinth: begun early on rumours of his death in Somirea. The Patriarch has halted the work until the Solar Citadel of Aura confirms whether he is dead and deified. (Ties to Councilor Drakomir's plot, [[Lake District]].)

### 6. Empty Plinths
- **Exits:** open hall.
- Plinths reserved, optimistically, for future Tarkauns. Roguish young patricians pose on them for their friends when the clerics are not looking.

### 7. Great Atlantid of Türas
- **Exits:** open south wing; the South Doors 14 to the north (map).
- 20’ white marble statue of **Türas**, Marshal of Justice, Judge of Valor (justice, strength, martial virtue); its head and arms carry the entablature under the wing roof. An altar before it takes libations.

### 8. Great Atlantid of Istreus
- **Exits:** open south wing.
- As 7, for **Istreus**, Master of Secrets, Eyeless Seer (knowledge, learning, magic).

### 9. Great Atlantid of Naurivus
- **Exits:** open south wing.
- As 7, for **Naurivus**, Patron of Explorers, Lord of the Winds (travel, weather, navigable waters).

### 10. Great Caryatid of Mityara
- **Exits:** open north wing.
- 20’ white marble statue of **Mityara**, Noble Lady, Mother of Mercy (community, charity, civic virtue); the entablature rests on her head. Small altar before her for sacrifice or prayer.

### 11. Great Caryatid of Calefa
- **Exits:** open north wing; stairs 17 beside it (map).
- As 10, for **Calefa**, Lady of Fortune, Mother of Mourning (fortune, wealth, funerary rites).

### 12. Great Caryatid of Ianna
- **Exits:** open north wing.
- As 10, for **Ianna**, Queen of Victory, Lioness of the Empire (love, war, victory).

### 13. Great Doors of the East
- **Exits:** W into the domed hall; E to the street.
- Polished brass, 20’ wide, 30’ tall. Inscription (Classical Auran), in sense: may my soul rise on the dawn's wings to dwell in your light. Flanked by two 10’ bronze statues of heroes of the conquest: south, **Tarkaun Mennic II**, conqueror of Cyfaraun, sword raised over kneeling, disarmed elven warriors; north, **Patriarch Balbus Ulmityus**, who came with the Tarkaun and stayed to commission the Citadel, with a winged-sun staff and an unrolled scroll of the Laws of the Light over grateful elves in rags.
- **2 Knights of the Winged Sun** on watch at all times → [[Knight of the Winged Sun (AX3)]]: MV 20’ / 60’, AC 6 (banded plate & shield), HD less than 1 (AX3 1-1), hp 5 each, spear or short sword 11+, D 1d6, Save NM, ML +4, AL L, XP 5 each.

### 14. Great Doors of the South
- **Exits:** N into the hall; S to the south wing and street.
- As 13; inscription, in sense: the righteous stand under Ammonar's light. **2 Knights of the Winged Sun** (as 13).

### 15. Great Doors of the West
- **Exits:** E into the hall; W to the street.
- 20’ × 30’ brass; inscription, in sense: cast down the fiends of the night and return in glory. **2 Knights of the Winged Sun** (as 13).

### 16. Great Doors of the North
- **Exits:** S into the hall; N to the north wing and the stairs 17.
- 20’ × 30’ brass; inscription, in sense: blessed is He who lights the valley of darkness. **2 Knights of the Winged Sun** (as 13); they collect the catacombs tithe.

### 17. Stairs
- **Exits:** up to the north wing; down to [[Catacombs of the Patriarchs]] room 1.
- By day the catacombs are open to all for a tithe of **1gp per visitor**, paid to the knights at 16 (for the upkeep of the catacombs and the liturgies for the dead). At night the guards refuse passage unless the party is on official business for Patriarch Arëtar.
