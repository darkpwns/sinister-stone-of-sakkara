# Catacombs of the Ancestors (Special Location #68u)

Under the Shrine of the Ancestors (#68, [[Temple District]]) in [[Cyfaraun]]: an excavated quarter of ancient elven Cyfaraun (c. 650 BE) cleared of volcanic ash, originally six separate buildings with stucco walls and tiled floors; pointed-arch window holes show unexcavated black tufa. The tunnels between follow the old elven streets, cobbles under the ash. The living come here to pray before the ashes of their dead, interred by class. Cared for by the **Sisterhood of Mourning** (Revered Mother Doarë), guarded from vandals by the **Knights of the Winged Sun**. A public holy place: the party is welcome by day as mourners; vandalism, theft or forcing the vault makes enemies of the Grey Sisters, the Knights and the temples. "(map)" = read from the map, AX3 p. 218 (PDF 220). Dungeon level 1 is a Judge's call (AX3 gives none; the Undercity is a shallow delve).

Running: hub [[AX3 Hub]]; **no wandering checks inside the catacomb rooms while the Grey Sisters and Knights keep them** (AX3 p. 64: occupied special locations keep random intruders out). In the sewer tunnels (the corridor past 1, the gated tunnel 9, the passage west to 8 and south to 10) use the Undercity rule: **1d6 every 3 turns, encounter on 6** (5-6 below the Old District), 1d100 on `Tables/ax3-undercity-encounters.yaml` ([[Undercity of Cyfaraun]]: sewer ledges, sludge and miasma rules apply).

**Lighting:** each of the five catacombs (2, 3, two halls marked 4, 5) is brightly lit by *continual light* in its ceiling ([[Illumination, Perpetual]]); the tunnels between are dimly lit by their glow. The **vault (6) is dark**; a Grey Sister going there brings *light* or a torch.

**Prayers for the dead:** at all times **2 novices of Calefa** chant prayers in one catacomb, equally likely 2, 3, 4 or 5 (roll 1d4).

## Layout
- **Entrance:** the stairs (1) from the Shrine of the Ancestors' nave (AX3's text says "#67"; the shrine is #68).
- **North of the stairs** (map): room 2 opens north into a central crossing hall. From the crossing: N to the northern hall 4; W to the hall 5; E by a winding passage to 3 (NE) and on S-E to the second hall 4. From the west side of 5 a passage turns N and E to the vault 6 (iron door); a short passage W ends at the Empyrean Temple Wall 7.
- **South of the stairs:** the stair foot meets an E-W **sewer tunnel**. West: to the **iron gate 8** and beyond it the Vagrant Colony (map label "To Vagrant Hideout & Death Cult": #75u, [[Death Cult Hideout]], its room 2). East: the tunnel runs a long way E then turns N; **iron gates 9** close it at the north end and at the far east end, with a knight patrolling between them. South off the tunnel just past the stairs: the **secret door 10** to the [[Eclipse Hideout]] (map label "To Eclipse Hideout").
- Keys: gate 8 and gates 9 — Aundëmë, Doarë, Cellë and the guards on duty; vault 6 and its chests — Doarë alone.

## Inhabitants and guards
- **Novices of Calefa (2, chanting; see above)** → [[Priestess]] 1: MV 40’ / 120’, AC 0, HD 1, hp 2 each, dagger 10+, D 1d4 piercing, Save C1 (Paralysis 13+, Death 10+, Blast 16+, Implements 13+, Spells 15+; +2 Divine Blessing), ML +1, AL L, XP 16 each (1 HD, ** priestess spellcasting; AX3 printed 13). Spells (ACKS II priestess 1): 2 1st. Proficiencies: Divine Blessing, Theology. Holy symbol (wheel of Calefa), grey chiton, low sandals.
- **Knights of the Winged Sun** (1 at the stairs, 2 at the tunnel north of 2, 2 at gate 8, 1 patrolling 9) → [[Knight of the Winged Sun (AX3)]]: MV 20’ / 60’, AC 6 (banded plate, shield), HD less than 1 (AX3 HD 1−1), hp 5 each, spear or short sword 11+, D 1d6 piercing, Save as 0-level (NM), ML +4, AL L, XP 5. Spear, short sword, shield with winged sun, banded plate with winged light helmet, yellow cloak with purple fringe.
- **Reinforcements:** a disturbance brings the House of Mourning's and Temple Barracks' guard in time (1d6+3 knights, 1d2+1 veteran knights, a subaltern; [[Temple District]] #62, #67) — timing is the Judge's call.

## Rooms (AX3 pp. 170-172)

### 1. Stairs
- **Exits:** up to the Shrine of the Ancestors' nave (#68); N into room 2; S foot opens onto the E-W sewer tunnel (map).
- **1 knight of the Winged Sun** on guard at all times (statline above, hp 5).

### 2. Catacomb of the Prominent
- **Exits:** S to the stairs 1; N tunnel to the central crossing (guarded) (map).
- Scores of cinerary urns in **8 large columbaria**: notable citizens whose remains get regular visits, and the city's **champion gladiators**, much visited by those praying for strength or courage.
- **2 knights** guard the tunnel to the north (hp 5 each).
- By day **25%: 1d4 worshippers** paying respects. Novices here on 1 (1d4).

### 3. Catacomb of the Travelers
- **Exits:** a winding passage W/SW to the crossing; S to the second hall 4 (map).
- Hundreds of urns in **18 columbaria**: travellers, adventurers, foreign merchants and vagrants who died in Cyfaraun. Few remember them; rarely visited; many urns thick with dust.
- Novices here on 2 (1d4).

### 4. Catacombs of the Commoners
- **Exits:** two halls marked 4 on the map — the northern hall off the N side of the crossing; the south-eastern hall reached from 3's passage (map).
- Hundreds of plain, undecorated urns in **30 columbaria** (both halls together): the ashes of commoners.
- Novices here on 3 (1d4).

### 5. Catacombs of the Clergy
- **Exits:** E to the crossing; a passage from its west side N and E to the vault 6; W to the wall 7 (map).
- **11 columbaria** with two centuries of urns: clerics, priestesses, paladins and lay servants.
- By day **25%: 1d4 worshippers**. Novices here on 4 (1d4).

### 6. Vault of the Grey Sisters
- **Exits:** locked iron door at the end of the passage from 5 (map). Unlit.
- **Iron door:** cannot be broken down **except with magical weapons**, nor forced except with **giant strength (−20 to the door-forcing proficiency throw)**. **Doarë holds the only key.**
- Repository for goods held in trust: the **treasuries of the other temples** and items deposited by testators with their wills. **Bookcases** of hundreds of scroll cases: secure copies of residents' wills and **property deeds** for lots in Cyfaraun and other Borderlands places.
- **Five locked chests**, each warded by an **8th-level blast glyph of warding** → ACKS II [[Rune of Warding]] (blast rune) at caster level 8: whoever opens a chest without the password takes **16 damage** (extraordinary; Doarë's choice of cold, electrical, fire or seismic — Judge's call), **save vs Blast for half (8)**; detectable as a trap or by [[Discern Magic]]; can be dispelled or destroyed by Trapbreaking where the rune rules allow. **Doarë has the only keys to the chests.**
  - **Chest 1:** 8,000ep.
  - **Chest 2:** 8,000gp.
  - **Chest 3:** 9,000gp.
  - **Chest 4:** 8,000pp.
  - **Chest 5:** an aquamarine (500gp); a crystal geode (2,000gp); amber holding a preserved extinct butterfly (1,500gp); a flawless facet-cut blue diamond (10,000gp); two wrought silver armbands (400gp each); a wrought gold circlet (1,100gp); a platinum necklace set with diamonds, sapphires and emeralds (5,000gp). Total 20,900gp.
- **Total vault:** 4,000gp (ep) + 8,000gp + 9,000gp + 40,000gp (pp) + 20,900gp = **81,900gp**. This is temple trust property: robbing it is sacrilege against every Empyrean order in the city (Judge's call: Temple of Justice charges, the Order of the Eclipse's attention).

### 7. Empyrean Temple Wall
- **Exits:** E back to 5 (map); the wall closes the passage W.
- A wall raised by the district's clergy to mark the limit of Temple authority and to keep the "undesirables" of the Vagrant Colony (#75u) out of the Temple's underground holdings.

### 8. Iron Gate
- **Exits:** E along the sewer tunnel to the stairs 1; W through the gate to the Vagrant Colony's room 2 ([[Death Cult Hideout]]) (map).
- **Locked by night; open but guarded by day**, so vagrants can pass up to the city through the Shrine of the Ancestors — a mercy ordered by the chief priestesses of the Keepers. **2 knights** here at all times (hp 5 each). Keys: Aundëmë, Doarë, Cellë, the guards on duty.

### 9. Iron Gates
- **Exits:** two gates, at the north end and the far east end of the long sewer tunnel (map).
- **Locked at all times.** **1 knight** patrols the tunnel between the gates, a round trip every turn (10 minutes). Keys: Aundëmë, Doarë, Cellë, the guards on duty. Sewer ledges and sludge: [[Undercity of Cyfaraun]].

### 10. Entrance to Eclipse Hideout
- **Exits:** N into the sewer tunnel just south-east of the stairs; S down the passage to the [[Eclipse Hideout]] (its room 1) (map).
- **Secret door** disguised as a panel of concrete wall (find as a secret door). Beyond lies the Order of the Eclipse; strangers are warned once, then attacked.
