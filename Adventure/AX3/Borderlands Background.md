# Borderlands Background (AX3)

Judge only, except the `[!player]` callouts. The current year is **381 IY** (IY = Imperial Year, BE = Before Empire). Sites: [[Borderlands Gazetteer]]; map: [[Region Map]].

## What every adventurer knows (AX3 p. 7)
> [!player] The Borderlands
> The Pillars of Dawn divide Northern Argollë from the rolling hills of **Southern Argollë**, once the forested heart of the elves' Argollëan Empire, now the Auran Empire's easternmost province, reaching east to the Tëhonaurë ("Deep Ocean"). South-west lies **Krysea** ("Arrow Land"), rugged home of archers and mercenaries, loyal now after a century of resisting Aura; old forts of the Krysean Wars still stand. South-east the black **Zaqāru Mountains** rise steeply and fall gently east into the plateau that was **Zahar**, now part of the **Waste**: dry, wind-blasted barrens dotted with ruins of Zahar, Thrassia and older things from before the Day Without Night, roamed by monsters, undead and beastman clans.
> Two rivers, the **Krysivor** ("Arrow River") and the **Mirmen** ("Black Rock"), run north-east from the Meniri Mountains to the sea; the land between mountains, ocean and rivers is **the Borderlands**, contested through all history and littered with fortresses, battlefields and ruins. A line of forts guards it, but never fully. **Two years ago most of its legions were sent west against the Skysos.** Monsters slip across the border, travel is perilous, the forts are isolated and outmatched, and power is passing to local warlords and private armies.

**Rumours** (AX3 pp. 7-8): `Tables/ax3-borderlands-rumors.yaml`. On a **Friendly** reaction an NPC shares one; a character with **Loremastery** starts with one. Roll 1d20, or **1d20+10** for an NPC of the south (Azen Radokh, Siadanos, Türos Tellë, Türos Erín, Türos Luin, Türos Tem, Türos Aster). Cyfaraun's own rumours: AX3 p. 44.

## Adventurer's timeline (AX3 p. 9)
Known to any character with at least one rank of **Knowledge (history)** or **Loremastery** (the Judge may hand it out).
> [!player] Timeline
> - **4000-1650 BE:** the elves' Argollëan Empire rules Aurëpos from Southern Argollë.
> - **1650-1075 BE:** the Thrassians overthrow it and conquer much of the land; Chthonic-worshipping Zaharans serve them as an upper caste of men.
> - **1075 BE:** Zahar overthrows the failing Thrassians and founds the Zaharan Empire, breeds the beastmen and rules mankind tyrannically.
> - **660-650 BE:** the **Empyrean War**: crusaders under the prophet **Azendor** and the war-chief **Valerian Bellësareus** fight the sorcerer-king **Sebek**; Zahar is sacked, its beastman armies routed, its last king driven into the Waste.
> - **650-245 BE:** Aura, founded by Azendor, rises. **244-215 BE:** the **Beastman Wars**: the warlord Thrax menaces the civilized lands until beaten by Exarch Cincanus; the Auran League unites the city-states.
> - **215-1 BE:** the League rules Tirenea, Krysea and Nicea, then wins Dappakosea and Ivorea from Celdorea. **0 BE-3 IY:** Audarius Valerian, first Tarkaun.
> - **3-214 IY:** the Empire crushes Krysean separatists and wins Southern Argollë. **215-257 IY:** Audarius Ulkyreus's three great wars bring the Empire to its height (Tirenea, Nicea, Krysea, Opelenea, Dappakosea, Ivorea, Kemesh, Southern Argollë).
> - **258-269 IY:** civil war; Kemesh lost to Somirea. **270-280 IY:** Gaius Tavus restores the Empire.
> - **281-283 IY:** the **Grey Death** kills a tenth of Aurëpos until Empyrean miracle-workers end it; the saved Somirean ruler Xandrames outlaws Chthonic worship and swears friendship with Aura.
> - **284-377 IY:** a century of chaos: barbarians take Dappakosea (Jutland), Ivorea becomes Rorn, Kemesh breaks free, Celdorea retakes the Syrnasos; a great kağan unites the **Skysos**, who invade Somirea.
> - **378-379 IY:** Somirea and Aura ally against the Skysos; Aura's eastern garrisons ship west. **380 IY:** Jutland raiders and Celdorean pirates strike the coasts; border forts in Krysea and Southern Argollë begin to fall to beastmen. **381 IY (now):** rumour says the Auran and Somirean armies have been encircled and destroyed; the Empire is fragmenting into city-states, warlords and private armies.

## Secret history (AX3 pp. 9-13) — Judge only
- The ancient elves of the Ammas Aurë, worshippers of the seven **Empyrean** immortals, met men (the **Zaharans**) in the southern highlands and **dwarves** in the Meniri. A being from before the Day Without Night, remembered only as **"the Great Teacher"** (a **Khepri Hierarch** woken in lava caves under Cyfarawn, 32nd c. BE), seduced the elves of Southern Argollë into the **Chthonic** faith: entombment instead of the Light, war, slavery, sacrifice, and alchemical breeding of monsters. The elves of Northern Argollë kept the old ways.
- The elves' finest creation, the reptilian **Thrassians**, revolted after an earthquake levelled **Cyfarawn** (mid 17th c. BE) and ruled from **Zahar**, sacrificing slaves on its ziggurats. Their degenerating race fell to their Zaharan human servants (**1075 BE**); the dying **Thrassian king entombed himself** to await the Awakening, and his people sank into the "lizard men".
- The Zaharan sorcerer-kings (Uragasi, Memnikare...) ruled from the Pillars of Dawn to Sabatea, bred **beastmen**, and may have meant to enslave all men. Under **Sebek**, the shepherd **Azendor** brought the **Seven Radiant Scrolls** down from Mount Audarammas; his first convert, the half-Zaharan **Valerian**, led the revolt, joined by the northern elves. Zahar was annihilated; Valerian died in the last battle ([[Morgot]]) and was buried in a great mound ([[Burial Mound of the Empyreans]]); the beastmen were left in the ruins, a mistake: they bred and spread, culminating in **Thrax's** invasion in the Beastman Wars.
- **Sebek survived.** Guarded by his **Ruinguards**, he fled to the [[Valley of the Sorcerer Kings]], became a **mummy lord**, and refused torpor. He studied necromancy and alchemy, learned the cosmology of the spheres and **how to bring about the Awakening**, returned to Zahar as the **Lord of Secrets**, and built a network of agents and spies among the pilgrims who dare the Waste. Kemesh, Zahar's last colony, carried on its culture; lizardmen venerate the Thrassian tombs.
- **Now:** Sebek is releasing a new breed of **dire beastmen**, settling them around Zahar and southward and enslaving the older tribes ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Stocking the chaotic domains]]). He means to **invade the Borderlands**, enslave its peoples and, at the **Conjunction of the Void**, bring the Awakening in a sea of slaughter. The warlock Narsürius ([[Lair of the Warlock]]) and the Stone of Sakkara's awakening ([[Buried Temple]]) belong to this stirring (the second: Judge's call).

### Secret timeline (AX3 pp. 12-13) — key points
Most of it concerns Cyfaraun and the **Nethercity** beneath it: see [[SOTN Hub]] (Secrets of the Nethercity).
- 41st c. BE elven city-states (Cyfarawn = present Cyfaraun, Siadanos, Cyfandir in the south); 39th c. contact with the Zaharans; 35th c. with the dwarves of **Azen Khador** (lasting coldness).
- 32nd-26th c. BE: the Great Teacher, the catacombs of the Nethercity, crossbreeding, the creation of the Thrassians (late 27th c.), the Argollëan Empire declared (mid 26th c.); the **Bitter War** with the Meniri dwarves (late 26th c.); the Empyrean faithful slaughtered (mid 24th c.); worship of **Dirgion**, god of death and undeath.
- Late 21st c. BE: the warlord Marcan destroys Azen Khador; the dwarves withdraw into the mountains; the Great Teacher enters cryostasis in the Nethercity. Late 18th c.: the hierophant **Orthanach** becomes the first elven mummy lord.
- Mid 17th c. BE: earthquake, Cyfarawn abandoned, Thrassian revolt. 1400 BE Thrassian empire; **1075 BE** Zahar's. **684-652 BE** Empyrean War; **651 BE** Zahar sacked, Sebek escapes.
- **650 BE** Cyfarawn II built on the ruins; **645 BE** a Temple of Demara built over the catacombs' entrance; **245 BE** Cyfarawn II buried in ash by an eruption from Lake Laman (the Lonely Island, [[Ruined Wizard's Tower]]); **244-219 BE** Beastman Wars; **225 BE** Orm takes Azen Kairn; **209 BE** Cyfarawn III ("Old Cyfaraun").
- **108 IY** the Great Drought drives settlers east; **134-166 IY** Argollëan War; **166 IY** Aisoth takes Cyfandir, the elves sign an armistice and withdraw; **165 IY** Cyfarawn III becomes Imperial "Cyfaraun"; **329 IY** a cesspit floor collapses into the Nethercity unnoticed; **378-381 IY** as above.
- **Who knows what:** one notable event per rank of **Black Lore of Zahar**, **Bright Lore of Aura**, **Knowledge (history)** or **Loremastery**, chosen by the Judge. The Great Teacher, the Nethercity and the elves' corruption are unknown even to sages: reveal them only through play.

## Geography (AX3 pp. 13-15)
- **Blood River:** springs in the Meniri, on slopes stripped by Azen Khador's dwarves; red-orange with silt in the rains; about 100 yards wide, 10' deep. Its meeting with the Krysivor, the **Bleeding Point** (Aedon Lann's great Beastman-War victory), is shallow and **fordable in droughts** ([[Turos Spen|Türos Spen]]).
- **Dark Wall:** the northern arm of the Zaqāru; black basalt cliffs eroded into parapet-like ridges and defiles, red with stonecrop in summer. Hundreds of **rock-cut fortresses, temples and tombs** of Thrassians and Zaharans, some miles deep, linked to ruins on the plateau and abyssal vaults below; many pillaged, some newly occupied, some never explored.
- **Istrith Forest** ("Secret Earth"): the last rump of the dawn-redwood forests (with beech, oak, elm, pine), east of the Krysivor to the coast; heath to the south, marsh in the lowlands; unsettled, beloved of the old elves.
- **Krysivor** ("Arrow River"): shallow and fast, 100-400 yards wide, usually no more than **25' deep**; marshy banks of reeds and poplars.
- **Lake Laman** ("Watergreen"): green and cloudy, **36 × 24 miles**, the largest lake in Southern Argollë, feeding the Mirmen; Viamir Marshes to the south. Cyfaraun's fishermen take carp and eel daily but shun the **Lonely Island**.
- **Lusaun Forest** ("Wood-Bright"): silver birch, cedar, oak and yew between the Mirmen and Krysivor; the roads round it are patrolled, the forest is overgrown and dangerous, **with many undead** ([[Sinkhole of Evil]]).
- **Madoan Hills** ("Yellow"): arid limestone foothills under sage, juniper and myrtle heath, white-pink-yellow with rockrose and broom in spring; praised in old hymns, now unsettled because of the Dark Wall.
- **Meniri Mountains** ("Mountains of Rock"): rugged volcanic peaks running over 1,000 miles east to Kemesh, shielding the Ammas Aurë from the Great Waste; cedar below, ash, evergreen oak and pine above, snow on the peaks all year.
- **Mirmen** ("Black Rock"): dark water, black soil; about 300 miles from the Meniri to the sea; **100 yards to 1 mile wide, 50-150' deep**, slow and steady; marshy banks.
- **Naungollë Forest** ("North-eastern"): dark, wet, mossy; low-branched valonian oak with alder, birch, elm; marshy to the south and west.
- **Viamir Marshes** ("Lily-Dark"): freshwater marsh of poplar, white willow, swamp lilies and reeds, named for its rare black lilies; from the Mirmen's east bank to Lake Laman's south shore.
- **Viaspen Forest** ("Lily Forest"): primeval cedar, yew and oak between the Viamir and the Krysivor; safe under the elves, wild now ([[Viaspen Forest]]).
- **The Waste:** Zahar's uplands, once forest, cleared for slave plantations, burned by the vengeful Aurans and stripped of soil by wind and rain: **barrens, not desert** (rain all year), bare bedrock, gnarled trees, thorn, and the ruins, statues and irrigation works of the old civilization.

## Military forces (AX3 pp. 15-17)
- The **Magerum Menotürum (Unbroken Line)**: forts in **three ranks**, each 24-36 miles behind the last, roughly **3:2:1** front to rear. Over 30,000 troops once; most now in the west.
- **Front-rank fort template:** a square or oblong compound of about **5 acres** on a high hill or mound; **15' embattled curtain walls**, **30' corner towers**, **20' gatehouses** in at least two walls; inside a headquarters, commander's palace, two granaries, two stable-barracks, four barracks, sometimes a hospital, chapterhouse or bathhouse; whitewashed stone (wattle or wood in remote forts). Stronghold value **150,000-200,000gp**. AX1's Türos Tem is the detailed example. Interior forts vary; Türos Orn predates Aura.
- Each fort is meant for a **cohort**: 2 cavalry companies (120 horse) and 4 infantry companies (480 foot). Now many south-eastern forts hold about **100**.
- **Watchtowers** about a mile apart between the front forts; palisade or earthwork with ditch where no river gives a line. Crews, stores, reinforcement times and stronghold values: [[Borderlands Gazetteer#Garrisons of the Unbroken Line]].
- **Then and now:** at full strength 25,000 infantry and 6,000 cavalry (1,500 veteran cataphracts, 1,500 cataphracts, 3,000 horse archers, 6,250 composite bowmen, 6,250 veteran heavy infantry, 12,500 heavy infantry); each of the 20 legates kept 30/30/60/120/120/240 in his fort plus 15 men per watchtower. Today's numbers are in the gazetteer's garrison table. Garrison expense per month (AX3 p. 16 table): prefect's realm 366,960gp, personal domain 27,600gp; palatine's realm 84,840gp, domain 6,090gp; legate's realm 15,750gp, domain 3,102gp; tribune's realm 3,162gp, domain 1,098gp; castellan 516gp.
- AX3's own caveat: domain size, casualties and the like make each ruler's order of battle vary; small differences in the domain records' garrison costs are spent on auxiliaries.
