# Watchtower at Eammon Mór (hex 26.26, AX3 #45)

A ruined **elven watchtower of white granite** on **Eammon Mór**, a mighty tor towering over the wooded **Madoan Hills**. Built after the elven victory in the Beastman Wars (219 BE) to watch the Dark Wall; abandoned when the elves lost Southern Argollë to the Aurans (166 IY). The tower and the **bunkers beneath it** are held by **Turtak**, an **ogre warlord** commanding a warband of some **170 ogres and gnolls**, a vassal of **Orm** ([[Ruins of Azen Kairn]], #41).
- [[Ogre]], [[Gnoll]] (MM): the split of the 170 is not given (Judge's call). Turtak: the MM ogre chieftain or stronger (no statblock in AX3).
- Shown on the ceiling map of the [[Shrine of the White Lady]]. Not keyed.
