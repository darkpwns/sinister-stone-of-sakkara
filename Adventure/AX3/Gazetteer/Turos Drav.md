# Türos Drav (hex 18.25, AX3 #44)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Türos Drav ("Defense-Castle") guards a major ford of the Krysivor into the Madoan Hills; on clear days the Dark Wall shows from its towers.

One of Ulkyreus's border forts, guarding a **major ford** of the Krysivor into the **Madoan Hills** and the wilderness beyond.
- **Legate Jonus Tacillus**: obsessed with war, drill and military history, to his soldiers' dismay; frustrated to be here instead of facing the Skysos; would almost welcome news of invasion.
- An excellent base for exploring the Madoan Hills and the Dark Wall. Its NPCs are detailed in **AX5 Eyrie of the Dread Eye** (not in this campaign's library; Judge's call).

## Domain and garrison
- **Ruler:** Legate Jonus Tacillus (class and level not given in AX3), vassal of **Palatine Theremus Augilian** ([[Siadanos]], #34); tribute **2,575gp** a month.
- **Domain** (AX3 p. 30): **720 peasant / 112 urban families**; domain 6 (wilderness) hexes; realm 3,932 families in 31 (wilderness) hexes (four tribunes, as in the template: inferred); realm garrison budget 15,504gp a month.
- **Settlement:** a village of camp followers, **Class VI** market (JJ p. 209: the forts' villages are Class VI).
- **Stronghold and budget:** as the legate border-fort template ([[Turos Tem]], AX3 p. 32): stone walls, barracks, headquarters, towers, granaries, hospital, stable-barracks; stronghold value 200,250gp.
- **Garrison:** the standard legate's fort under-strength ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Garrisons of the Unbroken Line]]): about 90 troops in the fort, 18 watchtowers on the line.
