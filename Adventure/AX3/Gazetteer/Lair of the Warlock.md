# Lair of the Warlock (hex 40.08, AX3 #13)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Orc warbands cross the Krysivor to burn Türos Gundan's watchtowers and raid caravans along the river.

The ruins of a centuries-old **Argollëan keep** in the Istrith Forest, occupied by the sinister warlock **Narsürius** with his apprentices and servants. His **orc** minions raid supply caravans along the Krysivor and probe [[Turos Gundan|Türos Gundan]]'s defences (and the watchtowers of [[Turos Augil|Türos Augil]]), but his true aim lies **south, in Zahar, where an ancient power stirs** ([[Ruins of Zahar]]).
- **Narsürius:** no class, level or statblock in AX3 ("warlock" is descriptive; Judge's call: an ACKS II mage, or the Warlock class of the RR, of level to suit). Apprentices and servants unnumbered.
- **Orcs:** [[Orc]] (MM): warbands of 2d6 gangs, lair as an orc village (number not given in AX3: roll the MM's lair).
- Not keyed: map and stocking still to do.

## Hooks
- [[Turos Gundan|Türos Gundan]]'s Legate Hilerno wants the orcs dealt with; letters at the keep point to Zahar and the Lord of Secrets.
