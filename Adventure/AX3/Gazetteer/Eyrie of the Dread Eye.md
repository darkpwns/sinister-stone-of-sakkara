# Eyrie of the Dread Eye (hex 30.27, AX3 #46)

A gorge in the **Dark Wall**, marked by an **80' serpent-man statue** carved into the cliff. A recent earthquake split the statue (a deep crack now parts one leg from the rest), which broke an enchantment that had **hidden the gorge for over a thousand years**. At its end lies the **Eyrie of the Dread Eye**, a citadel of chaotic power cut into the Dark Wall by the **Khepri** long before the Day Without Night.
- Detailed in **AX5: Eyrie of the Dread Eye** (not in this campaign's library); [[Turos Drav|Türos Drav]] is its base (its NPCs are in AX5). Not keyed.
