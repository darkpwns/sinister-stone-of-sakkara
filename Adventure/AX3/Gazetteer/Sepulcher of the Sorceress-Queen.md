# Sepulcher of the Sorceress-Queen (hex 21.30, AX3 #52)

**Semiramis**, the beautiful and wicked sorceress-queen of Zahar, lies here beside her **hundred suitors**. The sepulcher is a vast rock-cut structure dug into the **south face of a craggy cliff** jutting into the Madoan Hills from the Meniri Mountains; its front is carved like a free-standing Zaharan temple, partly hidden by great rocks fallen in an ancient earthquake. Her successor **Memnhisir** struck her from the annals, so the tomb has lain **unsought and unplundered for over a millennium**.
- Detailed in **AX6: Sepulcher of the Sorceress-Queen** (not in this campaign's library). Not keyed.
