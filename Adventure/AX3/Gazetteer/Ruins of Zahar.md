# Ruins of Zahar (hex 40.28, AX3 #48)

Amid the sands of the **Zaharan Waste**, by the Tiamak River, lie the ruins of the **capital of the Zaharan Empire**, destroyed in the Empyrean War over a thousand years ago. Shifting sands have ruined most buildings, but some stone structures survive, with an extensive network of **temples, catacombs and caverns** below, peopled by **slumbering undead**.
- **Sebek, the Lord of Secrets**, last sorcerer-king of Zahar (who passed into unlife in the [[Valley of the Sorcerer Kings]]), rules a chaotic realm of **dire beastmen bred for the wars ahead**. No statblock in AX3.
- **Sebek's domain:** one dire orc tribe in the ruined city, another in the hex around it, and six more in the adjacent hexes (8 dire orc tribes). **Vassals:** [[Fortress of Gorgbad]] (#32), [[Nurgarog]] (#38), [[Arwia Oasis]] (#47), [[Morgot]] (#49), [[Nabu-Kudur]] (#53). Muster and tribe table: [[Borderlands Gazetteer#Stocking the chaotic domains]].
- The warlock Narsürius ([[Lair of the Warlock]]) looks here: "an ancient power stirs". Not keyed.
