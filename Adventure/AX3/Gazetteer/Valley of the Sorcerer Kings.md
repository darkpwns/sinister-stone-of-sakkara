# Valley of the Sorcerer Kings (hex 46.27, AX3 #50)

A **hidden valley** where the Zaharan sorcerer-king **Memnhisir** had a majestic tomb dug to await the Awakening. His successor followed, and the valley became the burial ground of Zahar's rulers, high priests and viziers. Over two centuries six more sorcerer-kings were laid here: **Djarghul, Negirau, Ankhare, Balathos, Sahura and Mentu**. When Zahar fell to the bronze-armoured legions of Aura, the last sorcerer-king, **Sebek**, came here to pass into unlife ([[Ruins of Zahar]]).
- **Finding it:** exceptionally isolated; searching for it is at **-6** unless the searchers have a **map** (apply to the engine's hex search throw).
- Tombs not keyed. ([[Sepulcher of the Sorceress-Queen]]: Semiramis, struck from the annals by Memnhisir.)
