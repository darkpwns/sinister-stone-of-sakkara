# Nurgarog (hex 35.24, AX3 #38)

Nurgarog is a **ruined Zaharan fortress**, now the stronghold of a beastman clanhold.

- **Clanhold:** one **dire orc tribe** here under the chieftain **Otoug**, vassal of Sebek, Lord of Secrets ([[Ruins of Zahar]], #48), plus **six tribes of ordinary beastmen** in six adjoining hexes. Otoug has **two sub-vassals**, each with a clanhold of **seven beastman tribes** deep in the Waste: 20 ordinary tribes in all. Roll them on the beastman tribe table ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Stocking the chaotic domains]], `Tables/ax3-beastman-tribe.yaml`).
- **Dire orcs:** no MM entry and no statblock in AX3's gazetteer (Judge's call; see any `Dire Orc (AX3)` bestiary note if written).
- Not keyed.
