# Ruined Imperial Fortress (hex 26.19, AX3 #30)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Across the ford from Türos Aster, a ruined imperial fort once used as a camp by legion expeditions has been taken over by bugbears, who harass the settlements.

A keep built by the Imperial legions in the **Argollëan War** to guard the **east bank of the Krysivor**; the domain it protected has long since slipped from Imperial rule and the fortress is largely ruined. Visible from [[Turos Aster|Türos Aster]]'s watchtowers across the ford; legion expeditions used it as a temporary camp. Lately **re-occupied by a bugbear tribe** led by a **crusty war-chief** (AX1's seed site "Ruined Imperial Fortress" lands here).
- [[Bugbear]] (MM): numbers not given in AX3 (use the MM's lair; the war-chief as the MM's chieftain).
- **Reward:** Legate Nicus Balens offers adventurers who clear the ruins the Imperial rank of **patrician (baron)** with a **claim to the stronghold**.

## Running Ruined Imperial Fortress
- Dungeon level: 1
- Wandering monsters: none (a lair: the hex's wilderness tables outside; inside, the alarm below)
- **The tribe (built S47, day 665; MM Bugbear village):** the war-chief Ghorbash Old-Tusk, 3 sub-chieftains, 6 champions, 27 warriors (15 archers, 12 spearmen), the shaman Urzuk (Shaman 4), the witch doctor Grishna (Mage 4), 2 trained cave bears, 27 drudges, 54 whelps, 1 captive. About 4,960 XP of fighters (warriors 65, champions 140, sub-chieftains 260, chieftain 500; bears, drudges, casters as the MM).
- **Status (S49, day 743): cleared.** The war-chief, shaman, witch doctor, both bears, all three sub-chieftains, five champions and every warrior dead or taken (the last champion and the gate tower's watch surrendered and were hanged by the Legate); the drudges and whelps scattered into the forests; the hoard (H212-H214) taken; the sacellum (K6b) a hallowed shrine of Calefa (Rulings S49). Held by Tiberius Maro and the company's soldiers under the Legate's warrant of claim; the patent for Brennus due day 778.
- **The hex (26.19) cleared, day 743 (S50):** an elven fastness (the Willow Hall, nine companies) under treaty in the willow woods along the river; a foul spring; a ring of standing stones (a complex map: 31.17); dragon's blood in a rock hollow (2 doses). Land value 8 (hidden).
- **Losses:** day 738, warband 1 (sub-chieftain, champion, seven archers) slain raiding by the Imperial Vanguard: 20 warriors, 5 champions, 2 sub-chieftains left.
- **Alarm:** the gate tower's horn (K2) or any fight heard: the barracks turn out (K4 1d4 rounds, K5 1d4+1), the bears loosed (1d6), the war-chief at his door; drudges and whelps flee to K6 and the forest. Morale +2 while the war-chief lives (MM). The tribe sees by Lightless Vision (30'): they fight by night as well as by day.
- **Raids:** each week a warband of K5 is out raiding on 1-2 on 1d6 (1d3 days); after a raid the warriors sleep by day.
- **Response to a siege:** they shoot from the palisade, sally by the breach (K7) into the attackers' rear, and send the drudges and whelps into the forest east if the walls are taken.
- **Plain-text map:** a 300' × 200' legion fort, gate west (K2) toward the ford (900'), breach east (K7) toward the forest (600'); inside, the principia (K6) centre, barracks north (K4) and south (K5), the bailey's tents (K3) west and round them, the witch doctor's hut (K8) south-east by the well; ditch and palisade round all (K1).
