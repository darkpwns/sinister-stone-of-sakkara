# Ruined Imperial Fortress (hex 26.19, AX3 #30)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Across the ford from Türos Aster, a ruined imperial fort once used as a camp by legion expeditions has been taken over by bugbears, who harass the settlements.

A keep built by the Imperial legions in the **Argollëan War** to guard the **east bank of the Krysivor**; the domain it protected has long since slipped from Imperial rule and the fortress is largely ruined. Visible from [[Turos Aster|Türos Aster]]'s watchtowers across the ford; legion expeditions used it as a temporary camp. Lately **re-occupied by a bugbear tribe** led by a **crusty war-chief** (AX1's seed site "Ruined Imperial Fortress" lands here).
- [[Bugbear]] (MM): numbers not given in AX3 (use the MM's lair; the war-chief as the MM's chieftain).
- **Reward:** Legate Nicus Balens offers adventurers who clear the ruins the Imperial rank of **patrician (baron)** with a **claim to the stronghold**.
