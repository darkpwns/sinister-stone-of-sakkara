# Ruins of Azen Kairn (hex 04.25, AX3 #41)

> [!player] Commonly known
> The great red dragon Orm has ruled the dwarven ruin of Azen Kairn for centuries; his hoard is said to be the largest in Aurëpos.

Fire-blackened ruins deep in the **Mountains of Meniri**. Founded by dwarves from Azen Khador in the Bitter War (2500-2000 BE), it became the largest dwarven vault of the eastern Meniri after the Argollëan elves destroyed Khador. In **225 BE**, at the height of the Beastman Wars, the **great red dragon Orm** and an army of beastmen took it. Orm has ruled there since, spawning a **brood of lesser dragons**; for five centuries [[Azen Radokh]] has paid him tribute, and his hoard is said to be the **largest in Aurëpos**.
- **Orm:** [[Dragon]] (MM), red habitat (cone of fire); age not given; he has held the vault 606 years (225 BE to 381 IY), so he is at least **Ancient** (401-700), perhaps Venerable or legendary (Judge's call). Vassals: his daughter **Nindukugga** at the [[Redoubt on the Earthway]] (#51), the ogre warlord **Turtak** at the [[Watchtower at Eammon Mor|Watchtower at Eammon Mór]] (#45).
- [[Turos Telle|Türos Tellë]]'s legate dreads Orm and avoids provoking him. Not keyed.
