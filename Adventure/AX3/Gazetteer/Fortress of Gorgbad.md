# Fortress of Gorgbad (hex 43.20, AX3 #32)

A towering, gloomy **ancient Zaharan fortress** on a bare rocky platform at the **northern tip of the Dark Wall**. Home of a powerful **dire orc tribe** under the war-chief **Gorgan**, vassal of Sebek, the Lord of Secrets ([[Ruins of Zahar]], #48). Gorgan guards the **tombs of sorcerer-kings** slumbering in the **crypts below the fortress**.
- **Clanhold:** Gorgan's dire orcs here plus **six tribes of ordinary beastmen** in six adjoining hexes; **two sub-vassals**, each with a clanhold of **seven beastman tribes**, deep in the Waste. Roll the tribes on the beastman tribe table ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Stocking the chaotic domains]], `Tables/ax3-beastman-tribe.yaml`).
- **Dire orcs:** no MM entry and no statblock in AX3's gazetteer (Judge's call: stronger orcs; see any `Dire Orc (AX3)` bestiary note if written).
- The crypts are not keyed.
