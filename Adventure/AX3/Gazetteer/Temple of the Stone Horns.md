# Temple of the Stone Horns (hex 38.21, AX3 #39)

Two huge stone **monoliths** rise from the top of a cliff in the **Dark Wall** like a dragon's horns. Once a **Thrassian temple to Iskara, Dragon Queen of Chaos**, guarding the **burial vaults of tyrants**. Now **lizardmen**, led by a powerful **Thrassian shaman**, have reclaimed it for vile sacrifices and rituals.
- [[Lizardman]] (MM; numbers not given), [[Thrassian]] (MM; the shaman's level not given: Judge's call, a Thrassian with shaman spells). The burial vaults are not keyed.
