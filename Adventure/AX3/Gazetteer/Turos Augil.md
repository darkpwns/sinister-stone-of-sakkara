# Türos Augil (hex 34.09, AX3 #18)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Türos Augil ("Swift-Castle") overlooks the Krysivor and the road from a low ridge; the fort is nearly empty because its legate has doubled the watchtower crews.

One of Ulkyreus's Krysivor forts, on a low ridge over river and road. Relatively safe: the Naungollë is more than 10 miles off and the Krysivor is **not fordable** here.
- Lately its supplies from [[Turos Zera|Türos Zera]] have been hit by "bandits" (wererats of the [[Collapsed Elven Keep]]) and the watchtowers on the road to [[Turos Gundan|Türos Gundan]] probed by orcs (from the [[Lair of the Warlock]]).
- **Legate Peristo Pelagios**: young political appointee who replaced a veteran (now fighting the Skysos); unsure what to do, he has **doubled the watchtower garrisons**, leaving the fort nearly empty.

## Domain and garrison
- **Ruler:** Legate Peristo Pelagios (class and level not given in AX3), vassal of **Palatine Attenus Korbus** ([[Menicos]], #4); tribute **2,575gp** a month.
- **Domain** (AX3 p. 28): **722 peasant / 105 urban families**; domain 4 (borderlands) hexes; realm 3,927 families in 16 (civilized) hexes (four tribunes, as in the template: inferred); realm garrison budget 15,498gp a month.
- **Settlement:** a village of camp followers, **Class VI** market (JJ p. 209: the forts' villages are Class VI).
- **Stronghold and budget:** as the legate border-fort template ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Legate border-fort template]], AX3 p. 32): stone walls, barracks, headquarters, towers, granaries, hospital, stable-barracks; stronghold value 200,250gp.
- **Garrison:** the standard legate's fort under-strength ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Garrisons of the Unbroken Line]]): about 90 troops in the fort, 18 watchtowers on the line.

## Hooks
- The nearly empty fort is a tempting target; Pelagios would pay for advice or escorts (Judge's call).
