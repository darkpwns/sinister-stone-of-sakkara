# Menicos (hex 33.02, AX3 #4)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Menicos ("Little Rock Place") is a lumber town. Dawn redwood from the Istrith Forest, prized for its size and strength, is shipped from here to Arganos in the north-east. Clear-cutting has pushed the forest 15-20 miles back from the town.

Small town (568 urban families, **Class IV** market) built on lumberjacking; seat of one of the Borderlands' four palatines.
- **Palatine Attenus Korbus** (class and level not given): Krysean-born *Attenes Korbis*, Auranized to hide his low birth. Wants to exploit the huge forest **south of the Krysivor** and has ordered **Legate Vandelus Pylon** of [[Turos Veren|Türos Veren]] (#5) to protect lumberjacks on the far bank, a provocation Pylon expects to end badly.
- Vassal legates: [[Turos Aure|Türos Aurë]] (Natherian), [[Turos Augil|Türos Augil]] (Pelagios), [[Turos Gundan|Türos Gundan]] (Hilerno), [[Luseatum]] (Uctum), [[Turos Veren|Türos Veren]] (Pylon).
- **Classification:** borderlands (JJ p. 210 names Menicos as borderlands because it lies within 72 miles of an off-map city).

- Lawful, borderlands; **1,247 peasant / 568 urban families**; Class IV.
- **Stronghold** 204,750gp: 23 stone walls (100'×10'×5'), 4 large gatehouses, 4 small gatehouses, 4 large square towers (40' base, 30' tall), 2 fortified stone buildings (3,000 sq ft). Settlement investment 22,750gp.
- **Monthly revenue 33,346gp:** land 1,247 × 7gp = 8,729; service 7,260; tax 3,630; trade 568 × 1.5gp = 852; tribute from vassals 22,875.
- **Monthly expenses 18,719gp:** garrison 6,124; liturgies 1,815; upkeep 1,815; tithes 1,815; tribute to the Prefect 7,150. **Net income 14,627gp.** Base morale +1.
- **Realm** (Cyfaraun's vassal table, AX3 p. 27): 21,495 families, 7 domain hexes (borderlands), 80 realm hexes (mixed); realm garrison 83,734gp.
- Vassals (each pays 2,575gp): Natherian 735/120 families, Pelagios 722/105, Hilerno 720/110, Uctum 725/109, Pylon 723/111.
- Duty: a garrison worth 4gp per peasant family throughout the realm. Palatine's personal garrison: [[Borderlands Gazetteer#Garrisons of the Unbroken Line]].
