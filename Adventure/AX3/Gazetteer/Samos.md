# Samos (hex 10.08, AX3 #9)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Samos is an old Krysean trading town in decline: whole neighbourhoods stand empty, and everyone says the palatine is on the take.

Small Krysean trading town (532 urban families, **Class IV** market), once a major eastern outlet for elven goods from [[Cyfaraun]]. After the Great Drought (108 IY) it was a waystation for settlers heading to Southern Argollë, then a supply base for the legions in the Argollëan War. Since the Borderlands were absorbed into the Empire it has declined, faster now that the troops have gone west.
- Many neighbourhoods **deserted and ruined**; a **criminal syndicate** has almost unchecked control of markets and merchants.
- **Palatine Vason Krateros** (class and level not given): venal and corrupt, widely believed to be on the take; careful to keep his garrison, answer summons and pay tribute to the Prefect, so no imperial interference troubles his indulgences.
- Vassal legates: [[Andrastem]] (Elpides), [[Kruantem]] (Staios), [[Turos Quell|Türos Quell]] (Lubbo), [[Sarotem]] (Stodius), [[Zelictum]] (Mirasca).
