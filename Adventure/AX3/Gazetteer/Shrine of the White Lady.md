# Shrine of the White Lady (hex 23.16, AX3 #21)

An ivy-covered shrine of **white marble** sacred to **Demara, the White Lady**, built by the elves when they ruled Southern Argollë, hidden in a secluded clearing of the **Viaspen Forest** (AX1's seed site "Shrine of the White Lady" lands here; the hex keeps AX1's Viaspen forest check).
- **Guardian: a [[Unicorn]], 20 hp**, protecting it from the forest's monsters. MM: MV 80' / 240'; AC 7; HD 4*; 2 hooves 1d8 bludgeoning, horn 1d8 extraordinary piercing (attack throw 7+); save F8; ML -1; AL L; **XP 135**; Lightless Vision 60', acute hearing and smell, Listening 11+; *cure light injury* 3/day and *arcane shift* 1/day (self and rider up to 360'). Sapient: 25% speaks Common, 50% Elvish.
- **The pool** of crystal-clear water carries fey magic: a **Lawful** character who drinks is relieved of **one side effect of Tampering with Mortality**, and in exchange is laid under a ***quest*** (AX3: *geas*) to destroy Chaotic creatures in Southern Argollë. It works **once in a character's lifetime**.
- **The ceiling** bears a map of the Borderlands before the Argollëan War, showing [[Cyfaraun]] (#10), the [[Collapsed Elven Keep]] (#16), the [[Ruined City of Cyfandir]] (#25) and the [[Watchtower at Eammon Mor|Watchtower at Eammon Mór]] (#45).

## Hooks
- A character scarred by Tampering with Mortality seeks the pool; the quest then binds him to the war on Chaos.
