# Türos Quell (hex 15.13, AX3 #20)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Türos Quell ("Journeycastle") stands on a river island in the Mirmen, joined by fortified bridges to roads in four directions. Its legate no longer patrols the Viamir Marshes.

Where the Mirmen slows and widens to over a mile, silt has built an **eyot** (river island); the fort stands on it, joined by **fortified bridges** to roads running north, north-west, south-west and south. Originally Krysean, renamed and repurposed by Audarius XI Ulkyreus for the Unbroken Line.
- **12 naval skiffs** (small flat-bottomed open boats with pointed bows) for the Mirmen and the **Viamir Marshes**.
- **Legate Caros Lubbo**: cautious and reserved; with most troops sent west he no longer patrols the Viamir, but would **rent or loan the skiffs** to adventurers exploring it (no price in AX3: Judge's call).

## Domain and garrison
- **Ruler:** Legate Caros Lubbo (class and level not given in AX3), vassal of **Palatine Vason Krateros** ([[Samos]], #9); tribute **2,575gp** a month.
- **Domain** (AX3 p. 29): **730 peasant / 115 urban families**; domain 3 (civilized) hexes; realm 3,945 families in 15 (civilized) hexes (four tribunes, as in the template: inferred); realm garrison budget 15,550gp a month.
- **Settlement:** a village of camp followers, **Class VI** market (JJ p. 209: the forts' villages are Class VI).
- **Stronghold and budget:** as the legate border-fort template ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Legate border-fort template]], AX3 p. 32): stone walls, barracks, headquarters, towers, granaries, hospital, stable-barracks; stronghold value 200,250gp.
- **Garrison:** the standard legate's fort under-strength ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Garrisons of the Unbroken Line]]): about 90 troops in the fort, 18 watchtowers on the line.

## Hooks
- Exploring the Viamir Marshes by skiff.
