# Türos Morn (hex 30.14, AX3 #23)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Türos Morn ("Night-Castle") is the army's ill-omened posting: its first garrison was massacred in the Naungollë, and the Grey Death struck it in 281 IY.

Built by Ulkyreus 150 years ago as a strongpoint between forest and river. Its garrison was massacred on an expedition into the Naungollë weeks after it was built; other misfortunes followed, including a deadly outbreak of the **Grey Death** (281 IY). Officers and men all try to avoid the posting.
- **Legate Darbellus Opiternian**: gloomy and morbid, expects to die in the Borderlands; his mood sinks his superstitious troops' morale further.

## Domain and garrison
- **Ruler:** Legate Darbellus Opiternian (class and level not given in AX3), vassal of **Palatine Valanna Telpirion** ([[Turos Orn]], #15); tribute **2,575gp** a month.
- **Domain** (AX3 p. 31): **725 peasant / 111 urban families**; domain 6 (wilderness) hexes; realm 3,936 families in 31 (wilderness) hexes (four tribunes, as in the template: inferred); realm garrison budget 15,512gp a month.
- **Settlement:** a village of camp followers, **Class VI** market (JJ p. 209: the forts' villages are Class VI).
- **Stronghold and budget:** as the legate border-fort template ([[Turos Tem]], AX3 p. 32): stone walls, barracks, headquarters, towers, granaries, hospital, stable-barracks; stronghold value 200,250gp.
- **Garrison:** the standard legate's fort under-strength ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Garrisons of the Unbroken Line]]): about 90 troops in the fort, 18 watchtowers on the line.
