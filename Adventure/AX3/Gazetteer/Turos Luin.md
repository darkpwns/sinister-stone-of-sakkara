# Türos Luin (hex 20.23, AX3 #36)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Türos Luin ("Sister-Castle") is a reserve fort where the Krysivor is too wide and deep to cross; its legate spends his days overseeing the lumberjacks.

Another of Ulkyreus's 150-year-old forts. The Krysivor is too wide and deep to cross here, so Luin serves as redoubt and reserve for its sister forts [[Turos Drav|Türos Drav]] and [[Turos Tem|Türos Tem]].
- **Legate Victir Syrenictor**: able leader and cunning tactician, exiled here after an incident with a prefect's wife in Aura; bitter, thinks himself above his fellow legates. Spends most of his time supervising the **lumbering of the remaining elven forests** (the Lusaun).

## Domain and garrison
- **Ruler:** Legate Victir Syrenictor (class and level not given in AX3), vassal of **Palatine Theremus Augilian** ([[Siadanos]], #34); tribute **2,575gp** a month.
- **Domain** (AX3 p. 30): **729 peasant / 109 urban families**; domain 6 (wilderness) hexes; realm 3,936 families in 31 (wilderness) hexes (four tribunes, as in the template: inferred); realm garrison budget 15,534gp a month.
- **Settlement:** a village of camp followers, **Class VI** market (JJ p. 209: the forts' villages are Class VI).
- **Stronghold and budget:** as the legate border-fort template ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Legate border-fort template]], AX3 p. 32): stone walls, barracks, headquarters, towers, granaries, hospital, stable-barracks; stronghold value 200,250gp.
- **Garrison:** the standard legate's fort under-strength ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Garrisons of the Unbroken Line]]): about 90 troops in the fort, 18 watchtowers on the line.

## Hooks
- Syrenictor's lumbering stirs the Lusaun Forest (undead from the [[Sinkhole of Evil]]).
- A capable officer looking for a way back into favour.
