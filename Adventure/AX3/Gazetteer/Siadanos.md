# Siadanos (hex 11.21, AX3 #34)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Siadanos is an old elven town on the east bank of the Mirmen, the trading post between the dwarves of Azen Radokh and the northern cities, and the supply base for the south-west forts. Times are hard since the legions marched west; caravans to Türos Tem have been attacked by beastmen.

Like [[Cyfaraun]], an elven town captured in the Argollëan War, on the **east bank of the Mirmen**. Small town (551 urban families, **Class IV**).
- Controls the trade route between the dwarven vault of [[Azen Radokh]] and the northern cities (Cyfaraun, Pyrgos, Larisa); **supply base** for the south-west stretch of the Unbroken Line, [[Turos Tem|Türos Tem]] to [[Turos Telle|Türos Tellë]].
- Since the legions left two years ago, supplying the garrisons pays little and the town has fallen on hard times; the **local crime syndicate** is moving to seize the military trade.
- Threats: beastmen striking caravans to Türos Tem from the Viaspen ([[Buried Temple]], #27); hobgoblins from the Meniri now in [[Dragon's Tor]] (#33); undead wandering north from the [[Sinkhole of Evil]] (#35).
- **Palatine Theremus Augilian** (class and level not given) would welcome adventurers' help.
- Vassal legates: [[Turos Drav|Türos Drav]] (Tacillus), [[Turos Erin|Türos Erín]] (Nuvicerres), [[Turos Luin|Türos Luin]] (Syrenictor), [[Turos Telle|Türos Tellë]] (Mennictor), [[Turos Tem|Türos Tem]] (Ulrand Valerian).
- **Classification:** borderlands (JJ p. 210: a long-pacified former elven city).
- **AX1 tie:** the merchant caravans Türos Tem's legate mentions come from here.
