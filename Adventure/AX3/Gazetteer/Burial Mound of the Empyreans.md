# Burial Mound of the Empyreans (hex 39.16, AX3 #24)

> [!player] Commonly known
> East of the Krysivor, burial mounds of the Empyrean War rise from the windswept grassland, each marked by a weathered standing stone.

Barrows of the Empyrean War on the grasslands, each grave marked by weathered standing stones. The **largest** can be entered through wind-hammered stone doors in its side: the barrow of **Valerian, war-chief of the Empyreans** (see Morgot's tale, [[Morgot]]).
- **Azendor** guarded his friend's barrow with an **ancient ward** to shield the remains from the vengeance of Chaos. A **traitorous priest in league with Zahar** cursed the mound just before it was sealed, so the souls within linger in **eternal torment, trapped by the very ward meant to protect them**.
- Monsters, map and treasure are not given in AX3 (undead of the Empyrean dead: Judge's call). Not keyed.

## Hooks
- Breaking the curse (or the ward) to free the souls; Valerian's grave goods; the Bellësareus line ([[Turos Orn]] was raised by Valerian Bellësareus; Legate Ulrand Valerian of [[Turos Tem]] bears the name).
