# Türos Zera (hex 31.12, AX3 #17)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Türos Zera ("Bronze-Castle") stands on a hill over the Krysivor floodplain. Bandits from the Naungollë raid the road to Türos Augil, and the garrison refuses to patrol the woods.

A 150-year-old fort of Ulkyreus's line on a hill above the Krysivor floodplain. The river is **not fordable** here; the fort is a strongpoint housing troops for the river watchtowers, many of them now absent.
- Bandits in the Naungollë raid caravans between [[Turos Augil|Türos Augil]] and Türos Zera. **Legate Bellos Ionnicato** sent a punitive expedition; its scouts never returned, and his men now refuse to patrol the woods.
- The bandits are the wererats of the [[Collapsed Elven Keep]]. A company that roots them out earns Ionnicato's favour. (`Data/hexes.csv` names him "Legate of Türos Zera".)

## Domain and garrison
- **Ruler:** Legate Bellos Ionnicato (class and level not given in AX3), vassal of **Palatine Valanna Telpirion** ([[Turos Orn]], #15); tribute **2,575gp** a month.
- **Domain** (AX3 p. 31): **725 peasant / 111 urban families**; domain 6 (wilderness) hexes; realm 3,928 families in 18 (civilized) hexes (four tribunes, as in the template: inferred); realm garrison budget 15,496gp a month.
- **Settlement:** a village of camp followers, **Class VI** market (JJ p. 209: the forts' villages are Class VI).
- **Stronghold and budget:** as the legate border-fort template ([[Turos Tem]], AX3 p. 32): stone walls, barracks, headquarters, towers, granaries, hospital, stable-barracks; stronghold value 200,250gp.
- **Garrison:** the standard legate's fort under-strength ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Garrisons of the Unbroken Line]]): about 90 troops in the fort, 18 watchtowers on the line.
