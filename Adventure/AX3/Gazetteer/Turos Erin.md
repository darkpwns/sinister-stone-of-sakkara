# Türos Erín (hex 15.27, AX3 #43)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Türos Erín ("Castle of Glory") is, by the legions' ironic consensus, the worst posting in the Borderlands.

On a high hill at the foot of the Meniri Mountains, the only one of Ulkyreus's forts **not on a river**. Its troops face undead from the Lusaun Forest, beastmen from the hills and the icy mountain wind, and keep a proud misery about it.
- **Legate Lammirio Nuvicerres**: second-rate officer who took the post to avoid the war; replaced a beloved, experienced commander.

## Domain and garrison
- **Ruler:** Legate Lammirio Nuvicerres (class and level not given in AX3), vassal of **Palatine Theremus Augilian** ([[Siadanos]], #34); tribute **2,575gp** a month.
- **Domain** (AX3 p. 30): **726 peasant / 115 urban families**; domain 6 (wilderness) hexes; realm 3,939 families in 31 (wilderness) hexes (four tribunes, as in the template: inferred); realm garrison budget 15,534gp a month.
- **Settlement:** a village of camp followers, **Class VI** market (JJ p. 209: the forts' villages are Class VI).
- **Stronghold and budget:** as the legate border-fort template ([[Turos Tem]], AX3 p. 32): stone walls, barracks, headquarters, towers, granaries, hospital, stable-barracks; stronghold value 200,250gp.
- **Garrison:** the standard legate's fort under-strength ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Garrisons of the Unbroken Line]]): about 90 troops in the fort, 18 watchtowers on the line.

## Hooks
- Undead from the [[Sinkhole of Evil]]; beastmen from the hills.
