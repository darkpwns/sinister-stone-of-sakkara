# Dragon's Tor (hex 09.22, AX3 #33)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Hobgoblins from the Meniri Mountains have lately occupied Dragon's Tor near Siadanos.

A steep rocky hill about **350 feet** high, strewn with weathered black granite, looming over the **west bank of the Mirmen**. A **hobgoblin tribe** lives in warrens inside the tor and **worships a young red dragon**, descended from the monster that gave the tor its name. The hobgoblins came down from the Meniri recently and threaten [[Siadanos]].
- [[Hobgoblin]] (MM): tribe numbers not given (use the MM's lair).
- **Young red dragon** ([[Dragon]], MM p. 105; Young 16-25 years, red habitat): AC 5; HD 6** (hp: roll); drake form claw/claw/bite 1d4/1d4/2d6 extraordinary (attack throw 5+ by HD); save F6; ML 0; breath 90' × 30' cone of fire, 1d6 per HD (6d6) extraordinary, Blast save for half; 1 special ability plus the drake's bonus pick; 5% speaks (then 2/1 arcane spells); TT N; **XP 820 (mute) / 1,070 (speaking)**. Lair 50%, asleep 60%.
- AX3 suggests *The Grey Goblin Warrens* (one-page dungeon, Christopher Brackett, 2009) as the warrens; not in the library. Not keyed.
