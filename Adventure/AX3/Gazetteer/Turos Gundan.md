# Türos Gundan (hex 36.07, AX3 #12)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Türos Gundan ("Castle of Wolves") sits on a large hill over a road junction and a narrow ford of the Krysivor. Orc warbands have been crossing to burn its watchtowers and raid its caravans.

One of Ulkyreus's forward forts (150 years old) against beastmen from beyond the Dark Wall. It commands the road junction to the north-west and a **narrow ford** of the Krysivor to the south-east.
- Well-organized **orc warbands** from the [[Lair of the Warlock]] ferry across the river to burn watchtowers and raid supply caravans.
- **Legate Marcan Hilerno** would eagerly accept adventurers who cross the river and deal with the orcs.

## Domain and garrison
- **Ruler:** Legate Marcan Hilerno (class and level not given in AX3), vassal of **Palatine Attenus Korbus** ([[Menicos]], #4); tribute **2,575gp** a month.
- **Domain** (AX3 p. 28): **720 peasant / 110 urban families**; domain 6 (wilderness) hexes; realm 3,930 families in 22 (borderlands) hexes (four tribunes, as in the template: inferred); realm garrison budget 15,500gp a month.
- **Settlement:** a village of camp followers, **Class VI** market (JJ p. 209: the forts' villages are Class VI).
- **Stronghold and budget:** as the legate border-fort template ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Legate border-fort template]], AX3 p. 32): stone walls, barracks, headquarters, towers, granaries, hospital, stable-barracks; stronghold value 200,250gp.
- **Garrison:** the standard legate's fort under-strength ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Garrisons of the Unbroken Line]]): about 90 troops in the fort, 18 watchtowers on the line.

## Hooks
- Hilerno's commission: cross the ford and break the orcs (their trail leads to the warlock Narsürius).
