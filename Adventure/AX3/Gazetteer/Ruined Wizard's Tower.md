# Ruined Wizard's Tower (hex 20.10, AX3 #14)

> [!player] Commonly known
> On the Lonely Island in Lake Laman stands a broken tower; fishermen tell of flame-clad demons soaring over it.

The **Lonely Island** is a volcanic island in **Lake Laman**, formed after the eruption that buried Cyfaraun in ash: rocky, desolate, empty but for a ruined wizard's tower on its peak, now two storeys tall and blown apart from within. (The hex is lake in `Data/hexes.csv`: reach it by boat.) The mage died trying to bind an **efreet** into a bottle and only bound it to the **tower**, where it has stayed ever since.
- **The efreet** is bored, invisible most of the time, and torments anyone who lands with fire and illusions of flame-clad demons over the tower (AX3's *pyrotechnics* and *permanent image*: the MM's *weave fire* and *perpetual figment*). **If close to death it offers to serve for 101 days**, which frees it from the tower.
- [[Efreet]] (MM p. 148): MV 30' / 90', fly 60' / 180'; AC 6; HD 10*** (hp: roll); slam 2d10 extraordinary bludgeoning (attack throw 1+); save F15; ML +4; AL C; **XP 2,950**; immune to mundane damage and fire; spell-like 3/day each at CL 13: boil blood, burning sparks, wall of flame, weave fire, create food, create water, invisibility, perpetual figment, create objects; fiery pillar.
- **Its dwelling** (made with its powers): silken tapestries **250gp**, plush pillows **100gp**, rich fur carpet **300gp** (if conjured by create objects, they may vanish when it is freed: Judge's call).
- **Underground vault** (the library and workshop are mostly destroyed): polished brass lamp with gold inlay **1,450gp**; a **scroll of *summon efreeti*** (see `AX3 Magic Items`); on a lectern, one of 18 known copies of ***The Obsidian Shards*** (codex, Ancient Zaharan; Alchemy 2, Elementalism (fire); complexity 5; 600 pages, reading 4 days; **1,900gp**, 2 3/6 st); a **locked chest** with **5,000sp and 2,000gp**.
- **Furnace of elemental fire:** the tower stands over the island's magma reservoir, a place of elemental fire (JJ Places of Elemental Power; size and strength not given: Judge's call, minor).
