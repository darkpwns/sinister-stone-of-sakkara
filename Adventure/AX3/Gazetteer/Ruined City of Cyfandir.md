# Ruined City of Cyfandir (hex 47.13, AX3 #25)

A forest clearing in the Istrith holds the ruins of the tall-spired elven city of **Cyfandir**, conquered in **166 IY** by the ancient green dragon **Aisoth** while the elves were away fighting the Argollëan War. Most walls have fallen and the rest are clad in ivy and moss; the alabaster towers have collapsed; only the **great hall** keeps some of its grandeur. **Aisoth** lairs there on the treasure of the ancient elven kingdom; a **tribe of lizardmen** in the surrounding ruins worships her as a god.
- Detailed in **AX4: Ruined City of Cyfandir** (not in this campaign's library). Statblocks: [[Dragon]] (MM; green habitat: poison-vapour cloud breath; age and stats per AX4, "ancient" in AX3 suggests Ancient, 401-700 years: Judge's call); [[Lizardman]] (MM; number per AX4).
- Shown on the ceiling map of the [[Shrine of the White Lady]]; the treasure in the [[Lair of the Manticores]] was evacuated from here.
