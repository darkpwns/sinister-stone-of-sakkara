# Arwia Oasis (hex 36.27, AX3 #47)

A broad **lake** spreads across the plateau, and an **oasis** of acacias, date palms and tall shrubs hints at the uplands in Zahar's days. Now the centre of a **fortified beastman clanhold**: the dire orcs live in a **mudbrick fort** in the middle of the oasis; an **ancient stone Zaharan watchtower with a ballista** stands 100 yards south-west on a rocky bluff watching the approaches; the other beastmen live in mudbrick hovels in six adjoining hexes around the lake.

- **Clanhold:** one **dire orc tribe** here under the chieftain **Usur**, vassal of Sebek, Lord of Secrets ([[Ruins of Zahar]], #48), plus **six tribes of ordinary beastmen** in six adjoining hexes. Usur has **two sub-vassals**, each with a clanhold of **seven beastman tribes** deep in the Waste: 20 ordinary tribes in all. Roll them on the beastman tribe table ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Stocking the chaotic domains]], `Tables/ax3-beastman-tribe.yaml`).
- **Dire orcs:** no MM entry and no statblock in AX3's gazetteer (Judge's call; see any `Dire Orc (AX3)` bestiary note if written).
- Not keyed.
