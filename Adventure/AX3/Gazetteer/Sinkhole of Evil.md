# Sinkhole of Evil (hex 13.23, AX3 #35)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Undead wander north out of the Lusaun Forest toward Siadanos.

In a shadowy dale of wild ash and twisted oak in the **Lusaun Forest** lie the ruins of a **Zaharan shrine**: only a **black colossus of an ancient warrior-king** standing on a stone-tiled floor remains. A gaping hole in the tiles before the colossus breathes an **evil miasma**. Undead wandering north from it trouble [[Siadanos]] and [[Turos Erin|Türos Erín]].
- AX3 suggests *The Foetid Pit beneath the Abandoned Shrine* (Sean Morris, 2009 One-Page Dungeon Compendium) or *The Fetid Crypt of Anatu the Defiler* (Darren Wheeler, Autarch's Patreon); neither in the library. Not keyed.
- (The miasma may make it a sinkhole of Chaos, JJ Places of Power: Judge's call.)
