# Wyvern Cliffs (hex 32.24, AX3 #37)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Wyverns soar above the Dark Wall and swoop on the wild sheep of the hills to the north-west.

Cliffside **wyvern nests** in the Dark Wall, built of woven sticks, grass, bones, antlers and rubble, used year after year and grown huge. The apex predators of the region.

## Development
**The largest nest: 6 [[Wyvern]]s and 5 eggs.** They **attack anyone who comes within half a mile** of the nest. MM (p. 344): MV 30' / 90', fly 80' / 240'; AC 6; HD 7** (hp: roll); bite 2d8 extraordinary piercing and sting 2d8 extraordinary piercing + poison (Death save or die at the end of the wyvern's initiative), or in the air two talons 2d8 extraordinary slashing each (4d8 each in a dive; both hit a smaller creature: Paralysis save or grabbed); attack throw 4+; save F4; ML +1; AL C; **XP 1,140 each**. With eggs in the nest the adults **defend them to the death, no morale checks** (MM). Eggs: no stats; MM untrained value **5,275gp per egg** (E) to a buyer.
- **Treasure** (in place of the MM's type M: Judge's call): the nest was once a **Zaharan shrine**, which the wyverns do not know. Clearing away sticks and grass uncovers a **tarnished bronze chest** in the rubble: **3,000gp**, **2,200pp**, **12 alabaster-and-jet Kyrpeges pieces with jeweled eyes** (7,200gp the set, 2/6 st), and **3 amethyst seals** carved with Zaharan sacrifices (**1,200gp each**). Total 24,800gp.
