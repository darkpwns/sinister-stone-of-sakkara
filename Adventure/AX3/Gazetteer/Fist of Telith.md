# The Fist of Telith (hex 41.23, AX3 #40)

A massive natural **monolith**, about **1,200' long, 600' wide and 700' high**, rising from flat, barren ground in the Waste; smooth from erosion except for **three great cracks** along its south-eastern face that make it look like a closed fist. The remains of an **ancient temple of Telith** crown it, reached by a **staircase cut into the south-west face**.
- The temple has collapsed into rubble. **Excavating it is a 50gp construction project**, which opens the **dungeon below the temple**, where treasures of the old cult remain (not keyed: Judge's call).
- **Place of elemental earth:** AX3 calls it a *very large enduring edifice of elemental earth* (ACKS I), its powers available to anyone atop or inside (**720,000 square feet**). In ACKS II terms an **edifice of elemental earth**, size Large (JJ Places of Elemental Power, 3d6 × 50,000 sq ft); strength not given (Judge's call: **major**: earth research +2, earth spell effects at +2 class levels, 20% incursion every 1d12 days if unoccupied, 45% chance earth creatures present on a visit).
