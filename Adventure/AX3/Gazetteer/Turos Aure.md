# Türos Aurë (hex 43.01, AX3 #6)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Türos Aurë ("Sea-Castle") guards the mouth of the Krysivor and a small port. Ships have gone missing this season; the last came in empty, its deck bloody and its sails torn.

Easternmost fort of Ulkyreus's line, guarding the Krysivor's mouth and the coast, with a small **port**.
- **Legate Retunus Natherian**: greedy and grasping; squeezes the port and lets corsairs of the Free Cities of Ulrukan anchor if they pay. His profits are down: several ships never arrived, and the latest drifted in crewless, the deck splashed with blood and the topsails torn and pierced as if by great weapons. The cause is the four manticores of the [[Lair of the Manticores]].
- Adventurers "of a flexible disposition" may be hired to investigate.

## Domain and garrison
- **Ruler:** Legate Retunus Natherian (class and level not given in AX3), vassal of **Palatine Attenus Korbus** ([[Menicos]], #4); tribute **2,575gp** a month.
- **Domain** (AX3 p. 28): **735 peasant / 120 urban families**; domain 4 (borderlands) hexes; realm 3,935 families in 20 (borderlands) hexes (four tribunes, as in the template: inferred); realm garrison budget 15,580gp a month.
- **Settlement:** a village of camp followers, **Class VI** market (JJ p. 209: the forts' villages are Class VI).
- **Stronghold and budget:** as the legate border-fort template ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Legate border-fort template]], AX3 p. 32): stone walls, barracks, headquarters, towers, granaries, hospital, stable-barracks; stronghold value 200,250gp.
- **Garrison:** the standard legate's fort under-strength ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Garrisons of the Unbroken Line]]): about 90 troops in the fort, 18 watchtowers on the line.

## Hooks
- Natherian hires the party to find what is taking his ships (the manticores, 4 hexes south-east, on the coast in the Istrith).
- Corsairs of Ulrukan in port: smuggling, information, trouble.
