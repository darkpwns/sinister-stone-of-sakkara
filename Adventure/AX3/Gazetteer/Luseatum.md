# Luseatum (hex 29.06, AX3 #11)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Luseatum ("Fort Woodlands") guards a major crossroads by the Naungollë Forest. Bandits from the forest have been raiding nearby.

215 years old, built during the Argollëan War at a major crossroads near the Naungollë.
- **Legate Tavic Uctum** (AX3 p. 19; `Data/hexes.csv` has "Legate of Luseatum"): cautious; keeps his garrison concentrated in the fort to answer a breach of the border forts, and will not change that posture despite the "bandit" raids from the Naungollë, who are the wererats of the [[Collapsed Elven Keep]].

## Domain and garrison
- **Ruler:** Legate Tavic Uctum (class and level not given in AX3), vassal of **Palatine Attenus Korbus** ([[Menicos]], #4); tribute **2,575gp** a month.
- **Domain** (AX3 p. 28): **725 peasant / 109 urban families**; domain 4 (borderlands) hexes; realm 3,934 families in 16 (civilized) hexes (four tribunes, as in the template: inferred); realm garrison budget 15,518gp a month.
- **Settlement:** a village of camp followers, **Class VI** market (JJ p. 209: the forts' villages are Class VI).
- **Stronghold and budget:** as the legate border-fort template ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Legate border-fort template]], AX3 p. 32): stone walls, barracks, headquarters, towers, granaries, hospital, stable-barracks; stronghold value 200,250gp.
- **Garrison:** the standard legate's fort under-strength ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Garrisons of the Unbroken Line]]): about 90 troops in the fort, 18 watchtowers on the line.

## Hooks
- Uctum will not send men into the forest, but would welcome adventurers who deal with the bandits (Judge's call).
