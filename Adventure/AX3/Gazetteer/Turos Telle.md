# Türos Tellë (hex 11.27, AX3 #42)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Türos Tellë ("West-Castle") stands on an island in the Mirmen, watching the pass down from the Meniri Mountains; its legate is a beloved veteran.

Westernmost fort of Ulkyreus's line, on an **island in the Mirmen**, watching the pass down from the Meniri Mountains and the junction of the north- and east-bound military roads. Since Azen Kairn fell, the southern Meniri have been beastman country: goblins, orcs, ogres and giants are a constant threat.
- **Legate Aurius Mennictor**: cunning tactician and seasoned campaigner, loved by his troops; an ogre's mace-blow three years ago damaged his heart and lungs, so he was not sent west. His greatest fear is an attack by the great dragon **Orm** ([[Ruins of Azen Kairn]]), which he knows he could not withstand; so he is **cautious about helping adventurers who might provoke Orm**.

## Domain and garrison
- **Ruler:** Legate Aurius Mennictor (class and level not given in AX3), vassal of **Palatine Theremus Augilian** ([[Siadanos]], #34); tribute **2,575gp** a month.
- **Domain** (AX3 p. 30): **725 peasant / 108 urban families**; domain 6 (wilderness) hexes; realm 3,933 families in 31 (wilderness) hexes (four tribunes, as in the template: inferred); realm garrison budget 15,516gp a month.
- **Settlement:** a village of camp followers, **Class VI** market (JJ p. 209: the forts' villages are Class VI).
- **Stronghold and budget:** as the legate border-fort template ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Legate border-fort template]], AX3 p. 32): stone walls, barracks, headquarters, towers, granaries, hospital, stable-barracks; stronghold value 200,250gp.
- **Garrison:** the standard legate's fort under-strength ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Garrisons of the Unbroken Line]]): about 90 troops in the fort, 18 watchtowers on the line.

## Hooks
- Mennictor refuses aid to a party bound for Azen Kairn or the [[Redoubt on the Earthway]].
