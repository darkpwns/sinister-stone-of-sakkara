# Morgot (hex 44.25, AX3 #49)

The **Fields of Morgot**, site of the last great battle of the Empyrean War, where the Empyreans annihilated Zahar's beastman armies and **Valerian Bellësareus slew and was slain by his half-brother Suhgurim**, lord of the Zaharan Ruinguards and wielder of the **Scourge of Law** (AX3 p. 25 tells the duel: Valerian's fiery blade burned Suhgurim, the Scourge found Valerian's throat, both fell; Valerian's face stayed proud in death, Suhgurim's a ruin). Now a **fortified clanhold**: the dire orcs live in crumbling stone ruins, once Zaharan redoubts, and watch from **two wooden watchtowers with light ballistas**; the other beastmen live in mudbrick hovels in six adjoining hexes. A **ring of skulls on wooden poles** marks the border, which is aggressively defended.

- **Clanhold:** one **dire orc tribe** here under the chieftain **Bel-Zaidu**, vassal of Sebek, Lord of Secrets ([[Ruins of Zahar]], #48), plus **six tribes of ordinary beastmen** in six adjoining hexes. Bel-Zaidu has **two sub-vassals**, each with a clanhold of **seven beastman tribes** deep in the Waste: 20 ordinary tribes in all. Roll them on the beastman tribe table ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Stocking the chaotic domains]], `Tables/ax3-beastman-tribe.yaml`).
- **Dire orcs:** no MM entry and no statblock in AX3's gazetteer (Judge's call; see any `Dire Orc (AX3)` bestiary note if written).
- Not keyed.
