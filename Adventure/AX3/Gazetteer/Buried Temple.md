# Buried Temple (hex 19.19, AX3 #27)

The profane temple the Zaharan sorcerer-priests raised to house the **Stone of Sakkara**, with which they birthed monsters and commanded chaotic creatures. Dormant since Zahar fell, the Stone has **awakened**; beastman warbands gather sacrifices for its birthing pools, kidnapping whole families from the villages around [[Turos Tem|Türos Tem]] and attacking the caravans from [[Siadanos]].

This is AX1, already keyed for this campaign: **[[AX1 Hub]]** (procedures, the forest, the town), **[[Level 1 - Buried Temple]]**, **[[Level 2 - Catacombs]]**, [[Viaspen Forest]].
