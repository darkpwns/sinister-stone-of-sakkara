# Ruined Monastery (hex 31.17, AX3 #31)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Expeditions out of Türos Spen have glimpsed a Zaharan ruin among the rocky hills to the south-east; nobody has explored it.

Ruins of a **Zaharan monastery** among rocky hills south-east of [[Turos Spen|Türos Spen]], across the Krysivor. Most buildings have collapsed to rubble, but the **underground complex** below is intact, inhabited by **beastmen and chthonic cultists**.
- AX3 suggests a one-page dungeon not in the library; built judge-interim S54 (JJ ch. 8: a small dungeon, 10 areas).

- **Status (S54, day 857): cleared.** The cult of the Worm Below destroyed; the gnoll warband broken (four gnolls and six drudges fled); H226, H227 and the vault (H218, three tesserae) taken. The pit in R7 remains.

## Running Ruined Monastery
- Dungeon level: 2
- Wandering monsters: 1d6 every 2 turns, 6+
- **Inhabitants:** a gnoll warband (R1 watch, R4 hall, R5 drudges) serving a chthonic cult (R6 flock, R7-R8 the hierophant of the Worm Below, R9 his skeletons). The gnolls raid the Türos Spen road by night and bring captives for the pit. The legion's gate-priests sealed the tesserae in R10 when the fort at the Ford fell; the cult has not found it.
- **Alarm:** a fight at R1 is heard in R4 in 3 rounds (one gnoll runs); the R3 pit rings the bell in R6 (the flock arms in 2 rounds, the hierophant comes in 1d4 rounds); a fight in R4 brings R5 at once and R6 in 1d4 rounds; skeletons stay in R9.
- **Day and night:** the gnolls hunt by night (by day 1d4 awake in R4 on 1-3); the cult wakes at dusk (by day half the flock asleep in R6).
- **Plain-text map:** the gatehouse R1 (south) → the rubble court R2 → the crypt stair R3 (40 steps down, 60' passage east) → R4 the gnoll hall (60' × 30'; R5 north; R7 south door); R3's side door north at 30' → R6 the refectory (70' × 25') → R7 the shrine (round, 40' across, the pit) → R8 east, R9 south (the ossuary, 40' × 20') → R10 behind the secret door east.
