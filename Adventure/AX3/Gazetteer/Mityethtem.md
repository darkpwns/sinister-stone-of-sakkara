# Mityethtem (hex 24.02, AX3 #3)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Mityethtem ("Fort of the Dutiful Oath") keeps a large hospital run by disciples of the Noble Lady; its officers are bladedancers by tradition.

215 years old, built in the Argollëan War and once garrisoned only by bladedancers of Ianna and priestesses of Mityara. Audarius XI Ulkyreus made it a third-line fort and replaced the clerical garrison with a cavalry reserve, but the **large hospital** remains, staffed by disciples of the Noble Lady, and by tradition the commander and officers are always **bladedancers**.
- **Legate Zeodarë Cellëppes** (bladedancer; level not given): bright, earnest, young; proud of her post, sorry to have missed the war against the Skysos.
- **Recruiting:** characters may seek **bladedancer** and **priestess** henchmen here as if in a **Class IV** market (AX3 p. 18); other hiring and the market are Class VI.
- **Healing:** the hospital (no prices in AX3; Judge's call: treat as a temple's healing services of the market).

## Domain and garrison
- **Ruler:** Legate Zeodarë Cellëppes (a bladedancer by tradition; level not given in AX3), vassal of **Palatine Valanna Telpirion** ([[Turos Orn]], #15); tribute **2,575gp** a month.
- **Domain** (AX3 p. 31): **725 peasant / 111 urban families**; domain 3 (civilized) hexes; realm 3,945 families in 15 (civilized) hexes (four tribunes, as in the template: inferred); realm garrison budget 15,550gp a month.
- **Settlement:** a village of camp followers, **Class VI** market (JJ p. 209: the forts' villages are Class VI).
- **Stronghold and budget:** as the legate border-fort template ([[Turos Tem]], AX3 p. 32): stone walls, barracks, headquarters, towers, granaries, hospital, stable-barracks; stronghold value 200,250gp.
- **Garrison:** the standard legate's fort under-strength ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Garrisons of the Unbroken Line]]): about 90 troops in the fort, 18 watchtowers on the line.

## Hooks
- Cellëppes is eager for action: a party bringing news of beastmen could get her cavalry reserve's help.
