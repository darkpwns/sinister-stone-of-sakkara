# Lair of the Manticores (hex 45.04, AX3 #7)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Ships bound for Türos Aurë have vanished this season; the last came in with no crew, a bloody deck and sails torn as if by great weapons.

A dank **limestone cavern on a forested hill** in the Istrith Forest near the coast; bones of dozens of beasts and men litter the main cave. A pride of **4 manticores** preys on sailors and fishermen along the coast, which is what has angered **Legate Retunus Natherian** of [[Turos Aure|Türos Aurë]] (#6).

## Development
**Monsters: 4 [[Manticore]]s** (MM p. 233): MV 40' / 120', fly 60' / 180'; AC 5; HD 6+1* (hp: roll, AX3 gives none); 2 claws 1d4 extraordinary slashing and bite 2d4 extraordinary piercing (attack throw 4+), or 6 tail spikes 1d6 extraordinary piercing each (4+, range to 270', regrow 2 a day); save F6; ML +1; AL C; **XP 680 each**. Mimicry 3+ (lures with human voices), Tracking 7+, Lightless Vision 60', acute olfaction. They open with spike volleys in flight. MM lair check for young: 20% per manticore (1-2: 1d4 eggs, 3-4: 1d3 juveniles; guarding young they fight to the death).

**Treasure** (replaces the MM's type K: Judge's call): a **locked steel chest** engraved with leaf motifs, left by Istrith elves at the close of the Argollëan War as a store of treasure evacuated from Cyfandir: **5,000gp** (coins stamped with the Argollëan High Queen, dated to the war: antiquarian interest) and **2 ceremonial swords with gold-leaf blades, 500gp each**. Lock: no throw given (standard lock, Judge's call).

## Hooks
- Natherian's commission at Türos Aurë; elves of the Istrith might want the High Queen's coins back.
