# Redoubt on the Earthway (hex 08.29, AX3 #51)

The **Cesta Ithan, or Earthway**, is an **underground river** running from near Azen Kairn south-east to join the Mirmen south-west of [[Turos Telle|Türos Tellë]]. When dwarves held Azen Kairn it was a subterranean shipping lane for the great vault and powered its machinery; the dwarves built **redoubts** to protect it from beastmen. This was one of the largest, a bastion guarding the **confluence of the Earthway and the Mirmen**. Lost to Orm's forces when Azen Kairn fell, it is now ruled by **Orm's daughter Nindukugga** with a **tribe of ogres** that serves her.
- **Nindukugga:** [[Dragon]] (MM), red (daughter of Orm); age not given (Judge's call). **Ogres:** [[Ogre]] (MM), a tribe (number not given; the beastman table's ogre tribe has 38 families).
- Not keyed.
