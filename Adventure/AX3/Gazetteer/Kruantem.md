# Kruantem (hex 14.01, AX3 #2)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Kruantem ("Fort Blood") guards the road from Cyfaraun to Larisa.

Another fort of the Second Krysean Rebellion's aftermath, guarding the road from [[Cyfaraun]] to **Larisa**; repurposed by Audarius XI Ulkyreus as a third-line fort of the Unbroken Line and now thinly garrisoned because of the war in the west.
- **Legate Achon Staios**: lately married into a prominent Krysean family, which cost him the friendship of **Legate Cadom Stodius** of [[Sarotem]]. The two would refuse each other aid in a war.

## Domain and garrison
- **Ruler:** Legate Achon Staios (class and level not given in AX3), vassal of **Palatine Vason Krateros** ([[Samos]], #9); tribute **2,575gp** a month.
- **Domain** (AX3 p. 29): **731 peasant / 112 urban families**; domain 3 (civilized) hexes; realm 3,943 families in 15 (civilized) hexes (four tribunes, as in the template: inferred); realm garrison budget 15,548gp a month.
- **Settlement:** a village of camp followers, **Class VI** market (JJ p. 209: the forts' villages are Class VI).
- **Stronghold and budget:** as the legate border-fort template ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Legate border-fort template]], AX3 p. 32): stone walls, barracks, headquarters, towers, granaries, hospital, stable-barracks; stronghold value 200,250gp.
- **Garrison:** the standard legate's fort under-strength ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Garrisons of the Unbroken Line]]): about 90 troops in the fort, 18 watchtowers on the line.

## Hooks
- Staios's Krysean in-laws as patrons or rivals in Cyfaraun politics.
