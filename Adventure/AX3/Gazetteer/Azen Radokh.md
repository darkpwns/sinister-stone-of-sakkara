# Azen Radokh (hex 04.19, AX3 #26)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Azen Radokh is a fortified dwarven vault high in the Meniri Mountains, rich in precious metals, trading with Siadanos. It is said to pay tribute to a dragon.

A **fortified dwarven vault** high in the Mountains of Meniri; first a mining outpost of Azen Kairn. When Azen Kairn fell to the dragon **Orm** (225 BE), its refugees settled here and dug a great vault. It sits on rich strata of precious metals and trades with [[Siadanos]] to the east, but much of its wealth goes south as **tribute to Orm**, lest he burn the last dwarven vault in the east ([[Ruins of Azen Kairn]], #41).
- **Ruler:** Norden Radokh (class and level not given; a dwarven vaultguard or craftpriest is the Judge's call).

## Domain record (AX3 p. 33)
- Lawful, civilized (dwarven); **780 peasant / 905 urban families**; Class IV market.
- **Stronghold** 480,000gp: one vault (768,000 cubic feet, hewn stone walls, flagstone floors, with accessories; AC 10, 32 SHP per unit as printed).
- **Monthly revenue 17,725gp:** land 780 × 8gp = 6,240; service 6,740; tax 3,370; trade 905 × 1.5gp = 1,375 (as printed; 1,357.5 by arithmetic).
- **Monthly expenses 11,710gp:** garrison 3,370; liturgies 1,685; upkeep 1,685; tithes 1,685; tribute 1,600; other (special tax to Orm) 1,685. **Net 6,015gp.** Base morale **0**: reduced by 2 because the domain is part of Orm's chaotic realm. No vassals.

## Hooks
- Dwarven henchmen and dwarven goods; dwarves who would pay to see Orm or his brood dead.
- The road to Azen Radokh passes [[Turos Telle|Türos Tellë]] and the beastman-held southern Meniri.
