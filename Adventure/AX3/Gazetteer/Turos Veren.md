# Türos Veren (hex 39.04, AX3 #5)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Türos Veren ("Sharp-Castle") is a lumber camp and fort on the Krysivor, with daily ferries across to the Istrith Forest.

A fort of Audarius XI Ulkyreus's line, a redoubt between [[Turos Aure|Türos Aurë]] and [[Turos Gundan|Türos Gundan]]. The Krysivor is **not fordable** here, so beastman raids are rare. The settlement is a small **lumber camp**: wood cut just across the river is rafted down to Türos Aurë.
- **Legate Vandelus Pylon**, on the palatine's orders, guards the lumberjacks on the far bank; he thinks it a foolish provocation that will end badly.
- **Base for the Istrith:** daily ferries cross the Krysivor.

## Domain and garrison
- **Ruler:** Legate Vandelus Pylon (class and level not given in AX3), vassal of **Palatine Attenus Korbus** ([[Menicos]], #4); tribute **2,575gp** a month.
- **Domain** (AX3 p. 28): **723 peasant / 111 urban families**; domain 4 (borderlands) hexes; realm 3,934 families in 20 (borderlands) hexes (four tribunes, as in the template: inferred); realm garrison budget 15,514gp a month.
- **Settlement:** a village of camp followers, **Class VI** market (JJ p. 209: the forts' villages are Class VI).
- **Stronghold and budget:** as the legate border-fort template ([[Turos Tem]], AX3 p. 32): stone walls, barracks, headquarters, towers, granaries, hospital, stable-barracks; stronghold value 200,250gp.
- **Garrison:** the standard legate's fort under-strength ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Garrisons of the Unbroken Line]]): about 90 troops in the fort, 18 watchtowers on the line.

## Hooks
- Escorting lumberjacks in the Istrith; what the elves or the warlock's orcs ([[Lair of the Warlock]]) think of the cutting.
