# Sarotem (hex 08.02, AX3 #1)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Sarotem ("Fort Spear") is the quiet fort on the road north to Pyrgos; legionaries call it Soartem, "Fort Sunshine".

A 300-year-old fort raised after the Second Krysean Rebellion (79-82 IY) to hold down Krysea; it guards the road to **Pyrgos**. The safest posting in the Borderlands.
- **Legate Cadom Stodius**: Tirenean aristocrat, openly contemptuous of Kryseans; convinced the "rabble" may rise again after three peaceful centuries. He has broken with his old friend **Legate Achon Staios** of [[Kruantem]] since Staios married into a leading Krysean family. If war comes, neither will help the other.

## Domain and garrison
- **Ruler:** Legate Cadom Stodius (class and level not given in AX3), vassal of **Palatine Vason Krateros** ([[Samos]], #9); tribute **2,575gp** a month.
- **Domain** (AX3 p. 29): **726 peasant / 114 urban families**; domain 4 (borderlands) hexes; realm 3,940 families in 16 (civilized) hexes (four tribunes, as in the template: inferred); realm garrison budget 15,532gp a month.
- **Settlement:** a village of camp followers, **Class VI** market (JJ p. 209: the forts' villages are Class VI).
- **Stronghold and budget:** as the legate border-fort template ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Legate border-fort template]], AX3 p. 32): stone walls, barracks, headquarters, towers, granaries, hospital, stable-barracks; stronghold value 200,250gp.
- **Garrison:** the standard legate's fort under-strength ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Garrisons of the Unbroken Line]]): about 90 troops in the fort, 18 watchtowers on the line.

## Hooks
- A Krysean-born PC or henchman gets a cold reception (reaction penalty, Judge's call).
- Carrying messages or brokering a reconciliation between Stodius and Staios before the Waste stirs.
