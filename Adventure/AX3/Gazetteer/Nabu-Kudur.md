# Nabu-Kudur (hex 32.29, AX3 #53)

A **fortified beastman clanhold** on the eastern slopes of the Dark Wall. The dire orcs live in a **crumbling stone fort of ancient Zaharan make**; the other beastmen live in mudbrick dwellings strung along a **trail** from the ruins up toward the Dark Wall; a **watchtower with a ballista** guards the trail through to the cliffs to the north-east.

- **Clanhold:** one **dire orc tribe** here under the chieftain **Barashaku**, vassal of Sebek, Lord of Secrets ([[Ruins of Zahar]], #48), plus **six tribes of ordinary beastmen** in six adjoining hexes. Barashaku has **two sub-vassals**, each with a clanhold of **seven beastman tribes** deep in the Waste: 20 ordinary tribes in all. Roll them on the beastman tribe table ([[Borderlands Gazetteer#Stocking the chaotic domains]], `Tables/ax3-beastman-tribe.yaml`).
- **Dire orcs:** no MM entry and no statblock in AX3's gazetteer (Judge's call; see any `Dire Orc (AX3)` bestiary note if written).
- Not keyed.
