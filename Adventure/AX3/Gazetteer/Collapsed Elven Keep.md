# Collapsed Elven Keep (hex 28.10, AX3 #16)

> [!player] Commonly known
> Bandits from the Naungollë Forest raid the roads between Türos Orn and Luseatum and between Türos Augil and Türos Zera; Türos Zera's scouts went into the woods and never came back.

A remnant of the elves' two thousand years of war in Southern Argollë, in the **centre of the Naungollë Forest**. The north-east corner has collapsed and its walls are sinking into a **black bog** that is slowly swallowing the keep; the north-west tower and the west wall have fallen in three places where great trees grew; the main gate is smashed. In the ruined foundation of the central tower, dark stairs lead down to a **dungeon**: the lair of a **tribe of wererats**.
- Disguised as ordinary bandits, the wererats raid caravans on the roads **Türos Orn - Luseatum** and **Türos Augil - Türos Zera**. Survivors are carried back to the keep and **turned into wererats** to swell the clan.
- [[Wererat]] (MM): number not given in AX3 (use the MM's lair numbers).
- Quest-givers: Palatine Valanna Telpirion ([[Turos Orn]], her "bandit problem"; she might then permit exploring Türos Orn's hidden chambers), Legate Tavic Uctum ([[Luseatum]]), Legate Bellos Ionnicato ([[Turos Zera]]); [[Turos Augil]] suffers too. Shown on the ceiling map of the [[Shrine of the White Lady]].
