# Gnomish Trickster

## Concept and role
Gnomish pranksters, burglars and illusionists: small, sly folk who mix sneaking, locks and traps with illusion magic and sleight of hand, and who strike hardest from ambush. They leave their warrens out of curiosity, mischief or greed. Nearest RR classes: the [[Elven Nightblade]] (same HD 1 / Fighting 1b / Thievery 2 / racial 2 chassis, thief saves, 50% arcane) and the [[Thief]]; the trickster has no backstab (Ambushing instead), fewer climbing and acrobatic powers, and an illusionist's edge.

## The gnome race (JJ ch. 13; every value a Judge's call)
ACKS II has no gnome race. Built by [[Creating a Fantasy Race]] (JJ p. 333) from what the JJ itself says of gnomes and the Custom Power Index's gnome powers:
- **Concept:** small, long-lived burrow folk, tinkerers, alchemists and illusionists, friends of burrowing animals.
- **Requirements:** **INT 9+** (required, because the Gnome Value mimics Arcane: [[Racial Requirements]]).
- **Class category values** ([[Racial Class Category Values]]): **Arcane forbidden; the Gnome Value mimics Arcane** (the JJ's own example: "gnomes may never allocate build points to Arcane but can gain arcane spellcasting by allocating points to the Gnome value"). **Fighting:** narrow or broad selections must include arbalests, crossbows and war hammers (the JJ's example); small size bars two-handed swords and long bows (the JJ's example of a no-cost size restriction; Judge's call to apply it). Divine not restricted.
- **Gnome 0 powers:** **Gnome Tongues** (= Bonus Languages: Gnome, Dwarf, Kobold, Goblin; 1 power), **Speak with Animals** ([[Speak with Beasts|speak with beasts]] at will; a spell-like ability of a Divine 2 spell: 65 + 5 × 2 = 75 XP), **Nose for Potions** (identify a potion or oil by taste on 11+; from 5th level brews potions as a mage of his class level; 2½ powers), **Demi-Human Ancestry** (+1 on Paralysis and Spells saves; ½ power). The Custom Power Index lists Bonus Languages, Nose for Potions and Speak with Animals for gnomes; Demi-Human Ancestry is added as the demi-human save bonus elves and halflings have.
- **Cost of Gnome 0** (step 5): (1 + 2½ + ½) × 40 = 160, + 75 (spell-like), − 40 = 195 → **200 XP**.
- **Gnome 1-4** (step 6, mimicking Arcane 1-4): **825 / 1,450 / 2,075 / 2,700 XP**, giving Gnome + 33% / 50% / 66% / 100% Arcane. Gnomish casters may cast in any armour they may wear (as elves and Zaharans).
- **XP after 8th** (step 7a): 2,700 / 2,625 × 50,000 = 51,429 → **+50,000 XP per level after 8th**.
- No extra hp after 9th. Stacked Arcane + Gnome of 5+ cannot arise (Arcane is forbidden).

## Build (JJ ch. 12, Custom Racial Classes)
- **Build points:** HD 1, Fighting 1, Thievery 2 = 4 in the human categories, plus Gnome 2 = **6 build points → 11th level limit**.
- **Hit Dice 1:** d6 (no 1st-level HD bonus); +2 hp per level after 9th (thief). 500 XP.
- **Fighting 1b (Thief):** attack throws +2 per 4 levels, 1 cleave per 2 levels, no damage bonus. Broad weapons: (iii) any axes, flails, hammers and maces and (v) all missile weapons (meets the gnome rule; long bows barred by size). Light and very light armour. Fighting styles: single weapon, missile weapon and **weapon and shield**; the **two-handed weapon style is traded for 1 custom power**. 500 XP.
- **Thievery 2:** 8 selections: six thief skills (Hiding, Sneaking, Listening, Lockpicking, Searching, Trapbreaking) and two traded for custom powers. 500 XP.
- **Divine 0, Arcane 0** (forbidden to gnomes).
- **Gnome 2:** the gnome powers and **50% Arcane** (Arcane 2 progression, spells from 2nd level). 1,450 XP.
- **Trade-offs taken:** 1 fighting style → 1 power (no XP); 2 thief selections → 2 powers.
- **Custom powers:** Ambushing (1), Prestidigitation (1), and, for the style trade, Innate Illusion Mastery (½) with **Gnomish Furtiveness** (½, new: +2 on Hiding throws, priced by the JJ rule that +4 on a proficiency throw is one custom power, Race Creation Example - Dwarves, JJ p. 337). Starting general proficiency power: **Streetwise** (as the nightblade).
- **Drawbacks:** none.
- **Saving throws:** Thievery 2 is the highest category → **thief**, with Demi-Human Ancestry's +1 on Paralysis and Spells.
- **Magic items:** thief items; mage items too (Gnome 2 mimics Arcane 2; Judge's call by analogy with the elven rule); enchanted axes, hammers, maces, flails, missile weapons and light armour.
- **Key attributes:** **DEX** (thief progression) and **INT** (Gnome 2 mimics Arcane 2).
- **Proficiencies:** 42 − 11 = 31 on the class list. Thief-progression classes: class proficiency at 1st, 4th, 8th; general at 1st, 5th, 9th.
- **Stronghold:** qualifies for Castle, Hideout and Sanctum (Gnome 2); takes **Hideout**, renamed **Gnomish Warren**.
- **XP:** 500 + 500 + 500 + 1,450 = **2,950 XP for 2nd level**; doubled to 8th (7th rounded to 95,000); then +100,000 (thief) + 50,000 (gnome) = **+150,000 per level after 8th**.

## Class powers
**Starting (1st level)**
- **Thief skills:** Hiding, Sneaking (only in leather or lighter, no shield, as all classes: [[Thievery Value]]), Listening, Lockpicking, Searching, Trapbreaking, as the [[Thief]]'s, at the throws in the table.
- **Streetwise** (starting general proficiency power): one rank of [[Streetwise]]; may belong to a thieves' guild.
- **Ambushing:** attacking with surprise in melee or with missiles at short range, +4 to the attack throw and +1 die of damage ([[Ambushing]]).
- **Prestidigitation:** at-will petty illusions and tricks; magical pickpocketing as a thief of half his level ([[Prestidigitation]]).
- **Innate Illusion Mastery:** illusion spells calculated at +2 caster levels, targets −2 on their saves; illusion research +2 caster levels, +2 on research throws, +10% research rate.
- **Gnomish Furtiveness:** +2 on Hiding throws.
- **Gnome racial powers:** Gnome Tongues, Speak with Animals, Nose for Potions, Demi-Human Ancestry (above).

**Additional**
- **Arcane Magic (2nd level):** casts arcane spells from a repertoire at the table's rate and caster level; casts in light armour. Illusions suit the class (e.g. *[[Illusory Figment]]*, *[[Phantasmal Figment]]*).
- **Nose for Potions: potion brewing (5th level):** brews potions as a mage of his class level (racial).
- **Minor Magical Research (7th level):** at caster level 5, can research spells and scribe scrolls (as the nightblade on the same progression).
- **Gnomish Warren (9th level):** a hideout worth 5,000gp+ draws 2d6 1st-level tricksters within 1d4 weeks, to be paid henchman rates; they can found a syndicate ([[Hideouts and Hijinks]]).

**Class proficiency list (31):** [[Acrobatics]], [[Alchemy]], [[Alertness]], [[Bargaining]], [[Beast Friendship]], [[Cat Burglary]], [[Climbing]], [[Combat Reflexes]], [[Combat Trickery]], [[Contortionism]], [[Counterspelling]], [[Disguise]], [[Engineering]], [[Expanded Repertoire]], [[Familiar]], [[Gambling]], [[Illusion Resistance]], [[Lip Reading]], [[Lockpicking Expertise]], [[Magical Engineering]], [[Mastery of Enchantments & Illusions]], [[Mimicry]], [[Poisoning]], [[Precise Shooting]], [[Quiet Magic]], [[Running]], [[Siege Engineering]], [[Skulking]], [[Sniping]], [[Trapfinding]], [[Unarmed Fighting]].

- **From AX3's text:** only the class name and its place on the NPC Class table (Mage type 97-98, AX3 p. 53). No gnomish trickster appears in AX3's text.
- **From the JJ:** the Custom Power Index's class lists give the ACKS I Gnomish Trickster Ambushing, Innate Illusion Mastery (½ power) and Prestidigitation, and give gnomes Bonus Languages, Nose for Potions and Speak with Animals; JJ ch. 13 gives the gnome Arcane rule and weapon rule quoted above.
- **From my recollection of ACKS I** (uncertain, not a quotation): the trickster as a gnomish illusionist-thief with thief skills and illusion spells; I do not recall its build, XP or level limit, nor the ACKS I gnome's racial values.
- **Judge's calls:** the whole gnome race (requirements beyond INT 9, Demi-Human Ancestry, the Gnome Value costs and the size restriction); Thievery 2 with six skills kept; the style trade and the new Gnomish Furtiveness half-power; magic item use at Gnome 2; Gnomish Warren; level titles.
