# Thrassian Gladiator

Up: [[AX3 Hub]] · Builder: [[JJ 12 Custom Classes]], [[Custom Racial Classes]], [[JJ 13 Custom Races]] · Build record: `Classes/ax3-class-builds-human.yaml` (`Thrassian Gladiator`)

## 1. Concept and role
A [[Thrassian]] — one of the great reptilian warrior-race bred by ancient arcanists ([[Thrassian (People)]]) — raised or sold into the arena: scaled hide, claws and fangs, trained to the trident, net and blade, and a crowd's favourite killer. A heavy melee brute.
- **Nearest RR class:** the [[Fighter]] (same HD, attack, saves), with natural armour, claw/claw/bite, lightless vision and swimming, but medium armour, broad weapons, no missile damage bonus, and the inhumanity that makes men distrust it. The RR says Thrassians are not a player race in ACKS II (innately evil creations of dark magic): this class is for **NPCs** unless the Judge rules otherwise.

## 2. The Thrassian race (JJ ch. 13; every value a Judge's call)
Built by [[Creating a Fantasy Race]], with the powers the [[Custom Power Index]] assigns to Thrassians (Inhumanity, Lightless Vision, Scaly Hide, Swimming) plus Bonus Languages.
- **Requirements** ([[Racial Requirements]]): STR 9+.
- **Class category values** ([[Racial Class Category Values]]): no restriction; Fighting does not stack with the racial value; Divine and Arcane allowed (MM Thrassian leaders are shamans or mages).
- **Thrassian 0** (all Thrassian classes): Inhumanity (0: −2 to reactions, loyalty and morale of humans and demi-humans, +2 with lizardmen; the JJ gives no size, so ±2 is a Judge's call); Lightless Vision 60’ (2½); Scaly Hide, unarmoured AC 1 (1); Swimming 60’, hold breath 10 + CON modifier rounds (2); Thrassian Tongues ([[Custom Power Index (A-B)#Bonus Languages|Bonus Languages]]: Thrassian, Lizardman and one more; 1) = 6½ powers × 40 XP = 260, −40 (step 5f) = 220, rounded to **200 XP**.
- **Thrassian 1-4:** each value hardens the hide one step on the [[Custom Power Index (Q-S)#Scaly Hide|Scaly Hide]] table (+1 power each), priced at **100 XP per power** because natural armour applies directly to every Thrassian class ([[Racial Powers]]; Judge's call).

- Scaly Hide is an *unarmoured* AC: worn armour replaces it, it does not add (Judge's call, from "unarmored AC").

## 3. Build (JJ procedure)
1. **Build points:** Hit Dice 2, Fighting 2 (4 human points) + Thrassian 1 = **5 build points** → level limit **12** ([[Custom Racial Classes]]).
2. **Hit Dice:** HD 2 → **1d8**, Mortal Wounds +4, 1,000 XP.
3. **Fighting:** Fighting 2 → fighter attack, damage bonus, 1 cleave per level, 1,000 XP. Trade-offs:
   - Armour Heavy → **Medium**: 1 custom power (arena armour is light or medium).
   - Weapons Unrestricted → **Broad**: 1 custom power, **+250 XP**. Broad: (iv) any swords, daggers, spears and pole arms (tridents as spears); (vi) five weapons: cestus, hand axe, javelin, net, whip (Judge's call).
   - Damage bonus **melee only**: 1 custom power (it applies to the natural attacks).
   - Styles: all.
4. **Thievery, spellcasting:** none.
5. **Saving throws:** **fighter** (racial value not counted); fighter magic items; +2 hp per level after 9th. Key attribute **STR**; the race's STR 9+ is then moot.
6. **Stronghold:** **Castle**, renamed **Gladiator School** (a fortified ludus): 15,000gp; 5d6 × 10 0th-level troops and 1d6 Thrassian gladiators of 1st-3rd level. Castle bonus power at 5th: **Arena Reputation** ([[Custom Power Index (A-B)#Battlefield Prowess|Battlefield Prowess]]).
7. **Custom powers:** 3 bought → **Claws and Bite** at the 3-power row (both index entries that name the Thrassian gladiator: Claws and Bite, Battlefield Prowess). Starting general proficiency power: **Intimidation**. No drawbacks.
8. **XP:** 1,000 + 1,000 + 250 + 300 (Thrassian 1) = **2,550 XP** for 2nd level (calculator, Thrassian cost entered as `extra_costs`); doubled to 8th (7th 81,600 → 80,000); after 8th +120,000 (fighter) + 5,000 (Thrassian) per level, added by hand (the calculator adds racial XP for dwarves only).

**Proficiency progression** (fighter): one class and one general at 1st; class at 3rd, 6th, 9th, 12th; general at 5th, 9th, 13th.

**Thrassian Gladiator proficiency list** (30 = 42 − 12): the [[Fighter]] list — [[Acrobatics]], [[Alertness]], [[Berserkergang]], [[Blind Fighting]], [[Combat Ferocity]], [[Combat Reflexes]], [[Combat Trickery]] (disarm, force back, knock down, overrun, sunder, wrestling), [[Command]], [[Dungeonbashing Expertise]], [[Fighting Style Specialization]], [[Leadership]], [[Manual of Arms]], [[Military Strategy]], [[Mounted Combat (Proficiency)|Mounted Combat]], [[Precise Shooting]], [[Riding]], [[Running]], [[Siege Engineering]], [[Skirmishing]], [[Swashbuckling]], [[Unarmed Fighting]], [[Weapon Finesse]], [[Weapon Focus]] — plus [[Endurance]] and [[Survival]].

## 5. Class and racial powers
- **Thrassian 1 (race):** Inhumanity; Lightless Vision 60’; Scaly Hide, unarmoured AC 2, base move 30’ / 90’; Swimming 60’, hold breath 10 + CON modifier rounds; Thrassian Tongues.
- **1st — Claws and Bite** ([[Custom Power Index (C-D)#Claws and Bite|Claws and Bite]], 3 powers): claw 1d3−1, claw 1d3−1, bite 1d6−1, each + STR and the melee damage bonus; count as silver from 5th, deal extraordinary damage from 9th.
- **1st — Intimidation** (starting general proficiency power).
- **5th — Arena Reputation** ([[Custom Power Index (A-B)#Battlefield Prowess|Battlefield Prowess]]; Castle bonus): henchmen and mercenaries +1 morale when he leads them.
- **9th — Gladiator School** (stronghold).

## 6. Converted from ACKS I
- **From AX3's text:** only the class name, on the NPC Class table (p. 53, fighter column 99-100). AX3 has no classed Thrassian NPC; the escaped arena lizardmen of the Festival District (warriors and a champion, [[Lizardman Sewer Lair]]) are MM [[Lizardman|lizardmen]], not classed.
- **From the JJ Custom Power Index** (not recollection): the Thrassian gladiator's index powers are Battlefield Prowess and Claws and Bite; Thrassian racial powers are Inhumanity, Lightless Vision, Scaly Hide and Swimming.
- **From recollection of the ACKS I Player's Companion (unverified):** a Thrassian racial fighter class with natural weapons and hide, for campaigns that allow Thrassian characters. Its values, XP and level limit are not recalled; none is quoted.
- **Judge's calls:** the whole Thrassian race (requirement, every racial value and its cost, the ±2 inhumanity, 100 XP per hide step, the XP-after-8th progressions), Thrassian 1 for the gladiator, the trade-offs, Broad picks, Gladiator School, Intimidation, the proficiency list and titles; NPC-only use.
