# Items — magic, the Stone, new equipment

Pages are printed pages. Items without a description here are standard ACKS items (use the ACKS II rules: Treasure Tome / RR magic item entries); AX1 gives only appearance.

## The Stone of Sakkara (Appendix II, pp. 67-68) — L2 38, on the bone altar
Chaotic artifact made from a large fragment of the **Tablet of Destiny**: smooth blood-red gemstone with runes carved *inside*; now the size of a man's heart.
- **Hit points = power.** Minimum 5 hp (d20-sized) to 1,000 hp (torso-sized). **Now 20 hp.** History: woke ~2 years ago; Zakiti began sacrificing ~3 months later; 10 hp a little over a year ago, 15 hp three months ago, 20 hp last month (300 XP of victims: 60 humans).
- **Monthly sacrifice:** creatures worth XP ≥ 10 × current hp (now 200 XP). Less: −5 hp at month's end (min 5). Twice the requirement or more: +5 hp. Any Chaotic who makes at least one blood sacrifice gains its favour for the month; whoever makes the most sacrifices is its **owner**.
- **Favour:** necromantic and transformation rituals: at 20 hp +1 to magic research rolls and time and cost −¼; 100 hp +2, −½; 500 hp +3, −⅔; 1,000 hp +4, −¾. The owner automatically controls any abomination he sees (as charm monster) and controls undead as a 6th level chaotic cleric (those who already can: as 2 levels higher).
- **Taint:** while above 5 hp, taints 4,186 cubic feet per hp per day (one 10’-radius sphere per hp per day) through air, water and topsoil, at most 30’ above a surface; through rock only 1 cubic foot per hp per day; never beyond 24 miles. Level 2 is wholly tainted and it is seeping into the rock.
  - After a month: water in the tainted region causes mutations when drunk (Abominable Mutation table, [[Level 2 - Catacombs]] room 16).
  - Animals of less than 1 HD living in a tainted region a week swarm and merge into abominations (add HD/hp until an abomination's).
  - After 6 months (and every 6 months): a small 'tree' forms in a tainted body of water; that pool can make one abomination a month (afterwards the water is merely stagnant until next time; further attempts drown the victim). Fronds spread 1’ per month in all directions.
  - After a year: a large tree forms; any number of abominations can be made by holding a creature under its fronds until the change completes. 0-level humans staying more than a week in the area save vs Death or transform. Fronds spread 5’ per month; some surfaces grow chitin.
  - Abominations are source material for Chaotic transformation rituals (+1 research); a tainted birthing pool used as a vat gives +2 (cumulative with the Stone's bonus); a successful ritual exhausts a small pool for a month, not a large one.
- **Sentience:** Int 8, Ego 12 + 1 per 100 hp, AL C. Detects Chaotic creatures within 100 miles; once a week sends one a telepathic dream/vision with direction and range. Chaotic creatures messaged by it or seeing it save vs Spells or seek it out and protect it with their lives (they may leave it while they think it safe). The red-heart nightmares in Türos Tem are this call.
- **Yearly:** if pleased with its owner it grants a mostly beneficial mutation (Zakiti's cat eyes); if displeased (owner sacrificed no more than the minimum), a disfiguring one.
- **Sinkhole:** a year in one place makes that place a shadowed sinkhole as an altar worth 100gp per Stone hp.
- **Damaging it:** needs STR 16+ with a heavy implement, or a magic weapon. Any damage calls all abominations within 100’ × current hp (now 2,000’), who come at full speed to defend it.
- **Destroying it (0 hp):** shatters into three identical fragments; its corruption bursts into a crimson cloud: **the character who struck the blow saves vs Death or becomes an abomination**; everyone within 5’ saves vs Death or gains a mutation. The area reverts to normal after a year, or in 1d3 months if Lawful priests consecrate it with holy water and bless.
- **Fragments of the Tablet of Destiny (3):** each a special component worth **1,500gp**, usable for any ritual spell ("frozen" divine power). Sacred to most Lawful divine casters, whose strictures command them to hand fragments to their order (Genelen claims them for Mityara's order; the legate for the Empire's clerics). **Turning them over: 10,000 XP bonus** divided among participating adventurers. They could be sold for ten times component value (15,000gp each) — attracting thieves, cultists and rivals.

## Birthing pools (L2 16, 20, 25, 26)
See [[Level 2 - Catacombs]] room 16: wound-healing with disfigurement (−1 reactions), mutations on drinking (save vs Spells) or immersion (no save), −12 total → abomination; killing the large tree (L2 20, 50 hp) spoils the pool.
