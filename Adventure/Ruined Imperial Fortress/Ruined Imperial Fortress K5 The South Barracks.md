# K5. The South Barracks

The twin of the north barracks, half its roof fallen in: the bugbears live in the eastern half under hides and turf, the western half a rubble-floored yard where they fight for sport.

### Features
- 120' × 30'; the west half roofless; the doors as K4's.

### Monsters
**Warband 1: sub-chieftain S1** (29 hp, AC 6, leather and shield, morning star) and **gang 1 (archers)**: champion C1 (25 hp) and W1 (6), W2 (7), W3 (17), W4 (15), W5 (18), W6 (10), W7 (11). **Warband 2: sub-chieftain S2** (29 hp, AC 6) and **gang 2 (warriors)**: champion C2 (25 hp) and W25 (13), W26 (13), W27 (17), W28 (16), W29 (14). **The captive** of warband 2 is not here but in the bailey pen (K3): Lucius, a fourteen-year-old ferryman's boy of the ford village, taken at the autumn raid; he knows the fort's lanes, the horn, the pit and that "the old one sleeps under the big house". Roused by the horn they come out in 1d4+1 rounds. Raids: each week a warband of this barrack (1-3: warband 1; 4-6: warband 2) is out raiding on 1-2 on 1d6, gone 1d3 days (Judge's call; AX3's harassment of the settlements).

### Treasure
Coins carried (counted in the hoard).

### Traps
None.

### Checks and saves
Listening; Sneaking past by night.

### Exits
North door to K3; east to the lane by K7.
