# K1. Ditch and Rampart

A ditch ten feet deep, half silted, rings the fort 30' out from the walls; the old earth rampart behind it carries the stone curtain (12' high where it stands). Along the top the bugbears have staked a palisade of sharpened logs hung with skulls, men's, horses' and dogs'.

### Features
- The fort: 300' (east-west) × 200' (north-south), its west gate 900' east of the ford across open winter meadow (the old road runs straight to it); forest (27.19, 26.20) 600' off the east and south walls.
- Crossing the ditch: a turn and a Climbing throw for the rampart and palisade where no breach is (RR Climbing; armored -4 as the rule gives).
- Seen from Türos Aster's towers (across the river, 1,200'): smoke, the palisade, figures on the gate tower; nothing inside the walls.

### Monsters
None here (see K2's watch).

### Treasure
None.

### Traps
Bones and skulls only.

### Checks and saves
Climbing the palisade (RR Climbing); a Hiding throw to approach across the meadow by day is impossible (open ground) save in fog or snowfall (Judge's call).

### Exits
West to K2 (the gate); east to K7 (the breach); the ditch runs round.
