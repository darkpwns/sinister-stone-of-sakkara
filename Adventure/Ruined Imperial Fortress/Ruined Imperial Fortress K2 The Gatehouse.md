# K2. The Gatehouse

Two square towers flank the west gate. The north tower stands to its parapet; the south one is a stump of rubble. The gateway is choked with a barricade of logs and wagon-wheels that drudges drag aside for a raid.

### Features
- The north tower: 20' high; a horn of aurochs on a thong at the top; arrow slits west and over the gateway.
- The barricade: 2 turns for drudges to open; a Dungeonbashing throw at -4 to break, or burn it (oil; 3 turns).

### Monsters
**Gang 3c (archers) and their champion** on the north tower day and night (the watch is changed at dusk and dawn from K4): champion C6 (4+1 HD, 25 hp, AC 5, hide and shield, battle axe +3), archers W20 (13 hp), W21 (19 hp) (3+1 HD, AC 4, short bow and 20 arrows each, battle axe). Seeing intruders, one blows the horn (the whole fort is roused: K4 answers in 1d4 rounds, K5 in 1d4+1, the bears loosed in 1d6) while the others shoot. They cannot see the east breach (K7).

### Treasure
None.

### Traps
Barricade, horn.

### Checks and saves
Surprise (MM: anyone meeting bugbears is at -2 on surprise rolls). Their stealth applies to them, not to their watch's noticing approachers.

### Exits
The tower stair up; through the barricade east into K3 (the bailey).
