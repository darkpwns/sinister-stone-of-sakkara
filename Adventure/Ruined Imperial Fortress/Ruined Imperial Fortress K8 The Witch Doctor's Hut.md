# K8. The Witch Doctor's Hut

A turf-roofed hut against the south-east wall by the old well, its door-post hung with dried hands and bird skulls. Inside: smoke, bundles of herbs, a cauldron.

### Features
- 15' across; the well (60' deep, foul water) outside the door.

### Monsters
**The witch doctor Grishna** (champion stats: 4+1 HD, 25 hp, AC 5; Mage 4; spells prepared: Slumber, Fan of Flames; Webbing); she fights from the doorway, then runs for K6. A cunning old female; hates the shaman.

### Treasure
Her spellbook (a bundle of painted hides: her four spells' formulae for a mage who can read Ancient Zaharan); 40gp of charms and amber (part of the hoard's count).

### Traps
None.

### Checks and saves
Her Slumber and Fan (save as the spells give).

### Exits
North-west to K3.
