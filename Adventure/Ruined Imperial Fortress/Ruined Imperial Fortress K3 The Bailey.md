# K3. The Bailey

Inside the walls the old legion streets are lost under hide tents and turf-roofed roundhouses, middens and drying racks, smoke everywhere. Bugbear drudges work hides and fires; scores of young bugbears scuffle in the mud. A pit in the north-west corner reeks of bear.

### Features
- The drudges' and whelps' tents fill the west half; the bear pit (15' deep, a log grid over it) in the north-west corner; the captive pen (a wicker cage) beside the cookfires; the old well and K8 (the witch doctor's hut) in the south-east.
- Crossing the bailey unseen: by night only, with a Sneaking throw (the drudges sleep by their fires; whelps wake easily: Judge's call: a failed throw is heard).

### Monsters
**27 drudges** (fight as hobgoblins: 1+1 HD, AC 2, clubs and knives; hp rolled when they fight; morale -2: they flee unless the war-chief is there to see) and **54 whelps** (as kobolds; they do not fight: they run to K6's cellar). **Two trained cave bears** in the pit (B1 14 hp, B2 36 hp; MM Cave Bear: AC 3, 6 HD, 2 claws and a bite, morale 0): loosed by the drudge keepers when the horn blows (1d6 rounds), they go for the nearest strangers.

### Treasure
Nothing of value (bugbear chattel: hides, pots, a few stolen tools).

### Traps
None.

### Checks and saves
Sneaking by night; hearing whelps (Judge's call).

### Exits
West to K2; north to K4 (north barracks); south to K5 (south barracks); centre to K6 (the principia); south-east to K8; east to K7.
