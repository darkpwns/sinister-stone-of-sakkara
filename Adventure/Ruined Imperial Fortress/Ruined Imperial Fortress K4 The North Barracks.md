# K4. The North Barracks

A long stone barrack block whose roof has been rethatched with reeds and hides. Inside, a single smoky hall of fur pallets, weapons racks and a fire trench; it stinks of meat and wet fur.

### Features
- 120' × 30'; doors at each end (west to the bailey, east to the lane by K7).
- The warband sleeps by day if it raided by night, else by night; one stays awake at the door (Judge's call: 1 in 6 he dozes).

### Monsters
**Warband 3: sub-chieftain S3** (5+1 HD, 29 hp, AC 6, leather and shield, wields **the Sword +1** of H213 (an old legion gladius he took from a dead tribune)), **gang 3a (archers)**: champion C3 (25 hp) and W8 (17), W9 (16), W10 (14), W11 (22), W12 (11), W13 (12); **gang 3b (warriors: spear, hand axe, shield)**: champion C4 (25 hp) and W14 (10), W15 (12), W16 (17), W17 (12); **gang 3d (warriors)**: champion C5 (25 hp) and W22 (14), W23 (13), W24 (14). (Gang 3c is at the gate, K2.) Morale +2 while the war-chief lives (MM). Roused by the horn they come out in 1d4 rounds by the west door.

### Treasure
Each bugbear carries a few coins of his raids (part of H212-H214's count, kept in the hoard K6b).

### Traps
None.

### Checks and saves
Listening at a door (RR); Sneaking past by night.

### Exits
West door to K3; east door to the lane by K7.
