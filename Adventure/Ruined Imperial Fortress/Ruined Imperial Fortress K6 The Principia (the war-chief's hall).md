# K6. The Principia (the war-chief's hall)

The old headquarters: a square two-storey stone block in the middle of the fort, its colonnade fallen, its doorway hung with a bearskin. Inside, a pillared hall around a great fire, the legion's eagle-standard pole stuck upright behind a heap of furs, and the war-chief himself.

### Features
- Ground floor: the hall (60' × 40'), the fire, the war-chief's seat of piled saddles and shields; a stair down in the north-east corner to **K6b, the sacellum** (the old strongroom, 20' × 20', iron door rusted open, now the shaman's shrine and the treasure store); the upper floor fallen in.
- **The crusty war-chief, Ghorbash Old-Tusk**: grey-muzzled, one tusk broken, scarred over the eyes; slow to rise, quick to strike; respects strength and coin; he speaks Common (10% per HD) on a 1-7 on 1d10 (hidden roll at the first parley).

### Monsters
**The war-chief** (chieftain: 7+2 HD, 37 hp, AC 8 with **the Shield +1** of H212 on his scale armor, a great axe: weapon +4; while he lives the tribe's morale +2) and **the shaman Urzuk** (sub-chieftain stats 5+1 HD, 29 hp, AC 6; Shaman 4; wields **the Club +1** of H214; spells prepared: Cause Fear (Remove Fear reversed), Cure Light Injury, Holy Blessing (unholy, Chaotic)). By night the war-chief sleeps here with two drudge wives (as K3). At the horn he stands in his doorway and roars the warbands into line.

### Treasure
**K6b, the sacellum:** H212, H213, H214 (TT L ×3): **11,000sp** and **14,000ep** in hide sacks and a dented legion pay-chest; jewelry: alabaster and ivory pieces 700gp, glass and shell 90gp ×2, wrought orichalcum with opals 2,000gp, gold and ivory 1,100gp ×2 and 800gp, carved jade 700gp; gems: a coral 100gp, a topaz 500gp; **a Potion of Cure Disease** (a stoppered horn); **Ivorean War-Paint** (a pot of white paint in a bone box). (The Shield +1 is on the war-chief, the Sword +1 with S3, the Club +1 with the shaman.) The shrine: a bear skull on the legion's altar, painted red.

### Traps
None (the shrine is no trap).

### Checks and saves
Parley with the war-chief: reaction by the engine (bugbears Chaotic); his Common (hidden 1d10); morale.

### Exits
West to K3; the stair down to K6b.
