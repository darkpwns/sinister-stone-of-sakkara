# K7. The East Breach

A forty-foot length of the east curtain has slumped into the ditch, leaving a ramp of rubble to the forest edge. Strings of bones and tin hang across the gap and clatter in the wind.

### Features
- The bugbears' own path to the forest (their hunters and raiders use it); 600' to the trees.
- The bone chimes: anyone crossing the gap without a Trapbreaking or careful handling (a turn) sets them ringing (Judge's call: heard by K4 and K5 on 1-2 on 1d6, by night 1-3).

### Monsters
None posted.

### Treasure
None.

### Traps
The bone chimes (noise only; Judge's ruling).

### Checks and saves
Trapbreaking or a turn's care to pass quietly; Sneaking.

### Exits
West into the lane between K4 and K5 and the bailey; east down the rubble to the forest.
