# Level 1 — The Buried Temple

Zaharan temple 380’ × 500’, 12’6” tall (ziggurat 40’; printed also "50’ at the apex"), buried under a dome of black granite whose roof is 350’ above the open courtyards. Walls ochre stone with asphalt mortar; floors fired brick. Passages 10’ wide, rooms 10’ high unless noted. Windows in perimeter rooms open onto black rock. Pages are printed pages. Connections marked "(map)" are read from the low-resolution map, not stated in the text.

Running: hub [[AX1 Hub]] (wandering 1d6 every 2 turns, 6+; table `Tables/level1-wandering.yaml`). Area 1 and room 19 and Area 4: 5-6 on 1d6 **each turn**. Rooftops (from 13, 18, 19, 42, 61): only giant rats or stirges. Doors stuck when first met. Lore in brackets needs a lore throw (Loremastery, Knowledge (Ancient History/Occult), Theology).

## Layout
- **South:** the Entry Way (1) is a 130’ crack in the plateau; bronze double doors open north into the **Great Hall (2)**, a 20’ × 270’ east-west artery. Off it: kobolds west (Area 1: 3-13), brigands east (Area 2: 14-17 via double doors at the east end; 13 via a south door), and the **Crossroads (19)** north (door in the hall's north wall).
- **Crossroads 19** (open-air): doors W to 8 (kobolds), NW to the **Long Hall 20** (orcs, Area 3), NE to the **west corridor** that runs north beside Area 4 to the junction under 45 (hobgoblins).
- **Area 3 (orcs, 20-32):** Long Hall 20 → toll rooms 26/27 → door north into the northern **Great Hall 43**; 23 → 24/25; 27 → 28 → 29/30/31. Secret vault 32 between 31 and 9.
- **Area 4 (old goblin territory, 33-44):** a block of rooms ringed by corridors (west corridor from 19; north corridor under 45-49; east corridor with 36-38; south corridor). 42 is an open-air courtyard with the well shaft to L2. Great Hall 43 runs west from the west corridor to the Ogre's Cave 44.
- **Area 5 (hobgoblins, 45-60):** reached by the door from the north corridor into 45. Column 45-59-58-57, then rubble-strewn 56-55-54 along the north wall; 46-47 (preparation rooms) under the Chief's Chamber 60; 48-52 on the east side; stair room 53.
- **Area 6 (ziggurat, 61-75):** the open-air **Courtyard 61** (150’ square) with the **Ziggurat 75**. Doors from 61 to 62, 63, 64 (east), 66-69 (north), 70 and 72 (west), and two doors south into Great Hall 43.
- **Down to level 2:** (a) **18** spiral stair (50’) to L2 29; (b) **53** stair (50’, reverse C) to L2 18; (c) **75** vault under the shrine, long stair 35’ to L2 1 (Idimmu's tribute); (d) **42** well shaft, 50’ down at a 60° slope to the ceiling of L2 20.
- **Secret doors:** 9↔32, 31↔32, 44↔74, 74↔72, 65↔57. Hidden caches: 4 (ruby), 14 (gems under statue), 37 (copper key), 39 (compartment), 51 (silver key).
- **Rooftops:** open-air rooms 13, 18, 19, 42, 61 (climb 12’5”).

## Faction relations (level 1)
- **Kobolds (Area 1):** first to face intruders; mostly left alone by the rest; charge tribute (Urza: 10gp a head, then leave the party alone for good). Urza wants the Lady's approval: may attack, betray or conspire.
- **Brigands (Area 2):** induct the party if it seems "their sort"; defend hard (the noose awaits). Drusus allies readily, dislikes most beastman tribes, would use the party as cat's paws. Lost a patrol below (L2 wandering brigands).
- **Orcs (Area 3):** loyal to the Lady (have seen her strength); toll **20gp per person** to pass 26/27 (and generally "at least 20gp" through their area). Some orcs scheme to wipe out a rival faction for her reward.
- **Goblins (Area 4):** wiped out days ago by the hobgoblins; area unclaimed. Newly arrived goblins may want revenge.
- **Hobgoblins (Area 5):** victorious but weak (chief near death); bluff, **+1 to reactions** unless attacked; split: Thethamun + Shulgi vs Abgal + Hepteth (who would sacrifice the chief to the Lady).
- **Ogre (44):** independent; 100gp or meat or it attacks.
- **Idimmu (Area 6):** second only to the Lady; kobolds of Area 6 serve him in terror; tribute **100gp per character** to go below; covets the Stone.
- **Red Eye gnolls (from L2):** the Lady's favoured; bully the lesser humanoids for tribute. The Lady rewarded them for eliminating a faction.
- **Possessed crab spider (56):** serves the Stone and Zakiti; eats strays.

## Area 1 — Kobold territory (pp. 36-40)
Entry way and the south-west. Wandering: 5-6 on 1d6 each turn.

### 1. Entry Way
- **Exits:** open to the outside (130’ wide, 30’ high crack); N: massive bronze double doors (between serpent columns) to 2. Rubble along the south and west edges (map).
- Daylight lights the south wall: serpent columns; 10’ glazed-brick mosaics of a two-headed winged serpent encircling the earth (lore: the Chthonic goddess Sakkara, as in the Canticles of Xisuthros).
- **4 kobolds** on guard at the doors, passively watching → [[Kobold]]: MV 60’, AC 2, HD 1d4, hp 4, 3, 3, 2, D 1d6−1, Save 0-lvl, ML −2, XP 5. Two have short bows and 10 arrows.
- Challenge anyone not known; the party may talk its way in if it does not attack first. If attacked: two flee inside to warn guard room 3, two hold the door until morale breaks.

### 2. The Great Hall
- **Exits:** S double bronze doors to 1; N doors to 3 (west end) and 19 (middle); S door to 13 (east part); E double doors to 14; the west end turns south as a passage to 11's door and rubble at the crack's west edge (map; whether it is passable into 1 is unclear).
- 20’ × 270’. North wall frescoes, west to east:
  - **2a:** human slaves led by a lean, brown, long-headed man rising against lizardman masters (lore: Zaharan rebellion against the Thrassians, 1060 BE; Uragasi, conqueror of Thrassia, first sorcerer-king, then seen as a liberator).
  - **2b:** three slender dark brown-skinned kings — warrior, mage, priest (lore: Uragasi; Djarghul, creator of the beastmen; Mentu the death priest, at Zahar's apex).
  - **2c:** red canals flowing into a temple, above which an incomplete, broken figure sits (lore: matches no known Zaharan iconography).
  - **2d:** impaled prisoners while demons and Zaharans dance in their blood (lore: from the Canticle of Xisuthros).

### 3. Kobold Guard Room
- **Exits:** S door to 2; W curtain to 4; E curtain to 5.
- 30’ × 20’. **6 kobolds**, **Distracted** (two biggest tossing the smallest one's purse); they stop to investigate noise in 2. Morale break → flee to 4. Fight here wakes room 4 (arrive in 3 rounds).
- [[Kobold]]: hp 4, 4, 3, 3, 2, 1; AC 2, D 1d6−1, ML −2, XP 5.
- Treasure: 1d6sp each; the two toughest 10gp each in small purses. Bedding, one-legged table, gnawed bones.

### 4. Kobold Quarters

- [[Kobold]]: hp 3 each; AC 2, D 1d6−1, ML −2, XP 5.
- Treasure: scattered in bedding 57cp, 23sp, 10gp. **Ruby (250gp)** hidden in a crack in the south wall — found as a secret door (Athak knows of it).

### 5. Empty Chamber
- **Exits:** W curtain to 3; N open to 6.
- 20’ × 30’ antechamber; desiccated rat skins, smashed clay bowl.

### 6. King's Guards
- **Exits:** S open to 5; W door to 9 (braziers either side); E curtain to 7.
- **2 burly kobolds**, always actively watching. Each has a flask of oil. Expecting attack, they oil the floor between 5 and 6; when intruders come they topple the burning braziers to light it and flee into 9.
- [[Kobold]]: hp 4, 4; AC 2, D 1d6−1, **ML 0** (Urza's +2), XP 5.

### 7. Carnal Chamber
- **Exits:** W curtain to 6; E curtain to 8 (the room is the cell north of a U-shaped wall; map).

- **Optional rule (p. 39):** a character with Seduction who studies the frescoes 12 turns can use the Erotic Arts: the partner saves vs Spells or is charmed (as charm person) and loses 1 WIS; using them is worship of Nasga and shifts the user's alignment to Chaotic.

### 8. Lounge

- Ten chair-sized stone basins caked with very ancient dried blood along the south wall.

### 9. The King's Chamber
- **Exits:** E door to 6; open SW into 10; **secret door N wall (NW corner) to 32** (unknown to all kobolds).

- **Athak** (champion) → [[Athak (Sakkara)]]: AC 3, HD 1−1, hp 4, D 1d6, ML 0, XP 5. Tarnished silver ring (10gp). Offers the ruby's location (room 4) to save his life.

### 10. The King's Treasure

Among it: **1,000cp; 10 rolls red-dyed cloth (10gp, 4 st each); 14 bone fetishes (32gp each); red-gloss porcelain vase showing the city of Aura (700gp, 1 st when properly protected); 2 agates (25gp each); 7 Opelenean tapestries (20gp, 5 st each).**

### 11. Abandoned Room

- 30’ × 40’, SE corner collapsed. A dusty skeleton in "white rags" south of the curtain: the rags are thick webs holding **crab spider eggs** (from 12). Alchemy or Animal Training appraises them at **295gp** to an exotic animal trainer or mage. Finding them alerts the party (no surprise penalty in 12).

### 12. Old Storage Chamber
- **Exits:** E curtain to 11.

### 13. Old Garden
- **Exits:** N door to 2. **Open-air** (rooftop access); rock dome 20’ above here; collapsed rocks loom from the south wall.
- 20’ × 40’ bare earth.
