# SOTN Chartreuse Death Orb

Judge-only. SOTN item ([[SOTN Magic Items]]): a transparent oval orb of sickly yellow-green fluid. Thrown, it shatters: acid splashes a 15' radius, 3d6 acid damage, half on a Blast save; on a failed save 1d6 more on each of the next three rounds unless washed off with water. Single use. Value 1,500gp (SOTN). Apparent value 25gp (Judge's call). Found F17 ([[Nethercity F - Lower Level Central Complex]]).
