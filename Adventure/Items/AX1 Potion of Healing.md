# AX1 Potion of Healing

Judge-only. AX1 (ACKS I) "potion of healing", which ACKS II does not have. Ruling (Rulings.md 2026-11-20): one dose heals as *cure light wounds* (1d6+1 hp) when drunk; base cost 250gp (Judge's call: between the TT healing salve and a 1st-level spell scroll). Found in L1 39 (brown syrup, faint honey smell).
