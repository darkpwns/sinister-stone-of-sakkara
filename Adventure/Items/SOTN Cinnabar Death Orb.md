# SOTN Cinnabar Death Orb

Judge-only. SOTN item ([[SOTN Magic Items]]): a transparent oval orb of silver liquid. Thrown, it shatters: burning liquid in a 15' radius, 5d6 fire damage, half on a Blast save; sets flammables alight; a creature that failed takes 1d6 more fire damage the next round. Single use. Value 3,000gp (SOTN; F17's 'silver' orb read as cinnabar, Judge's call). Apparent value 25gp (Judge's call). Found F17 ([[Nethercity F - Lower Level Central Complex]]).
