# SOTN Cobalt Death Orb

Judge-only. SOTN item (D5 vault, two in a padded cedar box; [[SOTN Magic Items#Death Orb]]): an egg-sized sphere of
blue metal (D5 key; the general entry describes a transparent orb of glowing blue gas). Thrown, it shatters and
explodes for 6d6 fire damage in a 15' radius, half on a Blast save; may ignite flammables. Single use. Value 3,000gp
(SOTN); base cost here the same (Judge's call). Apparent value 25gp (a curious metal egg; Judge's call).
