# SOTN Flying Disk

Judge-only. SOTN item (F15, under the glass dome; [[SOTN Magic Items#Flying Disk]], SOTN pp. 138-139): a disc of
shimmering, rainbow-sheened metal (8' across in the room text, 6' in the item list: 8'), 1' thick, small scoop-like
machines underneath. Only an arcane spellcaster can steer it, telepathically, standing on it; hovers up to 30', moves
150' (50'); carries two man-sized riders (500 lb); one energy crystal runs it 6 hours (none in it: it hangs inert
until one is fitted; the F17 fire lance holds five). Value 78,000gp. Weight (Judge's call): 4 st, two bearers
(iridescent metal, lighter than steel). Apparent value 1,000gp (Judge's call: a strange metal disc of fine work).
