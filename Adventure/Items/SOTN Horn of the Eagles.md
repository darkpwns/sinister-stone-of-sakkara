# SOTN Horn of the Eagles

Judge-only. SOTN item (K6 tribute pile; [[SOTN Magic Items#Horn of the Eagles]], SOTN p. 132): a carved ivory war-horn.
Blown, it summons a large roc as a winged steed, which serves a Lawful blower for up to a day; once a week. Not the
ACKS II [[Horn of the Eagles]] (TT, 2d4 giant raptors, 1/day), whose base cost (25,000gp) is used here (Judge's call);
apparent value 750gp (SOTN K6). Weight 1/6 st.
