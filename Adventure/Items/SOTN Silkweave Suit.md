# SOTN Silkweave Suit

Judge-only. SOTN item ([[SOTN Magic Items]]): a suit of very light, breathing, bio-engineered fibre like spider silk; weighs as heavy clothing. AC 4 (as leather armour +2); wearable by any character able to wear leather armour. Protects from ambient temperatures from -20 to 50 C (as Protection from Temperature in that range). Value 15,000gp (SOTN). Taken from the Great Teacher's body, day 614 (S44).
