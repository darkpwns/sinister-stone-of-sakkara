# AX3 Potion of Giant Strength

Judge-only. AX3 p. 91 (ACKS I) "potion of giant strength", carried by Provost Mentenus Cavië; ACKS II has no such
potion. Reading (AX3 Magic Items: "spell potion of Giant Strength"): one dose has the effect of the spell [[Giant
Strength]] (Arcane 4) on the drinker for its duration (3 turns). Base cost 1,000gp (Judge's call: 250gp per spell
level, as the AX1 healing potion's 250gp for a 1st-level effect). A pale grey draught smelling of iron.
