# SOTN Spellsword's Spellbook

Judge-only. SOTN item (F9, the mummy spellsword's annex; [[SOTN Magic Items#Spellsword's Spellbook]], SOTN p. 137):
vellum sheets glued in a roll, Ancient Argollëan left to right in black ink, every page with tactical diagrams;
bronze handles shaped as sword hilts; leather tie. Worth 5,550gp (SOTN); not treasure for XP unless sold (the other
SOTN spellbooks were entered the same way: base cost the book's value, apparent 100gp). Spells (ACKS II readings in
SOTN Magic Items): 1st Fan of Flames, Mage Missile, unholy circle; 2nd Illusory Duplicates, Necromantic Potence, Ogre
Strength; 3rd Fireball, Flight, Thunderbolt; 4th Arcane Shift, Giant Strength, iron maiden; 5th Halt Monsters,
protection from normal weapons. Necromantic Potence: the Tower's oath requires black-magic formulas be handed over.
