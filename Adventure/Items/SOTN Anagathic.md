# SOTN Anagathic

Judge-only. SOTN item ([[SOTN Magic Items]]): a prepacked disposable automatic syringe of ultratech chemicals and nano-bots; one dose. Injected, the character lives five years without ageing (those years do not count toward his biological age). Value 3,000gp (SOTN). Taken from the Great Teacher's body, day 614 (S44).
