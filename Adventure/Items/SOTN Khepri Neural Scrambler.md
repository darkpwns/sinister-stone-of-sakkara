# SOTN Khepri Neural Scrambler

Judge-only. SOTN item ([[SOTN Magic Items#Khepri Neural Scrambler]]): a palm-sized device of black chitin-like metal with a socket for energy crystals. Activated (a combat action), it scrambles the minds of up to 24 HD of living creatures within 15', fewest HD first: each saves vs Implements or is paralysed 30 rounds, losing the memory of the 30 rounds before and forming none of the 30 rounds after. Holds up to five [[SOTN Magic Items#Energy Crystal|energy crystals]]; each activation uses one. Value 150,000gp (SOTN). Apparent value 100gp (Judge's call). Taken from the Great Teacher by Vestus on day 611 (S44), **empty** (no crystal left).
