# SOTN Verdigris Death Orb

Judge-only. SOTN item ([[SOTN Magic Items]]): a transparent oval orb of coiling green-blue gas. Thrown, it shatters: a greenish cloud of 10' radius; living creatures in it save vs Paralysis or fall unconscious for 1d6 turns; the gas disperses after one round. Single use. Value 2,500gp (SOTN). Apparent value 25gp (Judge's call). Found F17 ([[Nethercity F - Lower Level Central Complex]]).
