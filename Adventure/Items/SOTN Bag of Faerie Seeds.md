# SOTN Bag of Faerie Seeds

Judge-only. SOTN item (B4 chest; [[SOTN Magic Items#Bag of Faerie Seeds]]): a plain leather bag of 5 seeds. ACKS II
equivalent: [[Faerie Seed]] (TT), base cost 2,500gp per seed; base cost here 5 × 2,500gp (Judge's call). Apparent
value 5gp (SOTN B4: "a leather bag of tree seeds"). Anything else put in the bag destroys the seeds left in it.
