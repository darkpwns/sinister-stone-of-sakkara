# SOTN Funerary Barge of the Cults

Judge-only. SOTN item (P11; [[SOTN Magic Items#Funerary Barge of the Cults]]): a black flat-bottomed cedar river barge
with bronze panels and a brass skull on its prow, 25' x 10', 200 st. Value 68,000gp. Apparent value 2,000gp (Judge's
call: a fine small barge, RR Vessels). Teleports once a day within 360' with a corpse in its cabin when its oar is
rowed in its pattern (the rubbing from the P12 basin); moves by magic at up to 60' a turn (36 miles a day on open
water). AC 1, 45 SHP.
