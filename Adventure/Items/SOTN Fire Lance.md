# SOTN Fire Lance

Judge-only. SOTN item ([[SOTN Magic Items]]): a 4' lance with a wide mouth at one end and a crossbow-like grip at the other. Turns an energy crystal into a cone of liquid fire 30' long, 10' wide: 5d6 fire damage, half on a Blast save; anyone damaged takes 1d6 more fire the next round; flammables ignite. One crystal a blast; holds five (found loaded with five). Usable only by classes with the fighter's attack progression (Judge's call, SOTN). Value 25,000gp (crystals not included). Apparent value 100gp (Judge's call). Found F17 ([[Nethercity F - Lower Level Central Complex]]).
