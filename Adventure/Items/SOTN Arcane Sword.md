# SOTN Arcane Sword

Judge-only. SOTN item ([[SOTN Magic Items]]): a bladeless cylindrical hilt of dark metal. A telepathic command makes a blade of crackling blue lightning spring out (2d6 one-handed, 2d8 two-handed, extraordinary electrical damage; Judge's call); one energy crystal powers 30 rounds of activation (F17's has 8 rounds left). Usable by all and only arcane spellcasting classes. Value 29,500gp. Apparent value 100gp (Judge's call). Found F17 ([[Nethercity F - Lower Level Central Complex]]).
