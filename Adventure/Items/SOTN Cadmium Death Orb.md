# SOTN Cadmium Death Orb

Judge-only. SOTN item ([[SOTN Magic Items]]): a transparent oval orb of foggy red-orange vapour. Thrown, it shatters: a 10' diameter cloud of deadly poison gas as Cloud of Poison (each creature in it saves vs Death: under 5 HD dies on a failure, 5 HD or more takes 1 damage); drifts 10' a round away from the thrower for 1d6 rounds, then disperses. Single use. Value 1,500gp (SOTN). Apparent value 25gp (Judge's call). Found F17 ([[Nethercity F - Lower Level Central Complex]]).
